# Canonical content sections

This is the heading contract for content pages. It defines order, not a quota:
omit a section when no sourced material exists, and never create an empty
heading or pad a page to make the outline look complete. Facts remain in the
facts panel; prose explains context, evidence and conflicts rather than
restating that panel.

Pages with three or more actual h2 sections get a generated, inline “On this
page” navigation. Inline placement keeps it in reading and keyboard order,
works in static HTML without JavaScript, and does not compete with the rail
spine at desktop widths. H2 and H3 anchors come from the rendered headings.
The ToC is omitted from print because the headings and full primary content
print in place.

## Rail stations

Overview; Location and siting; Construction; Architecture and design; Exits
and facilities; Public art; Naming; Incidents; Connections and service;
References.

## Bus routes

Overview; Corridor; Termini; Interchanges; Service and class; Fares; Operator;
History; Stop sequence; References.

## Operators

Overview; History; Ownership and regulation; Fleet; Depots; Routes; Incidents;
References.

## Vehicle models and rolling stock

Overview; Procurement; Manufacturer and delivery; Technical specification;
Allocation and operation; Refurbishment; Withdrawal or current status;
References.

## Depots and operating sites

Overview; Site selection and location; Construction; Track connection;
Capacity and allocation; Workshops and facilities; Operational history;
References.

## Rail and gondola lines

Overview; Route and alignment; Planning and construction history; Engineering;
Stations; Rolling stock; Depots; Operations; Incidents and disputes;
References.

## Cross-cutting history

Overview; chronological or thematic sections specific to the subject;
conflicts in the evidence; References. History pages should not be forced into
an entity outline when a chronology or several parallel threads explain the
subject more honestly.

## Ticketing

Overview; Eligibility or media; Purchase and validation; Fares and transfers;
Refunds and replacement; Network coverage; Dated changes; References.

## Ferries

Overview; Route and piers; Operators and vessels; Service; Fares and payment;
Weather and operating constraints; History; References.

## YouBike

Overview; Coverage and station data; Eligibility and account use; Hire and
return; Fares; System history; Data limitations and conflicts; References.
Browse indexes may use geographic headings instead of this narrative outline.

## Taichung Metro

Use the same line, station, operator and rolling-stock contracts as the Taipei
rail system. Operator namespaces are mandatory wherever a code is reused;
headings do not encode the namespace.
