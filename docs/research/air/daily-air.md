## What is established

### Daily Air publishes five fixed-wing island route families

- **Claim:** Daily Air's fixed-wing service page says it operates five island routes: Taitung–Lanyu, Taitung–Green Island, Kaohsiung–Qimei, Kaohsiung–Wangan and Penghu–Qimei.
- **Source:** Service items / `服務項目`; Daily Air (德安航空股份有限公司); https://www.dailyair.com.tw/dailyair/page/FlightInfo/; PRIMARY; High — the operator's full page was fetched directly on 6 September 2026.
- **Original-language sentence:** `經營離島航線數：計五條航線：東部區塊：台東-蘭嶼、台東-綠島。西部區塊：高雄-七美、高雄-望安、澎湖-七美。`

### The operator identifies the aircraft types used

- **Claim:** The same page identifies 19-seat aircraft and DHC6-400 aircraft for these services.
- **Source:** Daily Air service items; same URL; PRIMARY; High.
- **Original-language sentence:** `本公司使用19人座及DHC6-400型機。`

### The published seasonal table gives different service patterns by route

- **Claim:** For 1 April–31 October, the operator's table states 16 round-trip sectors / 8 daily flights for Taitung–Lanyu, 6 sectors / 3 daily flights for Taitung–Green Island, 2 sectors / 1 daily flight for Penghu–Qimei, 4 sectors / 2 daily flights for Kaohsiung–Qimei, and 2 sectors / one Monday-and-Friday service for Kaohsiung–Wangan.
- **Claim:** For 1 November–31 March, the same table repeats the other four patterns but states Taitung–Lanyu as 16 round-trip sectors / 6 daily flights.
- **Source:** Daily Air service items; same URL; PRIMARY; High — these are the operator's own seasonal table entries, fetched in full.
- **Original-language sentences:** `每年 04 月 01 日～10 月 31 日` and the five route rows at the page's daily-service table; `每年 11 月 01 日～03 月 31 日` and the five winter rows.

## Conflicts

- The operator's winter table says Taitung–Lanyu has `往返計16架次（每日6班）`. Six daily flights and sixteen round-trip sectors do not form an unambiguous unit relationship in the text as published. This guide preserves the source wording and does not calculate a replacement number.
- The live page is a dated route-family presence surface, while the service page is a seasonal frequency summary. They are complementary publication layers, not evidence that every listed frequency was operating on every date in the live page's snapshot.
