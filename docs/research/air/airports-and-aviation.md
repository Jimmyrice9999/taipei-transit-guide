## What is established

**CAA's own airport list** (交通部民用航空局,「機場分佈圖」,
caa.gov.tw/Article.aspx?a=982&lang=1, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched in
full) names 17 airports under CAA administration, and gives two facts not
found elsewhere: Taoyuan is run by a dedicated corporation
(桃園國際機場股份有限公司) rather than a CAA aviation station directly, and
Songshan and Taichung are each explicitly marked 軍民合用 (joint
military-civil use) — Kaohsiung is not marked that way on the same list.

**Kaohsiung International Airport's history** (高雄國際航空站,
kia.gov.tw/filearea/history.html, PRIMARY, High confidence): 1944 airfield
(小港飛行場); CAA takeover 1965-07-01 forming 高雄航空站; upgraded to
international-station status 1969; international service opened October
1972 with China Airlines' Kaohsiung-Hong Kong route; new international
terminal 1997-01-11; Kaohsiung MRT Red Line connection 2008. Runway stated
elsewhere on the same operator's site as 3,150 x 60 m.

**Matsu's two fields** (馬祖航空站, msa.gov.tw/airport-info/66, PRIMARY, High
confidence): Nangan approved 1997-12-31, built 1998-2002 at a stated cost of
NT$2.276bn, opened 2003-01-23. Beigan opened 1994-01-17 (民國八十三年元月十七日
— independently re-converted this run and confirmed correct against a prior
run's flagged uncertainty). Beigan's runway was relocated 160m east and
rebuilt at 1,150 x 30m starting July 2000 (民國89年7月, not "1989" as an
earlier paraphrase in the corpus had it — this is a resolved misreading, not
a genuine two-source conflict; see Checked and failed).

**Kinmen Airport** (金門航空站, kma.gov.tw/DefPage/index.aspx?MenuID=472,
PRIMARY, High confidence): airfield built 1949; weekly flights approved June
1951; service stopped 1958-08-23 during the 823 artillery bombardment;
relocated to 尚義; first civil Kinmen-Taipei flight (遠東航空) September 1987;
formally established as a Class-C station 1994-03-01; new East Terminal
(NT$300m) opened February 2010; upgraded to Class-B status 2011-01-01.

**Penghu Airport** (澎湖航空站, mkport.gov.tw, PRIMARY, High confidence):
CAT began C-46 service 1957; terminal construction and a waiting room under
Kaohsiung Aviation Station began 1966; formally established as a Class-C
station 1977-08-01 (both dates are on the same page describing two different
events, not a conflict); took over Qimei/Wangan stations 1991-05-11;
upgraded to Class-B 1993-01-18; Magong/Qimei/Wangan stations merged into one
Penghu Aviation Station effective 2023-09-15.

**Island-fare subsidy law** (離島地區居民航空票價補貼辦法, law.moj.gov.tw,
PRIMARY statute text, High confidence): legal basis Civil Aviation Act
Art.55(6) and Offshore Islands Development Act Art.15-1(2). Subsidy rates:
20% Penghu-Magong/Kinmen-Shangyi, 30% Lienchiang (Matsu) Nangan/Beigan, 40%
Penghu Qimei/Wangan and Taitung Lanyu/Ludao, plus a 10% supplemental subsidy
for Penghu/Kinmen residents travelling to Taiwan proper. Last amended
2014-01-06.

**CAA's recurring operating-loss subsidy scheme** (caa.gov.tw/Article.aspx?a=1460,
PRIMARY, High confidence the scheme exists and is administered annually by
fiscal year; Low confidence on any specific amount, since the per-year files
were not opened this run).

**UNI Air** (uniair.com.tw, PRIMARY, High confidence): predecessor 馬公航空
(Makung Airlines); EVA Air Group bought in 1995; renamed 立榮航空 1996; 1998
merger absorbed 大華航空, 台灣航空 and EVA Air's own domestic routes, becoming
Taiwan's largest domestic-route carrier per its own account; new CI
2012-03-30. Ground service points at 11 airports (Songshan, Taichung,
Chiayi, Tainan, Kaohsiung, Taitung, Hualien, Penghu, Kinmen, Matsu Nangan,
Matsu Beigan) — not Lanyu or Green Island.

**Mandarin Airlines** (mandarin-airlines.com, PRIMARY, High confidence):
founded 1991-06-01, joint venture of China Airlines and 和信集團 (Hsin Hwa
Group). Domestic network per its own booking interface: Songshan (base),
Taichung, Kaohsiung, Hualien, Taitung, Penghu, Kinmen, Matsu Nangan — 7
destinations, narrower than and not identical to UNI Air's list (no Chiayi,
Tainan or Matsu Beigan).

**CAA aggregate passenger statistics** (caa.gov.tw/StatisticChart.aspx?a=215,
PRIMARY, High confidence, all-airport combined not broken out per airport):
2020 total 18,997,544 (int'l/cross-strait 8,884,958 / domestic 10,112,586);
2021 7,597,391; 2022 15,485,622; 2023 52,043,278; 2024 63,964,090; 2025
68,893,910 (int'l/cross-strait 58,329,512 / domestic 10,564,398). The
collapse and recovery is overwhelmingly an international/cross-strait
phenomenon; domestic volumes barely moved across the whole period.

## Conflicts found

None across independent primary sources this run. One apparent conflict
resolved on inspection: Penghu's 1966 and 1977 dates are on the same
primary page describing two different events (terminal construction start
vs. formal station establishment), not a contradiction between sources.

## Checked and failed

- **"Matsu Beigan runway relocated July 1989"** — a paraphrase circulating
  from an earlier pass at this subject. Checked 2026-08-29: the primary
  msa.gov.tw text reads 民國89年7月, which converts to July 2000, not 1989.
  Not republished; the 2000 date is what the site now carries.
- **"Kaohsiung airport is 軍民合用 (joint military-civil use)"** — a framing
  that circulates in general reputation/secondary coverage. Checked
  2026-08-29 against CAA's own airport list, which marks Songshan and
  Taichung that way explicitly but does not mark Kaohsiung the same way.
  Not published as a claim about Kaohsiung; the built page states CAA's
  actual list content instead.
- **Taoyuan runway dimensions (commonly cited 3,660m/3,800m) and a
  third-runway target date** — checked 2026-08-29, every fetch attempt at
  taoyuanairport.com.tw (root and the third-runway project page) returned
  only a bare page title with no body content (JS-rendered SPA). Not
  published; recorded as TBC on the built page instead.
- **Taichung Airport opening date (commonly cited March 2004) and 2016
  rename** — checked 2026-08-29, tca.gov.tw pages either 404'd or returned
  no extractable body text. Not published; TBC on the built page.
- **Daily Air (德安航空)'s fleet, route network and schedule for
  Lanyu/Green Island** — checked 2026-08-29 and repeatedly in Run 304,
  dailyair.com.tw returns ECONNREFUSED on every attempt across two runs now.
  Not published beyond noting the operator's name and general service area
  from secondary sources; a working fetch of this site remains the way to
  settle it.
