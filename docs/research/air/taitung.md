## What is established

### Airport role, administration and facilities

- **Taitung Airport was established on 1 October in ROC year 70, became responsible for Lanyu and Green Island airports in July of ROC year 79, and was upgraded to a Class-B airport on 1 June in ROC year 90.** **Source:** *Airport introduction*, titleOriginal `航站介紹`, Taitung Airport, Civil Aeronautics Administration, `https://www.tta.gov.tw/StaticPage/AboutAirport`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full official page was fetched. Original-language basis: `臺東航空站成立於民國七十年十月一日，並於七十九年七月兼管蘭嶼與綠島航空站。九十年六月一日升格為乙種航空站。`
- **The airport's own introduction gives a 2,438 m by 45 m runway, a 5,178 m² terminal, capacity of 345 passengers per hour and annual capacity of 860,000 passengers.** **Source:** the same full official airport-introduction page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `跑道 | 長2,438公尺、寬45公尺，適合B-757以下各型機起降。` and `航廈 | 本航站面積為5,178平方公尺，可容納每小時345人次，年運量八十六萬人次。`
- **The airport's published address is No. 1100, Minhang Road, Taitung City, postal code 95063.** **Source:** *Traffic information*, titleOriginal `聯外交通`, Taitung Airport, `https://www.tta.gov.tw/StaticPage/Traffic`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `航站地址：95063 臺東市民航路1100號`.

### Dated aviation snapshot

- **The airport's live home page dated 6 September 2026 displayed Daily Air services to Lanyu and Green Island and UNI Air service to Taipei at the time it was fetched.** **Source:** *Taitung Airport*, English home page, Taitung Airport, `https://www.tta.gov.tw/english`, PRIMARY, High confidence for that dated display only. Original-language/English basis: the dated `2026 / 09 / 06` live-flight table with `Daily`, `LanYu KYD`, `GNI GNI` and `UNI AIR`, `Taipei TSA` rows.
- **Daily Air's own fixed-wing service page says it operates five island route families, including Taitung–Lanyu and Taitung–Green Island, and identifies 19-seat and DHC6-400 aircraft.** **Source:** *Service items*, titleOriginal `服務項目`, Daily Air, `https://www.dailyair.com.tw/dailyair/page/FlightInfo/`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the operator's full page was fetched. Original-language basis: `經營離島航線數：計五條航線：東部區塊：台東-蘭嶼、台東-綠島。西部區塊：高雄-七美、高雄-望安、澎湖-七美。` and `本公司使用19人座及DHC6-400型機。`
- **The airport's own page warns that weather and temporary scheduling can affect flights and that actual operation follows airline announcements.** **Source:** the same full Taitung Airport home page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `航班可能受天候或臨時航班調度影響，航班實際飛航狀況仍以各航空公司公告為準。`

### Ground access and multimodal limits

- **The airport says passengers can reach it from the city bus terminal by an airport shuttle, and from the new Taitung Railway Station by continuing by city bus to the bus terminal or by taxi.** **Source:** *Traffic information*, titleOriginal `聯外交通`, Taitung Airport, `https://www.tta.gov.tw/StaticPage/Traffic`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `由客運總站搭乘機場支線專車即可到達` and `於臺東火車新站下車之後，可搭乘以下交通工具：1. 搭乘市區公車至客運總站... 2. 直接搭乘計程車至本航空站`.
- **The fetched airport pages do not establish a direct rail platform, airport-station complex or a current bus route number/frequency.** **Source:** the full airport traffic page and home page, PRIMARY, High confidence for the limits of those pages; no name or geography inference is promoted. The content page therefore leaves a direct TRA interchange and current bus frequency TBC.

## Conflicts

- The live airport page is a same-day operational display, while Daily Air's service page is a route-family and seasonal-service description. They are complementary snapshots, not a timeless national flight count. The content page keeps the airport's current route families dated and avoids treating the displayed flights as a guaranteed future timetable.
- The airport page says that it also manages Lanyu and Green Island airports, while this new page is specifically the Taitung mainland airport node. The three airports are therefore described as an administrative/connectivity relationship, not collapsed into one physical terminal.
