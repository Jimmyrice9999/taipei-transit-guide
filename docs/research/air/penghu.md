## What is established

### Airport administration and physical node

- **The airport is the Civil Aeronautics Administration's Penghu Aviation Station at Aimen Village, Huxi Township, Penghu County.** **Source:** *Airport introduction*, titleOriginal `機場簡介`, Penghu Airport, `https://www.mkport.gov.tw/DefPage/index.aspx?MenuID=7A216129C10C7C62`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full official page was fetched. Original-language basis: `名稱：交通部民用航空局澎湖航空站` and `地址：88593澎湖縣湖西鄉隘門村126-5號`.
- **The present Penghu Aviation Station consolidated Magong, Qimei and Wang'an airports effective 15 September 2023.** **Source:** the same full official introduction page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `同年9月15日生效改制...馬公航空站、七美航空站、望安航空站整併為澎湖航空站，統轄澎湖、七美、望安機場`.
- **The main airport page gives a 3,000 m by 45 m runway and a 44,048 m² station area.** **Source:** the same full official introduction page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `航站面積：44,048㎡` and `跑道長度：長3000公尺，寬45公尺`.
- **The official introduction describes the new terminal as designed for 4.4 million annual passengers and equipped with three air bridges and nine stands for aircraft up to and including A321.** **Source:** the same full official introduction page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `以年旅客量440萬人次為設計容量`, `配置空橋（三座）` and `可提供A-321型(含)以下飛機使用之停機坪九機位`.

### Dated scheduled aviation

- **The airport's live fixed-departure page was displaying a 1–13 September 2026 window when fetched and exposed current rows to Taipei, Taichung, Tainan, Chiayi, Kaohsiung and Kinmen.** **Source:** *Penghu Airport Schedule — Schedule*, titleOriginal `固定航班`, Penghu Airport, `https://www.mkport.gov.tw/English/schedule/FixFlight_Dep.aspx`, PRIMARY, High confidence for the displayed window, not for a timeless route list. Original-language basis: the table headers `Destination`, `Departure`, `Arrival`, `STARTDATE`, `ENDDATE`, the 2026/09/01–2026/09/13 dates, and rows such as UNI Air 8690 to Kaohsiung, 8676 to Tainan, Mandarin 362 to Taipei, UNI Air 8662 to Chiayi and UNI Air 8995 to Kinmen.
- **The Civil Aeronautics Administration's monthly domestic timetable page lists September 2026 as an available month and warns that its timetable is reference material whose correct time remains the airline's announcement.** **Source:** *Domestic scheduled-route flight timetable*, titleOriginal `國內定期航線班機時刻表`, CAA, `https://www.caa.gov.tw/RegularFlight.aspx?a=268&lang=1`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `1 - 2026年9月` and `僅供參考，如有異動，正確時間仍以航空公司公告為準`.

### Ground and maritime links

- **The airport's external-transport page places the public-bus stop at the south side of the terminal exit and links both Penghu County's Public Vehicle and Ship Management Office buses and the Taiwan Tourist Shuttle Penghu Airport Express.** **Source:** *Bus information*, titleOriginal `公車資訊`, Penghu Airport, `https://www.mkport.gov.tw/DefPage/index.aspx?MenuID=5579DE11BE410ED0`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `公共汽車招呼站設於航廈南側出口處`, `澎湖縣政府〖公共車船管理處〗` and `台灣好行〖澎湖空港快線〗`.
- **The airport's passenger-service page states that the service desk runs daily from 07:00 until the last flight and gives the airport address and telephone contact.** **Source:** *Monthly flight timetable subscription*, titleOriginal `訂閱每月份班機時刻表`, Penghu Airport, `https://www.mkport.gov.tw/flight/flightTable.aspx?MenuID=BD25D14AFDF52A24&p=4D0A3774EB58F390`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `服務台服務時間：每日07:00-末班機起飛`, `服務台電話：(06)922-9123` and the Aimen Village address.
- **The airport's transport page links the Penghu County Public Vehicle and Ship Management Office's Nanhai Star vessels and the Penghu Port Building's passenger-ship information.** **Source:** *Shipping links*, titleOriginal `船運連結`, Penghu Airport, `https://www.mkport.gov.tw/DefPage/index.aspx?MenuID=0B9F5C743A37A844`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `澎湖縣政府公共車船管理處（南海之星、南海之星2號交通船）` and `港埠大樓（澎湖港）` with `客船航班訊息`.

## Conflicts

- The airport's fixed-flight page, the CAA monthly timetable and airline announcements are different layers of the same current service: the airport page exposes a rolling operational window, while CAA warns that airline announcements control after changes. They are not merged into one permanent route count.
- The airport page calls itself an aviation station that governs Penghu, Qimei and Wang'an airports, while the national airport list names those fields separately. This page preserves both administrative statements rather than treating them as contradictory airport counts.
