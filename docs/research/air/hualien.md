## What is established

### Airport role and history

- **Hualien Airport is a Civil Aeronautics Administration Class-B airport for domestic aviation, established on 15 May 1962.** **Source:** *Airport introduction*, titleOriginal `機場簡介`, Hualien Airport, CAA, `https://www.hulairport.gov.tw/HualienAirport?program=170`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full official page was fetched. Original-language basis: `於五十一年五月十五日成立花蓮航空站為飛航國內線之乙種航空站`.
- **The airport’s current public address is No. 1, Jichang Road, Jiali Village, Xincheng Township, Hualien County.** **Source:** Hualien Airport home page, titleOriginal `交通部民用航空局花蓮航空站`, `https://www.hulairport.gov.tw/`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `地址：花蓮縣新城鄉嘉里村機場路1號`.
- **The airport’s new terminal opened ceremonially on 18 March 2004 and began operating on 19 March 2004.** **Source:** full official airport-introduction page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `花蓮航空站新航廈於93年3月18日舉行啟用典禮，3月19日正式營運`.
