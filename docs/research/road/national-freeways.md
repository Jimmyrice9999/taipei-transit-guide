# Taiwan's national freeway network (國道) and electronic tolling

**Status: researched and built 29 August 2026 (Run 306, Part 6e).** No prior
docs/research file existed for this subject. Built as
`content/road/network/national-freeways.md`.

## What is established

**Route list and generational framing** (交通部高速公路局/Freeway Bureau,
freeway.gov.tw, PRIMARY, High confidence, multiple pages fetched in full):
National Highway 1 (中山高速公路) opened 1978 after 7 years' construction.
Highway 2 (機場支線/桃園環線) 20km, Highway 2A 2km (opened 2023-01-09).
Highway 3 (福爾摩沙高速公路) to km 431; Highway 3A (Taipei-Shenkeng) ~6km.
Highway 4 (台中環線) 28km. Highway 5 (蔣渭水高速公路) operational section
55km, Nangang-Su'ao, containing the Xueshan Tunnel (~12.9km, completed
2006-06-16, stated cost NT$90.6bn). Highway 6 (水沙連高速公路) ~38km,
Wufeng-Puli, the Bureau's own example of a "third generation" ecological/
curved-routing design philosophy. Highway 8 (台南支線) 15km. Highway 10
(高雄支線) 33km. Highway 7 (Kaohsiung) is NOT yet operational — still in
design/tendering per the Bureau's own project page, stated last updated
2025-11-20, ~23km, ~NT$150.17bn budget.

**ETC history** (freeway.gov.tw/Publish.aspx?cnid=1482&p=509 and cnid=133,
PRIMARY, High confidence): first tollbooth 1974-07-30 (Taishan). Per-trip
ETC lanes alongside manual booths from 2006-02-10, under a BOT model adopted
2003. Full switch to distance-based all-electronic tolling 2013-12-30, every
manned tollbooth closed. The Bureau's own page states 947 toll-collection
staff were let go in the switch, with the contract (FETC) required to fully
absorb affected staff by 2014-06-30 and a stated NT$4.5m contractual penalty
for late completion, which FETC ultimately prevailed in litigating.

**FETC's current contract renewal** (CNA, cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202512170152.aspx,
SECONDARY but a reputable wire-service report, fetched in full,
independently by this session rather than trusting the scout's paraphrase):
new contract 2024-12-22 to 2034-12-31; per-vehicle-km rate cut from
NT$0.070143 to NT$0.062; projected annual government payment falls from
~NT$22.6bn to ~NT$2bn at current traffic; model converts BOT to ROT
(assets transfer to government); toll-gate-to-control-centre data
transmission time cut from 7 to 2 minutes.

## Conflicts found

- **National Highway 1 length**: the Bureau's own history page states 372.5
  km; the Bureau's own current interchange-mileage table shows the route
  terminating at km 373. Same agency, two different figures, neither page
  explains the 0.5km gap. Published as an open conflict on the built page.
  (zh.wikipedia gives a third figure, 374.3km — not independently verified
  and not published.)
- **National Highway 5 length**: 55km (Bureau, explicitly labelled as the
  "operational section") vs. 61.0km (zh.wikipedia, unlabelled as to what it
  measures). Not resolved; only the 55km Bureau figure is published, with
  the conflict not mentioned on the built page since the second value was
  never independently verified against a primary source this run.
- **National Highway 6 length**: ~38km (Bureau's own history page, whole
  route) vs. 37.6km (a search-synthesis figure that may describe a
  "Nantou segment" specifically rather than the whole route) — not
  independently fetched from a primary page; only the Bureau's ~38km figure
  is published.
- **Toll-collector layoff headcount**: 947 (Bureau's own page) vs.
  "approximately 942-965" with a 486/456 severance/transfer split
  (zh.wikipedia, secondary). Only the Bureau's own 947 figure is published.
