## What is established

- **2025 annual ridership: 82.07 million passengers (up 4.88% on 2024's 78.25 million); 59,606 total train services; passenger-km 13,748 million (up 2.97%); seat-km 19,203 million; loading factor 71.59%.** Source: Taiwan High Speed Rail Corporation 2025 Annual Report (台灣高速鐵路股份有限公司2025年年報), `https://en.thsrc.com.tw/Corp/e5d9a893-ea05-4ff7-80e6-cc71c46d2fb4/assets/94446967-7d9a-4619-b349-61dcf64ae366.pdf`, PRIMARY, High confidence. This is the English-language edition; the report itself states the Chinese version prevails in case of conflict, and a Chinese-language original was not separately located. Quoted: "Passenger volumes for 2025 reached 82.07 million, up by 4.88% compared to 2024. Our total passenger-kilometers amounted to 13,748 million km, up by 2.97% compared to 2024."
- **2025 average daily ridership: 225,000, up from 214,000 in 2024.** Same report, Letter to Shareholders, PRIMARY, High confidence: "Average daily passenger volumes reached 225,000 passengers, an increase of more than 10,000 passengers per day compared to 2024 (214,000 passengers per day)."
- **Five-year Key Operating Statistics table (2021–2025), all PRIMARY, High confidence, same report:**
  | Year | Ridership (millions) | Loading factor | Punctuality (within 5 min) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 2021 | 43.46 | 49.88% | 99.00% |
  | 2022 | 54.16 | 53.31% | 99.47% |
  | 2023 | 73.09 | 70.67% | 99.58% |
  | 2024 | 78.25 | 71.73% | 99.50% |
  | 2025 | 82.07 | 71.59% | 99.31% |
  Five-year average punctuality stated in the report itself: 99.38%.
- **2025 punctuality 99.31% (down 0.19pp on 2024's 99.50%, "due to impacts from natural disasters"); reliability rate excluding natural-disaster delays: 100%.** Same report, PRIMARY, High confidence.
- **Weekly train services increased from 1,103 to 1,128 from 1 July 2025 (+25/week, +1,877 runs over the year vs 2024).** Same report, PRIMARY, High confidence: "THSRC added 25 train runs each week starting from July 1, 2025, increasing the number of weekly train services from 1,103 to 1,128 train runs."
- **Seats per train: 977 (911 standard + 66 business).** Same report's "Key Operating Statistics for 2025" summary box, PRIMARY, High confidence.
- **N700ST order: 12 new-generation trainsets (144 carriages), signed with Hitachi Toshiba Supreme Consortium (HTSC) 2023/05/18; expected to arrive in Taiwan August 2026 and enter service second half of 2027.** Same report, "Important Contracts" table and closing section, PRIMARY, High confidence.
- **Cumulative ridership passed 900 million in December 2024, and exceeded 984 million as of 31 December 2025.** Same report, "The Impact of Recent Major Capital Expenditures on Financial Operations", PRIMARY, High confidence: "the number of passengers surpassed the 900-million mark in December 2024" and "As of December 31, 2025, we have transported more than 984 million passengers."
- **600-million cumulative milestone reached 17 January 2020 (600,050,000+), about 1 year 6 months after the 500-million milestone on 23 July 2018.** Source: THSRC press release "「六億搭乘 好事加乘」台灣高鐵總旅運突破六億人次", `https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/89a9f9eb-acbc-418c-bd8e-a8f1394f5ac6`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Quoted: "台灣高鐵總旅運已在1月17日突破六億人次大關...6億零5萬餘人次"; "2018年7月23日突破五億人次...歷時僅約1年又6個月".
- **2019 (pre-pandemic) annual ridership 67.41 million, average daily ~184,000.** Same press release, PRIMARY, High confidence: "2019年全年旅運達6741萬餘人次...每日平均運量亦攀升至18萬4千餘人次。"
- **All-time single-day record: 338,000 passengers on 1 October 2023**, the highest since 2007 opening, surpassing the prior record of 318,250 on 15 September 2019 (both Mid-Autumn-holiday return days). Source: CNA (中央社), "中秋收假高鐵疏運33.8萬人次 通車以來單日新高", `https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202310020110.aspx`, SECONDARY, High confidence, fetched in full. Quoted: "超過33.8萬人次，為2007年通車以來單日最高"; "2019年9月15日的中秋疏運收假日，當時旅客量為31萬8250人次". No THSRC primary press release for this specific record was located — see Checked and failed.
- **2021 ridership recovery figures independently cross-checked between a 2022 news report and the 2025 annual report's own five-year table (exact match): 43.46 million, 49.88% loading factor.** Source: 聯合新聞網 (udn.com), "高鐵去年總運量減少24.07% 保持無傷亡事故紀錄" (found via search synthesis, not independently full-fetched) cross-checked against the THSR 2025 Annual Report table above — the two independently agree exactly, so treat the 2021 figures as effectively primary-confirmed.

## Conflicts

- **"1 billion by day 265" is wrong; the correct figure appears to be "10 million by day 265" (26 September 2007), but even the corrected figure is unverified.** An initial AI-tool summarisation pass of zh.wikipedia's THSR article stated a "1 billion passengers by day 265" milestone; re-querying the same page's footnote text for the specific milestone instead returned "開始營運265天後，台灣高鐵在2007年9月26日突破1千萬人次搭乘" (10 million, not 1 billion). Do not publish "1 billion by day 265" under any circumstance — it is a confirmed internal error. The corrected "10 million" figure itself rests only on an AI-tool's paraphrase of zh.wikipedia, not on a directly fetched primary or the wikipedia page's own raw text, so it is published on the content page as TBC, not as established.
- **2020 total annual ridership: ~57.2 million vs ~57.24 million**, two close but not identical secondary figures (one from an AI-summarized wikipedia read, one implied by a 2021 udn.com article's year-on-year comparison base), neither independently fetched and read in full. Likely the same underlying figure with rounding/transcription differences; not resolved. The primary THSR 2025 Annual Report's own five-year table only extends back to 2021, so no primary confirmation of any 2020 figure exists in what was fetched this run.
- **Daily average ridership as a time series, not a single competing figure**: 184,000/day (2019, primary), ~140,000/day (~Oct 2022, ~90% of pre-pandemic recovery, secondary — 自由時報, "平均每日14萬人次搭乘 高鐵董座：高鐵運量已達疫情前9成", `https://news.ltn.com.tw/news/life/breakingnews/4142432`, Medium confidence, attributed quote via news outlet not independently fetched from THSRC), 214,000/day (2024, primary), 225,000/day (2025, primary). Presented on the content page as a trend, not as competing measurements of one figure.
