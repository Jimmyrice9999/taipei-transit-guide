# THSR accessibility and station facilities — research findings, 28 August 2026

## What is established

- **Car 7 of every 700T carries 4 wheelchair spaces: seats 13A/14A (powered wheelchairs, with charging outlets) and 13E/14E (folding wheelchairs).** Source: THSRC, 無障礙服務, `https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/dba4ecd0-5ef0-45d2-aa64-bcecdf7d5fde`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched directly. Quoted: "第７節車廂內設有４席輪椅座位"; "座位13A、14A（設有充電插座）可停放電動輪椅，座位13E、14E可停放摺疊式輪椅". Corroborated independently on two further primary pages: 車廂服務 (`.../feb91116-8a73-46b7-a6b7-f115a3137ccf`) and 服務設施 (`.../b249199d-badc-44bd-90af-9a90cff556a3`), both PRIMARY, High confidence.
- **Car 7 has an accessible restroom** with a vacuum toilet, sink, hand dryer, soap dispenser, automatic faucet, movable grab bars, two emergency call buttons, an infant seat and a diaper-changing table. Source: 車廂服務, PRIMARY, High confidence: "第7節車廂玄関處設有一間無障礙廁所...坐式真空式馬桶、洗手台、烘手機、給皂器及自動感應水龍頭，一應俱全...馬桶旁備有可動式扶手、兩個求助鈴，以及嬰兒座椅、嬰兒尿布台".
- **Seats 13A/14A rotate 90 degrees** so a wheelchair user can transfer to the seat or remain in a fixed wheelchair, easing companion assistance. Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence: "座椅可旋轉90度，方便同行者照料".
- **Car 5 has a dedicated nursing room; every station also has one.** Same source and 服務設施, both PRIMARY, High confidence: "第5節車廂設有哺乳室"; "各車站均有設置哺集乳室".
- **Wheelchair-space bookings: up to 29 days ahead including travel day, to 1 hour before departure**, via digital chatbot, phone (6am–midnight) or station counter; a companion can be booked at the same time for adjoining seating. Source: 無障礙服務, PRIMARY, High confidence: "可預訂29天內（含乘車當日）至發車前1小時為止之車次"; "如有同行陪伴者亦可同時訂位".
- **Since a 2023 change, accessible-seat tickets booked via digital/phone channels can be paid for and collected via the T-EX app, partner convenience stores or station ticket machines, not only station windows.** Source: THSRC, "台灣高鐵無障礙座位 購票更友善...", `https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/605287ae-5e25-4c75-8be1-c231c9f2e022`, PRIMARY, High confidence.
- **Powered mobility device size limit: 125cm × 90cm × 185cm (L×W×H).** Source: 無障礙服務, PRIMARY, High confidence: "長度不得超過125公分、寬度不得超過90公分、高度不得超過185公分".
- **A station boarding-guidance service (乘車導引服務)** escorts elderly-alone or mobility-limited passengers from the non-paid area to the platform and back, excluding luggage handling and wheelchair assembly/disassembly; must be requested at least 1 hour before departure, passenger must arrive 20 minutes early. Source: THSRC, "愛，無礙 台灣高鐵無障礙服務...", `https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/337ea246-d700-4309-ab95-47800ec8c5ca`, PRIMARY, High confidence: "服務範圍由約定之站內非付費區至月台候車...導引人員不代為搬運行李及拆卸組裝輪椅"; "至遲於發車一小時前完成申請". Whether the escort service itself is free of charge is not directly quoted from a primary source and stays unconfirmed — do not publish as free.
- **Station-wide facilities (company-wide policy, not a station-by-station breakdown): accessible restrooms, elevators with internal emergency intercoms, accessible parking, a health/first-aid room staffed by a nurse at every station, AEDs at every station and on every train, a nursing room and a parent-child restroom at every station.** Source: THSRC, 服務設施, PRIMARY, High confidence: "高鐵各車站均有設置保健室，並有專業護理師駐點"; "高鐵各車站及列車均配置自動體外心臟電擊去顫器（AED）"; "各車站均有設置親子廁所".
- **Discounted (愛心票) fares for disability-certificate holders: 50% of full fare**, and for a "necessary companion" only when the certificate's reverse carries the specific "國內大眾運輸工具" annotation. Source: THSRC, 普通/優待/團體票種說明, PRIMARY, High confidence — overlaps the separate fares research; not duplicated in depth here.
- **Guide/service dogs and mobility-assistance dogs are exempt from THSR's general animal-container rule.** THSR generally requires small pets to be caged (≤55×45×38cm); working police dogs, guide dogs (導盲犬), hearing dogs (導聾犬), mobility-assistance dogs (肢體輔助犬), and guide-dog puppies-in-training with a professional trainer are explicitly exempted. Source: THSRC, 乘車須知, `https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/2f73bfbb-d9bb-400e-b806-f6c5ba539368`, PRIMARY, High confidence: "執行任務之警犬、導盲犬、導聾犬、肢體輔助犬或由專業人員（訓練師）陪同之導盲幼犬不在此限".
- **October 2018 disability-rights campaign** ("推它一把，大夥一起上！") protested the then-reported 989-seat/4-wheelchair-space ratio (~0.4%), demanding unreserved-seat access for wheelchair users in any car, additional multi-purpose priority seating, stricter ID checks, more fixed wheelchair spaces, and CRPD-compliant spaces in all newly purchased cars including business class. Source: NPOst 公益交流站, "高鐵輪椅席不足的抗議事件，到底在抗議什麼？", `https://npost.tw/archives/54002`, SECONDARY, Medium confidence (advocacy journalism; the primary advocacy-group PDF this article itself cites could not be fetched — HTTP 403 — so the campaign's own first-party document remains unread). THSR's reported response (34.8% wheelchair-seat utilisation cited, favourable peer comparison) is NPOst's account of THSR's position, not an independently fetched THSR statement.
- **No Control Yuan investigation or lawsuit specifically naming THSR on accessibility grounds was found** — searches surfaced Control Yuan cases against 內政部營建署, 衛生福利部, and TRA/Alishan Forest Railway, and lawsuits against TRA, but nothing specific to THSR. Reported as not found, not as confirmed absent.
