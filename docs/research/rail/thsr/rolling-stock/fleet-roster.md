## What is established

- **Manufacturer split is more precise than the common shorthand.** Kawasaki Heavy Industries, Nippon Sharyo and Hitachi built the 700T carbody; Toshiba designed the traction/auxiliary power system, with Mitsubishi Electric fabricating much of the traction equipment (220 traction motors, 21 main transformers, 45 traction converters for the first batch). Source: zh.wikipedia, 台灣高速鐵路700T型電聯車, `https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/台灣高速鐵路700T型電聯車`, SECONDARY, Medium confidence (the article's own citation trail points to an archived MOTC Railway Bureau page that could not be fetched — see Checked and failed). Quoted: "由川崎重工業、日本車輛製造、日立製作所等3間日本公司承造"; "列車的牽引及輔助供電系統由東芝進行設計...三菱電機代工生產了用於首批列車的220台牽引馬達、21台主變壓器和45台牽引湧壓變流器". The commonly repeated "Kawasaki, Nippon Sharyo, Hitachi and Toshiba built the 700T" conflates two different scopes of work; flagged explicitly on the content page.
- **Fleet size: 34 trainsets in service (30 original + 4 ordered November 2008, delivered 2012–2015).** Source: zh.wikipedia, same article, SECONDARY, Medium confidence. Independently corroborated by arithmetic in a CNA report on the N700ST order ("車隊規模將增加至46組" minus the 12 new sets = 34): 中央社, 高鐵N700ST新車 2027、2028年各6組投入營運, `https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202607290157.aspx`, SECONDARY, Medium confidence.
- **12-car formation, confirmed by a primary car-by-car seat table.** Source: THSRC, 車廂設施, `https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/52ffe06e-3e38-4352-8170-93a768905dbd`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched directly. Per-car seats: Car 1: 63, Car 2: 96, Car 3: 85, Car 4: 96, Car 5: 80, Car 6 (business): 66, Car 7: 61 (incl. 4 wheelchair spaces), Car 8: 96, Car 9: 85, Car 10: 96, Car 11: 85, Car 12: 68. Sum 977 = 911 standard + 66 business, stated directly: "第6節為商務車廂（66座位）、其餘11節為標準車廂（911座位）".
- **Seating capacity conflict: 977 vs 989 — see Conflicts.**
- **Maximum speed: 300 km/h commercial; 315 km/h test/contractual maximum per zh.wikipedia's 700T-specific article** (a different figure than the 350 km/h "design speed" reported by zh.wikipedia's general THSR article — see the technology research file's own conflict on this). Source: zh.wikipedia 700T article, citing a 2005-10-30 test report, SECONDARY, Medium confidence.
- **Commercial service began 5 January 2007.** Source: zh.wikipedia 700T article, citing an MOTC Railway Bureau document not independently fetched, SECONDARY, Medium confidence.
- **N700ST order confirmed via primary press release and multiple secondary sources.** THSR board resolved 15 March 2023; contract signed 18 May 2023 in Tokyo with the Hitachi–Toshiba consortium (HTSC), 12 trainsets (144 carriages), based on the Shinkansen N700S. Source: THSRC, "台灣高鐵公司與日立東芝聯盟 簽署新世代列車組採購契約", `https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/70a0efbb-3842-475a-98c7-72378cfd521e`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Corroborated by 自由時報, `https://news.ltn.com.tw/news/life/breakingnews/4305973`, SECONDARY, High confidence: "台灣高鐵將採購12組（共144節車廂）高鐵新世代列車...新車將以日本東海道新幹線N700S為參考系統...2027年投入營運". Contract value: primary press release states ¥129.091 billion; secondary reporting (自由時報/CNA/TechNews, via search synthesis) states ¥124.091 billion — a minor, unresolved discrepancy, not independently reconciled.
- **N700ST livery confirmed via primary press release.** THSRC, "「全新世代 為你啟動」台灣高鐵新世代列車正式命名為N700ST", `https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/e1e25e6a-4139-44d4-ac30-3f9724f08310`, PRIMARY, High confidence: "N700ST延續700T橘黑配色的強烈對比...車頭以明亮白色為主體，橘、黑雙色曲線自車頭交會後，平行延伸至車身側面" — an evolution of, not a break from, the 700T scheme.
- **N700ST first unit arrived Kaohsiung Port 15 August 2026; targeted 6 sets entering service in 2027, 6 more in 2028.** Source: 中央社, `https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202607290157.aspx`, SECONDARY, Confidence Medium-High: "預計明年、2028年各有6組N700ST列車投入營運" (article dated July 2026, so "明年"=2027). Full 12-set delivery targeted by end of 2028; peak-hour capacity increase of approximately 25% once complete.
- **N700ST wheelchair spaces increase from 4 to 6, with wheelchair fixing devices, larger nursing room.** Source: same THSRC naming press release, PRIMARY, High confidence: "輪椅席數量則將由現行高鐵列車的4席增加到6席，並設置輪椅固定裝置". Additional restroom-upgrade detail (ostomy-cleaning facility, changing platform, bidet toilet) appears in 風傳媒 (Storm Media, `https://www.storm.mg/lifestyle/11152765`, SECONDARY, Medium confidence) but was not independently confirmed word-for-word in the primary press release fetch.
- **Maintenance depot: Yanchao Main Depot (燕巢總機廠), Kaohsiung — not near Taipei/Xinshi as initially hypothesised.** Source: zh.wikipedia, 燕巢總機廠, SECONDARY, Medium confidence, citing 交通部鐵道局 and a 2017 NOWnews article: "高雄市燕巢區高鐵總廠路500號", ~58 hectares, 22 buildings, "列車組裝、列車轉向架檢修、列車大修及不定期檢修". Directly confirmed as the current, active depot via a primary THSRC press release on its 2026 expansion for the incoming N700ST fleet: `https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/4d611950-b44d-43d8-95ae-fc7b3eb73740`, PRIMARY, High confidence, dated 26 March 2026: "台灣高鐵列車轉向架檢修、列車大修及新車組裝之核心基地"; 11,500+ m² of new workshop space, groundbreaking 26 March 2026.
- **Major incident: 4 March 2010 Jiaxian earthquake derailment — the system's first passenger-train derailment, corroborated by a primary Control Yuan report.** Source: Control Yuan (監察院), `https://www.cy.gov.tw/News_Content.aspx?n=796&s=5138`, PRIMARY, High confidence: "99年3月4日上午高雄甲仙鄉發生6.4級地震，導致高鐵列車於行進中出軌", approved 9 June 2010; report's main finding concerned prolonged evacuation procedures, not the derailment mechanism. Technical detail (train 110 northbound, Tainan Xinshi section, km 293, four wheelsets deviated 15–20cm, dragged ~2.7km) from NOWnews, `https://www.nownews.com/news/6583243`, SECONDARY, Medium confidence. Speed/mechanism (298 km/h, eddy-current brake caught the wheel) from 遠見雜誌, `https://www.gvm.com.tw/article/37745`, SECONDARY, Medium confidence, uncorroborated by a second source for the specific numbers. This resolves the task's original "Xinshi" hypothesis: it names the derailment location (Tainan's Xinshi District), not the depot, which is in Yanchao, Kaohsiung.
- **Two minor/recent incidents, not "major" but real:** a power-bank fire/smoke incident on train 630, 10 August 2026, no injuries (聯合新聞網, `https://udn.com/news/story/7266/9682175`, SECONDARY, High confidence); and a signalling-equipment fault causing THSR's largest-ever delay, 25–26 May 2026, ~21 hours of degraded single-track operation, ~240,000 passengers affected — infrastructure, not rolling stock (聯合新聞網, `https://udn.com/news/story/7266/9525473`, SECONDARY, High confidence).
- **Livery: no fleet-wide repaint since 2007; temporary special wraps applied to individual trainsets** (a Cartoon Network collaboration on trainset TR31, 2013–2014; a "Kanahera's Tiny Animals" collaboration on trainset TR33 in two separate periods, ~2019–Sept 2020 and August 2023–August 2025) — all sourced only to zh.wikipedia and unverified news leads, Low confidence, several underlying citations unfetchable (see Checked and failed).

## Conflicts

- **Total seating capacity: 977 vs 989.** 977 is THSR's own current "車廂設施" page, summing individually-quoted per-car counts (PRIMARY, High confidence, describing the present-day configuration). 989 traces to an archived MOTC Railway Bureau document titled "989個座位－高鐵列車座位總數", cited within zh.wikipedia's body text (which is internally inconsistent — its own infobox separately states 977): "第6車為商務車廂（66席）、第1車到第5車及第7車到第9車為對號座（671席）、第10車到第12車為自由座（252席）" = 989. The archived document itself could not be fetched (web.archive.org is unreachable in this environment). No source found states *why* the numbers differ; a hypothesis that seats were converted to luggage space around 2010–2015 is unconfirmed and not published as fact. Both figures published, neither resolved.
- **2010 derailment passenger count: 300 vs 2,400 vs "over 2,000".** 300 (遠見雜誌, GVM) appears to describe passengers aboard the specific derailed train. 2,400 (NOWnews) and "over 2,000" (Control Yuan, PRIMARY) closely match each other and appear to describe passengers stranded system-wide across six trains halted that day. Likely different scopes (one train vs. network-wide), not a true contradiction of the same measurement — published separately, not resolved into one figure.
- **N700ST contract value: ¥129.091 billion (primary THSRC press release) vs ¥124.091 billion (secondary reporting via search synthesis, not independently fetched).**
