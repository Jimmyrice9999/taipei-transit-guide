## Conflicts

1. **The older systems lead says 2007; the primary Circular investigation says
   December 2006.** The investigated viaduct's code date is retained as 2006
   (ROC year 95), with the older 2007 label recorded as a one-year corpus error.
   The two dates are not averaged or silently merged.

2. **DORTS publishes two nearby but non-identical underground parameter pairs.**
   The underground-station assessment gives ODE/MDE as 0.15/0.24 g, while the
   cut-and-cover tunnel page gives 0.14/0.23 g. Both are published with their
   page and structure scope. The checked sources do not establish whether the
   difference is an edition, structure type or design boundary.

3. **The public 475/2500-year levels are not a metro CEDC register.** DORTS's
   planning page, current national building code and older CEDM pages use
   overlapping hazard-level vocabulary, but none of those sources alone proves
   that every existing metro structure is governed by the current national
   building code.
