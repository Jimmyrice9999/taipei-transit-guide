# Taipei-region metro electrification

Research date: 27 August 2026 (Asia/Taipei).

This is a one-subject research record for traction voltage, collection method,
conversion stages and the published exceptions across Taipei Metro, New Taipei
Metro, the New Taipei light-rail lines and the Airport MRT. It keeps design
rules, line-specific vehicle data and current asset registers separate.

## What is established

### Taipei Metro is described as a 750 V DC third-rail system

**Claim — Confirmed:** DORTS describes Taipei Metro’s DC traction as 750 V DC
third rail, with conversion from 22 kV through transformer and rectifier
equipment, positive supply through the conductor rail and negative return through
the running rails.

- **Source title:** *Practical metro power-supply systems — DC traction supply*
- **Original title:** 《捷運供電系統實務》
- **Publisher:** 臺北市政府捷運工程局 / Taipei City Government Department of
  Rapid Transit Systems
- **URL:** <https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no24/files/basic-html/page364.html>
- **Archive snapshot:** not captured
- **Kind:** primary
- **Confidence:** High — official DORTS power-supply volume, full page read on
  27 August 2026
- **Original-language sentences:**
  > 臺北捷運採用的是 750VDC 第三軌供電系統。
  > 750VDC 牽引電力係由 22kV 電源先經三繞組整流變壓器降壓再經整流器整流而得。
  > 750VDC 牽引電力一般經由四組高速饋線斷路器分別供電至上行及下行之供電軌，再經電聯車之電刷引接電力至電聯車內供牽引馬達使用。其負電回流經行車軌流回至整流器之負電端。

The same page says 24-pulse rectifiers are used in high-capacity systems,
12-pulse rectifiers in completed and later medium-capacity systems, and usually
6-pulse rectifiers in depot workshops. It explains that higher pulse counts
reduce harmonic content.

### DORTS publishes substation spacing and redundancy rules

- **Archive snapshot:** not captured

The page also describes high-capacity 24-pulse conversion from AC 22 kV to DC
750 V and transfer arrangements when a traction substation is unavailable.
These are design rules, not a current substation asset register.

### Wenhu’s upstream supply chain is documented

  Rapid Transit Systems
- **URL:** <https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page139.html>
- **Archive snapshot:** not captured
- **Kind:** primary
- **Confidence:** High — official technical journal, full page read on
  27 August 2026
- **Original-language sentences:**
  > 臺北淡水線及捷運木柵線是臺北捷運最早規劃設計的高運量及中運量捷運系統。而所採供電模式均由台電以 161kV 雙迴路供電，將台電 161kV 電源送至捷運主變電站 (BSS)，再由主變電站將 161kV 降壓至 22kV 後，送至各車站變電站 (SSS/FPSS) 及牽引動力變電站 (TSS/PPSS)。

through diesel generator groups serving power-supply sections. This does not
establish a current emergency allocation for every line.

## Conflicts

### Airport MRT voltage and collection method

The Railway Bureau’s technical power page publishes 750 V DC lower-contact
third rail for the operating Airport MRT, while the Bureau’s Airport MRT project
overview publishes 1,500 V DC overhead catenary. Both are official primary
records read in full on 27 August 2026. The content page publishes both values as
an unresolved conflict and does not choose one by recency, plausibility or
frequency.

### Third rail and overhead are system-boundary differences, not a Taipei-wide contradiction

DORTS describes Taipei Metro third rail; New Taipei Metro describes third rail
for Circular/Sanying and overhead plus storage for Danhai/Ankeng. These sources
refer to different systems and are not merged into one universal collection
claim. The Airport conflict is different because both values are asserted for
the same named system by the same government bureau.
