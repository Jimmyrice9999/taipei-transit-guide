# Taipei-region metro control centres

Research date: 27 August 2026 (Asia/Taipei).

This is a one-subject research record for the operating-control arrangements
published by Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation (TRTC), New Taipei Metro
Corporation (NTMC) and Taoyuan Metro Corporation (TYMC). It separates physical
control-centre locations from organizational units and from the emergency
functions described in operator notices.

## What is established

### TRTC published two physical control centres in its 2014 arrangement

**Claim — Confirmed:** TRTC’s official 2014 corporate-responsibility report
describes two control centres, one in the Jiojiu Control Building and one at
Neihu Depot. It describes the centres as responsible for dispatch and
monitoring, emergency response and information collection and transmission.

- **Source title:** *2014 Taipei Metro corporate social responsibility report — control-centre operations*
- **Original title:** `2014年企業社會責任報告書`
- **Publisher:** 臺北大眾捷運股份有限公司 / Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation
- **URL:** <https://web.metro.taipei/ebook/2014ebook/files/basic-html/page48.html>
- **Archive snapshot:** not captured
- **Kind:** primary
- **Confidence:** High for the arrangement published by the report; Medium for treating the 2014 physical split as an unchanged current inventory, because the source is historical
- **Original-language sentence:**
  > 臺北捷運目前設立兩個行控中心，其一位於交九行控大樓，另一位於內湖機廠，為捷運系統運轉管理核心，負責「運轉調度與監控」、「緊急處理與應變」及「訊息彙集及傳遞」等任務。

The same page says different control positions rotate together so that
operating tasks can be coordinated and abnormal events handled and reported
quickly. It is evidence about the report’s operating description, not a current
staffing census.

physical location.

## Conflicts

No direct factual contradiction was found among the four operator records. The
TRTC 2014 report gives a physical two-centre arrangement, while the current TRTC
responsibilities page gives organizational control groups; those are different
record scopes, not competing counts. TYMC’s current page names two line control
units. NTMC’s chart names two operating divisions, not two control rooms.
