**Checked:** 27 August 2026. **Subject:** Taipei Metro platform screen doors.

## What is established

- TRTC's 2014 report says the initial high-capacity network's Tamsui, Xindian, Zhonghe and Bannan systems had no platform doors, that retrofit work for 58 stations began in 2006, that 13 stations had been completed by 2014, and that all works were planned for staged completion by the end of 2018. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation, [2014 Corporate Social Responsibility Report, p.47](https://web.metro.taipei/ebook/2014ebook/files/basic-html/page47.html), `2014臺北捷運企業社會責任報告書`. Original: `臺北捷運初期路網，除原木柵線12個車站採全罩式的月臺門設計外，其餘淡水線、新店線、中和線及板南線系統車站均未設置` / `本公司自2006年起針對未設置月臺門的58個車站辦理月臺門增設工程` / `累計已完成13個車站增設` / `規劃於2018年底前分階段完成全部車站之月臺門建置作業`.
- DORTS says the initial high-capacity routes, including Tamsui, Xindian, Zhonghe and Nangang, opened without platform doors; it says routes opened from Xinzhuang/Luzhou onward used full-height doors and that the operator completed the platform-door construction at all operating initial-network high-capacity stations in ROC107 after 14 years. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [Practical station planning and design — platform doors, p.127](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no2/files/basic-html/page127.html), `捷運車站規劃與設計實務`. Original: `初期路網除中運量木柵線採全高鏤空式月臺門，高運量於初期路線如淡水線、新店線、中和線、南港線等路線，均未設置月臺門` / `自新蘆線通車起之路線，均加裝全高式月臺門` / `歷時14年於民國107年全數完成閘門型式之月臺門建置`.
- DORTS's electromechanical review describes the full-height closed door arrangement beginning with Xinzhuang/Luzhou and the later full-glass design used on Xinyi/Songshan. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [Review and outlook of Taipei Metro electromechanical engineering, p.194](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page194.html), `臺北捷運機電工程回顧與展望`. Original: `自新莊／蘆洲線起臺北捷運高運量系統於各車站開始設置月臺門，型式採全高密閉式` / `信義／松山線月臺門採用全玻璃式月台門設計`.
- DORTS's signalling manual explains the high-capacity platform-door interface: a six-car usable length, powered sliding doors aligned to train doors, emergency doors and end/driver access doors. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [Practical signalling systems for Taipei Metro, p.167](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no22/files/basic-html/page167.html), `捷運號誌系統實務`. Original: `月臺門是界於月臺與軌道之間的一種隔離設施` / `月臺門（以臺北捷運高運量為例）須能提供六車長度使用` / `滑門是供一般乘客用，與車門對齊，且為電動式`.

The full pages were opened and read. Together they establish a 58-station retrofit boundary, a 14-year completion statement ending in ROC107 (2018), the full-height-door baseline for the Xinzhuang/Luzhou generation and the later Xinyi/Songshan glass design. They do not establish a complete line-by-line completion calendar or a current station asset register.

## Conflicts

- The 2014 TRTC report is a progress record: 13 of 58 retrofit stations were complete and completion was planned for the end of 2018. DORTS's later account is a retrospective completion statement: all operating initial-network high-capacity station doors were complete in ROC107 after 14 years. These are different reporting dates and scopes, not a contradiction; the page retains both.
- The older systems draft gives exact dates for individual lines and stations, including April, August and September 2018. Those dates were not found in the opened primary records used here, so the focused page does not promote them as established facts.

## Update, 29 August 2026 — a terminology distinction, a 1989 origin story, and a lot of unusable secondary detail

- **DORTS's own text uses two distinct terms**: "全高式" (full-height) for
  new-build doors from the Xinlu Line onward, and "閘門型式" (gate-type)
  specifically for the completed high-capacity retrofit — never
  "半高式" (half-height), the term commonly used elsewhere. Re-checked
  directly against `no2/files/basic-html/page127.html`, PRIMARY. Also
  gives the practical reason retrofit stations couldn't get full-height
  doors: underground ventilation design assumed no platform-track
  obstruction, so full-height would have required also modifying
  ventilation equipment.
- **DORTS `no2` pages 125/126/128/129/130 and `JRTST no48` pages
  193/195**, all individually re-fetched — none contain half-height
  terminology, cost figures or contractor names beyond what page 127/194
  already give. A real, confirmed absence, not unsearched.
