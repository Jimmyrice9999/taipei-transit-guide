Original: `臺北捷運初期路網，除原木柵線12個車站採全罩式的月臺門設計外，其餘淡水線、新店線、中和線及板南線系統車站均未設置` / `本公司自2006年起針對未設置月臺門的58個車站辦理月臺門增設工程` / `累計已完成13個車站增設` / `規劃於2018年底前分階段完成全部車站之月臺門建置作業`.

- DORTS's electromechanical review describes the full-height closed door arrangement beginning with Xinzhuang/Luzhou and the later full-glass design used on Xinyi/Songshan. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [Review and outlook of Taipei Metro electromechanical engineering, p.194](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page194.html), `臺北捷運機電工程回顧與展望`. The full pages were opened and read. Together they establish a 58-station retrofit boundary, a 14-year completion statement ending in ROC107 (2018), the full-height-door baseline for the Xinzhuang/Luzhou generation and the later Xinyi/Songshan glass design. They do not establish a complete line-by-line completion calendar or a current station asset register.

## Conflicts

- The 2014 TRTC report is a progress record: 13 of 58 retrofit stations were complete and completion was planned for the end of 2018. DORTS's later account is a retrospective completion statement: all operating initial-network high-capacity station doors were complete in ROC107 after 14 years. These are different reporting dates and scopes, not a contradiction; the page retains both.
- The older systems draft gives exact dates for individual lines and stations, including April, August and September 2018. Those dates were not found in the opened primary records used here, so the focused page does not promote them as established facts.
