# Corpus warning

`docs/research/` is a lead file, not an authority. Every claim below was checked
against the cited full primary page. Search snippets and the older systems draft
were leads only.

**Checked:** 27 August 2026. **Subject:** gauge boundaries across Taipei-region
metro and light-rail systems.

## What is established

### Taipei high-capacity metro: 1,435 mm steel-wheel track

**Claim - Confirmed:** DORTS's full track-engineering page gives 1,435 +/- 1.5
mm as the basic gauge for Taipei high-capacity steel-wheel track and defines the
measurement between the inner faces of the two rail heads at the T/R-14 mm
reference.

- **Source title:** *Taipei Metro track engineering practice - basic steel-wheel track planning*
- **Original title:** `捷運工程叢書 精進版 - 9 捷運軌道工程實務`
- **Publisher:** 臺北市政府捷運工程局 / Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems
- **URL:** <https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no39/files/basic-html/page11.html>
- **Archive snapshot:** not captured
- **Kind:** PRIMARY
- **Confidence:** High - full official technical page gives the engineering value and its measurement reference
- **Original-language sentences:**
  > 臺北高運量捷運系統在初期規劃階段，歷經英國捷運總顧問(BMTC)與美國捷運總顧問(ATC)的評估與近年來的經驗累積，已將軌道基本設計做完善規劃與釐定。
  > 軌距(Track Gauge or Gauge)：1435±1.5公厘，量測基準點在T/R-14mm處，同股軌道之兩根鋼軌頭部內緣間距。

The page is a technical description of the high-capacity steel-wheel standard;
it is not a current asset register for every Taipei-region route.

- **Source A URL:** <https://www.dorts.ntpc.gov.tw/cdn/file/20250821/af8c46bd-0806-46a2-b0ad-33a94f5e6428.pdf>

- **Source A original-language evidence:** the table headings `車輛規格`, `供電制式 DC 750V` and `軌距 1,435 mm`, in the section describing the Danhai and Ankeng light-rail ESS improvement.
- **Source B title:** *Light-rail trains*

## Conflicts

No direct numeric gauge conflict was found among the full official pages used
for this subject. The important non-conflict is scope: DORTS gives a numeric
steel-wheel standard for high-capacity track and a numeric value in the New
Taipei light-rail vehicle table, but the Circular FAQ gives only system type and
the Wenhu material gives a rubber guideway rather than a conventional steel-rail
gauge.
