# Taipei-region metro fire and evacuation systems

Research date: 27 August 2026 (Asia/Taipei).

This is a one-subject research record for fixed fire protection, smoke control,
vehicle egress, fixed evacuation paths and an operational Circular Line warning
response. It keeps the 1993 Wenhu test fires as existing rolling-stock history
and tests the separate systems boundary from full official records.

## What is established

## Conflicts

### The published airflow upper bounds differ by DORTS volume

The tunnel volume's page 227 prints a maximum of 11 m/s, while the MEP/fire
volume's critical-velocity page describes an upper value below 11.2 m/s as the
NFPA 130 recommendation. The pages do not identify whether this is rounding,
an adopted-practice value versus a recommendation, or a design-edition
difference. Both values are retained with their scopes.

### The 2021 design simulation is not a current line-wide register

The DORTS seminar's 578 m / 1,873-second and 542 m / 1,783-second values are
specific to the Y28–Y27 North Ring design model. They cannot be promoted to a
current first-stage Circular Line operating criterion without an as-built or
operator register.

### The 2025 wording conflicts with the old systems shorthand

The systems lead called the event a “walkway evacuation”. The full CNA account
says the walkway was used by the station manager to reach the train, while the
passengers were taken to Xinpu Minsheng by the train. The event is published with
that distinction and the CNA source remains secondary.
