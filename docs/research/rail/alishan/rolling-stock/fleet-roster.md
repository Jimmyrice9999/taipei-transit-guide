## What is established

- **Shay geared steam locomotive procurement, three waves, from the operator's own history page.** Source: 阿里山林業鐵路及文化資產管理處, 夏依式蒸汽機車, `https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/0000107`, PRIMARY, High confidence. A 13-ton two-cylinder Shay was ordered from Lima (USA) by the original timber concessionaire 藤田組 — no year given by this page. Eight 18-ton two-cylinder Shays were purchased 1910–1913 (Meiji 43 – Taishō 2). Twelve 28-ton (three-cylinder) Shays were purchased 1912–1917 (Taishō 1–6). Quoted: 「最初日本『藤田組』為輸運木材，向美國LIMA公司訂購了13公噸的SHAY式二汽缸蒸汽機車」; 「共購入18公噸的SHAY式二汽缸蒸汽機車8輛」; 「於大正元年(西元1912年)-大正六年(西元1917年)間，共購入了12部」.
- **A secondary date for the first locomotive (1907) conflicts with the operator's own undated account.** Source: zh.wikipedia, 阿里山林業鐵路, citing 蘇昭旭《阿里山森林鐵道(1912-1999)－車輛篇》(2001), SECONDARY, Low-Medium confidence, book citation not independently checked. Not published as established; the operator's own page states no year for this unit.
- **Current 14-locomotive Shay roster, with individual status.** Source: 阿里山林業鐵路及文化資產管理處, 蒸汽機車概況表, `https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/0000108`, PRIMARY, High confidence for the overall roster and the four directly-quoted operational entries; Medium for cells not independently re-verified verbatim (the fetch tool declined a second full verbatim reproduction). Four locomotives listed as dynamically operational ("動態運轉"): No. 21 (28-ton, built 1912, entered service 1913; stopped 1975, displayed at Chiayi Park 1976, returned to depot 2020, restoration completed and returned to service 2023 — the only one of the four not noted as diesel-converted); No. 25 (28-ton, built 1913; stopped 1973, resumed 2007 converted to diesel fuel); No. 26 (28-ton, built 1913; stopped 1976, resumed 1999; fuel type not stated); No. 31 (28-ton, built 1915; resumed 2007 converted to diesel fuel). Remaining seven are static display (12, 13, 18, 23, 24, 28, 29) and two are out of service awaiting repair (17, 32); No. 15 is listed with a status the fetch tool paraphrased as decommissioned/not displayed, tied to 1993 fire damage — not independently re-verified verbatim.
- **A genuine conflict on how many Shays actually run.** The dedicated Shay page (0000107) states only No. 26 can be dynamically operated, crediting in-house Chiayi Depot parts fabrication: 「本處嘉義車庫修理工廠，在沒有原廠零件補充下，自行開模翻製零件，使26號蒸汽機車仍可以動態運轉方式保存下來」. The fuller roster table (0000108) lists four locomotives as operational. Both are operator primary pages; they disagree. A plausible but unconfirmed explanation: 0000107 may describe a narrower category (e.g., coal-fired/unmodified operation) since two of the four on 0000108's list are explicitly diesel-converted. Publish both, do not resolve.
- **No current public excursion schedule for steam-locomotive runs was found** on either page or in the operator's visible news feed. Stated gap.
- **Diesel locomotive fleet, seven identified batches.** Source: 阿里山林業鐵路及文化資產管理處, 柴油機車之介紹, `https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/0000106`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Diesels began arriving 1953 (first batch, Mitsubishi). Batches: 1953–54 Mitsubishi 25t ×2 (retired); 1954–55 Mitsubishi 25t ×2 (retired); 1969 Mitsubishi 25t ×6, DL25–30, active, currently used on the Zhushan and Shenmu lines; 1972 Mitsubishi 25t ×4, DL31–34, active; 1975–76 West German O&K 25t ×3, DL35–37, retired (noted maintenance difficulty); 1982 Japan Sharyo 28t ×5, DL39–43, active; 2006–07 Japan Sharyo 29t ×7, DL45–51, active. The page's own summary states "六批" (six batches) through 1982, but its own content describes what reads as seven distinct batches including the 2006–07 addition — an internal inconsistency, not silently reconciled. **"DL-14" — a class named in the original task hypothesis — does not appear anywhere in this page or in zh.wikipedia's full article text; not confirmed to exist.**
- **A DL-32 unit was destroyed in the 1993 depot fire**, per zh.wikipedia (SECONDARY, Medium confidence, inline citation not captured), corroborated in date/location by the operator's own account of the fire (below) though the specific locomotive number rests only on the secondary source.
- **New/refurbished trains, 2024–2026:**
  - **栩悅號 (Vivid Express)**, launched 24 May 2024, built by repainting/refitting old carriages — described as the first newly refitted tourism train on a Taiwan forest railway. Source: SETN news, `https://www.setn.com/News.aspx?NewsID=1460500`, SECONDARY, Medium confidence (single outlet, not cross-checked against an operator press release). 5 cars, 60 passengers, 360-degree transparent driver's cab, rotating observation seats, 8-channel audio, wireless-headset narration. Runs the lower/flat section, calling at Liyuanliao, Jiaoliping, Zhangnaoliao, Zhuqi.
  - **福森號 (Formosensis) — a checked citation failure.** zh.wikipedia states this "cypress-wood train" entered service the same day as the July 2024 reopening, citing a CNA article. Directly fetching that CNA article confirms it contains no mention of "福森", "Formosensis" or "檜木列車" anywhere. **This is a verified secondary-citation failure per the sourcing rules — the claimed corroboration does not check out.** The name does independently exist as a real product (the operator's own site links a "福森號主題遊程" nav item to a Lion Travel booking microsite), but that page did not yield readable content, and no launch date or technical detail is confirmed for it. Not published as established; do not use 6 July 2024 as its launch date.
  - **6 July 2024 full-line reopening — confirmed directly.** Source: 中央社 (CNA), 「林鐵全線通車 首班7/6上午10時開往阿里山車站」, `https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202407050188.aspx`, dated 2024-07-05, SECONDARY, High confidence, fetched and read in full. First Alishan-bound train departed Chiayi 10:00, ~5 hours, ~72km. Four daily services. The inaugural train (4 coaches, 85 seats) sold out in under 10 minutes.
  - **森里號 EVI, next-generation train, confirmed via operator press release.** Source: 阿里山林業鐵路及文化資產管理處, 「『經典x傳承x榮耀』阿里山林鐵高階款列車『森里號』曝光 115年投入營運」, `https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/AllNews/0076847`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Manufacturer: 台灣車輛股份有限公司 (Taiwan Rolling Stock Co.). First unit (1 locomotive + 5 coaches) arrived at Chiayi Depot 29 August 2025; after trial-running, enters Chiayi–Alishan main-line service in 2026. Capacity 97 seats (incl. 2 accessible seats) vs. the existing fleet's 108. Total order: 5 locomotives, 26 passenger coaches. Cost not disclosed beyond "substantially increased" versus the existing fleet.
  - A NT$2.3 billion / 15-year restoration-cost figure attributed to the July 2024 reopening appeared only in an earlier, broader AI-paraphrase of the CNA article and was not re-confirmed on a targeted re-fetch — not published, flagged for re-verification.
- **Branch-line rolling stock beyond the shared DL25–30 diesel batch** is not established from any primary source read this run — stated gap.
- **1993 Beimen workshop fire, confirmed by both the operator and a secondary source.** Source: 阿里山林業鐵路及文化資產管理處, 嘉義車庫園區, `https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/0000149`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Quoted: 「1993年8月23日，北門修理工場發生一場大火燒掉木造廠房，部分老火車也付諸一炬。」
- **2003 Alishan derailment — the railway's worst accident, secondary-sourced only.** Source: zh.wikipedia, citing BBC Chinese News and TVBS among others (underlying articles not independently fetched), SECONDARY, Medium confidence. A train from Chaoping to Shenmu, reportedly due to an unopened brake-pipe angle cock leaving the coaches' brakes inoperative, went out of control on a curve and struck a rock face, 1 March 2003. 17 deaths, 205 injured — described as the most serious accident since the railway opened. Not yet corroborated against a primary investigation report.
- **Maintenance depots, three sites, all PRIMARY, High confidence, operator's own pages.** Chiayi Depot Park (`https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/0000149`): 4.47 ha, established 1912 as 北門修理工場, in-house parts/boiler/steam-engine fabrication capability, site of the 1993 fire, now a heritage-visit site (14-day advance booking). Fenqihu Depot (`https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/0000150`): 0.09 ha, historic water/coal/locomotive-exchange point, operations ceased ~1985, now a static exhibition space (2024 renovation). Alishan Depot (`https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/0000151`): 0.22 ha, rebuilt with steel-frame structure from 1997, currently the prep depot for the 3am Zhushan sunrise-line departures; not open to public tours.

## Conflicts

- **Number of currently operational Shay steam locomotives**: one (per 0000107) vs. four (per 0000108) — see above, both primary, unresolved.
- **Repair/diesel-conversion completion dates for Nos. 25 and 31**: this research's fetch of the Chinese-language 夏依式蒸汽機車 page (0000107) gives both locomotives the identical date ROC 96 (2007) for their return to service and diesel-fuel conversion. The site's existing `content/rail/alishan/_index.md` cites a different source — the English-language mirror of page 0000108 (`https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/EN/0000108`, fetched 2026-08-25, with a note that the Chinese page timed out during that check) — for the claim that repairs completed in 2006 (No. 25) and 2004 (No. 31), two different years. Both are operator primary sources (one Chinese, one English mirror of a different page), fetched on different dates, and they do not agree. Not resolved; both published, flagged explicitly rather than letting the new page silently override the existing one.
- **Diesel batch count**: "six batches" (page's own summary) vs. seven distinct batches described in the same page's own content — unresolved.
- **1907 vs. undated** for the first Shay locomotive's procurement year — operator page gives no date, a secondary book citation gives 1907, unverified.
- **福森號's launch date and description**: asserted by a Wikipedia sentence whose own cited source does not contain the claim — a verified citation failure, not published.
