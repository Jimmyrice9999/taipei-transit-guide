## What is established

### Route, gauge and mountain engineering

- **The railway opened in 1912, is 762 mm gauge and historically had a much
  larger branch system than the current passenger network.** Source: *Railway
  technology* / `鐵道技術`, Alishan Forest Railway and Cultural Heritage Office,
  PRIMARY, confidence High: the operator describes the 1912 opening, an
  approximately 72 km main line, up to 21 historical branches and the 762 mm
  gauge. Original-language evidence: `1912年通車的阿里山林業鐵路，擁有長達約72公里的本線與精密的支線系統，最多時曾有21條支線。`

- **The railway's characteristic alignment methods are a three-turn spiral,
  horseshoe/180-degree curves and Z-shaped reversals, used to gain elevation
  without a rack rail.** Source: *Alishan Forestry and Railway Cultural
  Landscape* / `阿里山林業暨鐵道文化景觀`, Bureau of Cultural Heritage,
  Ministry of Culture, PRIMARY, confidence High because the heritage body's
  full potential-site page describes the works. Original-language evidence:
  `獨立山螺旋形路線（三重螺旋），以及竹崎至樟腦寮間之180度大彎、屏遮那之Z字形迂迴路線。`
  The same page gives a maximum gradient of 6.25% and a 762 mm,
  non-electrified railway.

- **The current operator overview publishes the station chainage and elevation
  sequence from Chiayi to Alishan and the forest-park points.** Source:
  *Alishan Forest Railway station overview* / `阿里山百年鐵道`, Alishan Forest
  Railway and Cultural Heritage Office, PRIMARY, confidence High because it is
  the operator's current station page. Original-language evidence begins:
  `嘉義 0K 海拔30公尺；北門 1.6K 海拔31公尺；鹿麻產 10.84K 海拔82公尺` and
  ends the main line with `阿里山 71.6K 海拔2216公尺`; it lists Zhushan at
  `6.25公里` beyond Alishan and 2,451 m elevation.

### Current service and station layers

- **The currently published main-line timetable has 16 named stations and is
  effective from 10 January 2025.** Source: *Main-line timetable* / `林鐵本線`,
  Alishan Forest Railway and Cultural Heritage Office, PRIMARY, confidence High.
  Original-language evidence: `本線每日往返各2列次，停靠嘉義、北門、鹿麻產、竹崎、木履寮、樟腦寮、獨立山、梨園寮、交力坪、水社寮、奮起湖、多林、十字路、屏遮那、二萬平、阿里山等16站。`

- **The current branch operating layer schedules Shenmu, Zhaoping and Zhushan;
  Duigaoyue is named by the operator but is not established as a separately
  scheduled passenger stop by the branch timetable.** Sources: *Branch-line
  timetable and closure notes* / `林鐵支線` and *station overview*, Alishan
  Forest Railway and Cultural Heritage Office, both PRIMARY, confidence High.
  Original-language evidence: the branch page labels `神木線、沼平線、祝山線`
  and the station overview lists `對高岳 4.9K` as a platform/passing point on
  the Zhushan route. The normalized data therefore records Duigaoyue as
  `passenger-stop-tbc`, not as a fourth scheduled branch service.

- **Mianyue is a closed historical line, not an active passenger branch.**
  Source: *Branch-line timetable and closure notes* / `林鐵支線`, Alishan Forest
  Railway and Cultural Heritage Office, PRIMARY, confidence High. Original-
  language evidence: `眠月線...全長9.2公里...民國88年921地震後，因路基及隧道受損，後續又有多處崩塌，基於安全及自然保護區因素，目前停止營運。`

### Rolling stock

- **The operator's Shay history records an initial 13-ton two-cylinder unit,
  eight 18-ton two-cylinder units delivered from 1910 to 1913, and twelve
  28-ton three-cylinder units delivered from 1912 to 1917.** Source: *Shay
  steam locomotive history* / `夏依式蒸汽機車`, Alishan Forest Railway and
  Cultural Heritage Office, PRIMARY, confidence High. Original-language
  evidence: `1910年至1913年間陸續購入18噸二汽缸夏依式蒸汽機車8輛` and
  `1912年至1917年間陸續購入28噸三汽缸夏依式蒸汽機車12輛`.

- **The official English mirror says Nos. 25 and 31 were repaired and returned
  to operation after coal-to-diesel firing conversion; the FAQ separately
  records No. 26 as static display.** Sources: *Introduction of steam
  locomotive*, official English mirror, PRIMARY, confidence High for the
  conversion wording; and *Frequently asked questions* / `常見問答`, PRIMARY,
  confidence High for No. 26's display status. Original-language/official
  evidence: `No.31 locomotive was repaired and reused in 2004` and `No.25
  locomotive was repaired and reused in 2006`; the FAQ identifies `1912年製造
  的28噸三汽缸夏依式蒸汽機車26號` and says it is on display.

- **The everyday diesel roster is published in grouped rather than
  individual-set form.** Source: *Introduction of diesel locomotive* /
  `柴油機車之介紹`, Alishan Forest Railway and Cultural Heritage Office,
  PRIMARY, confidence High. Original-language evidence includes the table rows
  `DL25-30 ... 6 ... 現役`, `DL31-34 ... 4 ... 現役`, `DL38 ... 1 ... 現役`,
  `DL39-DL43 ... 5 ... 現役` and `DL45-51 ... 7 ... 現役`, alongside retired
  Mitsubishi and O&K groups. No individual depot allocation was found in the
  fetched roster pages.

### Typhoon damage, tunnel replacement and reopening

- **Typhoon Morakot and Typhoon Dujuan caused major interruptions, including two
  collapses totalling 55 m at the old No. 42 tunnel; the replacement tunnel is
  1,106 m long and construction began on 8 January 2021.** Source: *No. 42
  tunnel completion ceremony* / `林鐵42號隧道竣工典禮 祈願一路順遂`, Alishan Forest
  Railway and Cultural Heritage Office, PRIMARY, confidence High. Original-
  language evidence: `42號隧道...兩處崩塌總長約55公尺` and `新建全長1,106公尺之隧道`.

- **The scheduled passenger service from Chiayi to Alishan reopened on 6 July
  2024, after the line had been interrupted for about 15 years.** Source:
  *Alishan Forest Railway full-line opening on 6 July 2024* / `阿里山林鐵113年7月6日全線通車`,
  Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency, PRIMARY, confidence High. Original-
  language evidence: `阿里山林鐵未通車路段...於113年7月6日全線通車` and `每日開行4班次`.

- **The government chronology records 29 December 2023 as completion of the
  last-mile construction, while the passenger announcement records 6 July 2024
  as the first scheduled full-line reopening.** Sources: *Alishan Forest
  Railway* government recreation chronology and the Forestry and Nature
  Conservation Agency reopening announcement, both PRIMARY, confidence High.
  Original-language evidence: `112年12月29日...最後一哩工程完工` versus
  `113年7月6日全線通車`. These are retained as separate infrastructure and
  passenger-service milestones.

- **A specific later weather closure is documented for Typhoon Krathon:** the
  operator says the route inspection found no damage and the main line plus
  Shenmu, Zhaoping and Zhushan resumed on 5 October 2024. Source: *Alishan
  Forest Railway service resumption on 5 October 2024* / `阿里山林鐵10月5日恢復營運`,
  Alishan Forest Railway and Cultural Heritage Office, PRIMARY, confidence High.
  Original-language evidence: `中央氣象署已經解除山陀兒颱風警報，阿里山林鐵經路線巡檢結果無災損` and
  `明日（10/5）起恢復正常營運`.

### Heritage status

- **Alishan is presented by the Ministry of Culture as a World Heritage
  potential site, not as an inscribed UNESCO property.** Source: *Alishan
  Forestry and Railway Cultural Landscape* / `阿里山林業暨鐵道文化景觀`, Bureau
  of Cultural Heritage, PRIMARY, confidence High. Original-language evidence:
  the page labels the entry `世界遺產潛力點` and describes a `71.34公里` route
  scope and `32,900公頃` cultural landscape. A separate government chronology
  records domestic registration as an important cultural landscape in 2019.

- **Uniform all-station accessibility and facility inventory** — checked 25 August

## Stated gaps

The committed snapshot does not yet establish a unit-by-unit active train
roster, depot allocation, ridership series, complete historical branch-station
list, TDX/GTFS coordinates for every forest-railway point, or a uniform current
accessibility dataset. Those figures stay TBC until the operator, TDX or a
government technical/heritage record is fetched and supports them directly.
The current pages also do not claim that the 2025 timetable is the live service
on every date: dated suspension notices must be checked separately.
