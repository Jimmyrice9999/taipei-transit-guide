# Taoyuan Metro Corporation — operator history and organisation, 5 September 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. This report records fresh full-page reads of TYMC's own history and organisation pages; the URLs below are the sources.

## What is established

- **TYMC was established on 6 July 2010 by joint investment from Taoyuan, New Taipei and Taipei city governments.** Source: Taoyuan Metro Corporation, *TYMC company history* (`桃園捷運` / `公司沿革`), https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/tw/_pages/about/history.html, PRIMARY, High confidence: the operator states both the incorporation date and the three-government arrangement. Original-language basis: `桃園市政府邀集路線行經之臺北市政府、新北市政府召開公司籌備會議，決議三市政府共同出資，於民國99年7月6日奉經濟部核准設立「桃園大眾捷運股份有限公司」`.
- **The operator's pre-opening work included joining construction testing, taking over stations in stages, training staff on equipment and station layouts, rehearsing operations, revising operating rules, and assisting contractors with equipment improvements and maintenance.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `營運準備期間本公司積極參與興建單位之各項工程測試、逐步進駐及接管沿線各車站、進行設備操作及相關訓練...辦理演練及優化各項營運規章文件，亦進場協助廠商辦理各項設備改善及保養工作`.
- **The Airport MRT received its operating licence on 25 January 2017, trial operation began on 2 February 2017, the A1–A21 section formally opened on 2 March 2017, and service later extended to A22 on 31 July 2023.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence: these are the operator's own milestone dates. Original-language basis: `於民國106年1月25日交通部頒發機場捷運營運許可...民國106年2月2日試營運、民國106年3月2日正式營運...民國112年7月31日機場捷運延伸服務至A22老街溪站`.
- **The current organisation maintains separate control centres for the Airport Line and Green Line, two station-operation centres, a vehicle operations centre and a ticketing centre under Operations.** Source: Taoyuan Metro Corporation, *Organisational structure* (`組織架構`), https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/tw/_pages/about/organization.html, PRIMARY, High confidence: the page lists the secondary units under Operations. Original-language basis: `轄下分別有規劃組、管理組、機場線行控中心、綠線行控中心、車務中心、第一站務中心、第二站務中心及票務中心，共八個二級單位`.
- **TYMC's Systems division maintains trains, signalling and communications through a vehicle plant and electronics plant, while Facilities maintains power, station MEP, lifts/escalators, platform doors, track and civil infrastructure through separate specialist units.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence: the organisational page describes these responsibilities directly. Original-language basis: `系統處為本公司一級單位，主要負責電聯車、號誌、通訊等設備之維修業務` and `設施處...主要負責軌道、土建、供電、機電設施等設備之維修業務`.
