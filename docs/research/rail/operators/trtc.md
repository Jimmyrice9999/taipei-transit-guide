# Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation — corporate history, research findings, 29 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below was checked against the linked primary page, full PDF, government open-data record or committed data pull; no search snippet is used as evidence.

This is a one-subject report on TRTC's founding, legal basis, capital,
ownership and business scope, supplementing the existing operator entry
which previously covered only operating role and vehicle-plant
responsibility.

## What is established

- **Founding date: 27 July 1994 (ROC 83/07/27), independently confirmed by
  the operator's own timeline and the government company registry.**
  Source: TRTC, *Major events*, titleOriginal `大事紀`,
  `https://www.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=B27E7BC8CBBCDA61`, PRIMARY, High
  confidence (full page fetched). Original-language basis:
  `083.07.27 "台北大眾捷運股份有限公司成立。"`. Independently confirmed by
  the Ministry of Economic Affairs Department of Commerce Development's
  open-data company registry, `https://data.gcis.nat.gov.tw/od/data/api/5F64D864-61CB-4D0D-8AD9-492047CC1EA6?$format=json&$filter=Business_Accounting_NO eq 84894989`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence. Raw field: `"Company_Setup_Date":"0830727"`.
- **A preparatory office existed from 18 December 1990**, per the same
  operator timeline: `079.12.18 "台北捷運公司籌備處成立。"`. PRIMARY, High
  confidence.
- **The first chairman/general manager's recorded term begins 29 July
  1994**, two days after incorporation — internally consistent. Source:
  TRTC, *Past leadership*, titleOriginal `歷任首長`,
  `https://www.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=04240EFF11CFA390`, PRIMARY, High
  confidence.
- **TRTC's board and supervisors are established under the general Company
  Act**, not a bespoke transit statute, per TRTC's own organisational page.
  Source: TRTC, *Organisational structure*, titleOriginal `組織架構`,
  `https://www.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=3C7A1F15A9E6FA91`, PRIMARY, High
  confidence. Original-language basis: `本公司依公司法設置董事會及監察人`.
- **Capital: NT$10 billion registered and paid-in, 1,000,000,000 shares at
  NT$10 par value, as most recently amended.** Source: same GCIS open-data
  record, PRIMARY, High confidence. Raw fields: `"Capital_Stock_Amount":10000000000`,
  `"Paid_In_Capital_Amount":10000000000`, `"Share_Val":10`,
  `"Equity_Amt":1000000000`. The record's amendment-date field showed
  `"1150819"` (2026-08-19) in one fetch; a third-party mirror of the same
  registry (company.g0v.ronny.tw) showed a different amendment date,
  2026-04-24, for what should be the same field — an unresolved metadata
  discrepancy, not treated as affecting the capital figure itself.
- **Business scope includes property/commercial development as a core
  division, not just train operations.** TRTC's own organisational chart
  names an Asset Development Division (資產開發處) and a Commercial
  Development Division (商業開發處) as standing first-level departments.
  Source: same organisational-structure page, PRIMARY, High confidence.
- **Maokong Gondola entered revenue service 4 July 2007**; **TRTC took
  over operation of the Taipei Arena (臺北小巨蛋) on 1 September 2008**;
  **a dedicated recreation-business subsidiary, 北捷遊憩事業股份有限公司, was
  established 1 July 2025.** All from the operator's own timeline, PRIMARY,
  High confidence. Note: the Taipei Arena (小巨蛋) is a distinct, earlier
  and smaller venue than the later-built Taipei Dome (大巨蛋) — an initial
  secondary-sourced draft of this material mislabelled it "Taipei Dome";
  the primary source is unambiguous and corrects this.
- **Current chairman 趙紹廉 (since 17 January 2023) and general manager
  黃清信 (since 5 August 2021), out of full recorded lists of 14 chairmen
  and 9 general managers since 1994.** Source: same *Past leadership* page
  plus TRTC's current *Leadership introduction* page (`首長介紹`,
  `https://www.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=C02D4FAAC5B89EC2`), PRIMARY, High
  confidence.

## Not published: shareholding percentages

zh.wikipedia's infobox states Taipei City Government 73.75%, MOTC 17.14%,
New Taipei City Government 8.75%. Checking this citation against what it
claims to support (rule 4) found the attached reference tag resolves to an
unrelated TRTC FAQ page about what "MRT/TRTC/Metro Taipei" mean — not a
shareholding statement. This is a mis-citation in the Wikipedia article
itself. A director list obtained via a third-party mirror of the
government registry (company.g0v.ronny.tw, not the primary registry page
itself, which returned 403 Forbidden) showed share-count blocks whose
magnitudes arithmetically match those percentages closely (737,457,143 /
1e9 ≈ 73.75%; 171,428,571 / 1e9 ≈ 17.14%; 87,500,000 / 1e9 = 8.75%), but
nothing in what was read explicitly labels which government body each
block belongs to — the mapping is inferred, not read off a primary label.
**Not published on the content page.** A future pass with working access
to findbiz.nat.gov.tw's director page, or the correct GCIS
director/shareholder open-data resource, could settle this.
