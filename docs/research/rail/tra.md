# Taiwan Railways Administration / Taiwan Railways Corporation — research report

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below was checked against the linked primary page, PDF, or committed TDX response.

**Checked:** 24 August 2026. **Subject:** current TRA network data, line families, route construction history, gauges, and historical-line architecture.

## What is established

- The live TDX TRA Station endpoint returned 245 current station records; StationOfLine returned 12 line records; Shape returned 12 geometry records. The committed snapshot retains the raw fields and retrieval date rather than treating the count as a century-wide historical total. **PRIMARY, High:** direct authenticated TDX pull. Original-language basis: the response fields `StationID`, `StationName`, `StationPosition`, `Stations`, `LineName` and `Geometry`. [tdx-tra](https://tdx.transportdata.tw/api/basic/v2/Rail/TRA/Station?%24format=JSON&%24top=1000) [tdx-tra-lines](https://tdx.transportdata.tw/api/basic/v2/Rail/TRA/StationOfLine?%24format=JSON&%24top=1000) [tdx-tra-shape](https://tdx.transportdata.tw/api/basic/v2/Rail/TRA/Shape?%24format=JSON&%24top=1000)
- The current TDX line records are `WL` Western Main Line / 西部幹線, `EL` Eastern Main Line / 東部幹線, `SL` South Link Line / 南迴線, `WL-C` Western Main Line (Coast Line) / 西部幹線 (海線), and the branch records Chengzhui, Jiji, Liujia, Neiwan, Pingxi, Shenao, Shalun and Su'aoxin–Su'ao. The line IDs and order are source fields; the site does not infer a missing route record. **PRIMARY, High:** direct TDX Shape and StationOfLine pull. Original-language basis: the `LineID` and `LineName.Zh_tw` fields. [tdx-tra-shape](https://tdx.transportdata.tw/api/basic/v2/Rail/TRA/Shape?%24format=JSON&%24top=1000)
- TRA's full route-construction history defines a railway route as the track and its roadbed, bridges and culverts, and distinguishes standard gauge as 1,435 mm and narrow gauge as 1,067 mm or 762 mm; it gives TRA's gauge example as 1,067 mm. **PRIMARY, High:** current TRA official route-history page. Original-language basis: 「標準軌距：1,435mm」 and 「窄軌：軌距小於標準軌距…如臺鐵(1,067㎜)」. [tra-route-history](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/adr/about-1-5)
- The same TRA history lists construction/opening segments rather than one undifferentiated line date. It records, among others, Keelung–Taipei opening in 1891, Taipei–Hsinchu in 1893, Hsinchu–Zhunan in 1908, the mountain and coast alternatives between Zhunan and Changhua in 1908 and 1922, Hualien–Taitung widening in 1982, and later double-tracking, electrification and underground works. **PRIMARY, High:** official table, read in full. Original-language basis: the table headed 「臺灣鐵路建造一覽表」 with `地點`, `里程(公里)`, `通車年限`, `建築年份` and `說明`. [tra-route-history](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/adr/about-1-5)
- TRA's official operating-information page currently publishes timetable families as Western Main Line, Eastern Main Line, South Link Line, and branch groupings for Pingxi/Shenao, Neiwan/Liujia, Jiji and Shalun. **PRIMARY, High:** operator timetable index fetched in full. Original-language basis: 「西部幹線」, 「東部幹線」, 「南迴線」 and 「支線（非對號快車）」 followed by the four branch groups. [tra-timetables](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/tip00C/tipC21/view?proCode=8ae4cac3756b7b41017572e9077f1790&subCode=8ae4cac3756b7b41017573e352ae18f8)
- TRA's official 2023 report divides the network into 551.2 km of western main-line routes, 423.3 km of eastern main-line routes, and 90.5 km of branch lines, for 1,065 km total; the same report describes 997.7 km as electrified and 67.3 km as non-electrified, and says the network had 241 stations at the end of 2023, with one freight-only station, 69 passenger-and-freight stations and 171 passenger-only stations. **PRIMARY, High:** operator report PDF, full page/PDF fetch. Original-language basis: 「營運里程總長：1,065 公里（含支線 90.5 公里）」 and 「臺灣鐵路截至112年底共有241站」. [tra-report](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/486e23bd-740a-46a1-bb75-f68442125a2a)
- The Railway Bureau's route-construction PDF records historical alignments and later changes, including Wanhua–Xindian, Tanzi–Shengang, Fengyuan–Dongshi, Ruifang–Shen'ao, Taoyuan–Linkou, and Shen'ao–Liandong; it explicitly records the Linkou Line as stopped operating in 2013 and the Shen'ao–Haibin/Liandong history as partial dismantling and a 1990 stoppage. **PRIMARY, High:** official Railway Bureau/TRA construction-history PDF, read in full. Original-language basis: 「林口線…民國102年停駛」 and 「海濱至濂洞3.7公里…拆除…深澳至海濱…停駛」. [tra-construction-pdf](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/6a439dbd-9cee-4d4b-9c4b-215439bffbf5)
- The Railway Bureau's route terminology document distinguishes a trunk line from a branch line and an interval route, giving the TRA examples of Pingxi, Neiwan, Jiji and Linkou as branches and Taichung Line as an interval route. **PRIMARY, High:** government technical terminology PDF. Original-language basis: 「幹線：為鐵路網中之主要路線」, 「支線：由幹線分支而出」 and 「區間路線…臺中線」. [rail-terminology](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/9dcdec22-11a1-45e5-9da4-54b93fd03f7b)

## Architecture findings

The TDX snapshot is sufficient for current station identities, line memberships,
station order, chainage, and current geometry. It is not sufficient for a
historical TRA graph by itself. The import therefore uses namespaced `TRA:<id>`
station and route identities, stores memberships per line, keeps route versions
and former names as temporal fields, and attaches the present gauge value to
segments rather than routes. Current TDX station-of-line sequence is preserved;
the missing StationOfRoute dataset is not replaced by a guessed route table.

## Conflicts

- TDX returns 245 current station records, while the operator's 2023 report says 241 stations at the end of 2023. These are not silently reconciled: the TDX count is a current structured snapshot, while the report's count is dated and explicitly classifies passenger/freight service. [tdx-tra](https://tdx.transportdata.tw/api/basic/v2/Rail/TRA/Station?%24format=JSON&%24top=1000) [tra-report](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/486e23bd-740a-46a1-bb75-f68442125a2a)
- TDX exposes 12 line shapes and line-of-station records, while the operator's timetable page groups services into Western, Eastern, South Link and four branch families. The former is a current API representation; the latter is an operating/timetable grouping. The line page must show both layers rather than treating the 12 TDX IDs as a definitive historical list of every TRA alignment. [tdx-tra-shape](https://tdx.transportdata.tw/api/basic/v2/Rail/TRA/Shape?%24format=JSON&%24top=1000) [tra-timetables](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/tip00C/tipC21/view?proCode=8ae4cac3756b7b41017572e9077f1790&subCode=8ae4cac3756b7b41017573e352ae18f8)
- The operator report's 1,065 km network total, its 551.2/423.3/90.5 km family totals, and the per-line TDX `TraveledDistance` fields are retained as different measurements. The current TDX line records do not establish that summing their overlapping main/coast/branch records reproduces the report's network total. [tra-report](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/486e23bd-740a-46a1-bb75-f68442125a2a) [tdx-tra-lines](https://tdx.transportdata.tw/api/basic/v2/Rail/TRA/StationOfLine?%24format=JSON&%24top=1000)

## Checked and failed

- **TDX `/Rail/TRA/Route`** — returned HTTP 404 with `{"message":"Resouce Not Found"}` on 2026-08-24. The response is recorded in `data/tdx/TRA/meta.json`; no route record was invented.
- **TDX `/Rail/TRA/StationOfRoute`** — returned HTTP 404 with `{"message":"Resouce Not Found"}` on 2026-08-24. Current station order is taken only from the fetched `StationOfLine` records; no station-of-route response was invented.
- **Historical gauge-change chronology** — checked 2026-08-24. The full TRA route-history page confirms the meanings and current TRA example of 1,067 mm and separately defines 1,435 mm standard gauge, but this pass did not find a primary segment-by-segment chronology proving a TRA revenue segment changed between those two gauges. That chronology remains TBC rather than being inferred from the terminology page.
- **Complete closed-line inventory** — checked 2026-08-24. The fetched construction-history PDF confirms several stopped, dismantled or replaced alignments, but it is a construction table rather than a complete current legal closure register. A full inventory of closed lines and dates remains TBC.

## Stated gaps

- The current import still needs a claim-level alias table for the century of station renamings, with each former name and interval sourced from TRA or archival records.
- The current TDX snapshot lacks a route endpoint dataset and a historical geometry version. TRA route-history tables, Railway Bureau project records and National Railway Museum/Academia Historica archival material are the source families to settle branch edges, closed alignments and historical geometry.
- The current model does not assign accessibility, facilities, first/last trains, fares, ridership, depot allocation or rolling stock to TRA stations. Those are separate operator/MOTC datasets and will not be copied from the metro registry.
