# Taoyuan Airport MRT / Green Line land development (TOD) — research findings, 28 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. `content/rail/projects/tod-joint-development.md` covers Taipei/New Taipei's TOD framework in depth but names no non-Taipei operator; `docs/research/rail/krtc/projects/joint-development.md` (this session, same date) covers Kaohsiung's equivalent mechanism — this is the second of a planned set, covering Taoyuan.

This is a one-subject report on the legal basis and mechanism behind
Taoyuan's own metro land-development programme, as published by the
Taoyuan City Department of Rapid Transit Systems (DORTS, 桃園市政府捷運
工程局). `content/rail/tymc/projects/` does not yet exist.

## What is established

- **DORTS's own introduction page states that Taoyuan's metro land development was formerly called "聯合開發" (joint development) and is now termed "捷運土地開發" (metro land development) — a distinct method of land acquisition alongside general expropriation, in which the landowner provides land for the competent authority (Taoyuan City Government) to build metro facilities, and an investor is solicited through a formal process to fund and build a land-development building, so that landowner, investor and authority share the resulting development benefit.** Source: Taoyuan City Department of Rapid Transit Systems (桃園市政府捷運工程局), `土地開發簡介` (Land Development Introduction), `https://dorts.tycg.gov.tw/cp.aspx?n=23167`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched and read in full (raw HTML parsed directly). Original-language basis: `捷運土地開發（舊稱聯合開發），是政府為了興建捷運建設，除一般徵收外的另一種土地取得方式。由土地所有權人提供土地，供主管機關（桃園市政府）興建捷運設施，並依程序徵求投資人（建商）出資興建土地開發大樓，使參與開發案之土地所有權人、投資人及主管機關共享土地開發利益`. The same page states the programme's stated purpose is to promote effective urban land use, improve area environment and streetscape, reduce loss to metro-land landowners, and channel private capital into urban construction through public-private partnership. Original-language basis: `以促進都市土地有效利用、改善地區環境與景觀，並減少捷運用地地主損失。透過土地開發可誘導民間資金投入都市建設，經由公、私部門合作關係，積極有效地投資都會建設`.
- **The mechanism's central legal basis is the same two instruments Kaohsiung's equivalent programme cites — the Mass Rapid Transit Act (大眾捷運法) and the Mass Rapid Transit System Land Development Regulations (大眾捷運系統土地開發辦法) — supplemented by a further nine named Taoyuan-specific regulations, templates and committee charters, organised by DORTS into four process categories: land acquisition (用地取得), investor selection (投資人甄選), and rights allocation (權益分配), each with its own named instruments, plus the two central laws as a separate first category.** Source: Taoyuan City Department of Rapid Transit Systems, `土地開發相關法令` (Land Development Related Laws), `https://dorts.tycg.gov.tw/cp.aspx?n=23169`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched and read in full. Original-language basis, the four category headings: `一、中央主管法令 大眾捷運法 大眾捷運系統土地開發辦法`, `二、用地取得` (five named Taoyuan-specific negotiated-purchase regulations and agreement templates), `三、投資人甄選` (five named financial-criteria, cost-sharing, selection-document, interface-design and operating-contract instruments), `四、權益分配` (two named rights-allocation principle and committee-charter instruments).

## Conflicts

None found — no second source addressing this mechanism was located this
session. Kaohsiung's parallel programme, covered separately in this
project's own KRTC joint-development research, uses "聯合開發" as its
current working term rather than a superseded one; this is not treated as
a conflict between the two operators, since DORTS's own page frames it as
a naming change specific to Taoyuan's own programme, not a claim about
what any other city calls its equivalent.
