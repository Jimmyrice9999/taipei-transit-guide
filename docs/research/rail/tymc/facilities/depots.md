# Taoyuan Airport MRT maintenance depots — research findings, 28 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. `docs/research/rail/lines/airport-mrt.md` (claim 17) already names two depots from zh.wikipedia alone, without detail; this fills that gap from a primary source. `docs/research/rail/taoyuan-green.md` already records that the Green Line's depot allocation is TBC — this report does not re-open that, and covers only the operating Airport MRT line's own two depots.

This is a one-subject report on the Taoyuan Airport MRT's (機場捷運) two
maintenance depots: Luzhu Depot (蘆竹機廠) and Qingpu Depot (青埔機廠). It
draws primarily on the same 2013 High Speed Rail Engineering Bureau annual
report already used and cited for this project's signalling research
(`docs/research/rail/tymc/technology/signalling.md`), read in full via
`pdftotext` after the fetch tooling used could not extract it directly, and
therefore already verified once this session as a genuine primary
construction record.

## What is established

- **The project has exactly two maintenance depots, Qingpu and Luzhu, as part of a 22-station, 51.03 km line built for approximately NT$113.85 billion, planned at the time of this report for opening at the end of 2015 (民國104年).** Source: 交通部高速鐵路工程局 (High Speed Rail Engineering Bureau, MOTC), `高鐵局102年年報` (2013 Annual Report), `https://www.rb.gov.tw/public/files/artsinfo/1503058979-0.pdf`, PRIMARY, High confidence, full 83-page PDF extracted with `pdftotext`. Original-language basis: `沿途共設22座車站，包括15座高架車站、7座地下車站，並設置青埔與蘆竹2處維修機廠。路線全長約51.03公里，工程建設總經費約1,138.5億元，全線預定於104年底通車營運`.
- **Qingpu Depot's administrative building was completed and received its occupancy permit in January 2012 (民國101年1月).** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `青埔機廠行政大樓已於101年1月完工取得使用執照`.
- **As of the report's late-2013 snapshot, Qingpu Depot hosted the system's operations control centre (行控中心), alongside a main maintenance shop, a stabling yard, a locomotive repair shop, a sewage treatment plant, a car-wash shop, a main substation and a staff cafeteria — with the main structures of all these buildings already complete and only interior fit-out, landscaping, and utility/environmental-control work remaining.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `青埔機廠：主要建築物如主維修廠、行控中心、駐車廠、機車維修廠、污水處理廠、洗車廠、固定設施、主變電站、員工餐廳等之主體結構完已成`.
- **Luzhu Depot's 2013-listed buildings were a main maintenance shop, a traction-power substation, a separate stabling yard for express trains and a separate stabling yard for commuter trains, a car-wash shop, and a track-and-fixed-facilities maintenance shop — under construction (structural, fit-out, landscaping and utility work) at the time.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `蘆竹機廠：102年度主要建築物包括主維修廠、牽引動力變電站、直達車駐車廠、普通車駐車廠、洗車廠、軌道及固定設施維修廠等廠內各項主體結構、裝修作業、景觀工程、水電、環控工程進行中`.
- **By the end of 2013, civil-engineering progress on the depot buildings stood at 98.06% for Qingpu Depot and 82.36% for Luzhu Depot (against 99.91% for the off-site substations package).** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `機廠土建各分區分別為，青埔機廠：98.06％、蘆竹機廠：82.36％、廠外主變電站：99.91％`.
- **All 123 EMU carriages (comprising 11 express and 17 commuter trainsets) had physically arrived at Qingpu Depot by 11 September 2013, and the report separately confirms all had arrived by the time of the year-end summary.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `9月11日所有電聯車共123個車廂全數運抵青埔機場` and, in the report's chronology section, `機場捷運最後一節車廂(2511車)運抵青埔機廠。（全數123個車廂均已運抵青埔機廠）`. This establishes Qingpu, not Luzhu, as the depot where the full delivered fleet was initially received and stabled during commissioning — it does not by itself establish which depot each trainset is permanently allocated to in day-to-day operation.
- **Power supply to both depots is provided by dedicated facility substations, separate from the three main substations (Qingpu, A3, A8) and the 22 station substations; as of the report, Qingpu's main substation had been energised, while Luzhu Depot's substation equipment was still being installed and cabled.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `供電系統工程範圍主要包含3個主變電站(青埔及A3、A8)、22個車站變電站(15座高架車站及7座地下車站)及2個機廠(青埔及蘆竹)設施變電站` and `蘆竹機廠設備進場安裝及佈纜作業`.
- **Signalling installation at Luzhu Depot was, as of the report, still in the layout/installation/cabling stage — the same stage as A1 station, and behind the A21–A2 station section, which was fully complete.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence, already recorded in this project's signalling research. Original-language basis: `A1車站及蘆竹機廠目前正進行號誌設備放樣、安裝及佈纜作業`.
- **Maintenance equipment at the two depots — track-measurement cars, a locomotive, wheel-boring and wheel-lathe machines, train-wash equipment, an underground heavy-lift system, an overhead gantry crane rated at 10 tonnes safe load, and a wheel wear-detection system, among others — was 69.50% complete at Qingpu and 32.79% complete at Luzhu by the end of 2013.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `青埔機廠維修設備施工進度為69.50％；蘆竹機廠維修設備施工進度為32.79％`.
- **TYMC's registered headquarters address is given, in a company-registry-sourced Wikipedia article, as 251 Linghang North Road Section 4, Dayuan District, Taoyuan — the Qingpu Depot site — with a stated relocation date of 19 July 2014.** Source: 桃園捷運公司, zh.wikipedia, `https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/桃園捷運公司`, SECONDARY (search-engine synthesis of company-registry sources per the existing research file's own characterisation), Medium confidence — already recorded in `docs/research/rail/lines/airport-mrt.md` claim 16 and re-used here rather than re-verified independently this session. This is consistent with, and corroborated in kind by, the 2013 primary report's placement of the administrative building and operations control centre at Qingpu.
