# Taoyuan Airport MRT station accessibility — research findings, 27 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below records whether the underlying page was fetched and read in full, or only found via a search summary; the latter is not published as an established fact on the content page.

This is a one-subject report on the Taoyuan Airport MRT's (機場捷運, TDX line
A) station accessibility.

## What is established

- **TYMC's own accessibility-services page describes facility categories in detail but cites no named regulation and no numeric completion figure — checked specifically for both.** Source: Taoyuan Metro Corporation, `無障礙服務` (Accessible Services), `https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/tw/_pages/travel-guide/accessible.html`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched twice (once for content, once specifically checking for a named standard or percentage — neither found).
- **Elevators: every station is described as having an accessible-elevator design, with extended door-closing time for wheelchair users, the elderly, pregnant women, strollers, large luggage and other mobility-impaired passengers; elevator interiors include braille control panels, a voice system, a secondary low control panel, and side-wall handrails.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `各車站皆為無障礙電梯設計，除延長電梯關門時間，方便輪椅、年長者、孕婦、嬰兒車、攜帶大件行李及行動不便旅客搭乘`; `操作盤點字設施、語音系統、副操作盤及側牆扶手`.
- **Tactile paths: dotted warning tiles are laid at hazard zones (stair tops/bottoms, platform edges) and to guide use of key equipment such as elevator call buttons; anti-slip strips separately help low-vision and elderly passengers detect floor-level changes.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `針對危險區域(樓梯起訖端、月台邊緣)之警示或重要設施設備(如：電梯呼叫鈕前方)之使用引導鋪設點狀警示磚`.
- **Accessible restrooms with assistive equipment, including accessible urinals and dedicated stalls, are provided at every station.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `車站內之廁所皆設置供行動不便者使用之相關輔助設備，如無障礙小便斗及專用廁間`.
- **Additional services offered at station information desks: discounted senior/companion tickets, a wheelchair loan service, and a guide service for visually impaired passengers.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence.
- **Train wheelchair spaces are specified precisely: located at specific doors in each car (doors 1-3, 2-3, 3-1 and 4-1), sized 94.25 cm to accommodate a 90 cm-wide wheelchair, with a 140 cm door opening.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `每節車廂分別位於1-3、2-3、3-1、4-1之車門位置`; space `94.25公分，符合輪椅寬度90公分之要求`; door opening `140公分，符合輪椅規格90公分之需求`.
- **This project's own prior per-station research (`docs/research/rail/stations/airport-mrt-stations.md`, dated 14 August 2026, built from full fetches of all 22 TYMC station pages) records that individual station pages carry a recurring "無障礙電梯" (accessible elevator) field, and separately flags an unresolved internal conflict on station A8: its page states lifts serving four platform directions but a stated total of only two lift units.** This is reported here as an existing, previously-verified finding from that file, not independently re-checked in this pass.
