# Taipei Metro station naming and renames — research

## What is established

### The naming procedure

- **Source:** *Taipei Metro Engineering Series — Practical Station Planning
  and Design*, p. 109 / 「捷運工程叢書精進版－2 捷運車站規劃與設計實務」, Taipei
  City Department of Rapid Transit Systems (臺北市政府捷運工程局), PRIMARY,
  High confidence. The full page says that in 2007 the city promulgated the
  「臺北市臺北都會區大眾捷運系統捷運車站命名更名或車站站名加註名稱作業要點」
  so naming, renaming and appended station names would follow a defined
  procedure: 「96 年為落實捷運車站之命名、更名或車站站名加註名稱等作業符合民意及
  政府資訊公開原則……遂頒訂……作業要點，由該要點規定之程序辦理。」
  URL: https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no2/files/basic-html/page109.html

### Former names documented by official sources

- **BL07 Banqiao / 板橋站.** DORTS’s full technical-book page names the
  engineering drawing set for the segment between Xinpu and Banqiao as
  「板橋站(原名漢生路站)」. It establishes the former name, but the page does
  not give the legal instrument or date of the change. PRIMARY, High
  confidence. URL: https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no45/files/basic-html/page260.html
- **O12 Daqiaotou / 大橋頭站.** DORTS’s full land-development case page says
  「大橋頭站（原稱大橋國小站）」 and later says the land-development case was
  adjusted after the station name changed to 大橋頭站. It establishes the
  former name and the official record of a change, but not the gazette/order
  number or date. PRIMARY, High confidence. URL:
  https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no20/files/basic-html/page81.html
- **R18 Mingde / 明德站.** DORTS’s full station-design introduction describes
  「原名天母站之明德站」. It establishes that 明德 was formerly called 天母 in
  the source’s account, but the page does not identify the formal action or
  date. PRIMARY, Medium confidence: the source is official, but it is a
  retrospective design page rather than the naming instrument. URL:
  https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=DA11BCC514603804

### A formal rename and an appended name in the Sanying project

- **LB03.** The New Taipei City Gazette, winter issue 14 of 2020 / 「新北市政府
  公報 109年冬字第14期」, a full official gazette PDF, says: 「LB03站：挖子，更名為
  『長壽山』。」 The formal notice is dated 9 June 2020 in the project’s
  official record. PRIMARY, High confidence. URL:
  https://doc2-exthird.ntpc.gov.tw/NTPC_SODPUBLISH/FILE/0000000/PUBLISH/109/2/109214.pdf
- **LB05.** The same gazette says: 「LB05站：龍埔，加註『三峽廣行宮』。」 This
  is an appended name, not evidence that 龍埔 was renamed. PRIMARY, High
  confidence. URL: https://doc2-exthird.ntpc.gov.tw/NTPC_SODPUBLISH/FILE/0000000/PUBLISH/109/2/109214.pdf
- The current New Taipei DORTS route page publishes LB03 as 長壽山 and LB05 as
  龍埔（加註名稱：三峽廣行宮）; it also publishes other current appended names,
  including LB06 and LB07. That page is a current station list, not the legal
  act that created those names. PRIMARY, High confidence. URL:
  https://www.dorts.ntpc.gov.tw/about/routeInfo/1MJjdJr7dAv6

### Planning labels are not automatically renames

- DORTS’s full Circular Line technical-book page labels Y16 as
  「Y16車站（民生新埔站）」, while the operator’s current station list calls Y17
  新埔民生. These are conflicting planning/current labels; no formal rename
  notice was found, so this page does not call the difference a rename. PRIMARY,
  High confidence for the two published labels. URLs:
  https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no45/files/basic-html/page235.html
  and https://www.ntmetro.com.tw/basic/?node=10138
- DORTS separately records that Daqiaotou was not in the Executive Yuan’s
  original approved Xinzhuang Line station list: local residents and
  representatives petitioned for a station near Taipei Bridge, DORTS
  coordinated for Daqiao Elementary to provide land, and the addition was
  approved. This is a station-addition record, not a rename record. PRIMARY,
  High confidence. URL: https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no42/files/basic-html/page17.html

## Conflicts

1. **LB03 successive official values:** the 2017 planning-era record in the
   existing station corpus says 挖子, while the 9 June 2020 gazette explicitly
   says it was renamed 長壽山; the current NTPC/DORTS route page uses 長壽山. These
   are successive values, not silently interchangeable alternatives.
2. **Y17 label order:** the DORTS planning book says 民生新埔站; the current
   NTMC station list says 新埔民生. No action document was found to explain the
   change, so both labels remain visible and the rename is TBC.
3. **Former-name pages without instruments:** DORTS records former names for
   BL07, O12 and R18, but the fetched pages do not publish the corresponding
   gazette/order dates. Those missing dates are not inferred from opening dates
   or planning codes.
