This is a one-subject report on the former Taiwan Railways Taipei–Danshui line,
not a replacement of its historical record with the current MRT Tamsui Line.
Retrieval date: 2026-08-26. The complete TRA construction-history PDF, full

## What is established

- **The TRA construction-history table identifies Taipei–Danshui as a 21.2 km
  route, constructed in 1900 with operation starting in 1901, and says
  dismantling began on 8 May 1989.** Source: National Taiwan Railways
  Corporation / former Taiwan Railways Administration, *Chronological History
  of Railroad Construction*, titleOriginal `路線修築沿革`, publisher 國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司／臺灣鐵路管理局,
  `https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/6a439dbd-9cee-4d4b-9c4b-215439bffbf5`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete ten-page operator PDF was
  fetched. Original-language basis: `3. 臺北─淡水 Taipei-Danshui 21.2` and the
  corresponding row `光緒26年 1900`, `光緒27年 1901`, `民國78年5月8日起拆除。`.
- **The National Archives' full article describes the former TRA line as
  opening on 25 August 1901, changing from a materials-transport role into a
  major passenger corridor, operating until 1988 and continuing in a different
  form as MRT from 1997.** Source: National Archives Administration, National
  Development Council, *The branch lines of the Taiwan Railways Tamsui Line in
  archival records*, titleOriginal `從檔案資料看臺鐵淡水線之側線沿革與發展`,
  publisher 國家發展委員會檔案管理局, `https://www.archives.gov.tw/tw/arctw/76-6555.html`,
  SECONDARY, Medium confidence because the page is an archival research article
  summarising its underlying files rather than the railway's operating register.
  Original sentence: `於1901年8月25日通車啟用……且一路營運至1988年，並在1997年繼續以捷運的形式存在。`
- **DORTS describes the current MRT Tamsui Line as following the original
  Beidan railway right-of-way from Tamsui southward, and gives the current
  route as approximately 23.8 km including the 1.2 km Beitou–Xinbeitou branch,
  with 22 stations and one main depot.** Source: Taipei City Government
  Department of Rapid Transit Systems, *Tamsui–Xinyi Line*, titleOriginal
  `淡水信義線`, publisher 臺北市政府捷運工程局, `https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=0AC527D5FC9E596A`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the full builder page was fetched. Original
  sentence: `淡水線北起淡水站沿原北淡鐵路南行至北投以北之貴子坑溪為地面段……全長約23.8公里(含北投至新北投路段約1.2公里)，設22座車站及1座主機廠。`
- **A DORTS technical schedule gives the MRT Tamsui Line as 22.8 km, built
  from 1988 to 1997, with a 25 December 1997 opening date.** Source: Taipei
  City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems, *Overview of Taipei
  Metro project schedule control*, titleOriginal `臺北捷運計畫時程管制概論`,
  publisher 臺北市政府捷運工程局, `https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page170.html`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete page was fetched and its table
  read. Original table row: `淡水線 22.8 1988~1997 ... 1997.12.25`.
- **The Control Yuan report lists the Tamsui Line and Xinbeitou branch among
  former TRA railways whose rights-of-way were converted into MRT systems.**
  Source: Control Yuan, *Study of the activation and branch-operation
  efficiency of old Taiwan Railways tracks*, titleOriginal `臺鐵舊鐵道活化創新及支線營運效能提升之探討`,
  publisher 監察院, `https://cybsbox.cy.gov.tw/CYBSBoxSSL/edoc/download/44930`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete 25-page report was fetched.
  Original sentences: `臺鐵局已廢線停駛之鐵道共為23處` and the conversion table's
  `淡水線、新北投支線、中和線、東港線` under `捷運系統`.
- **Taipei City Government's cultural page records that the old Xinbeitou
  station entered history when the TRA Tamsui Line stopped in 1988, was moved
  to the Taiwan Folk Village in Changhua, and was reassembled and reopened in
  Taipei in 2017.** Source: Taipei City Government Department of Cultural
  Affairs, *Xinbeitou Station*, titleOriginal `新北投車站`, publisher 臺北市政府文化局,
  `https://culture.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=8A525F479E319D08&s=E2853B3322924CD2`,

## Conflicts and measurement boundaries

- **21.2 km, 22.8 km and approximately 23.8 km are all published.** The TRA
  table's 21.2 km is the historical Taipei–Danshui route entry. DORTS's 22.8
  km is an MRT construction-schedule row, while its route page's approximately
  23.8 km includes the Beitou–Xinbeitou branch and describes the current
  passenger route. The fetched sources do not justify choosing one value for
  all three scopes.
- **1988 operating-era boundary versus 8 May 1989 dismantling start.** The
  Archives article and the Taipei heritage page place the old TRA line's final
  operating era in 1988; the operator table says dismantling began on 8 May
  1989. These are retained as a service-era boundary and a physical-dismantling
  milestone, not collapsed into one unsupported closure date.
- **The current MRT is a reuse and reconstruction history, not a live TRA
  route identity.** DORTS says the current line follows the original Beidan
  railway right-of-way, while the Control Yuan groups the former route under
  railway lines converted into MRT systems. Neither source establishes that
  every current metre or station structure is the former TRA track.

## Stated gaps

An operator timetable archive, closure gazette, historical station register,
surveyed route plan and MRT construction as-built records could settle the last
train date, legal closure, former station sequence, gauge history and the exact
boundary between reused railway land and rebuilt MRT infrastructure. The
current evidence supports the phased conversion account and preserves all three
route-length values without selecting one.
