# Circular Line 0403 earthquake

Research date: 27 August 2026 (Asia/Taipei).

This is a one-subject record of the 3 April 2024 earthquake damage to the
Circular Line's viaduct and the Control Yuan's investigation. It distinguishes
the investigation's findings from the contractor's disputed position and keeps
the eleven-location and nine-span counts as separate published units.

## What is established

The official landing page identifies the report as an own-motion investigation by

**Claim - Confirmed: The earthquake damaged pot bearings and displaced eleven steel-box-girder locations, deforming track and damaging 139 equipment items.**

- **Source:** the same Control Yuan report above
- **Confidence:** High - explicit finding text
- **Original-language evidence:** `導致新北捷運環狀線板新站至橋和站間及景平路段，連接鋼箱梁與橋墩之盤式支承發生損壞，造成11處鋼箱梁產生錯位、軌道變形，139項設施受損`

The report says the 3 April 2024 earthquake was magnitude 7.2 and that the
affected service section between Banxin and Zhonghe could not operate. It also
states that no train was operating on the damaged section when the earthquake
occurred. The report's terms of reference mention the initial over-NT$400
million and one-year-plus recovery estimate as allegations it was asked to
investigate; those figures are not silently republished here as findings.

**Claim - Confirmed: The report's first finding is that BES milled the grade-adjustment plate through, leaving enough space for the locating pin to jump clear of the pot-bearing shear interface.**

- **Source:** the same Control Yuan report above
- **Confidence:** High - explicit 調查意見一 and measurement passage
- **Original-language evidence:** `本案施工單位中華工程股份有限公司未按施工圖說施工，任意將調坡板銑穿，使插銷上方留有贅餘空間，導致在0403地震作用下，插銷向上跳躍進入贅餘空間，使得插銷底部脫離支承上、下盤之交界面進而喪失抗剪能力`

The report gives the drawing recess as 5 mm, the field-measured recess as 41 mm,
the resulting excess space as 36 mm and the locating-pin height as 40 mm. It
says the New Taipei City Civil Engineers Association found pins lodged in the
upper bearing plate or fallen on the pier cap after jacking the damaged bridges.

**Claim - Confirmed: The Control Yuan identified design, review, inspection, monitoring and code problems in addition to the bearing construction finding.**

- **Source:** the same Control Yuan report above
- **Confidence:** High - findings two through six and their processing instructions
- **Original-language evidence:** `未對國內首次採用的疊式高架橋梁進行結構外審`; `調坡板皆未列為查驗項目`; `環狀線行控中心即無中原站地震儀測得相關地震數據紀錄`; `110年版「鐵路橋梁耐震設計規範」也是如此……依舊僅針對縱向(行車)方向規定`

The report describes the viaduct as a first-of-its-kind stacked elevated form,
finds no external structural review or added displacement-limiting devices,
records a double-deducted dead-load calculation in the uplift demand, finds

was not signed by the design reviewer or approving DORTS authority. It says the
concealed grade-adjustment plate was not an inspection hold point and that the
control centre had no Zhongyuan-station seismic record. It also says the 2021
bridge-seismic code did not address transverse displacement.

- **Source:** the same Control Yuan report above

This is why the page says the formal correction was directed at Taipei City

BES says its Y12 Qiaohe–Y14 Banxin labels cover the damaged section, that both
upper and lower bearing arrangements had milled-through slope plates but only
the upper support failed, and that the design should be examined for greater
strength and unequal seismic demand. This conflicts with the Control Yuan's
finding; the source set read here does not settle the dispute by a court merits
judgment.

## Conflicts

### Eleven locations versus nine spans

The Control Yuan and the 24 April 2024 DORTS notice use **11 locations** of
girder displacement, while the December operator reopening notice uses **9
spans** repositioned. These are contradictory if treated as the same unit, but
the sources read here do not provide the damage-location schedule needed to map
the eleven locations onto the nine spans. The page publishes both values with
their units rather than selecting one.

### Construction conformity and cause

The Control Yuan says BES did not follow the construction drawings and identifies
the over-milled plate and pin escape as the failure mechanism. BES says it did
follow the approved drawings and that the detail was not the main cause, pointing
to design strength and unequal seismic demand. The first is an official
investigative finding; the second is the contractor's primary statement. No court
merits judgment was found in this pass.

### Repair duration

New Taipei DORTS initially estimated more than a year, the operator later called
the repair 248 days, and the Control Yuan's report uses a 250-day account beside
its original one-year-two-month estimate. These are different stages and
rounding/starting conventions in official records; no source read here supplies a
single reconciliation, so they remain separately attributed.

## Stated gaps

The official record is sufficient to publish the earthquake mechanism, the design
and inspection chain, the monitoring gap, the recovery work and the competing
causal positions. It is not sufficient to state a final legal responsibility,
final financial settlement, current bearing-by-bearing inventory, or an exact
mapping between the eleven displacement locations and nine repaired spans. Those
would require court pleadings/judgment, the full commissioned structural report,
the repair as-built package and a current operator asset record.
