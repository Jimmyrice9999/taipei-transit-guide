# Taiwan Railways Zhonghe Line — research findings, 26 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below was checked against the linked primary page or full PDF; no search snippet is used as evidence.

This is a one-subject report on the former Banqiao–Zhonghe railway, also called
the Zhonghe Line or Bannan Railway in the municipal records. Full HTML pages
and the complete operator PDF were fetched and read on 2026-08-26. The sources
are kept separate where they describe different milestones.

## What is established

- **The TRA construction-history table records Banqiao–Zhonghe as a 6.5 km route and records physical dismantling in September 1993.** Source: National Taiwan Railways Corporation / former Taiwan Railways Administration, *Chronological History of Railroad Construction*, titleOriginal `路線修築沿革`, publisher `國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司／臺灣鐵路管理局`, `https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/6a439dbd-9cee-4d4b-9c4b-215439bffbf5`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete ten-page operator PDF was fetched. Original-language basis: `39.板橋─中和 Banqiao-Zhonghe 6.5` and the aligned explanation `民國82年9月拆除。`.
- **New Taipei DORTS records the line as freight-only from 5 March 1965 and links its construction to coal transport from Zhonghe.** Source: New Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems, *Railway roads changing shape — the urban texture formed by railways (3)*, titleOriginal `鐵路馬路變變變—鐵道型塑的都市紋理（三）`, publisher `新北市政府捷運工程局`, `https://www.dorts.ntpc.gov.tw/documentary/articleInfo/Qw92VjrK2jOk`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full municipal page was fetched. Original-language basis: `這條路線就是台鐵中和線；台鐵中和線全線於1965年3月5日開始營運，專辦貨運，這條路線的誕生主要是為了再運中和地區生產煤礦的需求。`.
- **Zhonghe District Office records the same 5 March 1965 opening, describes Zhonghe as a third-class station and says the branch replaced the Xindian Line's coal transport function.** Source: Zhonghe District Office, *Before administrative reform*, titleOriginal `改制前`, publisher `新北市中和區公所`, `https://www.zhonghe.ntpc.gov.tw/home.jsp?id=e32fc9bbd6f35420`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full district chronology was fetched. Original-language basis: `民國五四年 | 三月五日板南鐵路（中和線）竣工通車，三月二十五日新店線拆除。中和線取代新店線運煤功能，中和站開始營業，為三等站。`.
- **The DORTS route account places the former corridor from old Banqiao station through the Banxin Road, Bannan Road and Heping Street corridor to Zhonghe station.** Source: New Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems, *Did you know Zhonghe used to have a railway?*, titleOriginal `你知道中和以前有鐵路嗎?`, publisher `新北市政府捷運工程局`, `https://www.dorts.ntpc.gov.tw/documentary/articleInfo/apVdMxXj2KRw`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full municipal page was fetched. Original-language basis: `中和線從1965年開始營運，後來為配合台北捷運中和線興建而於1990年左右廢止拆除。當年中和線的路線，從舊板橋車站北上一公里左右往東南轉出，大致就是由縣民大道轉入板新路，再經由板南路、和平街進入中和站。`.
- **The district-office chronology dates the service stop to 23 September 1990 and describes conversion to Bannan Road toward Banqiao.** Source: Zhonghe District Office, *Before administrative reform*, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full chronology was fetched. Original-language basis: `九月二十三日起板南鐵路中線停止營業，改闢板南路通往板橋。`.
- **DORTS says the former Zhonghe station site became Nanshijiao station and Zhonghe depot, and that portions of the former corridor later carried the Circular Line.** Source: New Taipei DORTS, *Railway roads changing shape — the urban texture formed by railways (3)*, PRIMARY, Medium confidence for the modern reuse description because it is a municipal retrospective rather than a cadastral comparison. Original-language basis: `鐵路廢止後，原本的捷運線路則變成了板南路與板新路，原本的中和站位址則是改建為中和新蘆線的南勢角站及中和機廠；另一方面，部分的板南路與板新路段，也在2000年後開始建設捷運環狀線`.

## Conflicts and measurement boundaries

- **Dismantling date:** the operator's construction-history table records physical dismantling in September 1993. New Taipei DORTS describes the line as stopped and dismantled in 1990, while its route page says it was closed and dismantled around 1990. The Zhonghe District Office gives an exact 23 September 1990 service stop followed by road conversion. These may describe different administrative, service, road and physical-removal milestones; no source checked here reconciles them, so every value remains visible.
- **Route length:** the TRA operator gives 6.5 km. Neither checked DORTS page nor the district-office chronology gives a competing route-length figure. The length is therefore retained as the operator's route-table measurement, not silently extended along the modern road or metro corridor.
- **Historical identity versus modern reuse:** DORTS describes later Nanshijiao/Zhonghe depot and Circular Line relationships, but those statements concern corridor reuse. They do not establish that present MRT infrastructure has the same boundaries, station sequence or legal identity as the former TRA branch.

## Checked and failed

- **Complete former-station register** — checked 2026-08-26 in the full TRA PDF, both full DORTS pages and the Zhonghe District Office chronology. The sources identify the endpoints, one station class and a corridor, but do not supply one dated station list.
- **Legal closure instrument** — checked 2026-08-26 in the same operator and municipal records. They give service-stop, closure/dismantling and road-conversion statements, but no gazette or legal instrument was found.
- **Track-removal chronology** — checked 2026-08-26 in the full operator PDF and full DORTS pages. The operator gives September 1993, while municipal histories use 1990 or around 1990; no segment-by-segment removal record was found.
- **Exact right-of-way reuse boundary** — checked 2026-08-26 in the full DORTS route and history pages. They name present roads, Nanshijiao station, Zhonghe depot and later Circular Line works, but provide no surveyed parcel or track-centreline comparison.

## Stated gaps

A TRA timetable or closure gazette, a period-specific station register, engineering
completion and removal records, and cadastral or project drawings would settle the
service/dismantling chronology, station sequence and exact boundary between the
former railway, later roads and modern MRT works. Until those records are found,
the page retains the operator's 6.5 km and September 1993 figures alongside the
municipal 1990 and 23 September 1990 milestones.
