# The Matra dispute — research findings, 27 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below was checked against the linked primary page, full PDF or official publication; no search snippet is used as evidence.

This is a one-subject report on the Matra contract, the 1993 Muzha testing
fires and the later maintenance dispute. The earlier lead file
`docs/research/rail/lines/wenhu-research-part2.md` is useful for questions to
chase, but is not treated as evidence.

## What is established

- **Matra supplied the Muzha Line's VAL256 rubber-tyred, driverless system.**
  Taipei used two coupled two-car units as a four-car train, although the
  original VAL256 arrangement was a single car or two-car pair. DORTS's
  retrospective says the contractor failed to coordinate propulsion and brake
  signals between the two pairs; one pair could continue while the other
  braked, causing the brake caliper to overheat the rubber tyre. Source:
  Taipei City Government, Department of Rapid Transit Systems, *Journal of
  Rapid Transit Systems and Technology No. 50*, p. 56,
  `https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no50/files/basic-html/page56.html`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the full official page was fetched and read.
  Original-language basis: `木柵線測試中發生兩起火警事件` and `忽略了兩對車間推進及煞車訊號之同步協調` followed by `燃燒膠質輪胎`.

- **DORTS records two test-phase fire incidents and says the cause was later
  isolated through expert and contractor simulation and verification.** The
  same official account says the first incident did not remove the underlying
  cause, so two incidents occurred within six months. Source: DORTS,
  *Journal of Rapid Transit Systems and Technology No. 50*, p. 56, same URL,
  PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `而在第一次火警事件後，馬特拉公司並未就真正原因予以排除，導致半年內連續兩次火警事件` and `邀請學者專家會同馬特拉公司專業人員研討摸擬驗證`.

- **DORTS separately records the arbitration as a consequence of late civil
  works handing over the facilities to Matra.** Its account attributes the
  delay to overly fragmented packages, complex interfaces, difficult
  integration and contract execution, together with the domestic construction
  environment. The official page dates the arbitration finding to 6 October
  1993 and reports compensation of NT$1.04 billion. Source: DORTS, *Journal of
  Rapid Transit Systems and Technology No. 50*, p. 56, same URL, PRIMARY, High
  confidence. Original-language basis: `1993 年 10 月 6 日`;
  `土建設施延遲交付`; `分標過細、界面複雜、整合不易、合約執行困難`; and
  `應賠償馬特拉公司十億四千萬元`.

- **The official procurement case study supplies a more granular monetary
  breakdown and litigation summary.** It reports Matra's claim as over NT$2
  billion, the October 1993 award as NT$385,465,470 plus US$5,104,010 and
  FRF108,208,054 (approximately NT$1.025 billion at the stated conversion),
  with 5% annual interest. It says the Taipei DORTS action to set aside the
  award was finally lost in November 2000, followed by a debtor's objection
  action that was finally lost five years later; its estimate of principal plus
  interest is approximately NT$1.64 billion. Source: Public Construction
  Commission, *Using the Government Procurement Act v23*, pp. 51–52,
  `https://www.pcc.gov.tw/Download.ashx?icon=.pdf&n=MDBf5ZaE55So5o6h6LO85rOVIHYyMy5wZGY%3D&u=LzAwMS9VcGxvYWQvb2xkL2hvbWUvcGNjYXAvdXBsb2FkL2ZpbGVzL291dGVyL0QyMDA4MDAwMDAyLzAwX%2BWWhOeUqOaOoeizvOazlSB2MjMucGRm`, SECONDARY, High confidence for what this government case study reports because the complete PDF was fetched and read; it is not the court judgment. Original-language basis: `仲裁判斷結果，捷運局須賠償馬特拉公司新台幣10億多元，並須按年利率百分之五計算利息` and `最高法院於89年11月維持馬特拉勝訴確定`.

- **DORTS's later engineering-management text treats the Matra claim as an
  example of upstream civil delay producing downstream E&M extension claims.**
  It says most such claims arose from delayed handover of upstream works and
  recommends recording responsibility and evidence before mediation,
  arbitration or litigation. Source: DORTS, *Rapid Transit Engineering Series
  30 — E&M System Management and Interface Integration Practice*, p. 97,
  `https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no30/files/basic-html/page97.html`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the full official page was fetched and read.
  Original-language basis: `絕大部分機電時程展延之求償案件，均肇因於上游工程交付時程延誤` and `計有木柵線馬特拉求償案`.

- **The line's opening was preceded by an official inspection and reinforcement
  programme.** DORTS says the Muzha Line had both cap-beam cracks and vehicle
  fire incidents before opening; a committee's two-month inspection reported in
  May 1995 that cap-beam cracks had to be reinforced before opening, after which
  the line opened on 28 March 1996. Source: DORTS, *Journal of Rapid Transit
  Systems and Technology No. 48*, p. 185,
  `https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page185.html`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `於84年5月完成之體檢報告中確認，除帽樑裂縫必須於通車前補強完畢之外` and `85年3月28日正式通車`.

- **DORTS's project-history account treats the fires, Matra contract dispute
  and arbitration as separate crises handled through inspections, cap-beam
  reinforcement and further review before operation.** Source: DORTS,
  *Journal of Rapid Transit Systems and Technology No. 48*, p. 203,
  `https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page203.html`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `木柵線火警事件、與承包商馬特拉公司之契約爭議與仲裁糾紛` and `木柵線總體檢、帽樑補強、交通部複勘`.

- **The maintenance dispute was a distinct post-opening matter.** The Fair
  Trade Commission records that TRTC and Matra finished price negotiations on
  27 March 1996 for the VAL patented-system and interface-electrical-equipment
  maintenance agreement, but then had an inspection-and-acceptance dispute and
  did not sign the agreement. It records TRTC's allegation that Matra restricted
  subcontractors from supplying parts and maintenance, and says the alleged
  Fair Trade Law violation was probable. The Commission suspended its
  investigation conditionally so the parties could resume negotiations, with a
  21 March 1998 signing deadline. Source: Taiwan Fair Trade Commission,
  *Administrative settlement between Matra Transport Corporation and the Fair
  Trade Commission*,
  `https://www.ftc.gov.tw/internet/main/doc/docDetail.aspx?docid=3128&uid=287`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete official decision page was
  fetched and read. Original-language basis: `closed their price negotiations`
  on 27 March 1996; `did not sign the Agreement`; `restricted`; and `before 21
  March 1998`.

- **DORTS says the 1993 tyre incident affected the subsequent Neihu-system
  decision.** Its current Wenhu history records the incident during system
  testing and says the Taipei City Council then required a reassessment of
  whether the Neihu extension should use a high-capacity system; the council
  voted in 2001 to retain the approved elevated medium-capacity system. Source:
  DORTS, *Wenshan–Neihu Line*,
  `https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=DBAC040496EFAB94&s=864CDD262C4C443D`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete current page was fetched and
  read. Original-language basis: `因木柵線於82年系統測試階段發生輪胎起火意外事故` and `市議會於90年1月9日 ... 同意仍以 ... 高架中運量系統`.

## Scope and measurement boundaries

- The official DORTS account says NT$1.04 billion; the official procurement
  case study gives the currency components and approximately NT$1.025 billion.
  They are reported as two source values, not silently reconciled.
- The 1993 arbitration claim and the 1996–98 maintenance negotiations are
  different disputes. The first concerns delay-related cost compensation; the
  second concerns inspection/acceptance and maintenance access after opening.
- DORTS's technical account describes the physical failure mechanism as tyre
  ignition from unsynchronised four-car operation and braking. It does not
  identify either incident by a calendar date or station.
- The official pages support the 28 March 1996 opening and the inspection and
  reinforcement process. They do not by themselves establish the exact date
  Matra staff left Taiwan, whether the maintenance agreement was ultimately
  signed, or the full text of the court judgments.

## Conflicts

- **Award amount:** DORTS's engineering retrospective says NT$1.04 billion;
  the Public Construction Commission's case study itemises three currencies
  totalling approximately NT$1.025 billion before interest. The difference is
  retained as a source-precision or conversion conflict.
- **Fire wording:** DORTS's engineering account says `輪胎起火` and explains
  overheated rubber tyres; other secondary accounts call the events `火燒車`.
  The builder's more precise wording is retained alongside the secondary
  wording rather than treated as the same description.
- **Litigation duration and total payment:** the Public Construction
  Commission case study describes the set-aside action as nearly seven years,
  the overall sequence as twelve years, and approximately NT$1.64 billion with
  interest. Other secondary reports use slightly different elapsed-time and
  exchange-rate figures. The exact judgment text and payment record were not
  retrieved, so no more precise total is inferred.

## Checked and failed

- **Full Supreme Court judgment texts** — the set-aside and debtor-objection
  actions were checked 2026-08-27 through the Judicial Yuan judgment search and
  searches for 89年度台上字第2677號 and 94年度台上字第1348號. No retrievable
  full judgment record was found in the public results. The official PCC case
  study is retained as a secondary summary, not as a substitute for the
  judgments.
- **The Control Yuan's 1994 impeachment document** — the exact list of eight
  officials was checked 2026-08-27 in the Control Yuan public search and
  official PDF results using 木柵線, 馬特拉, 齊寶錚 and 齊寶銓. No matching
  original impeachment document was retrieved. Secondary accounts remain a
  lead only; the exact list, findings and relationship to the fires are not
  promoted here as established primary evidence.
- **Exact date of Matra's technical-staff withdrawal** — the final outcome of
  the maintenance agreement was checked 2026-08-27 in the full FTC decision,
  DORTS project-history pages and DORTS technical publications. The FTC page
  records the negotiation and conditional settlement process but not a staff
  withdrawal date or signed final agreement.
- **A primary accident-investigation report** — each fire's date, location,
  damaged components and causal finding were checked 2026-08-27 in the DORTS
  technical pages and current project history. DORTS gives the mechanism and
  the count but not the incident-by-incident investigation record.

## Stated gaps

The original contract, full arbitration award, Supreme Court judgments, Control
Yuan impeachment file, accident-investigation reports and final maintenance
agreement would settle the remaining legal, accountability and chronology gaps.
Until those are retrieved, the content page keeps the official DORTS mechanism,
award and inspection accounts separate from the secondary chronology and does
not invent a staff-withdrawal date, exact Control Yuan findings or a final
maintenance-contract outcome.
