# Taiwan Railways Houtong–Sandiaoling Double Track — research findings, 26 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below was checked against the linked primary page or full PDF; no search snippet is used as evidence.

This is a one-subject report on the operator's Houtong–Sandiaoling double-track
construction entry and its relationship to the current station/service layer
and the wider modern Houtong–Shuangxi curve-improvement study.

## Scope and measurement boundaries

- **The 2.5 km construction-table entry and the Houtong–Shuangxi safety-study scope are not interchangeable.** The former is a historical double-track construction segment; the latter is a wider modern curve-improvement corridor. The sources do not provide a surveyed comparison between them.
- **Current service grouping does not create a separate Houtong–Sandiaoling route identity.** The station pages and bicycle-train map show current adjacency and passenger-information groupings, while the construction table is the source for the historical segment.
- **The historical Chinese place form and current station page form differ:** the construction table has `侯硐`, while the current Houtong page has `猴硐`. The page keeps both spellings and does not infer two stations.

## Stated gaps

An operator construction contract, completion and commissioning records, track
diagrams, historic timetables and a current engineering as-built survey would
settle the exact double-track work boundary, the day-level opening record and
the relationship between row 42 and the wider Houtong–Shuangxi improvement
study. Until those records are found, the historical construction entry,
current service layer and modern proposal remain separate.
