# Taiwan Railways Hualien–Tianpu Line — research findings, 26 August 2026

This is a one-subject report on the former Hualien–Tianpu alignment and the
old Hualien station area. Retrieval date: 2026-08-26. The complete operator
construction-history PDF and the full Hualien government pages were fetched and
read; search snippets are not used as evidence.

## What is established

- **The Taiwan Railways construction-history table contains a Hualien–Tianpu
  entry of 2.4 km and records the Hualien–Tianpu section as dismantled in 1982.**
  Source: Taiwan Railways, *Chronological History of Railroad Construction*,
  titleOriginal `路線修築沿革`, publisher 國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司 / former
  臺灣鐵路管理局, `https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/6a439dbd-9cee-4d4b-9c4b-215439bffbf5`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete ten-page operator PDF was
  fetched and the route table and explanation were read in context.
  Original-language basis: `57. 花蓮─田浦 Hualien-Tianpu 2.4` and
  `花蓮至田蒲民國71年拆除。` The source uses both `田浦` and `田蒲` in its
  table text.
- **Hualien's old station was the northern hub of the former 762 mm narrow-gauge
  East Line and remained in use until the East Line widening was completed in
  1982.** Hualien County Government's Hakka Affairs Office says that the
  widening removed the railway from Tianpu to the old Hualien station and also
  reached the old station–Hualien Port area; it names Meilun, Xincun, old
  Hualien and Tianpu among the stations then retired. Source: Hualien County
  Government Hakka Affairs Office, *Hualien Rail Cultural Park*, titleOriginal
  `花蓮鐵道文化園區`, publisher 花蓮縣政府客家事務處, `https://hk.hl.gov.tw/News_Content.aspx?n=39458&s=175434&sms=28561`,
  SECONDARY, Medium confidence because the page is a local-government cultural
  history account rather than the railway operator's route register. Original
  sentence: `民國71年（1982）東線鐵路拓寬工程完工後，把田浦（今多寫成田埔）到花蓮舊站間的鐵路廢除……同時裁撤美崙、新村、花蓮舊站至田浦這些車站。`
- **The old station's passenger and station business moved to the new Hualien
  station on 27 June 1982.** Source: Hualien County Government Hakka Affairs
  Office, *Hualien City*, titleOriginal `花蓮市`, publisher 花蓮縣政府客家事務處,
  `https://hk.hl.gov.tw/cp.aspx?n=39464`, SECONDARY, Medium confidence because
  it is a municipal historical page. Original sentence: `持續使用直到民國71年
  (西元1982年)6月27日東線拓寬完工之後，車站業務遷移到花蓮新站辦理。`
- **The former Hualien station rail corridor survives in the street and heritage
  landscape, not as a current passenger route in the checked records.** Hualien
  County Cultural Affairs Bureau identifies today's Fucheng Road as the old
  station railway, says rails were progressively removed and paved after the
  railway offices moved, and identifies the former station water tower as a
  surviving structure. Source: Hualien County Cultural Affairs Bureau, *Former
  Hualien Station water tower*, titleOriginal `前花蓮火車站加水塔`, publisher
  花蓮縣文化局, `https://www.hccc.gov.tw/zh-tw/CulturalHeritage/Detail/32`,
  SECONDARY, Medium confidence because the bureau is documenting a heritage
  asset rather than publishing the railway's operating register. Original
  sentence: `鐵道上的軌道也陸續拆除，並鋪上柏油路面，即成為今日的福町路。`
- **The former station site became the Hualien Rail Cultural Park in 2002.**
  Source: Hualien City cultural-tourism site, *A short history of the former
  Hualien station and port line*, titleOriginal `淺談舊花蓮車站與臨港線歷史`,
  publisher 花蓮市公所, `https://culture-tourism.hualien.gov.tw/review_dt.php?id=19`,
  SECONDARY, Medium confidence because the page is a municipal cultural
  feature. Original sentence: `「舊花蓮車站」在廢止的20年後，化身為鐵道文化園區……從2002年開園。`

## Conflicts and measurement boundaries

- **2.4 km versus the wider old-station and port-line descriptions.** The TRA
  table gives a 2.4 km Hualien–Tianpu entry. The Hualien municipal accounts
  describe the old-station–Tianpu railway together with other former city and
  port segments; the cultural-tourism page separately gives 1.1 km for the old
  coastal line and 4.0 km for the 1939 old Hualien Port line. These are different
  route segments and must not be added to, or substituted for, the operator's
  2.4 km entry.
- **Dismantling year versus station-transfer date.** TRA gives 1982 as the
  dismantling year. The Hualien City page gives 27 June 1982 for completion of
  widening and transfer of station business to the new station. The dates are
  compatible as a year-level dismantling record and a dated operational
  transition, but the fetched sources do not establish the exact day of every
  track-removal action.
- **The operator table does not expose an unambiguous segment-level gauge
  chronology.** Municipal pages identify the old East Line as 762 mm and the
  later new Hualien connection as part of the widening transition, but the
  fetched operator table does not say which gauge applied on each Hualien–Tianpu
  phase. The page therefore keeps the 762 mm context and the dismantling record
  separate rather than inventing a conversion date.

## Stated gaps

An operator timetable archive, station register, gazette record, East Line
widening completion file and Railway Bureau right-of-way inventory could settle
the former station sequence, legal closure, exact removal dates, gauge changes
and surviving alignment. The current evidence supports the dated transition and
the heritage footprint, but not a complete historical station page for every
former stop.
