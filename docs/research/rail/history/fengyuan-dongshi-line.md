# Taiwan Railways Fengyuan–Dongshi Line — research findings, 26 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below was checked against the linked primary page or full PDF; no search snippet is used as evidence.

This is a one-subject report on the former Fengyuan–Dongshi railway, also called
the Dongshi Line, Dongshi branch and Dongfeng Railway in local records. Full
operator, government and municipal pages and PDFs were fetched and read on
2026-08-26. Operator records and later retrospective measurements are kept
separate where they describe different milestones or scopes.

## What is established

- **The TRA construction-history table records Fengyuan–Dongshi as a 14.1 km route, constructed in 1958, operated from 1959 and stopped in September 1991.** Source: National Taiwan Railways Corporation / former Taiwan Railways Administration, *Chronological History of Railroad Construction*, titleOriginal `路線修築沿革`, publisher 國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司／臺灣鐵路管理局, `https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/6a439dbd-9cee-4d4b-9c4b-215439bffbf5`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete ten-page operator PDF was fetched. Original-language basis: the row `37.豐原─東勢 Fengyuan-Dongshi 14.1` and its aligned fields `民國47年 1958`, `民國48年 1959`, `民國80年9月停運。`.
- **The operator chronology records all Dongshi Line stations stopping operation and being withdrawn on 1 September 1991.** Source: National Taiwan Railways Corporation, *Taiwan Railways chronology, 66–98*, titleOriginal `臺鐵大事紀`, publisher 國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司, `https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/adr/about-timeline?selectIndex=17`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full HTML chronology was fetched. Original-language basis: `80.09.01 | 東勢線各站停運裁撤。`.
- **Taichung’s Hakka-affairs retrospective records the last train as leaving on 31 August 1991.** Source: Taichung City Government Hakka Affairs Commission, *Taichung Dongshi Line shutdown 30th-anniversary retrospective exhibition*, titleOriginal `台鐵東勢線停駛30週年回顧展 百件展品回憶昔日風華`, publisher 臺中市政府客家事務委員會, `https://www.taichung.gov.tw/1867179/post`, SECONDARY, High confidence for the date as the city’s published retrospective because the full municipal page was fetched; it is not an operator register. Original-language basis: `曾盛極一時的台鐵東勢線於民國80年8月31日最後一班列車駛出後停駛`.
- **The Forestry Bureau’s 1991 chronology says the branch was built to move Dasyueshan Forestry Company materials and records an 11 km green-corridor plan after suspension.** Source: Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency, Ministry of Agriculture, *ROC year 80 (1991)*, titleOriginal `第十節　民國80年（1991）`, publisher 農業部林業及自然保育署（formerly Forestry Bureau, Council of Agriculture）, `https://www.forest.gov.tw/0001327`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full government chronology was fetched. Original-language basis: `本月東豐鐵路支線停駛，該線原為便利大雪山林業公司運材而築（4712d）；嗣後行政院環保署及台中縣政府計畫以長度11公里路段（占地面積36公頃）建成綠色走廊之環保公園。`.
- **The current Taichung tourism page describes the 13.6 km Dongfeng Bikeway as rebuilt from an abandoned railway and identifies surviving former railway features.** Source: Taichung City Tourism and Travel Bureau, *Dongfeng Bikeway*, titleOriginal `東豐自行車綠廊`, publisher 臺中市政府觀光旅遊局, `https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/zh-tw/experience/cyclingtrip/3`, PRIMARY, High confidence for the present tourism description because the full current page was fetched. Original-language basis: `東豐自行車綠廊為全國第一條由廢棄鐵道改建的自行車專用道，全長13.6公里` and the following descriptions of the former Shigang platform, retained rails and Dongshi tunnel.
- **A Taichung municipal magazine retrospective identifies the route as Fengyuan–Dongshi, names Meizi, Shigang and Puzikou as intermediate stops, gives a 14.1–14.7 km range, attributes the line’s purpose partly to Dasyueshan timber transport and dates the shutdown to 31 August 1991.** Source: Taichung City Government News Bureau, *Taichung Good Life, September 2018*, titleOriginal `臺中好生活2018年9月號`, publisher 臺中市政府新聞局, `https://www.taichung.gov.tw/media/366888/臺中好生活2018年9月號72dpi.pdf`, SECONDARY, High confidence as a complete municipal magazine PDF; the historical claims remain a retrospective account rather than a replacement for the operator table. Original-language basis: `東豐自行車綠廊原為東勢到豐原的鐵道，又稱「東勢支線」，從東勢出發，沿途停靠梅子、石岡、朴子口，最後抵達終點站豐原，全長約14.1至14.7公里，1958年完工啟用，主要是為了運送大雪山林場所採伐的林木，同時提供沿線居民對外的交通服務。 ... 最終在1991年8月31日停駛`.

## Conflicts and measurement boundaries

- **Final train versus operator withdrawal:** the city retrospective dates the last train to 31 August 1991, while the operator chronology records all stations stopping operation and being withdrawn on 1 September. The sources describe adjacent boundaries and are retained as last public train versus operator withdrawal, not collapsed into one date.
- **Railway length versus retrospective and reuse lengths:** the operator gives 14.1 km; the municipal magazine gives approximately 14.1–14.7 km; the Forestry Bureau records an 11 km planned green-corridor section; current Taichung tourism gives the bikeway as 13.6 km. These are not averaged. They describe an operator route, a retrospective historical range, a 1991 plan and a present cycle path.
- **Stop sequence:** the municipal magazine names Meizi, Shigang and Puzikou, but the checked operator records do not provide a complete historical station register. The named sequence is therefore presented as a secondary retrospective record, not as a complete station list.

## Checked and failed

- **Complete former-station register** — checked 2026-08-26 in the full TRA construction-history PDF, the operator chronology, the full Forestry Bureau chronology and the full Taichung municipal retrospective and tourism pages. The sources identify endpoints and three intermediate stops, but do not provide one complete dated station register.
- **Legal closure instrument** — checked 2026-08-26 in the same full operator and government records. They establish the last train, station withdrawal and September suspension, but no gazette or legal instrument was found.
- **Track-removal chronology** — checked 2026-08-26 in the full operator and government records. They establish suspension and later conversion planning/use, but no segment-by-segment removal schedule was found.
- **Exact railway-to-bikeway boundary** — checked 2026-08-26 in the Forestry Bureau chronology and current Taichung tourism page. They give 11 km and 13.6 km figures for different milestones, but no surveyed parcel or track-centreline comparison.

## Stated gaps

A TRA route register or historical timetable, the closure gazette, engineering and
removal records, and a right-of-way or cadastral survey would settle the full
station sequence, legal closure, track-removal chronology and the exact boundary
between the former railway and present bikeway. Until those records are found,
the page retains the operator’s 14.1 km and September 1991 values alongside the
municipal 31 August final-train date and the separate 11 km and 13.6 km reuse
measurements.

## Update, 29 August 2026 — construction schedule, decline narrative, disaster damage

- **zh.wikipedia, `東勢線`**, SECONDARY, citing two books not independently
  obtained (古庭維、鄧志忠《台灣舊鐵道散步地圖》2010; 蔡仁輝《閒話臺鐵五十年》1996).
  Construction schedule: began 14 March 1958, first test train 26 December
  1958, opened 12 January 1959, opening timetable 8 passenger + 2 mixed
  round trips daily. Full six-stop list with distances. Second construction
  purpose (Deji Dam hydroelectric materials) not found in the Forestry
  Bureau primary. Decline narrative: 1973 Dasyueshan Forestry Co.
  dissolution (chainsaw logging exhausted accessible timber), road
  competition, student-dominated final ridership. Closing day: 14,419
  system-wide tickets sold, last train left Dongshi 18:02.
- **A travel blog (apextraveller.com)** repeats the dual-purpose claim in
  near-identical wording to the Wikipedia article — treated as likely
  derivative, not independent corroboration.
- **A second travel blog (bobbytravel.tw)**, no sources cited on the page,
  names a third and different dam — Shigang Dam (石岡水壩), not Deji Dam —
  as a construction purpose. A three-way conflict on what "hydroelectric
  materials" purpose means: Forestry Bureau primary (timber only) vs.
  Wikipedia/apextraveller (Deji Dam) vs. bobbytravel (Shigang Dam). Not
  resolved; only the primary-vs-Wikipedia pairing is published on the
  content page, since the Shigang Dam claim's source cites nothing of its
  own.
- **A fourth, unverified figure**: a Yahoo search snippet only (not a full
  fetch) of `tune-shih-railroad.fuludun.tw` claimed ~100,000 tonnes/year of
  Deji Dam construction material moved via this line. Every fetch attempt
  failed (self-signed certificate; web.archive.org refused). **Not
  published anywhere**, per rule 2 — snippet-only, unverifiable.
- **zh.wikipedia, `石岡車站（台灣）`**, SECONDARY, citing TRA statistical
  yearbooks (1975-1991) not independently opened. Station-level downgrade
  timeline: 1959 opened 3rd-class, 1971-05-01 downgraded to simplified
  station, 1983-03-01 downgraded to request-stop, 1991-09-01 withdrawn.
  Corroborates 921 earthquake fault damage to the disused platform/track.
- **zh.wikipedia, `東勢車站`**, SECONDARY, same yearbook-citation caveat.
  Parallel downgrade timeline for the terminus: 2nd-class from 1959,
  3rd-class from 1981-01-01, simplified station from 1989-10-01, withdrawn
  1991-09-01.
- **zh.wikipedia, `東豐自行車綠廊`**, SECONDARY. Paving/planting began 1998
  under then-Taichung-County magistrate 廖永來; bikeway formally opened
  15 November 2000. A fifth length figure, ~12 km, for the present bikeway
  (against the operator's 14.1 km, the tourism board's 13.6 km, and the
  Forestry Bureau's 11 km plan — four now, not reconciled). Describes 921
  earthquake damage (destroyed townhouses on the fault-rupture zone next to
  the greenway; ~10 m vertical displacement at the former Shigang platform,
  now a fault-viewing site) and a 2006 flood-damage/2008 repair episode at
  the Meizi Iron Bridge, the latter citing an archived source (`emmm.tw`
  via web.archive.org) this page could not reach.
- **A separate news source** (自由時報, on 埤豐橋/Pifeng Bridge, Dongshi) was
  chased as a possible earthquake-damaged railway bridge and ruled out: it
  is a road bridge built in 1991, unrelated to this railway.
