# Taiwan Railways Tanzi–Shengang Line — research findings, 26 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below was checked against the linked primary page or full PDF; no search snippet is used as evidence.

This is a one-subject report on the Tanzi–Shengang route and the later
Tanyashen Greenway. The operator construction table and Taichung municipal
records use different descriptions of the former railway's identity. The
measurements and administrative descriptions are therefore kept separate.

## What is established

- **The TRA construction-history table lists Tanzi–Shengang as 8.7 km, constructed in 1956 and operated from 1957, with TRA as constructor.** Source: National Taiwan Railways Corporation / former Taiwan Railways Administration, *Chronological History of Railroad Construction*, titleOriginal `路線修築沿革`, publisher 國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司／臺灣鐵路管理局, `https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/6a439dbd-9cee-4d4b-9c4b-215439bffbf5`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete operator PDF was fetched and read. Original-language basis: the row `36.潭子─神岡 Tanzi-Shengang 8.7 臺灣鐵路管理局 TRA` and aligned fields `民國45年 1956` and `民國46年 1957`.
- **The Tanzi District Office describes the Tanyashen Greenway as originating from a military logistics railway discontinued in ROC year 87 (1998), about 14 km long, and extending from Tanzi Zhongshan Road through Fengyuan, Daya and Shengang to the Zhongke hinterland in Daya's Zhongyi Village.** Source: Tanzi District Office, *Tanyashen Greenway*, titleOriginal `潭雅神綠園道`, publisher 臺中市潭子區公所, `https://www.tanzi.taichung.gov.tw/981916/1854974/3252725/3252745/3252846`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full district-office HTML page was fetched and read. Original-language basis: `緣起於民國87年廢止使用的國防部聯勤地區運輸用鐵路，是我國第三條利用廢棄軍用運輸鐵道闢建的自行車道，全長約14公里。` and `從潭子區中山路起，沿線還經過了豐原、大雅、神岡，終點在大雅區忠義里的中科腹地`.
- **The Shengang District Office dates the greenway opening to 2 May 2004 and calls its predecessor the former military railway Shengang branch, about 14 km long.** Source: Shengang District Office, *Tanyashen Greenway*, titleOriginal `潭雅神綠園道`, publisher 臺中市神岡區公所, `https://www.shengang.taichung.gov.tw/982296/982509/982566/982611/982620/1179283/post`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full district-office HTML page was fetched and read. Original-language basis: `「潭雅神綠園道」自於2004年5月2日啟用，它利用原軍方舊鐵道神岡支線興建而成` and the following route/length text `全長約14公里`.
- **The Shengang page records the former Peace Station as a military-materials railway facility and says the Ministry of Defense agreed in May 2000 to let the railway serve as an external access route, after which the county government promoted conversion to a bicycle path.** Source: the same full Shengang District Office page, PRIMARY, High confidence because the page states the date and sequence directly. Original-language basis: `這個車站早期的用途為運送軍方物資的鐵道，不開放給民間使用，直到民國八十九年五月國防部同意將此鐵道作為聯外通路，於是縣府積極推動鐵道改造成一個供民眾休息用的自行車道。`
- **The Taichung Tourism and Travel Bureau also describes the roughly 14 km greenway as built on the former military railway Shengang branch from Tanzi Zhongshan Road through Shengang to Daya's Zhongke Park.** Source: Taichung City Tourism and Travel Bureau, *A healthy Mother's Day choice: a Tanyashen cycling trip*, titleOriginal `母親節踏出健康新選擇－潭雅神鐵馬樂活之旅`, publisher 臺中市政府觀光旅遊局, `https://www.tourism.taichung.gov.tw/16592/16596/1183991/1524538`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full current municipal release was fetched and read. Original-language basis: `利用軍方舊鐵道神岡支線興建而成，從潭子區中山路起，經神岡區，終點在大雅區中科公園，全長約14公里`.
- **The Shengang urban-plan review says the former longitudinal railway Taichung branch had ceased operation and was being used as the Tanyashen Greenway, with disused stations assigned as greenway rest stops.** Source: Taichung City Government Urban Development Bureau, *Book for the third comprehensive review of the expanded Shengang urban plan, first stage*, titleOriginal `變更神岡擴大修訂都市計畫（第三次通盤檢討）（第一階段）書`, publisher 臺中市政府都市發展局, `https://www.ud.taichung.gov.tw/media/1112635/%E7%A5%9E%E5%B2%A1%E9%83%BD%E5%B8%82%E8%A8%88%E7%95%AB-%E4%B8%89%E9%80%9A-%E7%AC%AC%E4%BA%8C%E9%9A%8E%E6%AE%B5-%E6%9B%B8.pdf`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full official PDF was fetched and the relevant land-use table and explanatory pages were read. Original-language basis: `原縱貫鐵路臺中支線業已停駛，目前為「潭雅神綠園道」使用` and `於原屬縱貫鐵路臺中支線停用車站劃設為公園用地，提供自行車逛遊系統之休憩站`.

## Conflicts and measurement boundaries

- **Former-railway identity:** TRA's construction table labels the 8.7 km Tanzi–Shengang route as a TRA-built route. Tanzi and Shengang district pages and the city tourism bureau describe the reused corridor as a military logistics railway or military Shengang branch. The urban-plan review calls it the former longitudinal railway's Taichung branch. These are not reconciled by the checked records; the page publishes the conflict instead of deciding which institutional label is correct.
- **Route length:** the operator table gives 8.7 km; the municipal reuse sources give approximately 14 km for the Tanyashen Greenway. No source checked here provides a surveyed mapping explaining the difference, so the figures are not averaged.
- **Status dates and milestones:** the Tanzi page dates military-railway discontinuation to 1998; the Shengang page dates the Ministry of Defense access agreement to May 2000 and greenway opening to 2 May 2004; the operator table supplies no corresponding stopping or transfer date for its Tanzi–Shengang row. These are retained as separate milestones.

## Checked and failed

- **Complete former-station register** — checked 2026-08-26 in the full operator construction-history PDF, the full Tanzi and Shengang district pages, the full tourism release and the full urban-plan PDF. The records identify the route endpoints and Peace Station, but do not provide one complete historical station register.
- **Legal closure or institutional-transfer instrument** — checked 2026-08-26 in the same full official sources. They provide discontinuation, access-agreement and reuse dates, but no closure order, transfer agreement or operator-to-military custody record was found.
- **Track-removal chronology** — checked 2026-08-26 in the full official sources. They establish discontinuation and greenway conversion but do not provide a segment-by-segment removal schedule.
- **Surveyed route comparison** — checked 2026-08-26 in the full operator PDF, urban-plan PDF and municipal greenway pages. The sources give 8.7 km and approximately 14 km with corridor descriptions, but no centreline, parcel or boundary comparison that explains the scope difference.

## Stated gaps

A TRA engineering or route register, a Ministry of Defense logistics-railway
record, the closure and transfer instruments, construction and removal records,
and a cadastral or centreline survey would settle the institutional identity,
station sequence, exact status dates, track-removal chronology and the
8.7-to-14 km measurement boundary. Until those records are found, the operator
route and municipal greenway accounts remain separate published descriptions.
