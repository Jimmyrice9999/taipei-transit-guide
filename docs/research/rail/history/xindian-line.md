# Taiwan Railways Xindian Line — research findings, 26 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below was checked against the linked primary page or full PDF; the one secondary feature is explicitly kept as a lead.

This is a one-subject report on the former Wanhua–Xindian railway, also called the
Wanxin Railway. The current TRA architecture keeps it separate from the modern
Taipei Metro Xindian Line. Full HTML pages and the complete operator PDF were
fetched and read on 2026-08-26; no search snippet is used as evidence.

## What is established

- **The TRA construction-history table records Wanhua–Xindian as a 10.4 km route built by Taipei Railway Company; the aligned explanation records purchase in 1949 and dismantling in 1965.** Source: National Taiwan Railways Corporation / former Taiwan Railways Administration, *Chronological History of Railroad Construction*, titleOriginal `路線修築沿革`, publisher 國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司／臺灣鐵路管理局, `https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/6a439dbd-9cee-4d4b-9c4b-215439bffbf5`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete ten-page PDF was fetched. Original-language basis: `33.萬華─新店 Wanhua-Xindian 10.4 臺北鐵路公司 Taipei Railway Co.` and the aligned explanation `民國38年收購，54年拆除。`.
- **New Taipei DORTS records the full opening as 25 March 1921, gives a 1,067 mm gauge, and says operation stopped on 20 March 1965.** Source: New Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems, *Urban texture formed by railways (5) — Xindian Line*, titleOriginal `鐵道型塑的都市紋理(五)-新店線`, `https://www.dorts.ntpc.gov.tw/documentary/articleInfo/VKgmOlr42nxA?page=3`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full municipal page was fetched. Original-language basis: `1921年3月25日全通，軌距1,067mm` and `台鐵局新店線︰1965年3月20日停駛`.
- **The same DORTS page says the railway was converted into ordinary roads in 1968 and that the modern Xindian MRT route south of Guting almost overlaps the historical route.** Source: the same full DORTS page, PRIMARY, High confidence for what the municipal account states. Original-language basis: `後續於1968年拆除改建成一般道路，為今日汀州路與羅斯福路及北新路` and `現在的捷運新店線，在古亭以南，許多站名與當年新店線完全相同，路線也幾乎重疊`.
- **Xindian District Office describes a 10.5 km single-track route with fourteen stations and four daily round trips, carrying timber, tea and coal before the line also served local travel.** Source: Xindian District Office, *Bitan scenery*, titleOriginal `碧潭風情`, `https://www.xindian.ntpc.gov.tw/home.jsp?act=be4f48068b2b0031&dataserno=bced6183b4d67af535c871cd71f636ef&id=2b519b334822681d`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched. Original-language basis: `全長10.5公里的單軌路線，自萬華起站經馬場町（和平）、古亭町（古亭）、螢町（螢橋）、水源地、公館、景尾（景美）、十五分、七張、大坪林、新店等十四站，每日往復四次` and `早期是以運送木材、茶業、煤為主`.
- **Taipei City Government gives a 10.7 km figure, says the Japanese-era account had fourteen stations and the post-retrocession account ten, and dates the end of operation to 1965.** Source: Taipei City Government, *Jingmei’s former splendour*, titleOriginal `景美風華再現`, `https://www.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=F0DDAF49B89E9413&s=EC29198AFE22C076&sms=72544237BBE4C5F6`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full city page was fetched. Original-language basis: `此路線由萬華火車站至新店，全長10.7公里，景美火車站為新店線之大站，在日治時期原設14站，光復後省政府減為10個車站` and `民國54年...新店線鐵路結束營運`.

## Conflicts and measurement boundaries

- **Route length:** the operator table gives 10.4 km, the Xindian District Office gives 10.5 km and Taipei City Government gives 10.7 km. They are all retained as source-specific route figures; no source establishes that the three measurements use the same endpoints or extension treatment.
- **Withdrawal versus dismantling:** New Taipei DORTS gives 20 March 1965 as the operating stop and 1968 as the road-conversion milestone. The TRA table gives 1965 as the dismantling year. These are not collapsed into one date: they leave a three-year difference between the municipal road account and the operator’s year-only dismantling entry.
- **Station count:** the Xindian District Office describes fourteen stations, while Taipei City Government distinguishes fourteen in the Japanese-era account from ten after retrocession. A complete period-specific station register was not found, so the counts are not merged.

## Checked and failed

- **Exact final passenger and freight service times** — checked 2026-08-26. The full NTU Alumni Bimonthly feature says passenger service ended on 24 March 1965 and freight service on 25 March, while New Taipei DORTS gives 20 March. The full TRA PDF confirms only the 1965 dismantling year. The times remain unverified and are not asserted in the content page.
- **Complete former-station register** — checked 2026-08-26 in the full TRA PDF, New Taipei DORTS page, Xindian District Office page and Taipei City Government page. They provide differing counts and examples, not one dated register.
- **Legal closure instrument and track-removal chronology** — checked 2026-08-26 in the same operator and municipal records. The sources establish purchase, withdrawal, dismantling year and a later road-conversion account, but no gazette or segment-by-segment removal schedule was found.
- **Exact reused-corridor boundary** — checked 2026-08-26 in the full municipal accounts. They describe roads and broad MRT overlap, but do not provide a surveyed parcel or track-centreline comparison.

## Stated gaps

A TRA route register, historical timetable, closure gazette, engineering or cadastral archive and a period-specific station list would settle the final-service date, station sequence, dismantling chronology and the boundary between former railway land, later roads and the modern metro. Until those records are found, the page keeps the 10.4, 10.5 and 10.7 km figures and the 1965/1968 milestones in their separate scopes.
