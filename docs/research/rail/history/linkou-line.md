This is a one-subject report on the former Linkou Line. The current TRA TDX
snapshot does not contain a live Linkou route record; this report therefore
uses the operator's construction-history table, the operator chronology and a
Ministry of Transportation and Communications yearbook. Retrieval date:
2026-08-26. Full PDF and HTML pages were fetched and read; no search snippet is
used as evidence.

## What is established

- **The construction-history table identifies the Linkou Line as a 19.2 km
  TRA project, constructed in ROC year 55 (1966), with operation started in ROC
  year 57 (1968), and stopped in ROC year 102 (2013).** Source: Taiwan
  Railways, *Chronological History of Railroad Construction*, titleOriginal
  `路線修築沿革`, publisher 國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司 / former 臺灣鐵路管理局,
  `https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/6a439dbd-9cee-4d4b-9c4b-215439bffbf5`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete ten-page PDF was fetched.
  Original-language basis: `101. 林口線 Linkou Line 19.2` and the row's
  `民國55年 1966`, `民國57年 1968`, `民國102年停駛`.
- **The operator's chronology records a passenger-service opening ceremony on
  27 October 2005.** Source: Taiwan Railways, *Taiwan Railways chronology,
  66–98*, titleOriginal `臺鐵大事紀`, publisher 國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司,
  `https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/adr/about-timeline?selectIndex=17`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the full HTML page was fetched. Original
  sentence: `94.10.27 | 舉辦林口線客運列車開行通車典禮。`
- **MOTC's 2012 yearbook records the closure as effective 31 December 2012
  in the context of the line's suspension and says all 45 Linkou Line level
  crossings were taken out of use.** Source: Ministry of Transportation and
  Communications, *101 Taiwan Transport Yearbook — Taiwan Railways*,
  titleOriginal `101年交通年鑑`, publisher 交通部,
  `https://www.motc.gov.tw/ch/app/yearbook/doc?detailNo=2&id=21&module=directory&serno=5807&type=s&year=101`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the complete ten-page PDF was fetched.
  Original sentence: `101年12月31日配合林口線停駛，全線45處平交道停用。`
- **A later Railway Bureau planning document describes the former Linkou Line
  corridor as approximately 19.2 km and says a corridor-revitalisation project
  was renamed and included in a wider Taoyuan urban project.** Source: Railway
  Bureau, *Comprehensive planning for the Taoyuan section underground railway
  project*, titleOriginal `臺鐵都會區捷運化桃園段地下化建設計畫綜合規劃`, publisher
  交通部鐵道局, `https://www.rb.gov.tw/public/files/artsinfo/1622619792-m0.pdf`,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the full 35-page PDF was fetched. Original
  sentence: `本案原屬「臺鐵林口線路廊活化工程」計畫，桃園市政府配合中央前瞻計畫－城鎮之心工程之推動，於106年申請獲營建署補助後更名，計畫範圍包括臺鐵林口線路廊…總長約19.2km。`
  This establishes planning treatment of the corridor, not a reopened railway.

## Conflicts and measurement boundaries

- **1968 operation-start row versus 2005 passenger opening ceremony.** The two
  official sources use different milestones. The construction-history table
  calls 1968 the year operation started, while the operator chronology records
  a passenger-train opening ceremony in 2005. The page therefore does not call
  either date the single line opening: 1968 is the table's operation-start
  field, and 2005 is the dated passenger-service ceremony.
- **2012 versus 2013 closure wording.** MOTC's 2012 yearbook records the
  31 December 2012 suspension and crossing shutdown, while the operator's
  construction-history table labels the stopped-operating year as 2013. Both
  values are published with their apparent scopes; the report does not average
  or choose between them.
- **19.2 km appears in both the construction-history table and the later
  corridor-planning document.** The repetition does not prove that the two
  documents measured the same surviving track or right-of-way; the later source
  explicitly describes a corridor project.

  provide one complete passenger-station register for the former line.
- **Exact legal closure gazette and track-removal date** — checked 2026-08-26
  in the same operator and MOTC records. The sources establish the suspension,
  crossing shutdown and the 2013 stopped-operating year, but no gazette or
  complete dismantling schedule was found.
- **Gauge and full historical geometry** — checked 2026-08-26 in the TRA route
  history and the current TDX geometry snapshot. The sources do not provide a
  segment-specific Linkou gauge record or a citable full historical geometry;
  neither is asserted here.

## Stated gaps

A TRA route register, historical timetable, gazette archive and Railway Bureau
right-of-way inventory would settle the former station sequence, legal closure,
track-removal chronology, gauge and surviving structures. The current page can
therefore document the sourced milestones and the later corridor-planning
record, but not invent a historical station page for every former stop.
