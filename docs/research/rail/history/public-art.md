### Xiaobitan cost and scope

Three primary sources publish different figures for `幸福知道`; none
reconciles them:

1. DORTS's general public-art table says `作品經費：1,000,000元`.
2. DORTS's dedicated work page says `約35,900,000元，另首獎獎金1,000,000元`.
3. DORTS's technical book, *捷運工程叢書精進版－2 捷運車站規劃與設計實務*, p. 249,
   https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no2/files/basic-html/page249.html, says
   `受經費1,500萬元之限制` and that the station-interior work was substantially
   reduced under budget and schedule pressure.

The table figure appears alongside the inventory fields; the dedicated page
describes the completed work and prize; the technical book describes a budget
limit and a later reduced scope. They are all published, not averaged or
selected. Confidence is High for each literal value and Medium for any attempt
to map the scopes together, because the sources do not do that mapping.

## Stated gaps

The checked primary inventories do not form one current, machine-readable
network register. DORTS splits its material between a general table, line
tables, station architecture pages and individual records; NTMC and TYMC use
operator pages with different fields. The catalogue is therefore complete for
the works named by the fetched sources, but it cannot claim that an unlisted
station has no public art.

To settle the remaining TBC fields, the project needs the underlying DORTS
commission files or current operator inventories for O12, the Daan Park works,
the Airport gaps, Danhai Blue Sea and the Ankeng/Sanying records. A signed
commission or acceptance record would also be needed to reconcile the three
Xiaobitan costs; none of the three checked sources supplies that reconciliation.
