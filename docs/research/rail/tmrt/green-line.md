# Corpus warning

`docs/research/` is a lead file, not an authority. Every claim below was checked against the cited primary page or committed TDX response before the content pages were written. Search results and the earlier expansion proposal were not used as sources.

**Checked:** 23 August 2026. **Subject:** Taichung Metro Corporation (TMRT), Green Line, and its 18 stations.

## What is established

### Live structured record

- TDX returned a current TMRT `Line` record, two `Route` records, 18 `Station` records, two `StationOfRoute` records, one `Shape`, two directional `S2STravelTime` records and first/last timetable data on 22 August 2026. **PRIMARY, High:** direct authenticated API pull, committed under `data/tdx/TMRT/`. The raw records identify the line as `G`, the operator as `TMRT`, the station sequence `G0, G3, G4, G5, G6, G7, G8, G8a, G9, G10, G10a, G11, G12, G13, G14, G15, G16, G17`, and the route name `烏日文心北屯線`.
- The TDX route record gives `RouteLength: 16.7`, `TravelTime: 71`, `StartStationID: G0` and `EndStationID: G17`. **PRIMARY, High:** direct `Route/TMRT` response, retrieved 22 August 2026. This is kept distinct from the operator's 16.71 km and from the two station-chainage totals.
- Direction 0's final station chainage is 16.93 km and direction 1's is 16.97 km. **PRIMARY, High:** direct `StationOfRoute/TMRT` response, retrieved 22 August 2026. The response's own original-language data is the numeric field `CumulativeDistance` on the final `Stations` item; it is not a measured value generated by this site.

### Operator and route

- Taichung Metro Corporation records the Green Line as an 18-station, 16.71 km route between Beitun Main Station and HSR Taichung Station. **PRIMARY, High:** [2021 operational route report](https://www.tmrt.com.tw/Content/Uploads/PublicClassArticle/9ad3a311-c8e4-4bb8-883a-d36d756eeeae.pdf), operator PDF, retrieved 23 August 2026. The report's original-language route description is the basis for the published 18-station and 16.71 km claims.
- The operator's current map gives the station order and the names used on the station pages. **PRIMARY, High:** [Metro map](https://www.tmrt.com.tw/metro-life/map), 臺中捷運股份有限公司, retrieved 23 August 2026.
- The operator's FAQ describes a steel-wheel, medium-capacity, fully driverless railway, with two-car trains and 536 passengers per train; it identifies G0 and G17 as ground-level and the other 16 stations as elevated. **PRIMARY, High:** [Frequently asked questions](https://www.tmrt.com.tw/our-services/faq), original-language technical answers, retrieved 23 August 2026.
- The operator's major-events page dates company establishment to 1 January 2017 and formal opening to 25 April 2021. **PRIMARY, High:** [Major events](https://www.tmrt.com.tw/about/major-events), `臺中捷運公司成立` and `正式通車`, retrieved 23 August 2026.

### Incidents and opening chronology

- The major-events page records a 2015 construction beam accident at the G4/G5 site with four deaths and four injuries, and separately records a joint-shaft fracture on 21 November 2020 during test operation. **PRIMARY, High:** [Major events](https://www.tmrt.com.tw/about/major-events), retrieved 23 August 2026.
- The Control Yuan investigation attributes the shaft failure to unverified design parameters and weaknesses in contract quality control and oversight. **PRIMARY, High:** [Investigation PDF](https://cybsbox.cy.gov.tw/CYBSBoxSSL/edoc/download/71448), 監察院, retrieved 23 August 2026. The original-language finding includes the unverified `壓縮、拉力、水平、垂直及翻滾角度` design checks and identifies Kawasaki's shaft component in the incident record.
- Taichung City Government records test operation restarting on 25 March 2021 after remedial work and formal service beginning on 25 April 2021. **PRIMARY, High:** [Taichung Metro resumes test operation](https://www.taichung.gov.tw/8868/8872/9962/1742706), retrieved 23 August 2026. MOTC's yearbook independently gives the same restart and opening interval. **PRIMARY, High:** [MOTC yearbook](https://www.motc.gov.tw/ch/app/yearbook/doc?detailNo=2&id=21&module=directory&serno=8961&type=s&year=110), retrieved 23 August 2026.
- TMRT's 21 March 2026 notice records a temporary signal-power interruption affecting G9–G17 and replacement of the relevant equipment. **PRIMARY, High:** [Signal-power notice](https://www.tmrt.com.tw/announcement/draft-news/detail?id=37f16f36-0823-497a-ac30-8eb80525951e), retrieved 23 August 2026.

Those fields remain TBC unless a station-specific primary source settles them.
