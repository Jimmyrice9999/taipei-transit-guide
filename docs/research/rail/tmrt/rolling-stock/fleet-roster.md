# Taichung Metro rolling stock — research findings, 28 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. `docs/research/rail/tmrt/green-line.md` already records the operator FAQ's train-configuration claims (claim 3) in brief; this fills the gap of exact car dimensions and the manufacturer, neither of which the existing lead file states.

This is a one-subject report on the Taichung Metro Green Line's rolling
stock: fleet size, car dimensions, capacity and manufacturer.
`content/rail/tmrt/` currently has no rolling-stock subdirectory.

## What is established

- **The Green Line runs 18 electric multiple units (EMUs), each a two-car set with a walk-through gangway between cars.** Source: Taichung Metro Corporation (臺中捷運股份有限公司), `常見問答` (Frequently Asked Questions), `https://www.tmrt.com.tw/our-services/faq`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched and read in full (raw HTML parsed directly). Original-language basis: `臺中捷運綠線電聯車共有18列，每列車由2節車廂組成，車廂間設有車間走道連通可供旅客通行`.
- **Each car is approximately 22.17 m long, 2.98 m wide and 3.78 m high; a full two-car train is approximately 44.34 m long; rated capacity is approximately 536 passengers per train (about 268 per car).** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `每節車廂之長度約22.17公尺、寬度約2.98公尺、高度約3.78公尺，列車總長度約44.34公尺，載客量約為536人（每節車廂約268人）`.
- **The system is a steel-wheel, medium-capacity, power-distributed EMU design: each car carries two bogies, and each bogie carries two wheelsets with a traction motor.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `臺中捷運屬鋼輪中運量系統，每列電聯車由兩節車廂組成，各配置兩組轉向架，而每組轉向架各配置2組車輪組及動力牽引馬達，以牽引列車移動`. A separate FAQ answer states the vehicle type is steel-rail steel-wheel with a stainless-steel car body. Original-language basis: `車輛型式為鋼軌鋼輪，車體材質為不銹鋼`.
- **The rolling stock (EMU subsystem) was supplied as part of the CJ900/CJ907 system-equipment contract, won by a three-firm joint venture: Kawasaki Heavy Industries (Japan), Alstom Transport (France) and China Engineering Corporation (中鼎工程, domestic).** Source: Taichung City Government Rapid Transit Systems Bureau (臺中市政府捷運工程局), `CJ900標/CJ907標` project record, `https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/form/Details?Parser=2%2C6%2C45%2C%2C%2C%2C31`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched and read in full (raw HTML parsed directly). Original-language basis: `得標廠商：由日商川崎重工業株式會社、法商阿爾斯通運股份有限公司及國內中鼎工程股份有限公司共同承攬`. The bureau's page names the consortium against the contract's whole six-subsystem scope (including EMU) and does not state which firm specifically built the cars. Kawasaki Heavy Industries is the consortium member with a well-known rolling-stock manufacturing business, but this page does not assert that Kawasaki specifically built the EMU fleet beyond what the primary source itself states — the primary source names the consortium, not a per-subsystem manufacturer split.
