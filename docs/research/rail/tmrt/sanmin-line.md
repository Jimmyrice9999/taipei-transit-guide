## What is established

### The requested name is not verified as a route record

Its planning list contains 民生汐止線 and 社子線; its construction list contains the Xinyi east extension, Wanda–Zhonghe–Shulin Phase 1 and Phase 2, Circular north/south, and Circular east. No route link or route heading named 三民線 appeared in that fetched page. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** the current network page was read in full. Original-language basis: `規劃中路網` with `民生汐止線` and `社子線`, and `興建中路網` with `信義線東延段`, `萬大中和樹林一期`, `萬大中和樹林二期`, `環狀線北環及南環` and `環狀線東環段`. [dorts-network]

No milestone in the fetched chronology identifies a route as 三民線. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full chronology page, not a search snippet. Original-language basis: `五泰輕軌`, `八里輕軌`, `深坑輕軌`, `三鶯線`, `環狀線`, `萬大線` and `汐止民生線`. [ntpc-chronology]

### The nearest likely name collision is Sanying, not Sanmin

- The official New Taipei project page names the operating route 三鶯線, not 三民線. It gives 14.29 km, 12 stations and 30 June 2026 as the opening date. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full current route page. Original-language basis: `三鶯線 115年6月30日通車營運`, `14.29公里` and `12座`. [ntpc-sanying]
- The same Sanying page describes a route from Dingpu through Tucheng, Sanxia and Yingge to Fengming, and says the scheme preserves a future extension to Bade and a transfer to the Taoyuan Green Line. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full route introduction. Original-language basis: `起於土城線頂埔站` and `保留未來延伸至桃園八德地區，與桃園綠線銜接轉乘`. [ntpc-sanying]
- Sanying is therefore a distinct existing route record, not a safe expansion of the requested English word “Sanmin.” The site already has a Sanying Line entry; this subject is not used to duplicate or rename it. **PRIMARY for the distinction:** the official Sanying title and route record. [ntpc-sanying]

### Minsheng–Xizhi is a separate named project

- The Taipei network page calls the planned route 民生汐止線, while the New Taipei chronology uses 汐止民生線 for its historical milestones. Neither full-page record calls it 三民線. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full pages from both bureaus. Original-language basis: `民生汐止線` and `汐止民生線`. [dorts-network][ntpc-chronology]
- The requested Sanmin label cannot be used as an alias for Minsheng–Xizhi without a source that makes that equivalence. No such source was found in this pass. **PRIMARY for the checked-source boundary:** the full network and chronology pages. [dorts-network][ntpc-chronology]
