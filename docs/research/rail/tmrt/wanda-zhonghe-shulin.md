## What is established

### Corridor and phase structure

- Phase 1 runs from Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall through the Taipei and New Taipei corridors to Juguang, with a 9.5 km route including an approximately 700 m depot branch, nine underground stations and one depot. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full New Taipei Phase 1 route page. Original-language basis: `全長約9.5公里（含機廠支線約700公尺），共設9座地下車站及1座機廠`.
- The New Taipei part of Phase 1 is 5.7 km with LG05, LG06, LG07, LG08 and LG08A, plus the depot. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full New Taipei Phase 1 route page. Original-language basis: `在新北市段共5.7公里，設有LG05、LG06、LG07、LG08及LG08A共5座地下車站及1座機廠`.
- The station-list page names the nine planning-stage station codes LG01 through LG08A, including the interchange at LG06 Zhonghe with the Circular Line, and warns that the codes may change under the operating company’s numbering rules. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full New Taipei station-list page. Original-language basis: `車站編號係規劃階段使用之代號，未來通車營運後將調整為捷運公司使用之編號原則編列`.
- Phase 2 continues from the Phase 1 route at Jincheng Road, rises near Mingde Road, crosses Chenglin Bridge and the Dahan River, continues through Shulin and returns along Zhongzheng Road to the Xinzhuang/Huilong connection. It is 13.3 km with two underground and 11 elevated stations. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full New Taipei Phase 2 route page. Original-language basis: `由第一期興建路段續沿金城路，並於過明德路口附近路線爬昇為高架後，續沿金城路並跨越城林橋至樹林中華路、八德街、大安路轉中正路直行與捷運新莊線迴龍站相接` and `全長13.3公里，共設2座地下車站，11座高架車站`.

### Approvals and programme record

- The Phase 1 route page records environmental-review agreement on 6 August 2008, Executive Yuan approval of the comprehensive plan on 12 February 2010, and a second plan revision approved on 29 November 2018 that moved the target to the end of ROC year 116 and set the cost at NT$74.178 billion. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full New Taipei Phase 1 route page. Original-language basis: `97年8月6日環境影響說明書定稿本獲行政院環保署同意備查`, `99年2月12日行政院核定綜合規劃報告書暨周邊土地開發計畫` and `107年11月29日行政院核定...修正期程至116年、經費修正為741.78億元`.
- The same page records a third Phase 1 plan revision submitted to the Ministry of Transportation in 2023–2024 and sent to the Executive Yuan for review on 18 May 2026. It gives a revised total of NT$84.801 billion, a completion adjustment to December of ROC year 116, and a programme period extending to December of ROC year 118. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full New Taipei Phase 1 route page. Original-language basis: `交通部於115年5月18日報行政院審查` and `總經費修正為848.01億元...計畫完工調整至116年12月，計畫期程調整至118年12月`.
- The Phase 2 record says its financial plan was approved on 17 October 2018 and its first revision on 21 October 2025. It lists urban-plan changes, completed DQ125 and DQ126 detailed-design work, and construction packages CQ880A, CQ880B and CQ890. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full New Taipei Phase 2 route page. Original-language basis: `第1次修正計畫114年10月21日奉行政院核定` and the `DQ125`/`DQ126` and `CQ880`/`CQ890` package entries.

### Construction packages and engineering

- The New Taipei government Phase 1 record says all four civil packages were under construction: CQ850 in Yonghe, CQ861 at the LG06–Y11 underground connection, CQ860 between LG06 and LG08 and along Tucheng’s Jincheng Road, and CQ870 for the Jincheng depot and LG08A. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full New Taipei government Phase 1 project record. Original-language basis: `各土建工程均已全面展開施作中` and the four package descriptions.
- The Phase 2 progress page labels CQ880B, CQ880A and CQ890 as construction projects. It gives CQ880B a 28 October 2025 award date, a NT$15.99 billion contract, and a 29 December 2025 start; CQ880A a 1 December 2023 award, a NT$11,597,832,043 contract and a 1 March 2024 start; and CQ890 a 17 August 2022 award, a NT$18,000,358,242 contract and an 11 November 2022 start. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full New Taipei Phase 2 progress page. Original-language basis: the three `施工中` package blocks and their `決標日期`, `契約金額` and `開工日期` fields.
- The Taipei package page divides Phase 2 into CQ880A, CQ880B and CQ890. It describes CQ880A as 2.84 km with two underground stations, LG09 and LG10; CQ880B as about 3.82 km with elevated LG11–LG13 and the Dahan River crossing; and CQ890 as about 6.6 km with eight elevated stations. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full Taipei DORTS Phase 2 package record. Original-language basis: `路線總長度約為2.84公里`, `設置LG11站...LG12站...LG13站3座高架車站` and `CQ890區段標之路線長約6.6公里...八座高架車站`.
- The same package page records technical challenges including utility relocation, crossing above operating TRA, passing below operating HSR, stacked elevated construction on Zhonghua Road, and co-construction of the Wanda Line and Taoyuan Brown Line elevated structures. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full Taipei DORTS Phase 2 package record. Original-language basis: `重大管線遷移、跨越營運中之臺鐵上方、穿越營運中之高鐵下方、中華路疊式高架橋施工、萬大線與桃園棕線雙層高架橋共構施工`.

### Public disagreement and status boundaries

The bureau says it selected the current location after considering alignment, roads, land, station spacing, interchange, service area, development and residential and commercial activity. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full Taipei DORTS LG11 Q&A. Original-language basis: `使用公園及停車場用地`、`軌道與民宅與學校距離` and `為最佳選擇方案`.

The New Taipei government project record separately says the four Phase 1 civil packages were under construction and lists active work examples; these are retained as different dated/status measures, not reconciled. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full Phase 1 progress page and full New Taipei government project record. Original-language basis: `本府管制計畫進度為0%` and `各土建工程均已全面展開施作中`.
- The New Taipei Phase 2 progress page reports 0% for its controlled programme through the end of ROC year 115 July while listing three package-level projects as under construction. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full Phase 2 progress page. Original-language basis: `本府管制計畫進度為0%` and the three `施工中` blocks.
