## What is established

### Planning boundary and purpose

- The current project page says no route has yet been planned, its location diagram is for reference, and the future result will follow the approved plan. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full page. Original-language basis: `目前尚未規劃路線，區位圖供參考，未來以計畫核定結果為準`. [tcrt-orange-sea]
- The same page describes an initial concept linking Taichung International Airport and Orange Line `O01` westward to Taichung Port, with intended connections to Aofeng Mountain, Ziyunyan, the Harbor Art Center, the Blue Line and TRA QingShui Station. It says the feasibility study is evaluating route, station locations and system type. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full page. Original-language basis: `橘線延伸海線地區初步規劃自臺中國際機場與橘線O01站銜接，往西延伸至臺中港` and `進一步針對相關路線、車站位置及系統型式等進行詳細規劃及評估`. [tcrt-orange-sea]
- The January 2026 release says the study covers route alternatives, stations, demand, engineering, environmental impact and financial feasibility, integrating coastal development and existing public transport. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full release. Original-language basis: `此次可行性研究內容涵蓋路線方案、車站設置、運量評估、工程技術、環境影響及財務可行性等面向`. [tcrt-orange-sea-jan]

### Interim route proposal

- The January release reports an interim proposal of approximately 16.50 km, 10 stations and one level-five depot. It divides the scheme into a main section, a Sanmin Road branch and a Wuqi Fishing Port branch, running from the airport through QingShui, Shalu and Wuqi and connecting the Blue Line and TRA QingShui Station. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full release. Original-language basis: `橘線全長約16.50公里，設置10座車站及1座五級機廠` and `主線段、三民路支線段及梧棲漁港支線段`. [tcrt-orange-sea-jan]
- The full public-meeting deck labels the interim stations `OE1`-`OE7`, `OE10`-`OE12`; it shows `OE1`-`OE3` as elevated, `OE4`-`OE7` at grade, `OE10` as the elevated Sanmin Road branch, and `OE11`-`OE12` as the at-grade Wuqi Fishing Port branch. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full 13-page PDF, page 5. Original-language basis: `主線：OE1~OE3(高架)、OE4~OE7(平面)`. [tcrt-orange-sea-deck]
- The same deck lists intended connections with the airport Orange Line station, the Blue Line and TRA QingShui Station, and illustrates the Harbor Art Center, Taichung Port Passenger Service Center, Taichung Port, Taichung Marine Museum, Aofeng Mountain and Ziyunyan. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full PDF, pages 5-8. Original-language basis: `與機場捷運交會`, `與藍線交會`, `與臺鐵清水站交會`. [tcrt-orange-sea-deck]

### Forecast and system type

- The January deck gives modelled full-day ridership of 58,363 passengers and a route ridership density of 3,507 passengers per kilometre for ROC 140. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full PDF, pages 5-6. Original-language basis: `全日運量58,363人次/日` and `運量密度3,507人次/公里`. [tcrt-orange-sea-deck]
- The deck places the 3,507 result beside reference thresholds of 6,000 for medium-capacity metro and 3,500 for light rail, and says the assessment meets the light-rail reference value. It also says the final route must follow the centrally approved version. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full PDF, pages 6 and 11. Original-language basis: `本案運量評估符合輕軌系統建議參考值` and `最終路線方案需以中央核定版本為準`. [tcrt-orange-sea-deck]

### Funding and review chronology

- The current page estimates the feasibility-study cost at NT$20 million, records MOTC support of NT$11.7 million approved on 24 January 2025 and a local matching share of NT$8.3 million, and records tender publication on 9 April 2025 and award on 9 June 2025. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full current page. Original-language basis: `總經費預估為2,000萬元`, `交通部114年1月24日核定補助1,170萬元，本府地方配合款830萬元`. [tcrt-orange-sea]
- The work plan was approved on 21 July 2025, an interim report was submitted on 17 October, a review meeting was held on 24 November, a revised report was submitted on 23 December, a local briefing was held on 15 January 2026, and a further revised interim report was submitted on 20 March. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full current chronology. Original-language basis: `本案工作執行計畫書已於114年7月21日核備` through `廠商已於115年3月20再次提送修正期中報告`. [tcrt-orange-sea]
- The current page adds that the bureau returned the report for correction on 9 June 2026, the consultant submitted a revised report on 6 July, and the bureau was reviewing it. The network page likewise places the extension in the feasibility-study group with its interim revised report under review. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full current pages. Original-language basis: `本局再次於115年6月9日退請廠商依意見辦理修正，廠商於115年7月6日提送修正報告，本局刻正審查中` and `可行性研究期中修正報告審查作業中`. [tcrt-orange-sea] [tcrt-network]

### Public objections and unresolved decisions

- The full 17-page January meeting record documents requests for a medium-capacity system or the same system as the Orange and Blue lines, concerns about mixed traffic and heavy trucks, questions about the `OE10`/`B05` transfer, and requests to move stations toward QingShui's population centres. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full official minutes. Original-language basis: `建議本案運量評估...中運量系統`, `強烈反對以輕軌型式` and `OE10站與臺中捷運藍線B05站銜接方式`. [tcrt-orange-sea-minutes]
- The official three-page meeting-summary PDF repeats the airport, Blue Line, TRA and Wuqi Fishing Port connections and the interim 16.50 km/10-station scope. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full official summary. Original-language basis: `整體路線全長16.50公里，共設10座車站`. [tcrt-orange-sea-summary]
