## What is established

### Purpose and preliminary alignment

- The bureau describes the Purple Line as a planned transport project for the Dali, Wufeng and Taiping areas, where population, industry and travel demand have grown. It says the line is intended to connect the main transport corridors of the Tun District, support commuting and school trips, tourism and economic development, and wider regional balance. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full current route page, read in full. Original-language basis: `大里、霧峰及太平地區近年人口及產業快速成長` and the page's `計畫緣起`. [tcrt-purple](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1%2C6%2C142)
- The preliminary route plan starts at TMRT Green Line Daqing Station, runs through Dali and Taiping, then turns toward Beitun to connect with Green Line extension station G1 and form a loop network. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** the current route page. Original-language basis: `路線方案規劃自捷運綠線大慶站行經大里、太平再轉至北屯銜接綠線延伸線G1站形成環狀路網`. [tcrt-purple](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1%2C6%2C142)
- A January 2026 official release gives more route context: the northern end connects to Green Line extension G1, the route passes through Beitun, Taiping and Dali, and the southern end connects with TRA and the Green Line at Daqing. It also describes planned transfers to the Blue Line Taiping extension and Airport MRT Orange Line. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** official release read in full. Original-language basis: `北起銜接捷運綠線延伸G1站` and the route/interchange paragraph. [tcrt-purple-jan](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/latestevent/Details?Parser=9%2C4%2C160%2C%2C%2C%2C1314)

### Feasibility-study chronology

- The project page records local explanation meetings in October 2021, an initial submission to the Transport Ministry on 20 June 2022, written ministry comments on 21 September 2022, a revised report on 1 May 2023, and further written comments on 30 May 2023. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** the page's complete dated table. Original-language basis: the rows beginning `110.10.20、10.24`, `111.06.20`, `111.09.21`, `112.05.01` and `112.05.30`. [tcrt-purple](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1%2C6%2C142)
- On 26 March 2024 the city split the former combined Tun District feasibility work into the Purple Line and the Blue Line Taiping extension, then submitted both together. The Railway Bureau held a site inspection on 15 May 2024 and an initial review on 20 June 2024. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full chronology table. Original-language basis: `依據交通部意見拆分為「屯區捷運環狀線」及「捷運藍線延伸太平」2計畫後，一併提送`, `113.05.15` and `113.06.20`. [tcrt-purple](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1%2C6%2C142)
- The city submitted revised reports on 9 January 2025, 4 July 2025 and 7 January 2026 after ministry comments on 11 April 2025 and 22 October 2025. The current page records another Railway Bureau response on 27 July 2026 and says the city is revising the report before resubmission. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** current status paragraph and chronology table. Original-language basis: `115.07.27 | 交通部函復書面意見` and `市府刻依審查意見修正報告內容，俟修正完成後重新提送交通部審查`. [tcrt-purple](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1%2C6%2C142)
- The January 2026 release says the city had completed a revised feasibility report after the Railway Bureau's 22 October 2025 comments and had resubmitted it for central review. The July 2026 release likewise says the study was resubmitted in January and was under review. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** two dated releases, retained as a sequence. Original-language basis: `完成捷運紫線可行性研究修正報告` and `今年1月再次提送交通部審議，目前正由中央相關部會審查中`. [tcrt-purple-jan](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/latestevent/Details?Parser=9%2C4%2C160%2C%2C%2C%2C1314) [tcrt-purple-july](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/latestevent/Details?Parser=9%2C4%2C160%2C%2C%2C%2C1549)
