## What is established

### Corridor and published scope

- The Taichung Rapid Transit Systems Bureau describes the Orange Line as a planned connection between Taichung International Airport, the city and the Tun District, serving major corridors and planned airport, Shuinan and redevelopment projects. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full current project page, read in full. Original-language basis: `機場捷運(橘線)是聯繫臺中國際機場與市區、屯區的重要交通建設` and the following purpose paragraph. [tcrt-orange](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1%2C6%2C99)
- The current route page places the origin at Taichung International Airport, passes through Daya, the Shuinan Economic and Trade Park, Yizhong, Taichung Station, National Chung Hsing University and the Taichung Software Park, and ends near the Legislative Yuan's Wufeng site. It gives approximately 29.23 km, 15 elevated stations, 11 underground stations and 26 stations in total. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full current project page. Original-language basis: `目前機場捷運(橘線)規劃起點設於臺中國際機場` and `路線全長29.23公里，規劃高架車站15座、地下車站11座，共設有26座車站`. [tcrt-orange](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1%2C6%2C99)
- A 5 August 2026 bureau release gives the same 29.23 km and 26-station scope, describes the route as airport-to-Wufeng service through Daya, Xitun, Beitun, North, East, South, Dali and Wufeng, and gives an estimated total cost of NT$1,602 hundred-million, approximately NT$160.2 billion. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full official release, read in full. Original-language basis: `捷運橘線全長29.23公里，規劃設置26座車站` and `總經費約1,602億元`. [tcrt-orange-aug](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/latestevent/Details?Parser=9%2C4%2C160%2C%2C%2C%2C1597)

Original-language basis: the rows from `109.06.09/10/11` through `113.07.02`. [tcrt-orange](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1%2C6%2C99)
- The same chronology records the July 2025 transfer to Executive Yuan review, requests for supplemental public-private-participation evaluation, the September and November 2025 participation-platform steps, a February 2026 re-submission and Executive Yuan feasibility approval on 18 August 2026. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** current page's later chronology rows. Original-language basis: `114.07.04 | 交通部核轉行政院審議`, `114.11.26 | 促參平台函知本案符合免送促參審議條件`, `115.02.02 | 交通部再度核轉行政院審議` and `115.08.18 | 行政院核定可行性研究`. [tcrt-orange](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1%2C6%2C99)
- The current whole-network page classifies the Orange Line feasibility study among Taichung's recently advancing routes and separately lists the Orange Line's Sea Line extension among the medium-term feasibility projects. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full current network-status page, read in full. Original-language basis: `機場捷運（橘線）可行性研究已於115年8月18日獲行政院核定` and the `中期路網` list. [tcrt-network](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1%2C6%2C44)
- The bureau began preparatory work before the feasibility approval: its 25 May 2026 release describes public reading for the comprehensive plan, environmental assessment, land-use conversion and basic-design technical-service procurement. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full public release. Original-language basis: `提前辦理「台中機場捷運（橘線）綜合規劃、環境影響評估等相關工作及基本設計委託技術服務」招標前置作業` and the public-reading dates. Original-language basis: `綜合規劃、環境影響評估等相關工作及基本設計委託技術服務` and `公開閱覽意見回覆`. [tcrt-orange-procurement](https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/latestevent/Details?Parser=9%2C4%2C31%2C161%2C%2C%2C1563)
