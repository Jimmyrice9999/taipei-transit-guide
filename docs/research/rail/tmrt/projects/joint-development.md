# Taichung Metro land development (TOD) — research findings, 28 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. `content/rail/projects/tod-joint-development.md` covers Taipei/New Taipei; `docs/research/rail/krtc/projects/joint-development.md` and `docs/research/rail/tymc/projects/joint-development.md` (this session, same date) cover Kaohsiung's and Taoyuan's equivalent mechanisms, both of which found the mechanism but not a case list. This report, the third of the set, found both.

This is a one-subject report on the legal basis, mechanism and concrete
Green Line station sites behind Taichung's own metro land-development
programme, published by the Taichung City Rapid Transit Systems Bureau
(TCRT, 臺中市政府捷運工程局). `content/rail/tmrt/projects/` does not yet
exist. Unlike this project's parallel KRTC and TYMC land-development
reports, TCRT's site yielded full, working content on every page fetched
this session, including a genuine case list with named investors, site
figures and timelines for seven Green Line stations.

## What is established

- **The bureau states the purpose of metro land development is to effectively use land resources and promote regional development, empowering the competent authority to develop MRT route, depot and station land plus adjacent land.** Source: Taichung City Rapid Transit Systems Bureau (臺中市政府捷運工程局), `簡介與作業程序` (Introduction and Operating Procedures), `https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1,7,47`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched and read in full (raw HTML parsed directly), page dated 17 July 2025. Original-language basis: `為有效利用土地資源，促進地區發展，主管機關得辦理大眾捷運系統路線、場、站土地及其毗鄰地區土地之開發`.
- **The bureau describes the mechanism as similar to private joint construction (合建): the competent authority (Taichung City Government) amends the urban plan to designate a "metro development zone," landowners within it provide land, and the authority solicits an investor through a formal process to fund construction of a metro land-development building — after completion, the landowner, the authority and the investor each receive a share of the building's real estate. The same mechanism can be used to acquire land the metro itself needs, for depots, stations or entrances.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `捷運土地開發與民間的合建類似...主管機關（臺中市政府）透過變更都市計畫程序，由劃定為「捷運開發區」範圍內之土地所有權人提供土地...完工後，權利關係人（土地所有權人、主管機關、投資人）則可分回捷運土地開發大樓不動產`.
- **The bureau names three reasons for the programme: protecting landowners' rights by offering an alternative to expropriation compensation; making effective use of land that would otherwise sit idle or under-used; and reducing the government's fiscal burden by using development returns to fund construction and operating costs.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `維護地主權益...有效利用土地資源...減輕政府財政負擔：樽節政府用地取得經費，藉由開發效益回饋挹注捷運建設財源及營運成本`.
- **A landowner inside a designated metro development zone, once a negotiated-purchase price is agreed, can choose either to forgo the cash payment and instead receive space in the completed development building, or to take the cash payment while keeping a priority right to later buy or lease space in the building.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `私有土地所有權人可選擇不領取協議價購土地款參與土地開發，於開發完成後以開發後之土地開發大樓抵付協議價購土地款，或選擇領取協議價購土地款，但保留後續優先承購、承租土地開發大樓之權利`.
- **If no qualified investor can be found in time, the city government may build the shared structural space itself first to avoid delaying the wider line's opening, then solicit an investor later to design and build the remaining undeveloped floor area.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `如未能徵求合乎資格的優良投資人，為避免影響整體通車期程，得由本府先行興建共構空間，並於通車使用後再徵求投資人進場設計興建未完成開發之容積`.
- **The bureau's legal-basis page names four general/central laws (Mass Rapid Transit Act, Mass Rapid Transit System Land Development Regulations, Urban Planning Act, Building Act) and nine Taichung-specific self-government ordinances and administrative rules covering implementation, negotiated-purchase pricing, investor financial-capacity criteria, cost-sharing for shared construction, evaluation-committee organisation, rights-allocation procedure for the Green Line specifically, a rights-allocation review-committee charter, public-property lease/sale procedure, and original-landowner priority-purchase procedure for the Green Line specifically — plus several named contract and manual templates.** Source: Taichung City Rapid Transit Systems Bureau, `相關法令規章` (Related Laws and Regulations), `https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1,7,48`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched and read in full, page dated 26 August 2026.
- **The bureau publishes a concrete case list for seven Green Line development sites (G5, G6, G8, G8a, G9-1, G9-2, G11), each with site area, building coverage ratio, floor area ratio, named investor(s), planned building use, current execution stage and a dated procedural timeline.** Source: Taichung City Rapid Transit Systems Bureau, `綠線土開場站簡介` (Green Line Land-Development Station Introduction), `https://tcrt.taichung.gov.tw/content/index?Parser=1,7,49`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched and read in full, page dated 12 December 2025. The seven sites, in the bureau's own words:
  - **G5 四維國小 (Siwei Elementary)**: 1,955 m², 70% coverage, 500% floor-area ratio, investor 冠德建設股份有限公司 (Kuo Der Construction), planned as office and retail space. Stage: building permit obtained, under construction. Timeline: announcement closed April 2021, evaluation July 2021, best-applicant announced July 2021, investment contract signed September 2021, construction start reported July 2023.
  - **G6 文心崇德 (Wenxin Chongde)**: 2,315 m², 70%/500%, investors 集順生活科技股份有限公司 and 日勝生活科技股份有限公司 jointly, planned as residential plus a post office and retail space. Stage: building permit obtained, under construction. Timeline: announcement closed August 2020, evaluation October 2020, best-applicant announced October 2020, contract signed December 2020, construction start reported June 2025.
  - **G8 文華高中 (Wenhua Senior High School)**: 2,228 m², 70%/500%, investors 冠德建設股份有限公司 and 世義貿易股份有限公司, planned as residential and retail space, developed by a single original landowner applying as priority investor. Stage: complete, marketed for sale/lease. Full lifecycle timeline: contract signed November 2014, construction start reported July 2017, completion and handover November 2021 — the only one of the seven with a complete build-to-handover record.
  - **G8a 文心櫻花 (Wenxin Cherry Blossom)**: 1,654 m², 70%/500%, investors 集順生活科技股份有限公司 and 日勝生活科技股份有限公司 jointly, planned as residential. Stage: applying for a building permit. Timeline: announcement closed March 2020, evaluation October 2020, best-applicant announced December 2020, contract signed December 2020.
  - **G9-1 市政府 (City Hall), north site**: 6,787.9 m², 70% coverage, 650% floor-area ratio, investors 冠德建設股份有限公司 and 中鹿開發股份有限公司, planned as retail, office and hotel space; the site already reserves a Blue Line entrance and transfer passage. Stage: applying for a building permit. Timeline: announcement closed June 2022, evaluation December 2022, best-applicant announced December 2022, contract signed April 2023.
  - **G9-2 市政府 (City Hall), south site**: 5,328 m², 70%/650%. Stage: still in pre-solicitation — no investor secured; the bureau states it continues gathering potential investors' input and periodically reviews solicitation terms, planning to relaunch the tender when conditions are appropriate.
  - **G11 南屯 (Nantun)**: 2,549 m², 70%/500%, investors 集順生活科技股份有限公司 and 日勝生活科技股份有限公司 jointly, planned as residential and retail space. Stage: applying for a building permit. Timeline: announcement closed April 2021, evaluation July 2021, best-applicant announced August 2021, contract signed September 2021.
- **A Taichung City Government public notice, dated 20 September 2011 (民國100年9月20日) and cited to Article 7 of the Mass Rapid Transit System Land Development Regulations, formally announced the development content and control regulations for the same seven station sites (G5, G6, G8, G8a, G9-1, G9-2, G11), posted for a 30-day public-comment period from 21 September to 20 October 2011.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `公告「臺中都會區大眾捷運系統烏日文心北屯線捷運系統用地捷G5、捷G6、捷G8、捷G8a、捷G9-1、捷9-2、捷G11用地開發內容及管制規定」...依據：大眾捷運系統土地開發辦法第7條`.

## Conflicts

None found — no second source addressing this case list or mechanism was
located this session. This project's separate KRTC and TYMC
land-development research each found the same central legal basis (Mass
Rapid Transit Act, MRT System Land Development Regulations) that Taichung
also cites, consistent across all three cities rather than conflicting.
