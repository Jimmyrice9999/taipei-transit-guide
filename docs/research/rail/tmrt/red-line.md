## What is established

### Corridor and preliminary network role

- The current bureau page says the Chongde–Fengyuan corridor was identified in the Ministry of Transportation-reviewed Taichung metropolitan rail-network assessment as engineering-feasible and above the demand threshold for a metro project. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full current project page. Original-language basis: `崇德豐原路廊初步評估具工程可行性，大眾運輸需求量亦滿足興建捷運系統之門檻`.
- The earlier planned alignment began at the Chongde Road–Sanmin Road intersection, followed Chongde Road, Chengde Road, Fengyuan Boulevard and Zhongzheng Road, and ended near the Fengyuan Boulevard–Zhongzheng Road intersection. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full current project page. Original-language basis: `原規劃自崇德路與三民路口起，沿崇德路、承德路、豐原大道一段及中正路等主要道路，止於豐原大道與中正路口附近`.
- The feasibility study brought the TRA Fengyuan Station connection forward from the Fengke axis, added two stations and preserved future integration flexibility. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full current project page. Original-language basis: `故紅線目前規劃延伸至豐原車站，將新增2座車站，合計設置11座車站，並保留與豐科軸線銜接整合的路網彈性`.
- The Fengyuan presentation identifies planned relationships with the Orange Line, Green Line, Fengke axis and TRA, and labels a provisional sequence R1–R11 with R3 at Wenxin Chongde and R11 at TRA Fengyuan Station. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full 15-page presentation PDF, pages 7–8. Original-language basis: `銜接路線：橘線 綠線 豐科軸線 臺鐵`, `R3 綠線文心崇德站` and `R11 臺鐵豐原站`.

### Scope and forecasts

- The preliminary scope is approximately 15.42 km with 11 stations and one level-five depot. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** current 18 December release, 24 December release and Fengyuan presentation. Original-language basis: `全長約15.42公里，規劃設置11座車站`, `設置11座車站及1座五級機廠` and the presentation's `15.42 11 1` table.
- The 18 December release gives a target-year service population of approximately 220,000 within 500 metres and a peak headway of 2.5 minutes. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full official release. Original-language basis: `主線500公尺範圍內服務人口約22萬人，尖峰時段班距可達2.5分鐘`.
- The 24 December release gives a target-year all-day forecast of approximately 108,000 passenger trips and a peak-hour forecast of approximately 16,000. The presentation gives 108,310 all-day, 16,910 peak-hour and 5,870 maximum interstation-load passengers. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full official release and full presentation. Original-language basis: `全日運量約10萬8千人次，尖峰小時運量約1萬6千人次` and the presentation table `108,310`, `16,910`, `5,870`.

### Fengyuan urban-section alternatives

- The Fengyuan presentation compares three urban-section alternatives toward TRA Fengyuan Station: two elevated options and one underground Zhongzheng Road option. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full 15-page presentation PDF, page 9. Original-language basis: `方案一：高架（圓環北路）`, `方案二：高架（圓環南路）` and `方案三：地下（中正路）`.
- The presentation describes the north-ring-road elevated option as approximately 600 m from the Fengyuan commercial area, less disruptive at the Huludun Park section and the lowest-cost option; it describes the south-ring-road elevated option as approximately 900 m away and more winding; it describes the underground Zhongzheng Road option as directly serving the commercial area but difficult and most expensive. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** same presentation, page 9. Original-language basis: `離豐原商圈約600m`, `葫蘆墩公園段不影響市區道路`, `工程經費最低`, `離豐原商圈約900m`, `路線最彎繞`, `地下工程經費最高`.
- The 24 December release says the three alternatives remain under evaluation and that the final route is subject to central approval. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full official release. Original-language basis: `最終路線方案將以中央核定結果為準`.
- Public records show requests for more transparent elevated/underground comparison, more station coverage, space for a future Houli extension, attention to Fengyuan station traffic and possible links to Orange Line, Green Line and event destinations. **PRIMARY, High confidence:** full Fengyuan and Beitun meeting records. Original-language basis: `建議於工程規劃階段預留銜接后里之彈性與空間`, `建議在中間增設一站`, `豐原火車站附近會設站` and the requests to explain the underground alternative and road-space effects.
