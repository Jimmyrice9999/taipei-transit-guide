### 2.1 Route length: 26.65 / 26.6 / 28.2 km

| Value | Source | Kind | What it appears to measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **26.65 km** | TDX `station-of-route`, route BL-1, cumulative distance BL01→BL23 | primary | Distance along the revenue route between the two end stations |

| **28.2 km** | zh.wikipedia, 板南線 infobox (`linelength_km`) | secondary | Unstated |
| **28.2 km** | Sum of the five DORTS section lengths (11 + 7.1 + 5.6 + 2.5 + 2) | primary components, this file's arithmetic | Constructed project lengths |

The gap is **1.55 km**. This has the same shape as the Wenhu Line case the

end stations against a set of construction project lengths, which normally run
past the end stations into depot leads, tail tracks and overlaps at the joints
between contracts. **That reading is a hypothesis, not a source** — the 板橋線
page describes its length from 西門 (a station shared with 南港線), which is one
place double-counting could occur, and nothing found states how DORTS bounds a
section. Publish all four values with what each says of itself.

### 2.3 "The busiest line" is asserted but not published

zh.wikipedia says 「也是目前全路網運量最大的路線」. In the raw wikitext this sits
in the lead with no footnote of its own.

TRTC's own published series does not settle it. 各年度暨前一月旅運量統計資料
(<https://www.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=FED7CC0F31E0A664>, primary, read
2026-08-10) reports two categories only — 文湖線 and 高運量 — under the note
「高運量包含淡水信義、松山新店、中和新蘆、板南線」. The Bannan Line is inside an
aggregate of four.

What TRTC *does* publish (旅運量 index,
<https://www.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=FF31501BEBDD0136>, primary): monthly
各站進出量統計 as ODS, one file per month back to 民國85年, at
`https://web.metro.taipei/RidershipPerStation/YYYYMM_cht.ods`; and 全系統旅運量統計
as HTML at `https://web.metro.taipei/RidershipCounts/c/YYMM.htm`. The June 2026
(115年6月) system file gives 總乘客數 **62,471,614** and 平均每日 **2,082,387**
— **whole network, not this line**.

So a line total is *derivable* by summing the per-station ODS over BL01–BL23,
with the obvious double-counting problem at the five interchanges. Nobody has
done it here. Until someone does, the line's ridership is **TBC** on the content
page and the "busiest" claim is attributed to zh.wikipedia or left off.

### 2.4 Inter-station distances

TDX gives 3.08 km for BL09→BL10 and 0.65 km for BL06→BL07. Corpus v1.0 gave
2,470 m and 557 m, attributed to NAVITIME. Same two pairs; different numbers,
by 610 m and 93 m. NAVITIME was not re-fetched in this session, so what it is
measuring is unknown — plausibly a straight-line or a timetable-derived
distance rather than track distance. See *Checked and failed*, below.

### 2.5 What DORTS means by 南港線

The 南港線 page gives 起訖 as 「西門站至南港」 with 約11公里 and 11座車站. Eleven
stations from BL11 西門 counts to BL21 昆陽, not to BL22 南港 — and 昆陽–南港 is
the separately-documented 東延段. The page's own openings (市政府–西門,
市政府–昆陽) also stop at 昆陽. So either 「至南港」 names the planned terminus
rather than the built extent, or the page is loose. **Not resolved here.** It
matters, because the 11 km is one of the five numbers in §2.1.

- **The sub-page fetch's Gregorian year conversions** — the fetch of the DORTS

  fetch summary of a Taiwanese government page, distrust it and find the 民國
  string.

## Stated gaps

- **Ridership for this line.** Not published by TRTC as such (§2.3). Derivable
  from the per-station ODS series; not derived here.
- **Archive snapshots.** None of the sixteen sources used has been captured to
  the Wayback Machine in this session. Six of them are government CMS URLs whose
  node ids carry no meaning — exactly the rot case the `snapshot` field exists
  for. Run `npm run archive` before this page is old.
- **The 2014 attack.** Recorded here as a dated fact only. What changed
  operationally afterwards — staffing, patrols, emergency procedure — is not
  established, and a transport reference should carry the consequences rather
  than the event. Not attempted in this session.
- **C341 contract number, the 321型 unit price, and any audit or Control Yuan
  record.** §1.10.
- **The 14 December 1989 Taipei City Council intervention** on the Nangang
  alignment, carried in v1.0 with the outcome unknown. Nothing found on the
  DORTS pages fetched mentions it.
- **The 2023 Zhangshuwan extension proposal and its rejection**, carried in
  v1.0. Not verified; no source located without search.
- **Platform doors across the line (September 2018)**, carried in v1.0. Not
  verified.
- **The 2020-02-10 power cable faults**, carried in v1.0. Not verified.
- **Fleet allocation between the two depots.** zh.wikipedia says both C321 and
  C341 are 配置土城機廠、南港機廠; no split is given anywhere found.
- **Whether the C321 and C341 both run six-car.** The 341型 article gives

  content page attributes six cars to the C341 only.
