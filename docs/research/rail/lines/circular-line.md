## Why this line matters

- **The network's only orbital line** — connects the radial lines to each other
  rather than to the centre
- **Driverless medium-capacity**, Taiwan's second such system after Wenhu
- **Severely damaged by the 3 April 2024 earthquake**, and the cause turned out
  to be a construction defect, not seismic design. The Control Yuan investigated
  and reported.
- Still only ~31% built — three of four sections incomplete
- Operation transferred **TRTC → NTMC on 23 May 2023**

## Rolling stock

### West Ring fleet

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Builder | **Hitachi Rail Italy + Taiwan Rolling Stock** |
| Sets | **17**, four cars each |
| Type | Medium-capacity distributed traction |

Design details:
- Nose raked at **20 degrees**, streamlined, reportedly modelled on a dolphin
- **No external LED destination displays**
- Anti-climbers on the lower body of every car
- Because the line has **full-height platform screen doors throughout**, the
  inter-car connections have only gangway bellows — **no anti-fall devices**,
  unlike TRTC stock
- Livery yellow, white and black, with large black window bands

### North/South Ring fleet — new design

Taipei DORTS unveiled it **3 November 2023**:
- Bright yellow, emphasising the line colour
- Revised nose angles, more contemporary
- Larger windows
- **Side-mounted destination displays** on the carriage exterior, so waiting
  passengers can read route and direction
- LCD passenger information system inside
- **Bottom-mounted luggage racks** at car ends, specifically for interchange
  passengers

### The Hitachi Rail Italy thread

HRI also built the **Sanying Line** fleet. HRI is the former **AnsaldoBreda**,
itself a merger of Ansaldo Trasporti and Breda Costruzioni Ferroviarie,
registered in Pistoia in 2001 as a Finmeccanica subsidiary. Hitachi acquired it
under an agreement reached **24 February 2015**.

**Two New Taipei lines from one Italian manufacturer with a Japanese parent** —
worth an article.

## The 403 earthquake — the flagship story

**3 April 2024, 07:58. Magnitude 7.2, offshore Hualien.** The sensor near
積穗國小 in New Taipei recorded intensity **5-strong (5強)**.

**The line is designed to withstand 6-weak.** It was damaged at 5-strong.

### The damage

Per the **Control Yuan (監察院) investigation report by 監委趙永清 and 葉宜津,
passed 11 March 2025**: between **板新 Banxin** and **橋和 Qiaohe**, the quake
caused **11 steel box girder displacements and 139 damaged installations**.

| Severity | Count | Displacement |
|---|---|---|
| Severe | **7** | **20 to 92 cm** |
| Minor | 4 | 3.5 to 6 cm |

### The root cause — a construction defect, not a material choice

This is the crucial finding and it is widely misreported.

Per NTMC director **李政安** (via UDN): the pot bearing pin (盤式支承插銷) is
about **40 mm** thick, and the tilt-adjustment plate (調坡板) should have been
milled only **5 mm**. In fact it had been milled through to the girder's lower
flange — a depth of **41 mm**. Under seismic load the pin jumped clear, the pot
bearing lost its function, and the girders displaced.

The Control Yuan found:
- Designer **台灣世曦 (CECI)** failed to provide displacement-limiting devices
- Contractor **中華工程 (BES Engineering)** "未按施工圖說施工" — did not build to
  the drawings

Contractor counsel 張冀明 argued only **22 of 882 pot bearings (2.49%)** failed.

Repairs replaced **32 bearings** and added a **second line of anti-uplift
defence**.

The line was designed to MOTC's **2007 railway bridge seismic code**.

### Costs and claims

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Initial repair estimate | "over NT$400 million" |
| Restoration works | NT$796 million (7.96億) |
| Other repairs | NT$645 million (6.45億) |
| Operating losses and bus bridging | NT$486 million (4.86億) |
| **Total claim against 中華工程** | **NT$1.927 billion (19.27億)** |

Mayor 侯友宜 / NTMC: "求償19.27億，一毛錢都不會放過" — claiming 1.927 billion,
not letting a single dollar go.

### Same-day response

- Whole line suspended
- **Danhai LRT unaffected**
- **Ankeng LRT** briefly affected, restored **16:30**, 15-minute headway
- Circular partially restored **17:00**, Banqiao to New Taipei Industrial Park,
  **single-track bidirectional**, 16-minute headway
- Free shuttle buses, New Taipei Industrial Park to Dapinglin, 06:00–24:00,
  10–15 min peak, 20–30 min off-peak, calling at every Circular Line station
- Parallel bus route **982** frequency increased

### Investigation process

Third-party assessment by the **中華民國結構工程學會** (Chinese Institute of
Structural Engineers), **nine experts**, jointly commissioned by both Taipei and
New Taipei metro bureaux. The New Taipei Civil Engineers Association inspected
beams and columns directly. First site meeting **19 April 2024**, second review
**26 April 2024**.

### Restoration

- **3 June 2024** — New Taipei Industrial Park to Banqiao returns to double-track
  bidirectional working, all stations, with arrival countdown displays added
- **12 December 2024, noon** — **full line reopened.** Mayor 侯友宜:
  "今天中午12時開始恢復板橋到中和站通車"
- Original estimate was about 1 year 2 months; **completed roughly 6 months
  early**

### Why the long timeline

The Circular Line is **elevated throughout**, so every repair is high-level work
on a structurally complex viaduct, and each step had to be proved safe before
proceeding.

### The Wenhu comparison — get this right

Taiwanese media asked directly: Wenhu is also elevated and came through the same
earthquake undamaged. Why?

**The answer is not "concrete beats steel."** The failure was a bearing and
construction-conformity issue. That said, engineers (GVM 城市學) note New
Taipei's winding terrain favoured flexible **steel box girders** (larger elastic
sway and seismic deflection), whereas Wenhu's **concrete superstructure** and
different bearing design, built to a different-era standard, rode out the same
quake.

**The decisive variables were bearing design, construction conformity and the
2007 code — not material alone.** Any article must say this clearly, because the
simple version is wrong.

## A later event

**December 2025**, magnitude 7.0 Yilan earthquake: suspended **23:05** with a
train stopped short of 新埔民生 Xinpu Minsheng. Passengers were **evacuated along
the elevated maintenance walkway**, taking about an hour. Staff walked the track,
found no anomalies, line reopened **23:25**.

Passenger accounts described the walkway as narrow and the height alarming —
worth including, since it's the human side of an evacuation procedure most
readers never think about.
