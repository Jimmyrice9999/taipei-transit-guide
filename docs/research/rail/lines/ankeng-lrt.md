## Why this line matters

- Contains **Taiwan's longest rail cable-stayed bridge** — 502 m, single
  asymmetric pylon, 130 m tall
- **Passengers must press a button to open the doors** — unusual on a metro-family
  system and a genuine "you need to know this" fact for visitors
- Its **depot platform is being evaluated for conversion into a station**, which
  is an unusual live experiment
- Serves a distinctive landscape of **1980s hill-top planned communities** that
  function as self-contained small cities

## Service patterns

Two patterns, and the short working is the busier one.

| Pattern | Route | Journey time |
|---|---|---|
| **Full line 全程車** | K01 雙城 ↔ K09 十四張 | **≈21 minutes** |
| **Short working 區間車** | K06 安康 ↔ K09 十四張 | **≈9 minutes** |

### Headways

| Section | Peak | Off-peak |
|---|---|---|
| **Overlap** K06–K09 | **≈7.5 min** | ≈15 min |
| **Non-overlap** K01–K05 | 10–15 min | ≈15 min |

### The first-anniversary change — a good data story

NTMC analysed per-station ridership after a year and found that demand on the
**non-short-working section (K05 景文科大 to K01 雙城) was growing steadily** —
both commuters and students — and that peak trains were becoming crowded.

**So the peak short workings were converted to full-line running and the headway
tightened to 6 minutes.**

That's an operator publicly explaining a timetable change with ridership data.
Concrete, sourced, and exactly the kind of operational detail LTG covers well.

*Source: NTMC press release — **primary**.*

### The livery was chosen by public vote

In **2018** New Taipei opened a public poll on the exterior design with four
options:

- **光耀金 Radiant Gold** — *winner*
- 祥紅金 Auspicious Red-Gold
- 高雅紫 Elegant Purple
- 青翠綠 Verdant Green

**A city letting the public pick a train livery, with the shortlist documented, is
a nice detail** — and the same practice recurs on the Sanying Line, whose livery
theme was also publicly voted.

### Delivery

**First trainset arrived at Ankeng Depot at around 01:00 on 30 September 2021.**
New Taipei's metro bureau planned deliveries **every 2–3 weeks** until all 15 were
in place.

**Doors:** passengers **press a button** to open them. Not automatic.

## 安心橋 An-Xin Bridge — the engineering highlight

Between **K08 新和國小** and **K09 十四張**, crossing the **Xindian River 新店溪**.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | **Single-pylon asymmetric cable-stayed** |
| Length | **502 m** |
| Pylon height | **130 m**, white |
| Status | **Taiwan's longest rail cable-stayed bridge** |
| Closure (合龍) | **2020** |

New Taipei's tourism material notes it sits alongside the **colourful Circular Line
light rail bridge** and the **curved 陽光橋 Sunshine Bridge**, with **Taipei 101**
in the distance — collectively forming the southern New Taipei skyline.

**This bridge alone justifies a page.** Taiwan's longest rail cable-stayed bridge,
built for a 7.5 km light rail line, and no English source mentions it.

| Item | Value |

## The opening

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **10 February 2023** | Opens; trial operation begins |
| 10 Feb – **12 March 2023** | **Free with any accepted e-ticket** (EasyCard, iPass, iCash, senior cards) |
| Trial pattern | Full-line only, **10-minute headway**, tightening to **6 minutes** on demand |

Trial-period services began at **10:00 daily**.

**Opening festivities:** a 通車嘉年華 carnival with singers, a farmers' market,
children's activities and free DIY workshops; the first train's passengers received
commemorative postcards and market vouchers. New Taipei sold a **NT$350 goody bag**,
plus commemorative name cards and stamps. A **foldable DIY guide booklet** was
available at the K09 passenger service centre.

**Five free feeder bus routes** were provided.

**Parking provision:** over **1,800 motorcycle spaces** and **250 car spaces** along
the line.

Estimated journey time saving to the Taipei conurbation: **15–20 minutes**.

## The depot platform experiment

**From July to December 2024**, Ankeng Depot opened for passenger boarding on
weekdays at **06:28** and **07:16**, as a trial to evaluate **whether the depot
platform should be converted into a proper station**.

That is a genuinely unusual thing for an operator to do, and the outcome isn't
established. **Worth chasing** — did it become a station?

## Place and context

**Ankeng's old name was 暗坑** — "dark valley" — referring to a densely wooded
valley. It sits on the left bank of the Xindian River, north of the 塗潭山 foothills.

From the **1980s**, large planned hill communities were developed: **玫瑰中國城
Rose China City, 臺北小城 Taipei Small Town, 青山鎮 Qingshan Town**. Each has full
local amenities and functions "like an independent small city," producing Ankeng's
distinctive hill-town landscape.

Two of those developments are station names (K02, K03), which makes the station
list itself a record of 1980s suburban development. **Good material.**
