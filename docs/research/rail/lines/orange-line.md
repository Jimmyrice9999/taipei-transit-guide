# Kaohsiung Orange Line - research report

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every published claim below was checked against the linked primary page or data response. This report concerns Kaohsiung's Orange Line; Taipei Orange Line material is not evidence for it.

## What is established

- **Joint origin and concession.** Source: *Mission and vision: company origin* (`使命與願景`), Kaohsiung Rapid Transit Corporation (`高雄捷運股份有限公司`), https://corp.krtc.com.tw/About/page?id=6df67da0dd21469da80e9528f32d7057, PRIMARY, confidence High - current operator history page. ## Stated gaps

The page now establishes the Orange Line's joint BOT origin, opening chronology, current station sequence, route-length boundaries, Daliao depot role, current operating pattern and the systems families named by the primaries. It still cannot publish the original signalling supplier or generation, original traction-voltage/substation register, design capacity, station-specific first/last timetable synthesis, platform-door retrofit history, complete accessibility inventory or Orange-only ridership. KRTC procurement and annual-report archives, MTBU technical publications, MOTC monthly ridership tables and station-level facility records are the source families most likely to settle those gaps.
