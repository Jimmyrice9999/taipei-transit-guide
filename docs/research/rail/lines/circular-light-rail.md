# Corpus warning

This is a research lead file, not an authority. The existing `circular-line.md`
file in this directory concerns New Taipei’s Circular Line and is not evidence
for Kaohsiung’s Circular Light Rail. Every claim below was checked against the
fetched Kaohsiung government or KRTC page named in its entry.

Research date: 2026-08-25.

## What is established

### Current scope and system

- **Claim:** The completed Kaohsiung Circular Light Rail route is 22.1 km,
  surface light rail, with 38 stations and transfers to the Red Line, Orange
  Line and TRA.
  - Source: *Circular Light Rail route description* (`路線說明`), Kaohsiung City Government Mass Rapid Transit Bureau (`高雄市政府捷運工程局`), https://mtbu.kcg.gov.tw/KaohsiungMetro/CompletedRoutes/LightRail/LightRail01.html, PRIMARY, confidence High — current completed-route page.
  - Original-language evidence: `長度：22.1公里`, `型式：平面輕軌`, `場站：38座車站` and the transfer rows for `C3、C24`, `C14、C32` and `C18、C30`.
- **Claim:** The route runs from the Qianzhen railway corridor through the harbour and Pier-2, along the western railway corridor and Art Museum/Dashun roads, and back to the southern corridor.
  - Source: same completed-route page and URL, PRIMARY, confidence High — route overview and station-position table.
  - Original-language evidence: `環狀輕軌路線，自凱旋三路旁台鐵臨港線路廊往南佈設...經過...西臨港線鐵路廊帶、美術館路、大順一～三路...沿路廊接回起點`.
- **Claim:** The approved combined plan gives a construction cost of NT$211.16 billion, central support of NT$70.13 billion and city responsibility of NT$141.03 billion including self-financing revenue.
  - Source: *Circular Light Rail planning explanation* (`規劃說明`), Kaohsiung City Government Mass Rapid Transit Bureau, https://mtbu.kcg.gov.tw/KaohsiungMetro/ApprovedRoutes/LightRail/LightRail0202.html, PRIMARY, confidence High — current planning page.
  - Original-language evidence: `總建設經費：211.16億元`, `中央補助：70.13億元` and `市府負擔：141.03億元(含自償性經費)`.
- **Claim:** The approved system uses a surface B-type right of way, 1,435 mm standard gauge and 100% low-floor vehicles.
  - Source: same planning page and URL, PRIMARY, confidence High — current planning specifications.
  - Original-language evidence: `路權：採平面 B 型路權，軌道與車道隔離`, `軌距：1,435 mm 標準軌距` and `車輛：車寬 2.65 公尺，100% 低底盤`.

### Two-phase construction and public dispute

- **Claim:** The plan was divided because the C17–C20 railway-garden section depended on urban railway undergrounding and the C32–C36 corridor depended on releasing Taiwan Railways East Harbour Line land.
  - Source: same planning page and URL, PRIMARY, confidence High — current explanation of the two interfaces.
  - Original-language evidence: `須配合高雄市區鐵路地下化計畫之施工時程興建` and `須俟鐵路地下化工程完工、空出東臨港線鐵路用地後方可興建`.
- **Claim:** The planning scope defined Phase 1 as 8.7 km from the Qianzhen shunting yard to Sizihwan and Phase 2 as the remaining 13.4 km, with a Phase 1 target at the end of ROC year 106.
  - Source: same planning page and URL, PRIMARY, confidence High — dated planning milestone, not an as-built date.
  - Original-language evidence: `第一階段：前鎮調車場～捷運西子灣站，長度 8.7 KM...預計106年6月底完工` and `第二階段...長度 13.4 KM`.
- **Claim:** DORTS says the route was planned from the 1990s, entered construction in ROC year 106, then encountered repeated resident protests over route and construction method; the mayor announced a pause on 18 January 2019 and broader hearings.
  - Source: *Response on the Phase 2 Circular Light Rail delay* (`高雄環狀輕軌二階美術館路及大順路段延宕回應說明`), Kaohsiung City Government Mass Rapid Transit Bureau, https://mtbu.kcg.gov.tw/Activities/C002100?CategoryID=ee7b8b6d-03c0-4e8f-b133-e0a7c97fa933&PageType=1&id=35f1845d-588f-4cce-b857-d6dafad25662, PRIMARY, confidence High — full DORTS response.
  - Original-language evidence: `於90年代開始規劃`, `於106年進場施工時遭當地民眾多次抗議並反對進場施工` and `市長遂於108年1月18日宣布輕軌二階工程具爭議性不宜貿然施工`.
- **Claim:** Five public hearings in February–March 2019 collected 1,874 opinion forms, and a nine-member rail/transport expert committee examined nine alternatives.
  - Source: same delay-response page and URL, PRIMARY, confidence High — DORTS reports both counts and the option list.
  - Original-language evidence: `於108年2至3月舉辦了5場公聽說明會`、`一共收集了1874份民眾意見單` and `外聘9位...專家學者委員會` with `九大方案`.
- **Claim:** DORTS’s account says the expert recommendations retained the southern section, used resident participation and human-centred streetscape treatment for Art Museum/Dashun, and considered a future north extension to Taiwan High Speed Rail Zuoying.
  - Source: same delay-response page and URL, PRIMARY, confidence High as an attributed government account.
  - Original-language evidence: `大南環調整先行完成`, `導入居民參與，建構人本環境造街計畫` and `推動輕軌向北延伸至高鐵左營站`.

### Environmental revision and operation

- **Claim:** The 2021 environmental-difference review followed more than 44 public briefings and 20 expert meetings and retained the route with a larger curve radius, a northward Art Museum Road shift, four road lanes, C21A and other pedestrian/station changes.
  - Source: *Circular Light Rail environmental-difference report approved* (`高雄輕軌環境影響差異分析報告 環評委員審查通過`), Kaohsiung City Government Mass Rapid Transit Bureau, https://mtbu.kcg.gov.tw/Activities/C002100?CategoryID=ee7b8b6d-03c0-4e8f-b133-e0a7c97fa933&Keyword=%E6%A8%B9&PageNumber=4&PageType=1&id=a5b4517e-8be6-4df0-a0cf-e1f7c81129d4, PRIMARY, confidence High — full 2021 DORTS release.
  - Original-language evidence: `舉辦逾44場公聽說明會...20場專家學者會議` and `加大轉彎半徑、輕軌路線(美術館路段)北移、美術館路維持雙向四車道、增設C21A站`.
- **Claim:** KRTC records a 1 January 2024 full-circle trial serving all 38 stations and formal full-line operation from 26 February 2024, with the service beginning and ending at C1.
  - Source: *Kaohsiung Light Rail riding and fare Q&A* (`高雄輕軌乘車及票價Q&A`), Kaohsiung Rapid Transit Corporation (`高雄捷運股份有限公司`), https://www.krtc.com.tw/Service/faq_more?id=10def821c28c4466a394be86edca7383, PRIMARY, confidence High — current operator page updated 30 June 2026.
  - Original-language evidence: `高雄輕軌於2024年1月1日正式成圓展開試營運` and `自2024年2月26日起輕軌全線正式營運，起迄站均為C1站`.
- **Claim:** The current operator timetable lists C1–C37 plus C21A and exposes current naming differences such as C26 Dashun Minzu and parenthetical names at C27 and C31.
  - Source: *KRTC Circular Light Rail timetable station list* (`各車站時刻表`), Kaohsiung Rapid Transit Corporation, https://www.krtc.com.tw/KLRT/timetable, PRIMARY, confidence High — current operator timetable page.
  - Original-language evidence: the `輕軌各站` row lists `C21A內惟藝術中心` and the current C26/C27/C31 names.

## Checked and failed

- **Specific quality-control and procurement allegations** — Checked 2026-08-25: Traditional-Chinese searching found DORTS notices about alleged defective work and alleged procurement misconduct, but direct full-page fetches returned Internal Error. Search snippets were not used, so no allegation, amount or finding is published from them.
- **Package-level contract values and actual civil-work completion dates** — Checked 2026-08-25: the current MTBU completed-route/planning pages, DORTS delay and environmental-review pages, and KRTC operating pages were fetched in full. They establish phases, dispute chronology and operation, but not a complete package ledger or verified package completion schedule.
- **Kaohsiung Circular Light Rail ridership series in TDX** — Checked 2026-08-25: the live TDX KRTC Metro line, station and shape records were checked alongside the KRTC timetable and operator pages. The committed Metro response exposes Red and Orange rather than a Circular Light Rail line; no LRT ridership series was found in those records.

## Stated gaps

The primary record now supports the construction controversy and operating milestone, but not a station-by-station construction package table, current ridership, full accessibility inventory, power-substation register, rolling-stock allocation or measured LRT geometry. The documents that would settle those gaps are the approved construction contracts and completion reports, KRTC operating statistics and asset records, and a TDX or operator LRT-specific data feed.
