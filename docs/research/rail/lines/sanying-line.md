# Sanying Line 三鶯線 — research

New Taipei Metro's newest line, opened five weeks ago. English coverage is
effectively nonexistent, and the free-trial period ends 31 August 2026.

**Not published. Verify before promoting to content.**

---

## Why this matters now

- Opened **30 June 2026**. Almost nothing exists about it in English.
- Free travel ends **31 August 2026** — anything published before then is useful
  to people right now, not just archivally.
- New Taipei's **first self-built (自辦) medium-capacity metro line** — previous
  lines were delivered by Taipei's DORTS. That's a structural first and nobody
  has explained it in English.
- Part of the 三環六線 "Three Rings, Six Lines" programme.
- Roughly **26 years** from first proposal, **10 years** of construction.

## The line

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Chinese name | 三鶯線 |
| Code | **LB** |
| Operator | New Taipei Metro Corporation (NTMC) |
| Length | **14.29 km** |
| Stations | **12, all elevated** |
| Termini | LB01 Dingpu 頂埔 ↔ LB12 Yingtao Fude 鶯桃福德 |
| Districts | Tucheng, Sanxia, Yingge |
| Type | Medium-capacity |
| Opened | **30 June 2026** (afternoon) |

Named for the two districts it serves — 三峽 Sanxia and 鶯歌 Yingge.

### Approvals timeline

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 26 April 2026 | 初勘 preliminary inspection passed |
| 7 June 2026 | 履勘 formal inspection by MOTC completed |
| 23 June 2026 | MOTC operating permit granted |
| 30 June 2026 | Trial operation begins, afternoon |
| 31 August 2026 | Free period ends |

The 初勘 / 履勘 distinction is worth explaining — it's standard Taiwanese
practice and opaque to English readers. 履勘 is the final pre-opening
inspection covering trains, stations, signalling and evacuation equipment.

### Naming conflict — resolve before publishing

**LB03 appears as both 挖子 (Wazi) and 長壽山 (Changshoushan).**

The 2017 Liberty Times report of the official naming announcement lists 挖子.
NTMC's current route page lists 長壽山. Several 2025–26 guides still print 挖子.

Almost certainly a rename between announcement and opening, but that isn't
established. **Verify, and publish the rename with its date** — this is exactly
the kind of detail an English reference should carry, and the kind that
propagates wrong when nobody checks.

### Station naming principles

The naming committee stated four criteria, which is unusually explicit and worth
covering as its own topic:

- **Local recognisability** — 三峽
- **Landmark prominence** — 鶯歌車站, 臺北大學
- **Historical significance** — 媽祖田, 挖子
- **Directional reference** — 鶯桃福德, named after a road junction

## Rolling stock

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Manufacturer | **Hitachi Rail Italy** |
| Cars per train | **2** |
| Train length | **~35 m** |
| Body | Lightweight aluminium alloy |
| Nose | FRP composite, for weight reduction |
| Fleet | **29 cars**, all delivered by September 2025 |
| Livery theme | 萬里藍天，悠遊峽谷 — "boundless blue sky, roaming the gorge" |

**Two themed interiors:** a Hakka train (客家列車) and an Indigenous train
(原住民列車), reflecting the cultural character of Tucheng, Sanxia and Yingge.
NTMC also commissioned public art from Taiwanese and international artists for
the stations.

**Unresolved:** run 3 found accounts disagreeing on which Hitachi plant built
them, and fleet size and maximum speed were marked TBC as single-source. Worth
another attempt — Hitachi Rail's own releases would settle it.

## Operations

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Peak headway (initial) | 5–6 minutes |
| Off-peak headway (initial) | 8–9 minutes |
| Design maximum | **27 trains/hour, 2.2 min headway** |
| Projected daily ridership | ~40,000 |
| Actual first month | **over 750,000 passengers** — verified, run 5 |

The gap between initial and design headway is worth stating — a new line runs
conservatively while stability is proven, exactly as Wenhu did in 2009.

### Fares (from 1 September 2026)

Banded by station count:

| Stations travelled | Fare |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | NT$20 |
| 5–6 | NT$25 |
| 7–8 | NT$30 |
| 9+ | NT$35 |

End to end: **NT$35.** Concessionary fares (senior, accessibility, child) are
**40% of the published fare**.

Accepted payment is unusually broad and worth documenting: EasyCard, iPass, some
contactless credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, LINE Pay, iPASS MONEY, Taishin
Pay, and TWQR ride codes.

## Interchanges

**LB01 Dingpu → Bannan Line (BL01).** Co-located structure with a direct
transfer passage; NTMC states **2 minutes** between systems.

**LB08 Yingge → TRA Yingge station.** Connected by a 彩虹通廊 "rainbow corridor"
and skybridge, also stated at 2 minutes, and linking to the New Taipei Museum of
Art. The city marketed this as a 三點一線 "three points, one line" arts corridor.

Estimated journey time saving to central Taipei: **about 20 minutes each way.**

---

## Extension to Bade

LB12 Yingtao Fude has **tail tracks already provided** for the extension, so
future construction won't require demolishing existing structure.

The extension to Bade, Taoyuan is **approved by the Executive Yuan** and will
connect to Taoyuan Metro's Green Line at G04. Timing is tied to Green Line
progress; Taoyuan's DORTS estimates roughly **nine years**, so around 2035.
