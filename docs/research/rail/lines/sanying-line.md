### Journey time and headway — different project stages

- **2014 forecast:** end to end no more than 23 minutes and three-minute peak
  interval. Source: early-works release. Original: 「尖峰班距3分鐘，總行車時間
  不超過23分鐘」.
- **2018 design-year pattern:** LB01–LB08 at 2.2 minutes and LB08–LB12 at
  4.4 minutes on weekdays; the extension pattern differs again. Source:
  technical report, table 8.
- **2026 opening service:** roughly 30 minutes end to end, six-minute peak and
  eight-minute off-peak. Source: operator opening notice.
- **2026 special event:** 2.2-minute service on 4 July. Source: first-month
  bureau release.

These are an early forecast, a mature design pattern, initial service and a
special event. They disagree numerically but do not measure the same operating
condition.

### Bade approval — one official page contradicts itself

The project history says 「114年8月1日行政院核定三鶯線延伸桃園八德段綜合規劃
報告書」. The introduction on the same live page still says 「本計畫尚未核定，
相關資料謹供參考」. The header also says completion is expected in 2034. The
second sentence is stale or uses a different unstated approval scope; the page
does not explain which, so both statements are published.

### Design requirement and delivered maximum speed

- The 2018 functional specification says 「最大營運速度 ≥70公里／小時」.
- The current operator fleet page says 「最大營運速度 80km/h」.

This is not resolved by selecting one: the first is a minimum tender requirement
and the second is the delivered fleet's published maximum.

## Contradicted corpus leads

- The earlier research table said **29 cars**. The current operator page says
  **29 trains**, each of two cars. The lead was wrong by both unit and implied
  fleet size.
- The earlier file called the fleet manufacturer **Hitachi Rail Italy** without
  separating design and manufacture. The operator says Hitachi Rail designed
  the trains and they were manufactured in Japan; no plant is named.
- The earlier file said the Bade extension was expected around **2035**. The
