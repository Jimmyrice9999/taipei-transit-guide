# Kaohsiung Red Line — research report

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every published claim below was checked against the linked primary page or data response. This report concerns Kaohsiung’s Red Line; Taipei Red Line material is not evidence for it.

## Checked and failed

- **Original Red Line signalling supplier and generation** — Checked 2026-08-25: the full service-indicator PDF lists signalling as an integrated subsystem and the full RK1 notices describe extension testing, but neither establishes the original Red Line supplier or generation. The page leaves both TBC.
- **Original Red Line traction-voltage and substation register** — Checked 2026-08-25: the full historical service-indicator PDF names DC and AC power, while the full RK1 power notice gives 22 kV and 380/220 V only for the extension interface. No original-line voltage/substation ledger was found.
- **Line-wide platform-screen-door retrofit history and accessibility inventory** — Checked 2026-08-25: KRTC’s full station-information page and MTBU’s full original-system and RK1 system records were read; they do not provide a station-by-station original Red Line retrofit/facility table.
- **Per-station and monthly Red Line ridership** — Checked 2026-08-25: the committed TDX Metro pull was checked and the KRTC anniversary page was fetched in full; the former provides route/timetable structure rather than a ridership table, while the latter gives network-level daily milestones. No per-station monthly series was found.
- **MTBU RK1 signalling-detail page** — Checked 2026-08-25: direct full-page fetch of `https://mtbu.kcg.gov.tw/Activities/C002100?CategoryID=ee7b8b6d-03c0-4e8f-b133-e0a7c97fa933&Keyword=&PageNumber=4&PageType=1&id=d6967579-0159-44cf-9294-98190ffa4b15` returned Internal Error. ## Stated gaps

The page now establishes the Red Line’s private-participation origin, opening chronology, R11 and R24 interfaces, RK1 extension milestones, depot locations and the systems evidence that is actually stated by the primaries. It still cannot publish the original supplier/generation, line-wide headways and capacity, a station-by-station first/last timetable synthesis, original platform-door retrofit history, complete accessibility inventory or per-station ridership. The next source families are KRTC procurement and annual-report archives, MTBU technical publications and approved construction contracts, MOTC monthly ridership tables, and station-level operator facility records.
