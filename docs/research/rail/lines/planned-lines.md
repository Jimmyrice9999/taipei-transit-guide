## 三環六線 — Three Rings, Six Lines

The umbrella capital programme for the Taipei conurbation, spanning the Circular
Line, Wanda–Zhonghe, Sanying, Ankeng, Danhai, Airport MRT extensions, Xidong,
Keelung MRT and others.

**The programme's own definition, scope and political history are TBC** and worth
establishing — it's the frame every planned line sits inside, and it's a political
commitment as much as an engineering one.

## Xinyi East Extension 信義東延段 — OPENING THIS MONTH

**The most time-sensitive item on the site.**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Line | Tamsui–Xinyi (R) |
| New station | **廣慈/奉天宮 Guangci / Fengtian Temple, R01** |
| Sub-name | 松山慈惠堂 |
| Length | **≈1.4 km** |
| Expected opening | **End of August 2026** |
| Structure | Three underground levels, island platform |
| Platform length | ≈213.9 m |
| Crossover | Between Fude St lanes 51 and 89, **119.4 m long** |
| Tail tracks | Shield tunnel beyond the station to Yucheng Park, for stabling |
| New service pattern | **Tamsui – Guangci/Fengtian Temple** |
| Rolling stock | **C382**, Hyundai Rotem, 7 trains / 42 cars |

### Project history

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 23 Dec 2009 | CEPD approves the extension — **two** stations planned |
| 28 March 2012 | Naming meeting proposes 中坡, 松山商職, 福德信義 |
| **31 March 2015** | DORTS **cancels the second station** |
| 20 July 2016 | Contract **CR580C** awarded to **BES Engineering + Dafeng Construction**, **NT$4.828 billion** |
| 7 November 2016 | Construction begins |
| 6 July 2017 | Final name 廣慈/奉天宮 approved |
| End August 2026 | Expected opening |

**A numbering trap:** the planning documents refer to "R03" and "R04" stations, but
R03 and R04 already exist as Taipei 101/World Trade Center and Xinyi Anhe. The
extension numbering was provisional; the station became **R01**. Don't conflate them.

**The South Ring procurement failure is worth documenting:** contract **CF670
failed six tenders**, with the budget rising from **NT$11.2 billion to NT$20.7
billion** before award on **27 September 2024**.

~2028–2031 and East ~2032; zh.wikipedia gives New Taipei ~2031 / Taipei ~2032;
independent projections run to **2035–2038**.

**Payoff on completion:** 象山 Xiangshan to 動物園 Taipei Zoo falls from **26 minutes
to 4 minutes**.

## Xidong Line 汐東線 — "the election MRT"

**The nickname is the story.**

Known for years as the **Xizhi–Minsheng line** and mocked as **選舉捷運** — "the
election MRT" — because it was promised at successive elections for **more than
twenty years** without being built.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Contract signed | **19 December 2024** |
| Length | **5.56 km** (4.75 in New Taipei, 0.81 in Taipei) |
| Stations | **6 elevated**, plus 1 depot |
| Construction start | **March 2025** |
| Target completion | **2032** |

### Connections

| Station | Connection |
|---|---|
| SB10 | Wenhu's **東湖 Donghu** station |
| SB14 | **TRA 汐科 Xike** |
| SB15 | **Keelung MRT** |

**Stations SB13, SB14 and SB15 are shared with the Keelung MRT**, which New Taipei
is also managing. Shared stations and shared track are being used to work around
narrow road corridors.

**Twenty years of election promises, then a contract, then shared infrastructure
with another line to fit the roads.** That's a complete article.

*Source: Yahoo Stock News quoting the signing ceremony — **single source, verify**.*

## Sanying extension to Bade 八德

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| From | **LB12 鶯桃福德** |
| To | Taoyuan Green Line **G04** |
| Distance | ≈3.9 km from the terminus to G04 |
| Status | **Executive Yuan approved November 2019** |
| Timing | Tied to Taoyuan Green Line progress; Taoyuan DORTS estimates ~9 years, so **~2035** |

**Tail tracks are already provided at LB12**, so future construction won't require
demolishing existing structure. That's good forward planning and worth noting —
Taipei has not always done this.

Approvals chain: Taoyuan commissioned New Taipei in **2013**; feasibility cleared
**MOTC (May 2019)**, **NDC (August 2019)** and the **Executive Yuan (November
2019)**.

## Rejected and abandoned proposals

Worth a section, because rejected plans explain the network as much as built ones.

- **Bannan extension to 樟樹灣 Zhangshuwan** for a Keelung MRT interchange — raised
  publicly **11 September 2023**, **rejected 27 December 2023** on schedule-impact
  grounds
- The **Circular Line East Ring** was itself a late addition, approved 2022–2023
- The **1992 rejection** of extending the heavy Tamsui line north, which produced
  the Danhai LRT instead

**More rejected proposals almost certainly exist and would make a genuinely
distinctive page.** Nobody catalogues what Taipei decided *not* to build.

## How to present this section

**Recommendation:** one index page listing everything with status and target, plus
individual pages only for lines actually under construction — Wanda–Zhonghe, Xidong,
Keelung MRT, and the Circular Line rings.

Proposals with no contract should stay on the index. Giving a page to a line that
may never be built creates 40 empty pages and an appearance of coverage the site
doesn't have.

**And label every date as a projection with its source.** The site already handles
conflicting figures well — the same discipline applies doubly to the future.
