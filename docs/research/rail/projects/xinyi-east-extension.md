## What is established

### Current scope and the cancelled station

- **Claim:** The current Xinyi Line East Extension continues from the east end of the Xiangshan station tail track as an underground high-capacity route, follows Xinyi Road Sec. 6 and Fude Street, continues by shield tunnel along Fude Street and Zhongpo South Road to Yucheng Park, and is approximately 1.4 km with one underground station and an operational tail track.
  - Source: *Xinyi Line East Extension project page* (`信義線東延段`), Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems (`臺北市政府捷運工程局`), https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=027cdb8c2a690039&s=475831800B81DB25, PRIMARY, confidence High — current full DORTS project page.
  - Original-language evidence: `信義線東延段係接續捷運信義線象山站尾軌東端以高運量地下方案向東延伸，沿信義路六段（原此區位規劃設置之車站已取消）至福德街廣慈博愛園區前增設橫渡線以及終點車站，並自車站以東起以潛盾隧道沿福德街、中坡南路至玉成公園止，全長約1.4公里，共設置1座地下車站及供營運調度使用之尾軌。`
- **Claim:** The original plan had stations at Xinyi Road Sec. 6 and Fude Street, but the Xinyi Road Sec. 6 station was cancelled because landowners opposed participating in land development and the station did not meet siting conditions; the project retained the Fude Street station and used a southern development site for metro facilities.
  - Source: same DORTS project page and URL, PRIMARY, confidence High — the page states the original two-station environmental statement and the reason for the scope change.
  - Original-language evidence: `環境影響說明書（原含信義路六段、福德街設置之2座車站）` and `因信義路六段設置之車站地主反對參與土地開發，未達設站條件，僅設置位於福德街之車站，並通過車站南側基地辦理土地開發以設置捷運設施。`
- **Claim:** The planned station is Guangci/Fengtian Temple beneath Fude Street in front of Guangci Bo'ai Park.
  - Source: same DORTS project page and URL, PRIMARY, confidence High — current planned-location field.
  - Original-language evidence: `廣慈/奉天宮站：臺北市廣慈博愛園區前福德街道路下方。`

## Stated gaps

The primary record establishes the current one-station scope, its approvals, engineering dimensions, trial operation and latest inspection boundary, but not the final opening date, contract value, public station code, revenue timetable or ridership. A later MOTC operating-permission notice and TRTC opening timetable would settle the first and fourth gaps; procurement or budget records would settle contract value; an operator station record would settle the public code; and post-opening operator or TDX data would establish ridership.
