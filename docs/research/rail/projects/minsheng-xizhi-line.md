- **Unified whole-project progress percentage** — Checked 2026-08-25: both current DORTS route/progress records. The only percentage found is New Taipei’s 0% controlled-programme measure for Xidong; no common denominator covering the Taipei and Xidong phases was published in the fetched pages.

## Stated gaps

The current primary record supports a phased project page, not final station stubs. A later approved Taipei comprehensive plan would settle the final alignment, station names and codes, station accessibility, timetable and Taipei construction schedule. Operator or TDX records after opening would be needed for observed ridership. A programme-control document defining a common denominator would be needed before any combined progress percentage could be stated.
