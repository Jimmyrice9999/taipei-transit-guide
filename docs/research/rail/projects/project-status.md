# Taipei-region rail projects — research findings, 24 August 2026

**Corpus warning: `docs/research/` is leads only. It has been wrong repeatedly.
Nothing here is citable until it has been checked against a primary source —
see `.claude/skills/transit-research/SKILL.md`.**

This report establishes a first project-status index from full official pages.
It keeps construction, approval, planning, rejection and unresolved historical
status as separate states. Retrieval date: 2026-08-24.

## Contradicted the corpus

- `docs/research/rail/lines/planned-lines.md` labels November 2019 as the
  Sanying-Bade comprehensive-plan approval. The current New Taipei project
  record assigns 2019-11-13 to feasibility approval and 2025-08-01 to
  comprehensive-plan approval. The corpus had collapsed two approval stages.
- The same corpus file presents the Danhai Blue Sea Phase 2 plan as approved
  in March 2024. The current New Taipei project record says the revised
  riverside plan is under review, while separately recording 2026 urban-plan
  approval and 2025 advance works. The page therefore publishes the revised
  status rather than the corpus wording.
- The corpus gives an end-August 2026 expectation for Xinyi East. The current
  DORTS page says the extension is entering service after preliminary
  inspection, while the dated FAQ gives ROC 115 as a target; no full-page
  primary source read in this pass confirms that exact Gregorian date.

## Conflicts

- North/South Ring status: the current route page says contract-document review
  and detailed design; DORTS's same-year policy page says civil works and
  systems design are being accelerated. Both values are published with their
  page context and retrieval date; neither is selected as the sole status.
- Xinyi East timing: the 2026 policy page describes final restoration and
  preliminary inspection, while the older FAQ's target is ROC 115. The first is
  a current work description; the second is a dated target, not a guaranteed
  opening.
- Danhai Phase 2 geometry: New Taipei publishes 4.4 km for the original route
  and 3.32 km for the revised riverside route. They are different alignments,
  not an arithmetic correction.
