# TOD and joint development — research findings, 24 August 2026

**Corpus warning: `docs/research/` is leads only. It has been wrong repeatedly.
Nothing here is citable until it has been checked against a primary source —
see `.claude/skills/transit-research/SKILL.md`.**

This report establishes a first joint-development index from full official
pages, technical-journal pages, a current rental table, current investor lists,
New Taipei portfolio records and the Judicial Yuan's interpretation of the
Xindian depot case. It does not treat the English label TOD as an official
project category. Retrieval date: 2026-08-24.

## What is established

- **Taipei's purpose and return model —** DORTS's full FAQ says land development
  can return property rent, sale and operating income to support MRT
  construction. Source: DORTS, `聯合開發` FAQ,
  https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/News_toggle.aspx?PageSize=200&n=2A66A485FACB0D5B&page=1&sms=87415A8B9CE81B16,
  PRIMARY, High confidence because the current full page was fetched. Original
  text: `透過土地開發分回不動產出租售、經營之收益，挹注捷運建設`.
- **Taipei incentive-volume split —** The same FAQ says the authority and
  landowners may each receive half of actual design incentive floor area under
  the applicable rules, subject to a different agreement or law. Same source,
  PRIMARY, High confidence. Original text: `主管機關與土地所有權人各可分得依捷運土地開發、都市計畫等相關獎勵法令所增加實際設計獎勵容積之半數`.
- **Taipei value conversion —** DORTS describes the building's total value,
  the authority's incentive-volume value and the investor's assessed
  construction/tax/expected-profit contribution as inputs to value conversion;
  it does not publish one universal investor revenue percentage. Same source,
  PRIMARY, High confidence. Original text: `開發大樓總權值扣除主管機關取得獎勵容積權值而得`.
- **Daan Forest Park J2 —** DORTS's full technical-journal page gives a 2,994
  m² private site, 34,644.77 m² of non-MRT floor area, 62 units, ROC 99 use
  permit, a station exit 6 and an estimated NT$56.77 million saving in land
  acquisition at the stated valuation basis. Source: *捷運技術 第51期* p.17,
  https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no51/files/basic-html/page17.html,
  PRIMARY, High confidence. Original text: `基地面積2,994m2`;
  `總樓地板面積34644.77m2`; `於99年領得使用執照`; `可節省約新臺幣5,677萬元土地徵收費用`.
- **Daan J3 —** The same full page gives an 890 m² site, 12,650.72 m² of

- **Daan design variance —** DORTS's full design-book page gives 23,499.96 m²

  https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no18/files/basic-html/page122.html,

## Contradicted the corpus

No previously published corpus statement was disproved in this pass. The
existing Xindian-depot research records the exact DORTS value of 92,560.7 m²,
while the later government research report describes the site as approximately
90,000 m²; that is recorded below as a precision/scope conflict, not silently
resolved. The existing station research already carries the Daan, Zhongshan,
Xinpu and other station-level land-development leads, so this unit adds the
cross-site process, current investor list, portfolio finance and legal-conflict
index rather than duplicating those station paragraphs.

## Conflicts

- **Meihe City building count:** New Taipei's 2014 news account says the design
  was reduced from 26 to 13 buildings; the later government research report
  says 16 towers. They describe different project records or stages; neither
  value is selected.
- **Meihe City area and history:** The existing DORTS depot research gives
  92,560.7 m² and an occupancy permit on 15 November 2012; the later research
  report rounds the site to about 90,000 m² and gives about 400,600 m² of
  building floor area. These are different measures and source purposes.
- **Zhongshan J1 progress:** DORTS's 2017 technical page says 95.40% complete,
  while the current rental table lists returned properties by use and occupancy
  without providing a final completion date. The current inventory cannot be
  substituted for the dated construction measure.
- **Taipei versus New Taipei value allocation:** Taipei publishes a half-and-
  half incentive-floor-area rule; New Taipei publishes value subtraction and
  selection-order rules. They are different administrative formulas, not a
  disagreement to average.
