## Conflicts

1. The DORTS route material describes the built Bannan sections as underground,
   but its Nangang planning narrative also records an earlier plan containing
   one elevated and one at-grade station before the underground treatment was
   adopted. This is a planned-versus-built conflict, not a reason to rewrite
   the current station structure: the station pages retain underground and
   the planning history is recorded here.

2. The DORTS Nangang table displays the BL14 engineering entry as 4BL9. The
   operating code is BL14 and the surrounding sequence is BL6 through BL16.
   The overlay preserves the table’s exact 4BL9 text rather than silently
   normalising it to BL9.

3. TDX and the current TRTC BL12 page expose the Bannan-to-Tamsui-Xinyi
   interchange. The DORTS line material also names the Airport MRT in the
   Taipei Main Station transfer context. Both are retained: the station page
   shows the current R interchange and labels Airport MRT with transfer mode
   TBC. The DORTS material also discusses a future Sanying connection at the
   western end; that planning statement is not rendered as a current
   interchange at BL01.

No conflict was found in the exit counts where the full TRTC and DORTS tables
both publish one. The DORTS values for BL01-BL23 and the current TRTC counts
match.
