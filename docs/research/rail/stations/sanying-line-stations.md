## Conflicts

1. **LB03 name:** the New Taipei City naming announcement of 12 July 2017
   published `挖子`; the 9 June 2020 city gazette explicitly changed it to
   `長壽山`; the current operator page and station sheet use `長壽山 /
   Changshoushan`. These values describe successive official names and both are
   published with dates.
2. **Trial hours:** the opening announcement published 10:00–20:00; New Taipei
   City published 08:00–22:00 from 1 August; the operator later published
   06:00–24:00 for 16–31 August. These are successive phases, so none is
   silently preferred or represented as covering the whole trial.
3. **Normal-fare start:** the previous line research asserted “from 1 September
   2026”; the operator publishes NT$20–35 but says the charging date will be
   announced separately. The fare values remain, but the start date is TBC.
