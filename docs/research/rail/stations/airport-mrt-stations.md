## Conflicts

1. **A3 station code.** TYMC and the RB report use engineering/current-project
   label Y19 in their connector text; the committed TDX operating registry uses
   Y20. Both are published values and both are shown.
2. **A8 platform-lift count.** TYMC’s full service table says 「往一、二月台各一，以及往三、四月台各一，共二台」: four platform destinations but a stated total of two lifts. The page keeps the operator’s two-unit total and the four destinations; it is not corrected by inference.
3. **A12 access count.** The RB report says there are two entrances, while the
   TYMC exit table has one numbered exit and a separately named Airport
   connector exit. The page retains both descriptions as two access points,
   not as a fabricated second numbered street exit.
4. **A14 versus A14a.** The RB report describes the unfinished A14 third-terminal
   station as a structure through which trains pass and separately describes
   A14a as the operating Airport Hotel station. TDX has A14a as the current
   page and reserves A14 for the future station. Neither is silently merged.
5. **Route-level measurements.** The line research records the TDX chainage
   value 51.76 km and the DORTS project value 51.03 km. This station report
   does not use either as a station-page structure fact; the conflict remains
   published in the line research.
