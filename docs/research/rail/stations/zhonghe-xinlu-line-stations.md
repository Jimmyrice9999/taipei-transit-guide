coordinates. None had a hand-researched station record for structure, exits or
facilities. TDX listed interchanges at O02, O05, O06, O07, O08, O11 and O17;

### Zhonghe route

Source: Zhonghe Line route and stations / 中和新蘆線：中和線路線說明 —
Taipei City Department of Rapid Transit Systems, PRIMARY, High confidence.

URL: https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=122C012A85C41404

The full page says: 中和線全長約5.4公里，設4座車站，全線採地下方式建造.
It identifies Zhonghe Depot, gives the CC560 main contract date as 81年5月13日,
and states that the line opened on 87年12月24日. Its station table lists the
engineering sequence O19 through O15 for O01 through O05 and identifies O05/G09
Guting as constructed under the Xindian Line project.

This establishes underground structure and the opening date for O01-O05. It
also establishes the route-level depot and engineering-history context, but
the prose count of four stations conflicts with the table's five O01-O05 rows.

### Xinzhuang route

Source: Xinzhuang Line route and stations / 中和新蘆線：新莊線路線說明 —
Taipei City Department of Rapid Transit Systems, PRIMARY, High confidence.

URL: https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=122C012A85C41404&s=33245A586D747A2C

The full page says: 新莊線全長約19.7公里，共設16座車站，全線採地下方式建造.
It records three operating phases: Taipei city Daqiaotou-Zhongxiao Xinsheng on
99年11月3日, Daqiaotou-Fu Jen on 101年1月5日, and Fu Jen-Huilong on 102年6月29日.
It also records the Dongmen east extension on 101年9月30日, a CK570D main contract
signed on 90年6月29日, Xinzhuang Depot, and engineering numbers O14 through O01
plus O60 and O59.

This establishes the underground structure and opening dates for O06-O19,
O20-O21, and the contract/depot history. It does not establish a station-level
fare-boundary transfer type or train-roster allocation.

## Conflicts

1. **Zhonghe route station count.** DORTS's route prose says 中和線 ... 設4座車站,
   while its station table lists O01 through O05, five rows. Both values are
   retained. The table includes O05/Guting, which the same page says was built
   under the Xindian Line project.

2. **Postal codes.** TDX gives O02 as 23582, while the current TRTC page and
   DORTS material give 235086. TDX gives O05 as 100032, DORTS gives 100031 and
   TRTC gives 100032. TDX gives O17 as 24251, while TRTC and DORTS give 242035.
   These are source values, not corrections inferred by the overlay.

3. **Depot area.** The official depot table gives Zhonghe approximately 1.2 ha,
   Xinzhuang approximately 13.9 ha and Luzhou approximately 15.9 ha. The route
   pages give approximately 1.47 ha, 17.5 ha initially and 13.9 ha after
   preservation for Xinzhuang, and 16.8 ha for Luzhou. No value was selected;
   the generated pages use the depot table for the published depot summary.

4. **O13 platform levels and current exit count.** The DORTS architecture page
   describes a six-level stacked station with upper and lower platform waiting
   levels, while the current TRTC page reports one street exit. These are
   different categories, not substitute exit counts; both facts are retained.

## Checked and failed

- **Station naming and renames** — Full TRTC station pages, DORTS route pages and
  station-design pages were checked for construction-era names, rename dates and
  announcements. Current names are published, but no rename chronology was
  established for O01-O21 or O50-O54. Checked 14 August 2026.
- **Paid-area versus street transfer** — Full operator and builder pages identify
  transfer lines and some physical connections but do not establish a
  consistent fare-boundary classification for O02, O05, O06, O07, O08, O11,
  O15 or O17. The generated transfer fields remain TBC where the source does
  not say. Checked 14 August 2026.
- **Station-specific depot roster** — The full depot table and route pages
  establish line-level depots but not the operating train roster assigned to
  each station. The pages retain the line-level depot fact and mark individual
  assignment TBC. Checked 14 August 2026.
- **Remaining platform and track counts** — The checked primary pages establish
  only the forms recorded above. They do not publish a complete platform or
  track-count table for all 26 stations. Unpublished values remain TBC. Checked
  14 August 2026.
- **Complete public-art metadata** — The checked DORTS public-art pages establish
  O01 and a partially described O12 set, but not a complete title, artist, year
  and medium record for the other stations. Their rows remain TBC; this is not
  an assertion that no work exists. Checked 14 August 2026.

## Stated gaps

The pages still cannot state most rename histories, fare-boundary transfer
classification, individual train-roster depot assignment, or platform and
track counts where the primary pages do not publish them. City gazettes,
operator rename announcements, a station equipment inventory and a depot
roster would settle those gaps.
