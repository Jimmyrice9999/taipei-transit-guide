# Ankeng LRT stations (K01–K09)

## Audit before editing

The nine generated pages all had TDX district and coordinates. K09 had a TDX
postal address; K01–K08 had no TDX address. The Part 1 operator-position
overlay already supplied NTMC station-position descriptions for all nine gaps,
but did not turn a position description into a postal address. TDX carried no
current interchange code; NTMC's full transfer and FAQ pages establish K09 as
the transfer with the Circular Line.

The nine NTMC detail pages, NTMC's full station table, FAQ, accessibility and
transfer pages, DORTS's full route and station-position pages, the K02 and K08
public-art records, the K09 public-art article, the An-Xin Bridge article, the
Shuang'an Tunnel article and the Ankeng Depot article were fetched and read.
The nine station pages expose identity and timetable records and link to
station-information documents; those linked documents and images were not
fetched under this run's no-image contract. The readable HTML therefore does
not establish station-specific numbered exits or track tables for this pass.

### Opening and structure

The DORTS Ankeng route page is PRIMARY, High confidence. It says:

> 「安坑線 112年2月10日 通車營運」

It gives 7.5 km and nine stations, and publishes the construction and
pre-opening milestones. This establishes 10 February 2023 for K01–K09 and
that they opened with the line.

The route introduction says the route runs along Anyi Road through K01–K05,
then turns to the Anhe Road branch and runs north in elevated form from K06:

> 「沿著安一路北行……於喜洋洋與甜蜜蜜社區附近設K1站，玫瑰路口設K2站，僑信路口設K3站，車子路口設K4站、安忠路口東側設K5站，續沿安一路安和支線轉至安和路，並以高架方式沿安和路北行，於安和路與安康路口設K6站」

This establishes the page's at-grade set K01, K03–K05 and elevated set K06–K09.
K02 is an independently documented exception within the Anyi section: the
DORTS public-art record says:

> 「玫瑰中國城站位於……安一路高架橋上」

K02 is therefore recorded as elevated. Platform form and track count remain
TBC for K01–K08. DORTS's full operating-pattern article says:

> 「特別注意2側月台皆可搭乘」

K09 is recorded with two side platforms usable for boarding; its track count
remains TBC. The operator's FAQ separately establishes that light rail uses
open platforms and card readers rather than closed gates:

> 「沒有封閉式的閘門，僅設置刷卡機」

### Exits and facilities

No readable full HTML primary source in this pass gives a numbered exit count
or street-emergence list for an Ankeng station. Exit count, exit positions
and station-specific accessible entry points remain TBC for all nine pages.

The NTMC accessibility page and FAQ are PRIMARY, High confidence for the
following operator-wide or explicitly station-specific facts:

- Every light-rail station has a passenger enquiry point; NTMC says the point
  has wheelchairs available for boarding assistance.
- Every light-rail station's automatic ticket machine is designed for
  accessibility.
- Elevated stations have accessible lifts; at-grade stations have accessible
  ramps. K01/K03–K05 are therefore recorded with the at-grade ramp provision,
  and K02/K06–K09 with the elevated lift provision. The number and position of
  each station's lifts or ramps are TBC.
- The FAQ says: 「淡海輕軌於紅樹林(V01站)與淡水漁人碼頭(V26站)；安坑輕軌於十四張(K09站)，有提供男女廁及無障礙廁所。其餘車站為簡約式設計，未設計廁所供旅客使用。」 K09 is recorded with public male/female and accessible toilets; K01–K08 are recorded with no toilet.
- The accessibility page places a nursing room at K09: 「環狀線Y06大坪林站、Y07十四張站(安坑輕軌K09站)……皆設有哺集乳室」. The other stations' nursing-room status is TBC.
- Bike parking is TBC. The checked FAQ publishes rules for bringing a bicycle
  on the light rail, not station parking facilities.

### Interchange

The NTMC transfer page is PRIMARY, High confidence. It says:

> 「安坑輕軌十四張站與新北環狀線共構，旅客可於輕軌十四張站相互轉乘其他運輸系統。」

K09 is recorded as an interchange with the Circular Line at Shisizhang. The
source establishes co-construction and transfer availability; whether the
passenger movement is paid-area or street transfer is TBC. No other current
interchange is established by the checked primary material.

### Depot and engineering history

The DORTS depot article is PRIMARY, High confidence. It publishes an Ankeng
Depot with a nine-track stabling yard, a five-track maintenance shop and an
administration building for system control, monitoring, dispatch, training
and administration. The station-level operating roster is TBC, so every page
names Ankeng Depot without assigning individual trains or shifts.

The DORTS Shuang'an Tunnel article is PRIMARY, High confidence for K01's
depot-side engineering context. It places the three-bore tunnel between K01
and Ankeng Depot and describes the light-rail track crossover inside it. The
tunnel is on the depot lead, not between two passenger stations, so K01 is not
classified as underground.

The DORTS An-Xin Bridge article is PRIMARY, High confidence for K09's
engineering history. It publishes a 502 m three-span continuous asymmetric
cable-stayed bridge, a 130 m pylon and a 400 m-wide river crossing connecting
Xindian and Ankeng. No station-specific construction history beyond the
route-level civil works was found for K02–K08.

The DORTS K09 article is PRIMARY, High confidence. It identifies the platform-

a perforated metal plate. Artist and artwork year are TBC. No station-specific
public-art record was established for K01, K03, K04, K05, K06 or K07, so their
public-art fields remain TBC.

## Conflicts

1. NTMC's current station list and DORTS's route introduction describe K09 as
   beside Circular Line Y8. DORTS's full station-position table instead says
   「環狀線Y7站旁」 and 「可轉乘捷運環狀線Y7站」. Both primary values are
   retained. The generated page uses the current NTMC/Y8 description and the
   interchange label is simply Circular Line.
2. DORTS describes the passenger route as an at-grade/elevated mixed system,
   while its separate Shuang'an Tunnel article places a tunnel between K01 and
   the depot. The tunnel article locates it outside the K01–K09 passenger
   station sequence; K01 is therefore retained as at-grade, and the tunnel is
   published as depot-lead engineering history. The secondary 0.4 km
   underground breakdown in the existing line research is not used as a
   station classification here.
3. DORTS's K02 public-art record directly calls K02 an Anyi Road elevated
   station, while the route prose describes the Anyi Road section before the
   K06 elevated transition. Both statements are retained: K02 is the
   separately documented elevated station, and K01/K03–K05 remain at-grade.
