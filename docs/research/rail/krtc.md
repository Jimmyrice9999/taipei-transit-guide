# Kaohsiung Metro and Circular Light Rail — research report

> Corpus warning: files under docs/research/ are leads and working notes, not sources. Every published claim below was checked against the linked primary page or data response.

## Conflicts

- TDX’s direction-zero Route records give 29.72 km for Red and 13.12 km for Orange. The government Red/Orange project page gives 28.3 km and 14.4 km. These values are all published: TDX is a current structured directional route field, while the government values are the completed-project description. The sources do not, in the checked pages, define enough measurement detail to reconcile them, so the content pages keep both values rather than averaging or selecting one. [tdx-krtc-route](https://tdx.transportdata.tw/api/basic/v2/Rail/Metro/Route/KRTC?%24format=JSON&%24top=1000&%24skip=0) [mtbu-red-orange](https://mtbu.kcg.gov.tw/KaohsiungMetro/CompletedRoutes/RedandOrange/RedandOrange01.html)
- The current TDX Metro pull contains 39 metro station records and no Circular Light Rail station records in the committed Metro response; the operator/government pages independently establish a 38-station LRT list. This is a dataset-boundary difference, not evidence that the LRT does not exist. The site publishes the two layers separately. [tdx-krtc-station](https://tdx.transportdata.tw/api/basic/v2/Rail/Metro/Station/KRTC?%24format=JSON&%24top=1000&%24skip=0) [krtc-lrt-guide](https://www.krtc.com.tw/KLRT/timetable) [mtbu-lrt](https://mtbu.kcg.gov.tw/KaohsiungMetro/CompletedRoutes/LightRail/LightRail01.html)

## Checked and failed

- **TDX Metro LRT geometry and station pull** — no Circular Light Rail records were present in the committed KRTC Metro Station, Line, Route, Shape or StationOfRoute responses fetched on 2026-08-24. The reason is a source coverage boundary in the response; the unit does not turn that absence into a claim that no TDX light-rail endpoint exists. Operator and government pages were used for the LRT identity and station list instead.
- **Station-facility transcription** — checked on 2026-08-24. The operator station-guide page was fetched, but a field-by-field extraction of lifts, tactile paths, accessible toilets, nursing rooms and platform gaps was not completed in this unit. Those fields remain TBC rather than being inferred from the navigation page.
- **Ridership** — checked on 2026-08-24. The checked KRTC/TDX pull used for this unit did not include a station ridership table. Per-station and per-line ridership therefore remain TBC here.

## Stated gaps

- The current layer does not yet establish LRT coordinates or a primary structured LRT geometry. A verified TDX light-rail response or operator/government GIS dataset would settle this.
- Signalling, power, depot/workshop allocation, fleet, platform screen doors, design capacity, construction chronology and the contested Circular Light Rail construction history remain TBC. DORTS/MTBU technical publications, procurement records, government approvals and audit/investigation records are the source families to search.
- Station accessibility and ridership remain TBC. A station-by-station operator facilities export and MOTC/operator monthly station table would settle them.
