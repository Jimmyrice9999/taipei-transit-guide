# Taiwan Railways electrification and traction power

Research date: 27 August 2026 (Asia/Taipei).

This is a one-subject research record for TRA electrification chronology,
traction-power architecture, power dispatch and the boundary between dated
network aggregates and a current asset register. The operator history is not
treated as a current line-by-line inventory, and the technical journal paper is
not treated as a binding current standard.

## What is established

### Electrification began in ROC 63 and the Western Main Line was complete in ROC 68

**Claim — Confirmed:** The operator's achievement page dates the beginning of
TRA electrification to ROC 63 and the full Western Main Line electrification to
26 June in ROC 68.

- **Source title:** *Railway electrification chronology and achievements*
- **Original title:** 臺鐵電氣化沿革及歷年建設
- **Publisher:** 國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司／臺灣鐵路管理局 / National Taiwan Railways Corporation and former Taiwan Railway Administration
- **URL:** <https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/adr/about-achievement-1?selectIndex=0>
- **Archive snapshot:** not captured
- **Kind:** primary
- **Confidence:** High — official operator achievement page, full page read on 27 August 2026
- **Original-language sentences:**
  > 民國63年開始推動臺灣鐵路電氣化，民國68年6月26日西部幹線全線實施電氣化。

The same page divides the Western Main Line work into three phases. Phase 1 is
Keelung–Zhunan at 125.7 km and opened on 24 February in ROC 67; phase 2 is
Zhunan–Changhua, with 91.2 km on the Coast Line and 89.3 km on the Mountain

the operator says was complete in June of ROC 68, two months early.

  > 第三期：彰化至高雄，68年6月全部完工，較原預定日期提早2個月。

### The technical study describes a 69/161 kV to 25 kV overhead supply chain

**Claim — Confirmed with scope limitation:** A 2014 Taiwan Railway Journal paper
describes the ordinary-speed railway catenary model as high-voltage three-phase
60 Hz supply entering railway substations and being transformed to 25 kV
single-phase 60 Hz overhead supply. The paper is primary evidence for what its
authors documented in that technical study, not a current signed asset register.

- **Source title:** *A Study on Reliability of Ordinary-Speed Railway Overhead Catenary System*
- **Original title:** 傳統鐵路電車線系統可靠度之研究
- **Publisher:** 臺灣鐵路管理局 / Taiwan Railway Administration
- **URL:** <https://tip.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/53dc2c5e-fdfa-49c4-b069-ddb662585738>
- **Archive snapshot:** not captured
- **Kind:** primary
- **Confidence:** Medium — official operator journal paper read in full, but the journal states that its views are not necessarily the Administration's
- **Original-language sentences:**
  > 其電力供應係由台電一次變電所…69kV、161kV…三相60Hz…降壓為25kV兩單相…60Hz…送至電車線…

The paper identifies the five parts as the transmission, substation, 25 kV

## Scope and measurement boundaries

The ROC dates in the achievement page describe construction, energisation,
testing or opening milestones, and those event types are not interchangeable.
The 2023 report's route totals are a dated statistical aggregate. The journal
paper describes a technical system model and explicitly carries a disclaimer
that its views are not necessarily those of the Administration. None of these
layers is a substitute for a current as-built electrical register.

The branch project figures also have different scopes. Shalun's 6.52 km,
Liujia's 11 km electrified branch description and the report's 90.5 km branch
aggregate are not added together: the source pages do not establish that they
measure the same network boundary or exclude shared approaches in the same way.

## Conflicts

- The 2023 report gives 997.7 km electrified and 67.3 km non-electrified out of
  1,065 km, while the achievement page supplies historical project and
  energisation milestones rather than a matching current route table. This is a
  date and measurement-layer boundary, not a reconciled line-by-line conflict.
- The technical paper describes an ordinary-speed catenary model, while the
  operator's achievement page describes the central dispatch scope. The two
  sources overlap on 25 kV catenary and do not publish competing traction
  voltage values; the study disclaimer means the paper is not promoted as a
  current operator standard.
- The report's 90.5 km branch aggregate and the achievement page's individual
  Shalun and Liujia project figures are retained separately. The checked sources
  do not define a common denominator for summing them.

## Stated gaps

A current as-built or maintenance record is needed to settle the electrification
status of each route, the neutral sections and switching boundaries, the active
substation and feeder inventory, and the dates and scope of renewals. Until such
a primary record is read in full, those fields stay TBC rather than being
inferred from the 2023 aggregate or the journal's system model.
