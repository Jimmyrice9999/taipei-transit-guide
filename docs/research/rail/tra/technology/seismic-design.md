# Taiwan Railways seismic design and earthquake response — research findings, 27 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below records whether the underlying page was fetched and read in full, or only found via a search summary; the latter is not published as an established fact on the content page. No prior `docs/research/` file on this subject existed to check against.

This is a one-subject report on TRA's seismic engineering standards and its
response to major earthquakes, kept separate from the Taipei Metro seismic
page already published on this project — TRA is a national conventional
railway across very different terrain and structures.

## What is established

- **MOTC issues a national Railway Bridge Seismic Design Code (鐵路橋梁耐震設計規範), first promulgated 15 July 1999, revised 7 April 2000, revised again 9 January 2007, and currently the 30 November 2021 edition.** Source: Ministry of Transportation and Communications, regulation index page for the code, `https://www.motc.gov.tw/ch/app/divpubreg_list/view?module=divpubreg&id=740&serno=435`, PRIMARY, High confidence — full page fetched, giving explicit order numbers and dates. Original-language basis: `交通部始於民國88年頒布本規範，期間分別於89年及96年進行修訂`.
- **The 2021 revision followed a 2017–2021 review the Railway Bureau ran at MOTC's instruction, bringing performance-based design concepts into the code; the code applies to general railway bridges with spans of 150 m or less, with special structures (suspension, cable-stayed, movable, military bridges) and longer spans requiring separate treatment.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence.
- **The code's static-analysis chapter defines three horizontal seismic design force levels — Level I, II and III — by site ground classification, plus vertical seismic force and lateral force distribution.** Same MOTC page, PRIMARY, Medium-High confidence: the three-level structure is corroborated on the MOTC page itself, but the specific numeric return-period/PGA thresholds for each level could not be confirmed — the actual code PDF (both MOTC's own copy and a civil-engineers'-association mirror) returned as an undecodable compressed binary. **The commonly assumed 30/475/2,500-year return-period pattern (used in Taiwan's building and highway-bridge codes) is NOT published here as an established fact for the railway code** — it appeared only in a search-tool auto-summary, not a fetched page, and is recorded as unconfirmed.
- **A DORTS (Taipei Metro) engineering ebook's bibliography cites the code with a "December 2006" edition date, one month earlier than MOTC's own 9 January 2007 date for the same revision.** Source: DORTS 精進版叢書, *捷運高架橋梁結構設計與施工*, reference-list page, `https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no32/files/basic-html/page297.html`, PRIMARY (DORTS's own publication), fetched in full, but only as a bare bibliography entry with no numeric content of its own. Both dates are published rather than one being chosen.
- **TRA applies a graduated post-earthquake speed-restriction rule tied to measured seismic intensity: at intensity 4, the first train runs at 30 km/h and subsequent trains at 60 km/h to the next station pending an inspection; at intensity 3, the first train runs at 60 km/h to the next station and, absent an abnormality, later trains resume normal speed.** This figure appears consistently, attributed to "台鐵規定," across multiple independently fetched news reports of separate 2026 earthquake events. Source example: Liberty Times, `台灣東部海域地震規模6.1 台鐵部分列車減速行駛`, `https://news.ltn.com.tw/news/life/breakingnews/5422586`, SECONDARY, Medium confidence — the rule's existence and application is corroborated by news coverage of real events (e.g., `東澳至和平間及台北至鶯歌間（含樹林調車場）按4級規定辦理`), but **the specific km/h figures were not traced to one single fetched primary sentence**, and the rule does **not** appear in the national 鐵路行車規則 regulation (see below) — its written source is likely an internal TRA operating manual not published online.
- **The national 鐵路行車規則 (Railway Operating Rules) regulation, fetched in full, does not contain the intensity-graduated speed rule above; it only requires (Article 14, explicitly scoped to high-speed rail) that operators install earthquake/typhoon/heavy-rain detection facilities.** Source: 全國法規資料庫, `https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=K0030005`, PRIMARY, High confidence for what it does and does not contain. Original-language basis: `鐵路機構應評估正線可能發生之潛在危險...其於高速鐵路至少應包括下列項目：一、地震、強風、豪雨等天然災害之偵測設施。`
- **TRA operates an AI-based slope rockfall-warning system (邊坡落石告警系統), reportedly developed after two September 2022 Pingxi Line rockfall strikes, first installed on the North Link Line in late 2022 and completed across 26 designated slope sections before the 2023 rainy season; it reportedly helped a Taroko Express train avoid a rockfall zone during the 3 April 2024 earthquake.** Source: CNA, `台鐵告警系統立功 助太魯閣號列車躲過落石`, `https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202404040160.aspx`, SECONDARY, Medium confidence — detailed and internally consistent, but not corroborated against a TRA primary technical page, and this is a general rockfall-detection system rather than an earthquake-specific one.
- **No primary source confirms TRA (as distinct from THSR) operates an automatic, CWA-triggered seismic early-warning train-stop system.** The Central Weather Administration's own FAQ page, fetched in full, names automatic control of transport infrastructure during earthquakes only as a forward-looking capability, without naming TRA or THSR as current implementers. Source: CWA Seismological Center, `地震百問-86`, `https://scweb.cwa.gov.tw/zh-tw/guidance/faqdetail/100`, PRIMARY, High confidence for what it does and does not say. Original-language basis: `重要設備的臨震自動控制，例如交通建設減速或停止`.
- **CWA and TRA signed an MOU on 30 September 2025 to strengthen railway disaster-prevention resilience, covering weather-data sharing and integrating railway-corridor meteorological/seismic observation information, explicitly citing the 0403 earthquake's aftermath as motivation.** Source: CNA, `氣象署攜手台鐵 客製化情資強化鐵道防災韌性`, `https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202509300209.aspx`, SECONDARY, Medium confidence — not independently corroborated by a CWA or TRA primary release.

### The 3 April 2024 (0403) Hualien earthquake

- **The earthquake struck 3 April 2024 at approximately 07:58, epicentre offshore Shoufeng, Hualien.** Multiple sources fetched in full agree on date and approximate location; the magnitude is a conflict (see Conflicts).
- **TRA suspended service Yilan–Fenglin the same day; damage included rockfall and rail buckling (軌道挫屈) at multiple points, including near K36+900 between Heren and Hualien and between Hualien and Fenglin.** Source: PTT repost of TRA's official press release, `https://www.ptt.cc/bbs/Railway/M.1712120009.A.FD0.html`, SECONDARY (a forum repost of a primary release; TRA's own news-detail pages could not be fetched — see Checked and failed), Medium confidence.
- **TRA targeted single-track restoration by the first train on 4 April and double-track restoration by noon 4 April; added 10 extra Yilan–Hualien inter-city express trains; substituted road shuttle buses Fenglin–Hualien; temporarily suspended long-distance through trains; permitted standing tickets on South Link Line services; and waived cancellation/refund fees for unused Hualien/Taitung-endpoint tickets purchased 3–4 April, valid one year.** Source: CNA, `台鐵宜蘭至鳳林4日中午前全恢復 增10列次區間快車`, `https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202404030454.aspx`, SECONDARY, Medium-High confidence — explicitly relays TRA's press release and is corroborated by the independent PTT repost of the same release. Original-language basis: `西正線可望今天深夜12時搶通，明天班車起恢復單線運轉，各班次正常，預計明天中午12時前恢復雙線行車。`

### Ongoing North Link Line instability and TRA's response, 2024–2026

- **After the initial restoration, the North Link Line's Heren–Chongde section suffered repeated further closures: 21 June 2024 (mudslide, derailing a train), 4 July 2024 (boulder collapse severing rails), and during Typhoon Gaemi in late July 2024 (extensive debris flows).** Source: CNA, `北迴線花蓮強震後變脆弱 台鐵啟動改線評估`, `https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202407280065.aspx`, SECONDARY, High confidence — detailed, dated wire-service account.
- **TRA's chairman said the remaining unstable material on the mountainside could not be estimated, and TRA initiated a realignment (改線) feasibility study while prioritising enhanced monitoring in the near term.** Same source, SECONDARY, High confidence for the quote as reported (not independently verified against a TRA primary statement).
- **TRA proposed three realignment options for Chongde–Heren (a mountain-side tunnel, optimising the existing route, or a coastal-side reroute); MOTC's preliminary review returned the proposal for further engineering-feasibility assessment; a cited geologist flagged debris-flow risk zones and high lateral rock pressure as obstacles to the tunnel option.** Source: Public Television Service (公視新聞網), `0403震後路線優化 台鐵北迴改線評估方案遭退回`, `https://news.pts.org.tw/article/745359`, SECONDARY, High confidence — named officials and an academic expert, though quotes are as reported by PTS rather than independently confirmed against a MOTC/TRA primary statement.
- **Pending realignment, TRA introduced an interim NT$7.799 billion "Yilan-Hualien-Taitung and South Link Railway Facility Safety Improvement Plan," covering vulnerable sections at several track-km markers with slope stabilisation, cut-and-cover tunnels and box culverts; the Executive Yuan's National Development Council approved the plan in principle, with construction reportedly targeted to start in 2026 and completion by 2031.** Source: United Daily News (聯合報/udn.com), `北迴線改線無下文 台鐵再推宜花東及南迴線改善計畫、最快今年啟用`, `https://udn.com/news/story/7266/9320969`, SECONDARY, Medium-High confidence — named quote and specific figures, not independently corroborated against a TRA/MOTC primary budget document.

## Conflicts

- **0403 earthquake magnitude:** M7.2, per two independently fetched CNA articles, versus M7.1, per a search-tool summary citing zh.wikipedia's event article (the figure was not independently confirmed by reading that page directly). Not resolved; CWA's own bulletin would settle this and was not fetched in this round.
- **Railway Bridge Seismic Design Code second-revision date:** 9 January 2007 per MOTC's own regulation-index page, versus "December 2006" per a DORTS bibliography citation. Both published; not resolved.
- **North Link Line debris-flow interruption count after 0403:** three dated events (21 June, 4 July, Typhoon Gaemi late July 2024) per a fully fetched CNA article, versus a fourth event (1 July 2024) that appeared only in a search summary and was not independently confirmed by a fetched page. The three-event account is treated as the more reliable basis; the fourth is noted as unconfirmed, not published as established.
