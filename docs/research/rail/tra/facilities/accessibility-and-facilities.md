# Taiwan Railways station accessibility and facilities — research report

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below was checked against the linked primary page or PDF.

**Checked:** 27 August 2026. **Subject:** TRA accessibility programme and station-specific facility records.

## What is established

### Accessibility facilities and service improvement plan

- **English title:** TRA accessibility facilities and service improvement plan
- **Original title:** `國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司無障礙設施及服務改善計畫`
- **Publisher:** National Taiwan Railways Corporation (`國營臺灣鐵路股份有限公司`)
- **URL:** https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/9988694f-a7ef-47a7-bea3-9f29bc0b835c
- **Class:** PRIMARY
- **Confidence:** High for the plan's stated programme, targets and service-design text; low for present-day completion because the document is a plan, not a current completion audit.
- **Original-language basis:** `部分車站因興建年代久遠，無障礙設施不符現代化需求。由於改善經費龐大，且考量既有環境條件之限制、施工難度等，在硬體上之改善須分年辦理。`
- **Original-language basis:** `以本公司進出站旅客數排名前 90 名之車站為對象優先建置，另配合各項鐵路建設計畫，預計 110 年以前完成計 184 站電梯建置，涵蓋本局服務旅客總數約 98.5%。`
- **Original-language basis:** `第 1 階段：月台提高至 92-96 公分。` / `第 2 階段：透過新購入列車及改造車廂，消除車廂台階。` / `第 3 階段：將全線車站之月台提高至 115 公分。`
- **Original-language basis:** `修訂「身心障礙旅客乘車服務作業要點」...內容詳列訂位、旅客到站、搭乘及出站等流程之服務要點以及各障別服務技巧。`

The full two-page PDF was opened. It establishes an accessibility-improvement programme, a historic elevator target, a three-stage platform and rolling-stock plan, and staff/service-procedure work. It does not provide a current station-by-station completion register.

Their different facility lists show the field-level boundary: the operator publishes station detail, but the opened records do not provide a unified completion or outage register.

## Conflicts

No direct numeric conflict was found among the opened primary records. The plan's 184-station and 98.5% figures are historic targets, while the station pages are current facility listings; they are different evidence scopes and are not substituted for one another. The four station pages also differ in facility type and location detail, but that is variation in the records rather than a contradiction.

## Checked and failed

- **Current network-wide accessibility completion percentage** — checked 27 August 2026. The full plan and four full station pages were opened, but they do not provide a current denominator-and-numerator register; no percentage was inferred from the historic target.
- **Station-by-station equipment and outage export** — checked 27 August 2026. The operator pages list facilities and some notices, but the opened material does not expose a unified machine-readable maintenance/status table.
- **Continuous accessible route and platform-gap register** — checked 27 August 2026. The opened plan and station pages do not publish a joined entrance-to-platform path graph or platform-to-vehicle gap measurement.

## Stated gaps

- A current operator export with station and facility identifiers, working status and maintenance timestamps would settle network coverage and outage questions.
- Station plans joined to platform and entrance identifiers would settle continuous step-free paths, tactile continuity, accessible toilets and platform gaps.
- A dated completion report would establish whether the plan's historic ROC 110 and ROC 111 targets were met, revised or superseded.
