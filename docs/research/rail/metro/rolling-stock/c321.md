**Checked:** 23 August 2026. **Subject:** Taipei Metro 321-type high-capacity electric multiple unit.

## What is established

### Procurement, fleet and manufacture

- C321 was manufactured by Siemens and comprised 72 three-car units, paired as 36 trains, for 216 cars. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Tracking the advancement of Taipei MRT E&M systems — transformation, innovation and growth in E&M planning and design”](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page138.html), `臺北捷運機電系統精進軌跡尋蹤—機電規設之蛻變、創新與成長`, *Rapid Transit Systems & Technology* no. 48, Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems (臺北市政府捷運工程局). Original: `321型是由廠商德國西門子製造的列車，共有72組（36列），合計216輛`.
- The three procurement lots were CH321 for 11 six-car trains / 66 cars, CN331 for 17 / 102, and CC361 for eight / 48. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“How many trains were procured for each route in the Taipei metropolitan MRT network?”](https://www.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=EEC70A4186D4C828&s=C8602F8588914E91&sms=87415A8B9CE81B16), `機電設計>台北都會區大眾捷運系統路網中，各路線所採購之列車數為何？`. Original: `新店線(CH321標)共採購11列六車列車(66輛)。` / `南港線(CN331標)共採購17列六車列車(102輛)。` / `中和線(CC361標)共採購8列六車列車(48輛)。`
- DORTS's 2013 article described the fleet as the principal type on the Xindian, Nangang, Banqiao and Zhonghe lines. **PRIMARY, High for that dated historical allocation; Low for any current-allocation inference:** no. 48. Original: `是新店、南港線、板橋線、中和線目前的主要車種`.
- China Steel assembled 108 C321 cars from semi-knocked-down kits at Beitou Depot under the industrial-cooperation programme. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“High-capacity EMU carbody materials and structural design”](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no35/files/basic-html/page26.html), `高運量電聯車車體材質及其結構設計`, *Rapid Transit Systems & Technology* no. 35. Original: `新店/南港/中和線電聯車同樣以「半散件」方式，由中鋼公司於捷運北投機廠完成108輛電聯車組裝工作。`
- DORTS states a 30-year high-capacity carbody design life and identifies structural calculations, prototype load tests and water-tightness testing as parts of assurance. **PRIMARY, High for the general design regime; not C321-specific dimensions:** same no. 35 carbody article. Original: `臺北捷運電聯車車體結構有30年的設計壽命。因此從車體材料的選用、車體結構主要組件的設計、結構強度受力計算、原型車之負荷測試，都是為確保電聯車在使用年限內的安全性。`

### Published specification

- Maximum operating speed was 80 km/h; published train capacity 2,200 people; gauge 1,435 mm; supply 750 V DC third rail; traction control VVVF-GTO; braking regenerative and disc-friction blended with spring parking; and ATC included ATO, ATP and ATS. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS no. 48. Original: `列車營運最高速度為80(km/h)…載客量2,200人，軌距1,435 mm，供電方式為直流750V（第三軌供電），控制裝置為VVVF-GTO Inverter，制動方式為再生電力、碟式摩擦混合式煞車，彈簧駐車，安全防護系統有ATC(ATO、ATP、ATS)等`.
- Compared with C301, DORTS identifies squarer body corners and square rather than circular door indicators. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS no. 48. Original: `車輛性能、外觀造型及內裝設備與301型電聯車類似，但車身稜角較為方正，開門指示燈由301型的圓形改為方形。`

### 15 September 2003 derailment and remedy

- Cars 155 and 156 derailed on 15 September 2003, after which DORTS convened domestic and overseas experts and investigated with Siemens. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Investigation of the 0915 derailment of a 321-type EMU”](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no35/files/basic-html/page27.html), `捷運321型電聯車0915脫軌事件之探討`, *Rapid Transit Systems & Technology* no. 35. Original: `台北捷運三二一型西門孖公司製造之電聯車155及156號車，於九十二年九月十五日發生脫軌事故後…積極進行事故原因之調查分析及改善措施`.
- AIDC inferred bolt-first then upper-lug failure; Siemens inferred upper-lug-first then bolt failure. DORTS considered Siemens's order more plausible from the sequence and locations at which fallen components were recovered. **PRIMARY, High that both analyses and DORTS's assessment are published:** [article page 29](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no35/files/basic-html/page29.html). Original: `依漢翔公司之檢驗結果，推論0915事故為螺栓先斷裂，進而造成齒輪箱扭力臂上部托耳斷裂；另西門孖公司所提報告，則推論0915事件為齒輪箱扭力臂上部托耳先斷裂，進而造成螺栓斷裂` and `故可推斷西門孖公司之推論應是較合理`.
- Siemens used a temporary steel cable to restrain the gearbox, and all fleet torque-arm retaining bolts were replaced by 30 October 2003. **PRIMARY, High:** page 29. Original: `西門子公司以臨時性鋼索固定齒輪箱…並於92.10.30完成全車隊齒輪箱扭力臂固定螺栓之更換作業`.
- The near-term reinforcement was installed across 36 trains from 5 December 2003 to 15 January 2004; a further missing-bolt and broken-lug event happened on 10 February 2004. **PRIMARY, High:** page 29. Original: `本改善方案自92.12.05展開至93.01.15完成全車隊36列車之安裝作業` and `93.02.10再發生一起螺栓遺失及托耳斷裂情事`.
- The final remedy enlarged the upper bush bar from 22 to 26 mm, added a 25 mm bracket reinforcement plate and redesigned the emergency retainer. DORTS states calculated safety factors of 2.1 and 2.016 times the original for the respective structures. **PRIMARY, High:** [article page 31](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no35/files/basic-html/page31.html). Original: `扭力臂上下托耳（Bush Bar）厚度 22 mm / 26 mm` and `增加一片加強板（厚度為25 mm）`; `安全係數可達到原設計之2.1` / `安全係數可達原設計之2.016`.
- The final work was completed across all 36 C321 trains. **PRIMARY, High:** [article page 32](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no35/files/basic-html/page32.html). Original: `新店/南港/中和線（即321型）共計三十六列電聯車之換裝工作，亦已完成改善作業。`
- DORTS measured wheel–rail vibration peaks of 30 g near Ximen against 20 g in the original consultant specification and revised shock/vibration requirements for later rolling stock. **PRIMARY, High:** page 32. Original: `板南線西門站附近路段之輪軌振動實測值有高達30g之峰值，高於當初美國總顧問在電聯車技術規範中所訂之20g` and `未來之新莊、蘆洲、信義及松山等路線電聯車設計將以本次經驗為基礎`.

### Renewal

- In 2015 TRTC completed overhauls on two C321 trains and was renewing onboard communications. **PRIMARY, High:** TRTC, [2015 corporate social responsibility report](https://web.metro.taipei/ebook/2015ebook/files/basic-html/page55.html), `2015臺北捷運企業社會責任報告書`. Original: `完成…321型2列…電聯車…大修作業` and `辦理…321型電聯車車載通訊設備重置`.
- The communications programme addressed rising fault rates, obsolete parts and passenger-information equipment. The Audit Office found work more than 30% behind at one point; TRTC reported completion on all 36 trains on 18 December 2015, 34 days late. **PRIMARY, High:** National Audit Office (審計部), [transportation audit chapter](https://auditreport.audit.gov.tw/ServerFile/Get/6379270128874995755bce8920a08e4970aa52b4f88fe32edb), `捷運公司辦理321型電聯車控制器採購施工進度嚴重落後，且計算違約金欠缺契約執行依據`. Original: `車載通訊（含列車旅客資訊系統）故障率逐年提高` / `承商於履約期間施作進度落後逾30％` / `民國104年12月18日將全部36列車安裝完成，最終僅逾期34日`.
- The original 2021–2026 propulsion-reset plan covered all 36 C321 and six C341 trains, budgeted NT$2.77798 billion, and responded to Siemens circuit-board discontinuation and limited repair support. **PRIMARY, High for the original plan, not proof of completion:** TRTC, [new continuing project statement](https://www-ws.gov.taipei/Download.ashx?icon=..pdf&n=6auY6YGL6YePMzIxLjM0MeWei%2Bmbu%2BiBr%2Bi7iuaOqOmAsuezu%2Be1semHjee9ri5wZGY%3D&u=LzAwMS9VcGxvYWQvMzg4L3JlbGZpbGUvMTc2MTkvMTM5MzA0L2Y2OTFkMjZiLTE2NTktNDRjZC04YTYzLTQ0MjliMTU4NGJhMC5wZGY%3D), `高運量321/341型電聯車推進系統重置(110-115年)`. Original: `原廠SIEMENS公司，除105年起陸續通知部份電路板停產` / `全案共計42列車推進系統` / `興辦時所需工程經費：27億7,798萬元`.
