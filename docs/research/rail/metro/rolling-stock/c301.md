## What is established

### Procurement and fleet

- The original Tamsui Line train contract was `CT301`, began on 20 December 1989 and had a stated value of NT$4.5 billion. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS engineers' retrospective, [“The First Generation Electric Multiple Units of Taipei Heavy Rail Rapid Transit System”](https://data.taipei/api/dataset/3812b03c-7e72-40c7-85cf-c67df9162f7e/resource/9b9c9bf1-b6e4-43b7-9d18-f2818b5d1718/download), `臺北捷運第一代高運量電聯車`, Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems (臺北市政府捷運工程局), *Rapid Transit Systems & Technology* no. 37, August 2007. Original: `淡水線電聯車工程於七十八年十二月二十日正式開工，工程金額為新台幣四十五億元，本承包商為美國聯合機車集團(URC, Union Rail Car Partnership)`.
- DORTS's current Tamsui–Xinyi project page also lists the rolling-stock package as `電聯車(CT301)`. **PRIMARY, High:** [Tamsui–Xinyi Line](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=0AC527D5FC9E596A), `淡水信義線`, DORTS. Original: `機電系統工程包括電聯車(CT301)、號誌(C302)、供電(C303)`.
- The contract supplied 132 cars forming 22 six-car trains. **PRIMARY, High:** the no. 37 article's abstract states `淡水線高運量捷運系統共有 22 列（132 車廂）電聯車`; its specification section states that every train is a fixed six-car formation.
- The contractor is identified as Union Rail Car Partnership (URC). A later DORTS engineering article describes URC as Kawasaki Heavy Industries' US subsidiary. **PRIMARY, Medium:** the contractor name is explicit in the no. 37 article; the corporate relationship is DORTS's own description but is not supported here by a corporate filing. Original in [“Tracking the advancement of Taipei MRT E&M systems”](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page137.html), `臺北捷運機電系統精進軌跡尋蹤—機電規設之蛻變、創新與成長`, DORTS, *Rapid Transit Systems & Technology* no. 48: `由廠商日本川崎重工的美國子公司－美國鐵路聯合機車集團（URC）所製造`.

### Specification decisions

- The procurement's first tender failed because the initial all-motored six-car specification was too expensive; DORTS then changed it to 4M2T. **PRIMARY, High:** no. 37 article. Original: `剛開始時電聯車採用 6M 動力車，價錢太貴了，以致於第一次流標…之後修改規範改成 4M2T`.
- Each train comprises two three-car units, each formed DM1–T–M2, and runs as a fixed six-car train at peak and off-peak. **PRIMARY, High:** no. 37 article. Original: `由二組『3-CAR』車組所組成，每一車組包括一輛 DM1 車、一輛非動力車(T 車)及一輛 M2 車。不論尖峰或離峰時段均以六車固定編組方式營運`.
- The published maximum design load is 370 people per car: 60 seated and 310 standing. Maximum operating speed is 80 km/h. **PRIMARY, High:** no. 37 article. Original: `每車有六十個座位、三百一十個立位，最高載客量為三百七十人。營運速度最高為每小時八十公里`.
- DORTS selected AC induction traction after an evaluation against DC motors, and selected stainless steel over aluminium because of corrosion and welding concerns in Taiwan's conditions. **PRIMARY, High:** no. 37 article. Original: `採用 AC 動力推進系統，採用 4M2T` and `以臺灣的酸雨，鋁合金不見得耐候抗腐蝕…所以當時淡水線電聯車車體採用不銹鋼`.
- The original system used 750 V DC bottom-contact third-rail collection, regenerative and pneumatic friction braking, bolsterless two-axle bogies with conical-rubber primary and air-spring secondary suspension, pneumatic doors, ATP and ATO, and two independent split air-conditioning systems per car. **PRIMARY, High:** no. 37 article, specification list. Original runs from `供電系統採用『第三軌供電(750V 直流電源)』` through `列車採用自動保護(ATP)及自動列車操作(ATO)`.
- The body sides taper inward above the waist; the 1,500 mm exterior-sliding doors are pneumatic; the driver's end is FRP and has an anti-climber. **PRIMARY, High:** [no. 48 page 137](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page137.html) and [page 138](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page138.html). Original: `車門則採用堅固易維修的 1,500 毫米寬的外側滑動式車門，門機採用氣動方式驅動` and `駕駛車車端面板以 FRP 材質製造，並於面板下方設有防爬裝置`.

### Manufacture, delivery and testing

- Final design review finished on 31 August 1992, and the prototype-train factory ceremony was held in the United States during 13–24 September 1992. **PRIMARY, High:** no. 37 article. Original: `八十一年八月三十一日完成最後設計審查` and `八十一年九月十三日至二十四日原型車(Proto Train)在美國舉行出廠典禮`.
- Prototype cars EMU 01/02 reached Beitou Depot on 1 December 1992. Production cars EMU 03–06 arrived on 1 March 1993, EMU 07–16 on 1 May, and EMU 17–28 on 1 August; the full 132-car order had reached Beitou by November 1995. **PRIMARY, High:** no. 37 article. Original: `八十一年十二月一日原型電聯車 EMU 01/02 運抵北投機廠` followed by the three dated production deliveries and `八十四年十一月…一百三十二輛電聯車全部運抵北投機廠`.
- Prototype testing ran from December 1992 to May 1996 and included coasting, abnormal operation, wheel-slip/slide, heat rise, regenerative power, coupler and bogie clearance, functional, noise, vibration, stability, ride-comfort, communications-interface, control-simulation and electromagnetic-interference tests. **PRIMARY, High:** no. 37 article. Original: `原型車測試工作於八十一年十二月開始展開，於八十五年五月份完成所有原型車測試` followed by ET01–ET14.
- Passenger service began with the first Tamsui Line opening on 28 March 1997. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS [Tamsui–Xinyi Line](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=0AC527D5FC9E596A). Original: `於86年3月28日通車營運`.

### Renewal

- In 2012 TRTC completed a 22-train communications and passenger-information renewal, adding three-colour LED displays, wider viewing angles, destination, door-side and time information, and coloured destination frames on front and side displays. **PRIMARY, High:** [2012 Taipei Metro annual report](https://www-ws.gov.taipei/001/Upload/public/Attachment/312916442785.pdf), `2012臺北捷運公司年報`, Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation (臺北大眾捷運股份有限公司). Original: `高運量301型22列電聯車車載通訊設備重置，顯示器採3色LED及擴大視角設計，並加入目的地、開門位置提醒及現在時間`.
- TRTC's 2014 report records preventive work on important 301 traction circuit boards and door-guide improvements shared with the 371 and 381 fleets. **PRIMARY, High:** [2014 corporate social responsibility report, page 47](https://web.metro.taipei/ebook/2014ebook/files/basic-html/page47.html), `2014臺北捷運企業社會責任報告書`, TRTC. Original: `301型推進系統重要電路板預防檢修專案` and `301/371/381型電聯車車門導槽改善專案`.
- TRTC's 2015 report records 301 traction and air-conditioning resets; its 2016 report still describes traction reset work. **PRIMARY, High:** [2015 CSR, page 55](https://web.metro.taipei/ebook/2015ebook/files/basic-html/page55.html), `2015臺北捷運企業社會責任報告書`, TRTC. Original: `辦理 301 型電聯車推進系統重置、空調系統重置`. The checked 2016 full PDF was readable through the search index but its page fetch returned an internal error, so it is not used in the content page.
- A DORTS/TRTC engineering article says the propulsion inverter modules changed from GTO to IGBT to reduce energy use. **PRIMARY, High:** [“Taipei Metro's energy-saving and carbon-reduction measures,” page 48](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no50/files/basic-html/page48.html), `臺北捷運公司節能減碳作法`, *Rapid Transit Systems & Technology* no. 50. Original: `301 型列車推進換流器模組元件由可關斷晶閘管(GTO)改為絕緣柵雙極電晶體(IGBT)型式，降低設備耗能`.
