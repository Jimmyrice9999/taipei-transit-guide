## What is established

Original: `於96年3月3日決標，係由日商川崎重工業株式會社、丸紅株式會社與國內中鼎工程股份有限公司共同承攬，其中的電聯車工程(CR381、CG391子施工標)係由川崎重工業株式會社負責製造總計23列電聯車。`
- DORTS breaks the base order into 13 CR381 six-car trains / 78 cars and ten CG391 six-car trains / 60 cars; CD311A is a separate six-car add-on. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [full FAQ page](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/news_toggle.aspx?PageSize=20&n=2A66A485FACB0D5B&page=7&sms=87415A8B9CE81B16), `台北都會區大眾捷運系統路網中，各路線所採購之列車數為何？`. - DORTS required the Xinyi trains to run across all existing high-capacity routes and couple to C301/C321/C341/C371 for rescue. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Electromechanical-system innovations on the Xinyi Line,” p.133](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no51/files/basic-html/page133.html), `臺北捷運信義線的機電系統創新`. ## Conflicts

- **23 versus 24 trains:** DORTS's contract report says 23 because it covers CR381/CG391. The manufacturer record adds one later six-car 381-type train, giving 24 / 144 cars across both scopes. Both numbers are published with their measurements.
- **CG391 versus CR391:** the Taipei Open Data dataset title and its substantive abstract consistently say CG391, while the metadata `計畫名稱` row says `CR391`. DORTS's quantity table independently says CG391. The page treats CR391 as a catalogue-row discrepancy and publishes CG391 without hiding the conflicting row.
- No conflict was found for the 102-car Taiwan Rolling Stock share: 96 base cars plus six add-on cars in the same manufacturer record.
