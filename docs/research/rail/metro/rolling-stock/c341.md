## What is established

- CP341 comprised six six-car trains / 36 cars. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“How many trains were procured for each route?”](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=2A66A485FACB0D5B&s=C8602F8588914E91&sms=87415A8B9CE81B16), `機電設計>台北都會區大眾捷運系統路網中，各路線所採購之列車數為何？`. Original: `土城線(CP341標)共採購6列六車列車(36輛)。`
- Siemens manufactured the trains in 2003; DORTS received them from September to November 2004 as extra fleet for opening the Tucheng Line. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Tracking the advancement of Taipei MRT E&M systems”](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page138.html), `臺北捷運機電系統精進軌跡尋蹤—機電規設之蛻變、創新與成長`. Original: `341型亦由廠商德國西門子於92年製造，93年9月至11月陸續交付本局，為土城線的開通增備用車輛。共有12組（6列），合計36輛。`
- DORTS calls this its first EMU package designed without general-consultant assistance. **PRIMARY, High:** same source. Original: `土城線電聯車為本局在無總顧問協助下，首次自辦規設之標案`.
- C341 introduced IGBT propulsion/static-inverter control, revised motors and braking resistors, collector gear, bellows rubber and T8 lighting; it used a serial data network for propulsion, doors and brakes. **PRIMARY, High:** same source. Original: `推進換流器及靜態換流器…改採…IGBT控制` and `列車同步控制（推進、車門及煞車系統）透過串列式數據通訊網路控制及傳送`.
- All six trains received the final strengthened gearbox torque-arm remedy by 3 October 2005. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Investigation of the 0915 derailment”](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no35/files/basic-html/page31.html), `捷運321型電聯車0915脫軌事件之探討`. Original: `土城線341標共6列電聯車業於94.10.03全部換裝最終改善方案`.
- DORTS's rolling-stock manual groups all six with the 36 C321 trains on the Bannan Line and states interior fire-design requirements. **PRIMARY, High at publication date:** [*EMU Practice*, p.171](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no21/files/basic-html/page171.html), `捷運工程叢書精進版—21 捷運電聯車實務`. Original: `土城線所採購案之6列電聯車（皆由德商西門子公司製造）目前全部行駛於板南線`.
- A 2010 city committee approved a mediated Siemens–TRTC settlement over C341 propulsion, auxiliary power and 19 procurement items; terms are not disclosed. **PRIMARY, High:** [96th Procurement Appeal Review Committee minutes](https://www-ws.gov.taipei/Download.ashx?icon=..pdf&n=OTkwMzE3LeesrDk25qyh5aeU5ZOh5pyD6K2wLnBkZg%3D%3D&u=LzAwMS9VcGxvYWQvODI1L3JlbGZpbGUvNjExNjYvODkwNjEwMC9lMmMyNTA5NC1iYTMzLTQxNmMtOTRmNC00MDRjMjYyN2I1YTQucGRm), `臺北市政府採購申訴審議委員會第96次委員會議紀錄`. Original: `西門子股份有限公司與臺北大眾捷運股份有限公司間就「341型電聯車推進、輔助電力系統等19項採購」履約爭議調解案調解成立`.
- The joint C321/C341 propulsion reset covers 42 trains and originally budgeted NT$2.77798 billion. **PRIMARY, High for the plan:** [project statement](https://www-ws.gov.taipei/Download.ashx?icon=..pdf&n=6auY6YGL6YePMzIxLjM0MeWei%2Bmbu%2BiBr%2Bi7iuaOqOmAsuezu%2Be1semHjee9ri5wZGY%3D&u=LzAwMS9VcGxvYWQvMzg4L3JlbGZpbGUvMTc2MTkvMTM5MzA0L2Y2OTFkMjZiLTE2NTktNDRjZC04YTYzLTQ0MjliMTU4NGJhMC5wZGY%3D), `高運量321/341型電聯車推進系統重置`. Original: `全案共計42列車推進系統` / `興辦時所需工程經費：27億7,798萬元`.
