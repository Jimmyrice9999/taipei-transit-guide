## What is established

- Kawasaki won CK371 on 1 September 2003; DORTS groups CK371, CL601, CE631, CH321A and TAA001 in the project. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS / Taipei Open Data, [“CK371-group propulsion and auxiliary-power tests”](https://data.taipei/dataset/detail?id=da06f518-74ca-497a-8b5b-b5e2b51c0166), `CK371/CL601/CE631/CH321A/TAA001電聯車標推進系統及輔助電力系統測試`. Original: `新莊、蘆洲線及其他路網之CK371高運量電聯車標係於92年9月1日決標，由日商川崎重工株式會社得標。`
- The five official component quantities total 52 six-car plus three three-car trains / 321 cars. **PRIMARY, High for entries; arithmetic is this file's synthesis:** DORTS, [frequently asked questions](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/news_toggle.aspx?PageSize=20&n=2A66A485FACB0D5B&page=7&sms=87415A8B9CE81B16), `機電設計>台北都會區大眾捷運系統路網中，各路線所採購之列車數為何？`. Original entries: `新莊線(CK371標)共採購20列六車列車(120輛)`; `蘆洲線(CL601標)共採購4列六車列車(24輛)`; `南港線東延段(CE631標)共採購4列六車列車(24輛)`; `新店線小碧潭站(CH321A標)共採購3列三車列車(9輛)`; `臺北捷運公司(TAA001標)增購24列六車列車(144輛)`.
- DORTS assigned 162 cars to Taiwan Rolling Stock under the industrial-cooperation complete-build-up programme. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“High-capacity EMU carbody materials and structural design”](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no35/files/basic-html/page26.html), `高運量電聯車車體材質及其結構設計`. - The 162 domestic cars equal 27 six-car trains in DORTS's production account; Kawasaki transferred its manufacturing and quality process and stationed supervisors at the plant. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Introduction to the Xinzhuang/Luzhou EMU and domestic assembly process”](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no41/files/basic-html/page194.html), `新蘆線電聯車介紹及國內組裝之產製流程`. Original: `新蘆線及捷運公司共33列(198輛)由日本川崎重工業株式會社承攬製造，其中27列車亦透過ICP之機制以整體組裝…於台灣車輛公司進行車體焊接、組裝，內裝工作等階段。`
- Taiwan Rolling Stock identifies cars 401–418 and 431–466, 162 cars, as its work and January 2010 as completion. **PRIMARY, High:** manufacturer, [“Taipei Metro 371 type”](https://www.trsc.com.tw/train/tran-58ace453a7ad8), `臺北捷運371車型`. - C371 introduced train monitoring, CCTV, smoke detection, side LED displays, electric doors, IGBT control, bicycle/luggage racks and added evacuation/fire provisions. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [FAQ full page](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/news_toggle.aspx?PageSize=20&n=2A66A485FACB0D5B&page=7&sms=87415A8B9CE81B16), `台北捷運新購之高運量列車有那些設計特色？`. ## Conflicts

- No primary-source conflict was found for the 321-car family total, Kawasaki award or 162-car domestic-production quantity.
- The older corpus calls C371 “the first metro EMU manufactured in Taiwan.” The opened primaries support only narrower firsts: Taiwan Rolling Stock's first industrial-cooperation production model and its first complete-build-up programme. The broad national first is removed, not silently reworded as established.
- `53.5列車` in the Control Yuan account is a fleet-equivalent count: the DORTS procurement table expresses the underlying fleet more precisely as 52 six-car trains and three three-car trains. Both descriptions are published because they measure formation differently.
