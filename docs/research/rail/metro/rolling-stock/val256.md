## What is established

- DORTS selected Matra's V256 system through international competition in March 1988 and opened the Muzha Line on 28 March 1996. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems (臺北市政府捷運工程局), [full “Mr Pao-Cheng Chi and Taipei MRT artefacts” report](https://data.taipei/api/dataset/3812b03c-7e72-40c7-85cf-c67df9162f7e/resource/75f441a8-9cbc-4bf6-8b8b-e8cee765b369/download), `齊寶錚先生與臺北捷運文物`. Original: `於民國77年3月以嚴謹的國際競標程序選定法國馬特拉V256系統` / `臺北捷運木柵線在民國85年3月28日通車`.
- Contract CC350 procured 51 pairs / 102 cars. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [full train-quantity FAQ](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=2A66A485FACB0D5B&s=C8602F8588914E91), `機電設計－台北都會區大眾捷運系統路網中，各路線所採購之列車數為何？`. Original: `原木柵線(CC350標)共採購51對(102輛)電聯車。`
- DORTS identifies Matra as producer, records manufacture in 1991, and says Taipei coupled two two-car pairs into four-car service for the first time in the family. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“The development of Taipei Metro electromechanical planning and design,” p.138](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page138.html), `臺北捷運機電系統精進軌跡尋蹤－機電規設之蛻變、創新與成長`. Original: `VAL 256型電聯車是法國馬特拉生產的VAL系統之衍生車種之一` / `係將VAL 256型電聯車以二對車（四車）運行，這是該系列首次在臺北都會區聯掛運轉` / `於80年出廠`.
- The principal dimensions are 13.78 m long, 2.56 m wide and 3.53 m high, with two doors per side per car; `256` denotes width. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [p.138](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page138.html), same title. Original: `每輛車廂長13.78公尺、寬2.56公尺（因此命名為「VAL 256」）、高3.53公尺，每輛車每邊有2扇門。`
- Cars are completely separated without gangways, so between-station evacuation uses side doors. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Practical guide to metro electric multiple units,” p.112](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no21/files/basic-html/page112.html), `捷運電聯車實務`. Original: `僅文湖線VAL256型及龐巴迪型電聯車車廂間完全隔離，沒有走道連通，此種設計在列車人員疏散時需利用側門逃生。`
- Each car has two separately excited, self-cooled, class-H insulated DC motors. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Electrical equipment of electric multiple units,” p.34](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no35/files/basic-html/page34.html), `電聯車電氣設備`. Original: `本局最早規劃之中運量木柵線Matra車輛，每車之推進系統有兩個直流馬達，每個馬達的電樞都有一支驅動軸連接，做為行駛或煞車力量的傳導，採並激自冷式H級絕緣之設計。`
- The original automatic driverless system used fixed blocks and DORTS published a 72-second minimum headway. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Review and prospects for Taipei Metro electromechanical systems,” p.202](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page202.html), `臺北捷運機電工程回顧與展望`. Original: `以雙對車自動控制模式運行，系統最小營運班距可達72秒` / `以傳輸線圈為主之固定式閉塞區間方式來控制`.
- Two test fires resulted from uncoordinated propulsion and braking across the first four-car use; DORTS says the cause survived the first incident and was corrected after expert simulation and verification. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“A proposal for DORTS knowledge management and knowledge upgrading,” p.56](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no50/files/basic-html/page56.html), `捷運局知識管理與知識升級芻議`. Original: `台北木柵線係配合台北運量需要首次採雙對車(四節車廂)行駛，統包商馬特拉公司設計時，忽略了兩對車間推進及煞車訊號之同步協調` / `第一次火警事件後，馬特拉公司並未就真正原因予以排除，導致半年內連續兩次火警事件` / `後經邀請學者專家會同馬特拉公司專業人員研討摸擬驗證，始找出答案，並加以改進。`
- Wenhu integration replaced the old cars' onboard control and communications, changed the line from Matra fixed block to Bombardier Cityflo 650 moving block, retained the old points and platform doors, and removed the original Matra control equipment after cutover. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [p.203](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no48/files/basic-html/page203.html), same title. - TRTC records all 51 converted pairs joining Wenhu service on 26 December 2010. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation (臺北大眾捷運股份有限公司), [2010 annual-report significant events](https://data.taipei/api/dataset/6ceca6ae-05ec-4995-bc1c-d3cd15f79ee1/resource/b04ee75d-55d8-4291-a96a-418f62c4e8a5/download), `臺北捷運公司99年年報－重要紀事`. Original: `2010年12月26日中運量原木柵線51對VAL256電聯車加入文湖線營運。`
- TRTC says a 2023 three-train interior trial removed 12 seats and the luggage rack from each of cars 2 and 3, added handholds, and raised approximate capacity from 400 to 420. **PRIMARY, High:** TRTC, [full interior-optimisation release](https://www.metro.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=30CCEFD2A45592BF&sms=72544237BBE4C5F6&s=86F98235BC105C44), `臺北捷運化身空間魔術師！繼龐巴迪列車改善後 文湖線馬特拉列車空間優化`.
