# Taipei-region metro fleet rosters — research findings, 24 August 2026

**Corpus warning: `docs/research/` is leads only. It has been wrong repeatedly.
Nothing here is citable until it has been checked against a primary source —

This report sets the evidence boundary for the fleet-roster page. The retrieval
date is 2026-08-24. The sources below establish vehicle families, procurement
lots, partial production identifiers, delivery milestones and selected renewal
work. They do not establish a current unit-by-unit asset register.

## What is established

### Why the page is a roster table rather than individual set pages

- **TRTC's primary material is fleet- and procurement-level.** DORTS publishes
  train quantities by contract and technical articles describe types, cars,
  formations and delivery sequences; the opened TRTC material describes fleet
  overhauls and equipment renewals by type or by number of trains. It does not
  provide a current public table mapping every set number to a line, depot,
  refurbishment state or withdrawal status. Source: DORTS, *捷運路網規劃實務*,
  *捷運技術* nos. 35, 48 and 51, and TRTC's 2014–2015 reports; PRIMARY,
  High confidence — each page was fetched in full. Representative original
  wording: `各路線所採購之列車數`, `CR381標13列電聯車完成驗收`, and
  `完成301型9列、321型2列、371型16列、381型6列電聯車...大修作業`.
- **NTMC's current fleet page is also type-level.** It publishes 30 light-rail
  trains split 15 Danhai and 15 Ankeng, 17 Circular Line trains, and 29 Sanying
  trains, with builders and technical specifications, but no train-number or
  depot-allocation list. Source: NTMC, *列車介紹* and *三鶯線電聯車*;
  PRIMARY, High confidence — the live operator pages were fetched in full.
  Original wording: `輕軌列車共30列車(淡海15列車+安坑15列車)`, `環狀列車共17列車`,
  and `三鶯列車共29列車`.
- **Decision:** publish one family/lot roster table and retain a field-level
  TBC for current unit allocation, withdrawal and preservation. Do not create
  individual pages for units whose public primary evidence does not identify
  the unit. This is a design conclusion from the evidence above, not a claim
  that the units do not exist.

### TRTC high-capacity fleets

- **C301:** DORTS's first-generation EMU history establishes 22 fixed
  six-car trains / 132 cars, contractor Union Rail Car Partnership, prototype
  cars EMU 01/02 arriving at Beitou Depot on 1 December 1992, all cars delivered
  by November 1995, and passenger service beginning with the Tamsui Line on
  28 March 1997. Source: DORTS, *臺北捷運第一代高運量電聯車*;
  PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language evidence includes
  `共22列，每列6輛，共132輛`, `EMU 01/02` and `所有量產車輛於84年11月全部抵達北投機廠`.
- **C321:** DORTS identifies Siemens, 72 three-car units forming 36 trains / 216
  cars, and the historical Xindian, Nangang, Banqiao and Zhonghe route group.
  DORTS's procurement table divides those 36 trains into CH321 (11), CN331
  (17) and CC361 (8). Source: DORTS, *臺北捷運機電系統精進軌跡尋蹤* p. 138
  and *各路線所採購之列車數*; PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language
  evidence: `72個三車編組，共36列216輛` and the three contract rows.
- **C341:** DORTS identifies six Siemens six-car trains / 36 cars, manufactured
  in 2003 and delivered between September and November 2004 for the Tucheng
  opening. The final gearbox torque-arm modification was complete on all six
  trains by 3 October 2005. Source: DORTS, *臺北捷運機電系統精進軌跡尋蹤*
  p. 138 and *321型電聯車0915脫軌事件之探討* p. 31; PRIMARY, High
  confidence. Original-language evidence: `6列6輛編組，共36輛`, `2003年製造`,
  `2004年9月至11月交車`, and `6列C341型電聯車...於94年10月3日完成`.
- **C371 family:** DORTS groups the procurement family as 52 six-car trains
  plus three three-car trains / 321 cars. Taiwan Rolling Stock's product page
  identifies its share as 162 cars, numbered 401–418 and 431–466, completed in
  January 2010. Source: DORTS, *新莊線／蘆洲線電聯車生產製造* and Taiwan
  Rolling Stock, *臺北捷運371車型*; PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language
  evidence: `52列6輛編組及3列3輛編組，共321輛` and `401~418、431~466號...共162輛，於2010年1月完工`.
- **C381 family:** DORTS establishes 13 CR381 trains for Xinyi, ten CG391
  trains for Songshan and a separate one-train CD311A purchase for Dingpu.
  Taiwan Rolling Stock identifies its industrial-cooperation production as
  cars 515–546 (96 cars), completed in January 2012, plus cars 547–548 (six
  cars), completed in August 2013. The DORTS chronology records CR381 trains
  one through six arriving at Beitou from May to August 2010, train seven in
  September 2011, trains eight through thirteen from April to July 2012,
  handover in October 2012 and acceptance in August 2013. Source: DORTS,
  *臺北捷運信義線的機電系統創新* pp. 133–134 and *信義線大事記要* pp.
  248–249, and Taiwan Rolling Stock, *臺北捷運381車型*; PRIMARY, High
  confidence. Original-language evidence: `共採購13列車`, `第一列電聯車...運抵北投機廠`,
  `CR381標13列電聯車完成驗收`, and `第515~546號之生產共96輛`.

### TRTC medium-capacity fleets

- **VAL 256:** DORTS establishes Matra selection in March 1988, 1991
  manufacture, opening service on 28 March 1996 and contract CC350's 51 pairs
  / 102 cars. TRTC records all 51 converted pairs joining Wenhu Line service
  on 26 December 2010. Source: DORTS, *臺北捷運機電系統精進軌跡尋蹤* pp.
  138–139 and TRTC 2010 annual-report extract; PRIMARY, High confidence.
  Original-language evidence: `51對／102輛`, `1991年製造`, and
  `51對舊車...於99年12月26日加入文湖線營運`.
- **C370 / Bombardier Innovia APM:** DORTS establishes contract CB370's 101
  married pairs / 202 cars for the Neihu extension and passenger service from
  4 July 2009. DORTS's production report gives the 100-car Pittsburgh / 102-car
  Tang Eng split. Source: DORTS, *內湖線新型中運量電聯車* pp. 29–31 and
  *文山內湖線*; PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language evidence:
  `101對車202輛` and `內湖線於98年7月4日通車`.
- **Current TRTC depot allocation is not established.** DORTS describes the
  high-capacity depots as mutually usable because the routes are connected by
  crossovers, and distinguishes main, secondary and storage-only depots. That
  is a depot-function description, not a current set roster. Source: DORTS,
  *捷運路網規劃實務* pp. 188–191; PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language
  evidence: `各機廠可相互利用` and `中和機廠...僅提供儲車功能`.

### NTMC fleets

- **Danhai and Ankeng light rail:** NTMC states that both fleets were designed,
  manufactured and tested in Taiwan through Taiwan Rolling Stock and Voith
  Engineering Services; each train has five cars, and the current fleet is 15
  Danhai trains plus 15 Ankeng trains. Source: NTMC, *輕軌列車*;
  PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language evidence: `由台灣車輛股份有限公司與德國福依特公司...跨國合作` and
  `輕軌列車共30列車(淡海15列車+安坑15列車)`.
- **Service dates:** DORTS's Danhai project records date the Green Mountain
  section to 24 December 2018 and Blue Sea phase 1 to 15 November 2020; NTMC's
  opening announcement dates Ankeng's first service to 10 February 2023.
  Sources: New Taipei DORTS project pages and NTMC opening release; PRIMARY,
  High confidence. Original-language evidence: `通車日期107年12月24日`,
  `營運開始日期109年11月15日` and `安坑輕軌將於2月10日正式通車`.
- **Circular Line:** NTMC states that 17 steel-wheel, steel-rail, driverless
  trains were mainly built by Hitachi Rail Italy, with 750 V DC third-rail
  supply and CBTC. Source: NTMC, *列車介紹*; PRIMARY, High confidence.
  Original-language evidence: `環狀列車共17列車`, `主要由義大利商日立軌道...承造`,
  `第三軌750VDC` and `通訊式列車控制(CBTC)`.
- **Sanying Line:** NTMC states that the two-car driverless trains were designed
  by Hitachi Rail and manufactured in Japan, and that the fleet is 29 trains.
  The operator's opening release dates revenue opening to 30 June 2026. Sources:
  NTMC, *三鶯線電聯車* and *三鶯線正式通車*; PRIMARY, High confidence.
  Original-language evidence: `由義大利商日立軌道...設計、日本製造`,
  `三鶯列車共29列車` and `6月30日正式通車`.
- **NTMC unit-to-depot allocation is not established.** The operator's current
  fleet page gives type totals, not unit identifiers or a depot register. Its
  2025 year-end release records three Ankeng trains temporarily at Danhai Depot
  for compatibility validation and then used them for peak passenger service;
  that demonstrates that a named line is not a permanent depot assignment.
  Source: NTMC, *新北捷運2025全年運量突破3,000萬人次*; PRIMARY, High confidence.
  Original-language evidence: `安坑輕軌3列正在淡海機廠進行相容性驗證的列車`.

## Conflicts

- **C301 fleet count:** DORTS's detailed first-generation history and the
  operator's later renewal report support 22 six-car trains / 132 cars. DORTS's
  later no. 48 article prints 132 cars but 21 trains. The values are published
  together: 22 trains / 132 cars from the detailed history, versus 21 trains /
  132 cars from the later article. The 21-train parenthetical is not silently
  corrected; it conflicts with the detailed count and the six-car arithmetic.
- **C381 base order versus combined fleet:** DORTS's 23-train base scope (13
  CR381 + 10 CG391) and separate one-train Dingpu add-on are different
  procurement scopes. Taiwan Rolling Stock's 96-car plus six-car production
  statement describes its share, not the whole fleet. The page therefore shows
  23 base trains and 24 combined trains as distinct values.
- **Depot boundary and allocation:** DORTS's planning table gives depot
  functions and capacities, while current TRTC/NTMC fleet pages give vehicle
  types and totals. Neither source set supplies the same unit-level measure.
  A depot's role, a historical procurement line and a temporary validation
  location are not interchangeable; the roster preserves them in separate
  columns.

## Stated gaps

- A TRTC `車輛處` asset or stabling register would settle current set numbers,
  line assignment, depot allocation, refurbishment state and withdrawal status.
- NTMC procurement handover records or an operator vehicle register would settle
  light-rail and Circular/Sanying set numbers, depot allocation and preservation
  status.
- The table can be expanded to individual pages if those registers become
  public and stable. Until then, individual pages would repeat family-level
  facts while implying unsupported unit-level certainty.
