# Zhonghe Depot (中和機廠)

Research date: 27 August 2026 (Asia/Taipei).

This is a one-subject research record for the missing Zhonghe Depot page. The
three DORTS sources below were fetched in full. Measurements and functions are
reported with their source and design context; they are not merged into one
apparently precise value.

## What is established

### Location and function

**Claim - Confirmed: the depot occupies the former Taiwan Railways Zhonghe Station site south of Heping Street, and the older DORTS route page includes Nanshijiao Station in its approximately 1.47-hectare area.**

- **Source title:** *Zhonghe-Xinlu Line - Zhonghe Line route description*
- **Original title:** 《中和新蘆線 - 中和線路線說明》
- **Publisher:** 臺北市政府捷運工程局
- **URL:** <https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=122c012a85c41404>
- **Archive snapshot:** not captured
- **Kind:** primary
- **Confidence:** High - current DORTS route page, full text read on 27 August 2026
- **Original-language sentence:**
  > 「中和機廠位於中和市和平街南方之原臺灣鐵路管理局中和車站基地內，佔地約1.47公頃(包括捷運南勢角車站)。」

The same paragraph states that land-area limits led to a stabling yard,
maintenance-equipment room and main substation, providing train stabling,
general maintenance and cleaning:

> 「因用地面積之限制，設駐車廠、維修設備房及主變電站，提供營運列車停放、一般維修及清潔之功能。」

### Planning classification

**Claim - Confirmed: DORTS's planning inventory classifies Zhonghe as a secondary Zhonghe-Xinlu depot that provides storage only.**

- **Source title:** *Taipei Metro network planning practice - depot roles and locations*
- **Original title:** 《捷運工程叢書 精進版－1 捷運路網規劃實務》
- **Publisher:** 臺北市政府捷運工程局
- **URL:** <https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page200.html>
- **Archive snapshot:** not captured
- **Kind:** primary
- **Confidence:** High - DORTS technical publication, full page read on 27 August 2026
- **Original-language sentence:**
  > 「中和機廠位於新北市中和區捷運路、和平街、興南路一段所圍土地為中和新蘆線次機廠僅提供儲車功能。」

The route page and this planning description are both official, but they do not
state the same facility function. The difference is retained as a source-stage
conflict below.

### Inventory values

**Claim - Confirmed: the DORTS depot inventory gives Zhonghe Depot level one, approximately 1.2 hectares and storage space for six trainsets.**

- **Source title:** *Taipei Metro network planning practice - depot inventory*
- **Original title:** 《捷運工程叢書 精進版－1 捷運路網規劃實務》
- **Publisher:** 臺北市政府捷運工程局
- **URL:** <https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page202.html>
- **Archive snapshot:** not captured
- **Kind:** primary
- **Confidence:** High - the values are in the DORTS table, read in full on 27 August 2026
- **Original-language table row:**
  > 「中和新蘆線　中和機廠　高運量（鋼輪）　一級廠　約 1.2 公頃　6 列」

The table's storage-space column uses 列; this is retained here as trainsets.
The source does not provide a car count in this row.

### Track package and commissioning boundary

**Claim - Confirmed: the Zhonghe track package included the route to Nanshijiao and Zhonghe Depot, four stations and a simple parking/maintenance depot; the track was underground and ballastless, and the line entered service on 24 December 1998.**

- **Source title:** *Review and prospects of Taipei Metro track engineering, issue 39*
- **Original title:** 《捷運技術 第39期 - 臺北捷運軌道工程之回顧與展望》
- **Publisher:** 臺北市政府捷運工程局
- **URL:** <https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no39/files/basic-html/page20.html>
- **Archive snapshot:** not captured
- **Kind:** primary
- **Confidence:** High - official technical publication, full page read on 27 August 2026
- **Original-language sentences:**
  > 「中和線軌道工程標號為CT561標：主要為古亭站(不含)至南勢角站及中和機廠之軌道工程，全線軌道長12.8公里，共計4座車站及1座南勢角簡易停車維修機廠，全線皆為地下段土建型式，簡易停車維修機廠一座位於南勢角終點站，軌道型式為現代無道碴道床軌道。」
  > 「中和線軌道工程CC561標於民國81年完成發包作業，得標廠商為東怡營造工程有限公司及BILFINGER + BERGER公司共同承攬，契約金額為新臺幣490,000,000元整，於87年12月24日完工通車。」

The source uses both CT561 and CC561 in adjacent parts of its account. The
technical description is therefore quoted as written; no normalization is
introduced here.

The current DORTS route page separately records the Zhonghe Line's initial
inspection on 6 November 1998, final inspection on 8 December and opening on 24
December. It does not state a separate depot handover date.

## Conflicts

### Area and boundary

- **Approximately 1.47 ha including Nanshijiao Station:** current DORTS route
  description, primary.
- **Approximately 1.2 ha:** DORTS planning inventory, primary.

The sources do not define the boundary difference. The first value explicitly
includes a passenger station; the second appears in a depot-only inventory row.
Both are published, not averaged.

### Function and grade

- **Stabling, general maintenance and cleaning:** older DORTS route page,
  primary.
- **Level-one secondary depot, storage only:** later DORTS planning pages,
  primary.

The retrieved pages do not give the date or engineering change that explains the
shift. The current page therefore reports both descriptions and does not infer
that the older maintenance functions still operate.

### Package code

- CT561 appears in the technical article's Zhonghe track-package heading.
- CC561 appears in the contract-award sentence on the same page.

No source retrieved in this subject pass resolves whether this is a typographic
variation or two related identifiers.

- **a reconciliation of the 1.2-hectare and 1.47-hectare boundaries** — the 1.47-hectare sentence explicitly includes Nanshijiao Station, but the planning row does not define its boundary; no primary explanation was found. Checked 27 August 2026 against all three full DORTS sources.
- **the CT561/CC561 identifier difference** — both labels occur in the same technical account, and no retrieved primary document explains whether one is a typographical error or a related contract identifier. Checked 27 August 2026 against the full Issue 39 page.

## Stated gaps

The page can establish location, line role, planning grade, the two area
measurements, storage capacity and the line's commissioning sequence. It cannot
yet establish a current train roster, a road count, a standalone depot handover
date, or which of the two function descriptions reflects the present operating
layout. A current TRTC depot inventory or an as-built depot completion record
would settle those gaps.
