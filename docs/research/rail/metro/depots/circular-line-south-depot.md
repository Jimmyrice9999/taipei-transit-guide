# Circular Line South Depot (南機廠)

Research date: 27 August 2026 (Asia/Taipei).

This is a one-subject research record for the South Depot page. It covers the
wider Taipei metropolitan MRT inventory rather than only the nine depots used
by the current TRTC-only inventory. The operator boundary is stated separately
from the facility's DORTS planning history.

## What is established

### Name, location and function

**Claim - Confirmed: South Depot (南機廠) is the Circular Line Phase 1 main
maintenance depot in Xindian's Shisizhang agricultural zone, and DORTS gives
14.33 hectares for the site including Shisizhang Station and the elevated
bridge, with approximately 12.19 hectares laid out as depot.**

functions and boundaries. It does not itself establish the current operating
company or a separate depot handover date.

## Conflicts

### Area denominator and boundary

- **Approximately 14.33 ha:** DORTS Phase 1 site area, explicitly including
  Shisizhang Station and the elevated bridge.
- **Approximately 12.19 ha:** DORTS Phase 1 area available for depot layout.
- **Approximately 12.9 ha:** older DORTS equipment-practice table, with no
  boundary definition in the opened page.

These are not averaged. The first two have explicit labels in the Phase 1
description; the third comes from a different equipment table whose denominator
is not explained.

### Planning capacity and equipment-table count

- **64 trainsets:** DORTS planning inventory for the first-phase depot.
- **17 trainsets:** older DORTS equipment-practice table.

The opened sources do not explain whether the difference reflects a change in
planning horizon, a procurement-stage fleet assumption, or different meanings
of the train-count column. Both values are retained with their source context;
neither is called a current fleet total.

### Operator scope

Older Taipei DORTS publications place the facility in the Taipei metropolitan
engineering network. The current New Taipei Metro announcement identifies it as
a site under New Taipei Metro management. This is a scope/operator transition,
not a contradiction in the depot's physical identity.

## Stated gaps

The page can establish the official name, site context, main-depot and Level-5
functions, current NTMC management evidence, the planning 64-trainset value,
and the older 17-trainset/32-road equipment-table values. It cannot yet
establish a current unit roster, a standalone completion or handover date, the

cadastral plan and a formal asset or operations handover record would settle
