## What is established

- Nangang is the Bannan Line's secondary depot, south of Zhongxiao East Road Section 7. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems (臺北市政府捷運工程局), [“Taipei Metro network planning practice,” p.200](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page200.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `南港機廠位於臺北市南港區忠孝東路七段南側土地板南線次機廠`.
- The detailed route page places the narrow strip east of Xiangyang Road and Dongxin Street, south of Zhongxiao East Road Section 7 and north of Muzhi Mountain. It gives approximately 7.83 ha, storage, inspection and washing functions, and reserved development above the stabling-shed roof. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [full “Nangang Line route description”](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=5E61169F8C735065), `南港線路線說明`. Original: `南港機廠位於向陽路、東新街以東、忠孝東路七段以南、姆指山以北的狹長地帶上，面積約7.83公頃，提供南港線電聯車儲放、檢修、清洗等功能，同時在駐車廠頂版上方預留開發使用。`
- The planning inventory gives level three, approximately 7.8 ha and storage space for 14 trainsets. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [p.202](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page202.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `板南線　南港機廠　高運量（鋼輪）　三級廠　約 7.8 公頃　14 列`.
- CN339A supplied ten categories of main maintenance equipment. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Taipei Metro depot equipment practice,” p.106](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page106.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `南港機廠 CN339A 標：主維修設備－共 10 大項`.
- The equipment table gives level three, 13 stabling-road positions, no test track and 7.9 ha; its note says track 302 can hold one train. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [p.107](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page107.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. The design review raised water-exclusion height from 0.5 m to 1.1 m and used the Nari level as a check. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [same manual, p.154](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no4/files/basic-html/page154.html), `捷運土木工程實務`. ## Conflicts

- **Area:** approximately 7.8 ha in the planning inventory, 7.83 ha on the detailed route page and 7.9 ha in the equipment table. They are compatible with rounding but no opened source explicitly explains the precision difference, so all three are retained.
- **Capacity:** 14 trainsets of `儲車空間` versus 13 `駐車軌列數`, with a note that track 302 can hold one train. The note may reconcile the arithmetic, but that is an inference; the published measures remain separate.
