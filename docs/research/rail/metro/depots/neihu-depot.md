## What is established

- Neihu Depot is in Nangang, on land bounded by Huandong Boulevard, Gangdong Street and Jingmao 1st Road; after it entered use it became the Wenhu Line's main depot while Muzha became secondary. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems (臺北市政府捷運工程局), [“Taipei Metro network planning practice,” p.200](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page200.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `內湖機廠位於臺北市南港區環東大道、港東街及經貿一路所圍土地` / `內湖機廠啟用後成為文湖線主機廠，而木柵機廠則改為文湖線次機廠。`
- The long 7.11-ha site is beside the Keelung River and provides stabling, washing, inspection, servicing, repair, testing, emergency recovery and train control while supporting Muzha. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [full Wenshan–Neihu Line page](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=DBAC040496EFAB94), `文山內湖線`. Original: `內湖機廠位於南港經貿園區特定專用區東北側，緊鄰基隆河為一西北往東南走向之狹長基地，面積約為7.11公頃` / `與木柵機廠相互搭配支援，共同分擔文湖線電聯車之儲車及維修功能` / `機廠主要功能有電聯車之停駐、清洗、檢查、保養、維修、測試、緊急修復、行車控制等功能設施。`
- DORTS classifies it as a rubber-tyred main depot with storage for 144 married pairs. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [planning inventory, p.202](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page202.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `文湖線　內湖機廠　中運量（膠輪）　主機廠　約7.11公頃　144對車`.
- A separate technical table gives 16 stabling roads, a test track and 7.1 ha, and repeats the 144-pair capacity. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Taipei Metro depot equipment practice,” p.107](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page107.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `內湖機廠　50列　主級　16　有　7.1公頃` / `內湖機廠可駐144對車（中運量）`.
- CB370 included 18 groups of complete heavy-maintenance equipment for 202 cars. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [equipment list, p.106](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page106.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `中運量內湖機廠　CB370標（統包）：含完整維（大）修設備（針對202輛車）共18群組`.
- The line plan was approved on 7 August 1993; after an eight-year system review the council retained medium capacity on 9 January 2001; CB420 and CB410 were signed on 11 April 2002 and 12 June 2003; the line opened on 4 July 2009. **PRIMARY, High for project chronology, Medium as depot context:** DORTS, [full line page](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=DBAC040496EFAB94), `文山內湖線`. ## Conflicts

- **Area: approximately 7.11 versus 7.1 ha.** The line page and planning inventory publish `約7.11公頃`; the equipment table publishes `7.1公頃`. The shorter value appears rounded, but no source explicitly labels it rounding, so both are published.
- **Capacity units: 144 pairs, 16 roads and 50 trains.** The equipment table publishes all three: 144 is expressly paired-car capacity, 16 is the road count and 50 is the table's line-fleet count. They are not interchangeable.
