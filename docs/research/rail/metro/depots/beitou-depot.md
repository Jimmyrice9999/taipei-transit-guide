## What is established

- Beitou is the Tamsui–Xinyi Line's main depot and lies south of Zhongyang North Road Section 3 in Beitou District. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems (臺北市政府捷運工程局), [“Taipei Metro network planning practice,” p.200](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page200.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `淡水信義線設 1 座機廠，北投機廠為淡水信義線主機廠，位於臺北市北投區中央北路三段南側土地`.
- DORTS classifies it as a level-five high-capacity steel-wheel depot and gives approximately 41 ha with storage space for 33 trainsets. **PRIMARY, High that the table says this; Medium for comparison with differently labelled figures:** DORTS, [planning inventory, p.202](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page202.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `淡水信義線　北投機廠　高運量（鋼輪）　五級廠　約 41 公頃　33 列`.
- The equipment table also gives level five and 41 ha, but lists 30 stabling-road positions and a test track in a 22-train Tamsui Line context. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Taipei Metro depot equipment practice,” p.107](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page107.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `淡水線 22列 五級 30 有 41公頃`.
- CT309A supplied 28 categories of main maintenance equipment; CT309B four dual electric/battery locomotives; CT309C twelve work cars; CT309D 65 road–rail/auxiliary items; CT309E/F/G and CT501A supplied small equipment, a driving simulator, test instruments and track-maintenance machinery. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [equipment list, p.106](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page106.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `北投機廠 CT309A 標：主維修設備－共 28 大項` / `CT309B 標：電力／電瓶兩用機車頭 *4 輛` / `CT309C 標：工作臺車 *12 輛` / `CT309D 標：淡水線鐵公路兩用車及輔助設備－共 65 項`.
- CT107 Beitou earthworks was the earliest Tamsui Line construction contract; DORTS dates signing and construction commencement to 19 January 1988. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [full “Tamsui Line route description”](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=0ac527d5fc9e596a), `淡水線路線說明`. Original: `最早CT107標北投機廠土方工程，自77年1月19日簽約開始施工。`

Original: `臺北捷運自1988年7月22日於最大維修廠-北投機廠動土開工後`.
- The site is a roughly 350 m by 1,720 m strip on the Guandu alluvial plain, with Guizikeng Creek to the east and Zhongyang North Road to the north. Highly compressible clay thickens from 15 m to 50 m north-to-south; DORTS recorded about 1.46 m maximum settlement in the western part of fill area seven and damage or impairment to utilities and track. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Taipei Metro geotechnical engineering practice,” p.243](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no6/files/basic-html/page243.html), `捷運大地工程實務`. - Before filling, most of the approximately 40 ha site was farmland with ponds and a few one- or two-storey houses. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [same manual, p.254](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no6/files/basic-html/page254.html), `捷運大地工程實務`. ## Conflicts

- **Area:** DORTS geotechnical pages publish approximately 40 ha; the planning inventory and depot-equipment table publish approximately 41 ha. No opened source explains whether this is rounding or a boundary difference, so both are retained.
- **Capacity terminology:** the planning table publishes `儲車空間 33列`; the equipment table publishes `駐車軌列數 30`. These are differently named measures. No conversion or preferred value is inferred.
- **Construction start is not a conflict:** 19 January 1988 is the CT107 contract signing/construction commencement; 22 July 1988 is explicitly the groundbreaking. Both dated milestones are retained.
