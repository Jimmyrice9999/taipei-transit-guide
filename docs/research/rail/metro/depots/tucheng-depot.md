## What is established

- Tucheng is the Bannan Line's main depot, east of Huanhe West Road and Baijiebao Road. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems (臺北市政府捷運工程局), [“Taipei Metro network planning practice,” p.200](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page200.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `土城機廠位於新北市土城區大漢溪環河西路與擺接堡路東側土地為板南線主機廠`.
- The site occupies former Dahan River flood-control land and is bounded by Xinxing Bridge, the newer Tucheng levee, the river authority and Tucheng Industrial District. DORTS gives about 26.8 ha and says it works with Nangang Depot to provide Bannan stabling, washing and maintenance. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [full “Tucheng Line route description”](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=5E61169F8C735065&s=12C5B1574D868732), `土城線路線說明`. Original: `土城機廠位於土城大漢溪防汛河川舊址` / `面積約 26.8 公頃` / `與南港機廠共同提供藍線全線營運所需之維修功能。`
- Planning began in 1990; the Taipei meeting approved it on 14 December 1990, the provincial–city meeting on 9 January 1991, referral through MOTC occurred on 25 March 1991 and Executive Yuan approval on 18 October 1993. The line opened to Yongning on 31 May 2006. **PRIMARY, High:** same [full DORTS route page](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=5E61169F8C735065&s=12C5B1574D868732). Original: `自民國79年開始規劃` / `82年10月18日奉行政院核定` / `於95年5月31日通車營運`.
- CD550 covered civil/building-services work from BL2 to Tucheng Depot and line-wide track, electromechanical systems and rolling stock including the depot; CD551 covered only station civil/building-services work. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Taipei Metro construction management practice,” p.39](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no15/files/basic-html/page39.html), `捷運工程施工管理實務`. Original: `CD550 標之工作內容包含：BL2（車站建築）至土城機廠之土建及水電、BL2（車站建築）至 BL37 全線（含機廠）之軌道工程及機電系統、電聯車等。` / `CD551 標之工作內容則只有：BL39 至 BL37 站之土建及水電工程。`
- The planning inventory gives level four, approximately 26.8 ha and storage space for 34 trainsets. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [p.202](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page202.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `板南線　土城機廠　高運量（鋼輪）　四級廠　約 26.8 公頃　34 列`.
- CD319A supplied twelve categories of main maintenance equipment. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Taipei Metro depot equipment practice,” p.106](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page106.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `土城機廠 CD319A 標：主維修設備－共 12 大項`.
- The equipment table gives level four, 36 stabling-road positions, no test track and 26.8 ha. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [p.107](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page107.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `板橋（土城）線 6 列 四級 36 無 26.8 公頃`.
- TRTC currently assigns Bannan rolling-stock maintenance and management of the Tucheng and Nangang workshops to Vehicle Plant Two. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation (臺北大眾捷運股份有限公司), [full duties page](https://www.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=195274f164a8ee0b&s=EAC107016EAB69E7), `業務職掌－車輛處`. Original: `辦理板南線電聯車維修業務。` / `辦理土城、南港機廠維修工廠管理業務。`

## Conflicts

- **Capacity:** 34 trainsets of `儲車空間` in the planning inventory versus 36 `駐車軌列數` in the equipment table. No opened source reconciles or converts the differently named measures.
- **Area:** no conflict found; the detailed route, planning and equipment records all publish approximately 26.8 ha.
- **CD551 date is not a depot date:** DORTS calls CD551 the earliest main contract in the wider project, signed 29 April 1999, while the construction manual puts the depot in CD550. The CD551 date is therefore not published as Tucheng Depot's start.
