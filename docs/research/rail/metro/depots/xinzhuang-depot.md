## What is established

- Xinzhuang is a secondary Zhonghe–Xinlu depot north of Zhongzheng Road. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems (臺北市政府捷運工程局), [“Taipei Metro network planning practice,” p.200](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page200.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `新莊機廠位於新北市新莊區中正路北側土地為中和新蘆線次機廠`.
- The depot is mainly on the former Losheng site, straddles New Taipei and Taoyuan beside Huilong, is level three, and provides storage, inspection, maintenance, washing and storage for power/track-maintenance equipment. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [full “Xinzhuang Line route description”](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=122c012a85c41404&s=33245A586D747A2C), `新莊線路線說明`. Original: `新莊機廠主要位於樂生療養院舊址，為3級機廠` / `基地跨越新北市與桃園市，緊鄰捷運迴龍站`.
- DORTS gives an original 17.4 ha reduced to 13.9 ha for the Losheng preservation plan; another paragraph gives about 17.5 ha for the earlier layout. **PRIMARY, High:** same [full DORTS route page](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=122c012a85c41404&s=33245A586D747A2C). Original: `原始基地面積約17.4公頃，配合樂生療養院保存案，基地面積縮減為13.9公頃` / `佔地約17.5公頃`.
- The pre-reduction facility list includes a wash, entry/exit tracks, maintenance and civil/track workshops, test track, stabling, utilities, administration and wastewater treatment. **PRIMARY, High for that described layout:** same route page. Original: `機廠區域內設列車清洗場、進出廠線、維修工廠、土木/軌道工廠、測試軌、儲車區`.
- Preservation-driven rearrangement prevented completion with the main line. Preliminary inspection occurred 25 October 2020, final inspection 23 December 2020 and activation 4 January 2021; DORTS calls it the only such inspection without a route section. **PRIMARY, High:** same route page. Original: `因配合樂生療養院保存案計畫，調整機廠設施的配置` / `於109年10月25日初勘、109年12月23日履勘、於110年1月4日啟用營運` / `唯一沒有路線段的初履勘案例`.
- The planning inventory gives level three, approximately 13.9 ha and storage space for 16 trainsets. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [p.202](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page202.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `中和新蘆線　新莊機廠　高運量（鋼輪）　三級廠　約 13.9 公頃　16 列`.
- CK379A supplied fourteen categories of main maintenance equipment. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Taipei Metro depot equipment practice,” p.106](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page106.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `新莊機廠 CK379A 標：主維修設備－共 14 大項`.
- The equipment table gives level three, 17 stabling-road positions, no test track and 17.2 ha. **PRIMARY, High that the table says this; Low for its layout date/boundary:** DORTS, [p.107](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page107.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `新莊線 20 列 三級 17 無 17.2 公頃`.
- The Ministry of Culture dates Losheng to 1930 and describes it as Taiwan's first public Hansen's-disease prevention/isolation institution; it records government selection of the site for the depot, metro work from 2002, the 30 May 2007 preservation decision, heritage registration and human-rights legislation. **PRIMARY, High for the government's heritage record and decisions:** Bureau of Cultural Heritage, Ministry of Culture (文化部文化資產局), [full “Losheng Sanatorium — potential World Heritage site”](https://twh.boch.gov.tw/taiwan/intro.aspx?id=14&lang=zh_tw), `樂生療養院－臺灣世界遺產潛力點`. Original: `該院創建於西元1930年` / `為臺灣第一所公立漢生病防治與隔離機構` / `臺北捷運新莊線工程於西元2002年展開後`.
- TRTC currently assigns Zhonghe–Xinlu rolling-stock maintenance and management of Luzhou, Xindian and Xinzhuang workshops to Vehicle Plant Three. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation (臺北大眾捷運股份有限公司), [full duties page](https://www.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=195274f164a8ee0b&s=EAC107016EAB69E7), `業務職掌－車輛處`. Original: `辦理中和新蘆線、松山新店線、新北投支線、小碧潭支線電聯車維修業務。` / `辦理蘆洲、新店、新莊機廠維修工廠管理業務。`

## Conflicts

- **Area/design stages:** 17.4 and 17.5 ha describe the original layout to different precision; 13.9 ha is explicitly the post-preservation reduction. The equipment table's 17.2 ha is not dated or defined, so its relationship to those stages remains unresolved.
- **Capacity:** 16 trainsets of `儲車空間` versus 17 `駐車軌列數`. The differently named measures are not converted.
- **Test track:** the detailed pre-reduction facility list includes a test track; the equipment table says none. A layout-stage explanation is plausible but not stated, so both are published.
- **Preservation building count inside one government page:** the Ministry of Culture overview gives 39 retained in place, six demolished and nine rebuilt; the longer history gives 39 retained, six demolished and ten relocated/reassembled or rebuilt. The nine-versus-ten conflict is not resolved.
