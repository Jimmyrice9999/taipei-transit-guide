## Conflicts

- **Area:** about 3.5 ha in the planning inventory; about 12 ha in the route description and depot-equipment table; 92,560.7 m² for the named development base; and about 9 ha for the development parcels. The latter two are explicitly property-development boundaries. No opened source defines why the operational 3.5 ha and 12 ha figures differ, so neither is selected.
- **Capacity terminology:** 18 trainsets of `儲車空間` versus 15 `駐車軌列數`. The headings name different measures; neither is converted.
- **Dates:** the 2006–12 property milestones and 2014 track completion are later works, not conflicting dates for original depot commissioning.
