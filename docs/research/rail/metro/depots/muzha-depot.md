## What is established

- Muzha Depot is between Xinguang Road Section 2 and the Jingmei River in Wenshan District; it changed from the original Muzha Line's main depot to the Wenhu Line's secondary depot after Neihu Depot opened. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems (臺北市政府捷運工程局), [“Taipei Metro network planning practice,” p.200](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page200.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `木柵機廠位於臺北市文山區新光路二段，與景美溪間，原為木柵線的主機廠` / `內湖機廠啟用後成為文湖線主機廠，而木柵機廠則改為文湖線次機廠。`
- The depot is near the Taipei Zoo end, occupies approximately 6.2 ha and provides storage, maintenance, operations control, washing, wayside maintenance and parts warehousing. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [full Wenshan–Neihu Line page](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=DBAC040496EFAB94), `文山內湖線`. Original: `機廠位於木柵動物園出口附近，佔地約6.2公頃，木柵機廠具有車輛儲存、維修、行控、清洗、沿線道傍維修及零件倉儲等多種功能。`
- DORTS classifies it as a rubber-tyred secondary depot with storage for 84 married pairs. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [planning inventory, p.202](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page202.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `文湖線　木柵機廠　中運量（膠輪）　次機廠　約6.2公頃　84對車`.
- A separate DORTS table gives 12 stabling roads, a test track and 6.2 ha, and repeats the 84-pair capacity in its note. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Taipei Metro depot equipment practice,” p.107](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page107.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `木柵機廠　25列　次級　12　有　6.2公頃` / `木柵機廠可駐84對車`.
- CM350 supplied complete heavy-maintenance equipment for 102 cars; CM351A and CM351B covered non-VAL test instruments and small maintenance equipment/tools. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [equipment list, p.106](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page106.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `CM350標（統包）：含完整維（大）修設備－針對102輛車` / `CM351A標：非VAL系統測試儀器` / `CM351B標：非VAL系統小型維修設備及工具`.
