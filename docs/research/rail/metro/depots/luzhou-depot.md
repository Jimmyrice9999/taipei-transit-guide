## What is established

- Luzhou is the Zhonghe–Xinlu Line's main depot. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei City Government Department of Rapid Transit Systems (臺北市政府捷運工程局), [“Taipei Metro network planning practice,” p.200](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page200.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `蘆洲機廠位於新北市蘆洲區環堤大道南側為中和新蘆線主機廠`.
- The site is bounded by the Tamsui River levee, Shuinangou drainage channel, Luzhou pumping station and a planned road; it is about 800 m long, up to 280 m wide and approximately 16.8 ha. DORTS lists the wash, entry/exit tracks, maintenance and civil/track workshops, test and storage tracks, utilities and administration. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [full “Luzhou Line route description”](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=122c012a85c41404&s=3843E83F882E79FD), `蘆洲線路線說明`. Original: `基地全長約800公尺，最寬處約280公尺，佔地面積約16.8公頃` / `機廠用地區域內設有列車清洗場、進出場線、維修工廠、土木/軌道工廠、測試軌`.
- CL700A was the earliest principal line works contract, signed and begun on 25 October 2001; the line opened on 3 November 2010. **PRIMARY, High for line milestones:** same [full route page](https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=122c012a85c41404&s=3843E83F882E79FD). Original: `最早施工的主體工程為90年10月25日簽約開始施工的CL700A區段標工程` / `99年11月3日通車營運`.
- The planning inventory gives level four, approximately 15.9 ha and storage space for 22 trainsets. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [p.202](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no1/files/basic-html/page202.html), `捷運路網規劃實務`. Original: `中和新蘆線　蘆洲機廠　高運量（鋼輪）　四級廠　約 15.9 公頃　22 列`.
- CL609A supplied fifteen categories of main maintenance equipment. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Taipei Metro depot equipment practice,” p.106](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page106.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `蘆洲機廠 CL609A 標：主維修設備－共 15 大項`.
- The equipment table gives level four, 22 stabling-road positions, a test track and 16 ha. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [p.107](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no27/files/basic-html/page107.html), `捷運機廠設備實務`. Original: `蘆洲線 4 列 四級 22 有 16 公頃`.
- The earthworks paper also gives approximately 16 ha. It says the acquired land had been an illegal construction-waste dump, vegetable plots and some factory land; unsuitable material was excavated/replaced and fill sequencing prioritised settlement-sensitive and urgent work. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Luzhou Depot earthworks overview,” p.83](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no38/files/basic-html/page83.html), `蘆洲機廠土方工程概述`. Original: `佔地面積約16公頃` / `該用地徵收前為非法營建廢棄土丟棄場、菜園及部份工廠用地`.
- DORTS records reuse of excess mixed material and construction spoil in the adjacent linear park under a cut-and-fill balance, reducing haulage delay and cost. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [“Taipei engineering exemplars,” p.19](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no41/files/basic-html/page19.html), `共好團隊之捷運楷模`. Original: `運用「挖填平衡」理念` / `將其運用於鄰近之線型公園`.
- CL604 depot supervision was under way in 2001; after replacement of troubled electrical subcontractors, depot low-voltage power was achieved in August 2007. **PRIMARY, High:** DORTS, [same article, p.21](https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/JRTST/ebook/no41/files/basic-html/page21.html). Original: `2001年奉派加入水電環控第一工務所，擔任副主任及CL604標蘆洲機廠監造工作` / `終於2007年8月順利達成機廠低壓供電工作`.
- TRTC currently assigns Zhonghe–Xinlu rolling-stock maintenance and management of the Luzhou, Xindian and Xinzhuang workshops to Vehicle Plant Three. **PRIMARY, High:** Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation (臺北大眾捷運股份有限公司), [full duties page](https://www.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=195274f164a8ee0b&s=EAC107016EAB69E7), `業務職掌－車輛處`. Original: `辦理中和新蘆線、松山新店線、新北投支線、小碧潭支線電聯車維修業務。` / `辦理蘆洲、新店、新莊機廠維修工廠管理業務。`

## Conflicts

- **Area:** approximately 15.9 ha in the planning inventory, 16 ha in the equipment and earthworks records, and 16.8 ha on the detailed route page. No opened source defines a common boundary or explicitly says these are rounding variants, so every value is retained.
- **Capacity terminology:** both sources publish 22, but one is `儲車空間` and the other `駐車軌列數`. The identical number is not treated as proof that the units are generally interchangeable.
- **Dates:** the CL700A line-contract date, CL604 depot supervision and August 2007 low-voltage milestone describe different packages/stages; none is silently relabelled depot completion.
