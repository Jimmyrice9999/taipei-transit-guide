# Kaohsiung Metro joint land development (TOD) — research findings, 28 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. `content/rail/projects/tod-joint-development.md` already covers Taipei/New Taipei's TOD framework in depth but names no non-Taipei operator; this is the first report on Kaohsiung's own joint-development mechanism.

This is a one-subject report on the legal basis, philosophy and process
structure behind Kaohsiung Metro's joint land development (聯合開發)
programme, as published by the Kaohsiung City Government Mass Rapid
Transit Bureau's dedicated joint-development office section.

## What is established

- **Kaohsiung's joint land development programme rests on two named legal instruments: Article 7 of the Mass Rapid Transit Act (大眾捷運法第七條) and the Mass Rapid Transit System Land Development Regulations (大眾捷運系統土地開發辦法).** Source: Kaohsiung City Government Mass Rapid Transit Bureau (高雄市政府捷運工程局), `開發機制概要` (Development Mechanism Overview), `https://mtbu.kcg.gov.tw/JointDevelopmentOffice/JDO01.html`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched and read in full (raw HTML parsed directly). Original-language basis: `法令依據：大眾捷運法第七條、大眾捷運系統土地開發辦法`.
- **The bureau states its joint-development philosophy in a four-character phrase, "合建分坪、利潤共享" (joint construction with divided floor space, shared profit): investors, landowners and the competent authority develop the site together as three parties, allocating the completed building according to each party's contribution-cost ratio.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `聯開理念【合建分坪、利潤共享】，投資人、地主、主管機關，三方合作開發，依貢獻成本比例分配開發建築物`. The same page separately frames the programme's public-interest goal as protecting landowners' rights by combining private and public-sector capacity, and sharing the site's development benefit through varied participation methods. Original-language basis: `結合民間與公部門力量保障土地所有權人權益，提供多元參與捷運建設方式，分享土地開發效益`.
- **The bureau describes the process in three chronological stages: land acquisition, investor solicitation and development-construction management, and rights allocation — with land acquisition further split into four steps (urban-planning review, appraiser land valuation, urban-planning announcement, and negotiated purchase with landowners), investor solicitation/construction split into two steps (investor selection, development construction), and rights allocation split into two steps (rights allocation itself, then property registration and operations management).** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `聯合開發作業依時序可分為「用地取得」、「招商開發工程管理」與「權益分配」3個階段，「用地取得」階段下有「都市計畫審議」、「委託估價師查估地價」、「都市計畫公告」及「與土地所有人協議價購」4個流程，「招商開發工程管理」階段下有「投資人甄選」及「開發興建」2個流程，「權益分配」階段下有「權益分配」及「產權登記與營運管理」2個流程`.
