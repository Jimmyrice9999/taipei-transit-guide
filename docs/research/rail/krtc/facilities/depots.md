Every claim below records whether the underlying page was fetched and read in full, or only found via a search summary; the latter is not published as an established fact on the content page. `docs/research/rail/krtc.md` (the general KRTC lead file) explicitly lists "depot/workshop allocation" as unaddressed — this fills that gap. This is a one-subject report on Kaohsiung Metro's maintenance depots across
the Red Line, Orange Line and Circular Light Rail.

## What is established

- **KRTC's Red/Orange network has exactly three depots: North Depot (北機廠), South Depot (南機廠) and Daliao Depot (大寮機廠).** Source: Kaohsiung City Government Mass Rapid Transit Bureau, `Red and Orange Line route description` (路線說明（含路網及車站）), `https://mtbu.kcg.gov.tw/KaohsiungMetro/CompletedRoutes/RedandOrange/RedandOrange01.html`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched in full. Original-language basis: `計畫中並設置有北機廠、南機廠以及大寮機廠等三個捷運機廠`. Independently cross-confirmed by KRTC's own land-development index page listing the same three named depots with their own sub-pages.
- **South Depot: south end of the Red Line, Qianzhen District, at the Zhongshan Rd/Zhong'an Rd intersection; 26 hectares; functions as a Level 3 maintenance depot (三級維修機廠); the site also houses KRTC's own headquarters building and the metro system's operations control centre.** Source: Kaohsiung Rapid Transit Corporation, `South Depot` (南機廠), `https://corp.krtc.com.tw/Business/land_development_more?id=db843544a4ce473bb05505ce84013ca8`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched in full. Original-language basis: `南機廠位處捷運紅線南端，屬高雄市前鎮區，基地位於高雄市中山路及中安路口`; `南機廠用地面積為26公頃`; `作為三級維修機廠`; `機廠範圍內設置捷運公司總部大樓及捷運系統行控中心`.
- **North Depot: north end of the Red Line, spanning both Gangshan District and Ciaotou District; 34 hectares total (about 9.4 hectares available for commercial development, about 24.6 hectares as the depot/access-road/station facility zone); functions as a Level 3 maintenance depot; supplies traction power for the Gangshan-Luzhu extension's RK1 station via interconnection with the existing power system.** Source: Kaohsiung Rapid Transit Corporation, `North Depot` (北機廠), `https://corp.krtc.com.tw/Business/land_development_more?id=ec242f99d99949be84f3cfdef6d56b0b`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched in full. Original-language basis: `北機廠位於捷運紅線北端，跨高雄市岡山區及橋頭區二行政區`; `北機廠用地面積為34公頃`; `作為三級維修機廠，設施區、聯絡道路及捷運車站等佔地面積為24.6公頃`. Power-supply role separately confirmed by MTBU, `RK1 extension power energisation notice`, `https://mtbu.kcg.gov.tw/Activities/C002100?CategoryID=fb12bab3-733e-4218-9c6d-0375d3101dc9&Keyword=&PageNumber=3&PageType=1&id=8cad73f8-0149-4839-94a3-7b2ab628fc56`, PRIMARY, High confidence: `透過捷運紅橘線北機廠完成既有供電系統之電力延伸介接，提供RK1岡山車站通車所需電聯車牽引電力`.
- **Daliao Depot: east end of the Orange Line, Daliao District; 54 hectares total; functions as the main repair depot at the highest maintenance tier named among the three (五級維修機廠, Level 5); roughly 36 hectares is the depot-plus-station zone and roughly 18 hectares is a commercial-service development zone.** Source: Kaohsiung Rapid Transit Corporation, `Daliao Depot` (大寮機廠), `https://corp.krtc.com.tw/Business/land_development_more?id=278b8920c45c4ac78f3e010646cf4f4f`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched in full. Original-language basis: `位於捷運橘線東端，屬高雄市大寮區`; `用地總面積為54公頃`; `作為捷運維修主機廠使用為主` and `五級維修機廠`.
- **The Circular Light Rail's depot is officially named only "輕軌機廠" (LRT Depot) by the government primary source, at the Ruixi St/Ruibei Rd intersection, forming station C37.** Source: Kaohsiung City Government Mass Rapid Transit Bureau, `Circular Light Rail route description` (路線說明), `https://mtbu.kcg.gov.tw/KaohsiungMetro/CompletedRoutes/LightRail/LightRail01.html`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched in full. Original-language basis: `C37、輕軌機廠站、瑞西街與瑞北路口（輕軌機廠內）`. The government primary source does not use the more specific secondary name "前鎮機廠" (Qianzhen Depot) anywhere on this page.

## Conflicts

- **Daliao Depot's site area: 54 hectares (KRTC's own page) versus 55.0 hectares (a zh.wikipedia depot-comparison table, not independently traced to its own footnote).** Both published; not resolved.
- **North Depot's district coverage: two districts — Gangshan and Ciaotou — per KRTC's own page, versus Ciaotou only, per zh.wikipedia's depot table.** A direct primary-versus-secondary contradiction on a factual point; both recorded rather than the secondary value silently dropped.
- **The Circular Light Rail depot's name and precise location: "輕軌機廠" at the Ruixi St/Ruibei Rd intersection (the only government primary source read) versus "前鎮機廠" at the Kaisyuan 3rd Rd/Xianming Rd intersection, with a stated 3.8-hectare area and coordinates (zh.wikipedia's dedicated article, citing a 2015 environmental impact statement archived on web.archive.org, which the tooling used cannot fetch).** These are three different location descriptions across sources for what is presented as one facility; whether they describe the same site under different reference points, or a genuine discrepancy, is unresolved — not assumed either way.
