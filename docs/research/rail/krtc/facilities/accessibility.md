# Kaohsiung Metro station accessibility — research findings, 27 August 2026

> Corpus warning: files under `docs/research/` are leads and working notes, not sources. Every claim below records whether the underlying page was fetched and read in full, or only found via a search summary; the latter is not published as an established fact on the content page. No prior `docs/research/` file on KRTC accessibility existed to check against.

This is a one-subject report on Kaohsiung Metro (Red Line, Orange Line and
the Circular Light Rail) station accessibility.

## What is established

- **KRTC's own accessibility page states that all stations provide accessible elevators, framed as a universal-provision statement, not a measured completion percentage.** Source: Kaohsiung Rapid Transit Corporation, `無障礙設施`, `https://www.krtc.com.tw/Guide/safety_guide?id=12828c60abbc465bac2d2c608f4cab0a`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched and read in full. Original-language basis: `為顧及視障、肢障、高齡或其他暫時性行動不便者如提重物、推嬰兒車者等之安全性和可及性，各車站皆提供無障礙電梯`. No numeric "100% of stations" figure appears anywhere on this page — that framing is not published.
- **The same page enumerates a defined set of station accessibility facilities: an accessible ticket gate and an accessible add-value/ticketing machine near the non-paid-area elevator exit; accessible restrooms in both men's and women's facilities; accessible ramps continuing street-level infrastructure into the station; one accessible car/motorcycle parking space at stations with park-and-ride facilities; and platform tactile guidance, accessible door-position signage, and audible door-closing warnings.** Same source, PRIMARY, High confidence.
- **The same page, checked specifically, does not cite any named law, regulation or design standard (e.g. 建築物無障礙設施設計規範, 大眾運輸工具無障礙設施設置辦法, 身心障礙者權益保障法) anywhere in its content.** Checked 2026-08-27. This is a "not found," not a "confirmed absence": a further search of MOTC's own accessibility-standard pages was not completed (search-tool budget was exhausted mid-task).
- **Whether the "all stations have accessible elevators" statement is meant to include the at-grade Circular Light Rail, or refers only to the Red/Orange heavy-rail network, is unresolved.** The accessibility page sits under KRTC's heavy-rail "乘車指南" site section; the separate light-rail (KLRT) station-guide pages checked did not themselves mention accessibility, low-floor vehicles or wheelchair access. Not published either way.
