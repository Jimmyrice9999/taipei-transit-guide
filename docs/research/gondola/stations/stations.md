# Maokong Gondola stations — research file

**⚠ Corpus warning.** `docs/research/` is leads, not sources. Nothing in this
file should be cited onward without following its own citation to the primary
underneath it. New as of 20 August 2026, written from primary fetches. The
line itself is already researched in depth in

## What is established

1. **The operator's own site publishes a dedicated page per station**, reached
   from a shared base URL with a station-specific `s=` query parameter:
   Zoo Station (`n=320D5F8758DECEFA`, base/default), Zoo South Station
   (`s=667271124A37B1D3`), Zhinan Temple Station (`s=FA83495732CB810C`), and
   Maokong Station (`s=C5B2835A5D68E150`). All four fetched in full 20 August
   2026. **PRIMARY. Confidence: High** for each station's own address and
   access description, quoted on the relevant content page.

2. **Zhinan Temple Station sits at 264.3 m elevation and Maokong Station at
   299.3 m**, both confirmed by direct full-page fetches of their own
   zh.wikipedia articles (指南宮站, 貓空站), each stating 「海拔 264.3m」 /
   「海拔 299.3m」 verbatim. **SECONDARY (Wikipedia), Confidence: Medium-High**
   — full-page fetches, specific figures, no internal contradiction found.

3. **Zoo South Station opened as 動物園內站 and was renamed to its current
   name on 26 October 2015**, confirmed independently on this run by a full
   fetch of zh.wikipedia's own 動物園南站 article: 「啟用時名為『動物園內站』」
   ... 「2015年10月26日：將中文站名從『動物園內站』改為現名」. This corroborates,
   with an exact date, the same rename already used on the line page (which
   cited the same article). **SECONDARY, Confidence: High** — the date is
   specific and internally consistent with the line page's existing use of
   this source.

4. **Zhinan Temple Station's own service suspension and reopening dates match
   the line-wide T16 closure already established from the Control Yuan's
   resolution** — zh.wikipedia's 指南宮站 article independently gives 1
   October 2008 and 30 March 2010, the same dates the line page sources to a
   government primary document. **SECONDARY, corroborating a PRIMARY-sourced
   claim already on the line page. Confidence: High.**

5. **A dragon sculpture, 「金龍獻瑞」, was unveiled at Zhinan Temple Station's
   迎仙亭 pond on 15 June 2014**, per the same zh.wikipedia article, fetched
   in full. **SECONDARY. Confidence: Medium** — specific and dated, but not
   independently corroborated against a second source this run.

6. **Zoo Station and the Wenhu Line's own Taipei Zoo MRT station (BR01) are
   physically separate buildings, roughly 350 m apart**, linked by an
   out-of-station transfer (站外轉乘) rather than a shared platform or
   in-system link. Confirmed two ways: the gondola operator's own station
   page states the walking distance (「捷運動物園站西南方約350公尺」), and
   zh.wikipedia's article on the MRT station (動物園站 (臺北市)), fetched in
   full, independently gives each building's own separate address (gondola:
   新光路二段8號; MRT: 新光路二段32號) and describes the link explicitly as an
   out-of-station transfer. **PRIMARY + SECONDARY agreement. Confidence:
   High.**
