# Taiwan national transport statistics

**Status: researched and built 29 August 2026 (Run 306, Part 6c).** Run
304's brief note said this subject was researched but "not yet transcribed
to a docs/research file" — that prior transcript was not preserved into
this run's context, so all figures below were independently re-derived from
primary sources this run rather than assumed. Built as three pages under
`content/statistics/national/`: modal-share.md, vehicle-registration.md,
traffic-safety.md.

## What is established

**Modal share** (113年民眾日常使用運具狀況調查摘要分析, 交通部統計處,
motc.gov.tw, PRIMARY, High confidence, full PDF read): 2024 vs 2022 by
trip-leg share — motorcycle 43.7%/45.8%, private car 26.6%/25.0%, walking
9.9%/10.0%, rail (MRT+THSR+TRA combined) 6.6%/5.5% (first time exceeding
bus), bus 6.0%/6.0%, bicycle 2.7%/3.0%, taxi 2.5%/2.7%. Aggregates: public
transit 15.2%/14.3% (below the 16.0% recorded in 2020); private motorised
72.0%/72.3%. By city, 2024: Taipei City highest at 38.3%, Nantou lowest at
3.3%. Survey period 2024-09-01 to 2024-12-31, effective sample 34,236.

**Vehicle registration, 1988-2025** (two MOTC PDF tables, motc.gov.tw,
PRIMARY, High confidence, both read in full): 1988: 8,426,868 total
(2,089,270 cars / 6,337,598 scooters). 1998: 15,959,135. 2008: 21,092,358.
2016: 21,510,650. 2021: 22,597,694. End of 2024: 23,335,950 (8,679,866 cars
/ 14,656,084 scooters). January 2025: 23,342,799.

**Traffic-death statistics, the three-track disagreement**: MOTC's own
public clarification (motc.gov.tw/ch/app/argues_list/view, PRIMARY, High
confidence, published 2026-02-24) states directly that it has not changed
its accident-statistics methodology and instead publishes A1 (24-hour), A30
(30-day) and the Ministry of Health and Welfare's own figure in parallel,
attributing the divergence to "fundamental differences in statistical
definitions and data sources" without itself restating the numbers. A30
2024 total: 2,950 deaths (財團法人道路交通安全教育基金會, republishing
official statistics, SECONDARY but a direct NGO republication of primary
figures). MOHW's own official page (mohw.gov.tw/cp-7177-82775-1.html,
PRIMARY, High confidence, published 2025-06-16) states 2024 "運輸事故死亡"
at 2,932, down 4.4% from 2023.

## Conflicts found

- **The 2024 MOHW transport-death figure has two different values, both
  attributed to MOHW**: 2,932 (MOHW's own official page) vs. 2,426 (a udn.com
  news comparison table, citing the same "運輸事故死亡人數" term). The 524-
  person/17.7% gap the news comparison reports between MOTC (2,950) and its
  own MOHW figure is arithmetically consistent with the 2,426 value
  (2,950−2,426=524) and not with MOHW's own page value (2,950−2,932=18) —
  itself evidence the two "MOHW" figures measure different things (possibly
  all-mode transport deaths vs. a narrower road-only subset), but this was
  not confirmed against a primary source that states which is which. Both
  values published on the built page; neither resolved.
- **A specific claim that roughly 1 in 5 MOTC 30-day records are later
  found inaccurate** (attributed to MOTC official Wu Donglin via udn.com) —
  appeared only in a WebSearch auto-summary, not confirmed in a full-page
  fetch of the same URL. Not published on the built page for that reason.

Nothing was chased and disproved this run — this is a fresh research pass
with no prior corpus entry to check against, so there is no "Checked and
failed" section: a search for an existing docs/research file on this

## Stated gaps

A full-year A1 (24-hour) national total for 2024 from a primary page (only
a partial January 2024 figure was found). The specific MOHW data table that
would resolve the 2,932-vs-2,426 conflict. A national aggregate public-
transit ridership figure summing all modes and operators for a single
recent year — MOTC publishes mode share by percentage, not an absolute
combined headcount; reconstructing one would mean summing every operator's
own annual report, most of which this site already covers on their own
pages.
