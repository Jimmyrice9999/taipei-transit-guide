# 2030年客運車輛電動化推動計畫 — the 2030 bus/coach electrification programme

Research pass, 28 August 2026.

## What is established

**The programme exists, approved 26 May 2023 (112年5月26日), covers
113–119年 (2024–2030), total budget NT$64.327 billion.** Source: 立法院
committee document quoting the MOTC submission, https://www.ly.gov.tw/Pages/Detail.aspx?nodeid=55052&pid=236686,
PRIMARY, High (fetched in full; the split figures sum correctly:
450.835 + 192.432 = 643.267億). Original: "行政院於112年5月26日核定交通部提報
『2030年客運車輛電動化推動計畫』…本計畫期程113年至119年，分7年辦理，總經費643億
2,670萬元(含公路局450億8,350萬元及環境部192億4,320萬元)。" Corroborated by
CNA, https://netzero.cna.com.tw/news/202501080032/ (SECONDARY, High,
8 Jan 2025), with the same figures at rounder precision.

The Executive Yuan's own policy page (PRIMARY, https://www.ey.gov.tw/Page/5A8A0CB5B41DA11E/fbaa04ca-a430-48e7-8ba1-0b35d1dc4879)
gives the NT$64.3 billion total but not the funding split, and is dated
20 June 2023 — later than the 26 May approval date, plausibly an
"approved" vs. "published" distinction not explained by any source. It
also still names 環保署 (the pre-August-2023 EPA), predating or straddling
the reorganisation into 環境部 (Ministry of Environment) — a naming
artefact, not a different funder.

**Vehicle target: 14,500 electric buses by 2030** — 11,700 city buses,
2,800 intercity/highway/national coaches. Source: same LY document, PRIMARY,
High, corroborated by CNA, SECONDARY, High. Original: "預計2030年補助燃油大客車
（市區公車、一般公路客運、國道客運），汰換或新購電動大客車達1萬4500輛（市區公車
1萬1700輛、一般公路客運及國道客運2800輛）。"

**Three programme phases**: 先導期 pilot (109–111年/2020–2022, complete),
推廣期 rollout (112–115年/2023–2026, current), 普及期 universalisation
(116–119年/2027–2030). Source: ARTC (財團法人車輛研究測試中心),
https://www.artc.org.tw/tw/knowledge/articles/13723, SECONDARY, Medium-High
(a government-linked testing foundation, not MOTC itself; this phase
breakdown was not found on any MOTC/EY primary page fetched).

## Legislative Yuan oversight documents — target vs. actual, in detail

Three LY committee documents, all PRIMARY, High for what each states:

**(a)** https://www.ly.gov.tw/Pages/Detail.aspx?nodeid=55052&pid=236686 —
cumulative subsidy approvals FY2010–Aug 2023: "1,901輛" ("99年度至112年8月底
核定補助汽車客運業者購置電動大客車共計1,901輛，電動大客車數量成長緩慢，成效未如
預期"). FY2024 (113年度) 公路局 budget NT$2.7802 billion, targeting 1,070
vehicles. FY2024–2025 combined need: NT$11.3559 billion. Barriers cited:
battery degradation/range on long intercity routes; high lifecycle cost;
infrastructure/policy dependency.

**(b)** https://www.ly.gov.tw/Pages/Detail.aspx?nodeid=55731&pid=244887
(reviewing to Aug 2024) — target: 11,700 city buses / 100% by 2030,
NT$64.327bn total. Actual as of Aug 2024: "市區電動公車1,817輛(比例17.31%)，
與市區電動公車數3,300輛(比例25%)之目標相差甚遠". Manufacturing capacity:
"113年1至8月實際產能僅50輛" against a 1,070-vehicle annual target; full-year
capacity estimated 1,700–1,900 vehicles. Purchase-subsidy execution:
"4億1,241萬1千元，占全年度預算數僅為17.20%". Several counties near 0%
(基隆, 新竹市, 苗栗, 南投, 澎湖).

**(c)** https://www.ly.gov.tw/Pages/Detail.aspx?nodeid=55626&pid=242941
(reviewing to May 2024, FY2025 budget) — FY2025 (114年度) budget NT$6.8575
billion: 購車補助 NT$5.92bn, 營運補助 NT$797.2m, 路網優化補助 NT$130.8m,
維修保養體系 NT$9.5m. FY2025 targets: subsidise 1,600 vehicles; reach 5,000
city electric buses; 35% penetration. As of end-May 2024: "市區電動公車比例
17.13%，與所訂目標3,400輛，市區電動公車比例25%相差甚遠". Cites an unrelated
legacy predecessor for comparison: MOEA's FY2014–2016 programme achieved
only 38.25% execution, 82 vehicles.

## Conflicts, published not resolved

**Annual vehicle-count targets have moved between announcements** — whether
from genuine revision, rounding, or a different denominator is not stated
by any source:
- e-info.org.tw (2022, pre-formalisation): 2025 target "4,700 vehicles/35%".
- LY doc (b): 2024 target "3,300 vehicles/25%".
- LY doc (c): 2024 target elsewhere given as "3,400 vehicles/25%" — same
  percentage, 100-vehicle difference from doc (b).
- LY doc (c): 2025 target "5,000 vehicles/35%".

**FY2025 programme budget: two figures, not reconciled.** LY doc (c),
fetched in full, gives NT$6.8575 billion for the whole programme (purchase
+ operating + network + maintenance subsidies combined). A separate,
unverified search-summary figure of NT$3.757 billion for "公路局's 114年度
line" specifically was never confirmed against a full-page primary fetch —
carried at lower confidence, not asserted as fact.
