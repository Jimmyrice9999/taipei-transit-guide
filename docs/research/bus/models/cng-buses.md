# CNG buses in Taiwan

Research pass, 28 August 2026 (Run 303, Part 5 — CNG bus manufacturers,
per the standing brief). Bottom line up front: this is one of the
thinnest subjects the project has researched. After extensive Mandarin
searching across government primaries, zh.wikipedia, Taiwanese news,
enthusiast wikis and academic leads, almost nothing exists. No `docs/research/`
lead file existed for this subject before this pass.

## What is established

**The one documented pilot.** Taipei introduced 6 CNG buses in 1999
(民國88年) with a slow-fill CNG station in Neihu; the programme is
described as having failed. Source: 財團法人車輛研究測試中心 (ARTC), 「古老、
卻長遠的汽車燃料--天然氣在車輛上的應用」, https://www.artc.org.tw/tw/knowledge/articles/67,
author 王順立 (車輛中心環保能源部/柴油車課), dated 2009/12/11. SECONDARY for
the Taipei claim (ARTC reporting on a third party's history), PRIMARY for
ARTC's own conversion-kit testing work described in the same article.
Medium confidence — single source, fetched in full, not independently
corroborated by any Taipei city, MOTC, news or operator source despite an
extensive search. Quoted: 「台北市於民國88年引進6輛壓縮天然氣公車，並且在台北
內湖蓋了一座慢速加氣站，但因為車輛保養不易、巡航距離較短、加氣站太少且加氣速度過慢、
天然氣品質不穩定等因素導致計畫最後宣告失敗。」

**No manufacturer, importer, operator or model named anywhere.** Searched:
operator-name-specific queries (首都客運, 三重客運, 大都會, 光華巴士, 大有巴士,
中興巴士), chassis-brand queries (三菱扶桑, 五十鈴, 日野), zh.wikipedia
articles for 光華巴士 and 臺北市市區公車 (both fetched in full, neither
mentions CNG), general 「CNG公車 台灣」/「天然氣巴士」searches. Not found, not
contradicted — genuinely absent from what could be reached.

**ARTC's own conversion-kit testing.** Same article, PRIMARY for ARTC's
own work: diesel-to-CNG conversion kit testing, pollutant-emission
comparison against unconverted diesel, and a planned LNG fuel-system test
rig to help domestic manufacturers localise conversion kits. Medium-High
confidence (fetched in full; undated beyond the 2009 article; no test
report itself located). Frames CNG in Taiwan bus context as a retrofit
technology, not a purpose-built model — consistent with the absence of
any VSCC type-approval entry.

**No VSCC CNG type-approval list exists.** VSCC's only large-bus
type-approval zone found is the electric-bus programme
(https://www.vscc.org.tw/Home/List/485). Its separate coach-chassis
registry (https://www.vscc.org.tw/Home/List/5) lists exactly three
documents, none referencing natural gas. PRIMARY, High confidence — the
actual index page was fetched and read, not just a web search of the
domain.

**Taiwan's actual documented alternative-fuel programme in this era used
LPG, not CNG.** An EPA-run "油氣雙燃料車" programme ran roughly 2008–2012.
Found via search-result summaries (磁誌/能源知識庫 article and 環境部 pages),
NOT independently fetched in full this pass — Low confidence, carried
only as a boundary/conflation risk, not asserted as fact. A caught error:
one WebSearch AI summary conflated taxi LPG conversion ("瓦斯車") with the
CNG bus claim in the same paragraph; a direct re-fetch of the ARTC source
confirmed it does not discuss taxi conversion at all. Published as a
caught error, not repeated.

## Stated gaps

No manufacturer, chassis brand, body builder or operator is named
anywhere for the 1999 pilot — without a name to search on, no further
verification path is open from what has been found. A 1999–2001 Taipei
city government press release or budget document, if one survives in an
accessible archive, would settle this. Whether "壓縮天然氣公車" meant
purpose-built vehicles or diesel conversions is not stated. VSCC did not
exist until 2009; pre-2009 large-bus type-approval ran through a
different body under different rules, not verified this pass. Current
status, vehicle counts, route numbers and an operating end date are all
TBC. Whether any other Taiwan city ever ran CNG buses was searched only
for Kaohsiung (nothing found); not exhaustively searched for Taichung or
Taoyuan.
