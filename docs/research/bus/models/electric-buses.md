## The VSCC/MOTC qualified-model registry

交通部 authorises 財團法人車輛安全審驗中心 (VSCC) to run the type-approval
process (車輛型式安全審驗) that certifies vehicles for production/import sale,
and VSCC hosts the "交通部電動大客車推動計畫專區" web zone on MOTC's behalf.

**What VSCC's own page says about the process** (PRIMARY, High confidence,
fetched in full): "交通部授權本中心針對各式車輛在量產或進口上市銷售前，應對車輛
之安全及規格符合性執行必要之檢測及審查，經審驗合格後核發「車輛型式安全審驗合格
證明書」" — https://www.vscc.org.tw/Home/Content/520.

**The governing regulation** is 車輛型式安全審驗管理辦法, issued under 公路法
第63條第5項. Source: 全國法規資料庫, https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCODE=K0040065,
PRIMARY, High. Certificate validity is two years from approval (Art. 11①:
"有效期限為審驗合格日起二年") — this explains why the current disclosure list
(below) carries a section for models whose certificate lapsed without renewal.
Bus-specific submission requirements (Art. 6①(8)-(9)) require chassis-body
construction specifications and, for Class A/B buses, durability-performance
test verification documents.

## Model T — corrected: manufactured by Foxtron (鴻華先進科技), not Tron Energy

**This corrects a hypothesis in the run brief that assumed Tron Energy made
"Model T."** It does not. Model T was independently developed by 鴻華先進科技
股份有限公司 (Foxtron, the Hon Hai/Yulon EV joint venture) and delivered to
高雄客運 (a 三地集團/Sandie Group subsidiary), 3 March 2022, initial target 30
buses. Source: Hon Hai Technology Group press release,
https://www.foxconn.com/zh-tw/press-center/press-releases/latest-news/747,
PRIMARY, High — the manufacturer's own group publishing about its own
product. Original: "鴻華先進科技自主開發的電動巴士MODEL T…於3日正式交付三地集團
旗下高雄客運，初期目標數量30輛。"

Independently confirmed by the VSCC registry above: 鴻華先進科技股份有限公司
(brand "鴻華先進(順益)", model ZA1CMA) is listed as a certified manufacturer
**separately from** 華德動能 in the same government table — the two companies
are distinct applicants under the same programme.

The existing content page `model-t-electric.md`, sourced only to a New
Taipei government press release naming 13 San Chung Bus vehicles on route
857, never named a manufacturer at all — the run brief's assumption that
Tron Energy made it was a hypothesis, not something the existing page
asserted. It should be corrected to name Foxtron as the manufacturer, citing
the Foxconn press release above, with a note distinguishing it from Tron
Energy's unrelated RAC-700.
