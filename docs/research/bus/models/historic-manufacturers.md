# Historic and domestic Taiwan bus manufacturers

Research pass, 28 August 2026 (Run 303, Part 5). Genuinely thin subject —
the detailed operator-by-operator fleet documentation that exists
(zh.wikipedia + zh.wikiversity "使用車輛"/"歷史車種" pages) is almost
entirely post-2001. No prior `docs/research/` lead file existed for this
subject. Does not overlap `docs/research/bus/models/imported-diesel-chassis.md`
(current-era Hino/Isuzu/Mercedes/Volvo/Scania/MAN only).

## What is established

**1976 Mercedes-Benz L508D, 1980 Hino BM400/BY420.** zh.wikipedia,
「公共小型巴士」, https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/公共小型巴士, SECONDARY, Medium.
Taipei's first air-conditioned route (中1/601) began 1 July 1976 with 30
Mercedes-Benz L508D; 60 Hino BM400 small air-conditioned buses were added
6 September 1980, plus Hino BY420 large air-conditioned buses on new
routes the same year, described as "自強公車" (self-strengthening buses)
after the 1979 US–Taiwan derecognition. Quoted verbatim on the second
fetch: 「1980年9月6日起，添購60輛[日野]BM400型小型冷氣巴士」. The 1976 figures
recurred consistently across two fetches but could not be locked to an
exact verbatim sentence the way the 1980 sentence was. **A sibling
Wikipedia article, 「空調巴士」, fetched in full, contains neither claim** —
recorded because it means the claim is not corroborated even within
Wikipedia's own coverage.

**1995 光華巴士 Hino LRG2KRB.** zh.wikiversity, 「光華巴士」,
https://zh.wikiversity.org/zh-tw/光華巴士, SECONDARY, Medium (uncited
enthusiast page). The oldest specific model+year found at operator level
for any of the 14 聯營 operators: a 1995-model-year Hino LRG2KRB, plus
retired Hino LRK2JMA (scrapped) and LRK2JMB (transferred to 基隆客運).

**Metropolitan Bus's post-2004 Isuzu/日盈機械 pairing.** zh.wikipedia,
「大都會客運」, https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/大都會客運, SECONDARY, Medium
(fetched twice, consistent both times; the article carries Wikipedia's own
missing-sources banner for this passage). After the 2004 privatisation,
new purchases used imported Isuzu large-bus chassis bodied domestically
by 日盈機械, later renamed 國際山霖汽車, which subsequently bought shares in
Metropolitan Bus from former employees. After 日盈機械/國際山霖汽車's closure,
purchases shifted to Daewoo vehicles via 成運汽車 (2010 Flower Expo livery,
2011 first Daewoo low-floor since 2001, 2011 Foton hybrid low-floor).

**國際山霖汽車's closure date — a real, unresolved conflict.** zh.wikipedia
(above) says "late 2009" (bankruptcy). An independent registry mirror,
台灣公司網, https://www.twincn.com/27781611, SECONDARY, Low-Medium
(registry mirror, not the Ministry of Economic Affairs' own portal),
gives a formal dissolution date of 23 October 2013. Neither source
explains the gap. Published as a conflict, not resolved.

**Domestic body-builders named at operator level** (all zh.wikiversity,
uncited, Medium at best, fetched in full per operator): 健益 (大有巴士's
Mitsubishi Fuso RK127JL, 60 units 2002–2004); 三坤/金龍車體 (光華巴士's Hino
RN8JRSA, 10 units 2008 — two different name readings for what may be the
same builder, not resolved); 柏昇車體 (a body variant for 指南客運's
Mitsubishi Fuso RM11FN2XC, 2026 batch); 豐榮 (大南汽車's Hino ERK2JRL/
ERK2JML, and separately a Daewoo BC211MA for 指南客運); 成運 (成運汽車),
named repeatedly across 大都會客運, 大南汽車, 指南客運, 中興巴士 as both Daewoo
distributor and body-builder in its own right.

**中興巴士's own brand characterisation.** zh.wikipedia, 「中興巴士」,
https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/中興巴士, SECONDARY, Medium, quoted
verbatim: 「中興巴士採用的車輛廠牌以日本製造的日野及三菱扶桑為主」 — undated, no
footnote. Same article/company-history section: founded as 大業客運 (June
1963) → 中興大業巴士 (18 January 1966) → joined 聯營公車 (1977) → 淡水客運
took control (January 1981) → shortened to 中興巴士 in the late 1990s.

## What was searched and found nothing

**China Motor Corporation (中華汽車工業)** — zh.wikipedia's own article,
fetched in full, covers 1969 onward: trucks, light commercial vehicles,
cars, vans, electric motorcycles. No bus or bus-chassis product anywhere
in the article. Not found, not a claim it never existed.

**Ford Liuho / 六和汽車** — founded 4 March 1969, Toyota-technology light
trucks/sedans from 1970, became Ford Liuho after Ford's 1972–73 share
acquisition. No source found connects it to bus/coach chassis, historic
or current.

**San Fu / 三富汽車** — founded 1966, Fuji Heavy Industries then Renault
licences, later contract-assembled Isuzu Trooper/Panther and Hyundai
Accent (1997), ceased 1999. No mention found of San Fu building bus
bodies — a dead end against the run brief's own lead.

## Stated gaps

1970s bus models for the municipal operator and the four private
operators admitted from 1969 (欣欣, 大有, 大南, 光華): nothing found for any
of them before joint operation began in 1977. The fifteen years between
the 1980 Hino purchase and the 1995 光華巴士 record: no source at all.
Whether 裕隆 (Yulon)'s "平頭大客車" product line ever reached Taipei
city-bus service, as opposed to intercity/tour coach or export: unstated
by Yulon's own history page. Company registration, founding dates and
whether any predate the 2000s for 健益, 三坤/金龍車體, 柏昇車體, 豐榮: none
found beyond a single uncited enthusiast page each.
