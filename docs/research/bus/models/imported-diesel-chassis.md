# Imported diesel bus and coach chassis in Taiwan

Research pass, 28 August 2026, covering Hino, Isuzu, Mercedes-Benz, Volvo,
Scania and MAN — all chassis suppliers for Taiwan's highway-coach
(國道客運), tour-coach (遊覽車) and, for Hino/Isuzu, city-bus market, with
Taiwanese coachbuilders constructing the bodies. No existing
`docs/research/` file covers any of these six brands; this is a first
pass on all of them.

**A structural finding worth stating up front**: VSCC publishes a
searchable disclosure list for electric-bus type approvals (see
`electric-buses.md`), but no equivalent public list was found for diesel
chassis. The one relevant VSCC page located, "大客車底盤重心位置圖" (large
coach chassis centre-of-gravity diagrams), https://www.vscc.org.tw/Home/List/166,
PRIMARY, lists MAN documents (19.400 CO, 19.510 CO, ~10 documents
2017–2023) but no Volvo or Scania documents on the same page. Whether
Volvo/Scania simply aren't there, or are listed elsewhere not found this
pass, is unresolved.

## Hino (日野)

**和泰汽車 (Hotai Motor) has been Hino's Taiwan distributor since 1952**
(zh.wikipedia, "和泰汽車", SECONDARY, Medium — corroborated by Hotai's own
site: "總代理：和泰汽車股份有限公司", https://www.hino.com.tw/, PRIMARY, High).
**國瑞汽車 (Kuozui Motors)**, a Toyota (65%)/Hotai (30%)/Hino (5%) joint
venture founded 9 April 1984, domestically assembles Hino trucks and
buses at its 觀音 plant (zh.wikipedia, "國瑞汽車", SECONDARY, Medium).
**長源汽車 (Chang Yuan Motor)** is Hotai Group's dealer specialising in
Hino/Toyota commercial-vehicle sales (17 branches nationwide), per its own
site, https://www.cymotor.com.tw/about, PRIMARY, High; whether it is
100%-owned by Hotai is unconfirmed.

**HS8J** — Taiwan-first low-floor city bus, assembled by Kuozui, Euro-5
version launched late 2015 after Hotai lobbied Hino directly; succeeded by
the Euro-6 **HS2A** in 2020 (zh.wikipedia, "日野HS8J", SECONDARY, Medium —
the article's own two cited sources both failed to fetch, so this rests
on an unverified-against-primary wiki claim). **RK8J/RK2A** — described as
"the mainstay large-bus type in the Taipei metro area," but the wiki
article carries zero citations of its own; treat every figure in it as an
unsourced lead. Current live-site chassis (fetched directly, PRIMARY,
High, https://www.hino.com.tw/): **RK2ARXA-PJF** (A05C-TL engine, 260ps,
GVW 14,500kg) and **RM1ASXU** (360P-class, A09C-VN engine, 360ps, GVW
18,000kg); the low-floor HS/HS2A model does not currently appear in the
live site's large-bus navigation — unclear whether discontinued or a
fetch-tool limitation on a JS-driven page.

**Conflict**: 台北客運's own wiki article names a Hino low-floor bus (route
795, 弘鉅 body) in a photo caption with no citation; deployment quantities
(46 units at a Taichung-area operator, 109 at Taipei Bus) exist only in an
unfetched search summary and are **not published**. Every TWBusforum wiki
page attempted for Hino returned HTTP 402.

## Isuzu (五十鈴)

**Current distributor: 台北合眾汽車有限公司.** Two sources conflict on when
it took over the agency: **2012** (isuzu-tw.com, an apparent dealer/salesperson
site, SECONDARY, Low-Medium) versus **2014** (the distributor's own "About"
page, hosted at an unusual `jetbean.com.tw` domain rather than the main
`isuzu.com.tw`, PRIMARY-but-Medium-confidence given the hosting anomaly).
Not resolved. The 2012-dated account additionally gives a fuller history:
first Taiwan exports 1952 via 國際汽車; domestic manufacture 1996–2007 by
太子汽車工業 under a joint arrangement, halted after 太子's parent bank
collapsed in a fraud scandal in 2007.

**Current bus chassis** (fetched directly from isuzu.com.tw, PRIMARY,
High): **NQR(Bus) 8.5t** — engine 4HK1, 190PS, Euro-5-equivalent without
urea injection; **LT Bus 14.5ton** — tonnage only, no further specs
rendered. No full-size route-bus or highway-coach models (Erga, Gala,
Journey) were found sold or deployed in Taiwan — zh.wikipedia's own Isuzu
article lists these as part of the global lineup but names no Taiwan
connection at all. No named operator or deployed-quantity claim survived
to a fetched source; every U-CAR/TWBusforum lead 403'd or 402'd.

## Mercedes-Benz (賓士/戴姆勒)

**裕益汽車股份有限公司 (Yuyi Motors)**, part of 順益集團 (Shun Yi Group, founded
1967), is the general distributor for Daimler Mercedes-Benz large buses —
"連續30年銷售NO.1(1992～2021年)" for commercial vehicles ≥3.49 tonnes in its
own framing (裕益汽車 "Company Profile", https://daimler.truck-bus.com.tw/about.php,
PRIMARY, High). Group timeline (順益集團's own site,
https://www.auto.com.tw/about_20.htm, PRIMARY, High): 2005 Actros
truck-assembly licence; 2017 "台灣戴姆勒亞洲商車股份有限公司" begins operating;
2018 the **O500** coach series ("全球頂級大客車") introduced.

**O500 (MB 1843/MB1843AE)**: OM457 engine, 428ps/315kW @ 2,000rpm, 214kgm
@ 1,100rpm, MB GO240-8 transmission, BlueTec SCR (裕益汽車 spec page,
PRIMARY, High). **OC500**: the first bus Daimler/EvoBus designed
specifically for Taiwan (chassis and body together), assembled locally by
Shun Yi, launched 10 March 2021, 33/35/36/45-seat configurations
(corroborated by two independent secondary sources — TravelerLuxe and
ETtoday — agreeing on the date and seating, but **no primary distributor
page dating the launch was found**, so this specific date rests on
secondary corroboration only). OC500 spec page gives OM470 engine, "符合
中華民國環保署六期排放標準" — **the power/torque figures (428ps/214kgm) are
numerically identical to the O500's OM457 spec despite a different engine
and different peak rpm**, an internal peculiarity of the source worth
independent verification, not smoothed over here.

**Deployment**: 文境通運旅遊集團 received the 500th OC500 unit under this
programme on 21 February 2025 (裕益汽車's own press release, PRIMARY, High,
corroborated independently by a trade-press article giving the same date/
count and additional testing detail — 20,000km durability run at ARTC).
文境通運 operates 312 vehicles across 6 bus operators, 2 car-rental
companies and 1 travel agency, for charter/long-term-rental/shuttle work —
**no source found names an OC500/O500 in fixed public-route service**, only
charter/tour/contract use.

## Volvo

**太古商用汽車 (Taikoo Motors Group)** has been Volvo's Taiwan
truck/bus distributor since 1978 (own site,
https://www.taikoomotors.com.tw/distributor/, PRIMARY, High). **大吉汽車
(Daji Motors)**, founded 1975, has been Volvo's sole certified/
technology-transfer coachbuilder for Taiwan since 2014, building current
models New B8R, B8RLE and All New B13R (own site, PRIMARY, High). The
**B11R** coach (Euro 6, 460hp) uses a Volvo D11 engine and 12-speed
I-Shift transmission per Daji's own product page (PRIMARY, High); Volvo's
own regional B11R page timed out on fetch, so its spec claims are Medium
confidence only.

**唐榮車輛科技 (Tang Eng Vehicle Technology)** was licensed by Volvo in 2004
as its 16th global assembly plant, assembling roughly 2,000 Volvo trucks
and buses at Luzhu, Kaohsiung under a 1,350-unit contract with Taikoo
(via a Hong Kong entity), ending November 2011 (CTWANT, SECONDARY,
Medium — corroborates, independently, the same 2004/Luzhu facts already
in `electric-buses.md`'s Tang Eng section, sourced there to the same
outlet). Tang Eng's own site remains unreachable (`ECONNREFUSED`),
consistent with the Tang Eng scout's finding in the electric-buses
research pass.

**Conflict, published not resolved**: Daji's own client list and
zh.wikipedia's 大吉汽車 article both name 國光客運 (Kuo-Kuang) as a
Volvo-body recipient, but zh.wikipedia's own 國光客運 fleet article (as of
its stated "December 2024" snapshot) lists only Daewoo models and makes no
mention of Volvo at all. Neither source explains the discrepancy — a small/
older Volvo batch not reflected in the fleet summary, an affiliated
company operating them instead, or an incomplete Wikipedia fleet list are
all unconfirmed possibilities. Other named Volvo-body clients from the
same wiki list (金浦運輸, 三重客運, 府城客運, 興南客運, 新竹客運, 臺西客運) are
not independently verified.

## Scania

**永德福汽車** (a Taiwan branch of a British Virgin Islands-registered
entity) is Scania's Taiwan distributor, confirmed on Scania Taiwan's own
site footer (PRIMARY, High). zh.wikipedia's Scania article (SECONDARY,
Medium, unfootnoted) states Scania took back general-agency rights from
福方集團's 商富貿易 subsidiary at the end of 2005 and invested NT$600
million from 2006 building 10 service centres — not independently
corroborated by a news primary.

**K400IB4X2NB and K380IB4X2NB** coaches are used by 統聯客運 (Tonglian/
Ubus)'s dedicated tour-coach subsidiary, which bought 30 Scania units in
2012 per zh.wikipedia (SECONDARY, Medium, unfootnoted) — but Ubus's own
site names no vehicle brand at all, so this is not independently
corroborated by the operator itself. A far larger, more granular fleet
breakdown (hundreds of units, 2014–2026 registrations, by route-service
vs. tour-service) appears only on zh.wikiversity, a lower-authority
community wiki — **published here only as a flagged, Low-confidence lead,
not a fact.**

## MAN

**德曼汽車集團 (Deman Auto Group)**, via subsidiaries 至遠企業 (trucks),
德揚實業 (luxury coaches/chassis) and 至懋汽車 (after-sales), holds
exclusive Taiwan distribution for MAN Truck & Bus effective 1 July 2018
(trade-press interview, ChatCV, SECONDARY, Medium, independently
corroborated on the 2018 date by a second trade-press article). By H1
2018, MAN coaches ranked second in Type-A coach sales — "the best sales
performance since MAN's Taiwan entry" — and by late 2019, 400 units sold
in eighteen months, per the same interview.

**MAN 19.400 and MAN 19.510** coach chassis carry Taiwan government
centre-of-gravity type-approval documents (VSCC, https://www.vscc.org.tw/Home/List/166,
PRIMARY, High) — the only manufacturer of the six with a confirmed public
VSCC document trail found this pass. 德揚實業 markets four MAN-based coach
body/finish variants under Taiwanese coachbuilder names (own site,
PRIMARY, Medium — marketing copy, no engine/transmission/seating specs
given). 怡心遊覽 introduced MAN 19.400 coaches in 2016 per an unfetched
search summary only (the operator's own page 403'd) — **not published as
a confirmed fact.**
