## Conflicts

1. **小黃公車 route count and district coverage:** the full Q&A page says 52 routes and 32 districts, while the full 5 August 2026 expansion notice says that adding 黃3, 黃16 and 黃23 took the network from 46 to 49 routes and 31 districts. Both are official Tainan Transportation Bureau pages. The sources do not say whether this is a publication date, route-scope or counting-method difference. The content page publishes both values and the JSON preserves the Q&A's 52 unique route labels without treating it as a resolution.
2. **Static catalogue versus live service:** the data.gov.tw route and stop pages describe TDX-backed structured data, while the city portal exposes dynamic bus information and timetable navigation. The TDX snapshot has source update timestamps, but neither full page establishes that a static snapshot is a current departure timetable. The content therefore keeps timetable history TBC.
3. **Ridership year scope versus route snapshot scope:** the ridership catalogue is an annual route-month series in Minguo years 108–114, while the route and stop responses are current structural snapshots with `UpdateTime` values. The annual sums are not presented as a current live total and are not used to infer route service frequency.

## Stated gaps

- Route-level departure times and a historical timetable series: the fetched TDX route and stop records carry structure and update timestamps, but not a complete dated schedule history. The official portal timetable resources or the city's daily timetable dataset would settle this.
- Vehicle allocation, depot assignment, accessibility inventory, reliability and contract history: the fetched route, stop, operator and ridership sources do not provide a complete series for these fields. Tainan Transportation Bureau, operator, vehicle and accessibility records would settle them.
- The 52-versus-49 小黃公車 disagreement: a dated route registry or an official statement explaining the two counts would settle whether the difference is timing, scope or counting method.
- Transfer hubs: the official catalogue page was read, but the full JSON resource failed to fetch in this environment. A successful full resource fetch would settle the current hub count and field values.
