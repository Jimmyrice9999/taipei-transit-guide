## What is established

### 1. Trunk-bus colours are direction codes, and the PTO says so

- **Claim.** On 幹線公車 trunk routes, colour encodes the *compass direction of
  the corridor*: green for north–south, blue for east–west, yellow for anything
  that is neither.
- **Source.** "Promoting the Taipei trunk-bus identity design programme" /
  `titleOriginal`: 推動臺北市幹線公車識別設計計畫
- **Publisher.** 臺北市公共運輸處 (Taipei City Public Transport Office)
- **URL.** https://pto.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=D065CCB1467288C8&sms=72544237BBE4C5F6&s=57ADA5C84D54162C
- **Date.** 發布日期 106-04-20 (20 April 2017)
- **Kind.** PRIMARY — the regulator publishing its own design programme.
- **Confidence.** High; fetched in full, and the sentence quoted below was
  returned verbatim on a second fetch that asked for untranslated Chinese.
- **Original sentence.**
  > 幹線公車依幹線路廊方向賦予其專屬代表色彩，南北向為綠色，東西向為藍色，非屬前述者為黃色

- **And the colours carry stated symbolism**, which is the tell that this is a
  branding scheme and not a wayfinding code inherited from the metro:
  > 藍色代表「幹線系統」、「城市」，綠色代表「綠意」、「生活美好」，黃色代表「陽光」、「希望」

**This settles the collision the site has been chasing for two runs.** Trunk
green means *north–south*; feeder 綠 means *Songshan–Xindian Line*. Same colour
word, two unrelated meanings, on the same network, both current.

**Caveat that must travel with it:** only *half* of the collision is
primary-sourced. Nothing found this run is a PTO publication stating that feeder
prefixes 紅/綠/藍/棕/橘 are metro line colours. That side rests on zh.wikipedia
and on the news reports at §3 below. Do not describe the feeder rule as
PTO-published.

### 4. How the money actually moves — four primary regulations, read in full

The assigned question was the revenue pool. **The pool is not sourced** (§5).
What *is* sourced, and was not on the page before this run, is a complete and
primary account of the four money-and-route mechanisms the city actually
operates. All four were fetched in full from the Taipei city law database.

**(a) Routes are allocated to operators, by points or by tender.**

- 臺北市聯營公車申請分配調整路線及車次處理原則 (法規類號 北市07－04－3006),
  臺北市公共運輸處, 修正日期 民國102年03月04日 (4 March 2013).
- URL: https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL055169
- PRIMARY. Confidence High.
- 第三點, verbatim fragments returned:
  > 本處分配聯營公車路線或重新分配被撤銷之路線時，得由累計獲有路線積分分數最多之
  > 聯營公車業者優先選線 1條，並扣除路線積分10分

  > 公開徵詢公車業者經營意願，由「臺北都會區市區及公路客運路線審議委員會」評選經營業者
- 第四點 — **the city can take a profitable route off an operator and re-let
  it.** The heading is 「本處收回業者高營收路線接駛原則」, and the successor
  operator gets 5 years:
  > 接駛業者營運期限 5年，營運期屆滿前 1年向本處提出續營申請並提送「臺北都會區市區
  > 及公路客運路線審議委員會」審議，續營期限最多 3年

  > 營運路線應維持原動線且 1年內不得申請調整，經主管機關要求調整者不在此限
- Definitions worth keeping: 里程 is **single-direction** km
  (「以單程之公里數計算」); 車次 is a **round trip**
  (「往返2班次等於1車次」). Both matter for reading the subsidy formula below.
- Predecessor rule, now repealed, same points logic:
  臺北市聯營公車路線分配作業須知 (北市07－04－2006), 臺北市政府交通局,
  發布 民國88年9月20日, 停止適用 88年9月30日.
  https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL012594
  > 分配聯營公車路線時，由累計獲有路線積分權分數最多之聯營公車單位優先選線。

**(b) Operating losses are subsidised route by route, on a stated formula.**

- 臺北市聯營公車服務路線營運虧損補貼審議作業規定 (法規類號 北市07－04－3017),
  臺北市政府交通局, 發布 民國102年3月25日, 文號 102北市交運字第10210926400號.
- URL: https://laws.gov.taipei/law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL026380
- PRIMARY. Confidence High.
- Made under a **central** regulation, not a city one:
  > 特依交通部令頒大眾運輸事業補貼辦法訂定本作業規定
- 第七條, the formula:
  > （每車公里合理營運成本－每車公里實際營運收入）×（車次數）×（路線往返里程）
- 第五條, the eligibility test:
  > 公車業者至前1年度12月底仍繼續經營之本市聯營公車路線，因其每車公里營收低於
  > 合理營運成本，得申請補貼
- 第八條 — cost sharing between the two levels of government, for routes ≤30 km
  and ≤30 車次/day: **central 1/3, Taipei City 2/3**.
- 第五條 also sets three priority tiers: 第1優先, 第2優先, and **捷運接駁** —
  metro feeders are a named subsidy priority class.
- 第九條 — an operator drawing more than **NT$10 million** in approved subsidy
  across two years can have its route put out to open recruitment. New-route
  franchises run **5 years**.

**This is the load-bearing structural point.** The subsidy is computed against
**每車公里實際營運收入 — actual operating revenue per bus-kilometre, on that
route, for that operator.** Revenue is therefore attributed to individual routes
and individual operators in the city's own regulation. Any account of a
central pool has to be reconciled with that, and none of the sources that assert
the pool attempts to.

**(c) Concessionary fare differences are reimbursed monthly, per operator.**

- 臺北市聯營公車票價差額補貼作業執行要點, 臺北市政府交通局,
  發布 民國103年4月7日 (北市交運字第10330749201號令), 生效 103年5月1日.
- URL: https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL021697
- PRIMARY. Confidence Medium-High — fetched in full, but the fetch returned
  short quoted fragments inside an English scaffold rather than continuous
  Chinese.
- Basis: 發展大眾運輸條例 第九條. The subsidy is the gap between a concessionary
  fare and the single tariff: 「票價與單一運價間之差額，由政府編列預算補貼之金額」.
- Recipients: 「經營本市聯營公車路線之公車業者」 — the operators, individually.
- Cycle: 「價差補貼款之審核核撥週期：每月1次」, operators file by the 20th of the
  following month.
- Audited against the **card issuers'** records — 路線別補貼申請表, 營運明細表,
  刷卡紀錄表 — and docked for 異常刷卡, over-age vehicles, insurance lapses,
  missing announcement equipment and wage non-compliance.

**(d) A standing city committee reviews costs, fares and subsidies.**

- 臺北市聯營公車營運與服務品質督導及評鑑委員會設置要點 — the version fetched is
  marked **(廢) repealed**; a successor exists but was not read this run.
- URL: https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL012279
- PRIMARY. Confidence High for the text as read; **Low that it is the current
  rule.**
- 17 members, convened by the 交通局局長, including one seat each for 交通部,
  交通部運輸研究所, 消費者文教基金會 and — notably — **臺北市公車聯營管理委員會**,
  the operators' own body.
- 第三點 tasks include 「定期檢討聯營公車營運成本及票價事項」 and
  「聯營公車單位營運服務補貼案之審核事項」.

### C5 — Where did the 幹線 colour policy leave the feeder prefixes?

Not a source conflict but a real network-level contradiction, now documented on
both sides:

- **棕9** (a Wenhu Line feeder) became **南京幹線**; **紅32** became **民權幹線**;
  **220** became **中山幹線** (zh.wikipedia, SECONDARY).
- Trunk routes then take a *directional* colour under the PTO's April 2017 rule.
- So a route can lose a colour that meant "metro line" and gain a colour that
  means "compass bearing", and neither the PTO nor zh.wikipedia notes the clash.
