# New Taipei Large Rehabilitation Bus

## Conflicts and measurement boundaries

- The page publishes `每車每小時606元` as a charter price and a separate
  mileage/segment formula. They are different fare methods and are not averaged.
- The three listed companies and their daily booking availability are contact
  channels, not proof of the total number of vehicles operated by the programme.
- The four-city service boundary belongs to this large group programme and is
  not generalized to Taipei's small rehabilitation bus or to universal taxis.
