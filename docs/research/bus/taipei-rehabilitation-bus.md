# Taipei Small Rehabilitation Bus

**Checked 6 September 2026.** This pass fetched and read the full Taipei Public
Transportation Office booking-rules page and service-current page. It records
the open/eligibility boundary, booking and fare rules, late-night extension,
special-area expansion and named outsourced operators. It does not establish a
current fleet total or vehicle-by-vehicle accessibility census.

## What is established

- **Claim.** Taipei describes the service as point-to-point, door-to-door,
  advance-reservation transport, with service-level categories based on the
  disability certificate.
- **Source.** `How to reserve the small rehabilitation bus and what are the
  rules?` / `小型復康巴士如何預約訂車?管理規定為何?`.
- **URL.** https://pto.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=6B4D38874E971F4B&s=552F428E9CF5508D&sms=87415A8B9CE81B16
- **Kind.** PRIMARY. **Confidence.** High — full page fetched; page data was
  updated 25 August 2026 and reviewed 26 August 2026.
- **Original-language evidence.** `提供點對點預約運輸服務` and `屬戶到戶及門運輸`.

- **Claim.** The standard service area is the Taipei joint-operation bus service
  area with one endpoint in Taipei. The standard fare is one-third of Taipei
  taxi fare, and a shared trip uses 66% discounted mileage.
- **Source and URL.** The same full booking-rules page above.
- **Confidence.** High.
- **Original-language evidence.** `臺北市聯營公車服務區域，起點或迄點必須位於臺北市內`,
  `比照臺北市之計程車費率之3分之1計算` and `以66%優惠計之`.

- **Claim.** The ordinary service window is 06:00–23:00, with a limited 23:00–06:00
  extension of ten vehicles; the page also gives booking windows and the
  customer-centre/voice-booking numbers.
- **Source and URL.** The same full booking-rules page above.
- **Confidence.** High.

- **Claim.** The Public Transportation Office's service-current page says all
  small rehabilitation buses have wheelchair-lift equipment and names Eden
  Social Welfare Foundation, Yuan San Car Rental and Shi Hao Passenger Car
  Rental as outsourced operators.
- **Source.** `Service current status` / `服務現況`.
- **URL.** https://pto.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=3B812B093D7FF7E8
- **Kind.** PRIMARY. **Confidence.** High for the page's stated arrangement;
  the page's displayed content was last updated 6 August 2024 and reviewed 13

- **Original-language evidence.** `臺北市小型復康巴士每輛皆裝有輪椅昇降設備` and
  `分別委由財團法人伊甸社會福利基金會、元三租車股份有限公司及世豪小客車租賃有限公司營運`.

## Conflicts and measurement boundaries

- The booking-rules page gives the current operational booking/fare boundary;
  the service-current page is older and is used only for the named outsourced
  operators and wheelchair-lift statement. Their dates are not silently merged.
- The expanded service area for the highest-priority category has different
  booking and fare rules from the standard Taipei service area. It is kept as a
  labelled exception, not a national or all-user boundary.
