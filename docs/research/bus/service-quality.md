# Municipal bus service-quality evaluation — research

**Corpus warning: `docs/research/` is leads only. Nothing here is citable until it has been checked against a primary source — see `.claude/skills/transit-research/SKILL.md`.**

**Checked 7 September 2026.** Full official HTML and PDF material was read for Kaohsiung, Taichung, Tainan and Pingtung. The page deliberately compares publication state and measurement boundaries, not scores.

## What is established

- **Kaohsiung maintains a multi-period bus service-quality archive.** The full Transportation Bureau page lists the 113年度 report dated 114-09-16, reports for 112, 111, 110, 109 and 108, and older evaluation documents. **Source:** `https://www.tbkc.gov.tw/Service/PublicTransport/BusPlan`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched 2026-09-07. Original-language basis: `歷年公車服務品質評鑑成果` and the archive rows for `113年度` through `108年度`.
- **Taichung's complete 109年度 summary defines a five-category, 32-indicator evaluation and names 17 operators with 234 assessed routes.** **Source:** `https://www.traffic.taichung.gov.tw/df_ufiles/b/109%E5%B9%B4%E5%85%AC%E8%BB%8A%E8%A9%95%E9%91%91%E7%B5%90%E6%9E%9C%E6%91%98%E8%A6%81.pdf`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched/read 2026-09-07. Original-language basis: `五大項評鑑項目共計32個評鑑指標`, `17家客運業者` and `受評路線數計234條(含聯營路線)`.
- **Tainan's official listing publishes an 114年度 city-bus evaluation plan dated 115/04/21 and earlier 113年度 and 112年度 plans.** **Source:** `https://traffic.tainan.gov.tw/FileList/plansresearch?PageIndex=1&SubCategoryID=1776f9b7-3838-41dd-8689-76e65b32ae05`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched/read 2026-09-07. Original-language basis: `114年度臺南市市區公車營運與服務評鑑計畫`, `115/04/21`, and the rows for 113 and 112.
- **Pingtung adopted its evaluation execution rules on 18 November 2025 and completed its 112 and 113年度 city-bus evaluations in June 2026.** **Source:** National Audit Office / Pingtung County Audit Office, `https://www.audit.gov.tw/p/406-1000-11766,r12.php?Lang=zh-tw`, PRIMARY, High confidence, fetched/read 2026-09-07. Original-language basis: `縣政府已於114年11月18日訂定「屏東縣市區汽車客運業營運與服務評鑑執行要點」` and `於115年6月完成112及113年度市區公車服務品質評鑑作業`.

## Conflicts and boundaries

- The sources expose different measurement units: Taichung gives one detailed 109年度 report with 17 operators and 234 routes; Tainan's fetched page is a plan/document index; Kaohsiung's fetched page is an archive index; Pingtung's fetched page is an audit follow-up. No score is averaged or treated as comparable across cities.
- Pingtung's audit says results were to be published on the county transport-tourism site, but the fetched audit page itself contains no operator scores. Completion status is published; a current score/ranking remains TBC.
