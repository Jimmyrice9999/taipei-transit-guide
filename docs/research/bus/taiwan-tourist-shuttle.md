# Taiwan Tourist Shuttle — 台灣好行

**Research date: 6 September 2026.** This pass establishes the national
government-backed tourist public-transport layer and its boundary with ordinary
city buses, intercity coaches and attraction-only tours.

## What is established

### 1. Taiwan Tourist Shuttle is a public-transport tourism service

- **Claim.** The Tourism Administration describes Taiwan Tourist Shuttle as a
  public-transport tourism service planned from 2010 to connect HSR and TRA
  stations, coach-transfer centres and tourist attractions, including northern,
  central, southern, eastern and offshore areas.
- **Source.** “Taiwan Tourist Shuttle brand identity 3.0 wins 2026 German Red Dot
  Brand & Communication Design Award” / `titleOriginal`: 台灣好行品牌識別3.0榮獲2026德國紅點品牌與傳達設計獎
- **Publisher.** Ministry of Transportation and Communications, Tourism
  Administration (交通部觀光署)
- **URL.** https://www.motc.gov.tw/ch/app/news_list/view?id=14&module=news&serno=26b361d1-da19-480d-bacd-612c7b58fcfa
- **Date.** Published and updated 5 August 2026 (115-08-05).
- **Kind.** PRIMARY — the responsible national tourism authority describes its
  own public-transport service.
- **Confidence.** High — full official page fetched and the statement below was
  returned in the page body.
- **Original sentence.**
  > 「台灣好行景點接駁服務」是觀光署自99年起，為提升自由行便利性所規劃的公共運輸旅遊服務，透過串聯高鐵、臺鐵車站、客運轉運中心等重要交通場站及全臺各大觀光景點，提供旅客便利的接駁服務，解決觀光景點「最後一哩路」的交通問題，服務範圍涵蓋北、中、南、東部及離島地區。

## Boundary decisions

- The guide treats Taiwan Tourist Shuttle as scheduled or bookable,
  government-backed tourist public transport. It is linked from the bus section
  because the official service is a bus-based public-transport layer, but it is
  not merged into the ordinary city-bus route count.
- A route selector entry is a discovery record. A route page is not created for
  each row until a stable, route-specific primary record has been fetched and
  the page can carry its own current timetable, fare and service qualification.
- “郵輪式公車” entries remain visibly labelled as tourist/scheduled excursion
  services. The guide does not describe them as ordinary all-day city transit.
- Tourist Shuttle is distinct from a sightseeing cruise, a private charter or a
  ticketed attraction ride. Those may connect to the public network but are not
  silently included in this atlas.

## Conflicts and limits

The official homepage and selector are dynamic interfaces; the selector's
rendered route list and the Tourism Administration's “more than 100 themed
passes” figure measure different objects. Neither is used as a universal count
of all tourist buses in Taiwan.

The selector snapshot does not show Hsinchu City or Kaohsiung. No conclusion
about absence is published. Settling those gaps requires an official local
Tourist Shuttle or municipal tourism/bus source that can be fetched in full.

Current route-by-route fares, timetables, operators, accessibility details and
temporary suspensions remain outside this national overview unless the linked
official route page has been separately checked. The homepage's controls are
not treated as a substitute for those route-level records.
