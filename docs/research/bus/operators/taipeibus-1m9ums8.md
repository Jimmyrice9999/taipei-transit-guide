## Conflicts

1. **Route counts.** The operator's undated profile says 20 Taipei joint-operation
   routes, 63 New Taipei routes and four highway/road routes (87 in those three
   categories), while the committed TDX NewTaipei record has 126 associated route
   records. They are not silently reconciled: the first is a company-published
   operating snapshot with categories; the second is a later normalized data pull
   containing route records and operator associations. TDX duplicates, variants,
   and cross-municipality records may account for part of the difference, but this
   run did not establish a one-to-one mapping.

2. **Fleet count.** The company profile says 877 vehicles, while the 2023 New
   Taipei release establishes only 26 electric buses added to route 656 and the
   TDX operator record has no fleet field. The 877 figure is retained as the
   operator's published snapshot, not treated as a current count or as an electric
   fleet count.
