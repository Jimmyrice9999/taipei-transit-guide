## Conflicts

1. **Index date versus route-page dates.** The New Bus index shows an update date of 1 February 2023, while the fetched route-detail pages for F623, F625, F627, F628, F629 and F630 show 8 January 2026 updates. These are retained as different publication dates rather than collapsed into one “current” date.
2. **F626-0815 versus the official F626 detail page.** TDX has a distinct `F626-0815` route record, while the official F626 page presents one route with a note that the 13:30 and 18:30 trips do not run to Ziwei Tianhou Temple. The fetched sources do not establish whether the TDX suffix is a dispatch variant, a partial route, or a separate public timetable.
3. **Outsourcing evidence versus contract evidence.** The 2026 holiday table includes a `委外營運業者` field, but the fetched official pages do not publish a contractor name, tender number, contract value, award term or route-by-route allocation. The holiday-plan field is therefore not converted into a complete contract claim.
