## Conflicts

1. **Eight TDX associations versus nine official HTML labels.** The committed TDX snapshot has eight associations, including two separate F807 records, while the official page displays one F807 entry and additionally displays F813. Both representations are retained; neither is converted into a single “true” roster.
2. **Six-route holiday pilot versus the broader published route page.** The 2025 holiday notice names six pilot routes (F802, F802 auxiliary, F803, F805, F807 and F808), while the official New Bus page displays additional F801, F806 and F813 entries. The notice describes a dated pilot scope, not the complete New Bus network.
3. **Route 808 versus F808.** The road-bus page attributes regular route 808 to Keelung Bus, while the district office's New Bus and railway pages list F808 separately. The shared number is not treated as shared operation.
4. **Page ages.** The New Bus page says it was updated 6 November 2025, the road-bus page 29 October 2025, the holiday notice 29 May 2025 and the railway-transfer page 8 February 2021. These dates are published with the sources and are not presented as one undated current schedule.
