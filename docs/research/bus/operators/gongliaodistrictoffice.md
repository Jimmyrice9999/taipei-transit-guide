## Conflicts

1. **Current New Bus versus Happiness Bus scope.** The 2025 New Bus PDF presents F835–F839 and the Gongliao Junior High student service. The 2026 Happiness Bus PDF separately presents 福連線, 雙福線, 桃源谷線, 龍崗線, 吉林線, 美豐線 and 雙玉線, with different service-day and vehicle notes. The TDX snapshot combines the F835–F839/student rows with the route-name records 福連線, 龍崗線 and 吉林線; the page does not silently treat those records as one timetable.
2. **F831 history.** The January 2022 New Bus PDF includes F831 District Office–Fulian in both directions. The 2025 New Bus PDF fetched for the current route page does not include F831. The current TDX snapshot also has no F831 association; the historical route is retained as historical evidence, not a current service claim.
3. **Publication versus effective dates.** The district-office index labels the New Bus timetable as updated from 28 July 2025, while the PDF itself says it took effect from 16 July 2025. Both dates are retained with their roles rather than treated as a contradiction.
