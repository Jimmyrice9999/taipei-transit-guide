# 三重客運 / San Chung Bus — operator research

**Researched 20 August 2026.** The content pages are the Taipei and New Taipei
TDX operator records, not a claim that the two records are separate legal
entities.

## Conflicts

- **Establishment date:** the operator's own profile says 7 May 1968; the
  Workforce Development Agency employer record says 2 April 1968. Both values
  are published, and this page does not choose between them.
- **Evaluation scope:** the 2015 Highway Bureau report gives two different San
  Chung Bus results—81.87 / 甲 for the general-road table and 79.87 / 乙 for the
  national-road table. They measure different service categories, so neither
  replaces the other.
- **Route-record count:** TDX has 39 Taipei associations and 72 New Taipei
  associations. They are municipality-specific records, not one total fleet or
  an audited count of every route operated by the legal company.
