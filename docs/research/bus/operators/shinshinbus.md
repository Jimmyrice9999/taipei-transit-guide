## Conflicts

1. **Network count.** TDX has 57 Taipei associations; the operator page says Taipei/New Taipei have more than 70 routes. They represent different municipality/time scopes.
2. **Company and legal dates.** The company page says ROC year 58 January; MOEA gives 18 May 1969. January describes the operator’s stated founding month, while May is the registered establishment date.
3. **Current versus historical repair sites.** The live operator page publishes a Muzha repair shop; MOEA’s historical factory records show older Wanhua entries as closed/revoked. They are not silently treated as the same facility.
