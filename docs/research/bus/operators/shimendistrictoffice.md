## Conflicts

1. **TDX associations versus official service families.** TDX contains 36
   route/departure records, while the current office pages present four community
   route families plus F161 medical service. The source models differ: the TDX
   rows include departure labels, while the office pages group them by route family.
2. **F161 service scope.** The TDX record includes F161 and F161-0615, while the
   office medical-bus page describes F161 as both a school-term F153 support trip
   and a hospital service with a separate 08:00/12:00 pattern. The pages do not
   define a common route-record counting rule.
3. **Route-change chronology.** The December 2025 notice says F151 and F153 were
   adjusted and F154 was added; February and July 2026 notices record further F153
   and F151 changes. These are successive dated notices, not one consolidated
   route-history statement.
4. **Contract responsibility versus contract evidence.** The duties page names
   contract-execution management as an office responsibility, but the fetched
   primary pages do not name a contractor, award date, value or service term.
