## Conflicts

- TDX has one route association for the Royal Bus record, while the Taipei route page exposes six timed 1717 variants. These are different levels of representation: one parent route in the committed operator pull versus six subroute patterns in the current timetable, not six independently counted company routes.
- The company address in the official 2023 change list is a registered address in Jinshan, while the route page publishes separate Taipei and Jinshan service phones. No source says that either the registered address or a service-phone location is a depot, so no depot value is selected.
- The 2023 committee record says the company reported that electric buses could not climb 1717; the Yangmingshan page says low-floor buses are available on 1717. These describe different vehicle attributes and do not establish whether the low-floor vehicles are electric.
