## Conflicts

1. **Eight TDX records versus six official route families.** The committed TDX snapshot has eight associations, while the July 2026 Yingge introduction and the 2026 timetable name six route families. The difference is retained: TDX separately records F652 Extended Route and F652, and separately records F653A and F653B; the municipal page groups these under F652 and F653.
2. **Route-detail publication dates versus timetable effective date.** The F651, F652, F653, F655/F657 and F656 notices were published on 2 January 2026, 1 January 2025, 23 October 2025 and 20 May 2025, while the timetable says it takes effect from 1 January 2026 or an agency-notified date. These are retained as separate publication/effective-date statements rather than collapsed into a single date.
3. **Contract notice versus contractor evidence.** The office's contract page publishes passenger rules but no current route award; the TDX pull likewise has no contractor field. No contractor identity or contract term is inferred from either source.
