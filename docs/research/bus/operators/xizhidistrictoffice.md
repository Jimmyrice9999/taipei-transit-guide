## Conflicts

1. **TDX associations versus the current office index.** Both the committed TDX snapshot and the current official index contain 12 route entries, but they use different naming detail: the TDX route records include F909 Bo jiue / 伯爵 and F919 Ba lian / 八連, while the office page presents the official route names 伯爵線 and 八連線. The two are retained as separate data representations, not silently normalised into a new route source.
2. **Historical outsourced grouping versus current scope.** The 2020 government table groups older route sets as F901–F908 and F909–F920 and labels them with outsourced-operator contacts. The 2026 office index lists F901, F902, F903, F905, F907, F909, F910, F911, F913, F915, F919 and F920. The historical table includes route IDs no longer in the current index and does not establish today's contractor or route grouping.
3. **Fleet character versus fleet register.** The riding guidance documents a 19-seat rule for tour-bus vehicles and distinguishes dispatch groups, but neither it nor the current route index publishes a complete vehicle count, model roster or propulsion breakdown.
