## Conflicts

1. **Route count and naming.** TDX has ten operator associations, while Citiair's
   official route page displays eight labels. The sources do not define a common
   counting rule, so both values remain published.
2. **Evaluation scope.** Taipei's 114 city-bus sheets give Citiair 乙 in both
   periods. The Highway Bureau's highway table gives 112/109/107 乙 and 104 甲.
   These are different regulatory programmes and are not combined into one grade.
3. **Site scope.** Citiair's history page says Wenshan is the operating headquarters
   and names Xinzhuang and Nangang management stations; the current result sheets
   repeat the Wenshan address. No source provides the site capacities, opening dates
   or route allocations needed to turn these into a depot inventory.
4. **Fleet scope.** The operator page gives brand/year/assignment categories, while
   the TTSB report gives one specific 2016 Yutong 6128HG vehicle. The latter is not
   treated as a fleet-wide model count.
