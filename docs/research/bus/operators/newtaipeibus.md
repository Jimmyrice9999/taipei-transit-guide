## Conflicts

1. **Operating origin versus legal record.** The group history says New Taipei Bus was founded in 1967 and documents route milestones through 1990; MOEA records statutory establishment on 15 November 1979; the same MOEA record says the prior legal name was 欣和汽車客運 until 23 June 2011. These are retained as operating, legal and naming milestones rather than collapsed into one founding date.
2. **Municipality-scoped route snapshots.** TDX carries 14 New Taipei associations and 2 Taipei associations for the same operator code. The group history describes a longer historical route development, so neither current TDX count is treated as a lifetime route total.
3. **Oversight scope.** New Taipei’s city-bus evaluation gives 81.65/甲等 and records a 藍22 headway violation, while the Highway Bureau page is a sightseeing-bus record with grade 乙（112） and no displayed accident/violation rows. Taipei’s notice says the operator participated but was not graded. These are different authorities, dates and business scopes.
