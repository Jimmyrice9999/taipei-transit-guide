## Conflicts

1. **Eight TDX records versus six official route families.** The committed TDX snapshot has eight associations, while the current official page groups them into six published families: F212Sub and F212 Fengjiang are presented under F212, and F213 Liming and F213 are presented under F213. Both representations are retained.
2. **Current route page versus service history.** The official page lists F211, F212, F212 Subline, F213, F217 and F218 as current timetable families but also records that F215 and F216 left New Bus for the city-bus network on 1 July 2020. The historical conversion is not treated as a current route association.
3. **Historical deposit records versus current contractor evidence.** The 2016 settlement ledger documents bid and performance-deposit entries for the 2016 and 2017 service cases, but the fetched pages do not identify the successful operators, vehicle assignments or contract terms. Deposit records are not converted into an operator claim.
