## Conflicts

1. **TDX associations versus official route pages.** The TDX snapshot has 25
   associated route records, while the current Jinshan index presents three route
   notices, F931–F933. The source models differ: the TDX rows include departure
   labels, while the office pages group them as route notices. Neither count is
   substituted for the other.
2. **Service telephone.** The 2021 PDF says 2498-5966; current 2026 notices and
   the New Taipei contact page say 2498-5965. These are dated source values.
3. **Route detail over time.** The 2021 F931 timetable includes stops such as
   金山國小 and 三界里(10鄰), while the 2026 F931 notice lists 金山中學 and a
   different set of intermediate stop wording. The sources do not explain whether
   these reflect schedule revisions, stop renaming or both.
4. **Schedule count versus route geography.** The 2021 and 2026 pages both show
   8/7/10 departures for F931/F932/F933, but identical trip counts do not establish
   identical paths; the dated route tables must remain separate.
