## What is established

### History and operating scope

- **Claim.** The operator’s official related-enterprise page says Kuang Hua Bus was established in ROC year 58 (October 1969) by Shen Shui-mu, initially serving residents travelling between Tianmu, Shilin, Neihu and Taipei. It says the company joined Taipei’s joint-operation bus system in April 1977 and, in December 1997, entered highway operation jointly with Keelung Bus on the Shilin–Keelung corridor via National Freeway 1.
