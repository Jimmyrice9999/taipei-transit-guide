## Conflicts

1. **TDX associations versus the current office index.** The committed TDX snapshot has 13 route records, while the current office index presents nine service entries, counting two separate F138 entries. TDX splits service variants and timetable labels; the office page groups them as current service entries. Neither count replaces the other.
2. **Dated route scope.** The 2025 official account says nine community-bus routes, with seven outsourced and two self-operated. The March 2026 office index presents nine current service entries, but the TDX association count is 13. The sources use different record groupings and dates.
3. **Fleet additions versus fleet register.** The 2025 account records one replacement bus and the 2026 activity note records another new bus, both assigned to F133/F139. These are dated additions, not a complete fleet total.
4. **F139 schedule descriptions.** The 2025 narrative describes five daily trips between 19:55 and 23:30; the fetched timetable specifies Monday-Saturday operation and Sunday suspension. The narrative does not state a Sunday rule, so the two statements are retained with their different scopes.
