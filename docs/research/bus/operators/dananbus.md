## Conflicts

1. **Founding date.** Danan’s own profile says ROC58 August; the MOEA record says statutory registration on ROC58 24 April. Both are retained as separate operator-stated and legal-record dates.
2. **Network scope.** TDX has 48 Taipei associations, while Danan’s profile describes a broader Taipei/New Taipei service area. These are different scopes, not competing route totals.
3. **Guandu status.** The procurement document gives a 15 July 2026 completion deadline, but it does not prove that the depot was completed by that date. Completion remains unverified.
