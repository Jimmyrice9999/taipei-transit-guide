## Conflicts

1. **Historical and statutory chronology.** The group’s 1952/1954/1957 history is an operator-group account of predecessor and naming milestones. The factory record provides factory-registration dates in 1983/1985 and a 2004 公告廢止 status. These are different records and are not merged into a single founding date.
2. **Evaluation periods.** New Taipei records 基隆客運 as 乙等 in 109 first period and 甲等 in 114 first period. Both dated results are published; neither is treated as a permanent label.
3. **Site versus depot scope.** The group directory publishes five station entries, but no capacities, opening dates or route allocations. The page does not turn the five-entry list into a depot total.
