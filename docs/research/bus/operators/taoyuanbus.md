## Conflicts

1. **1903/1947 history versus 1950 registry approval.** The city cultural record's company-internal chronology starts in 1903 and says the present name was adopted in 1947, while the MOEA registry gives 7 January 1950 as the approval date. Both values are retained: the first is an organizational/operating chronology and the second is a registration field.
2. **45 county-wide electric buses in 2014 versus 15 Taoyuan Bus 華德 buses in 2025.** These are not combined. The first is a dated county-wide total across ten routes; the second is a later company/brand row in a national dataset.
3. **Public terminals versus depot evidence.** The city tourism page names three passenger terminals, while the registry exposes an active repair-factory record and a closed maintenance-factory record. Neither source supplies a complete garage location, opening date or route allocation.
4. **TDX one-route association versus route-page joint contacts.** The committed NewTaipei pull has one association, while the full route 952 page lists both 首都客運 and 桃園客運 contact points. The TDX association is preserved as a data snapshot and is not expanded into a claim of exclusive operation.
