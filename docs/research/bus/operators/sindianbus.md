## Conflicts

1. **Founding chronology.** The operator’s history page says the company was established in August 1980. The current MOEA record gives 15 September 1980 as the approved establishment date. These are retained as an operator-published founding month and a statutory registration date, not collapsed into one date.
2. **TDX route scope.** The committed Taipei record has six associations and the New Taipei record has 18. The operator’s history page discusses 642 and 643 in its 1990s account, while the current municipality-scoped pull is a different dated data snapshot. No unique all-municipal route total is inferred.
3. **Office versus dispatch sites.** MOEA registers the company at 松林路9號, while the operator contact page publishes the Sindian and Jinxiu dispatch stations at two other addresses. These are different record types and are not collapsed into one depot location.
4. **Evaluation date.** The published grade is 優等 for Taipei’s 108 second period, announced in 2020. Later evaluation titles exist, but no later grade is asserted here without a directly fetched detailed result.
