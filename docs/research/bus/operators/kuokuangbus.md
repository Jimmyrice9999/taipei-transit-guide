## Conflicts

- The self-authored profile says the company began operating on 1 July 2001;
  the MOEA registry gives the approved-establishment date as 15 June 2001.
  Both are published as separate dates and neither is selected as the sole
  “founding” date.
- The company profile’s more-than-860-bus / more-than-96-route snapshot and
  the 2021 New Taipei notice’s 30 electric buses describe different dates and
  scopes from the ten-route TDX municipality snapshot.
- New Taipei city-bus grades move from 甲 in 112 first period to 乙 in both 113
  periods, while the Highway Bureau’s separate sightseeing-bus record shows
  甲 in 112 and 優 in 111. These are not reconciled into one grade.
