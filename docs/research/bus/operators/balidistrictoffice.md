## Conflicts

1. **F123 timetable values.** The dated 9 January 2026 district-office announcement says the F123 main line's morning and afternoon departure times were adjusted to 06:40 and 15:25 from 1 January. The full health-center text page lists weekday F123 departures at 07:00, 12:05 and 15:50, and a holiday pattern at 06:40, 12:05 and 15:50. Both values are retained with their source and date scope.
2. **Current schedule representations.** The district-office page says the timetable is effective from 1 January 2026 and publishes image controls for F122, F123 and F126 plus a route map; the health-center page publishes text rows for F121, F122, F123 and F126. The TDX pull has 11 route/departure/variant records, so none of these representations is substituted for another.
3. **Historical route scope.** The older New Taipei feasibility report lists Bali F121, F122, F123, F125 and F126, while the 2026 district-office page and fetched health-center text do not publish an F125 row in the material used here. The older table is retained as historical evidence, not a current route roster.
4. **Districtwide electric buses versus the operator record.** The Transport Department reports 24 electric city buses in Bali District, but does not attribute them to Bali District Office's New Bus service. The figure is therefore not used as this operator's fleet count.
