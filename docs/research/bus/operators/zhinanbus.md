## Conflicts

1. **Predecessor and statutory chronology.** The group history gives a 1953 predecessor and a 1966 reorganisation as Zhinan Bus. The current MOEA company record gives 1 June 1954 as the registered establishment date. These are different chronology records and are published separately, not collapsed into one founding date.
2. **Company relationship.** The group presents Zhinan Bus among its related enterprises and the MOEA board table shows four corporate representatives, but neither fetched source publishes a complete parent-company shareholding chart or a merger instrument. Group relationship is established; control percentage is TBC.
3. **Sites versus depots.** The group directory publishes 14 sites but no capacity, opening-date or route-allocation table. The 14 entries are not treated as 14 depots.
4. **Fleet evidence.** The municipal vehicle-art notice identifies one Zhinan 880 vehicle without a model, while the audit records eight electric buses stopped pending inspection. These are different kinds of fleet evidence and do not establish a current model roster or propulsion split.
