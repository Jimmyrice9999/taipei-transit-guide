# Community transport — Hualien Happiness Bus

**Research file — leads, not sources.** The Hualien programme page was fetched in full before the claims below were promoted.

**Version 1.0 — 7 September 2026**
**Status: current official verification for `content/bus/drt/hualien-happiness-bus.md`.**

## What is established

- **Hualien Yo真行’s current Happiness Bus page says the programme is primarily operated by county/city governments or township offices, with some services commissioned to bus companies, taxi companies, local groups or individuals.** **Source:** *Happiness Bus service introduction*, titleOriginal `幸福巴士服務介紹`, Hualien County Government, `https://www.hly.tw/HappinessBus/info`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched on 2026-09-07. Original-language basis: `營運主體以縣市政府或鄉鎮公所為主` and the following sentence naming bus/taxi operators, social groups and individuals.

- **The same page describes the service model as fixed-route/fixed-schedule operation combined with flexible reservation service, and displays 80 townships/areas and 281 routes, including 47 rural areas and 209 routes.** **Source:** the same full page. Original-language basis: `以定班定線並搭配彈性預約服務併行，已通車80個鄉鎮區、281條路線（含47個偏鄉，209條路線）`.

- **The Hualien page displays route-link groups for 11 townships/areas: Guangfu, Ji’an, Xiulin, Zhuoxi, Fuli, Xincheng, Ruisui, Wanrong, Shoufeng, Fenglin and Fengbin.** **Source:** the same full page. Original-language basis: the township headings `光復鄉`, `吉安鄉`, `秀林鄉`, `卓溪鄉`, `富里鄉`, `新城鄉`, `瑞穗鄉`, `萬榮鄉`, `壽豐鄉`, `鳳林鎮` and `豐濱鄉`.

- **Those groups display 30 named route links, including Guangfu’s whole-town flexible reservation line, Xincheng medical lines, Ruisui village lines, Shoufeng’s three lines and Fengbin’s student, medical, Jingpu, Fengfu, Baliwan and Kavalan lines.** **Source:** the same full page. This count is a link count on the page, not a claim that the county’s entire programme has only 30 services.

## Conflicts

- **The Hualien county programme page displays 281 routes across 80 areas, while the MOTC February 2026 national programme measurement reports 507 services across 193 townships/districts.** These are different publication scopes and dates: the Hualien page is a county MaaS directory view, while the MOTC figure is a national programme measurement. The values are retained without addition or replacement.
