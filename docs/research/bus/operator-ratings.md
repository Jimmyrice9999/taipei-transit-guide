# Taipei bus operator service-quality evaluation (營運服務指標評鑑)

Research pass, 28 August 2026. `docs/research/bus/bus-network.md` already
covers the 路線積分 route-scoring mechanism (from FL055087/FL055169,
independently re-fetched here with identical text — no contradiction). It
does not cover the five-category/26-indicator methodology, the grading
scale, the 1992 start date, actual grade tables, or New Taipei's parallel
scheme — this file fills those gaps.

## What is established

**Current legal basis and five categories.** 《臺北市市區汽車客運業營運與服務
評鑑執行要點》, effective 1 September 2022 (民國111年9月1日), defines five
categories: 場站設施與服務, 運輸工具設備與安全, 旅客服務品質與駕駛員管理, 無障礙
之場站設施/服務/運輸工具設備與安全, and 公司經營與管理. Source: Taipei City
Government legal database, https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL055087,
PRIMARY, High (fetched in full; corroborated word-for-word by two separate
PTO/DOT press releases below).

**Grading scale.** 優等 ≥90; 甲等 80–<90; 乙等 70–<80; 丙等 60–<70; 丁等 <60.
Same source, Article 4, PRIMARY, High.

**Running since 1992 (民國81年), twice yearly.** 臺北市公共運輸處's own
113年第1期 press release states directly: "公運處表示本市公車自81年開始辦理評鑑
作業." Source: https://www.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=F0DDAF49B89E9413&s=86B7001D1FAC5ABA,
PRIMARY, High. **Caution: an earlier automated fetch-summary of this exact
page misread 81年 (ROC year 81 = 1992 AD) as "1981"** — corrected here
after requesting the verbatim Chinese sentence; recorded so the same
misreading is not repeated. Current rounds are numbered 第1期/第2期 within
each ROC year (two rounds/year), confirmed across three separate press
releases below.

**Route-scoring consequences.** 路線積分 points, assigned per period's
results, are the reference for reallocating routes and for the order in
which operators may choose routes when routes are (re)allocated — already
sourced in `bus-network.md` to FL055087/FL055169, independently
re-fetched this pass with an exact-text match, no contradiction found.

**An earlier, four-category predecessor.** 《臺北市聯營公車營運服務指標及獎懲
作業要點》 (repealed effective 20 April 2001) used four categories: 車輛及
站場績效指標, 量化乘客服務水準指標, 質化乘客服務水準指標, 配合交通局重要措施指標,
with a more elaborate points reward/penalty structure (top-3 scorers per
period earned 10/6/3 points; two consecutive 丙等 grades, or last place
with no improvement for two periods, could mean losing a high-revenue
route). Source: https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL012589,
PRIMARY, High, explicitly marked repealed in the source itself. Not to be
conflated with the current five-category regime.

## Actual grade results, three rounds

**113年第2期 (announced 27 Feb 2025).** 優等 (8): 首都客運, 大南汽車, 東南客運,
欣欣客運, 大都會客運, 臺北客運, 三重客運, 中興巴士. 乙等 (1): 大有巴士. Source:
https://pto.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=D065CCB1467288C8&sms=72544237BBE4C5F6&s=2E6214F9FA1FAA7F,
PRIMARY, High.

**113年第1期 (announced 30 Jul 2024).** 優等 (7): 欣欣客運, 首都客運, 三重客運,
臺北客運, 大南汽車, 中興巴士, 大都會客運. 乙等 (1): 大有巴士. Source:
https://www.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=F0DDAF49B89E9413&s=86B7001D1FAC5ABA,
PRIMARY, High.

**111年第2期.** 優等 (6): 首都客運, 臺北客運, 指南客運, 大都會客運, 中興巴士,
大南汽車. 乙等 (1): 欣欣客運. Not ranked (small-fleet exclusion): 淡水客運,
新北客運, 皇家客運. Source: 臺北市政府交通局 press release, as above,
PRIMARY, High.

**A genuine finding, not a source conflict**: 欣欣客運 went from 乙等 in
111年第2期 to 優等 in both 113年 rounds — the grading demonstrably moves.
Small-fleet operators are sometimes excluded from ranking entirely and
sometimes (大有巴士, both 113年 rounds) given an actual 乙等 grade — the
exact threshold distinguishing the two treatments was not found in any
source read.

**Coverage extension, 2019.** The evaluation was extended to cover four
non-joint-system routes: 1717 (皇家客運), 1503 (指南客運), and 三重客運's
tourist blue/red lines. Source: data.taipei catalogue metadata for the
110年 commissioned evaluation report, SECONDARY (catalogue description,
not the report itself — the underlying PDF was not fetched), Medium.

## New Taipei runs a separate, parallel scheme

New Taipei's Department of Transportation maintains its own "公車評鑑專區"
with reports from 101年 (2012) through 114年 (2025). Source:
https://www.traffic.ntpc.gov.tw/home.jsp?id=40c85471efd9ce2f, PRIMARY,
High for existence and archive; Medium for methodology detail (functions
as an index page).

**113年第1期 New Taipei results**: 優等 (5): 臺北客運, 大都會客運, 首都客運,
三重客運, 桃園客運 — noting 臺北客運 and 首都客運 achieved "連續16年獲得優等."
Source: https://www.traffic.ntpc.gov.tw/home.jsp?id=54fa46e9e522dde4&act=be4f48068b2b0031&dataserno=21851f1227da466e4cb9087ae8c9107f&mserno=39e5192ff77897e0ae099c1886ca9b09,
PRIMARY, High.

**This is a genuinely separate scheme, not a republication of Taipei's
results**: New Taipei's 113年第1期 優等 list includes 桃園客運 (absent from
either Taipei list) and excludes 東南客運/欣欣客運/中興巴士/大南汽車 (all on
Taipei's 優等 lists). Neither source explains the jurisdictional scoping —
plausibly each city grades only the portion of an operator's fleet/routes
within its own boundary, but this is inference, not a sourced claim.
