## What is established

### Kaohsiung names the contracted operator and booking channels

- **Claim:** The page dated 30 June 2026 names Kaohsiung Bus as the contracted
  operator. It publishes a general application/reservation telephone number,
  a temporary booking/cancellation number, an upload/fax process for new users,
  and a linked reservation system.
- **Source:** *Rehabilitation Bus passenger service* / `復康巴士交通接送服務`;
  Kaohsiung City Government Transportation Bureau (`高雄市政府交通局`);
  https://www.tbkc.gov.tw/Service/PublicTransport/Accessibility?id=bd4fffc4-21ce-4ec5-8b1d-58f390dfe25e;
  PRIMARY; High — the complete HTML page was fetched directly.
- **Original-language basis:** `日期：115-06-30`, `復康巴士委託營運單位：高雄汽車客運股份有限公司`,
  `復康巴士申請及預約專線：07-7407977`, `復康巴士臨時訂車/取消專線：07-7406033`,
  and the new-passenger upload instructions.

### Eligibility is limited and has two stated routes

- **Claim:** The programme accepts people holding a disability certificate, and
  people with a medical document stating that wheelchair use is required when
  the trip is for medical care.
- **Source:** same full official page; PRIMARY; High.
- **Original-language basis:** `領有身心障礙證明者` and `持有需使用輪椅行動之診斷證明文件，而基於就醫用途者`.

### The service boundary is municipal plus specified neighbouring areas

- **Claim:** The service covers Kaohsiung's administrative area. It also allows
  trips from Kaohsiung to neighbouring townships, urban townships, cities or
  districts in Pingtung and Tainan, with a narrower list of Tainan medical
  institutions for trips starting in Kaohsiung districts adjacent to Tainan.
- **Source:** same full official page, Articles 1–3; PRIMARY; High.
- **Original-language basis:** `本市行政轄區` and the boundary text in Article 3,
  including `屏東縣及台南市` and the named Tainan districts.

### Advance-booking windows depend on the user's category

- **Claim:** The page gives a seven-to-one-day booking window for users with a
  severe-or-more disability certificate who use a wheelchair and for the stated
  severe visual-impairment category; a six-to-one-day window for the next
  listed groups; and a five-to-one-day window for other eligible users.
- **Source:** same full official page, Articles 5–8; PRIMARY; High.
- **Original-language basis:** Articles 6–8 use `用車前七日至前一日`,
  `用車前六日至前一日`, and `用車前五日至前一日`.

### The published fare is a distance-based fraction of the daytime taxi rate

- **Claim:** The page calculates the charge from the passenger's distance and
  Kaohsiung's daytime taxi rate. A solo trip uses one-half of that rate, or
  one-third for a low-income passenger; a shared trip uses one-quarter for a
  full shared journey and one-third for a partially shared journey. Fractions
  are rounded up to an integer, and motorway tolls are charged separately unless
  another subsidy rule applies.
- **Source:** same full official page, Article 13; PRIMARY; High.
- **Original-language basis:** `按每位服務對象乘坐里程數乘以高雄市計程車日間費率`,
  `單獨乘坐車輛者：費率為二分之一`, `低收入戶者，費率為三分之一`,
  `全程共乘者：費率為四分之一`, `非全程共乘者：費率為三分之一`, and the
  separate highway-toll sentence.
