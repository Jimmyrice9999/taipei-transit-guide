# Pingtung Happiness Bus and Happiness Taxi — research

**Corpus warning.** This is a research lead file, not a source. The Pingtung directory is paginated and the Ministry case study is separately dated; neither is used as a timeless county-wide total.

## What is established

- **The Highway Bureau's full Pingtung directory page displays ten records on page 1 of 56.** **Source:** *Pingtung Happiness Bus and Happiness Taxi operating information*, `https://www.thb.gov.tw/News.aspx?_CSN=4022&n=10738&sms=13903`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched and read on 7 September 2026. Original-language basis: the page heading `幸福巴士及幸福小黃營運資訊`, columns `類型`, `區域`, `路線名稱`, `路線行駛站點`, `修改時間`, and pager `1 / 56`.
- **The first page separates Happiness Bus from Happiness Taxi and includes Sandimen, Mudan, Mudan–Manzhou and Laiyi records.** **Source:** the same full Highway Bureau page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: rows labelled `幸福巴士` and `幸福小黃`, including `三地門小學路線`, `固定路線`, `預約路線`, `德文達來線`, `牡丹鄉全鄉預約線`, `幸福滿州2路（龍滿線）` and the three `幸福來義` rows.
- **The Sandimen records publish school, fixed, reservation and taxi-bus patterns with named stops including Shuimen Transfer Station, hospitals and Pingtung Transfer Station.** **Source:** the same full page, PRIMARY, High confidence. This is a transcription of the displayed stop fields, not a timed-connection claim.
- **The Ministry of Transportation's full 24 April 2025 release reports 12 Laiyi Happiness Bus routes, three mid-size buses and four small vehicles.** **Source:** *MOTC minister affirms Laiyi Township Happiness Bus*, `https://www.motc.gov.tw/ch/app/data/view?id=14&module=news&serno=118f87f2-ecd3-4c3c-9df5-f6bd50330411`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `現有12條幸福巴士路線`, `目前有3輛中巴及4輛小型車`.
- **The same release describes Laiyi's connections to Chaozhou, Donggang, Fangliao and Pingtung, accessible mid-size operation, and more than 52,000 passenger trips by the end of March 2025.** **Source:** the same full Ministry release, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `提供鄉內民眾至潮州、東港、枋寮與屏東地區接駁需求`, `無障礙中巴` and `截至今年3月底已服務超過5.2萬人次`.

## Conflicts

- No numerical conflict is asserted between the ten visible Highway Bureau rows and the 12 Laiyi route figure. They measure different things: one paginated directory page versus a township programme total in a 24 April 2025 Ministry case study.
