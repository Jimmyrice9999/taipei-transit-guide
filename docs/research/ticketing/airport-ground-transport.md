## What is established

### National airport boundary

- **The Civil Aeronautics Administration's full service-desk table lists 17 airport stations, from Taoyuan, Kaohsiung, Songshan and Taichung through Hualien, Penghu, Tainan, Taitung, Kinmen, Chiayi, Nangan, Beigan, Lanyu, Ludao, Qimei, Wangan and Hengchun.** **Source:** *Airport service-desk telephone list*, titleOriginal `各機場服務臺電話`, Civil Aeronautics Administration, `https://www.caa.gov.tw/Article.aspx?a=983&lang=1`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched on 2026-08-26. Original-language basis: the table beginning `機場｜電話｜地址` and the rows for the 17 named airport stations.
- **The CAA page is a service-desk directory, not a national ground-transport timetable.** **Source:** same full page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: the page heading `各機場服務臺電話` and table columns `機場｜電話｜地址`.

### Taoyuan Airport MRT

- **The full operator timetable names A12 Airport Terminal 1, A13 Airport Terminal 2 and A18 Taiwan High Speed Rail Taoyuan Station.** **Source:** *Taoyuan Metro timetable and stopping patterns*, titleOriginal `時刻表`, Taoyuan Metro Corporation, `https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/tw/_pages/travel-guide/timetable.php`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched. Original-language basis: the station selector rows `A12 機場第一航廈站`, `A13 機場第二航廈站` and `A18 高鐵桃園站`.
- **The operator defines express service as A1/A3/A8/A12/A13, peak enhanced express as those stations plus A18/A21, and ordinary service as every station.** **Source:** same full page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `直達車(停靠A1、A3、A8、A12、A13)`, `尖峰增停直達車(停靠A1、A3、A8、A12、A13、A18、A21)` and `普通車(每站停靠)`.
- **The timetable warns that actual arrival times depend on daily operations and tells airport-bound passengers to leave a buffer before the flight.** **Source:** same full page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `實際到站時間仍應視當日列車運行狀況而定` and `自行評估並預留緩衝時間`.
- **The operator's full fare PDF reaches NT$160 in the longest single-fare row.** **Source:** *Taoyuan Metro single-ticket fare table*, titleOriginal `票價表`, Taoyuan Metro Corporation, `https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/tw/_images/document/travel-guide/price.pdf`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full five-page PDF was fetched. Original-language basis: the fare matrix rows containing `160` and the PDF heading `票價表`.
- **The operator's ticket page says EasyCard, iPASS and icash 2.0 are accepted and that the Airport MRT is covered by the four-city 基北北桃 monthly pass.** **Source:** *Taoyuan Metro ticket guide*, titleOriginal `票種與票價`, Taoyuan Metro Corporation, `https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/tw/_pages/travel-guide/ticket.html`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched. Original-language basis: `包含悠遊卡、一卡通、icash2.0等3種電子票證` and the `基北北桃都會通` section.

## Conflicts

No direct numerical conflict was found among the fetched primary pages. The sources describe different layers: the CAA table is a national service-desk boundary; airport pages describe terminals and access; operators publish fares and departures. Those scopes are kept separate rather than merged into a synthetic national timetable.

## Stated gaps

- Current route numbers, operators, fares, frequencies and last departures for Taichung, Songshan and the smaller CAA-listed airports need direct airport/operator pages or a dated official data pull.
- Taoyuan's current airport-bus/taxi pages and terminal-shuttle operating table need a successful full fetch.
- A national airport-ground data layer needs a defined source contract: CAA supplies the airport boundary, while TDX, airport authorities and municipal operators publish the route and timetable layers separately.
- Accessibility needs per-airport lift, step-free, tactile, accessible-bus and terminal-transfer records; the Taoyuan sheet is one dated example, not a national inventory.
