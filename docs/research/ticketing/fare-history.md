# Taipei-region fare history and accessibility — research findings, 24 August 2026

**Corpus warning: 'docs/research/' is leads only. It has been wrong repeatedly.
Nothing here is citable until it has been checked against a primary source —
see '.claude/skills/transit-research/SKILL.md'.**

This report records dated passenger-fare and concession evidence, current
operator fare tables, and accessibility fields checked on 2026-08-24. Full
pages, PDFs, ODT content and the TRTC API were fetched; search snippets were
not used as evidence.

## What is established

### Taipei Metro and transfer history

- Taipei City's official chronology records the 28 March 1996 Muzha Line
  opening with four fares: NT$20, NT$25, NT$30 and NT$35. Source: Taipei City
  Government, *大事記*, https://wsdo.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=3D33E9D6085135B8;
  PRIMARY, High confidence — full HTML fetched. Original sentence:
  '民國85年（1996年） ... 票價共分20元、25元、30元及35元等四種。'
- The official fare-setting paper says the opening distance-banded fare had
  not been increased for more than ten years when it was written. Source:
  DORTS, *臺北捷運票價之訂定*,
  https://www-ws.gov.taipei/001/Upload/405/attachment/7592/910191134944.pdf;
  PRIMARY, High confidence — full PDF fetched. Original sentence:
  '該價格實施10餘年未曾調漲。'
- The official Taipei transport report records MRT–bus transfer discounts
  beginning on 25 November 1996 with transfer vouchers and an initial NT$4
  discount; the 1 July 1999 stored-value transition made the one-way transfer
  free within two hours. Source: Taipei City Government, *臺北市運輸價格調整及價格優惠對運量影響之研究*,
  https://www-ws.gov.taipei/Download.ashx?icon=..pdf&n=MTA1MTLoh7rljJfluILlpKfnnL7pgYvovLjlg7nmoLzoqr%2FmlbTlvbHpn7%2FoqZXmnpAucGRm&u=LzAwMS9VcGxvYWQvMzY3L3JlbGZpbGUvNDUwMDAvNzY2MjY1My84MjlmOGRlMC1iMDQ5LTQ5YjctODM1ZC1jOThjNTQwNDdlZDcucGRm;
  PRIMARY, High confidence — full 30-page PDF fetched. Original sentences:
  '自85年11月25日開始實施' and '自88年7月1日起以公車儲值票取代原用之轉乘券'.
- The same report records the 1 November 2003 first two-way stage
  (NT$8 full fare, NT$6 student), 27 December 2003 second-stage NT$4
  welfare-card discount, and 1 April 2005 reduction of the transfer window
  from two hours to one while keeping the discount unchanged. Source: same
  official report; PRIMARY, High confidence. Original sentence:
  '92年11月1日起 ... 全票每趟可享有8元之優惠，持學生卡者可享6元之優惠；92年12月27日實施第二階段優惠方案 ... 每次可享轉乘4元優惠。'
- TRTC's chronology records the NT$1,280 30-day unlimited pass on 16 April
  2018, its 30 June 2023 end of sales, and the NT$1,200 TPASS on 1 July 2023.
  Source: TRTC, *大事紀*,
  https://www.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=B27E7BC8CBBCDA61&s=9ABADF3671A79331;
  PRIMARY, High confidence — full chronology fetched. Original sentences:
  '107.04.16 ... 定價1,280元' and '112.07.01 ... 基北北桃都會通'.
- The official Circular Line Phase 1 notice sets NT$20 to 5 km, increments
  through 31 km, and NT$65 above 31 km. Source: TRTC / Taipei City Government,
  *公告臺北捷運環狀線第一階段通車後之票價*,
  https://www-ws.gov.taipei/Download.ashx?icon=..pdf&n=5o236YGL55Kw54uA57ea56ys5LiA6ZqO5q616YCa6LuK5b6M56Wo5YO55WsgZKLnBkZg%3D%3D&u=LzAwMS9VcGxvYWQvNDA1L3JlbGZpbGUvMTgyODgvNzYxOC9jMzI2ZmQwMi04MWJlLTQ5MDMtOWUxOS1mNDZmODYyMTZjNmQucGRm;
  PRIMARY, High confidence — full one-page PDF fetched. Original sentence:
  '基本里程5公里（含）以內，票價20元 ... 31公里以上為65元。'
- Taipei's open-data page identifies the current TRTC fare CSV, with coverage
  through 2026-03-31, and says the current record follows Circular Line Phase
  1 charging from 1 March 2020. Source: Taipei City Data Platform,
  *臺北捷運系統票價*, https://data.taipei/dataset/detail?id=4acb4911-0360-4063-808d-fcee629508b3;
  PRIMARY, High confidence — full dataset page fetched. Original sentence:
  '係配合環狀線第一階段自109年3月1日正式收費實施。'

### Buses and New Taipei Metro

- The joint Taipei/New Taipei bus portal publishes NT$15 full fare, NT$12
  student EasyCard fare and NT$8 concessionary fare per segment. Source:
  *票價資訊*, https://ebus.gov.taipei/RelatedLinks/TicketInfo; PRIMARY, High
  confidence — full page fetched. Original sentences: '全票 ... 每段次新臺幣15元整',
  '即每段次12元整' and '老殘孩童優待票：每段次新臺幣8元。'
- Taipei PTO records 1 July 2020 as the latest joint-operation bus operating
  rate approval while passenger fares remained 15/12/8 and the city subsidised
  the difference. Source: *臺北市聯營公車票價維持 運價依程序審議*,
  https://pto.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=D065CCB1467288C8&s=FB42176B7B44EDED;
  PRIMARY, High confidence — full page fetched. Original sentence:
  '近次臺北市聯營公車運價核定實施日期為109年7月1日' and '仍維持既有票價收費。'
- NTMC's linked ODT fare tables publish Danhai NT$20/25/30, Ankeng
  NT$20/25 and Circular Line NT$20/25/30/35/40 station-pair fares. Sources:
  *淡海輕軌票價資訊*, https://www.ntmetro.com.tw/archive/file/%E6%B7%A1%E6%B5%B7%E8%BC%95%E8%BB%8C%E7%A5%A8%E5%83%B9%E8%B3%87%E8%A8%8A%282%29.odt;
  *安坑輕軌票價資訊*, https://www.ntmetro.com.tw/archive/file/%E5%AE%89%E5%9D%91%E8%BC%95%E8%BB%8C%E7%A5%A8%E5%83%B9%E8%B3%87%E8%A8%8A.odt;
  *環狀線票價資訊*, https://www.ntmetro.com.tw/archive/file/%E7%92%B0%E7%8B%80%E7%B7%9A%E7%A5%A8%E5%83%B9%E8%B3%87%E8%A8%8A.odt;
  PRIMARY, High confidence — all linked ODTs fetched and content.xml read.
- NTMC's Sanying fare page says electronic-ticket users ride free from 16–31
  August during trial operation while other passengers buy a single ticket;
  the fetched HTML has no normal post-trial table. Source: *三鶯線票價*,
  https://www.ntmetro.com.tw/basic/?node=10166; PRIMARY, High confidence —
  full page fetched.

### Accessibility

- TRTC's stationdisabledinfo API was fetched for all 121 records returned by
  its station-label endpoint. It publishes station form, enquiry point, exit
  lift/ramp location, accessible-toilet location, accessible lift, wide gate,
  stair anti-slip strip, platform-arrival announcement, visual-priority seat,
  tactile paving and passenger-information fields. Source: TRTC, *車站無障礙設施查詢*,
  https://web.metro.taipei/pages/tw/stationdisabledinfo/ and
  https://web.metro.taipei/apis/metrostationapi/stationdisabledinfo; PRIMARY,
  High confidence — complete API responses fetched. Original labels include
  '出口電梯/無障礙坡道位置', '無障礙廁所', '無障礙電梯', '樓梯止滑條' and '導盲磚'.
- NTMC's full accessibility page says every Circular Line station has
  accessible ramps, wide gates, accessible toilets, accessible ticket
  machines, lifts and tactile guidance from entrances to platforms. It says
  elevated light-rail stations have lifts, at-grade stations have ramps,
  warning blocks are at stairs/platform ends/platform edges, and accessible
  toilets are at V01, V26 and K09; it lists nursing-room station codes.
  Source: NTMC, *無障礙服務*, https://www.ntmetro.com.tw/basic/?node=10041;
  PRIMARY, High confidence — full page fetched. Original sentences include
  '環狀線各車站皆設有無障礙廁所' and '自車站出入口起 ... 引導 ... 至月台層'.
- TYMC's full accessibility page says every station has accessible elevators,
  accessible gates, accessible toilets, tactile warnings and ramps at level
  differences; it also describes Braille, voice announcements, secondary
  panels, handrails and lift intercoms. Source: TYMC, *無障礙服務*,
  https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/tw/_pages/travel-guide/accessible.html;
  PRIMARY, High confidence — full page fetched. Original sentences include
  '各車站皆為無障礙電梯設計' and '各車站皆設有無障礙自動收費閘門'.

## Conflicts

- The 1996 chronology gives four opening fares, while the current TRTC
  dataset and 2020 Circular notice describe a 20–65 network structure. These
  are different dates and scopes, not values to average.
- PTO's 2020 page distinguishes passenger fares (15/12/8) from the regulated
  bus operating rate. Both are published with their meanings.
- Sanying's published trial rule is a current fare rule, but its normal
  post-trial station-pair table is absent from the fetched HTML. Both states
  remain visible.

## Checked and failed

- **TRTC ticket-history API** — The type endpoint returned 12 ticket
  categories, but every full POST to the two result endpoints returned HTTP
  404 on 2026-08-24; those endpoints are not used as evidence.
- **Complete fare-gazette series** — Full-page checks on 2026-08-24 of TRTC fare
  and chronology pages, Taipei's law database, PTO, MOTC and NTMC fare pages
  found the events above but no complete official index for every historic MRT
  and bus passenger-fare change. No plausible number was substituted.
- **TYMC nursing-room locations and platform-gap measurements** — The full
  operator pages/API fields checked on 2026-08-24 did not publish these
  station-level values, so the public page marks them TBC.
