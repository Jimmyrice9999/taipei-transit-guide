## Why this is high-value

Ticketing is what visitors search for most and what English sources get most
wrong. Two specific errors are near-universal in English coverage:

1. That you need a **TPASS card** to buy a TPASS pass. **You don't** — it loads
   onto any existing EasyCard.
2. That the **NT$100 card fee** is a cost. **It's refunded** on real-name
   registration.

Correcting those two things alone would make the page worth linking to.

# EasyCard 悠遊卡

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Issuer | **悠遊卡股份有限公司** (EasyCard Corporation) |
| Predecessor | **台北智慧卡票證股份有限公司** |
| Founding meeting | **25 February 2000** |
| Registered | **6 March 2000** |
| Launched on Taipei Metro | **June 2002** (12 June, initially 10 stations) |
| Full rollout | **September 2002** |
| 1 million cards | **December 2002** |
| Cards issued cumulatively | **110 million+** |
| Technology | **RFID / NXP MIFARE** |

**Founding structure:** incorporated by **TRTC** with a consortium — 神通
(Mitac), 台新 (Taishin), 國泰世華 (Cathay United), 中信 (CTBC), 富邦 (Fubon),
三商行, and 所羅門 (Solomon).

**A metro operator founding a payments company** is an unusual origin and worth
stating plainly.

### Regulatory history

- **2007** — integrated with 基隆交通卡 (Keelung transport card)
- **2009** — the **電子票證發行管理條例** (Electronic Ticket Issuance Management
  Act) passes; EasyCard becomes the **sole non-bank stored-value issuer**

### The 李博文 connection

EasyCard corporate history identifies **李博文** — bus association chairman and a
former TRTC chairman — as having "推動艱難的交通整合…第二次合作", pushing through
difficult transport integration.

**The same figure appears in the bus research** as the Capital Bus chairman who
took 7 of 11 Metropolitan Transport board seats in December 2010. One person
central to bus consolidation, EasyCard and TRTC. **That's a thread worth pulling.**

# TPASS 行政院通勤月票

An **Executive Yuan** programme. The T is officially glossed as **Taiwan,
Transport, Together, Tour and Trust**.

## The Taipei-region product: 基北北桃1200都會通

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Price | **NT$1,200 / 30 days** |
| Coverage area | **Keelung, Taipei, New Taipei, Taoyuan** |
| Rides | Unlimited |
| Card fee | **NT$100, refunded on real-name registration** |
| Predecessor | 雙北 **1280** pass |

### Timeline

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **18 May 2023, 11:00** | Card pre-orders open at four convenience store chains and Books.com. First run **20,000 cards at NT$100** |
| **15 June 2023** | Card collection begins; monthly pass pre-orders open at MRT stations |
| **30 June 2023** | The predecessor 雙北1280 pass withdrawn from sale |
| **1 July 2023** | **Service starts** |
| **2 October 2023** | Second phase adds Taoyuan–Hsinchu–Miaoli, Yilan, Hualien and Taitung zones; Taoyuan adds 16 more 國道 and 公路 routes |
| Autumn 2023 | Uptake **over 1.42 million** |

## Coverage — broader than the pass it replaced, for less money

**The old NT$1,280 pass covered:** Taipei and New Taipei metro, city buses,
YouBike, and the Danhai and Ankeng light rail lines.

**The NT$1,200 pass adds:** **TRA** (within-zone), **national highway coaches**,
**intercity buses**, **Taoyuan Airport MRT**, and the **Blue Highway ferries**
(藍色公路，含渡輪).

Condition: journeys must start and end within the 基北北桃 zone.

**Cheaper and broader** is a genuinely unusual policy outcome and worth stating as
the headline fact.

## The two things English sources get wrong

**1. The card fee is refunded.** The NT$100 is returned by the government on
real-name registration. Effectively free.

**2. You don't need the TPASS card.** Via the **SuperCard** mechanism, the pass
loads onto **any existing EasyCard**. The SuperCard also allows purchase and
renewal in the **EasyWallet app** rather than at a station machine.

## The caveat that matters

**One card holds one pass type.** This is why switching from the 1280 to the 1200
required a refund first — you couldn't hold both. Unused 1280 passes were refunded
pro-rata **without a fee**.

# Fares

## Metro

Distance-banded. **Detailed tables TBC** — needed for a tariff page.

## Sanying Line (for comparison — banded by station count)

| Stations travelled | Fare |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | NT$20 |
| 5–6 | NT$25 |
| 7–8 | NT$30 |
| 9+ | NT$35 |

Concessionary fares: **40% of the published fare**.

## Airport MRT

Taipei Main to Taoyuan Airport **NT$160** single — also the **maximum fare** on
the line. **Express and commuter are priced identically**, which surprises people.

Combination tickets: airport line return + Taipei Metro 48-hour pass **NT$520**;
+ 72-hour pass **NT$600**.

## Danhai LRT

Distance-based **NT$20 / NT$25** single. E-ticket 20% discount. **Bike carriage
single NT$50.** One-day pass NT$50.

## Bus

Joint-operation system fare structure, **TBC in detail**. Transfer discounts
between bus and metro exist and need documenting properly.

## YouBike

First 30 minutes free within 1 hour of an MRT or bus journey (transfer discount,
from April 2018). TPASS and 1280 holders get the first-30-minutes-free benefit.

## Payment methods — unusually broad

The Sanying Line launch listed: **EasyCard, iPass, some contactless credit cards,
Apple Pay, Google Pay, LINE Pay, iPASS MONEY, Taishin Pay, and TWQR ride codes.**

That breadth is itself notable and worth documenting — most metro systems accept
far fewer.
