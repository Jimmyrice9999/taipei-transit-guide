## What is established

### TPASS 1.0 and TPASS 2.0 are different products

- **Claim:** TPASS 1.0 is a prepaid regional public-transport pass that allows unlimited rides during its validity period; TPASS 2.0 is a nationwide frequent-rider rebate tied to a registered stored-value card or electronic-payment account. The current government explainer was updated 25 August 2026.
- **Source:** *Updated 2026: A commuter's guide to TPASS 1.0 passes and 2.0+ frequent-rider benefits* / `〖115年更新〗通勤族必看！一篇搞懂TPASS 1.0定期票與2.0+常客優惠！`; My E-Government, Digital Development Department; https://www.gov.tw/News_Content_37_817176; PRIMARY; High — the full page was fetched on 2026-09-06 and carries `更新日期：民國115年8月25日`.
- **Original-language basis:** `TPASS 1.0為各地生活圈「效期內無限次使用」公共運輸定期票` and `TPASS 2.0則為全國通用先乘車再領取回饋的常客優惠回饋`.

### The current government explainer publishes ten regional scheme rows

- **Claim:** The 25 August 2026 government table publishes rows for 基北北桃, 北宜, 桃竹竹苗, 中彰投苗, 雲林, 嘉嘉南, 南高屏, 花蓮, 臺東 and 澎湖. The listed examples include the NT$1,200 基北北桃 cross-city pass, NT$2,300 北宜 cross-city-and-Taipei pass, NT$1,200 桃竹竹苗 pass, NT$999 嘉嘉南 pass, NT$999 南高屏 pass, NT$299 臺東 city pass, and NT$150/400 澎湖 bus or bus-and-ship passes.
- **Source:** same full My E-Government page; PRIMARY; High — the table was read in full, not inferred from a search result.
- **Original-language basis:** the table headed `各縣市生活圈 | 定期票方案 | 查詢網頁 | 購買與續購方式`, with rows from `基北北桃生活圈` through `澎湖縣`.
- **Scope note:** this is a dated published table of scheme rows, not a timeless count of every local pass or a claim that every current price has been independently re-fetched from each issuing government.

### Existing cards can carry TPASS 1.0

- **Claim:** The Highway Bureau FAQ says riders may use an existing eligible electronic ticket to purchase a commuter pass, rather than needing the specially designed TPASS card. A new TPASS card instead represents a separate central-government card design.
- **Source:** *Monthly pass FAQ* / `月票常見問答`; Directorate General of Highways, MOTC; https://www.thb.gov.tw/News_Toggle.aspx?_CSN=4797&n=12594&sms=13070; PRIMARY; High — the FAQ question and answer were fetched directly on 2026-09-06.
- **Original-language basis:** `考量民眾使用便利性及避免票卡大量重複製作...開放民眾可以使用原有卡片購買公共運輸通勤月票`.

### TPASS 2.0 registration and accounting are card/account specific

- **Claim:** The current government explainer says the rebate month runs from the first day through the last day of the month; rides are counted from the day after registration; each physical card or electronic-payment account is treated separately; only the actual post-discount amount is counted; TPASS pass rides are excluded; each ride needs a complete tap-on/tap-off or purchase record; and the monthly rebate is capped at NT$2,500.
- **Source:** *Updated 2026 TPASS explainer*; My E-Government; https://www.gov.tw/News_Content_37_817176; PRIMARY; High — the calculation section was read in full on 2026-09-06.
- **Original-language basis:** `回饋金每月計算期間：每月1日至月底`, `自登錄完成次日起算`, `使用TPASS定期票搭乘紀錄...皆不納入`, `每月回饋金上限2,500元`.

### The normal and long-distance rebate layers have different thresholds

- **Claim:** The Highway Bureau's 24 November 2025 release describes the ordinary TPASS 2.0 public-road-transport condition as at least 11 rides per month for up to 30% cashback, with an additional 2% for the then-covered TRA, Taipei Metro and New Taipei Metro rail records. From 1 December 2025, TPASS 2.0+ added a separate threshold for 91 medium- and long-distance highway-coach routes: two rides receive 15% back and four rides receive 30% back.
- **Source:** *From 1 December TPASS 2.0 upgrades again: two medium/long-distance highway-coach rides per month receive a rebate* / `12/1起TPASS 2.0再升級，每月搭乘2次中長途國道客運即可享回饋`; Directorate General of Highways, MOTC; https://www.thb.gov.tw/News_Content_table.aspx?n=12181&s=285774; PRIMARY; High — the full release was fetched on 2026-09-06 and dated `114-11-24`.
- **Original-language basis:** `搭乘公路公共運輸(市區公車及公路客運)達11次以上者提供最高30%乘車金額回饋` and `搭乘91條中長途國道客運路線...2次可享15%...搭乘4次可享30%`.
- **Definition:** The release defines the western qualifying routes as over 70 km and includes eastern highway-coach routes. The attached 91-route list is a route-family list with main and branch routes, not a count of all coach records in the site's structured snapshot.

### Electronic payments are part of the 2.0+ account layer

- **Claim:** The Highway Bureau release says EasyWallet, iPASS MONEY and icash pay can be registered as electronic-payment accounts for the 2.0+ rebate; the government explainer says electronic-payment rebates are automatically deposited on the 25th of the following month.
- **Source:** Highway Bureau TPASS 2.0+ release; PRIMARY; High — the paragraph on electronic-payment inclusion was read in full.
- **Original-language basis:** `同步開放電子支付(悠遊付、iPASS MONEY、icash pay)納入累計回饋` and `並於次月25日將由票證公司自動匯入回饋金至電子支付帳戶`.

## Conflicts

- **Card subsidy timing:** The Highway Bureau FAQ states the general procedure of paying NT$100, registering and then collecting a NT$100 subsidy. The current iPASS page records that the older colour-circle-card subsidy deadline ended on 31 December 2025, while a separate green-circle TPASS 2.0 offer was limited to 40,000 cards. These are different card programmes and dates, so the guide publishes both rather than treating the FAQ as a current universal redemption guarantee.
- **Regional pass price presentation:** The current government summary table and the older Highway Bureau FAQ do not always show the same historical starting price for the Keelung city-only scheme (the FAQ records NT$320 in its planning answer; the updated government table lists NT$288). They are dated publication layers. The guide uses the updated 25 August 2026 table for the current snapshot and retains the older value only as a documented conflict, not as a current fare.
