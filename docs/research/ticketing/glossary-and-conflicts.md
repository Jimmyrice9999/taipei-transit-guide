# Taipei-region transport glossary and generated conflict index — research findings, 24 August 2026

**Corpus warning: `docs/research/` is leads only. It has been wrong repeatedly.
Nothing here is citable until it has been checked against a primary source —
see `.claude/skills/transit-research/SKILL.md`.**

This report checks the terms required for the reader-facing glossary and
defines the boundary of the generated conflicts index. Full pages and the
official TRA timetable PDF were fetched; search summaries were not used as
evidence. The conflict page is generated from conflict-bearing content
sections, with each copied citation namespaced to its source page.

## What is established

- **區間車 and 區間快 are distinct Taiwan Railways timetable categories.** The
  official Eastern Trunk/North Link timetable labels individual rows 區間車 and
  區間快 rather than treating them as one generic local service. Source:
  Taiwan Railways Corporation, *東部幹線/北迴線*,
  https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/file/5322d613-4dd7-4a35-9c9a-4257face1d25,
  PRIMARY, High confidence — the one-page PDF was fetched in full. Original
  lines include `區間車 4102 宜蘭－花蓮` and `區間快 4006 桃園－南澳`.
- **幹線 is mode-sensitive.** TRA uses 東部幹線/北迴線 as a railway-line
  grouping, while the Taipei bus portal lists 幹線公車 as a bus family. Source:
  the TRA timetable above and the full bus catalogue,
  https://ebus.gov.taipei/ebus?ct=tpc, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original
  labels include `東部幹線/北迴線` and `幹線公車`.
- **捷運先導公車 is an early service for a planned MRT corridor.** New Taipei's
  full project page says its purpose is to cultivate early demand on planned
  MRT corridors and records three current pilot-bus routes as of the page's
  stated date. Source: New Taipei City Government, *捷運先導公車*,
  https://wedid.ntpc.gov.tw/Governance/Detail/Eg8MLyOJvRkq, PRIMARY, High
  confidence — full page fetched. Original sentence: `捷運先導公車目的在培養規劃中捷運路廊之先期運量，以利後續捷運建設之推動`.
- **通勤專車 and 市民小巴 are portal route families, not translations of
  every commute-oriented or small vehicle service.** The full current bus
  catalogue lists `內科通勤專車`, `南軟通勤專車` and `市民小巴` as separate
  labels. Source: Taipei/New Taipei bus portal,
  https://ebus.gov.taipei/ebus?ct=tpc, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original
  headings include `市民小巴` and `內科專車`.
- **預約公車 means a reservation-service category with an operational rule.**
  The full programme page says the service targets low and irregular demand,
  places a reservation placard on the bus, still accepts a passenger who did
  not reserve when the bus runs, and requires card tapping. Source: Taipei/New
  Taipei bus portal, *臺北市預約公車*,
  https://ebus.gov.taipei/ProjectBus/Taipeidrts, PRIMARY, High confidence.
  Original sentence begins `臺北市預約公車係針對運量低且搭乘需求不固定之路線班次`.
- **繞 is a live route-variant label, not an inferred historical claim.** The
  full 市民小巴1 page publishes separate `小巴1去繞溪山班表` and
  `小巴1返繞溪山班表` rows beside the base service. Source: Taipei/New Taipei
  bus portal, https://ebus.gov.taipei/EBus/VsSimpleMap?rid=11217&sec=1,
  PRIMARY, High confidence. Original lines include `點擊查看附屬路線` and the
  three separate timetable labels.
- **段次 and 分段緩衝區 are fare-structure terms.** The project's full
  passenger-fare page and bus-fare research establish that the Taipei/New
  Taipei bus network uses segment fares and exposes a segment-buffer field on
  route pages. Source: Taipei/New Taipei bus portal,
  https://ebus.gov.taipei/RelatedLinks/TicketInfo, PRIMARY, High confidence;
  the route-page field reads `分段緩衝區`.
- **悠遊卡 and 一卡通 are separate electronic-ticket products with separate
  issuers.** EasyCard Corporation and iPASS's own company pages were fetched
  in the ticketing research; the glossary does not collapse the card/product
  name into the company name. Sources:
  https://www.easycard.com.tw/about and
  https://www.i-pass.com.tw/page/aboutus, both PRIMARY, High confidence.
  Original-language company names are `悠遊卡股份有限公司` and `一卡通票證股份有限公司`.
- **TPASS is a regional pass product, not a synonym for EasyCard.** The full
  metropolitan-pass operating notice lists eligible card products and covered
  modes; the pass can be carried on eligible media. Source: EasyCard
  Corporation on behalf of the four city governments, *基北北桃都會通使用須知*,
  https://www.easycard.com.tw/_upload/files/Megacity%20Pass.pdf, PRIMARY,
  High confidence — full PDF fetched. Original title: `基北北桃都會通使用須知`.
- **固定閉塞, 移動閉塞, 第三軌 and 聯合開發 are separate technical or land
  terms.** Full DORTS technical pages establish fixed-block high-capacity
  signalling, moving-block CBTC on Wenhu, 750 V DC third-rail traction, and
  the land-development mechanism. Sources:
  https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no22/files/basic-html/page73.html,
  https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no22/files/basic-html/page225.html,
  https://ebook.dorts.gov.taipei/ebook/no24/files/basic-html/page364.html and
  https://www.dorts.gov.taipei/News_toggle.aspx?PageSize=200&n=2A66A485FACB0D5B&page=1&sms=87415A8B9CE81B16,
  all PRIMARY, High confidence. Original titles include `捷運號誌系統實務`,
  `捷運供電系統實務` and `聯合開發`.

## Conflicts and generation boundary

The glossary preserves a distinction between a confirmed category and a
route-specific suffix whose exact history is not published. `區`, `副`, `繞`,
`延` and `預` are retained as route identity material; the index does not
invent one universal English expansion for each suffix. The current portal
catalogue shows examples such as `38區`, `286副`, `市民小巴1` with `繞溪山`,
and reserved-bus material, but the full pages do not state one rule that
governs every route carrying each suffix. That is a stated gap, not a source
conflict.

The conflict index itself is generated from 41 headings/sections in the
content corpus that explicitly publish a conflict, disagreement, contradiction,
dispute or conflicting measurement. It carries the original values and
measure descriptions, prefixes each copied citation with its source-page
identity, and links back to the source page. It is not a second hand-written
summary that could drift from the page where the conflict was found.

## Stated gaps

This glossary does not yet define every TRA service class, every bus-category
label, or every rail engineering abbreviation that will arrive with the THSR
and TRA expansions. The official TRA timetable establishes the two categories
used here, but a complete service-class glossary would require the railway's
current fare, timetable, rolling-stock and operating-rule pages to be read as a
separate research unit.
