## Conflicts

- **The historical 2023 Jin–Lie notice and the fetched official timetable PDF describe different service shapes:** the former announces morning/evening windows after the bridge opening, while the latter exposes broad Jiugong/Shuitou time columns. They are dated records from different operating contexts; neither is silently substituted for the other, and no current domestic timetable date was asserted.

- **The passenger-service fee is not treated as the Jin–Lie or mini-three-links ticket fare.** The 2026 Kinmen tourism notice publishes NT$100/160 as a passenger-service fee, while the county FAQ sends domestic vehicle-fare questions elsewhere. The sources do not support adding or collapsing these categories.
