## What is established

### Network shape and departure centres

- **Penghu’s island-hopping services are organised through North Sea, East Sea and South Sea departure centres.** **Source:** *South Sea ferry services and island-hopping transport*, titleOriginal `南海船班及航空`, 交通部觀光署澎湖國家風景區管理處, `https://www.penghu-nsa.gov.tw/ChiHoOneLer/transport/Traffic/IslandHoppingTraffic/IHT02.htm`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched on 2026-08-26 and updated 2026-08-14. Original-language basis: `前往北海、東海、南海各離島，可分別由位於白沙鄉赤崁村的北海遊客中心、岐頭村的岐頭遊客中心及馬公巿第三漁港的南海遊客中心購票前往，船班隨季節與天候狀況，會有所調整。`

- **The tourism authority publishes current-looking fare guidance but directs readers to the operating authority for rules and timetables.** **Source:** same full page. Original-language basis: `相關開航規定、票價及時間請參考單位公布資訊為主。`

### South Sea public timetable

- **The county’s live timetable displayed the date query 2026-08-26 to 2026-09-01 when fetched.** **Source:** *Public ferry timetable*, titleOriginal `交通船時刻表`, 澎湖縣政府公共車船管理處, `https://www.phpto.gov.tw/home.jsp?id=106`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched on 2026-08-26; page update date shown as 2026-08-18. Original-language basis: `※查詢結果：2026-08-26~2026-09-01之交通船班資料如下列示。`

- **A regular pattern shown for multiple dates is Magong 09:30, Wangan 10:20 and 10:50, Qimei 11:35, then Qimei 13:30, Wangan 14:15 and 14:30, Magong 15:20.** **Source:** the same full timetable page. Original-language basis: the 08-26 entry beginning `南海之星2號`, including `馬公09:30`, `10:20望安`, `望安10:50`, `11:35七美`, `七美13:30`, `14:15望安`, `望安14:30`, `15:20馬公`, and the repeated entries for 08-29, 08-30 and 09-01.

- **The displayed week also includes services continuing to Kaohsiung, overnight Qimei boats and a Friday special boat.** **Source:** the same full timetable page. Original-language basis: `延航高雄`, `夜泊七美`, and `亞曼尼(週五專船)` in the entries dated 08-27, 08-28 and 08-31/09-01.

- **The live page publishes separate weather-stop values and says the captain may decide from sea conditions; it also warns that cargo loading or weather can delay or adjust a sailing.** **Source:** the same full timetable page. Original-language basis: `馬公─望安─七美航線浪高3公尺或風速每秒超過15.6公尺`, `七美─高雄航線浪高2公尺或風速每秒超過13.6公尺`, `夜泊七美、週五專船風速每秒超過10.5公尺`, `船長亦可判斷海象決定是否停航`, `因當日裝卸貨物量多，開航時間則需延後`, and `得視狀況機動調整時間`.

### Ticketing

- **The county’s ticket guidance is effective from 19 July 2025.** **Source:** *Public ferry ticket-purchase guidance*, titleOriginal `澎湖縣政府公共車船管理處交通船船票購買須知`, 澎湖縣政府公共車船管理處, `https://www.penghu.gov.tw/userfiles/03/files/%E6%BE%8E%E6%B9%96%E7%B8%A3%E6%94%BF%E5%BA%9C%E5%85%AC%E5%85%B1%E8%BB%8A%E8%88%B9%E7%AE%A1%E7%90%86%E8%99%95%E4%BA%A4%E9%80%9A%E8%88%B9%E8%88%AA%E7%8F%AD%E8%B3%BC%E7%A5%A8%E9%A0%88%E7%9F%A5114_07_19.pdf`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full one-page PDF was fetched on 2026-08-26. Original-language basis: `自114年7月19日起適用`.

- **Outbound travel is not reserved online; return reservations are accepted, with 60 online places per sailing divided as 30 for Wangan and 30 for Qimei, maximum four tickets per person.** **Source:** same full PDF. Original-language basis: `每航次60名(望安、七美各30名)`, `去程不接受預約購票`, `返程接受預約購票`, and `每人限購4張`.

- **Online purchase closes at 21:00 the day before sailing, and a mobile QR ticket plus identity document can be used to board.** **Source:** same full PDF. Original-language basis: `開船前一日21：00前` and `購票完成可持手機數位QR船票及備妥身分證明文件登船。`

- **The guidance gives a 10% refund fee before departure and a no-fee force-majeure refund within its stated 30-day period; it says timetable changes are posted by the office and sailing weather follows Dongji sea conditions.** **Source:** same full PDF. Original-language basis: `於開船前30分...收取該票價10％手續費`, `因颱風或天候等不可抗力因素...30日內辦理退票，免扣收退票手續費`, `如有變動請自行上本處網站查看`, and `依據交通部中央氣象局東吉海象為依據`.

Original-language basis: the table rows `桶盤嶼 | 單趟120元/人`, `虎井嶼 | 單趟120元/人`, `馬公-望安272元/人`, `馬公-七美437元/人`, `望安-七美167元/人`, `馬公-望安300元/人`, and `馬公-南方四島460元/人`.

- **The same page links current timetable responsibility to Magong City Office for Tongpan/Hujing, the county vehicle-and-ship office for Wangan/Qimei, and Wangan Township Office for the Southern Four Islands.** **Source:** same full page. Original-language basis: the `時間表` column and links to `https://www.penghu.gov.tw/Tmakun/ch/home.jsp?id=247`, `https://www.phpto.gov.tw/home.jsp?id=106`, and `https://www.wangan.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=125`.

### Taiwan–Penghu sea access

- **The tourism authority describes summer light passenger ferries between Chiayi Budai and Magong at about 90 minutes, winter Taiwan Navigation service between Kaohsiung Xinbin and Magong at about four hours, with possible summer night sailings.** **Source:** *Ferry information for reaching Penghu*, titleOriginal `輪船資訊`, 交通部觀光署澎湖國家風景區管理處, `https://www.penghu-nsa.gov.tw/ChiHoOneLer/transport/Traffic/Traffic/ship01.htm`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched on 2026-08-26 and updated 2026-08-14. Original-language basis: `輕型客輪僅於夏季時開航，行駛於嘉義布袋港與馬公商港間，航行時間約90分鐘；冬季僅有台華輪...行船時間約4小時，夏季期間亦有夜間航次`.

- **The page’s port table lists Budai–Magong, Budai–Longmen, Kaohsiung–Magong, Kaohsiung–Qimei/Wangan/Magong and Tainan–Dongji.** **Source:** same full page. Original-language basis: the `航點資訊` table row beginning `嘉義布袋-澎湖馬公` and ending `台南-澎湖東吉`.

- **The page lists Taiwan Navigation, Hai An Hai You, Manten Star, Kaixuan, Bailey and Yongjie as operator families associated with the routes and vessels.** **Source:** same full page. Original-language basis: the `船班相關資訊` table beginning `台灣航業公司`, `海安海有集團`, `滿天星航運`, `凱旋海運`, `百麗海運`, and `詠傑海運`.

## Conflicts

- **Wind-stop thresholds differ between the current live timetable and the older ticket guidance.** The live timetable updated 2026-08-18 gives 15.6 m/s for Magong–Wangan–Qimei, 13.6 m/s for Qimei–Kaohsiung and 10.5 m/s for overnight/Friday-special services. The ticket PDF effective 2025-07-19 displays 15.5, 10, 10 and 13.5 m/s across its service columns. The extracted PDF text does not preserve a clean heading-to-column mapping, so the values are published as a dated conflict, not aligned by inference.

- **The official tourism fare table has two Magong–Wangan values in different service rows:** NT$272 in the Wangan/Qimei row and NT$300 in the Southern Four Islands row. These appear to be category-specific service values, not a single fare history; the page does not provide enough detail to collapse them, so both remain published.
