## What is established

### The Small Three Links reporting point moved to Kinmen Port Passenger Transport Center

- **Claim:** The official Kinmen tourism page says passenger reporting for the Xiamen Wutong and Quanzhou Shijing Small Three Links was changed to the Kinmen Port Passenger Transport Center.
- **Source:** Kinmen Port Passenger Transport Center / `金門港旅運中心`; Kinmen County Tourism Department, Kinmen Travel (金門縣政府觀光處金門觀光旅遊網); https://kinmen.travel/zh-tw/diary/detail/2007; PRIMARY; High — the full page was fetched directly.
- **Original-language sentences:** `往返 廈門五通碼頭、泉州石井碼頭` and `統一改至「金門港旅運中心」報到`.

### The official page gives a dated trial-operation date and address

- **Claim:** The page, dated 14 January 2026, says trial operation was scheduled for 3 February 2026 and gives the address as No. 300, Jinfeng Road, Jinshui Village, Jincheng Township, Kinmen County.
- **Source:** same full official page; PRIMARY; High.
- **Original-language sentences:** `2026-01-14`, `試營運時間：115年2月3日試營運` and `地址：金門縣金城鎮金水里金豐路300號`.

### Passenger flow is described by floor

- **Claim:** The page says inbound processing is on the first floor and outbound processing on the third floor.
- **Source:** same full official page; PRIMARY; High.
- **Original-language sentence:** `入境：1 樓｜出境：3 樓`.

### The page describes the facility as a four-storey building and a larger replacement

- **Claim:** The page describes a four-storey steel-frame building, approximately 3.5 times the space of the old passenger service center, with future harbour operations, tourism/leisure and duty-free functions.
- **Source:** same full official page; PRIMARY; High.
- **Original-language sentences:** `地上 4 層鋼構建築`, `空間為舊旅客服務中心 約 3.5 倍` and `未來結合：港埠營運＋觀光休閒＋免稅商店`.
