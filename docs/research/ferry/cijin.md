## What is established

### Public-transport scope and operator

- **The city identifies three Cijin-related transport-ferry route families:** Gushan–Cijin, Fronten–Zhongzhou and KW2–Cijin. **Source:** *Supervising the shipping company*, titleOriginal `督導輪船公司`, 高雄市政府交通局, `https://www.tbkc.gov.tw/Service/PublicTransport/ship?id=eff9b637-d56f-4817-bc2f-e140465e1876`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full government service page was read on 2026-08-26. Original-language basis: `高雄市輪船股份有限公司(下稱輪船公司)負責本市鼓山-旗津、前鎮-中洲、棧貳庫-旗津交通渡輪航線，另有愛河太陽能船...`.

### Gushan–Cijin

- **Published hours, interval and distance:** 06:00–24:00, approximately every 4–6 minutes, approximately 0.5 nautical mile. **Source:** *Cijin District transport information*, titleOriginal `交通資訊`, 旗津區公所, `https://cijin.kcg.gov.tw/Content_List.aspx?n=7957C12CCD70E4E1`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full district page was fetched. Original-language basis: `◎鼓山旗津航線：06:00~24:00，每4-6分鐘一班（往返行駛），浬程約0.5海浬。`.

- **Current full and motorcycle cash fare:** NT$30 for each, with the operator page dating the fare change to 1 January 2021. **Source:** *Gushan–Cijin route*, titleOriginal `鼓山-旗津航線`, 高雄市輪船股份有限公司, `https://kcs.kcg.gov.tw/Content_List.aspx?n=2EDF685BBC9D2857`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the operator page was fetched in full and updated 25 August 2026. Original-language basis: `◎110年元月1日起全票、機車現金票價優惠各為30元。`.

- **Fare rules and accepted media:** children under six or under 115 cm ride free; the concession groups are ages 6–12, 65 and over, disabled passengers and one necessary companion; iPASS, EasyCard, icash and Cijin Card are accepted. **Source:** the same full operator page. Original-language basis: `①6歲以下或115公分以下之兒童，乘船免費。`, `②一卡通、悠遊卡、愛金卡及旗津卡，可於此處使用。`, and the concession paragraphs beginning `④優待票資格為6~12歲、65歲以上...`.

- **Long-holiday fuel-motorcycle restriction:** on holidays of three days or more, 11:00–19:00, with exemptions for Cijin residents, electric motorcycles, bicycles and official-duty travellers. **Source:** the same full operator page. Original-language basis: `如遇三天以上(含)連假，每日11時至19時將實施遊客燃油機車禁登渡輪管制` and the following exemption sentence.

### Fronten–Zhongzhou

- **Published hours, interval and distance:** 05:45–22:10, approximately every 20–30 minutes, approximately 1.05 nautical miles. **Source:** *Cijin District transport information*, titleOriginal `交通資訊`, 旗津區公所, same URL, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `◎前鎮中洲航線:05:45~22:10，每20-30分鐘一班，浬程約1.05海浬。`.

- **Published fare and fare media:** the operator page gives NT$30 for the full and motorcycle cash fares and repeats the stored-value-card and concession rules. **Source:** *Fronten–Zhongzhou route*, titleOriginal `前鎮-中洲航線`, 高雄市輪船股份有限公司, `https://kcs.kcg.gov.tw/Content_List.aspx?n=C0BCB904EB284223`, PRIMARY, High confidence; full page fetched on 2026-08-26. Original-language basis: `◎110年元月1日起全票、機車現金票價優惠為30元。` and the complete purchasing notes.

### KW2–Cijin sea bus

- **Operator hours:** weekdays 12:30–16:15; weekends and public holidays 12:00–18:45. **Source:** *KW2–Cijin sea bus route*, titleOriginal `棧貳庫-旗津航線〖海上巴士〗`, 高雄市輪船股份有限公司, `https://kcs.kcg.gov.tw/Content_List.aspx?n=6DA8A39BB8402A6D`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full operator page was read; it was updated 25 August 2026. Original-language basis: `》平日（週一至五）12:30 - 16:15` and `》假日（週六日例假日）12:00 - 18:45`.

- **Current published fare:** full NT$80, half NT$40, with half-fare eligibility for ages 6–12, 65 and over, and disabled passengers plus one companion. **Source:** *KW2–Cijin ferry fare and service notice*, titleOriginal `海上巴士〖棧貳庫KW2⇆旗津〗渡輪航線`, 高雄港埠旅運中心股份有限公司, `https://www.kpld.com.tw/notice/53`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full official notice published 10 July 2026 was fetched. Original-language basis: `全票80元、半票40元` and the following `半票適用對象` lines.

- **The operator page does not expose a numeric KW2 fare in its fetched text:** it gives payment methods and concession eligibility, while the current KW2 notice supplies the numeric fare. This is a source-coverage boundary, not a fare contradiction. **Sources:** the full KCS operator page above and the full KPLD notice above.

### Piers and access

- **Gushan pier:** 1 Binhai 2nd Road, bus routes 50, 219A, 219B, 248, 248 District and Orange 1B, with MRT Xiziwan, light-rail Hamasen and the No. 1 Canal Landscape Bridge public-bike station listed. **Source:** *Gushan Ferry Pier and transport access*, titleOriginal `鼓山輪渡站〖西子灣〗`, 高雄市輪船股份有限公司, `https://kcs.kcg.gov.tw/Content_List.aspx?n=539EE52615393A8C`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `地址：高雄市鼓山區濱海二路1號` and the numbered transport lines.

- **Cijin pier:** 10 Coastal Road, served by Red 9A, Red 9B, Red 9C, Red 9D and Red 9F. **Source:** *Cijin Ferry Pier and transport access*, titleOriginal `旗津輪渡站`, 高雄市輪船股份有限公司, `https://kcs.kcg.gov.tw/Content_List.aspx?n=6A079D4DDA1F70C1`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `地址：高雄市旗津區海岸路10號` and `公車(旗津輪渡站)：紅9A、紅9B、紅9C、紅9D、紅9F`.

- **The district page also lists bus connections:** routes 1, 31 and 50 and Red 9, with Red 9 shown as 06:30–22:20. **Source:** *Cijin District transport information*, same full page. Original-language basis: the `公車` section beginning `1. 鼓山輪渡站` and `2. 旗津輪渡站`.

### Fleet and operating forecast

- **Electric ferries:** between 2022 and 2024, Kaohsiung and the Environment Ministry jointly built three electric ferries: 49-ton Xiongzhan 1, 49-ton Alice and 99-ton Xiongzhan 2. Xiongzhan 1 entered service on 24 January 2024; the release assigns Xiongzhan 2 to Gushan–Cijin and Alice to KW2–Cijin. **Source:** *Two electric ferries begin service to Cijin*, titleOriginal `往返旗津雙星啟航！ 電力驅動渡輪雄棧2號、艾莉絲號交船啟用`, 高雄市輪船股份有限公司, `https://kcs.kcg.gov.tw/News_Content.aspx?n=0B3BE2A2D8AF984E&s=C6EC2EADEFC3E69F&sms=5F57400441267452`, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `高雄市政府與環境部於111年至113年間共同建造三艘電力驅動渡輪...` and the sentences assigning service.

- **115-year budget forecast:** nine operating ferries, three sightseeing vessels and 4,278,838 annual passenger trips forecast; passenger revenue is broken out for the Gushan, Fronten and sea-bus route families, and the budget includes NT$50 million for free Cijin-resident rides. **Source:** *115 fiscal-year budget and operating plan*, titleOriginal `高雄市輪船股份有限公司115年度預算`, 高雄市輪船股份有限公司, `https://orgws.kcg.gov.tw/001/KcgOrgUploadFiles/316/RelFile/0/94817/fc7ad4c7-51ea-4556-a449-351de07fe8ac.pdf`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full 80-page PDF was fetched. Original-language basis: `本年度預計參加營運渡輪9艘、觀光遊輪3艘，預計全年載客人數427萬8,838人次` and `旗津居民免費乘船成本補貼費用 5,000萬元`.

## Conflicts

No conflicting current route value was found in the official pages fetched on 2026-08-26. The NT$30 Gushan–Cijin and Fronten–Zhongzhou fares and the NT$80/40 KW2–Cijin fare belong to different published routes; they are not competing measurements of one fare. The KCS KW2 page’s omission of a numeric fare from its text is recorded as a coverage gap rather than promoted as a contradiction.

## Stated gaps

- The reviewed official pages do not provide a vessel-by-vessel capacity table, a station-level accessibility inventory, or historical annual actual passenger counts. The 4,278,838 figure is a 115-year budget forecast, not an observed series.
- The reviewed pages do not provide a machine-readable full timetable for every route. The route pages should be rechecked before a dated trip, especially where the KW2 notice makes sailing subject to field dispatch.
- The official pages reviewed here do not provide a complete fare-history gazette trail before the current operator notices. A fare-history page would require the relevant Kaohsiung gazettes, council records or operator notices.
