# Corpus warning

`docs/research/` is a lead file, not an authority. This report treats Fugang
Fishing Port as a multimodal access node only where the full official pages
explicitly make the join. It does not infer a same-site airport or railway
interchange from proximity, and it does not turn a tourism access page into a
complete terminal inventory.

## What is established

### Fugang is the Taitung transfer point for the two island ferry families

- **Claim:** Taitung County's official island-transport page calls Fugang
  Fishing Port the transfer station for travel between Taitung and Green Island
  and Orchid Island.
- **Source:** *Outlying-island transport* / `離島交通`; Taitung County Government
  Tourism Department; https://tour.taitung.gov.tw/zh-tw/traffic/island; PRIMARY;
  High — the complete current HTML page was fetched directly.
- **Original-language basis:** `富岡漁港一直是臺東進出綠島和蘭嶼的轉運站`.

### The county page publishes ground-access modes from the airport and station

- **Claim:** The same page says passengers can travel from Taitung Airport or
  the railway station to Fugang by Taiwan Tourist Shuttle, Xingdong Bus or
  taxi, then board a ferry.
- **Source:** same full county page; PRIMARY; High.
- **Original-language basis:** `可由臺東航空站、火車站出發可搭乘台灣好行、興東客運(原鼎東客運海線)或計程車去富岡漁港搭船`.

### The official route descriptions give different levels of crossing time

- **Claim:** The East Coast National Scenic Area Administration prints 50
  minutes from Fugang to Green Island's Nanliao Fishing Port and 120 minutes
  from Fugang to Orchid Island's Kaiyuan Fishing Port. The county page prints
  the same Green Island and Orchid Island figures, and separately gives
  120–150 minutes for Houbihu–Orchid Island.
- **Source:** *Passenger ships* / `客船`; East Coast National Scenic Area
  Administration; https://www.eastcoast-nsa.gov.tw/zh-tw/service/ferry/; PRIMARY;
  High — complete page fetched directly. The county figures are from the full
  `離島交通` page above, also PRIMARY and High.
- **Original-language basis:** `臺東富岡漁港 ↔ 綠島南寮漁港 50分鐘`,
  `臺東富岡漁港 ↔ 蘭嶼開元漁港 120分鐘`, and the county entries
  `墾丁後壁湖 ↔ 蘭嶼開元漁港 120~150分鐘`.

### The scenic-area page publishes operator contact groups, not a complete live schedule

- **Claim:** The scenic-area page lists four operator/contact entries for the
  Green Island service and three for the Orchid Island service, while telling
  passengers that schedules change with season, weather or tide and should be
  checked with the operator.
- **Source:** full *Passenger ships* page; PRIMARY; High.
- **Original-language basis:** `綠島與臺東之間每日有多艘客船行駛，常因天候或海潮改變航行的時間`,
  `蘭嶼客船行駛船班依淡旺季不同會做調整`, and the operator lists under the
  Green Island and Orchid Island headings.

## Conflicts

No direct conflict was found for Fugang's transfer-node role. The two official
pages use the same 50-minute Green Island and 120-minute Fugang–Orchid Island
headline figures. The scenic-area prose also gives a broader 2–3 hour range for
Fugang–Orchid Island, so the page preserves the exact published headline and
the broader operational range rather than averaging them.

The airport/station access statement is an access relationship, not proof of a
direct rail or airport platform at Fugang. The sources do not give a timed
airport-to-port transfer or a single all-day bus timetable.
