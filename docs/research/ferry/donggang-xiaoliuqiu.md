## What is established

### Route and operator structure

- **The Maritime and Port Bureau’s current passenger-shipping ticket page lists Donggang–Xiaoliuqiu as a passenger route family with four operator entries.** The entries are Liuxing (`琉興`), Taifu (`泰富`), the Dongliu joint operation (`東琉線交通客船聯營處`) and Blue-White Shipping (`藍白航運`). **Source:** *Passenger-shipping ticket information*, titleOriginal `購票資訊`, Maritime and Port Bureau, MOTC, `https://www.motcmpb.gov.tw/Article?nodeId=376&siteId=1`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full current page was fetched on 2026-09-06; the page displayed last checked 2026-08-07 and last modified 2026-07-17. Original-language basis: the Donggang–Xiaoliuqiu entries under `東港-小琉球` naming `琉興`, `泰富`, `東琉線交通客船聯營處` and `藍白航運`.

- **The same page separately lists a Yancheng–Xiaoliuqiu family with the Dongliu joint operation and Dafu Shipping (`大福琉球航運`).** It is not silently merged into the Donggang family. **Source:** same full official page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: the adjacent `鹽埕-小琉球` entries naming `東琉線交通客船聯營處` and `大福琉球航運`.

### Published terminal and accessibility record

- **The Bureau’s vessel record for Liuxing identifies the route as Donggang–Xiaoliuqiu (Dafu Fishing Port) and names the Liuxing service centre in Donggang and the Dafu Fishing Port waiting room as both station and shore-connection facilities.** **Source:** *Donggang–Xiaoliuqiu (Dafu Fishing Port) — vessel information*, titleOriginal `東港-小琉球(大福漁港) — 船舶資訊`, Maritime and Port Bureau, MOTC, `https://www.motcmpb.gov.tw/PassengerShip/Detail?NodeId=610&PassengerShipSno=016441&ShipLaneNo=X277&SiteId=1`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full detail page was fetched and its route, station and shore-connection fields were read. Original-language basis: `東港-小琉球(大福漁港)`, `小琉球大福漁港候船室` and `東港東琉線船運服務中心`.

- **That same record marks the Liuxing passenger vessel as providing accessible shore facilities.** This is a record for that vessel/route entry, not evidence that every operator or sailing has identical accessibility. **Source:** same full official vessel page, PRIMARY, High confidence. Original-language basis: `本載客船舶提供無障礙岸接設施`.

### One dated vessel record

- **The official Liuxing record names the English vessel name as LIU XIN, official number 016441, passenger certification 149, gross tonnage 178, construction month October 2021, and survey validity through 25 October 2026.** **Source:** same full official vessel page, PRIMARY, High confidence because each field was visible in the fetched record. Original-language basis: `英文船名 Name LIU XIN`, `船舶號數 Official No. 016441`, `建造年月 Date of Built 110年10月`, `乘客定額 ... 149 人`, `總噸位 ... 178` and `檢查效期 到115年10月25日止`.

## Conflicts

- The Maritime and Port Bureau groups both Donggang–Xiaoliuqiu and Yancheng–Xiaoliuqiu on the same ticket page, but assigns different operator sets to them. This page keeps them as separate route families: Donggang–Xiaoliuqiu has four listed entries; Yancheng–Xiaoliuqiu has two. No source read here justifies treating every Xiaoliuqiu sailing as interchangeable.
- The official ticket page is an operator/ticketing directory rather than a single dated timetable. It does not provide one route-wide frequency or fare value that can safely be promoted as current for all four Donggang operators. The content page therefore leaves those fields TBC.
