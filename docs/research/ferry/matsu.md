## What is established

### Island network and operating pattern

- **The official island-transport page identifies Fuao, Baisha, Qiazi, Zhongzhu, Mengao and Qingfan as Matsu ferry ports, and says schedules vary with season and weather.** **Source:** *Island-to-island transport*, titleOriginal `島際往來交通`, 交通部觀光署馬祖國家風景區管理處, `https://www.matsu-nsa.gov.tw/zh-TW/transport/island`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched on 2026-08-26. Original-language basis: `南竿福澳港、北竿白沙港與橋仔港、東引中柱港、東莒猛澳港與西莒青帆港` and `船班時刻會隨季節與天候調整`.
- **The official page publishes a Nangan–Beigan crossing of about 20 minutes, with the listed adult, group, county and half fares of NT$160, NT$150, NT$110 and NT$80.** **Source:** same full page. Original-language basis: `約 20 分鐘` and `成人｜160元 ... 半票｜80元 ... 縣民全票｜110元`.
- **The published Nangan–Beigan departure pattern has 15 listed Nangan departures and a different 15-entry Beigan return pattern.** **Source:** same full page. Original-language basis: the `南竿→北竿` and `北竿→南竿` time rows.
- **Nangan–Juguang is about 50 minutes, has three daily round trips in the published pattern, and changes its Xiju/Dongju order by odd and even month.** **Source:** same full page, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full route section was fetched. Original-language basis: `約 50 分鐘`, `每日 3 班往返`, and `單月(先西後東)` / `雙月(先東後西)`.
- **Xiju–Dongju is about 10–15 minutes with four daily return departures in the published pattern.** **Source:** same full page. Original-language basis: `約 10-15 分鐘`, `每日 4 班往返` and the four time columns.
- **Nangan–Dongyin is about two hours, normally one or two sailings daily, with odd/even-date vessel and departure changes.** **Source:** same full page. Original-language basis: `約 2 小時`, `每日1-2 班（視淡旺季調整）`, and the `單日` / `雙日` departure rows.

### Daqiu and date-sensitive disruption

- **The official page treats Daqiu as seasonal services from Nangan and Beigan, with published fares, times, minimum-passenger rules and wind restrictions.** **Source:** *Island-to-island transport*, `https://www.matsu-nsa.gov.tw/zh-TW/transport/island`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full Daqiu sections were fetched. Original-language basis: `南竿 ⇄ 大坵`, `北竿 ⇄ 大坵`, `未達7人不開航`, and `陣風9級時停航、8級視海象決定`.
- **Hongyu Marine’s full 25 August 2026 notice cancelled all Nangan–Daqiu sailings on 26–27 August because of poor sea conditions during the typhoon’s outer circulation and said online-booked passengers would be automatically refunded.** **Source:** *26–27 August 2026 Daqiu sailing suspension*, titleOriginal `8/26-8/27鴻宇海運南竿⇔大坵全日停航`, 連江縣政府交通旅遊局 booking portal, `https://www.matsuebs.com/news/Detail/202608250000006`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full notice was fetched. Original-language basis: `8月26日至8月27日大坵航班全日停航` and `會自動全額退刷`.

### Fuzhou and Keelung routes

- **MOTC gives approximate crossing times of 90 minutes for Nangan Fuao–Fuzhou Langqi and 30 minutes for Beigan Baisha–Fuzhou Huangqi.** **Source:** *Mini-three-links sailing table*, titleOriginal `小三通—航班表`, 交通部航港局, `https://www.motcmpb.gov.tw/BoatSchedule?nodeId=380&siteId=1`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched. Original-language basis: `馬祖(南竿福澳)-福州(琅岐)航線航程時間約為 90 分鐘` and `馬祖(北竿白沙)-福州(黃岐)航線航程時間約為 30 分鐘`.
- **The county booking fare page lists NT$1,000 for Nangan–Langqi and NT$650 for Beigan–Huangqi, and says the mini-three-links fares include ticket, insurance, terminal service and cleaning fees.** **Source:** *Matsu sea-transport booking system fare table*, titleOriginal `馬祖海上交通訂位購票系統 — 票價`, 連江縣政府交通旅遊局, `https://www.matsuebs.com/BoatNotice?contentType=notice01`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full current page was fetched. Original-language basis: the `南竿 - 琅岐航線(小三通)` and `北竿 - 黃岐航線(小三通)` tables and `以上票價包含船票、保險費、碼頭服務費、清潔費…等費用`.
- **The county booking FAQ says mini-three-links tickets cannot be collected at a convenience store because the passenger must bring original cross-border documents to the port counter.** **Source:** *Matsu sea-transport booking system*, `https://www.matsuebs.com/`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full FAQ was fetched. - **The official visitor ferry page describes the Keelung–Matsu overnight route as 8–10 hours and says the route order is Nangan-first on odd-date departures and Dongyin-first on even-date departures.** **Source:** *How to reach Matsu — ferries*, titleOriginal `如何前往馬祖-渡輪`, 交通部觀光署馬祖國家風景區管理處, `https://www.matsu-nsa.gov.tw/zh-TW/transport/ferry`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full page was fetched. Original-language basis: `航程約8至10小時` and the `先馬後東` / `先東後馬` route rows.
- **The full county booking FAQ says a morning Fuzhou connection requires the odd-date, Nangan-first sailing, which arrives before about 07:00.** **Source:** *Matsu sea-transport booking system*, `https://www.matsuebs.com/`, PRIMARY, High confidence because the full FAQ was fetched. Original-language basis: `單號日期出發先抵達南竿` and `約早上 07:00 前抵達`.

## Conflicts

- **The official Keelung–Matsu fare table publishes direction-specific values rather than one symmetric fare:** Keelung-to-Matsu first class is NT$1,890, while Matsu-to-Keelung first class is NT$1,800, with corresponding resident and concession differences. Both are retained as directional fare rows.
- **The static Daqiu timetable and the live county notice are different dated states:** the visitor page publishes seasonal Daqiu departures and the 25 August 2026 operator notice cancels 26–27 August sailings for weather. The cancellation is not used to erase the seasonal timetable.
- **The visitor ferry page describes South-North Star as a charter-form Taipei-Port service after 2024, while MOTC’s ticket directory still lists Taipei–Matsu booking contact information.** These are retained as separate descriptions of service and ticketing context; the page does not infer a fixed scheduled service from the directory entry.
