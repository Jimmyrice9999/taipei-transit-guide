## Conflicts and measurement boundaries

- The five-entry Southern Navigation Centre page is a notice/index count, while
  the ticketing directory is an operator/contact directory and the Penghu page
  is a corridor/access table. They are not competing measurements of one
  national route total and are kept separate.
- The Penghu page says seasonal and weather-sensitive services change over time;
  the ticketing directory's operator rows do not establish a departure on every
  day of September 2026. No timeless frequency is published.
