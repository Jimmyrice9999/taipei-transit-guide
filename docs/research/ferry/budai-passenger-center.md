# Budai Passenger Service Center

**Checked 6 September 2026.** This pass fetched and read the full Maritime and
Port Bureau terminal case page and the Taiwan International Ports Corporation
notice. It establishes a terminal node and its published building facilities;
it does not establish a current timetable, fare, land-transfer timetable or

## What is established

- **Claim.** The Maritime and Port Bureau identifies the Budai Port Passenger
  Service Center at 334-1 Zhongshan Road, Budai Township, Chiayi County, on the
  Chiayi Budai–Penghu Magong route, with a 5,050-square-metre passenger
  building.
- **Source.** `Budai Port Passenger Service Center` / `布袋港旅客服務中心`.
- **URL.** https://www.motcmpb.gov.tw/Article?NodeId=50041&SiteId=1
- **Kind.** PRIMARY. **Confidence.** High — full page fetched and read; the
  page records a 2025-10-22 last check by the Bureau.
- **Original-language evidence.** `地 址：嘉義縣布袋鎮中山路334-1號`,
  `航 線：嘉義布袋至澎湖馬公`, and `場站面積：5050平方公尺`.

- **Claim.** The same page lists the passenger-building facilities including a
  priority ticket counter, control/check-in facilities, waiting areas, a
  nursing room, Wi-Fi, toilets, ATM, drinking water, phone charging, an
  electronic sailing board and an accessible toilet.
- **Source and URL.** The same full primary page above.
- **Confidence.** High — the facilities are listed in the page's numbered
  station-facilities field.
- **Original-language evidence.** `設有愛心售票櫃檯、通關設施、候船室、親子候船區、哺集乳室、免費 Wifi、智慧廁所、ATM、自動防疫門、商業空間、飲水機、手機充電區、電子航班資訊看板、無障礙廁間`.

- **Claim.** A 21 October 2025 Taiwan International Ports Corporation notice
  describes Budai as a passenger and goods hub for Penghu, says the passenger
  center was completed, and describes planned passenger/cargo separation and a
  proposed passenger-service-area expansion from 0.5 to 2.5 hectares.
- **Source.** `Budai Port expands passenger/cargo zoning and begins passenger-
  service-area leasing` / `布袋商港擴大客貨分區管理，啟動客運服務區招商作業`.
- **URL.** https://kh.twport.com.tw/zh-tw/news/News?A=248&Id=10431&P=1
- **Kind.** PRIMARY. **Confidence.** High for what the dated notice says; the
  expansion is retained as a plan, not treated as proof that the final layout
  is complete.
- **Original-language evidence.** `布袋商港為往返澎湖載運旅客及運送民生物資之重要樞紐` and
  `將客運服務區範圍由原有之0.5公頃大幅增加至2.5公頃`.

## Conflicts and measurement boundaries

- The terminal case page reports the completed building's 5,050-square-metre
  area. The port-company notice reports a wider passenger-service-area
  redevelopment scope of 0.5 to 2.5 hectares. These measure different things
  (building area versus port-area planning scope) and are not averaged.
- A building's accessible-toilet listing is not a vessel-level or end-to-end
  step-free guarantee.
