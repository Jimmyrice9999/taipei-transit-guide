## What is established

### Origins and the 2009–2012 pilot

- **The system began as a BOT (build–operate–transfer) scheme let to Giant
  under Mayor Hao Lung-pin, piloted in Xinyi District from 11 March
  2009.** Source: 臺北市公共自行車租賃系統, 維基百科 (secondary).
  「郝龍斌市府任內以BOT模式委託本土自行車大廠巨大機械（捷安特）建置和營運」.
  Confidence: Medium (encyclopedic secondary, not independently checked
  against the tender record).
- **The pilot's start date, 11 March 2009, is independently confirmed by
  YouBike's own operator timeline.** Source: YouBike 微笑單車－認識
  YouBike－大事記 (youbike.com.tw), publisher 微笑單車股份有限公司 (primary).
  Entry: 「2009-03-11：臺北市公共自行車租賃系統信義計畫區示範營運」.
  Confidence: High (primary, operator's own dated milestone list).
- **The pilot's fleet — 11 stations, 500 bikes — is not independently
  corroborated**: it appears only in the zh.wikipedia article (secondary),
  which cites no source for the figure at that point in its text. TBC on the
  content page.
- **Citywide operation began 30 August 2012, and the trial period had passed
  one million cumulative rides by 30 November 2012.** Source: YouBike 大事記
  (primary). 「2012-08-30：臺北市公共自行車租賃計畫試營運啟動」;
  「2012-11-30：臺北市YouBike試營運期結束已超過百萬騎乘數」.
  Confidence: High.
- **The original contract scope, as reported by zh.wikipedia, called for 163
  stations and 5,350 bicycles within seven years** — 「預計在七年內建置163個
  站點，提供5,350輛公共自行車」. This is a specific enough figure to look like
  a contract citation, but the article gives no footnote for it and the
  underlying tender document was not located this run. Confidence: Low
  (secondary, unsourced within its own source).

### Three fare eras, not one

This is the strongest correction this run makes to the existing lead file,
which described only "April 2018: transfer discount introduced" as though it
were the system's origin story. The actual sequence, as far as this run
established it, has three distinct eras:

1. **An unconditional first-30-minutes-free policy ran from the early system
   until April 2015.** Multiple secondary accounts agree on this and on its
   ending: 風傳媒 (storm.mg), 「歷史風數據》免費騎的YouBike回來了！柯文哲當年
   為何寧願挨罵也要收5元？」; 關鍵評論網 (The News Lens),
   「北市YouBike免費取消：前30分收5元、新北不跟進」(18 April 2015); 環境資訊
   中心 (e-info.org.tw), citing Mayor Ko Wen-je's 2015 decision explicitly.
   No primary (city or operator) source for the *original* 2009–2015 policy
   itself was located this run — only secondary reporting of its 2015
   cancellation. Confidence: Medium (three independent secondary outlets
   agree; no primary read).
2. **1 April 2015: Mayor Ko Wen-je ended the free period, introducing a flat
   NT$5 charge for the first 30 minutes.** Date confirmed by 關鍵評論網 (The
   News Lens), 「北市YouBike免費取消：前30分收5元、新北不跟進」(fetched in
   full, not a snippet): 「4月1日起實施」. The same article states **New
   Taipei did not follow** — it kept its own free first 30 minutes through
   the end of 2015, citing its system still being in a "construction and
   promotion phase," while budgeting roughly NT$70 million/year to subsidise
   it. 風傳媒 (storm.mg), also fetched in full, corroborates the April 2015
   change and adds Ko's administration's stated rationale: survey data
   showing commuting trips up 52% and school trips up 24% after a comparable
   fee change, leisure trips down 31%, and that even after the change only
   "18%" of usage was for regular commuting — short of what officials hoped.
   Confidence: High for the 1 April 2015 date and the New Taipei divergence
   (both from full-page secondary fetches); Medium for the rationale
   figures (secondary, no primary transcript of the survey or Ko's remarks
   found).
   - **CONFLICT — the budget saving cited for the 2015 change.** 風傳媒
     states the city's subsidy fell from roughly NT$20 million/month to
     NT$8 million/month; 關鍵評論網 states it was expected to fall from
     roughly NT$20 million/month to NT$13 million/month. Same policy change,
     two different secondary figures for the resulting saving. Published as
     a conflict, not resolved.
3. **A transfer discount (NT$5 off when connecting to/from bus or MRT) began
   in 2018** (民國107年) and ran until superseded in 2024. Source: 中央社 (CNA),
   「北市YouBike前30分鐘免費28日起實施 轉乘公車捷運不再優惠」(27 Feb 2024),
   secondary, reporting the transfer discount 「自107年上路」. No dedicated
   2018 primary launch announcement for this specific discount was located
   this run — the existing lead file's claim of an "April 2018" date was not
   independently confirmed or refuted; CNA gives only the ROC year.
   Confidence: Medium.
4. **28 February 2024: Mayor Chiang Wan-an restored an unconditional first
   30 minutes free, and the transfer discount was simultaneously
   cancelled.** Sources, both primary: YouBike's own service announcement,
   「2024/02/28起，臺北市YouBike 2.0 前30分鐘免費」(youbike.com.tw) gives the
   tiered pricing after the free period (NT$10/30min to 4h, NT$20/30min to
   8h, NT$40/30min beyond) and confirms registered members qualify citywide;
   the Taipei DOT's own FAQ on YouBike 2.0E pricing states 「自113年2月28日
   實施YouBike 2.0前30分鐘免費後，即同步取消轉乘優惠」. Confirmed by
   secondary reporting from CNA and 環境資訊中心 (e-info.org.tw), the latter
   citing projected effects: a 15% ridership increase and roughly 7,000
   tonnes/year of avoided carbon emissions. Confidence: High (primary
   operator and city sources, corroborated by two independent secondary
   outlets).

**This means the existing lead file's picture — a single durable "transfer
discount from April 2018" — was incomplete.** It omitted the original
2009–2015 free period, its 2015 cancellation, and the 2024 policy reversal
that replaced the very discount the lead described as current. See
"Contradicted the corpus" in the run report.

### Why YouBike 2.0 exists: the power-connection story

This part of the lead file's account **is well corroborated**, and by primary
sources on both ends:

- **The 1.0 station-siting rules (23 March 2015) explicitly required a
  nearby power connection**, alongside a ≥600 m spacing principle, ≥2 m clear
  sidewalk width where sited on a footpath (else ≥6 m clearance off it), and
  a minimum 30 m / 15-dock station footprint. Source: 臺北市政府交通局-新聞稿
  -YouBike設站有準則 今後選點標準一致 (dot.gov.taipei, primary). The
  rationale given: a survey found roughly 48% of users walked to a station
  within 5 minutes, supporting a 350 m service-radius principle. Confidence:
  High (primary, directly fetched).
- **The redesign rationale for 2.0 is stated directly in the city's own
  planning press release**: the 1.0 system needed a municipal power
  connection and a minimum scale to be economic, both of which capped
  expansion; 2.0 「免除市電需求，控制器移轉車機」 — eliminates the mains
  requirement and moves the controller to the bike itself — letting station
  siting instead respond to "地形、人行道寬度等空間狀態" (terrain and
  sidewalk width). Source: 臺北市政府交通局-新聞稿-臺北市YouBike2.0計畫說明
  (dot.gov.taipei, primary). Confidence: High.
- **The trial launched 15 January 2020, 10:00, around Gongguan MRT and NTU**:
  102 stations sharing about 1,800 docks. Source: 臺北市政府交通局-新聞稿-
  YouBike 2.0試辦計畫啟動 (dot.gov.taipei, primary). This release states
  **500** new bicycles deployed for the trial. Confidence: High for the date
  and station/dock counts.
- **CONFLICT — bike count for the same trial.** A second DOT press release
  about the same 102-station Gongguan pilot, dated 25 February 2020 (臺北市
  政府交通局-新聞稿-YouBike 2.0使用率佳，歡迎共同體驗, primary), states
  **600** new bicycles ("600輛新式公共自行車") for what appears to be the
  identical pilot area, alongside a stated NT$2-for-30-minutes introductory
  rate for the first three months. Both figures come from the same primary
  publisher about the same scheme six weeks apart; this may reflect a fleet
  top-up between the two releases rather than a contradiction, but nothing
  found says so explicitly. Published as a conflict, not resolved.
- **2.0's dock system uses one bicycle per post ("1柱1車"), against 1.0's two
  bicycles per post ("1柱2車").** Source: 臺北市政府交通局-新聞稿-YouBike 2.0
  正式營運就是今天 (dot.gov.taipei, primary — the 8 May 2021 full-launch
  release). Confidence: High.
- **Full service began 8 May 2021 at 10:20, with 160 new stations added that
  day on top of the existing 103 Gongguan/NTU stations (263 total), and a
  year-end 2021 target of 750 stations.** New stations were to go live in
  batches every Tuesday and Friday at 10:00, announced the day before.
  Source: same release, primary. Confidence: High.
- **The 2020 trial rate was NT$2 for the first 30 minutes for the first
  three months**, per the DOT's trial-launch release (primary), separate
  from the later citywide fare policy described above.

### The changeover and 1.0's retirement

- **By October 2022, YouBike 2.0 had reached its 1,200-station citywide
  target, and 1.0 accounted for only 8.5% of system ridership with a
  turnover of 1.8** rides/bike/day. Source: 臺北市政府交通局-新聞稿-臺北市
  YouBike1.0系統將全面退場 (dot.gov.taipei, primary, the retirement
  announcement). Confidence: High.
- **YouBike 1.0 stopped operating in Taipei at 00:00 on 3 December 2022
  (Saturday), after more than 13 years of service and over 200 million
  cumulative rides.** Same source, primary. The city coordinated with New
  Taipei on 51 cross-boundary "hotspot" locations to raise YouBike 2.0
  availability ahead of the cutover. Confidence: High.
- **1.0's fleet at retirement is given as 13,072 bikes** by zh.wikipedia
  (secondary); no primary figure for the exact fleet number at the retirement
  date was independently found this run, though it is consistent in
  magnitude with the lead file's unsourced "≈13,000." Confidence: Medium
  (secondary only).
- **Disposition of retired 1.0 bikes**: 1,300 were donated to Indigenous
  communities (原鄉) and 1,106 transferred to Taipei city agencies for
  neighbourhood patrols and school cycling-safety education, marked at a
  memorial installation at MRT Taipei City Hall Station Exit 3 (the system's
  first station). Source: 臺北市政府交通局-新聞稿-臺北市YouBike紀念意象揭幕
  退場車輛捐贈原鄉 (dot.gov.taipei, primary). Separately, bicycles judged
  unfit for further use were sold to a recycling contractor in late 2021 (ROC
  110) at **NT$41 per bike**, which the city's own clarification states
  reflects disassembly, transport, labour and waste-disposal costs rather
  than under-pricing. Source: 臺北市政府全球資訊網-即時新聞澄清-YouBike1.0
  不堪用報廢車輛說明 (gov.taipei, primary). Confidence: High for both figures,
  each from a directly fetched primary clarification.
- **CONFLICT / no corroboration — original purchase cost.** The existing
  lead file's claim that "each bike costs roughly NT$10,000" was not found in
  any source this run. zh.wikipedia separately gives the 1.0 bike's original
  price as **NT$13,000**, with a NT$9,000 loss/replacement fee (secondary,
  unattributed within the article). The NT$41 recycling figure above measures
  scrap/disposal value, not original cost, and should not be conflated with
  either. Three different figures, three different things measured: TBC on
  the content page for original unit cost; the recycling figure is the one
  actually confirmed by a primary source.
- **A NT$350 cross-boundary "misplacement fee" applied when a 1.0 bike was
  ridden from New Taipei into Taipei and returned there after Taipei's
  cutover**, because New Taipei retained YouBike 1.0 for longer. Source:
  今周刊 (Business Today), secondary, quoting the mechanism in detail:
  「民眾日後從新北騎乘橘色車身的YouBike1.0，騎到台北市就得換白色車身的
  2.0，不小心騎錯的話，就得自付350元的調度費協助還車」. No Taipei DOT primary
  press release stating this specific fee was independently located this run
  — the retirement press release describes the cross-boundary coordination
  but does not itself state the NT$350 figure in the portion fetched.
  Confidence: Medium (secondary only for the fee amount itself).
- **New Taipei's own YouBike 2.0 reached full operation on 20 October
  2021**, roughly five and a half months after Taipei's full launch. Source:
  YouBike 大事記 (primary).

### YouBike 2.0E

- **2.0E launched in Taipei and New Taipei on 30 August 2024.** Confirmed
  independently by two primary sources: YouBike's own service announcement
  「2024/08/30起，臺北市及新北市 YouBike 2.0E電輔車正式上線」
  (youbike.com.tw) and the operator's 大事記 milestone list, which carries
  the same date. Confidence: High.
- **The 2024 launch announcement states a range of 80–100 km on a full
  charge on flat ground, tiered pricing (NT$20/30min for the first two
  hours, NT$40/30min beyond, no free period or subsidy), and that existing
  YouBike members need no separate registration.** The lead file's "up to 80
  km" figure is the lower end of what the primary announcement actually says
  (80–100 km); worth correcting upward, not down. Source: same YouBike
  announcement, primary.
- **2.0E does not receive the first-30-minutes-free benefit or TPASS
  discounts in Taipei**, on the stated grounds that e-bikes already turn
  over faster than standard bikes elsewhere and the fleet is small relative
  to demand from genuine long-distance/hill-climbing users; Taipei-issued
  senior/disability transit cards are the one carve-out, with a free first 30
  minutes for e-bike rental specifically. Source: 臺北市政府交通局-常見問答
  -電動輔助自行車YouBike2.0E為什麼沒有前30分鐘免費及TPASS都會通定期票優惠
  (dot.gov.taipei / gov.taipei, primary). Confirms the corpus lead's implicit
  assumption that 2.0E shares 2.0's fare benefits was **wrong** — it does
  not, in Taipei.
- **TPASS 基北北桃1200 does cover the first-30-minutes-free benefit for
  standard YouBike 2.0** (not 2.0E) across Taipei, New Taipei, Taoyuan and
  Keelung. This is corroborated by a search synthesis of operator FAQ
  material rather than a single fetched primary page; treat as Medium
  confidence pending a direct fetch of the TPASS-specific YouBike FAQ.

### Scale, current as of the sources found

- **By 22 May 2025, cumulative rides nationwide (all cities operating
  YouBike) stood at 995,333,210, with 15,693,709 issued member cards**,
  according to zh.wikipedia's citation of the operator's live statistics
  page (secondary, describing a primary source not independently re-fetched
  this run — the live stats page was not fetched directly). **The operator's
  own 大事記 (primary) separately states cumulative rides passed one billion
  on 4 June 2025** — consistent with, and slightly later than, the
  Wikipedia-cited figure, which is plausible as the same counter ticking
  over. Confidence: High for the 1-billion milestone (primary, directly
  fetched); Medium for the more granular 22 May 2025 figures (secondary
  citation of a primary source not independently checked).
- **As of a Taipei DOT press release dated 14 August 2025**, Taipei alone
  operated **1,633 stations and 23,860 YouBikes (including 2,000 e-assist
  bikes)**, approaching 400 million cumulative Taipei rides — a milestone
  the release states took roughly 3.5 years to go from 100 million to 200
  million, 2.5 years to reach 300 million, and only 17 months to reach 400
  million after the February 2024 free-30-minutes policy. Source: 臺北市政府
  交通局-新聞稿-臺北市YouBike前30分鐘免費引領騎乘風潮 累計騎乘次數即將突破4億
  人次 (dot.gov.taipei, primary). Confidence: High.
- **CONFLICT / apparent revision — the 2026 expansion target.** Secondary
  reporting from February 2024 (環境資訊中心/e-info.org.tw and 中央社/CNA,
  both citing city officials at the time of the fare-policy announcement)
  gives the 2026 target as **27,500 bikes and 2,000 stations**, a 66%
  increase on the pre-policy fleet of 16,562. The August 2025 primary DOT
  press release above instead frames the 2026 target as **2,000 stations
  with 4,500 YouBike 2.0E bikes** — a figure for the e-bike sub-fleet only,
  not a restated total. These may not be in tension — 4,500 could be the
  e-bike component of a still-current 27,500 total — but the 27,500 total
  figure was not repeated in the later primary release, and nothing found
  states outright that both targets still hold simultaneously. Published as
  a conflict rather than reconciled.

### Operator and system structure

- **The operating company is 微笑單車股份有限公司 (Smile Bike Co., Ltd.)**,
  under contract to the Taipei City Government Department of Transportation.
  Source: 臺北市政府交通局-公共自行車介紹-簡介 (dot.gov.taipei, primary):
  「前已於111年底完成全市YouBike2.0系統升級（另於111年12月3日起停止營運
  YouBike1.0系統）」, and the page names the operator directly as running the
  system under the department's commission. Confidence: High for the
  operator's name and role.
- **Smile Bike Co. appears to be organisationally linked to Giant**: a
  search-engine synthesis of company-registry listings (twincn.com and
  recruitment-site company pages, not independently fetched and read in
  full this run) states that Giant Manufacturing directors/supervisors have
  also sat on Smile Bike's board. This was not verified against the primary
  company registry filing itself. Confidence: Low — flagged as a lead, not
  a checked fact.
- **微程式資訊 (Microprogram Information)** is widely described in secondary
  material as the systems/electronics designer for both the 2018–19 E-YouBike
  trial and 2.0. This was not independently verified against a primary
  Microprogram or city source this run. Confidence: Low.

### Current five-city static station layer

- **TDX publishes a static Bike/Station/City feed for Taichung and Kaohsiung
  with the same structured fields used for the existing Taipei-region pull.**
  A direct authenticated pull on 25 August 2026 returned 1,817 Taichung rows
  and 1,500 Kaohsiung rows. The normalized records contain StationUID,
  StationID, StationName, StationAddress, StationPosition, BikesCapacity,
  ServiceType, SrcUpdateTime and UpdateTime. Confidence: High (MOTC TDX
  primary data, fetched directly; the response's original field names are the
  evidence rather than a search snippet). The observed TDX UpdateTime values
  were 2026-08-25T23:37:06+08:00 for Taichung's first page and
  2026-08-25T23:38:06+08:00 for the later Taichung and Kaohsiung pages.
  Source URL: `https://tdx.transportdata.tw/api/basic/v2/Bike/Station/City/{City}`.
- **The 25 August five-city build contains 7,403 static rows and 192,573
  published docks.** By city: Taipei 1,794 / 50,876; New Taipei 1,593 /
  48,355; Taoyuan 699 / 24,214; Taichung 1,817 / 38,746; Kaohsiung 1,500 /
  30,382. Confidence: High for the retrieved snapshot (TDX primary; sums
  calculated from the returned `BikesCapacity` fields). The current pull
  produced 364 unique nearest-coordinate joins to the Taipei-region rail
  registry; Taichung and Kaohsiung received none because that registry's
  candidate systems are Taipei Metro, New Taipei Circular/Danhai/Ankeng and
  Taoyuan Airport MRT, not a claim that those cities lack rail interchanges.
- **Taichung's municipal open-data catalogue publishes a citywide YouBike2.0
  station and live-parking dataset, updated every ten minutes, with station,
  address, coordinate and capacity fields.** Source: 臺中市公共自行車
  (YouBike2.0)租借站&即時車位資料, 臺中市政府交通局, primary,
  `https://opendata.taichung.gov.tw/search/6e38eb56-0e9a-4b9e-806d-23cd35d44d6b`.
  Original-language text: 「提供目前臺中市公共自行車(YouBike2.0)租借站點與即時車位資料」;
  「更新頻率｜即時(每10分鐘)」. Confidence: High (municipal catalogue
  page fetched in full on 25 August 2026).
- **Kaohsiung's national open-data catalogue publishes a YouBike2.0 station
  real-time information dataset supplied by the city Transportation Bureau.**
  The page lists district, address, station code, total capacity, coordinates,
  available-bike/return-bike and operating-state fields and says the data is
  updated every ten minutes in its description. Source: 高雄YouBike2.0公共自行車
  租賃站即時資訊, 高雄市政府交通局, primary,
  `https://data.gov.tw/dataset/173477`. Original-language text: 「包含租賃站
  場站名稱、行政區、地址、場站代號、總車位、可借車輛、場站資料修改時間、緯度、經度、可還車輛及營運狀態等資訊，資料每10分鐘更新一次」.
  Confidence: High (catalogue page fetched in full on 25 August 2026).
- **TDX did not expose a usable district field in the Taichung or Kaohsiung
  Station/City records pulled on 25 August 2026.** All 1,817 Taichung rows and
  all 1,500 Kaohsiung rows therefore remain in an explicit Unclassified group
  in the generated indexes. Confidence: High for the data shape and grouping
  decision; no district was inferred from address text or station name.
