Explainer pages rather than entity records: how the stored-value cards
differ, what the regional commuter pass actually covers, and how Taipei
Metro's own fare rules work. Each figure is cited to the operator or
regulator that publishes it.

See also [Taiwan airport ground transport](/ticketing/guides/airport-ground-transport/),
which keeps the national airport boundary and the different operator layers
separate, and [payment and ticketing infrastructure](/ticketing/guides/payment-infrastructure/),
which covers the card technology, on-bus validator hardware and the
2020–2023 merger of the electronic-ticket and electronic-payment
regulatory regimes[^fsc-merger] that the cards themselves sit on top of.
