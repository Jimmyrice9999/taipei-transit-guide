## How to use this glossary

Chinese transport labels are often more precise than an English translation
suggests. A route suffix may identify a current timetable variant without
proving when that variant began, and a ticket name may describe a product that
can be carried on more than one kind of card. This glossary therefore explains
how each term is used on this site and links to the page where the term does
real work, rather than pretending that every short label has one universal
definition.[^tdx-api]

## Bus and fare terms

| Chinese | Plain English | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 段次 | Fare segment or fare-stage count. A ride that crosses the published segment structure can be charged for more than one segment; it is not the same as the number of stops.[^bus-fare] | [Bus fare stages](/ticketing/guides/bus-fare-stages/) |
| 分段緩衝區 | Segment buffer zone. The portal exposes this as a fare-relevant field on route pages; the exact boundary is route-specific.[^bus-minibus-route] | [Route data](/bus/routes/) |
| 幹線公車 | Trunk bus. The portal uses it as a named bus family alongside ordinary routes and other service categories.[^bus-catalogue] | [Bus network](/bus/network/) |
| 先導公車 | MRT pilot bus. New Taipei defines it as early service intended to cultivate demand along a planned MRT corridor; it is not a claim that the rail line is already open.[^ntpc-mrt-pilot] | [Rail projects](/rail/projects/project-status/) |
| 通勤專車 | Commuter shuttle. The portal lists 內科 and 南軟 commuter services as named route families, so “commuter bus” here is a service label, not a promise that every peak-only route has the same operating rule.[^bus-catalogue] | [Bus routes](/bus/routes/) |
| 市民小巴 | Community minibus. The portal uses this as a route family and a live route page carries the label directly; it may also publish a separate detour working.[^bus-catalogue][^bus-minibus-route] | [Bus routes](/bus/routes/) |
| 區 | A route-name suffix used by the portal for a variant such as `38區` or `202區`. The suffix is preserved as part of the route identity; its exact operating meaning must be read from that route's own schedule.[^bus-catalogue][^tdx-api] | [Bus route pages](/bus/routes/) |
| 副 | A route-name suffix used for an attached or alternate working such as `286副`. It is not silently merged with the unsuffixed route.[^bus-catalogue][^tdx-api] | [Route identity](/bus/network/joint-operation/) |
| 繞 | A detour or routing variant. The current 市民小巴1 page publishes separate 繞溪山 workings, so the guide treats it as a scheduled variant rather than an inferred historical extension.[^bus-minibus-route] | [市民小巴1](/bus/routes/minibus/minibus-1-12m6pyo/) |
| 延 | An extension-labelled variant where it appears in a route name. The suffix alone does not establish the date, length or reason for the extension; those require the route's notice or schedule.[^tdx-api] | [Bus route history](/bus/routes/) |
| 預 | A reservation-labelled variant. Taipei's current reserved-bus page describes reservation handling and says a passenger who did not reserve may still board when the service runs.[^bus-reservation] | [Reserved bus programme](/bus/routes/) |

The most important rule for the suffixes is identity: a number alone is not a
route. The municipality, written route name, suffix and TDX identifier stay
together in the data layer, which is why `區`, `副`, `繞`, `延` and `預` are not
flattened into a single English “branch” label.[^tdx-api]

## Rail, ticket and operating terms

| Chinese | Plain English | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 區間車 | Regional or all-stations train. Taiwan Railways' official timetable labels individual services 區間車 and places them beside the faster 區間快 category.[^tra-timetable] | [Rail section](/rail/) |
| 區間快 | Fast regional train. The official timetable uses it as a distinct train category from 區間車; the guide does not translate both as simply “local train.”[^tra-timetable] | [Rail section](/rail/) |
| 幹線 | Trunk line or main line. TRA uses it in names such as 東部幹線, while Taipei's bus portal uses 幹線公車 for a bus family; the mode is therefore part of the meaning.[^tra-timetable][^bus-catalogue] | [Rail and bus indexes](/rail/) |
| 悠遊卡 | EasyCard, a stored-value electronic ticket product and payment brand; it is not the same string as the issuer's corporate name.[^easycard-about] | [Cards and passes](/ticketing/guides/cards-passes-and-fares/) |
| 一卡通 | iPASS, a separate stored-value electronic ticket product and payment brand with its own issuer.[^ipass-about] | [Cards and passes](/ticketing/guides/cards-passes-and-fares/) |
| TPASS | A regional public-transport pass product. The operating notice lists eligible media and modes; TPASS is not used here as a synonym for “EasyCard.”[^tpass-notice] | [Cards and passes](/ticketing/guides/cards-passes-and-fares/) |
| 轉乘優惠 | Transfer discount. TRTC uses the term for a two-way MRT–bus discount with a time window and explicit exclusions; it does not mean that every pair of modes has an integrated fare.[^trtc-transfer] | [Fare history](/ticketing/guides/fare-history/) |
| 固定閉塞 | Fixed block. The signalling source describes it as a train-separation arrangement using fixed track sections; it is distinct from the line's supplier and from automatic train operation.[^dorts-signal] | [Metro operations](/rail/technology/metro-operations/) |
| 移動閉塞 | Moving block. Wenhu's CBTC is described as a continuously updated train-control arrangement; “moving block” is a signalling/control method, not a claim that the train has no physical block sections anywhere.[^dorts-cbtc] | [Metro operations](/rail/technology/metro-operations/) |
| 第三軌 | Third rail. It is the traction-current collection method documented for the Taipei Metro power system; the upstream 22 kV supply and substations are separate layers.[^dorts-power] | [Metro operations](/rail/technology/metro-operations/) |
| 聯合開發 | Joint development. DORTS uses the term for development combining rail facilities with private or public building rights; the guide keeps its land, value-allocation and completion records separate from generic station-area development.[^dorts-framework] | [TOD and joint development](/rail/projects/tod-joint-development/) |

## What the abbreviations do not settle

`區`, `副`, `繞`, `延` and `預` are useful reading keys, not a substitute for
the current route page. A current portal record can show the label while
leaving the creation date, predecessor, operator change or exact reason for a
variant unstated; this guide marks those historical fields TBC rather than
turning a plausible English gloss into a sourced history.[^tdx-api]
