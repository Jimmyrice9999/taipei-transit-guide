TPASS has two layers that are easy to confuse. **TPASS 1.0** is a prepaid
regional pass: the current government explainer describes it as unlimited
public-transport travel during the pass's validity period.[^egov-tpass-2026]
**TPASS 2.0** is a registered frequent-rider rebate: ride first, then receive a
monthly rebate when the recorded trips meet the applicable conditions.[^egov-tpass-2026]

## The dated 1.0 scheme table

The government summary updated on 25 August 2026 publishes ten regional or
local rows: 基北北桃, 北宜, 桃竹竹苗, 中彰投苗, 雲林, 嘉嘉南, 南高屏, 花蓮,
臺東 and 澎湖.[^egov-tpass-2026] Examples in that table include the NT$1,200
基北北桃 pass, NT$2,300 北宜 cross-city-and-Taipei pass, NT$999 嘉嘉南 and
南高屏 passes, NT$299 臺東 city pass, and the NT$150 bus / NT$400 bus-and-ship
澎湖 options.[^egov-tpass-2026]

Those are dated values in a government summary, not a promise that every local
issuer's price, card artwork, purchase channel or refund rule is unchanged.[^egov-tpass-2026]
The complete all-jurisdiction fare matrix remains TBC until each linked scheme
page is fetched and checked.[^egov-tpass-2026]

The Highway Bureau says riders may use an existing eligible electronic ticket
to buy a commuter pass. The specially designed TPASS card is therefore not a
universal prerequisite for TPASS 1.0.[^thb-tpass-faq-current]

## The national 2.0 rebate layer

The current accounting window runs from the first day to the last day of each
month.[^egov-tpass-2026] Counting begins on the day after registration, each physical card or
electronic-payment account is treated separately, only the actual discounted
transaction amount is counted, and TPASS pass rides themselves do not count
towards the rebate condition.[^egov-tpass-2026] A complete ride or purchase
record is required, and the current explainer states a monthly cap of NT$2,500
per card.[^egov-tpass-2026]

The Highway Bureau's 24 November 2025 release describes the ordinary road-
transport threshold as 11 rides per month for up to 30% back.[^thb-tpass-plus] It also records
additional 2% treatment for the then-covered TRA, Taipei Metro and New Taipei
Metro rail records.[^thb-tpass-plus] This is a dated policy description; the
site does not infer a complete current percentage table from the explainer's
image or from the 91-route policy release.[^egov-tpass-2026]

## The long-distance coach extension

TPASS 2.0+ began on 1 December 2025 for 91 medium- and long-distance highway-
coach routes: the Highway Bureau defines the western qualifying routes as over
70 km and includes eastern highway-coach routes.[^thb-tpass-plus] Two qualifying
rides in a month receive 15% back; four receive 30%.[^thb-tpass-plus] This is a
different threshold from the ordinary 11-ride public-road-transport rule, not a
new count of all coach records in the site's intercity snapshot.[^thb-tpass-plus]

The policy release also says EasyWallet, iPASS MONEY and icash pay can be
registered as electronic-payment accounts. The current government explainer
says electronic-payment rebates are deposited automatically on the 25th of the
following month.[^thb-tpass-plus][^egov-tpass-2026]

## Cards and purchase surfaces

EasyCard's current TPASS 2.0 page allows ordinary and virtual EasyCards to be
registered; it says one person can register up to five named cards and each card
once.[^easycard-tpass-20] iPASS documents a different set of card surfaces: its
current page lists a NT$100 TPASS card, registration and one per-person subsidy,
but separately records that older colour-circle-card subsidies ended on 31
December 2025 and that a green-circle TPASS 2.0 offer was limited to 40,000
cards.[^ipass-tpass-card]

iPASS's current app instructions, modified 1 September 2026, support purchasing
and refunding MeN Go / South Kaohsiung–Pingtung monthly passes in iPASS MONEY
1.11.0.[^ipass-app-tpass] The instruction is explicitly limited to named regional card designs
and requires MeNGo membership and card binding.[^ipass-app-tpass]

## What is still TBC

complete current ordinary-mode rebate percentage table and the complete 91-route
attachment are not presented as settled here.[^egov-tpass-2026][^thb-tpass-plus] Those require separate full-page
