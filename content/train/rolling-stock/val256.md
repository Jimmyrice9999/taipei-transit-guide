The **VAL256** is the original rolling stock type of the Taipei Metro's Muzha
Line, now the [Wenhu Line](/train/lines/wenhu-line/). It belongs to the French
VAL family of automated, rubber-tyred people movers developed by Matra, and the
number in the designation refers to the car body width of 2.56 metres — the
wider of the two standard VAL profiles.

## Design

VAL vehicles run on rubber tyres over concrete running surfaces, with
horizontal guide wheels bearing on a central guideway. The arrangement allows
steeper gradients and tighter curves than conventional steel-wheel metro, which
suited the hilly alignment through Wenshan District and the constrained
elevated corridor across the city.

Cars are built as permanently coupled married pairs; two pairs make a
four-car train in normal service.

## Fleet history

The fleet was ordered for the Muzha Line and entered service when the line
opened on 28 March 1996.

Ahead of the 2009 Neihu extension, the fleet was modified so that it could
operate over the extended route alongside the incoming
[Innovia APM 256 (C370)](/train/rolling-stock/innovia-apm-256-c370/) trains.
The most significant change was the replacement of the original Matra
signalling equipment with the Bombardier CITYFLO 650 system adopted for the
combined line.

*Delivery dates, unit numbering, exact fleet size and the scope of the
conversion programme are still to be confirmed.*
