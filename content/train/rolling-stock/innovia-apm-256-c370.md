The **Innovia APM 256**, procured under contract designation **C370**, is the
second rolling stock type on the Taipei Metro's
[Wenhu Line](/train/lines/wenhu-line/). It was ordered for the Neihu extension
and entered service when that section opened on 4 July 2009.

The type descends directly from the Matra VAL design: Bombardier acquired the
VAL product line and marketed the developed version as Innovia APM. The 256
suffix carries the same meaning as in [VAL256](/train/rolling-stock/val256/) —
a car body 2.56 metres wide — which is what allows the two fleets to share the
same guideway and platform screen doors.
