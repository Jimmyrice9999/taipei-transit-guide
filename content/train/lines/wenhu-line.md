The **Wenhu Line** (文湖線), also known as the Brown Line or Line 1, is a
medium-capacity, rubber-tyred, fully automated line of the Taipei Metro. It
runs predominantly on elevated structure across the eastern half of the city,
connecting the hillside district of Muzha in the south with the Neihu
technology corridor in the north-east.

It was the first metro line to open in Taipei, and remains the only line in the
network using rubber-tyred automated people mover technology rather than
conventional steel-wheel heavy metro.

## History

The line opened in two distinct stages, and the combined name *Wenhu* is a
contraction of **Wen**shan and Nei**hu**, the two districts at either end.

| Stage | Section | Opened |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Muzha Line | Taipei Zoo — Zhongshan Junior High School | 28 March 1996 |
| Neihu extension | Zhongshan Junior High School — Nangang Exhibition Center | 4 July 2009 |

When the Neihu extension opened, the two sections were operated as a single
through line and the combined route was renamed the Wenhu Line. The original
VAL256 fleet was retained and modified to work alongside the newly delivered
Innovia APM 256 trains.

## Route

The line is elevated for almost its entire length, with a short underground
section. From Taipei Zoo it climbs north through Wenshan District, crosses
central Taipei on an east-west alignment, then turns north-east across the
Keelung River into Neihu.
