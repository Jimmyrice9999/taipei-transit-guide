**Muzha Depot** (木柵機廠) is the original maintenance and stabling facility of
the Taipei Metro's [Wenhu Line](/train/lines/wenhu-line/), located in Wenshan
District at the southern end of the line near Taipei Zoo station. It opened
with the Muzha Line in 1996 and was the network's first depot.
