**Neihu Depot** (內湖機廠) is the second depot on the Taipei Metro's
[Wenhu Line](/train/lines/wenhu-line/), built in Neihu District as part of the
Neihu extension project and brought into use when the extension opened on
4 July 2009.

The depot was a contentious element of the extension scheme during planning,
and the siting debate is worth covering here once sources are gathered.
