Three of Taiwan's four main-island trunk airports outside Kaohsiung share a
research problem this page states rather than papers over: their own
operator sites (taoyuanairport.com.tw, tsa.gov.tw, tca.gov.tw) returned
either a bare page title with no body content, or pages with no runway,
history or traffic figures, to every fetch attempted for this research. The
runway lengths, opening dates and terminal histories that circulate for all
three in secondary and enthusiast sources are, as a result, not published
here.

## Taoyuan

Taoyuan International Airport is administratively distinct from every other
airport CAA administers.[^caa-airport-list] CAA's own airport-distribution
page lists every other station under this section as a directly-run CAA
aviation station, but names Taoyuan's operator separately as 桃園國際機場股份有限公司
(Taoyuan International Airport Corporation) — a dedicated state-owned
corporation.[^caa-airport-list] That corporate structure, stated on CAA's
own list, is the one fact this research could confirm about Taoyuan from a
primary source; runway dimensions and a widely-reported third-runway project
timeline remain TBC.

## Songshan

Taipei Songshan Airport serves the capital's close-in domestic and
regional-international traffic. CAA's own airport list marks it directly as
軍民合用 — joint military-civil use — the same classification it gives
Taichung.[^caa-airport-list] Beyond that classification, a direct fetch of
the station's own site returned only address, hours and a phone number, with
no runway, history or IATA/ICAO code content usable for this page.

## Taichung

Taichung International Airport is CAA's other main-island field explicitly
marked 軍民合用 on the regulator's own list.[^caa-airport-list] The commonly
cited opening date (March 2004) and 2016 rename to its current
"international" title were not confirmed against a primary tca.gov.tw page
during this research — the relevant pages either 404'd or returned no
extractable body text — and are not published here pending a working fetch.

## What the aggregate traffic figures show

CAA does publish a national, all-airport passenger trend, though not broken
out by individual airport in the table fetched for this page.[^caa-passenger-stats]
Total passengers collapsed from 18,997,544 in 2020 to 7,597,391 in 2021 as
international/cross-strait travel fell to almost nothing, then recovered
past the pre-pandemic baseline by 2023 (52,043,278) and reached 68,893,910
in 2025 — more than triple the 2021 trough and well above the 2020
figure.[^caa-passenger-stats] Domestic-passenger volumes moved far less over
the same period (10,112,586 in 2020 versus 10,564,398 in 2025), which is
consistent with the collapse and recovery being driven almost entirely by
international and cross-strait routes rather than the domestic network this
section otherwise describes.[^caa-passenger-stats]

## What remains TBC

Individual-airport passenger counts, runway dimensions for all three fields,
and Taoyuan's third-runway project status are all TBC, not unsearched: this
page's own research notes record each as searched specifically and failed
for the reason stated above, a JavaScript-rendered operator site or a
404'd/empty regulator page.
