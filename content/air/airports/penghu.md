Penghu Airport (澎湖機場), officially Penghu Aviation Station (澎湖航空站), is at Aimen Village in Huxi Township, Penghu County.[^penghu-introduction] Its own introduction says the station now governs Penghu, Qimei and Wang'an airports, following the 15 September 2023 consolidation of the former Magong station and the two auxiliary stations.[^penghu-introduction] This is an administrative relationship, not a claim that the three runways are one physical airport.

The main terminal is a substantial island-connectivity node rather than a small airstrip: the official page gives a 44,048 m² station area, a 3,000 by 45 m runway and a terminal designed for 4.4 million passengers per year.[^penghu-introduction] It also describes three air bridges, nine stands for aircraft up to and including A321, and passenger services including travel information and assistance for passengers with reduced mobility.[^penghu-introduction]

## A dated flight snapshot

The airport's live fixed-departure table was showing September 2026 validity windows when fetched on 6 September.[^penghu-fixed-schedule] Its rows identify airline, flight number, aircraft, destination, departure and arrival times, plus start and end dates.[^penghu-fixed-schedule] The displayed window included route families to Taipei, Taichung, Tainan, Chiayi, Kaohsiung and Kinmen, with examples including Mandarin Airlines to Taipei and UNI Air services to Kaohsiung, Tainan, Chiayi and Kinmen.[^penghu-fixed-schedule] This is deliberately a rolling snapshot: the Civil Aeronautics Administration's monthly timetable also lists September 2026 but warns that it is reference material and that the airline's announcement controls when times change.[^penghu-caa-timetable]

The page therefore does not publish a timeless number of daily flights or a permanent airline-by-airport matrix. Seasonal capacity, extra flights and cancellations belong to the dated source window; the current complete route-family inventory remains TBC pending a stable official export.[^penghu-fixed-schedule]

## Ground and maritime context

The airport's own bus page puts the public-bus stop beside the south-side terminal exit.[^penghu-ground] It links the Penghu County Government Public Vehicle and Ship Management Office's airport timetable and route map, and separately identifies the Taiwan Tourist Shuttle Penghu Airport Express, its timetable and its live vehicle map.[^penghu-ground] The page does not itself state a current fare or frequency, so those are not inferred from the links.

The airport also keeps a shipping-links page. It points readers to the county office's Nanhai Star and Nanhai Star 2 passenger vessels and to Penghu Port Building passenger-ship information.[^penghu-shipping-links] That makes the airport part of the island's wider air-and-sea reference network, but the official link page does not establish a direct airport-to-port bus, walking transfer or timed connection. This guide keeps that join TBC rather than converting a set of useful links into a false interchange.[^penghu-shipping-links]

The service desk is listed as open daily from 07:00 until the last flight, with a dedicated telephone number.[^penghu-flight-service] For trip planning, the dependable answer is therefore: use the airport's live flight table for the dated air leg, then follow the county and Taiwan Tourist Shuttle links for ground service details that can change independently.[^penghu-flight-service][^penghu-ground]
