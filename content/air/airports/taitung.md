Taitung Airport is the Civil Aeronautics Administration's mainland Taitung airport node at 95063, No. 1100 Minhang Road, Taitung City.[^tta-traffic] The airport's own introduction records its establishment on 1 October in ROC year 70, its assumption of responsibility for Lanyu and Green Island airports in July of ROC year 79, and its upgrade to a Class-B airport on 1 June in ROC year 90.[^tta-about] That administrative relationship matters for public-connectivity research: the Taitung airport page is about the mainland terminal, while Lanyu and Green Island are separate island fields reached by their own transport services.[^tta-about]

## Facilities and role

The official airport introduction gives a 2,438 m by 45 m runway, a 5,178 m² terminal, capacity for 345 passengers per hour and stated annual capacity of 860,000 passengers.[^tta-about] Those are facility specifications, not a current passenger forecast or a promise that every route listed in a schedule will operate. The airport also publishes a separate flight-status warning: weather and temporary scheduling can change operations, and the actual flight situation follows airline announcements.[^tta-live]

Taitung's importance is therefore best read as a network role. Daily Air's own service page identifies five fixed-wing island route families, including Taitung–Lanyu and Taitung–Green Island, alongside Kaohsiung–Qimei, Kaohsiung–Wangan and Penghu–Qimei.[^dailyair-flight-info] The operator identifies 19-seat and DHC6-400 aircraft for this island network.[^dailyair-flight-info] The airport's live page, fetched with a 6 September 2026 date, displayed Daily Air services to Lanyu and Green Island and UNI Air service to Taipei.[^tta-live] That display is retained as a dated snapshot rather than converted into a timeless list of daily departures.[^tta-live]

## Ground access

The airport's access page says passengers can reach the terminal from the city bus terminal by an airport-shuttle branch service.[^tta-traffic] It also explains that passengers arriving at the new Taitung Railway Station can continue by city bus to the bus terminal or take a taxi to the airport.[^tta-traffic] This is an official transfer description, but it is not evidence of a direct rail platform, a same-complex station or a named current bus frequency.[^tta-traffic] The direct airport–TRA interchange and current airport-bus frequency therefore remain TBC here rather than being inferred from the shared Taitung name.[^tta-traffic]

The airport's own page gives a useful operational rule for visitors: live flight status and reservations should be checked with the airlines on the day, because the airport's display is affected by weather and temporary scheduling.[^tta-live] This page consequently points readers toward dated airport and operator sources, and keeps fares, per-flight times and a stable bus timetable outside the claims supported by the sources fetched for this entry.

## Scope note

This is an airport node and route-family page, not an airline encyclopedia. It records the official physical and administrative context, the dated live evidence and the published transfer logic. It does not claim that a displayed flight is guaranteed, that Taitung Airport has a direct TRA interchange, or that Lanyu and Green Island use the same terminal.[^tta-about][^tta-traffic][^tta-live]
