Kaohsiung International Airport traces to a 1944 airfield, 小港飛行場, at the
site.[^kia-history] The Civil Aeronautics Administration took it over on 1
July 1965, forming 高雄航空站 (Kaohsiung Aviation Station), initially served by
China Airlines, Far Eastern Air Transport and Civil Air Transport.[^kia-history]
It was upgraded to international-station status in 1969.[^kia-history]
International passenger service began in October 1972, when China Airlines
opened a Kaohsiung-Hong Kong route — the station's own history page records
this as "開放國際客運業務，華航首航「高雄－香港」航線."[^kia-history]

A new international terminal opened on 11 January 1997.[^kia-history] The
Kaohsiung MRT's Red Line reached the airport in 2008, giving the station a
rail connection into the city.[^kia-history] CAA's own airport-distribution
page lists Kaohsiung International Airport among the 17 airports under its
administration.[^caa-airport-list]

The operator's own facility page states the runway as 3,150 by 60
metres.[^kia-runway]

CAA's own electronic Aeronautical Information Publication gives the
station's ICAO location indicator as RCKH.[^caa-eaip-rckh] CAA's own
international-flight lookup tool separately lists the airport under the
IATA code KHH, though that is the code in operational use on a CAA search
tool rather than a formal designation record the way the eAIP is for the
ICAO code — IATA codes are assigned by IATA, a private industry body, not
by CAA.[^caa-flight-lookup-khh]

## Passenger traffic, 2020-2025

CAA's own six-year trend chart gives total passenger counts for 109年-114年
(2020-2025): 1,891,762 in 2020, 836,594 in 2021, 1,238,674 in 2022,
4,225,403 in 2023, 5,865,413 in 2024 and 6,971,162 in
2025.[^caa-khh-6yr-trend] Traffic collapsed during 2020-2021 and then grew
sharply from 2022 onward, with international and cross-strait traffic
alone rising 1,821% year-on-year in 2023 (from a low base) as cross-strait
routes resumed.[^caa-khh-6yr-trend] For 2025, CAA's chart splits the total
as 5,739,332 international and cross-strait against 1,231,830
domestic.[^caa-khh-6yr-trend] Chinese Wikipedia's own traffic-statistics
table, which states it draws from CAA's annual statistical report, agrees
with CAA's chart on the 2025 total (6,971,162) but gives a different
domestic/international split — 1,223,601 domestic against 5,747,561
international — an 8,229-passenger discrepancy in both directions that
cancels out in the total.[^zhwiki-kia-traffic] Both name CAA as their
source; this page cannot identify which underlying CAA table each drew
from, so both splits are published rather than one being
picked.[^caa-khh-6yr-trend][^zhwiki-kia-traffic]

## A classification CAA states, not infers

CAA's own airport-distribution page marks several of the airports it
administers as 軍民合用 — joint military-civil use — including both Taipei
Songshan and Taichung.[^caa-airport-list] Kaohsiung International Airport is
not marked that way on the same list; CAA lists it simply as 高雄國際機場,
with no joint-use annotation attached.[^caa-airport-list] This page reports
that distinction as it appears on the primary source rather than assuming a
military role for Kaohsiung on the general reputation the airport has in
secondary coverage — CAA's own list is the more specific evidence, and it
draws the line differently than reputation might suggest.

## What remains TBC

A current domestic or international route network and a construction cost
for the 1997 terminal are both TBC. CAA's own by-airport passenger-statistics
page and the station's own performance-data, annual-report and flight-schedule
pages all exist, but a static primary page giving current scheduled routes
was not found — every one of those pages renders only via client-side
scripts that a page fetch cannot read. Search for the 1997
terminal's construction cost found only cost figures for an unrelated,
much later project — a new terminal costing NT$88.08 billion targeted for
completion in 2032 — which this page does not conflate with the 1997
building. Those figures, together with any post-2008 terminal or runway
work, remain TBC pending a further primary fetch.
