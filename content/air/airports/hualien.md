Hualien Airport is a Civil Aeronautics Administration airport in Jiali Village, Xincheng Township, Hualien County.[^hualien-home] The airport’s own introduction says it was established on 15 May 1962 as a Class-B station for domestic aviation.[^hualien-history]

The airport’s new terminal opened ceremonially on 18 March 2004 and began operating the following day.[^hualien-history] The site’s public role is therefore larger than a tourist-airport label: it is a dated east-coast domestic-aviation node whose current routes should be read as monthly snapshots, not permanent promises.[^hualien-history]

## Current route families — September 2026

The airport’s live Taipei timetable, with database time 6 September 2026, shows UNI Air flight 8977 Taipei→Hualien at 19:00–19:50 and 8978 Hualien→Taipei at 20:20–21:00 on Mondays, Fridays and Sundays.[^hualien-schedules-taipei]

The live Taichung timetable, with database time 5 September 2026, shows Mandarin Airlines 731/732 in both directions at 18:00–19:00 and 19:40–20:40 on Wednesdays, Fridays and Sundays.[^hualien-schedules-taichung] The live Kaohsiung timetable, with database time 5 September 2026, shows Mandarin Airlines 7931/7932 daily, at 18:50–19:55 and 20:30–21:35 respectively.[^hualien-schedules-kaohsiung]

These pages are the airport’s own monthly schedule surfaces, so the dated database times are part of the fact. They do not justify a timeless statement that every route runs every month, nor do they provide fares in the material used here.[^hualien-schedules-taipei][^hualien-schedules-taichung][^hualien-schedules-kaohsiung]

## Ground access and passenger services

The airport homepage links separately to railway information and bus information.[^hualien-home] That is evidence of the airport’s intended transport-information scope, not proof of a direct rail interchange; no airport–TRA walk connection is promoted here.[^hualien-home][^hualien-ground]

The airport’s external-transport page identifies Ubus 311A as an airport bus and records a temporary four-trip Thursday/Sunday enhancement running from 13 November 2025 through 28 March 2026.[^hualien-ground] Because that printed enhancement has expired, the current bus frequency is TBC rather than being carried forward as live service.[^hualien-ground]

The service desk page was updated 4 September 2026.[^hualien-services] It says the desk provides flight, transport and facility information, lost-and-found, wheelchair use, free bicycle rental, travel information and other passenger services.[^hualien-services] Its stated service hours are 07:00–21:00.[^hualien-services] The page says those hours are adjusted to flights.[^hualien-services]

## What this page does not claim

The page does not claim a current airport–TRA transfer, a route-wide fare, a current bus frequency after the dated 311A notice, or an airport code. Those are separate evidence tasks, not fields to fill from a nearby station name or a stale schedule.[^hualien-ground]
