Daily Air (德安航空) is one of the small-aircraft operators that makes Taiwan's offshore-island network legible as public passenger connectivity rather than as a generic airline destination list.[^dailyair-flight-info] Its own fixed-wing service page groups the network into eastern and western blocks and says it operates five island routes.[^dailyair-flight-info] The eastern block is Taitung–Lanyu and Taitung–Green Island; the western block is Kaohsiung–Qimei, Kaohsiung–Wangan and Penghu–Qimei.[^dailyair-flight-info]

The operator identifies 19-seat aircraft and DHC6-400 aircraft for these services.[^dailyair-flight-info] That is useful context for the route layer: these are short island links with a different operating scale from the ATR and jet services listed on the main-island carrier pages, not evidence that every route has the same aircraft or capacity on every departure.[^dailyair-flight-info]

## Current route-family snapshot

Daily Air's live flight-information page was dated 6 September 2026 when fetched.[^dailyair-live] It listed the same five families: Taitung ↔ Lanyu, Taitung ↔ Green Island, Kaohsiung ↔ Wangan, Kaohsiung ↔ Qimei and Penghu ↔ Qimei.[^dailyair-live] The date matters because an operator's live route-family page is a current snapshot, not a promise that weather, aircraft availability or seasonal operations will never change.[^dailyair-live]

The service page separately provides a seasonal frequency summary. For 1 April–31 October it prints 16 round-trip sectors / 8 daily flights for Taitung–Lanyu, 6 sectors / 3 daily flights for Taitung–Green Island, 2 sectors / 1 daily flight for Penghu–Qimei, 4 sectors / 2 daily flights for Kaohsiung–Qimei and 2 sectors / one Monday-and-Friday service for Kaohsiung–Wangan.[^dailyair-flight-info] For 1 November–31 March it repeats the other four rows but prints the Taitung–Lanyu row as 16 round-trip sectors / 6 daily flights.[^dailyair-flight-info]

That Lanyu winter row is preserved exactly rather than “corrected” by arithmetic. The source uses both a sector count and a daily-flight count without making their unit relationship clear, and sixteen round-trip sectors does not transparently reconcile with six daily flights.[^dailyair-flight-info] The guide therefore treats it as a source-level conflict or ambiguity. The current page does not support a derived annual total, a guaranteed daily timetable or a complete national air-network count.[^dailyair-flight-info][^dailyair-live]

## Stations, tickets and limits of the current record

Daily Air's station page gives daily 08:00–15:30 advance-ticket counter hours at Taitung, Lanyu, Green Island, Kaohsiung and Qimei.[^dailyair-presale] Wangan's counter is listed for Monday 08:00–09:30 and Friday 09:00–12:00.[^dailyair-presale] The operator also says that island-origin flights from Lanyu, Green Island, Qimei and Wangan back to Taiwan or between those islands can receive an island-area exemption from the 5% business tax, and that each domestic passenger receives 10 kg of free checked baggage.[^dailyair-presale]

Current route fares and per-flight times are TBC on this page. The linked fare and timetable pages could not be fetched to full-page, source-quotable strength during this research, so search-result text has not been promoted into a fare or schedule claim. That is a useful boundary: the five-family list is sourced, while a traveller should still verify the operator's booking surface before treating any fare or departure time as current.
