The generational split is the technical heart of the system: the two dock
families are incompatible, the newer one needs no mains connection, and the
intelligence moved from the dock to the bike. There is also a China-market
variant that reuses the older dock design — a genuine oddity worth a
paragraph when it can be sourced properly. Each generation will get the same
treatment the rolling stock pages give a fleet: what it is, what it replaced,
and what is still unverified.
