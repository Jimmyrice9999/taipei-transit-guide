Waits on a data pipeline, deliberately: dock locations churn too fast for
hand-maintained pages, and the whole point of this site's station data is
that it regenerates from source rather than rotting. When the pipeline
exists, this section gets density maps and per-district listings generated
the way the rail station pages are.
