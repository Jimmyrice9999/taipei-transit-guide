The vessel layer is deliberately smaller than the route layer. A ferry route
can have several vessels, seasonal substitutions and charter movements; a page
belongs here only when the Maritime and Port Bureau publishes an identifiable
passenger-ship record that can be joined to a scheduled service without guesswork.[^mpb-passenger-routes]
