This is a dated timetable roster, not a permanent vessel register.[^mpb-kinmen-xiamen-september][^mpb-kinmen-quanzhou-september] The September 2026 Kinmen–Xiamen attachment lists 馬可波羅1號, 金星6號, 迅安, 新東方, 新捷安, 金瑞龍, 新五緣, 新武夷, 新金祥龍 and 和平新星 in its date-specific sailing table.[^mpb-kinmen-xiamen-september]

The Kinmen–Quanzhou attachment identifies 八方輪 on its published service rows.[^mpb-kinmen-quanzhou-september] The two PDFs are linked from the Bureau's September notice, whose publication date is 17 August 2026 and whose last checked date is 1 September 2026.[^mpb-kinmen-september-index]

The Xiamen timetable prints twelve departure slots for each direction, from 08:30 through 17:30, and assigns the vessel name in the row for the relevant date.[^mpb-kinmen-xiamen-september] This is useful operational detail for September travel, but it is deliberately not rewritten as a fixed all-year frequency or fleet statement.[^mpb-kinmen-xiamen-september]

The same Xiamen attachment preserves booking contacts by terminal and operator. It names separate contacts for the operators associated with 馬可波羅1號, 新金祥龍, 金瑞龍, 新東方, 金星6號, 迅安, 新捷安, 和平新星, 新武夷 and 新五緣.[^mpb-kinmen-xiamen-september] Those contacts are included in the route-family snapshot rather than copied into this identity page as if they were permanent vessel ownership records.[^mpb-kinmen-xiamen-september]

The Quanzhou timetable is a different pattern: it prints Kinmen departures at 09:50 and 16:35, Quanzhou departures at 08:30 and 11:10, and service rows on the even-numbered dates from 2 through 30 September.[^mpb-kinmen-quanzhou-september] Its Kinmen booking note says passengers should buy at the terminal one hour before departure, and its operator contact is 五大洲（福建）外代 at 0595-86097861.[^mpb-kinmen-quanzhou-september]

The route-family page and the snapshot file are therefore separate canonical layers: the route page describes the durable Shuitou–Xiamen and Shuitou–Quanzhou service families, while this page records names actually printed in the September attachment.[^mpb-small-three-links][^mpb-kinmen-september-index] A vessel appearing in a monthly table may be substituted later, and the monthly PDF does not provide a complete registry of every vessel that could serve the route.[^mpb-kinmen-xiamen-september][^mpb-kinmen-quanzhou-september] The permanent active fleet total remains TBC.
