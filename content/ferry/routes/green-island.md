Green Island (綠島, also called Lyudao) is reached by public ferry from Fugang Fishing Port (富岡漁港) in Taitung City, landing at Nanliao Fishing Port (南寮漁港) on the island — a pairing the East Coast National Scenic Area Administration's own page states directly, adding that Fugang "has consistently served as the transfer station for Taitung's access to Green Island and Orchid Island."[^eastcoast-nsa-ferry] The crossing takes about 50 minutes, a figure repeated identically across every operator and government page read for this page.[^eastcoast-nsa-ferry][^eastcoast-nsa-green-island] No scheduled service connects Kenting's Houbihu port to Green Island directly; two operator ticketing sites that sell both Green Island and Orchid Island tickets keep the two routes structurally separate, and the only Houbihu-area link to Green Island found in any source read for this page is an occasional peak-season waypoint stop on the Orchid Island–Taitung return leg, not a standalone route.

## Operators — a genuine count conflict

The scenic-area administration's own contact page names four ferry brands serving Green Island: Kaixuan (凱旋), Green Island Star (綠島之星), Uranus (天王星) and Golden Star/Hengxing (金星、恆星).[^eastcoast-nsa-ferry] Several other sources — including two of the operators' own sites and a booking aggregator — describe only three companies as running the actual pooled, scheduled service: Kaixuan (registered as Farnlin Shipping, 帆利航運), Green Island Star (registered as Long Hong Shipping, 龍鴻航業) and Uranus (registered as Changjie Shipping, 長杰航運, whose new vessel Tianwangxing 21 began sailing 28 March 2025).[^tsang-jai] No operational detail for Golden Star/Hengxing was found in any source beyond a phone number on the administration's own contact list. Both counts are published here rather than one being picked.

The administration's page separately lists two different phone numbers for Uranus across its two hosted pages (089-281617 on the general ferry page, 089-281618 on the Green Island subsite), and assigns that same 089-281618 number to Golden Star/Hengxing on the subsite while the general page gives Golden Star/Hengxing a third number, 089-281477.[^eastcoast-nsa-ferry][^eastcoast-nsa-green-island] Neither discrepancy is resolved here.

## Fares and schedule

Green Island Star's own current fare table gives a one-way adult fare of NT$560 and a round-trip fare of NT$1,120, with half fares (children under 12, seniors 65+, disabled) at NT$280/NT$560 and an infant/insurance fare for under-3s at NT$100/NT$200; the page states the price includes NT$2,000,000 of passenger accident insurance.[^greenstar-fares] Farnlin's own fare page for its Kaixuan service states the identical structure independently, and Taitung County's own tourism page corroborates the same one-way figures — three sources, two of them competing operators, agreeing exactly.[^farnlin-fares][^taitung-tourism-island]

Green Island Star's own schedule page gives a base pattern of five sailings each direction daily — outbound 07:30, 09:30, 11:30, 13:30, 15:30; return 08:30, 10:30, 12:30, 14:30, 16:30 — adding that peak-season holidays may see an extra simultaneous sailing and that off-season sailings are "reduced accordingly."[^greenstar-fares] This conflicts with a frequency of "two fixed daily sailings" that recurs across several secondary summaries; the five-sailing figure comes from a fetched primary operator page and the two-sailing figure does not trace to one, but neither claim is discarded here.

A search-derived claim that Uranus raised its fares from 5 March 2026, becoming more expensive than the other two pooled operators (NT$670 one-way / NT$335 half fare, versus NT$560/NT$280 for Kaixuan and Green Island Star, with no round-trip discount), could not be verified: Uranus's own site states no fares at all in the page read for this entry.[^tsang-jai] This is recorded as an unconfirmed claim, not as an established current fare.

## Weather suspension: no published route-specific threshold found

The scenic-area administration's page says sailing times "often change due to weather or tidal conditions; travellers should telephone the ferry company before departure to check the situation," without stating a number.[^eastcoast-nsa-green-island] Farnlin's own fare page states the company "may, for safety reasons, temporarily suspend sailings" in bad weather or unavoidable circumstances, again without a figure.[^farnlin-fares] The Central Weather Administration publishes a general "Blue Highway" marine-safety forecast with numeric thresholds (winds of force 6 or waves of 3 m for a general caution, force 8 or 6 m for a stronger warning), but that page does not name Green Island and is not stated by any source read here to govern this specific route's actual suspension decisions.[^cwa-bluehighway] No operator- or route-specific published wind or wave threshold was found; this stays TBC rather than being inferred from the general forecast page.

## A second, seasonal port: Chenggong

A newer "Blue Highway" route links Chenggong Township's harbour (成功新港漁港), further up Taiwan's east coast, to Green Island, launched 4 September 2024 and running Wednesdays and Fridays only, about 90 minutes each way — corroborated independently by the scenic-area administration's own Green Island subsite alongside a dated trade-press report of the inaugural sailing.[^eastcoast-nsa-green-island] Its current 2026 operating status was not confirmed to primary-source-quote strength in the sources read for this page and stays TBC.

## On the island

Both the scenic-area administration and Taitung County's own tourism page describe scooter rental — increasingly electric — as the dominant way to get around Green Island, whose ring road is about 20 km.[^eastcoast-nsa-green-island][^taitung-tourism-island] A round-island public bus from Nanliao is reported by several secondary sources citing the Green Island Township Office, but the township's own site could not be reached in the research for this page (a repeated connection failure), so its fare and exact seasonal schedule — two mutually inconsistent versions circulate in secondary summaries — stay TBC pending a working fetch of that primary source.
