Orchid Island (蘭嶼, also called Lanyu, home to the indigenous Tao people) is reached from the same primary port as Green Island — Fugang Fishing Port (富岡漁港) in Taitung City — landing at Kaiyuan Harbour (開元漁港).[^eastcoast-nsa-ferry-lanyu] A second, seasonal port operates from Houbihu Yacht Harbour (後壁湖遊艇碼頭) in Kenting, Pingtung County, running roughly mid-March to mid-October only; a private tourism portal states the route is suspended the rest of the year because of northeast-monsoon winds and rough seas, a claim not independently confirmed against a government primary source for the exact date range, though the general winter-suspension fact is corroborated elsewhere.[^lanyu-info-blue-door] Three shipping companies are named for the Orchid Island route by the scenic-area administration's own operator list — Kaixuan (帆利航運/Farnlin), Green Island Star (龍鴻航業/Long Hong) and Golden Star (大發輪船/Dafa) — one fewer than the four named for the Green Island-only route.[^eastcoast-nsa-ferry-lanyu] Farnlin's own page for its combined Fugang-Green Island-Orchid Island-Houbihu route names its vessel Kaixuan No. 1, an aluminium catamaran built in Japan, rated for 261 passengers and 8 crew at a service speed of 32 knots.[^farnlin-lanyu-route]

## Crossing time: a genuine spread, not one number

Sources disagree substantially on the Fugang–Orchid Island crossing time, and this page publishes the spread rather than picking one. Taitung County's own tourism page states 120 minutes.[^taitung-tourism-island-lanyu] Dafa Steamship's own operator page states about 150 minutes for the same route.[^dafaship] The East Coast National Scenic Area Administration's page gives "about 2-3 hours."[^eastcoast-nsa-ferry-lanyu] A booking aggregator separately reports 150 minutes outbound and 200 minutes on the return leg when the return is routed via Green Island. For the Houbihu–Orchid Island leg, Taitung County's page gives "120-150 minutes," roughly consistent with a private portal's "about 2 hours."[^taitung-tourism-island-lanyu][^lanyu-info-blue-door] None of these figures is treated as more authoritative than another; the range itself is the finding.

## No standalone Green Island–Orchid Island route — a peak-season triangle instead

There is no year-round, dedicated point-to-point ferry between Green Island and Orchid Island. In peak season, some sailings extend the mainland routes into a "triangle" pattern instead — Fugang → Green Island → Orchid Island outbound, or Houbihu → Orchid Island → Green Island — a routing the scenic-area administration's own page attributes particularly to the Golden Star service.[^eastcoast-nsa-ferry-lanyu] Return legs from Orchid Island in peak season likewise sometimes call at Green Island first, adding roughly 30-40 minutes to the crossing, per the same source.[^eastcoast-nsa-ferry-lanyu] This triangle service reportedly cannot be booked online and requires a direct phone reservation with the operator; that specific booking-mechanics detail was not independently confirmed against a fetched primary page and stays at reduced confidence.

## Fares, and an unconfirmed resident subsidy

Dafa Steamship's own fare table gives a one-way full fare of NT$1,200 (ages 12-64), with a half fare of NT$600 (ages 3-11 and 65+, and a companion/disability fare), and an infant fare of NT$100; round-trip bookings are effectively double the one-way fare.[^dafaship] A booking aggregator independently lists the identical NT$1,200/NT$2,400 one-way/round-trip figures for all three named operators, and a PTS News report — sourced to Lanyu Township Office officials directly — separately confirms NT$1,200 as the standard full fare in the context of describing a resident subsidy.[^pts-lanyu-subsidy]

That subsidy mechanism itself rests only on the PTS report, which states that Orchid Island residents previously received a point-of-sale deduction (NT$450 off a full ticket, NT$225 off a half ticket) at the point of purchase, but that from March 2026 the process changed to "residents pay the full fare first, then apply for reimbursement afterward" within one month of returning to the island, with a further subsidy reportedly added by Taitung County government on top of the township's own.[^pts-lanyu-subsidy] The Lanyu Township Office's own website could not be reached to verify this directly (repeated connection failures during research), so the mechanism is published here as PTS's own reporting, not as independently confirmed.

## Schedule and weather suspension

Long Hong's own traffic page gives peak-season outbound sailings from Fugang at 07:00 and 12:30; a booking aggregator separately gives return times of 09:30 and 15:00 and a similar pattern for the Houbihu leg, though these return-time and Houbihu figures were not independently confirmed against a fetched primary page.[^longhong-traffic] The scenic-area administration's own page states only that schedules "are adjusted between peak and off-peak season" without giving off-season clock times.[^eastcoast-nsa-ferry-lanyu] Two incompatible claims about off-season Fugang frequency circulate in secondary sources — one describing a flat two-days-a-week pattern (itself an uncited sentence within zh.wikipedia's own article), another describing a government-subsidised fixed Monday/Wednesday/Friday-plus-alternate-Sunday pattern — and neither traces to a primary source read for this page; both stay TBC rather than being merged into one.

On suspension decisions: a Ministry of Transportation and Communications release on one specific episode states the Maritime and Port Bureau "will closely monitor sea and weather conditions and coordinate with operators to resume service as soon as it meets sailing-safety standards," and reported that a section of Orchid Island sailings had resumed the following day once conditions allowed — without stating a numeric wind or wave threshold for this route.[^motc-lanyu-resumption] No route-specific published threshold was found in any source read for this page, consistent with the same gap found on the Green Island route.

## The air alternative

Taitung County's own tourism page states that Daily Air (德安航空) operates Taitung Airport–Orchid Island Airport flights using a 19-seat DHC-6 aircraft, in roughly 25 minutes.[^taitung-tourism-island-lanyu] This is noted here only as context; fares, seat-booking rules and schedule are outside the scope of this ferry-focused page.
