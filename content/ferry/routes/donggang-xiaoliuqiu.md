Donggang–Xiaoliuqiu is a scheduled passenger-shipping family between Pingtung’s Donggang area and Xiaoliuqiu, rather than one undifferentiated boat service.[^motcmpb-ticketing] The Maritime and Port Bureau’s current ticket directory lists four operator entries for this family: Liuxing, Taifu, the Dongliu joint operation and Blue-White Shipping.[^motcmpb-ticketing]

The same directory separately lists Yancheng–Xiaoliuqiu with a different pair of operator entries. This guide keeps the two mainland departure families separate; a ticket directory is not evidence that every operator uses the same pier, fare or timetable.[^motcmpb-ticketing]

## Terminals and accessibility

The Bureau’s Liuxing vessel record identifies the route as Donggang–Xiaoliuqiu (Dafu Fishing Port). It names the Dafu Fishing Port waiting room on Xiaoliuqiu and the Dongliu Line shipping service centre in Donggang as both station and shore-connection facilities.[^motcmpb-liuxing] The same record marks that passenger vessel as providing accessible shore facilities; that is a vessel/route record, not a blanket guarantee for every sailing or operator.[^motcmpb-liuxing]

## A dated vessel record

One official record provides a useful example of the route’s regulated vessel layer: Liuxing’s English vessel name is LIU XIN.[^motcmpb-liuxing] Its official number is 016441, its certified capacity is 149 passengers and its gross tonnage is 178.[^motcmpb-liuxing] The record gives the build month as October 2021 and shows survey validity through 25 October 2026.[^motcmpb-liuxing]

## Fares and sailings

The official directory provides operator ticket links and contacts, but the material read for this entry does not expose one route-wide current fare or one common timetable for all four operators.[^motcmpb-ticketing] Those values remain TBC here rather than being copied from one operator and presented as the family-wide rule.

For a particular trip, check the named operator’s own booking surface and the Bureau’s current passenger-shipping directory before travelling. Weather, vessel assignment and operator-specific ticket rules are not flattened into a permanent schedule on this page.[^motcmpb-ticketing]
