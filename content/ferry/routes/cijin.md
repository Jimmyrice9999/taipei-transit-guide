Kaohsiung’s ferry network is a public-transport system serving Cijin as well as a visitor route. The city’s Transportation Bureau assigns the Kaohsiung City Shipping Co. the Gushan–Cijin, Fronten–Zhongzhou and KW2–Cijin transport-ferry routes, while also listing a separate Love River solar boat service.[^kcg-shipping-supervision]

## The Gushan–Cijin crossing

The main crossing links Gushan and Cijin from 06:00 to 24:00, with a published interval of about four to six minutes and a distance of approximately 0.5 nautical mile.[^cijin-transport] The operator describes the route as both Cijin residents’ public transport and a way for visitors to reach seafood, historic sites and old streets, so it should not be treated as a sightseeing-only boat.[^kcs-gushan-cijin]

The operator’s current route page says that, from 1 January 2021, the full cash fare and the cash motorcycle fare are each NT$30.[^kcs-gushan-cijin] Children under six or under 115 centimetres ride free, while the published concession categories include children aged 6–12, people aged 65 or over, disabled passengers and one necessary companion; concession passengers must show qualifying identification.[^kcs-gushan-cijin] iPASS, EasyCard, icash and Cijin Card are listed as accepted media on the route page.[^kcs-gushan-cijin]

The operator also publishes a holiday operating rule rather than an all-purpose motorcycle guarantee: on holidays of three days or more, fuel motorcycles are barred from boarding from 11:00 to 19:00, while Cijin residents, electric motorcycles, bicycles and official-duty travellers are exempt.[^kcs-gushan-cijin]

## The other Cijin routes

Fronten–Zhongzhou is the longer, less frequent public connection. The Cijin District Office lists service from 05:45 to 22:10, an interval of roughly 20–30 minutes and a distance of about 1.05 nautical miles.[^cijin-transport] The operator’s route page publishes the same NT$30 full and motorcycle cash fare and the same broad payment and concession rules, but its timetable is an image rather than machine-readable text.[^kcs-fronten-zhongzhou]

The KW2–Cijin sea bus is a separate, shorter-window service from the Pier-2 Art Center area to Cijin Ferry Pier. The operator lists 12:30–16:15 on weekdays and 12:00–18:45 on weekends and public holidays.[^kcs-kw2-cijin] A current Kaohsiung Port Land Development notice gives that route a full fare of NT$80 and a half fare of NT$40; the half category includes ages 6–12, age 65 and over, and disabled passengers plus one companion.[^kw2-cijin-fare] The published NT$30 Gushan crossing and NT$80 KW2 sea bus fare are therefore route-specific prices, not competing claims about one service.[^kcs-gushan-cijin][^kw2-cijin-fare]

## Piers and onward connections

Gushan Ferry Pier is at 1 Binhai 2nd Road. The operator lists bus routes 50, 219A, 219B, 248, 248 District and Orange 1B, alongside MRT Xiziwan, light-rail Hamasen and the No. 1 Canal Landscape Bridge public-bike station.[^kcs-gushan-pier] Cijin Ferry Pier is at 10 Coastal Road and is served by Red 9A, Red 9B, Red 9C, Red 9D and Red 9F buses.[^kcs-cijin-pier]

The city government describes the Cijin pier as having undergone a nine-month renewal focused on structural safety and the exterior, including six VEM viscoelastic seismic walls; the project frames the terminal as a coastal-travel gateway rather than publishing a new ferry timetable.[^kcg-cijin-terminal]

The Cijin District Office also lists local bus access to the ferry area, including routes 1, 31 and 50 and Red 9 services, with the Red 9 operating 06:30–22:20 in the information shown on that page.[^cijin-transport] These are access-planning records, not a replacement for the live bus timetable.[^cijin-transport]

## Electric ferries and the operating record

The operator reported in December 2024 that Kaohsiung and the Environment Ministry had built three electric ferries between 2022 and 2024: two 49-ton vessels, Xiongzhan 1 and Alice, and one 99-ton vessel, Xiongzhan 2.[^kcs-electric-ferries] Xiongzhan 1 had entered service on 24 January 2024, while Xiongzhan 2 and Alice were assigned to the Gushan–Cijin and KW2–Cijin routes respectively in that release.[^kcs-electric-ferries]

The 115-year budget is a forecast, not a measured ridership series.[^kcs-115-budget] It plans for nine operating ferries, three sightseeing vessels and 4,278,838 passenger trips for the year, while separating forecast passenger revenue for the Gushan, Fronten and sea-bus route families.[^kcs-115-budget] The published plan also includes NT$50 million for the cost of free rides for Cijin residents, which should not be read as a statement that every passenger rides free.[^kcs-115-budget]

## What remains date-sensitive

The KW2 notice says actual sailing is subject to on-site dispatch and that the operator may change the arrangement, so its hours are a dated service notice rather than a timeless promise.[^kw2-cijin-fare] The operator’s current Gushan page likewise says that matters not covered by the page follow the on-site announcement and that ticket counters do not provide change.[^kcs-gushan-cijin] The sources reviewed here do not provide an official station-by-station accessibility inventory, vessel-by-vessel capacity table, historical annual actuals or a machine-readable timetable for every route; those fields remain TBC.[^kcs-gushan-cijin][^kcs-fronten-zhongzhou][^kcs-115-budget]
