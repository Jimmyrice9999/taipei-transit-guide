Kinmen’s ferry picture has two different scales. Jin–Lie ferries connect the
main island’s Shuitou with Jiugong on Lieyu (Little Kinmen), while the
mini-three-links carry passengers from Shuitou to Xiamen’s Wutong and Quanzhou’s
Shijing.[^motc-mini-links][^kinmen-ferry-timetable] Treating them as one route
would hide both the domestic island connection and the separate border-crossing
booking process.

## Jin–Lie: Shuitou–Jiugong

The official county timetable PDF fetched for this research has ferry rows for
Jiugong departures and Shuitou departures. Its extracted timetable shows a
Jiugong sequence beginning at 06:30 and extending through 21:30, and a Shuitou
sequence beginning at 07:00 and extending through 22:00.[^kinmen-ferry-timetable]
The PDF also warns that each sailing’s motorcycle or bicycle carriage is
handled according to the day’s on-site situation, so the printed time columns
do not guarantee that a particular vehicle will be loaded.[^kinmen-ferry-timetable]
The source PDF does not expose an issue date in its extracted text; this page
therefore labels it as an official timetable record retrieved on 26 August
2026, not as a timeless promise of half-hourly service.[^kinmen-ferry-timetable]

The route’s operating history changed after the Kinmen Bridge opened. A full
official notice from the Public Vehicle and Ship Management Office, dated 22
February 2023, says that from 1 March 2023 the ferry was adjusted to morning
07:00–08:30 and evening 17:00–18:30 windows.[^jinlie-history] That announcement
is historical evidence of the post-bridge service policy, not a current
schedule; the later timetable PDF preserves a different, broader set of
departure columns.[^jinlie-history][^kinmen-ferry-timetable]

The current visitor fare is TBC in this guide. The county government’s full
vehicle-fare FAQ does not print a number; it sends the reader to a separate
vehicle-and-ship-office FAQ, and that linked endpoint was not safely fetchable
during this verification.[^kinmen-ferry-faq] This is an evidence boundary,
not a claim that the service is free or that no fare exists.

## Mini-three-links to Xiamen

The MOTC’s full cross-strait operations page identifies two Kinmen passenger
routes: Kinmen–Xiamen and Kinmen–Quanzhou.[^motc-cross-strait] It says that,
from 14 January 2025, the Kinmen–Xiamen route was increased to 12 round trips
per day and Kinmen–Quanzhou to two round trips per day, with later changes to
follow policy.[^motc-cross-strait] The same page lists named vessels and their
published capacities: the Xiamen-route entries range from 280 to 350
passengers, while the Quanzhou vessel 八方 is listed at 238 passengers.[^motc-cross-strait]

The August 2026 Xiamen PDF turns that operating summary into a dated roster.[^motc-august-xiamen]
Its heading exposes 08:30 through 17:30 departure columns and its date rows
assign vessels such as 金星6號, 馬可波羅1號, 新武夷, 新金祥龍, 迅安 and 和平新星
to the Kinmen–Wutong and Wutong–Kinmen services.[^motc-august-xiamen] The
26 August row is therefore useful for identifying the scheduled vessel
rotation, but it is not a substitute for the booking system if a sailing is
changed or sold out.[^motc-august-xiamen]

The August 2026 Quanzhou PDF is much smaller: it lists 八方輪 for every date
and four time columns, 09:50 and 16:35 in the Kinmen-to-Shijing direction and
08:30 and 11:10 in the return direction.[^motc-august-quanzhou] The MOTC’s
full sailing page gives the approximate crossing times as 30 minutes for
Kinmen–Wutong and 60 minutes for Kinmen–Shijing.[^motc-mini-links]

## Booking, service fees and documents

The Kinmen tourism notice updated 12 June 2026 links both the downloadable
mini-three-links timetable and the 金廈泉小三通售票網 and i海台 booking sites.[^kinmen-travel-mini-links]
That notice also records a passenger-service-fee change effective 1 May 2026:
NT$100 per trip for Kinmen-registered passengers and NT$160 for non-Kinmen
passengers.[^kinmen-travel-mini-links] It calls these amounts 旅客服務費,
or passenger service fees, rather than presenting them as the vessel ticket
fare; the two should not be silently added together or treated as competing
fare claims.[^kinmen-travel-mini-links]

The notice says a 金馬證 can establish the resident category directly, while
other passengers seeking the NT$100 rate must show a household-registration
document; passengers without acceptable proof are charged the NT$160 category.[^kinmen-travel-mini-links]
Travellers still need the documents required for cross-strait entry and exit,
and the notice specifically mentions passports or Mainland Travel Permits for
the People’s Republic of China.[^kinmen-travel-mini-links] The official August
schedule PDFs publish booking telephone numbers by vessel/operator, so a dated
booking check is preferable to copying a generic departure pattern.[^motc-august-xiamen][^motc-august-quanzhou]
