Matsu’s ferry network is an island system, not one route. The main ports are
Fuao on Nangan, Baisha and Qiazi on Beigan, Zhongzhu on Dongyin, Mengao on
Dongju and Qingfan on Xiju.[^matsu-island-transport] The official visitor
transport page says schedules change with season and weather: summer and
holidays can add sailings, while winter can reduce them.[^matsu-island-transport]
The county booking system is therefore the right place to check a date, while
the timetable below is a published operating pattern rather than a guarantee
of departure.[^matsu-booking][^matsu-island-transport]

## Nangan, Beigan and Daqiu

Nangan–Beigan is the short, high-frequency core crossing. The official page
gives an approximate 20-minute journey and lists adult, group, county-resident
and half fares of NT$160, NT$150, NT$110 and NT$80 respectively.[^matsu-island-transport]
Its published departures are 07:00, 07:30, 08:00, 08:30, 09:00, 10:00, 11:00,
11:30, 12:30, 13:30, 15:00, 16:00, 16:30, 17:10 and 17:30 from Nangan,
with a slightly different return pattern from Beigan.[^matsu-island-transport]
The page names Nanbei Marine and Hongyu Marine as the operators for this
crossing.[^matsu-island-transport]

Daqiu is a separate, seasonal excursion rather than a continuation of the
regular Beigan ferry. The Nangan service takes about 40 minutes, charges
NT$400 for an adult, NT$230 for a county resident and NT$200 for the stated
discount category, and returns through Beigan; the page lists one daily sailing
in April and September and two in May through August.[^matsu-island-transport][^matsu-fares]
The Beigan–Daqiu service takes about 10 minutes, lists an NT$300 adult fare and
NT$200 Beigan-resident fare, and publishes 08:30/14:30 outbound and
10:30/16:30 return departures, subject to seasonal adjustment.[^matsu-island-transport]
Both Daqiu services publish a minimum-passenger rule and wind restrictions.[^matsu-island-transport]

The date boundary matters in practice. Hongyu Marine’s full 25 August 2026
notice cancelled all Nangan–Daqiu sailings on 26 and 27 August because of poor
sea conditions during the typhoon’s outer circulation and said online-booked
passengers would receive automatic full refunds.[^matsu-daqiu-alert] That is
why this page records the alert as an event, not as a permanent route status.

## Juguang and Dongyin

Nangan–Juguang takes about 50 minutes and costs NT$250 for an adult, NT$125
for a half fare and NT$200 for a county-resident full fare.[^matsu-island-transport][^matsu-fares]
The official pattern has three daily round trips: Nangan departures at 07:00,
11:00 and 14:30.[^matsu-island-transport] The sequence changes by month: in
odd-numbered months the route goes Nangan–Xiju–Dongju–Nangan, while in
even-numbered months it goes Nangan–Dongju–Xiju–Nangan; the page gives
35–50-minute outer legs and a 15-minute Xiju–Dongju leg.[^matsu-island-transport]
The first and last Juguang sailings also have local ticket-counter deadlines,
so a published departure time is not the same thing as a late-arrival
guarantee.[^matsu-island-transport]

Xiju–Dongju is the inter-island shuttle. It takes about 10–15 minutes, costs
NT$80 for an adult and NT$40 for a half fare, and has four daily return
departures at 07:30, 11:00, 14:30 and 17:10 from Xiju, with returns ten minutes
later.[^matsu-island-transport][^matsu-fares] The official page warns that the
boat briefly calls at Dongju before returning, so passengers should arrive
early.[^matsu-island-transport]

Nangan–Dongyin is the long island connection: approximately two hours, NT$400
adult and NT$200 half fare, normally one or two sailings daily.[^matsu-island-transport][^matsu-fares]
On odd dates Taima Star leaves Nangan at 08:30 and New Taima leaves Dongyin at
06:30; on even dates the corresponding vessels and times are New Taima at
09:30 from Nangan and Taima Star at 07:30 from Dongyin.[^matsu-island-transport]
The official page says Donghai Pearl or South-North Star may substitute during
maintenance, with the actual departure set by a temporary notice.[^matsu-island-transport]

## Fuzhou and Keelung routes

MOTC gives approximate crossing times of 90 minutes for Nangan Fuao–Fuzhou
Langqi and 30 minutes for Beigan Baisha–Fuzhou Huangqi.[^motc-matsu-mini-links]
The county fare page lists NT$1,000 and NT$650 adult fares respectively and
says these mini-three-links figures include the ticket, insurance, terminal
service and cleaning fees.[^matsu-fares] The booking system says mini-three-links
tickets cannot be collected at a convenience store because passengers must
bring the original cross-border documents to the port counter.[^matsu-booking]

The overnight Taiwan connection runs from Keelung to Nangan and Dongyin, with
the order changing by date: the odd-date pattern reaches Nangan first, while
the even-date pattern reaches Dongyin first.[^matsu-ferry-access][^matsu-booking]
MOTC’s ticket directory points both Keelung–Matsu and Taipei–Matsu passengers to
the county booking portal and lists the operator contact numbers for those
routes.[^motc-ticketing]
The visitor page gives an 8–10-hour Keelung–Matsu journey and a roughly two-hour
Nangan–Dongyin leg; the booking system warns that a morning mini-three-links
connection requires the odd-date, Nangan-first sailing.[^matsu-ferry-access][^matsu-booking]
The official fare table separates the outbound and return directions: Keelung
to Matsu first class is NT$1,890, while Matsu to Keelung is NT$1,800, with
different resident and concession values beside them.[^matsu-fares] Those are
published directional values, not an arithmetic error to be averaged.

## Accessibility and live information

MOTC’s vessel records provide ship-level evidence rather than an island-wide
accessibility claim. Donghai Pearl is recorded at 204 passengers with three
wheelchair positions, one accessible toilet, staff assistance, a ramped shore
connection and a complete-accessibility label.[^matsu-east-sea-pearl]
South-North Star is recorded at 321 passengers with four wheelchair securing
positions, one accessible toilet, staff assistance and a ramped shore
connection.[^matsu-south-north-star] Taima Star is recorded at 580 passengers
with two wheelchair positions, two accessible toilets, staff assistance and a
complete-accessibility label; Ji Shun No. 10, used on the Baisha–Huangqi route,
is recorded at 149 passengers with two wheelchair positions, one accessible
toilet, staff assistance and a ramp.[^matsu-taima-star][^matsu-ji-shun]
The records do not establish that every sailing, pier or substitute vessel has
the same facilities.[^matsu-east-sea-pearl][^matsu-south-north-star][^matsu-taima-star][^matsu-ji-shun]

## The two current vessels, and the one they replaced

Taima Star was delivered to Keelung Port on 1 August 2015, with a
maiden-voyage ceremony on 12 August; it is a 4,982-ton vessel with 380
berths across five cabin classes plus 200 seats, giving the 580-passenger
capacity recorded in its MOTC vessel record above.[^taimastar-about] New
Taima was built at Miura Shipyard in Saeki, Japan — keel laid 24 March 2022,
named and launched 2 August 2022, delivered and sailed to Keelung 9 March
2023 — and held its official inaugural-voyage ceremony on 16 April 2023,
presided over by President Tsai Ing-wen; it is a 5,341-tonne vessel carrying
up to 642 passengers across 386 berths and 256
seats.[^newtaima-about] The vessel these two replaced, the original Taima
Ferry (台馬輪), made its final voyage on 27 April 2023 — departing Nangan at
11:00 and Dongyin at 15:00 — after 26 of its 37 years spent on this route; it
had itself begun as a 1985-built Japanese Seto-Inland-Sea ferry, bought
secondhand by Lienchiang County in 1997.[^ltn-taimalun-retirement]

## What remains date-sensitive

Weather, seasonal demand, maintenance substitutions and temporary notices can
change the published pattern.[^matsu-island-transport][^matsu-booking] Check
the county booking portal and operator notices immediately before travel; the
portal says other-route cancellations follow actual sea conditions and official
announcements, while the Taiwan route has its own operator notice timing.[^matsu-booking]
