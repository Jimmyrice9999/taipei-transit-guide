Kinmen Port Passenger Transport Center is the dated terminal node published by
Kinmen's official tourism portal for the Small Three Links.[^kinmen-port-center]
The page says that passengers travelling to Xiamen's Wutong Port or Quanzhou's
Shijing Port should report at this center, rather than treating the older
Shuitou wording in schedule PDFs as the complete current passenger-facing
instruction.[^kinmen-port-center]

## What the official notice gives

The full page is dated 14 January 2026 and says trial operation began on 3
February 2026.[^kinmen-port-center] It gives the address as No. 300, Jinfeng
Road, Jinshui Village, Jincheng Township, Kinmen County.[^kinmen-port-center]
That address is the stable location record used here; the page does not provide
a street-level map, walking route from a bus stop or a timed transfer to
Shuitou's domestic Jiugong ferry.[^kinmen-port-center]

The page describes passenger flow by floor: inbound processing on the first
floor and outbound processing on the third.[^kinmen-port-center] This is useful
for a cross-border passenger node because “Kinmen ferry terminal” is not one
undifferentiated place in the traveller's journey: arrival and departure are
published as separate levels.[^kinmen-port-center]

The facility is described as a four-storey steel-frame building, with space
approximately 3.5 times that of the old passenger service center.[^kinmen-port-center]
The notice says the future programme combines harbour operations, tourism and
leisure, and duty-free shops.[^kinmen-port-center] Those are official facility
and programme descriptions, not a claim that every listed commercial function
was open at the date of this page.

## Join boundaries

This is a verified terminal-to-route-family association because the official
page names both Small Three Links destinations and explicitly instructs
passenger reporting at the center.[^kinmen-port-center] It is not a confirmed
bus interchange, taxi stand, parking join or step-free route: none of those
details is supplied by the full page.[^kinmen-port-center]

The terminal is also not a substitute for the route page's dated schedules.
MOTC's route records separately carry the Xiamen and Quanzhou route families,
while the Kinmen county notice carries dated passenger-service-fee and booking
links.[^motc-mini-links][^kinmen-travel-mini-links] The site keeps those route, terminal and
booking layers separate so that a new terminal address does not accidentally
turn a dated vessel roster into a permanent timetable.

## What remains TBC

The official notice says a formal opening ceremony would be announced after
Lunar New Year but does not itself supply that later date.[^kinmen-port-center]
This page therefore leaves formal opening, current check-in deadlines,
security/immigration procedure, bus and taxi access, parking, luggage handling
and a terminal-specific accessibility inventory TBC. A later port-authority or
operator notice is needed before those are presented as current travel
instructions.
