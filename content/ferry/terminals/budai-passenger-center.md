The Maritime and Port Bureau identifies the Budai Port Passenger Service Center
as the passenger building for the Chiayi Budai–Penghu Magong route.[^motc-budai-center]
It gives the building's address as 334-1 Zhongshan Road in Budai Township,
Chiayi County, and records a floor area of 5,050 square metres.[^motc-budai-center]
This is the canonical terminal node for the guide's Budai–Magong route-family
references; it is not a claim that every operator uses an identical sailing
calendar.[^motc-budai-center]

The Bureau's facility list is unusually useful for a passenger reference. It
names a priority ticket counter, check-in and control facilities, a waiting
room, family waiting area, nursing room, free Wi-Fi, toilets, ATM, drinking
water, phone-charging space and an electronic sailing-information board. It
also identifies an accessible toilet among the published facilities.[^motc-budai-center]
Those are building-level facilities on the official case page, not a promise
that every vessel, boarding route or seasonal sailing has the same accessibility
provision.[^motc-budai-center]

A Taiwan International Ports Corporation notice dated 21 October 2025 describes
Budai as an important hub for passenger movement to Penghu and for daily goods.[^twport-budai-port]
It says the passenger service center was completed and that the port company was
reorganising passenger and cargo areas so vehicle and passenger movement could
be separated.[^twport-budai-port] The same notice describes a planned increase
of the passenger-service area from 0.5 to 2.5 hectares and a leasing programme
for passenger-area uses; the guide preserves that as a dated development
statement rather than converting it into a completed current layout.[^twport-budai-port]

The terminal page does not assert a confirmed TRA, intercity-bus or timed
transfer join. The official terminal page read for this entry describes the
building and its passenger facilities but does not provide a TDX stop ID, a
rail connection, a current land timetable or a universal sailing fare.[^motc-budai-center]
