Fugang Fishing Port (富岡漁港) is a transport node rather than a fourth ferry
route. Taitung County's official island-transport page calls it the transfer
point for travel between Taitung and Green Island and Orchid Island.[^taitung-island-transport]
That role makes the port worth a canonical page: two island route families use
the same mainland departure area, while the port's ground access is a separate
question from the ferry itself.

## The documented joins

The county page says passengers can start at Taitung Airport or the railway
station, take Taiwan Tourist Shuttle, Xingdong Bus or a taxi to Fugang, and
then board the island ferry.[^taitung-island-transport] This is a published
access relationship. It should not be read as a direct airport or railway
platform at the port, and this page does not promote a timed transfer because
the fetched county source supplies no such duration or complete timetable.

The East Coast National Scenic Area Administration identifies the two main
ferry endpoints explicitly: Fugang to Nanliao Fishing Port on Green Island is
printed as 50 minutes, and Fugang to Kaiyuan Fishing Port on Orchid Island as
120 minutes.[^eastcoast-ferry] Taitung County's page repeats those headline
figures and separately prints 120–150 minutes for the Houbihu–Orchid Island
route, which does not depart from Fugang.[^taitung-island-transport]

## Seasonal and operational caution

The ferry node is not a promise of a fixed departure pattern. The scenic-area
page says Green Island has multiple passenger ships each day and that sailing
times can change with weather or tide; its Orchid Island section says schedules
change between low and high seasons and advises passengers to contact the
operator before travelling.[^eastcoast-ferry] The county page gives the same
practical warning and links the Maritime and Port Bureau's fixed-schedule
information alongside operator contacts.[^taitung-island-transport]

The scenic-area page prints four Green Island operator/contact entries —
Kaixuan, Green Island Star, Uranus and Golden Star/Hengxing — while its Orchid
Island section lists Kaixuan, Green Island Star and Golden Star.[^eastcoast-ferry]
Those are published contact groups, not a claim that every company runs every
departure, or that the list is a current national vessel census. Route pages
retain the more detailed operator and fare conflicts for each island service.

## What remains open

The current sources read for this node do not provide a stable berth number,
facility inventory, accessibility statement, port fare table or airport-to-port
journey time. These fields remain TBC. They should be filled from a port
authority or dated operator timetable, not reconstructed from map distance or
from the fact that the airport, railway station and port all serve Taitung.[^eastcoast-ferry][^taitung-island-transport]
