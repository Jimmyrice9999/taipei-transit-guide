This page has two evidence layers: an operator improvement plan and current station-information records.[^tra-accessibility-plan][^tra-station-gushan-4380][^tra-station-yongle-7110][^tra-station-xike-0970][^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370] The first describes a programme and targets; the second describes what the operator lists for particular stations.[^tra-accessibility-plan][^tra-station-gushan-4380][^tra-station-yongle-7110][^tra-station-xike-0970][^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370]

## What the improvement plan establishes

The operator's two-page plan says that some stations were built long ago and that their accessibility facilities do not meet modern needs.[^tra-accessibility-plan] It says hardware improvements must be phased because of cost, existing environmental conditions and construction difficulty, while service staff require continuing training.[^tra-accessibility-plan]

The plan prioritises elevator construction at the 90 stations ranked highest by passenger entries and exits, alongside railway-construction projects.[^tra-accessibility-plan] It states a target of elevator construction at 184 stations by ROC 110, with planned coverage of about 98.5% of the operator's passengers.[^tra-accessibility-plan] Those are targets printed in the plan, not a current audit of which stations have working lifts.[^tra-accessibility-plan]

The plan describes a three-stage approach to step-free boarding.[^tra-accessibility-plan] The first stage raises platforms to 92–96 cm, the second uses new or modified rolling stock to remove vehicle steps, and the third raises platforms across the network to 115 cm.[^tra-accessibility-plan] The plan gives ROC 111 as the target year for completing that three-stage objective.[^tra-accessibility-plan] This page does not convert those historical targets into a present-day percentage or claim that every stage was completed on schedule.[^tra-accessibility-plan]

The service layer in the same plan includes staff training in accessibility service, etiquette, service technique and boarding-ramp operation.[^tra-accessibility-plan] It also describes a revised procedure covering reservation, arrival at the station, boarding and exit, with service techniques differentiated by disability type.[^tra-accessibility-plan] The plan describes telephone assistance for wheelchair travellers and a text-message service for deaf or hard-of-hearing travellers.[^tra-accessibility-plan] These statements describe the plan's service design; the plan is not used here as a current response-time or availability guarantee.[^tra-accessibility-plan]

## What current station records publish

The current station-information pages use an accessibility category and separate it from general facilities, ticketing services and other station services.[^tra-station-gushan-4380][^tra-station-yongle-7110][^tra-station-xike-0970][^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370] That structure is useful because a station page can state both the facility type and its location or count when the operator provides those details.[^tra-station-gushan-4380][^tra-station-xike-0970][^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370]

The Gushan record, station code 4380, lists a ramp at the Railway Street 20 Lane entrance, one entrance lift and two platform lifts.[^tra-station-gushan-4380] It also lists service bells at the ramp and lifts, a breastfeeding room, and escalators for entry and exit.[^tra-station-gushan-4380] Its general-facility section lists iTaiwan Wi-Fi, temporary charging, a delay-certificate machine, stairs and platforms.[^tra-station-gushan-4380]

The Yongle record, station code 7110, lists a ramp, a service bell and a platform lift under accessibility facilities.[^tra-station-yongle-7110] Its general-facility section lists iTaiwan Wi-Fi, temporary charging, stairs and platforms, while its ticketing section lists the multi-card electronic ticket system and both student and general TR-PASS products.[^tra-station-yongle-7110]

The Xike record, station code 0970, lists a ramp at the Xike North Station forecourt, a lift in the centre of the platform for changing platforms, and a service bell.[^tra-station-xike-0970] Its general-facility section lists iTaiwan Wi-Fi, a delay-certificate machine, stairs and platforms, and its other sections list multi-card ticketing and a book-crossing activity.[^tra-station-xike-0970]

The Kaohsiung Museum of Fine Arts record, station code 4370, lists a ramp at the Cuihua Road entrance, one lift at that entrance and one at each of platforms 1 and 2.[^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370] It also lists an accessible ticket window, service bells, a breastfeeding room, entry and exit escalators, and a two-platform island platform in the general-facility section.[^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370]

These records show why a station-specific facility list cannot be replaced by a single network-wide label.[^tra-station-gushan-4380][^tra-station-yongle-7110][^tra-station-xike-0970][^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370] The operator's records differ in the facility types displayed and in whether a location or count is supplied.[^tra-station-gushan-4380][^tra-station-yongle-7110][^tra-station-xike-0970][^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370]

## Boundary of this record

The sources used here do not provide a current, machine-readable completion register for every TRA station, a dated equipment-outage register, a continuous step-free path from every entrance to every platform, or a platform-to-vehicle gap measurement.[^tra-accessibility-plan][^tra-station-gushan-4380][^tra-station-yongle-7110][^tra-station-xike-0970][^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370] They also do not establish that a listed lift or ramp is operating at the exact time a passenger travels.[^tra-station-gushan-4380][^tra-station-yongle-7110][^tra-station-xike-0970][^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370]

Current network-wide accessibility coverage, station-by-station equipment status, tactile-way continuity, accessible toilet inventory, platform gap and live outage status remain TBC here.[^tra-accessibility-plan][^tra-station-gushan-4380][^tra-station-yongle-7110][^tra-station-xike-0970][^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370] A current operator export with facility identifiers, maintenance status and an accessible-route graph would settle those gaps.[^tra-accessibility-plan][^tra-station-gushan-4380][^tra-station-yongle-7110][^tra-station-xike-0970][^tra-station-kaohsiung-4370]
