This page keeps two operator publications separate: a dated network-performance report and a timetable-family index.[^tra-monthly-2026-06][^tra-timetable-2026-07] The figures describe the reporting network and period named by the source; they are not a current station ranking or a promise that every listed train stops at every station.[^tra-monthly-2026-06][^tra-timetable-2026-07]

## Passenger performance

The June 2026 report records 18,583,868 passenger journeys for the month and an average of 619,462 passenger journeys per day.[^tra-monthly-2026-06] It records 792,603,914 passenger-kilometres, NT$2,248,767,058 in passenger revenue and an average passenger journey of 42.7 km for the same month.[^tra-monthly-2026-06] Seat utilisation was 49.62%, and passenger-train punctuality was 93.00%.[^tra-monthly-2026-06]

The report's January–June 2026 cumulative row records 119,278,790 passenger journeys and 5,167,715,332 passenger-kilometres.[^tra-monthly-2026-06] Compared with the same period in 2025, the operator reports a 2.73% decrease in passenger journeys and a 6.32% decrease in passenger-kilometres.[^tra-monthly-2026-06] The report attributes the June year-on-year decreases in daily passenger journeys and passenger-kilometres to a fare adjustment, while recording heavy rain as the main month-on-month factor.[^tra-monthly-2026-06]

The same report's annual comparison row records 242,772,738 passenger journeys and 10,764,554,799 passenger-kilometres in 2025, compared with 237,070,271 passenger journeys and 10,791,380,719 passenger-kilometres in 2024.[^tra-monthly-2026-06] Those annual values are historical comparison points in a June 2026 report, not a live counter.[^tra-monthly-2026-06]

## What the timetable index publishes

The operator's timetable index was last updated on 13 June 2026 and says the listed timetable files take effect on 1 July 2026.[^tra-timetable-2026-07] Its reserved-express section lists both directions of the Western Main Line, Eastern Main Line and South Link Line.[^tra-timetable-2026-07] Its Western local section lists ten directional groups between Keelung, Hsinchu, Changhua, Chiayi, Kaohsiung and Fangliao, while its Eastern local section lists Yilan, North Link and Taitung groups.[^tra-timetable-2026-07] The branch section lists Pingxi/Shenao, Neiwan/Liu-jia, Jiji and Shalun groups.[^tra-timetable-2026-07]

The index links separate PDF and ODS files for those timetable families.[^tra-timetable-2026-07] A family label therefore establishes the operator's published service grouping and direction, but does not by itself establish a station's stopping pattern, a headway, or first and last train.[^tra-timetable-2026-07] Those details belong to the linked timetable files or to a dated timetable query and should not be reconstructed from the TDX station sequence.[^tra-timetable-2026-07]

## Boundary of this record

The operator's public statistical index separates monthly reports, annual reports and public statistical tables.[^tra-statistics-index] The opened June report contains network operating, financial, engineering, rolling-stock and staffing tables, but this pass did not establish a current station-by-station ridership table from it.[^tra-monthly-2026-06] The timetable index exposes downloadable route-family files, but this page does not transcribe every station departure, seasonal exception or service-day rule.[^tra-timetable-2026-07]

Current station ridership, line-level passenger allocation, a stable station-by-station service matrix, and first/last-train values remain TBC here.[^tra-monthly-2026-06][^tra-timetable-2026-07] A current station ridership dataset, the linked timetable files, and a documented rule for joining train stopping patterns to TDX station identities would settle those gaps.[^tra-monthly-2026-06][^tra-timetable-2026-07]
