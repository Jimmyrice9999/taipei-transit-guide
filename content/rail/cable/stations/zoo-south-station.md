Zoo South Station is the only one of the Maokong Gondola's four stations
that does not carry the name it opened under[^wiki-zoo-south]. It began
service on 4 July 2007 as 動物園內站 — "Zoo Interior Station" — and was not
renamed to its current 動物園南站 ("Zoo South Station") until **26 October
2015**[^wiki-zoo-south]. That rename is why the Control Yuan's own November
2009 corrective resolution on the line's T16 tower failure, which predates
the rename by six years, refers to this same station as 園內站 rather than
the name this page uses (see
[Maokong Gondola](/rail/cable/lines/maokong-gondola/)) — a naming gap
worth knowing before cross-referencing older documents against current
signage.

The station sits at 新光路二段30之2號, immediately beside the wetland
ecology pond on the south side of Taipei Zoo, and the operator's own
guidance treats it as a natural combined stop with the zoo itself: ride the
gondola from here after a zoo visit, or the reverse, with a shuttle bus
covering the walk between the station and the zoo's own
gates[^gondola-stations-zoo-south]. Structurally it is an elevated station
with two above-ground floors and side platforms[^wiki-zoo-south]. It is the
second of the line's four passenger stations, between Zoo Station below and
Zhinan Temple Station above[^gondola-system].
