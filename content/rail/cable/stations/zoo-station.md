Zoo Station is the Maokong Gondola's lower terminus, and the point most
riders reach on foot from the Wenhu Line rather than by any other
vehicle[^gondola-stations-zoo]. It sits at 新光路二段8號 in Wenshan
District[^gondola-stations-zoo] — a genuinely separate building from the
Wenhu Line's own Taipei Zoo MRT station at 新光路二段32號, roughly 350
metres away[^gondola-stations-zoo][^wiki-zoo-station-mrt]. The connection
between the two is what Chinese Wikipedia's own article on the MRT station
calls an out-of-station transfer (站外轉乘) — a walking link between two
separate fare-gated buildings, not a shared platform or an in-system
transfer the way an interchange station usually works on this
site[^wiki-zoo-station-mrt]. Multiple city bus routes also serve the
immediate area, per the operator's own access
information[^gondola-stations-zoo].

As the first of the line's four passenger stations, Zoo Station is where a
rider buys a ticket and boards for the climb toward Zhinan Temple and
Maokong[^gondola-system]. This site's separate research on the line's T16
tower failure and closure history is on the
[Maokong Gondola](/rail/cable/lines/maokong-gondola/) page, not repeated
here.
