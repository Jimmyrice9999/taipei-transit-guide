The red line runs the network's most varied ground: a riverside alignment
inherited from a demolished conventional railway in the north, the underground
core through the main station, and the newer Xinyi arm under the financial
district. Its northern half carries the system's scenic reputation; its
southern half carries its rush hour.

*This is a scope page.* The station list beside it is generated from the
government registry and is current; everything else about the line — its
construction history, the branch to Xinbeitou, its fleets and service
patterns — is still to be written, against primary sources, the way the
[Wenhu Line](/rail/lines/wenhu-line/) was. The [C301](/rail/rolling-stock/c301/)
fleet that opened it and [Beitou Depot](/rail/depots/beitou-depot/) that
maintains it have scope pages of their own.

*Not established here yet: opening dates by section, lengths, ridership, and
the relationship between the present line and the historic Tamsui railway.
None of that is asserted on this page.*
