The Circular Line is the network's orbital: it exists to connect the
radial lines to each other across New Taipei, so its value shows up in
transfer counts rather than terminus ridership. It is run by
[New Taipei Metro](/rail/operators/ntmc/), not Taipei's own operator — a
corporate boundary that matters for everything from ticketing promotions to
whose announcements this site cites for it.

*This is a scope page.* The station list is generated and current; the line's
construction, its driverless fleet, and the under-construction northern and
southern extensions are still to be written against the operator's and
builder's own records.

*Not established here yet: opening dates, lengths, fleet detail, extension
status. Nothing of that is asserted on this page.*
