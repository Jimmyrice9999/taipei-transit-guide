The orange line is the network's Y: one trunk south to Zhonghe, two arms
north-west to Luzhou and to Huilong, with trains alternating between them —
the reason the platform signs at its core stations matter more than on any
other line. Its western end passes [Xinzhuang Depot](/rail/depots/xinzhuang-depot/),
whose siting dispute is one of the network's genuinely important stories and
is flagged on that page rather than half-told here.
