The Ankeng light rail threads a valley of hillside housing into the network
at Shisizhang on the [Circular Line](/rail/lines/circular-line/), doing for
the Ankeng district what the [Danhai LRT](/rail/lines/danhai-lrt/) does for
the Tamsui new town: feeding the heavy network rather than reaching the core
itself. Run by [New Taipei Metro](/rail/operators/ntmc/).
