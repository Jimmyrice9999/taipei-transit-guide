The blue line is the network's east–west workhorse, the corridor under the
city's densest employment, and the line any capacity conversation starts
from. It is also where the network's most interesting procurement story sits:
part of its fleet was bought not by the city's builders but by a civil
contractor settling a contract obligation — the [C341](/rail/rolling-stock/c341/)
story, flagged on that fleet's page and still to be verified and written.

*This is a scope page.* The station list is generated and current. The line's
history, its [C321](/rail/rolling-stock/c321/) and C341 fleets, and
[Tucheng](/rail/depots/tucheng-depot/) and [Nangang](/rail/depots/nangang-depot/)
depots are all scope pages waiting on sources.

*Not established here yet: opening dates by section, lengths, ridership.
Nothing of that is asserted on this page.*
