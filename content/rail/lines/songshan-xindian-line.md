The green line crosses the city from the Keelung riverside at Songshan to the
hills at Xindian, and carries one of the network's two short branches — the
single-stop spur to Xiaobitan, worked by three-car trains off the
[C371](/rail/rolling-stock/c371/) fleet.

*This is a scope page.* The station list is generated and current; the line's
history, engineering and service pattern are still to be written. Its fleet
and [Xindian Depot](/rail/depots/xindian-depot/) — inside which the Xiaobitan
branch climbs from underground to an elevated platform — have scope pages of
their own.
