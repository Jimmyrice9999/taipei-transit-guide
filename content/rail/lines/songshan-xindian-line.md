The green line crosses the city from the Keelung riverside at Songshan to the
hills at Xindian, and carries one of the network's two short branches — the
single-stop spur to Xiaobitan, worked by three-car trains off the
[C371](/rail/rolling-stock/c371/) fleet.

![The green-signed main entrance to Xindian Station, at the foot of forested hills](/images/songshan-xindian-line/station-800.webp "Xindian Station, the line's southern terminus, at the foot of the hills. — Foxy1219, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons")
