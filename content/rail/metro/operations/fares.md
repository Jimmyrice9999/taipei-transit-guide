## A 10-band distance fare, and a discount that no longer exists

TRTC's official fare table sets ten distance bands running from NT$20 for
journeys up to 5km to NT$65 for journeys beyond 31km, rising in NT$5
increments roughly every three kilometres.[^trtc-fare-table] What may
surprise a rider used to other cities: **TRTC's own site states plainly
that an EasyCard-type stored-value card is charged the exact same fare as a
paper single-journey token** — "普通卡...搭乘捷運以單程票價全額扣款," no
discount at all.[^trtc-stored-value-page] A secondary account (unverified
against a primary source that could be independently confirmed — archive.org
access is blocked in this research environment) describes a flat 20%
EasyCard discount that existed until it was replaced by the current
percentage-tiered cashback scheme on 1 February 2020; if accurate, the
"EasyCard is cheaper" assumption was once true and stopped being true on
that date, rather than never having been true at all.

Seniors, disability-certificate holders (plus one companion), and children
aged 6–12 registered in Taipei all qualify for a concession single-journey
fare at 40% of the full price.[^trtc-single-journey-ticket]

## Passes, and a frequent-rider scheme built on cashback, not discount

TRTC sells four unlimited-ride passes: a Day Pass at NT$150 (active from
first use until service ends that day), and 24/48/72-hour passes at NT$180,
NT$280 and NT$380 respectively, each valid for its stated continuous
duration from first tap.[^trtc-passes] All four are confirmed usable on
both TRTC and NTMC's Circular Line; whether they also cover NTMC's three
light-rail lines (Danhai, Ankeng, Sanying) is not stated either way on the
page found.

Rather than a flat discount, TRTC's current frequent-rider program pays
cash back the following month, tiered by how many full-fare rides a card or
QR code makes in a calendar month: 5% back for 11–20 rides, 10% for 21–40,
and 15% for 41 or more — calculated on the prior month's total spend and
excluding social-welfare cards, digital student IDs, TPASS, and bicycle or
pet tickets.[^trtc-frequent-rider]

## A genuine conflict: how many social-welfare points, exactly

Senior (敬老卡) and disability (愛心卡) cardholders get a monthly allowance
of social-welfare points, usable across TRTC, NTMC's Circular Line and
light rail, Maokong Gondola, and joint bus operators, before falling back to
a 40% MRT discount or 50% bus discount once exhausted.[^trtc-welfare-points]
TRTC's own FAQ states
this allowance as **600 points a month**; NTMC's own FAQ, describing what
reads as the identical benefit for the identical population, states **480
points a month**.[^trtc-welfare-points][^ntmc-faq] Both are primary sources
directly contradicting each other on a figure that affects a specific,
sizeable population of riders. Neither is picked here.

## NTMC: its own bands, its own authority, its own rebate math

New Taipei Metro publishes its own distance-banded fare tables for each of
its four lines, all in the same NT$5-increment style as TRTC's: Circular
Line NT$20–40, Danhai LRT NT$20–30, Ankeng LRT NT$20–25, and Sanying Line
NT$20–35.[^ntmc-fare-tables] No NTMC page states outright that this follows
the same formula as TRTC's — the resemblance is this page's own
observation, not a quoted claim. NTMC's fares go through a separate
statutory process: New Taipei City's own Mass Transit System Fare Review
Committee reviews and the city government ratifies each rate, distinct from
Taipei's own process for TRTC.[^ntmc-faq] NTMC matches TRTC's 40% concession
rate, but its frequent-rider mechanic is structurally different — a flat
rebate of NT$50 for every NT$200 spent, rather than a percentage
tier.[^ntmc-faq]

## Transfers

TRTC's current transfer-discount scheme applies to QR-code and
mobile-payment instruments specifically — 悠遊付, iPASS MONEY, icash Pay,
LINE Pay Money and TWQR — giving a bidirectional discount between bus and
MRT (or NTMC light rail) when the transfer completes within one hour with
no other mode in between, credited retroactively into the payment
provider's wallet the following month rather than deducted at the
gate.[^trtc-transfer-discount] Whether a plain physical EasyCard tap still
carries any transfer discount is not confirmed either way by TRTC's current
site. Separately, at the Circular Line's in-station interchanges (大坪林,
景安, 頭前庄) no tap-out is needed to continue a journey; at its two
out-of-station interchanges (板橋, 新埔) a rider must tap out and back in
within 20 minutes, and the fare is reconciled to the shortest whole-journey
path on final exit.[^trtc-circular-interchange]
