## TRTC: 2.1 million riders a day, most of the way back from COVID

TRTC's own FY2025 performance report puts average daily ridership at
2,101,700 — up 3.7% on FY2024's 2,026,843 — split into 2,276,587 on an
average weekday and 1,740,170 on an average holiday or
weekend.[^trtc-fy2025-performance] That figure is 97.2% of the pre-pandemic
FY2019 average of 2,163,285, a recovery TRTC states directly rather than
leaving a reader to infer it.[^trtc-fy2025-performance] Service quality
stayed within the operator's own statutory floors across all five lines:
punctuality (arrival within schedule) ranged from 99.69% to 99.93%, and
train-availability rates from 91.85% to 95.83%, both comfortably above the
99% and 90% floors respectively.[^trtc-fy2025-performance] System
reliability, measured in mean distance between failures (MKBF), reached
23.009 million car-kilometres in FY2024 — the best year on record — and
19.678 million in FY2025, the second-best.[^trtc-fy2025-performance] A
widely repeated shorthand claiming MKBF "exceeds 20 million car-km" holds
for FY2024 but not FY2025, which fell just short of that threshold — worth
stating precisely rather than rounding up.

TRTC's cumulative ridership has passed several billion-scale thresholds
since the system opened in 1996: 500 million by January 2001, 2 billion by
August 2005, 4 billion by April 2010, 5 billion by February 2012, 10 billion
by March 2019, 13 billion by November 2023, and 15 billion by 24 June
2026.[^trtc-chronicle] TRTC's own operational report, written earlier in
2026, separately states the system had "surpassed 14.5 billion" trips as of
that writing — consistent with, not contradicting, the chronicle's later
15-billion milestone a few months on.[^trtc-fy2025-performance][^trtc-chronicle]

## One number TRTC doesn't break down

TRTC's own statistics tables split ridership only two ways: the Wenhu Line
(the medium-capacity Innovia system) against the other four lines combined
as "高運量" — Tamsui-Xinyi, Songshan-Xindian, Zhonghe-Xinlu and
Bannan.[^trtc-annual-line-stats] FY2025 annual totals were 72,495,000 for
Wenhu and 694,624,000 for the combined four.[^trtc-annual-line-stats] No
source found gives Red, Green, Orange and Blue Line ridership separately —
a real limit on what this page can report, not an oversight in gathering
it.

## NTMC: full per-line figures, and a network back from an earthquake

Unlike TRTC, New Taipei Metro publishes true per-line ridership, though
through press releases rather than a single statistics page. FY2025's full
year brought the system total past 30.09 million trips — the Circular Line
carrying over 22.04 million, Danhai LRT over 6.01 million, and Ankeng LRT
2.03 million — up more than 40% on 2024's 21.35 million
total.[^ntmc-2025-yearend] The growth has a specific cause: the Circular
Line was disrupted by an earthquake in April 2024, and a December 2025
release marking one year since full restoration reports the network not
merely recovered but at record levels — Circular Line December 2025 average
daily ridership of 71,688, up 15.5% on its pre-earthquake baseline and over
22% year-on-year; Ankeng LRT at 7,006/day, up 32.7% on its own pre-earthquake
baseline; and Danhai LRT climbing from 14,000/day in January 2025 to
18,090/day in December, a 24% rise over the year.[^ntmc-dec2025-release]

NTMC's newest line, Sanying, began trial operation on 30 June 2026 and
carried more than 900,000 passengers in its first month, running fare-free
through 31 August 2026.[^ntmc-sanying-firstmonth]

## Remaining gaps

Per-line ridership for TRTC's four heavy lines individually (Red, Green,
Orange, Blue) is not published anywhere found for this page. Whether the
31 December 2025 24-hour figure of 3,043,167 system-wide riders is TRTC's
genuine all-time single-day record is not established — TRTC's own
chronicle states the figure without record-specific language. TRTC's
FY2025 annual total, calculated here by summing its own two-category table,
sits within rounding distance of a different figure attributed to a
city-government statistical yearbook, but that yearbook's own tables could
not be independently read to confirm the match precisely — not
established.[^trtc-annual-line-stats]
