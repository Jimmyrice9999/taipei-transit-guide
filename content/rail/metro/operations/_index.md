The [ridership record](/rail/metro/operations/ridership/) covers TRTC's
recovery from the pandemic and NTMC's per-line figures a year after an
earthquake shut the Circular Line down.

The [fare record](/rail/metro/operations/fares/) covers the distance-fare
bands, a common assumption about EasyCard pricing that TRTC's own site
contradicts, and a genuine conflict between TRTC and NTMC over how many
social-welfare points a senior or disability card carries each month.
