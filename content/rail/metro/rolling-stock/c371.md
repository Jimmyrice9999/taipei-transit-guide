## Overview

C371 is best understood as a five-contract procurement family rather than one
line's fleet. DORTS groups CK371, CL601, CE631, CH321A and TAA001 under the
same Kawasaki rolling-stock project.[^dorts-award] Its official quantities add
to 52 six-car trains and three three-car trains, or 321 cars in total: 120 for
Xinzhuang, 24 for Luzhou, 24 for the Nangang eastern extension, nine for
Xiaobitan and 144 bought directly for TRTC.[^dorts-quantity] That makes it the
largest high-capacity procurement family in DORTS's published fleet table.[^dorts-quantity]

## Procurement

DORTS awarded the project to Kawasaki Heavy Industries on 1 September 2003.[^dorts-award]
The agency's test record divides the design into ten subsystems: carbody,
propulsion, bogies, auxiliary power, braking, air conditioning, doors,
lighting, interiors and couplers.[^dorts-award] Contractors submitted detailed
design documents for approval before DORTS witnessed propulsion and
auxiliary-power verification in Japan.[^dorts-award]

The signed price remains TBC. A contemporary secondary report gives a budget
and winning bid, but no primary award notice containing those monetary values
was recovered in this search.

## Production in Taiwan

Domestic manufacture was an explicit industrial-cooperation objective, not a
later marketing description. DORTS says 162 cars were assigned to Taiwan
Rolling Stock for complete-build-up production to develop domestic rail-vehicle
capability.[^dorts-local-production] Its later production account narrows the
same quantity to 27 of the 33 six-car trains in the Xinzhuang/Luzhou and TRTC
portion of the order.[^dorts-production]

Taiwan Rolling Stock identifies cars 401–418 and 431–466 as its 162-car share
and dates completion to January 2010.[^trsc-profile] The manufacturer says its
work covered steel cutting, parts fabrication, six-panel and complete carbody
assembly, interior fitting, equipment and electrical installation, joining the
body to the bogies, quality tests and final completion.[^trsc-profile] DORTS
adds that Kawasaki transferred its manufacturing, quality and management
process and placed supervisors at the Taiwan plant while inspection documents
and assembly drawings were translated into Chinese.[^dorts-production]

The older claim that C371 was Taiwan's first domestically manufactured metro
train is too broad for the opened manufacturer record.[^trsc-profile] That
primary record establishes a narrower first: C371 was
Taiwan Rolling Stock's first industrial-cooperation production model and its
first complete-build-up programme.[^trsc-profile]

## Vehicle and passenger systems

The six-car formation is 4M2T, drawing 750 V DC from a third rail on standard
1,435 mm gauge.[^trsc-profile] Taiwan Rolling Stock specifies 80 km/h maximum
operating speed, 90 km/h design speed, 175 kW continuous output per traction
motor and a 301L stainless-steel body.[^trsc-profile] Each car is 23.5 m long
and 3.18 m wide; the published empty masses differ by car type at 40 tonnes for
DM1, 35 tonnes for T and 39 tonnes for M2.[^trsc-profile]

Compared with the preceding fleets, DORTS lists a colour touchscreen train
monitoring system, passenger-compartment CCTV and smoke detection, four side
LED route displays per car, electric passenger doors and IGBT propulsion.[^dorts-quantity]
The end cars added multi-purpose racks for bicycles and luggage, while the fire
response included low-smoke flame-resistant interior material, four
extinguishers per car and an exterior control able to open four doors on one
side during evacuation.[^dorts-quantity]

## Crashworthiness

The particular technical specification required analysis of a loaded six-car
train striking a stationary six-car train at 25 km/h, with the stationary set
tested at both empty and loaded weights.[^dorts-crash-scope] It required a
mechanical fuse to buckle below half the main structure's column strength and
to confine further deformation to cab or equipment-locker areas rather than
passenger space.[^dorts-crash-scope]

Kawasaki modelled the coupled train, fuse and carbody system and checked the
fuse behaviour with compression tests.[^dorts-crash-scope] The reported fuse
buckling strengths stayed below half the 6,769 kN carbody-column value, and
both collision cases remained within the specification's deformation limits.[^dorts-crash-result]

## Safety retrofits

After its investigation of the 2014 Bannan Line attack, the Control Yuan
recorded that C371 initially had two cameras per car and required driver camera
adjustment to cover the whole interior.[^control-yuan-safety] TRTC expanded the
fleet to four cameras per car, completing all 53.5 train-equivalents by the end
of May 2018.[^control-yuan-safety] Three-way emergency intercoms connecting
passengers, the driver and control centre were completed on the C341/C371
series-3 group in December 2018 and on the C321/C371 series-4/C381 group in May
2019.[^control-yuan-safety]

## Operations

The procurement purpose establishes the intended Xinzhuang, Luzhou, Nangang
east, Xiaobitan and wider TRTC requirements.[^dorts-award][^dorts-quantity]
DORTS also states that high-capacity fleets are designed for cross-line use,
so contract purpose is not a current allocation roster.[^dorts-quantity]
Current line-by-line and depot-by-depot assignments therefore remain TBC rather
than being reconstructed from enthusiast observations.
