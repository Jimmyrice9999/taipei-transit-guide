## Overview

The base C381 procurement comprised 23 Kawasaki six-car trains: 13 under CR381
for the Xinyi Line and ten under CG391 for the Songshan Line, 138 cars in
total.[^dorts-contract][^dorts-quantity] DORTS separately lists one six-car
CD311A purchase for the Dingpu extension.[^dorts-quantity] Taiwan Rolling
Stock's own C381 product record identifies that later six-car add-on as cars
547–548, which establishes a combined C381 fleet of 24 trains / 144 cars.[^trsc-profile]

This distinction matters: 23 describes the original CR381/CG391 subcontract;
24 includes the later add-on.[^dorts-contract][^trsc-profile] They are different procurement scopes, not
competing fleet totals.[^dorts-contract][^trsc-profile]

## Procurement and production

DORTS awarded the wider Xinyi/Songshan electromechanical system contract on 3
March 2007 to a joint team of Kawasaki Heavy Industries, Marubeni and CTCI.[^dorts-contract]
Within its five subcontracts, Kawasaki was responsible for manufacturing all 23
CR381/CG391 trains.[^dorts-contract] A separate DORTS test record identifies
Kawasaki specifically with carbody and bogie manufacture.[^dorts-bogie]

Taiwan Rolling Stock participated through the industrial-cooperation programme,
producing 96 cars numbered 515–546 and completing them in January 2012.[^trsc-profile]
It then produced the six add-on cars numbered 547–548, completed in August
2013.[^trsc-profile] The records opened here do not give the final-assembly
location for the other 42 cars in the base order, so that production split
remains TBC rather than being inferred from subtraction.

## Design and passenger systems

DORTS required the 13 Xinyi trains to operate across every existing
high-capacity route and to couple with C301, C321, C341 and C371 for rescue.[^dorts-innovation-start]
The exterior moved away from C371 with a rounded cab and windscreen, a lower
nose skirt and a strengthened-glass panel in the emergency door.[^dorts-innovation-start]
A folding seat opposite the wheelchair position could be raised to reach the
adjacent equipment cabinet.[^dorts-innovation-start]

Inside, single poles became three-branch uprights with circular or elliptical
handrails, and six LCD passenger-information displays per car could show route,
interchange and live operating information.[^dorts-innovation-detail] DORTS
also records linear-motor passenger doors, a wireless mode for portable test
equipment, LED cab lamps, T5 saloon lighting and recessed extinguishers.[^dorts-innovation-detail]
Four rubber-and-aluminium fall-protection elements guarded each inter-car gap;
most gangway linings became one-piece curved enclosures.[^dorts-innovation-detail]

The often-repeated claim that C381 shared about half its parts with C371 was not
found in the opened primary design, manufacturer or test records and remains
TBC.

## Vehicle specification

The six-car formation is 4M2T, supplied at 750 V DC by third rail on 1,435 mm
gauge.[^trsc-profile] Taiwan Rolling Stock publishes an 80 km/h operating
maximum, 90 km/h design speed, 175 kW continuous output per traction motor and
a 301L stainless-steel body.[^trsc-profile] The driving car is 23.71 m long,
while the intermediate types are 23.5 m; all are 3.18 m wide and 3.585 m high.[^trsc-profile]
Published empty masses are 40.1 tonnes for DM1, 34.8 tonnes for T and 39.2
tonnes for M2.[^trsc-profile]

## Testing, delivery and acceptance

The CR381 chronology begins design review in January 2008, followed during 2009
by floor-fire, bogie static and fatigue, carbody compression, propulsion,
auxiliary-inverter, electrical, mechanical and climate tests.[^dorts-chronology-a]
The prototype reached Beitou Depot in May 2010; trains two through six followed
from June to August.[^dorts-chronology-a] The seventh arrived in September 2011,
and trains eight through thirteen arrived between April and July 2012.[^dorts-chronology-b]

All 13 CR381 trains were handed to TRTC in October 2012.[^dorts-chronology-b]
DORTS records reliability and maintainability verification, project completion
and acceptance in August 2013, with warranty beginning the following day.[^dorts-chronology-b]
Equivalent primary delivery and acceptance dates for the ten CG391 trains were
not found in this search and remain TBC.

## Operations

CR381 and CG391 were procured for the Xinyi and Songshan extensions, while the
add-on was procured for Dingpu.[^dorts-quantity] Their interoperability
specification permits network-wide high-capacity operation, so those contract
purposes do not establish a current line or depot roster.[^dorts-innovation-start]
Current assignments remain TBC pending an operator asset allocation.
