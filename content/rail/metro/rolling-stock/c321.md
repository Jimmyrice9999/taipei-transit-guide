## Overview

The 321 type is a 216-car Siemens fleet procured as 36 six-car trains for the
original Xindian, Nangang and Zhonghe projects.[^dorts-fleet-profile] DORTS's
contract inventory divides that total among CH321 (11 trains), CN331 (17) and
CC361 (eight); those three official rows sum to the same 36 trains and 216
cars given in its engineering history.[^dorts-fleet-quantity][^dorts-fleet-profile]

DORTS described the fleet in 2013 as the principal type on the Xindian,
Nangang, Banqiao and Zhonghe lines.[^dorts-fleet-profile] That is a dated
historical allocation, not evidence that every C321 is assigned to a particular
depot or corridor today.

## Manufacture and design

Industrial-cooperation requirements gave the order a substantial local
component: China Steel assembled 108 cars from semi-knocked-down kits at
[Beitou Depot](/rail/metro/depots/beitou-depot/).[^dorts-carbody] DORTS's
carbody study says Taipei's high-capacity structures were designed for a
30-year life, supported by structural calculations, prototype load testing and
water-tightness tests before release.[^dorts-carbody]

The published fleet specification gives an 80 km/h maximum operating speed,
2,200-person train capacity, 1,435 mm gauge and 750 V DC third-rail supply.[^dorts-fleet-profile]
The original propulsion control used VVVF-GTO inverters; braking blended
regeneration with disc friction and added spring parking brakes.[^dorts-fleet-profile]
ATC incorporated ATO, ATP and ATS functions.[^dorts-fleet-profile]

The exterior deliberately stayed close to the 301 type, but DORTS identifies
squarer body corners and square rather than circular door indicator lights as
visible distinctions.[^dorts-fleet-profile] The fetched primary material does
not establish the old page's alleged body-shell strength failure, extra weight
or a side-impact deformation episode, so none is presented as fleet history.

## The 2003 derailment

On 15 September 2003, C321 cars 155 and 156 derailed, prompting DORTS to convene
domestic and overseas specialists and require Siemens to investigate and
remedy the failure.[^dorts-derailment-intro] The evidence did not initially
produce one agreed failure sequence. AIDC's examination inferred that a bolt
fractured first and then the gearbox torque arm's upper lug failed; Siemens
inferred the lug failed first and caused the bolt fracture.[^dorts-derailment-analysis]
DORTS judged Siemens's sequence more plausible from where components were
recovered, while recording both analyses.[^dorts-derailment-analysis]

The immediate protection used a temporary steel cable to restrain the gearbox,
and the full fleet's torque-arm retaining bolts had been replaced by 30 October
2003.[^dorts-derailment-analysis] A near-term reinforcement was fitted to all
36 trains between 5 December 2003 and 15 January 2004; another missing-bolt and
broken-lug event occurred on 10 February, which Siemens attributed to a fine
crack predating that work.[^dorts-derailment-analysis]

## Final remedy and design consequence

The final design enlarged the upper bush bar from 22 to 26 mm, added a 25 mm
reinforcement plate at the upper bracket and redesigned the emergency retaining
structure.[^dorts-derailment-remedy] DORTS reported that the first two changes
raised the calculated safety factor to 2.1 times the original and that the new
emergency structure reached 2.016 times the original.[^dorts-derailment-remedy]
The final work was completed across all 36 C321 trains.[^dorts-derailment-remedy]

The incident also changed later procurements. DORTS measured wheel–rail
vibration peaks of 30 g near Ximen, above the 20 g used in the earlier technical
specification, and rewrote vibration and shock requirements for equipment on
underframes, bogies and axles.[^dorts-derailment-remedy] The article explicitly
says that experience was to inform the Xinzhuang, Luzhou, Xinyi and Songshan
fleets.[^dorts-derailment-remedy]

## Renewal

TRTC renewed the ageing onboard communications and passenger-information
equipment after fault rates rose and parts became obsolete.[^audit-communications]
The operator recorded two C321 overhauls and continuing communications work in
2015.[^trtc-2015] The Audit Office found the contractor more than 30% behind at
one point; TRTC reported all 36 trains installed by 18 December 2015, 34 days
late, while the audit criticised ambiguity in how inspection days affected
liquidated damages.[^audit-communications]

A later programme addresses propulsion obsolescence across all 36 C321 and six
C341 trains.[^trtc-propulsion-reset] The 2020 project statement says Siemens had
begun announcing circuit-board discontinuations in 2016 and would only promise
repair support through 2025, creating a parts-supply risk.[^trtc-propulsion-reset]
It budgeted NT$2.77798 billion for the combined 42-train programme and originally
scheduled work from 2021 to 2026.[^trtc-propulsion-reset] The 2026 city budget
proposal extends the programme through 2027, so the original schedule is not
presented as a completion record.[^taipei-budget-2026]

## Source wording conflict

The Audit Office first describes the communications work as covering
36輛電聯車, literally 36 EMU cars, but TRTC's response on the same page says installation
was complete on 全部36列車, all 36 trains.[^audit-communications] DORTS's two
independent fleet records establish 36 six-car trains and 216 cars, but the audit's
two wordings remain published here as an internal conflict rather than silently
normalised.[^dorts-fleet-profile][^dorts-fleet-quantity]
