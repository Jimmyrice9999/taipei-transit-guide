## Overview

CP341 supplied six Siemens six-car trains, 36 cars in total, as additional
rolling stock for the Tucheng Line opening.[^dorts-profile][^dorts-quantity]
DORTS dates manufacture to 2003 and delivery from September through November
2004.[^dorts-profile] Its later rolling-stock manual says the six trains were
operating on the Bannan Line when that manual was published.[^dorts-interior]

## Procurement and design responsibility

DORTS calls CP341 its first rolling-stock package planned and designed
without assistance from the system's general consultant.[^dorts-profile] The
agency says it used that responsibility to specify products more advanced than
the C321 fleet for operational needs, stability and service quality.[^dorts-profile]

An official DORTS statistical report places CP341 in the Banqiao electrical-works
table, describes it as the electric-train detailed-design/system-engineering
package, and names Continental Engineering Corporation as the contractor.[^dorts-contract-record]
That confirms Continental's role in the CP341 project record, but it does not by
itself establish that Continental was the legal purchaser.[^dorts-contract-record]

An official Taipei government data record also logs CP341's first product
inspection for the carbody, bogie, brake, door and coupler subsystems, with a
5–18 March 2003 inspection trip to Austria, Britain and the United States.[^cp341-product-inspection]
The public metadata does not expose the acceptance report or supplier decision.

The claimed civil-contractor purchase, proposed Hyundai Rotem builder, DORTS
rejection and 1.5-times-C321 price therefore remain TBC. A signed CD550/CP341
subcontract, formal rejection or contemporaneous primary procurement record would
be needed to publish that sequence.

## Engineering changes

C341 kept the C321 exterior form but introduced a bellows-style gangway cover,
larger characters on the front LED destination display and, from December 2006,
side destination and other display equipment.[^dorts-profile] Inside, LED
next-stop displays appeared above the doors and announcements changed to link
the terminus with the following stop.[^dorts-profile]

The electrical changes were more structural. Both propulsion and static
inverters used IGBT control; a revised motor and gear coupling aimed to reduce
noise and improve reliability, and self-cooled braking resistors simplified
maintenance.[^dorts-profile] DORTS also lists revised collector arms and shoe
fasteners, longer-lived gangway rubber and T8 fluorescent lighting.[^dorts-profile]

For the first time in Taipei's high-capacity fleets, propulsion, doors and
brakes were synchronised over a serial data communications network rather than
traditional hard wiring.[^dorts-profile] DORTS presents that change as a way to
reduce weight, operating cost and energy use.[^dorts-profile]

## Torque-arm modification

C341 inherited the safety response to the C321 derailment investigation. The
final gearbox torque-arm design enlarged the upper bush bar, added a 25 mm
reinforcement plate and redesigned the emergency retaining structure.[^dorts-remedy]
DORTS records all six C341 trains as having received the final modification by
3 October 2005, before the same remedy was completed across C321.[^dorts-remedy]

## Operations and renewal

DORTS's interior-design manual groups the six C341 trains with the 36 C321
trains on the Bannan Line.[^dorts-interior] The same manual describes the shared
high-capacity interior principles as fire safety, lower weight and human-centred
comfort, with NFPA 130 material requirements and a 45-minute floor fire test.[^dorts-interior]

A later procurement dispute shows the original Siemens equipment continuing to
matter in maintenance. In 2010, Taipei's procurement appeal committee approved
a mediated settlement between Siemens and TRTC concerning C341 propulsion,
auxiliary-power and 19 procurement items; the public minutes disclose neither
the underlying claim nor the settlement terms.[^procurement-mediation]

The fleet is included in the same propulsion-obsolescence programme as C321.[^reset-plan]
The original plan covered all six C341 and 36 C321 trains, cited discontinued
Siemens circuit boards and limited repair support, and budgeted NT$2.77798
billion for the combined programme.[^reset-plan] Its original dates are a plan,
not evidence of final acceptance; contractor, replacement supplier and actual
completion remain TBC.
