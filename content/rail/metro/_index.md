The 捷運 network as this site files it: TRTC's lines, New Taipei Metro's
Circular, Sanying and light rail lines, and Taoyuan Metro's Airport MRT. Three
companies, and from a rider's point of view one railway — one stored-value
card, one interchange network, one station-numbering scheme, one map.

That is the boundary this system draws, and it is the boundary the fare system
draws. It is not drawn by technology: the network contains rubber-tyred metro,
steel-wheel metro and street-running light rail, and none of those is a reason
to file a line somewhere a rider would not look for it. Taiwan Railway and
high speed rail are outside it because they are separate networks with separate
ticketing, separate stations and separate operators — not because they are a
different kind of train.
