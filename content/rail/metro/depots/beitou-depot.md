By the accounts this page exists to verify, the whole Taipei Metro began
here: the depot south of Fuxinggang was the project's first construction
site, and it remains the largest and highest-graded maintenance facility on
the network — the place a new fleet is typically delivered, tested and
commissioned before it carries anyone. It serves the
[Tamsui–Xinyi Line](/rail/metro/lines/tamsui-xinyi-line/) and its branch, and is
home to the [C301](/rail/metro/rolling-stock/c301/) fleet.

*This is a scope page.* The first-construction claim, the depot's grade and
size superlatives, and its commissioning role are **still to be verified
against primary sources** — DORTS construction records exist for exactly
this.

*Nothing numerical is asserted on this page.*
