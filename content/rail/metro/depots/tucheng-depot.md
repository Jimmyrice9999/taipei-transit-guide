## Overview

Tucheng Depot, 土城機廠, is the Bannan Line's main depot, on the east side of
Huanhe West Road and Baijiebao Road in Tucheng District.[^dorts-planning]
DORTS's detailed route account places it on former Dahan River flood-control
land, with Xinxing Bridge to the north, the newer Tucheng levee to the west and
the industrial district and river authority to the east.[^dorts-line]

The site works with Nangang Depot to provide the maintenance needed for the
whole Bannan Line.[^dorts-line] TRTC currently groups both workshops under
Vehicle Plant Two, the unit responsible for Bannan rolling-stock
maintenance.[^trtc-duties] Neither source publishes a current model-by-model
stabling roster, so depot fleet allocation remains TBC.

## Planning and construction

DORTS says planning began in 1990.[^dorts-line] Taipei's metro construction supervision
meeting approved the scheme on 14 December 1990, a provincial–city coordination
meeting followed on 9 January 1991, and the proposal went through the Ministry
of Transportation and Communications on 25 March 1991.[^dorts-line] The
Executive Yuan approved the construction plan on 18 October 1993.[^dorts-line]

The project used two section contracts. DORTS defines CD550 as covering civil
and building-services work from `BL2` to Tucheng Depot and line-wide track and
electromechanical systems—including the depot—as well as rolling stock.[^dorts-contract]
CD551 covered a different station section.[^dorts-contract] The route page says
CD551 was the first main works contract signed, on 29 April 1999, but does not
identify that as the depot's start.[^dorts-line] Exact CD550 award, depot start,
handover and commissioning dates remain TBC.

The Tucheng Line from Xinpu to Yongning opened on 31 May 2006 after preliminary
inspection beginning 21 April and final inspection beginning 20 May.[^dorts-line]
Those are line milestones and are not relabelled as depot acceptance dates.

## Site and functions

DORTS consistently gives an area of approximately 26.8 hectares.[^dorts-line][^dorts-capacity][^dorts-equipment-table]
The route description identifies Tucheng as one of the principal sites for
stabling, washing and maintaining trains used across the Banqiao, Tucheng and
Nangang sections.[^dorts-line] The planning inventory classifies it as a
level-four steel-wheel depot.[^dorts-capacity]

## Capacity and equipment

Capacity differs by table heading. The network-planning inventory publishes
storage space for 34 trainsets, while the equipment table gives 36 stabling-road
positions.[^dorts-capacity][^dorts-equipment-table] No opened source converts
between those measures or explains the two-position difference, so both remain
labelled rather than collapsed into one capacity.

The equipment table records no test track.[^dorts-equipment-table] DORTS's
procurement list identifies CD319A as a package of twelve categories of main
maintenance equipment.[^dorts-equipment-list] That establishes original
equipment scope, not a current inventory; the active machinery and division of
heavy work between Tucheng and Nangang remain TBC.
