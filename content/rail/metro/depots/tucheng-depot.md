Tucheng Depot maintains the western end of the
[Bannan Line](/rail/metro/lines/bannan-line/) and shares its fleets — the
[C321](/rail/metro/rolling-stock/c321/) and the oddly procured
[C341](/rail/metro/rolling-stock/c341/) — with [Nangang Depot](/rail/metro/depots/nangang-depot/)
at the line's other end.

*This is a scope page.* Site, functions, grade and allocation are **still to
be verified against primary sources**. Nothing numerical is asserted on this
page.
