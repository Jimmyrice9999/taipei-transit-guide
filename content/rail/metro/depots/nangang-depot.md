## Overview

Nangang Depot, 南港機廠, is the Bannan Line's secondary depot, south of
Zhongxiao East Road Section 7 in Nangang District.[^dorts-planning] The detailed
route page places the narrow site east of Xiangyang Road and Dongxin Street and
north of Muzhi Mountain.[^dorts-line]

DORTS lists train storage, inspection and washing as its principal
functions.[^dorts-line] TRTC now places management of both Nangang and Tucheng
workshops in Vehicle Plant Two, the unit responsible for Bannan rolling-stock
maintenance.[^trtc-duties] That establishes present organisational
responsibility, but not which train model is stored on each site; current fleet
allocation remains TBC.

## Area and capacity

Three official area values differ only in precision: approximately 7.8 hectares
in the planning inventory, 7.83 on the detailed route page and 7.9 in the depot-
equipment table.[^dorts-capacity][^dorts-line][^dorts-equipment-table] Rounding
is a plausible explanation, but no opened source says so; all three values keep
their original precision.

The planning inventory gives storage space for 14 trainsets.[^dorts-capacity]
The equipment table lists 13 stabling-road positions and separately notes that
track 302 can hold one train beside the second-series stabling area.[^dorts-equipment-table]
That note may explain the 14-versus-13 difference, but the reconciliation is an
inference, so the two labelled values and the extra-track note remain separate.

## Maintenance and development provision

DORTS identifies CN339A as the package for ten categories of main maintenance
equipment.[^dorts-equipment-list] The depot-equipment table classifies Nangang
at level three and records no test track.[^dorts-equipment-table]

The detailed route page says development was reserved above the stabling-shed
roof slab.[^dorts-line] It establishes provision for over-site use, not that a
particular development was completed. The opened sources do not publish a
current asset list for the original equipment or a completed over-site scheme,
so neither is inferred.

## Construction constraint

A DORTS track manual records that, during Bannan rail installation, drainage-
box-culvert work was still under way at Nangang Depot and the invert of the
emergence section remained under construction and unhanded-over.[^dorts-track]
The passage explains why long welded rail had to be assembled elsewhere, but it
does not give a calendar date for the depot work.[^dorts-track] Exact original
construction, handover and commissioning dates therefore remain TBC.

## Typhoon Nari and flood rebuilding

Typhoon Nari turned the depot into an entry point for floodwater on 17 September
2001.[^dorts-nari] DORTS records track-area flooding at midnight and an emergency attempt to
move trains to Beitou and Xindian depots.[^dorts-nari] By 03:00 the water had
deepened and entered the main-line tunnel, preventing further train movements;
staff withdrew toward Kunyang and abandoned Nangang Depot at 04:30.[^dorts-nari]

The rebuilding changed both the site and later design rules. DORTS says the
depot perimeter wall was rebuilt as a reinforced-concrete floodwall and a
floodgate was added where the emergence section meets the depot.[^dorts-flood-rebuild]
The same review raised the adopted water-exclusion height from 0.5 to 1.1 metres
and used the Nari flood level as an additional design check for later
facilities.[^dorts-flood-rebuild] The incident is therefore part of the depot's
engineering history, not merely a line-wide service disruption.
