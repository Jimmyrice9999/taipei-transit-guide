Nangang Depot maintains the eastern end of the
[Bannan Line](/rail/metro/lines/bannan-line/), opposite
[Tucheng Depot](/rail/metro/depots/tucheng-depot/) at the western end, and shares
the line's [C321](/rail/metro/rolling-stock/c321/) and
[C341](/rail/metro/rolling-stock/c341/) fleets.
