## Overview

Xindian Depot, 新店機廠, is the Songshan–Xindian Line's main depot. DORTS
locates it north of Huanhe Road and east of Zhongyang Road in Xindian
District.[^dorts-planning] Its planning inventory classifies the steel-wheel
facility at level three.[^dorts-capacity]

TRTC currently places Xindian workshop management in Vehicle Plant Three,
whose remit covers the Songshan–Xindian and Zhonghe–Xinlu lines and the
Xinbeitou and Xiaobitan branches.[^trtc-duties] That is an organisational
maintenance assignment, not a depot-by-depot train roster; current model
allocation remains TBC.

## The Xiaobitan branch

The passenger railway passes through the maintenance site. DORTS describes the
approximately 1.5-kilometre Xiaobitan branch leaving the underground main line,
rising to ground level and then climbing inside the depot to elevated G03A.[^dorts-line]
It attributes the station proposal to campaigning by residents and elected
representatives in 1998, records Executive Yuan approval on 17 September 1999,
and dates passenger opening to 29 September 2004.[^dorts-line]

The branch therefore does more than serve the depot: it converts a maintenance
connection into public railway and places an elevated passenger terminus within
the depot complex.[^dorts-line] The route page gives the physical sequence, but
the opened records do not identify the designers of the climbing structure or
publish a depot cross-section; those engineering details remain TBC.

## Area and capacity

Four official areas describe overlapping but differently labelled land. A
network-planning table gives approximately 3.5 hectares, while the DORTS route
description and equipment table give approximately 12 hectares.[^dorts-capacity][^dorts-line][^dorts-equipment-table]
The property-development manual specifies 92,560.7 square metres for the
development base; another DORTS design manual calls the development parcels
approximately 9 hectares.[^dorts-development][^dorts-development-design]

The two development figures are explicitly narrower property-development
measures. No opened source explains why the operational table's 3.5 hectares
differs so sharply from the 12-hectare depot description, so every value and
its label are retained rather than selecting one.[^dorts-capacity][^dorts-line]

Capacity also uses two measures. The planning inventory gives storage space for
18 trainsets, while the equipment table lists 15 stabling-road positions and no
test track.[^dorts-capacity][^dorts-equipment-table] Neither number is converted
into the other.

## Maintenance and later track work

CH329A supplied ten categories of main maintenance equipment.[^dorts-equipment-list]
During the later Songshan Line programme, contract CG591 added about 590 metres
of track work inside Xindian Depot, including three ballasted stabling
roads.[^dorts-track] DORTS dates that depot-related work to 7 February 2014,
separately from the original depot's construction and opening.[^dorts-track]

## Over-site development

DORTS describes shops built structurally together with the depot and an
artificial platform covering its track area to reduce train noise and
vibration.[^dorts-development][^dorts-development-design] The wider development
programme put one six-storey shopping building above depot structures, with
office and residential towers on adjacent parcels.[^dorts-development-design]

For the development base, DORTS records a building permit on 16 June 2006,
construction from 23 January 2007 and an occupancy permit on 15 November
2012.[^dorts-development] Those are property-development milestones, not the
original depot's commissioning dates. The original depot civil completion and
handover chronology remains TBC.
