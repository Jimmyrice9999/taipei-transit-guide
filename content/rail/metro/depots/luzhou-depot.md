## Overview

Luzhou Depot, 蘆洲機廠, is the Zhonghe–Xinlu Line's main depot, at the outer end
of the Luzhou branch.[^dorts-planning] DORTS places it between the Tamsui River
levee to the north, Shuinangou drainage channel to the south, Luzhou pumping
station to the west and a planned road to the east.[^dorts-line] The site is
about 800 metres long and up to 280 metres wide.[^dorts-line]

TRTC assigns management of Luzhou, Xindian and Xinzhuang workshops to Vehicle
Plant Three, whose rolling-stock remit spans the Zhonghe–Xinlu and
Songshan–Xindian lines and both short branches.[^trtc-duties] That shared remit
does not publish a current model-by-depot roster, so present fleet allocation
remains TBC.

## Site and facilities

The detailed route record lists a train wash, entry and exit tracks, maintenance
workshop, civil/track workshop, test track, outdoor storage tracks, stabling
area, perimeter road, car park, main substation and distribution room,
administration/training and guard facilities, and a wastewater-treatment
plant.[^dorts-line]

Official area values differ: approximately 15.9 hectares in the planning
inventory, 16 hectares in the equipment table and earthworks paper, and 16.8
hectares on the detailed route page.[^dorts-capacity][^dorts-equipment-table][^dorts-earthworks][^dorts-line]
No opened source defines the boundary or rounding behind those values, so all
are retained.

## Ground and earthworks

Before acquisition, DORTS says the land included an illegal construction-waste
dump, vegetable plots and some industrial use.[^dorts-earthworks] Poor soil and
waste therefore had to be excavated and replaced with suitable fill. Because
compression beneath later structures and equipment was a risk, the earthworks
plan prioritised areas with low settlement tolerance, urgent follow-on works or
larger expected settlement.[^dorts-earthworks]

Another DORTS account records a cut-and-fill balancing solution: excess mixed
material and construction spoil were reused in the neighbouring linear park,
reducing external haulage, delay risk and cost.[^dorts-cut-fill] This is specific
to the difficult inherited ground, not a generic depot-building method.

## Construction and power

The route page identifies CL700A as the earliest main Luzhou Line contract,
signed and starting work on 25 October 2001.[^dorts-line] A separate DORTS
account places CL604 depot construction supervision in 2001.[^dorts-power]
After financial trouble disrupted electrical subcontractors in 2007, the main
contractors replaced them and accelerated work; depot low-voltage power was
achieved in August 2007.[^dorts-power]

The Luzhou Line opened on 3 November 2010 after preliminary inspection beginning
4 September and final inspection beginning 16 October.[^dorts-line] Those are
line milestones; exact depot civil handover and final acceptance remain TBC.

## Capacity and maintenance

The planning inventory and equipment table both publish 22, but under different
headings: storage space for 22 trainsets and 22 stabling-road positions.[^dorts-capacity][^dorts-equipment-table]
The equality is reported without assuming the units are interchangeable.

DORTS classifies Luzhou at level four and records a test track.[^dorts-capacity][^dorts-equipment-table]
CL609A supplied fifteen categories of main maintenance equipment.[^dorts-equipment-list]
The active equipment register and present division of work among Vehicle Plant
Three's three depots remain TBC.
