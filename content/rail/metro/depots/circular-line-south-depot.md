## Overview

South Depot, 南機廠, is the Circular Line's main maintenance depot in the
Shisizhang agricultural zone of Xindian.[^dorts-phase1] DORTS describes the
site as including Shisizhang Station and the elevated bridge, with an overall
area of approximately 14.33 hectares; the area laid out for the depot is
approximately 12.19 hectares.[^dorts-phase1]

The depot is now evidenced in New Taipei Metro's operating sphere: the
operator's own announcement calls it a facility under the company's management
and offers space inside it for vending machines.[^ntmc-management] That proves
current management of the named site, but does not by itself establish when
operational responsibility changed hands.

## Functions and design grade

DORTS describes South Depot as the Circular Line's main maintenance facility
with Level-5 inspection capability. Its listed functions include train
stabling, washing, inspection, servicing and maintenance, testing, emergency
repair and traffic control; the site also contains an administration building,
stabling area, fan-track area, civil-and-track maintenance workshop, substation
and wastewater-treatment plant.[^dorts-phase1]

The planning inventory gives the first-phase depot storage space for 64
trainsets.[^dorts-planning-inventory] An older equipment-practice table instead
lists 17 trainsets, 32 stabling-road positions, a test track and approximately
12.9 hectares.[^dorts-equipment-table] Those rows belong to different planning
and equipment-publication contexts. They are reported as published values, not
collapsed into a current fleet or capacity claim.

## Boundaries and open dates

The 14.33-hectare figure explicitly includes the station and viaduct, while the
12.19-hectare figure is the depot-layout area.[^dorts-phase1] The equipment
table's approximately 12.9-hectare field has no boundary explanation in the
opened page.[^dorts-equipment-table] A cadastral or as-built plan would be
needed to reconcile the three denominators.

The Phase 1 page dates the railway's inspection and opening sequence, but the
sources read for this depot do not give a separate standalone civil completion
or handover date. A current depot-level fleet allocation is likewise not
published in these sources, so both remain TBC.
