## Overview

Zhonghe Depot, 中和機廠, is the small secondary depot of the Zhonghe-Xinlu Line.
DORTS places it south of Heping Street on the former Taiwan Railways Zhonghe
Station site, with the Nanshijiao passenger station included in the older area
description.[^dorts-zhonghe-line][^dorts-planning-roles]

The planning inventory classifies it as a level-one depot and gives it storage
for six trainsets.[^dorts-planning-inventory] That makes it a storage facility
at the constrained end of the line's depot system, not a substitute for the
larger maintenance sites elsewhere on the line.

## A depot shaped by its site

DORTS's route description gives an older boundary of approximately 1.47
hectares, including Nanshijiao Station.[^dorts-zhonghe-line] It says that the limited land allowed a
stabling yard, maintenance-equipment room and main substation, supporting train
stabling, general maintenance and cleaning.[^dorts-zhonghe-line]

The later planning table gives approximately 1.2 hectares, calls the depot a
level-one facility and says it provides storage only.[^dorts-planning-inventory]
Those are not silently reconciled: the sources differ both in area and in the
function they assign to the facility. The 1.47-hectare figure includes a
passenger station, while the table's boundary is not explained in the page
text.[^dorts-zhonghe-line][^dorts-planning-inventory]

## Track and opening record

The DORTS track-engineering review describes the Zhonghe track package as
covering the route from Guting, excluding Guting station, to Nanshijiao and the
depot. It records 12.8 kilometres of track, four stations and one simple
parking-and-maintenance depot, all in underground civil works, using modern
ballastless track.[^dorts-track-ct561]

The same article says the Zhonghe Line track contract was awarded in 1992 to
Dong Yi Construction and Bilfinger + Berger, at NT$490 million, and that the
line opened on 24 December 1998.[^dorts-track-ct561] Its text calls the package
CT561 in one heading and CC561 in the contract sentence; this page preserves
the source's two labels rather than silently choosing one.

The line route page records initial inspection on 6 November 1998, final
inspection on 8 December and revenue service on 24 December.[^dorts-zhonghe-line]
It does not provide a separate civil handover date for the depot, so that date
remains TBC.

## What is not published here

No primary source retrieved for this page gives a current depot-level train
roster, a separate stabling-road count, or a standalone depot completion date.
Those fields remain TBC rather than being reconstructed from fleet models or
