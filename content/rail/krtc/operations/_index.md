The [ridership record](/rail/krtc/operations/ridership/) covers 2025 annual
totals for the MRT and Circular Light Rail separately; monthly and
per-station figures remain TBC — the underlying PDF archive is
JavaScript-rendered and its reports could not be extracted in this pass.

The [fare history record](/rail/krtc/operations/fares/) covers several ticket
products with real NT$ figures, while the base single fare itself sits
on a chart posted at station machines rather than being stated as text on
the operator's own site.
