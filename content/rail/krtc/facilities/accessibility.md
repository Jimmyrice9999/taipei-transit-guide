## What the operator states, and what it doesn't

KRTC's own accessibility page states that every station provides an
accessible elevator, framed to serve visually impaired, physically
disabled, elderly and other temporarily mobility-limited passengers — those
carrying heavy items or pushing strollers are named explicitly alongside
disabled passengers.[^krtc-accessibility-page] This is a statement of
universal provision in words, not a measured completion percentage: no
"100% of stations" figure, or any numeric count, appears anywhere on the
page.

The same page enumerates a defined set of facilities at every station: an
accessible ticket gate and an accessible add-value/ticketing machine
positioned near the non-paid-area elevator exit; accessible restrooms in
both men's and women's facilities; accessible ramps that continue existing
street-level infrastructure into the station as a connection point for
wheelchair users; one accessible car or motorcycle parking space at
stations that have park-and-ride facilities; and platform-level tactile
guidance, accessible door-position signage, and audible door-closing
warnings.[^krtc-accessibility-page]

Checked specifically: the same page cites no named law, regulation or
design standard anywhere in its content — not Taiwan's general building
accessibility code, not the public-transport accessible-facilities
regulation, not the disability-rights protection act. This is recorded as
not found on this page, not as confirmed absent from KRTC's practice more
broadly; a KRTC internal policy document or tender specification might
state one that this page did not reach.

## The light rail is a separate question

KRTC's accessibility page sits within the site's heavy-rail ("乘車指南")
section. The separate Circular Light Rail station-guide pages checked for
this page did not themselves mention accessibility, low-floor vehicles or
wheelchair access. Whether the heavy-rail page's "all stations" statement
is meant to cover the light rail too, or refers only to the Red and Orange
Lines, is not stated by any source found and is not assumed either way
here.

## Remaining gaps

No numeric elevator-completion figure and no named regulatory basis for
KRTC's accessibility programme were found in any source read for this page.
Whether the Circular Light Rail is covered by the same "all stations"
statement is TBC.
