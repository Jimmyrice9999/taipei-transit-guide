The [accessibility record](/rail/krtc/facilities/accessibility/) covers what
KRTC's own accessibility page states — and what it does not: no numeric
completion figure or named regulatory standard appears on it.

The [depots record](/rail/krtc/facilities/depots/) covers the three named
Red/Orange Line depots — North, South and Daliao — each at a different
maintenance tier, plus the Circular Light Rail's own depot, whose name a
government primary source and secondary material disagree on.
