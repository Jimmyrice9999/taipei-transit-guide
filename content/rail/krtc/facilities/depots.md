## Three depots, three maintenance tiers

Kaohsiung Metro's Red and Orange Line network has exactly three named
maintenance depots — North Depot (北機廠), South Depot (南機廠) and Daliao
Depot (大寮機廠) — confirmed by the Mass Rapid Transit Bureau's own route
description.[^mtbu-red-orange-route] Each is classified by KRTC at a
different maintenance tier, and each carries a different site area.

**South Depot** sits at the south end of the Red Line in Qianzhen District,
at the Zhongshan Road/Zhong'an Road intersection. Its 26-hectare site is
classified a Level 3 maintenance depot (三級維修機廠), and — beyond train
maintenance — it also houses KRTC's own corporate headquarters building and
the entire metro system's operations control centre.[^krtc-south-depot]

**North Depot** spans the boundary between Gangshan District and Ciaotou
District at the Red Line's north end. Its total site is 34 hectares, of
which roughly 24.6 hectares forms the depot, access-road and station
facility zone (the remainder is reserved for commercial
development).[^krtc-north-depot] It is
also classified Level 3.[^krtc-north-depot] North Depot has a second role
beyond maintenance: it is the point where the existing power system
interconnects to supply traction power for the Gangshan-Luzhu extension's
RK1 station.[^mtbu-rk1-power]

**Daliao Depot**, at the east end of the Orange Line in Daliao District, is
the largest of the three at 54 hectares total, and carries the highest
maintenance tier of the three — Level 5 (五級維修機廠) — functioning as the
network's main repair depot.[^krtc-daliao-depot]

## The light rail depot: one government name, two others in circulation

The Circular Light Rail's own depot forms station C37. The Mass Rapid
Transit Bureau's own route page names it only "輕軌機廠" (LRT Depot), at the
Ruixi Street/Ruibei Road intersection — the sole government primary source
read for this page.[^mtbu-circular-route] A more specific name, "前鎮機廠"
(Qianzhen Depot), with a different intersection and a stated area, appears
in secondary material citing a 2015 environmental impact statement hosted
only on `web.archive.org`, which this page's research could not fetch —
that name and location are not published here as established.

## Conflicts

Kaohsiung Metro's own comparison table for Daliao Depot's site area is 54
hectares.[^krtc-daliao-depot] A zh.wikipedia depot table gives 55.0 hectares
for the same facility, not independently traced to its own
footnote.[^zhwiki-depot-table] Both values are published; neither is
picked.

North Depot's district coverage is stated by KRTC's own page as spanning
two districts, Gangshan and Ciaotou.[^krtc-north-depot] The same
zh.wikipedia depot table gives Ciaotou District only.[^zhwiki-depot-table]
Both are published as a direct primary-versus-secondary disagreement.

## Remaining gaps

No source found states a train-stabling capacity — a number of trains or
maintenance bays — for any of the four depots described here. Whether a new
or expanded depot is planned to serve the Hyundai Rotem fleet arriving via
the Gangshan-Luzhu and Xiaogang-Linyuan extensions is TBC; North Depot is
confirmed only as an electrical interconnection point for the Gangshan-Luzhu
extension, not as a maintenance base for the new trains. Construction
start/completion dates for all three metro depots are TBC — each depot's
own KRTC page states only later commercial-development dates for its land,
not the depot's own construction chronology. The Circular Light Rail
depot's precise identity, alternate name and construction history remain
unresolved pending access to the environmental impact statement that
`web.archive.org` currently blocks.
