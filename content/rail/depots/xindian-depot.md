Xindian Depot maintains the [Songshan–Xindian Line](/rail/lines/songshan-xindian-line/),
and carries the network's oddest piece of alignment: the Xiaobitan branch
rises from the underground main line to the surface and climbs to its
elevated terminus **inside the depot site itself** — a passenger station in
the middle of a maintenance facility.

*This is a scope page.* Site area, functions, allocation and the branch
arrangement's engineering detail are **still to be verified against primary
sources**.

*Nothing numerical is asserted on this page.*
