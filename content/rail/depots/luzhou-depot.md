Luzhou Depot sits at the end of the [Zhonghe–Xinlu Line](/rail/lines/zhonghe-xinlu-line/)'s
Luzhou arm, sharing the line's [C371](/rail/rolling-stock/c371/) fleet with
[Xinzhuang Depot](/rail/depots/xinzhuang-depot/) on the other arm.

page.
