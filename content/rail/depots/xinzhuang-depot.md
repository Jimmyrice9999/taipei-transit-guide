Xinzhuang Depot serves the [Zhonghe–Xinlu Line](/rail/lines/zhonghe-xinlu-line/)
at the network's western edge, straddling the New Taipei–Taoyuan boundary.
What makes it matter beyond its function is the ground it stands on: the site
of the **Losheng Sanatorium**, the former leprosarium whose residents fought
the demolition of their home, in a preservation battle that reshaped the
depot's own footprint and became one of Taiwan's defining heritage
controversies. That story deserves a history article of the Matra dispute's
depth — written from the public record of the dispute, not from a summary of
it — and it is on the backlog by name.

*This is a scope page.* The depot's grade, size, the boundary-straddling
siting and the sequence of the preservation fight are **still to be verified
against primary sources**.

*Nothing numerical is asserted on this page.*
