Taishan Guihe is the sixth stop from Taipei Main Station and the last of the A5–A6 elevated civil package before the line turns underground toward National Taiwan Sport University.[^rb-102-annual-report] No rail interchange is established at this station by any source read for this page.

TYMC's own timetable page does not list A6 among the express (直達車) stops; only the commuter (普通車) service, calling at every station, serves this stop.[^tymc-express-timetable] TYMC's own A6 timetable page separately records a single weekday early-morning working, a "peak-hour skip-stop commuter" (尖峰跳站普通車) departure at 07:54, distinct from the standing express service — a once-daily special working of the all-stop type rather than a second express pattern.[^tymc-a6-timetable]

The Railway Bureau's architecture report groups A6 with Sanchong, New Taipei Industrial Park, Xinzhuang Fuduxin and Taishan under a single design contract, DE01, covering all five of the line's northernmost elevated stations.[^rb-airport-station-architecture] TYMC's own current station page gives the address as New Taipei Boulevard Section 6 No. 460, and lists local bus connections rather than any rail transfer.[^tymc-a6-station] No public-art record appears for this station on TYMC's own current allocation page, unlike several of its elevated neighbours; the operator's page simply has no row for A6, which this page records as not found rather than as proof no work exists.[^tymc-a6-station]

Taishan Guihe opened with the rest of the initial line on 2 March 2017.[^tymc-history] Like Sanchong, New Taipei Industrial Park, Xinzhuang Fuduxin, Taishan and Linkou, it sits within New Taipei City rather than Taoyuan — six of the line's 22 current stations lie in New Taipei, per this project's own committed TDX administrative-district data, and one (Taipei Main Station) lies in Taipei City itself.[^tdx-tymc-station]

TDX's own committed route data places A6 12.833 km along route A-1 from Taipei Main Station by cumulative surveyed distance, the same measure this project uses for every station on the line.[^tdx-tymc-sor]
