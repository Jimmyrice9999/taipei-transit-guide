Dayuan is the first station in Dayuan District heading south, sited beside Xinjie Creek amid farmland that has since been reclassified from "Taiwan Gateway Zone" to a transit-tourism and agricultural-trading zone under the city's Aerotropolis (桃園航空城) plan.[^ltn-a15-aerotropolis] It was built under civil package CE03B, which also covered Hengshan, Linghang, Taoyuan HSR Station and the Qingpu Depot's administration building along an elevated bridge running from Qingpu to Zhongli; a Railway Bureau annual report records that package's structural, station and rain-protection work complete, building-management-system integration in June and 99.95% overall progress at year-end — a package-wide figure covering four stations and a depot building, not this station alone.[^rb-102-annual-report]

Sources disagree on the station's own dimensions: zh.wikipedia's dedicated article gives a station body about 170.0 m long and 26.4 m wide,[^zhwiki-a15] while the Railway Bureau's architecture report gives 87 m by 24.68 m.[^rb-airport-station-architecture] Both describe the same elevated, two-level, side-platform, two-track station; neither source states what the two length figures each measure, and both are published here rather than one being picked.

No rail interchange is established at this station. TYMC's own timetable page does not list A15 among the express (直達車) stops; only the commuter (普通車) service, calling at every station, serves this stop.[^tymc-express-timetable]

Dayuan opened with the rest of the initial line on 2 March 2017.[^tymc-history]

TDX's own committed route data places A15 39.13 km along route A-1 from Taipei Main Station by cumulative surveyed distance, the same measure this project uses for every station on the line.[^tdx-tymc-sor] Daily ridership and the platform screen-door type at this station are not established by any source read for this page and stay TBC. The accessible-lift count at this station is likewise not itemised beyond what the operator's general facility listing states.
