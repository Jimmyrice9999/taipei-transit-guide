Taoyuan Sports Park sits within the same HSR special-district planning area as Taoyuan HSR Station itself, one stop south, and was built within civil package CE03B's elevated bridge running from Qingpu toward Zhongli.[^rb-102-annual-report] TYMC's own attractions page for this station lists the Taoyuan Children's Art Museum, Qingtang Garden (青塘園, described elsewhere as a popular photo destination), GlobalMall Taoyuan A19 and Rakuten Taoyuan Baseball Stadium among nearby destinations.[^tymc-a19-attractions] zh.wikipedia separately describes the stadium connection as making it the first ballpark in Taiwan directly connected to a metro system — a superlative this page records as an unverified secondary claim rather than a sourced fact, since no primary source read for this page corroborates it.

No rail interchange is established at this station. TYMC's own timetable page does not list A19 among the express (直達車) stops; only the commuter (普通車) service, calling at every station, serves this stop.[^tymc-express-timetable]

TYMC's own station page gives the address as HSR South Road Section 2 No. 350 in Zhongli District, with a single exit on the west side of that road.[^tymc-a19-station] The station is elevated with two side platforms each about 27.0 m wide, reached by six staircases, two escalators and two elevators between the concourse and platform levels, according to zh.wikipedia's dedicated article — a level of dimensional detail not independently restated by any primary source read for this page.

Taoyuan Sports Park opened with the rest of the initial line on 2 March 2017.[^tymc-history]

TDX's own committed route data places A19 46.31 km along route A-1 from Taipei Main Station by cumulative surveyed distance, the same measure this project uses for every station on the line.[^tdx-tymc-sor] Daily ridership at this station is not established by any source read for this page and stays TBC.

The current timetable's combined Taipei/airport display begins at 06:14, with the next shown departures at 06:29, 06:44 and 06:59; the late added airport-bound departures shown are 23:31, 23:46 and 00:01.[^tymc-a19-timetable] These are commuter-display facts, not a claim that the station is served by the fixed express pattern, which the operator separately excludes.[^tymc-express-timetable]

The operator's rows continue the :14, :29, :44 and :59 pattern through the displayed daytime hours, then retain a regular 23:14 departure before the three added airport services.[^tymc-a19-timetable] The page labels those added services as all-stop airport trips, so the late entries should not be read as an express replacement for the station's normal service. The timetable therefore gives a more precise account of A19's late-night airport access than the general route map alone.[^tymc-a19-timetable]
