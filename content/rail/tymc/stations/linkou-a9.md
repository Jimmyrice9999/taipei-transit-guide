Linkou serves the Linkou plateau new town and crosses above the Sun Yat-sen Freeway's Linkou interchange on an elevated structure after departing Chang Gung Memorial Hospital.[^rb-airport-station-architecture] The station's four-track arrangement, with the two middle tracks carrying express services through without stopping and the two outer tracks serving commuter trains, is a configuration this project's sources describe as otherwise unused elsewhere on the line.

No rail interchange currently exists at this station. TYMC's own timetable page does not list A9 among the express (直達車) stops; only the commuter (普通車) service, calling at every station, serves this stop.[^tymc-express-timetable] A Railway Bureau annual report records an approximately 0.4914-hectare joint-development site at this station, with entrance co-construction, a building permit dated 5 September 2012 and handover of the co-constructed MRT facilities to the developer on 17 January 2013.[^rb-102-annual-report]

Sources disagree on this station's elevation, both agreeing only that it is Taiwan's highest metro station: zh.wikipedia's dedicated article gives 253 m, while a New Taipei City government documentary article gives approximately 255 m; neither figure traces to a published as-built survey in the sources read for this page, and both are recorded rather than averaged.

TYMC's own current public-art page names one work here, "A Free Wandering" (一個自在的游晃) by the collective 豪華朗機工, without a stated year or medium.[^tymc-art] TYMC's own station page gives the address as Bade Road No. 290, with two exits reaching Bade Road and Wenhua 3rd Road Section 1.[^tymc-a9-station]

Linkou opened with the rest of the initial line on 2 March 2017.[^tymc-history]

TDX's own committed route data places A9 21.35 km along route A-1 from Taipei Main Station by cumulative surveyed distance, the same measure this project uses for every station on the line.[^tdx-tymc-sor]

The current A9 timetable displays commuter first departures at 06:11 toward the airport and 06:06 toward Taipei.[^tymc-a9-timetable] The ordinary late departures are 23:39 toward the airport and 23:53 toward Taipei, followed by added airport services at 23:54 and 00:09.[^tymc-a9-timetable] Its detailed rows show the normal 15-minute all-stop pattern; A9 is not a fixed express stop, although the shared service note identifies it in the weekday skip-stop pattern toward A1.[^tymc-a9-timetable][^tymc-express-timetable]

The timetable's A9 heading combines the airport and Laojie River on the southbound side, so the 00:09 extra airport service cannot be assigned to a particular southern terminal from this page alone.[^tymc-a9-timetable] The northbound display remains an A1-bound all-stop table and ends at 23:53 in the fetched version.[^tymc-a9-timetable]

No fixed express row appears for A9.[^tymc-a9-timetable]
