Taishan sits beside a road viaduct, its elevated structure co-built with the roadway below.[^rb-airport-station-architecture] No rail interchange is established at this station by any source read for this page. The A5–A6 civil package (CE01C) also carried the water, electrical and environmental-control works for this pair of stations; a Railway Bureau annual report records completed fit-out, partial final acceptance for A5/A6 landscape and planting, and cleaning and handover of an existing road-crossing drainage culvert in the area, at 99.98% overall package progress by the reporting year's end.[^rb-102-annual-report]

TYMC's own timetable page does not list A5 among the express (直達車) stops; only the commuter (普通車) service, calling at every station, serves this stop.[^tymc-express-timetable] The Railway Bureau's architecture report groups A5 with Sanchong, New Taipei Industrial Park, Xinzhuang Fuduxin and Taishan Guihe under a single design contract, DE01, covering the line's five northernmost elevated stations.[^rb-airport-station-architecture]

TYMC's own station page gives the address as New Taipei Boulevard Section 4 No. 431, with a grouped set of three exits (1/2/3) there and a fourth, separate exit on Tailin Road Section 2 — a layout the operator's page does not explain further.[^tymc-a5-station]

Taishan opened with the rest of the initial line on 2 March 2017.[^tymc-history] It sits within New Taipei City, one of six current Airport MRT stations there.[^tdx-tymc-station]

TDX's own committed route data places A5 10.029 km along route A-1 from Taipei Main Station by cumulative surveyed distance, the same measure this project uses for every station on the line.[^tdx-tymc-sor] Daily ridership at this station is not established by any source read for this page and stays TBC. The accessible-lift count at this station is likewise not itemised beyond what the operator's general facility listing states.

The operator's current timetable shows A5 as commuter-only: first departures are 05:57 toward Taipei and 06:07 toward the airport, while the displayed last departures are 00:10 and 23:54.[^tymc-a5-timetable] The two last airport departures are marked as additional airport-service trains, so the station's late service is not silently described as a standard express operation.[^tymc-a5-timetable] The source gives no station-specific ridership figure, which remains TBC here.[^tymc-a5-timetable]

The detailed A5 table shows a 15-minute ordinary commuter rhythm for most of the day.[^tymc-a5-timetable] Airport-bound trains begin at 06:07, 06:22, 06:37 and 06:52, while Taipei-bound trains begin at 05:57, 06:12, 06:27 and 06:42.[^tymc-a5-timetable] The final two airport-bound entries, 23:39 and 23:54, are specially marked as extra airport services rather than regular direct trains.[^tymc-a5-timetable]
