Taipei Main Station is the line's northern terminus and its busiest interchange, sitting at the same complex as Taipei Metro's own Taipei Main Station, Taiwan Railways' Taipei Station and Taiwan High Speed Rail's Taipei Station.[^tymc-a1-station] TYMC's own station page states the connection reaches Taipei Metro's Tamsui–Xinyi, Bannan and Songshan–Xindian lines directly, and separately reaches Beimen Station (Taipei Metro's Songshan–Xindian line) via an electric walkway to its basement level; the page gives approximate walking times of 10–15 minutes to the Taipei Main Station lines and to Beimen, and 5–10 minutes to TRA and THSR via the west parking area.[^tymc-a1-station] Which of these routes is fastest in practice is not established by a primary source read for this page and is left TBC rather than repeated from secondary travel-blog claims.

The station's construction is recorded separately from the rest of the line: unlike every other Airport MRT station, which the then High Speed Rail Bureau built directly, A1 was delegated to Taipei City Government for planning, design and construction.[^rb-airport-station-architecture] Taipei's own project record names one design package (DA115) and six construction lots — CA441A, CA450A, CA450B, CA441H, CA384 and CA386 — with CA441A the earliest, signed 15 September 2006, and describes roughly 4.4 km of route including the underground station, a storage area and a joint-development structural body.[^dorts-airport-a1]

TYMC's own timetable page lists A1 among the express (直達車) stops, alongside the all-stop commuter (普通車) service that calls at every station on the line.[^tymc-express-timetable] A dedicated Taoyuan tourism-portal page gives express journey times from A1 of 35 minutes to Airport Terminal 1 (A12) and 39 minutes to Terminal 2 (A13).[^tycg-tourism-airport-mrt]

Taipei Main Station opened with the rest of the initial line on 2 March 2017 — the only current Airport MRT station within Taipei City itself.[^tymc-history][^tdx-tymc-station]
