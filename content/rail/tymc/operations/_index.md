The [ridership record](/rail/tymc/operations/ridership/) covers the
operator's monthly whole-system and per-station reports, station-level
figures for July 2026, and a Wikipedia citation this page caught not
supporting the figure it was attached to.

The [fare history record](/rail/tymc/operations/fares/) covers a season-pass
scheme replaced outright on 2 January 2025 — different formulas, different
discount tiers, a fee-free exchange window — while the specific NT$ figures
behind every formula stay behind a fare-query tool this page's tooling
cannot read.
