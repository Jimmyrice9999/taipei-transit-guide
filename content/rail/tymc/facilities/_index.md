The [accessibility record](/rail/tymc/facilities/accessibility/) covers what
the operator's own accessibility page describes in detail — down to
wheelchair-space dimensions on trains — while noting it cites no named
regulation or completion figure.

The [depots record](/rail/tymc/facilities/depots/) covers Qingpu and Luzhu
Depots from a primary construction report — Qingpu doubling as
headquarters and operations control centre, Luzhu trailing behind on the
report's own progress figures, with neither depot's site area found
sourced anywhere.
