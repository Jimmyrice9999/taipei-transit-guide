## What this page records

This page is a source-boundary record for the requested “Sanmin Line” name, not a route description.[^dorts-network][^ntpc-chronology][^ntpc-route-table] The full Taipei DORTS network page separates named planning routes from named construction routes; it lists Minsheng–Xizhi and Shizi among the planning routes and the Xinyi east extension, Wanda phases and Circular sections among the construction routes.[^dorts-network] No route link or heading named Sanmin Line or 三民線 appeared in that fetched network page.[^dorts-network]

The New Taipei DORTS chronology was also read in full.[^ntpc-chronology] Its dated entries cover named projects including Wugu–Taishan, Bali, Shenkeng, Danhai, Sanying, Wanda, the Circular Line and the Xidong/Minsheng work.[^ntpc-chronology] The chronology does not provide a dated milestone for a route called 三民線.[^ntpc-chronology] The separate New Taipei route table supplies a second status boundary: its planned rows are Shenkeng, Wugu–Taishan, Bali, Taishan–Banqiao and Keelung; its design rows are Danhai Blue Sea Phase 2 and Xidong; and its construction rows are Wanda, Circular north/south and Sanying.[^ntpc-route-table]

## The likely name collisions

The closest official spelling is Sanying, 三鶯線, not Sanmin, 三民線.[^ntpc-sanying] The current Sanying page gives 14.29 km, 12 stations and service beginning 30 June 2026.[^ntpc-sanying] It describes a distinct route from Dingpu through Tucheng, Sanxia and Yingge, with a future Bade extension and Taoyuan Green Line connection.[^ntpc-sanying] This site already records Sanying as its own operating line, so this page does not duplicate it under a new English spelling.[^ntpc-sanying]

Minsheng–Xizhi is another distinct name in the checked record.[^dorts-network][^ntpc-chronology] Taipei DORTS uses 民生汐止線, while the New Taipei chronology uses 汐止民生線 for related historical milestones.[^dorts-network][^ntpc-chronology] Neither full-page source equates that name with 三民線, so the requested label is not used as an alias.[^dorts-network][^ntpc-chronology]
