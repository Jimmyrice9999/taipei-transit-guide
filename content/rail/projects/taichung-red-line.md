## Corridor and purpose

The Chongde–Fengyuan Line is a feasibility-stage proposal in Taichung's northern transport corridor. The current bureau page traces its origin to the Ministry of Transportation-reviewed Taichung metropolitan rail-network assessment, which identified the corridor as engineering-feasible and above the demand threshold for a metro project.[^tcrt-red] The stated purpose is to connect established and developing communities while improving the overall network's transport effect.[^tcrt-red]

The earlier network concept began at the Chongde Road–Sanmin Road intersection and ran north along Chongde Road, Chengde Road, Fengyuan Boulevard and Zhongzheng Road, ending near the Fengyuan Boulevard–Zhongzheng Road intersection.[^tcrt-red] The feasibility study brought the TRA Fengyuan Station connection forward from the longer-term Fengke corridor, adding two stations and preserving a future integration point with the Fengke axis.[^tcrt-red] The public presentation places the corridor through North District, Beitun, Tanzi, Shengang and Fengyuan, with planned relationships to the Green Line, Orange Line, Fengke axis and TRA.[^tcrt-red-fengyuan-deck]

## Preliminary scope and forecasts

The current preliminary scope is approximately 15.42 km, 11 stations and one level-five depot.[^tcrt-red][^tcrt-red-dec24] The December presentation labels the provisional stations `R1` through `R11`, with `R3` at the Green Line Wenxin Chongde connection and `R11` at TRA Fengyuan Station.[^tcrt-red-fengyuan-deck] These labels are planning labels, not a published operating station registry.[^tcrt-red-fengyuan-deck]

The December 2025 release estimates about 220,000 people within 500 metres of the line in ROC 140 and a peak headway of 2.5 minutes.[^tcrt-red-dec18] The later meeting release gives a target-year model of approximately 108,310 passengers per day and 16,000 passengers in the peak hour.[^tcrt-red-dec24] The presentation reports the same daily figure, 16,910 peak-hour passengers and a maximum interstation load of 5,870 passengers per hour.[^tcrt-red-fengyuan-deck] These are forecasts for a target year, not current ridership.[^tcrt-red-dec18][^tcrt-red-dec24]

## Fengyuan alternatives and public response

The public materials do not show a single settled Fengyuan urban alignment. The Fengyuan presentation compares two elevated alternatives and one underground alternative for the connection toward TRA Fengyuan Station.[^tcrt-red-fengyuan-deck] Its comparison describes the north-ring-road elevated option as farther from the Fengyuan commercial area but less disruptive and lower-cost, the south-ring-road elevated option as closer to the commercial area, and the Zhongzheng Road underground option as the most direct but the most expensive and difficult to build in a narrow, congested street.[^tcrt-red-fengyuan-deck]

The bureau's meeting release says the three options differ in service area, construction form, construction impacts and land conditions, and that the final route remains subject to later evaluation and central approval.[^tcrt-red-dec24] The Beitun presentation likewise says its material describes current planning results and that the final plan depends on Executive Yuan approval.[^tcrt-red-beitun-deck] The meeting records show why that decision is consequential: residents and elected representatives asked for more stations, provision for a future Houli extension, better treatment of the Fengyuan station traffic problem, and fuller comparison of elevated and underground construction.[^tcrt-red-fengyuan-record][^tcrt-red-beitun-record] Beitun comments also raised road-width, lane, parking, pedestrian, noise and vibration effects along Chongde Road, as well as possible connections to the Orange Line, Green Line and major event destinations.[^tcrt-red-beitun-record]

## Study status and data boundary

The bureau's current chronology records a NT$20 million feasibility study, MOTC support of NT$11.7 million and a city match of NT$8.3 million.[^tcrt-red] It records the tender announcement on 26 February 2025, award on 21 April, work-plan approval on 9 June, interim-report submission on 2 September, review on 9 October, interim approval on 12 December and the two December public meetings.[^tcrt-red] The consultant submitted the final report on 24 July 2026; the bureau says it is under review and will be sent for central consideration after that review.[^tcrt-red]

The live TDX TMRT response checked for this page contains current Green Line records, not a Red Line project or operating dataset.[^tdx-tmrt] The official pages provide an aggregate station count and provisional labels but no final bilingual station inventory, coordinates, exits, accessibility facilities, timetable, construction contract, opening date or observed ridership.[^tcrt-red][^tcrt-red-fengyuan-deck][^tdx-tmrt] This unit therefore publishes a project page rather than manufacturing 11 station pages from an unapproved alignment.

The opening date, construction progress, final urban-section alternative, final station names and current ridership remain TBC.[^tcrt-red][^tcrt-red-dec24][^tdx-tmrt] A completed feasibility review, central approval, comprehensive-plan record, procurement documents and a later TDX publication would establish those fields.
