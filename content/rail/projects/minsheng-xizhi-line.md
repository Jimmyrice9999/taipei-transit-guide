## One name, two current project records

The Minsheng–Xizhi project is a cross-city LRRT plan, but its current official records divide it into a Taipei section and the Xidong section in New Taipei.[^dorts-minsheng-current][^ntpc-xidong-route] Taipei DORTS describes the corridor through Daitong, Zhongshan, Songshan and Neihu before reaching Xizhi, with links to the Tamsui–Xinyi, Zhonghe–Xinlu, Wenhu, Circular and TRA systems.[^dorts-minsheng-current] Its current Taipei record gives approximately 11.02 km and nine stations, eight underground and one elevated.[^dorts-minsheng-current] The same page says the system is LRRT, consistent with the Xidong and Keelung MRT projects, and assigns the route number SB.[^dorts-minsheng-current]

New Taipei’s current route page uses the name Xidong MRT, while retaining “formerly Xizhi–Minsheng” in its title.[^ntpc-xidong-route] It gives the Xidong section as 5.56 km and six stations, of which 4.75 km are in New Taipei and 0.81 km are in Taipei.[^ntpc-xidong-route] The page describes the route through Shehou, Tongxing and Xike to the Xizhi District Office, with transfers to Wenhu, Keelung MRT and TRA.[^ntpc-xidong-route] The two records therefore describe related phases and interfaces, not a single final station list that can safely be assembled by adding their counts.[^dorts-minsheng-current][^ntpc-xidong-route]

## Approval and construction status

The feasibility report was submitted to the Ministry of Transportation and Communications on 3 January 2011 and approved on 19 December 2011.[^dorts-minsheng-current][^ntpc-minsheng-route] That feasibility approval does not mean that the current Taipei comprehensive plan has been approved: Taipei DORTS records the Taipei section’s fourth resubmission on 2 July 2026, while its urban-plan change still has no approval date.[^dorts-minsheng-current] New Taipei’s Taipei-section page likewise labels the plan as not yet approved and says its route information is for reference.[^ntpc-minsheng-route]

The Xidong section has advanced further. New Taipei records its main turnkey contract as awarded on 22 October 2024 and started on 20 March 2025, with a contract value of NT$31,961,983,967 and a target completion in April 2032.[^ntpc-xidong-progress] The same progress page reports 0% for the city-controlled programme as of July 2026, while separately listing critical water-relocation and road-widening works that began in December 2023.[^ntpc-xidong-progress] That percentage and those package dates use different reporting measures; they should not be turned into one inferred construction percentage.[^ntpc-xidong-progress]

## Earlier alignments and changing scope

An earlier DORTS technical-journal record described a 17.52 km main route and a 2.26 km branch, with 15 main stations and three elevated branch stations.[^dorts-tech44-p65] Its alignment ran underground from Dadaocheng through Minsheng and the central east-west corridor, then crossed the Keelung River, passed through Neihu and a mountain tunnel, and continued via Shehou, Xike and Xizhi.[^dorts-tech44-p65] The current Taipei page’s 11.02 km and nine-station figure is a later Taipei-section scope, not a correction that erases the earlier planning record.[^dorts-minsheng-current][^dorts-tech44-p65]

The older environmental-planning material labels early stations SB01–SB06 but explicitly says those station names were not formally determined and were only planning-stage identifiers.[^dorts-tech3-p33] Current official pages do not publish a final Taipei station-name and station-code list, so those labels are not promoted to station pages or structured station data.[^dorts-tech3-p33][^dorts-minsheng-current][^ntpc-minsheng-route] The DORTS feasibility index confirms that the feasibility study is available as a multi-chapter report, but the current project pages remain the controlling evidence for present scope and status.[^dorts-feasibility-index][^dorts-minsheng-current]

## What remains open

No fetched primary page supplies a complete-corridor opening date, current Taipei construction percentage, Taipei timetable, observed ridership series, final Taipei station codes, or a unified accessibility and interchange inventory.[^dorts-minsheng-current][^ntpc-xidong-route][^ntpc-xidong-progress][^dorts-feasibility-index] Those values remain TBC until later approved-plan, procurement, operator or TDX records establish them.[^dorts-minsheng-current][^ntpc-xidong-progress]
