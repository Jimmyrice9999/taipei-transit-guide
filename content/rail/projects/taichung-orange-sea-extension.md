## Planning boundary

The current Taichung Rapid Transit Systems Bureau page says that no route has yet been planned for the Orange Line Sea Line extension, that its location diagram is for reference, and that the future result will follow the approved plan.[^tcrt-orange-sea] The same page describes an initial concept connecting Taichung International Airport and Orange Line `O01` westward to Taichung Port, with intended links to coastal attractions, the Blue Line and TRA QingShui Station.[^tcrt-orange-sea] It also says that the feasibility study is evaluating route, station locations and system type.[^tcrt-orange-sea]

The study is meant to connect Taichung city with the coastal districts and integrate airport and port transport.[^tcrt-orange-sea-jan] The bureau's January release says the study covers route alternatives, stations, demand, engineering, environmental impact and financial feasibility, so the public proposal is broader than a line drawing.[^tcrt-orange-sea-jan]

The bureau's official public-meeting summary repeats the airport, Blue Line, TRA and Wuqi Fishing Port connections and the interim 16.50 km, 10-station scope.[^tcrt-orange-sea-summary]

## Interim route proposal

The January release reports an interim proposal of approximately 16.50 km, 10 stations and one level-five depot.[^tcrt-orange-sea-jan] It divides the scheme into a main section, a Sanmin Road branch and a Wuqi Fishing Port branch, extending from the airport through QingShui, Shalu and Wuqi and connecting the Blue Line and TRA QingShui Station.[^tcrt-orange-sea-jan]

The full public-meeting deck labels the interim stations `OE1`-`OE7`, `OE10`-`OE12`.[^tcrt-orange-sea-deck] It shows `OE1`-`OE3` as elevated, `OE4`-`OE7` at grade, `OE10` as the elevated Sanmin Road branch, and `OE11`-`OE12` as the at-grade Wuqi Fishing Port branch.[^tcrt-orange-sea-deck] These are interim planning labels, not a current TDX station registry.[^tcrt-orange-sea-deck][^tdx-tmrt]

The deck illustrates intended connections with the airport Orange Line station, the Blue Line and TRA QingShui Station, alongside destinations including the Harbor Art Center, Taichung Port Passenger Service Center, Taichung Port, Taichung Marine Museum, Aofeng Mountain and Ziyunyan.[^tcrt-orange-sea-deck] The project page's warning and the deck's statement that the final route follows the centrally approved version mean that the 16.50 km scheme should be read as a public interim proposal.[^tcrt-orange-sea][^tcrt-orange-sea-deck]

## Forecasts and system-type question

The January deck gives modelled full-day ridership of 58,363 passengers and a route ridership density of 3,507 passengers per kilometre for ROC 140.[^tcrt-orange-sea-deck] It places that density beside reference thresholds of 6,000 for medium-capacity metro and 3,500 for light rail, and says the assessment meets the light-rail reference value.[^tcrt-orange-sea-deck] That is a forecast comparison, not an adopted final system decision.[^tcrt-orange-sea-deck]

The public meeting record shows why the mode remains unresolved.[^tcrt-orange-sea-minutes] Elected officials and residents requested a medium-capacity system or the same system as the Orange and Blue lines, raised safety concerns about mixed traffic and heavy trucks, questioned the `OE10`/`B05` transfer, and asked that stations be moved toward QingShui's population centres.[^tcrt-orange-sea-minutes] The record documents those requests; it does not establish that the bureau accepted them.[^tcrt-orange-sea-minutes]

## Funding and review chronology

The current page estimates the feasibility-study budget at NT$20 million, with MOTC support of NT$11.7 million and a local matching share of NT$8.3 million.[^tcrt-orange-sea] It records tender publication on 9 April 2025 and award on 9 June 2025.[^tcrt-orange-sea]

The work plan was approved on 21 July 2025, an interim report was submitted on 17 October, a review meeting was held on 24 November, a revised report was submitted on 23 December, a local briefing was held on 15 January 2026, and another revised interim report was submitted on 20 March.[^tcrt-orange-sea] The bureau then returned the report for correction on 9 June 2026, received a revised report on 6 July, and was reviewing it on the page's last update.[^tcrt-orange-sea] The current network page likewise places the extension in the medium-term feasibility group with its interim revised report under review.[^tcrt-network]

## Station data boundary

The live TDX TMRT response checked for this page contains the operating Taichung Green Line records, not an Orange Sea extension project or operating dataset.[^tdx-tmrt] The official pages and the interim deck provide a proposal, aggregate station count, planning labels and forecast outputs, but not an approved final route, current station registry, machine-readable geometry, timetable, actual ridership, construction award or opening date.[^tcrt-orange-sea][^tcrt-orange-sea-deck][^tdx-tmrt] Those fields remain TBC until the feasibility and later approval records establish them.[^tcrt-orange-sea][^tcrt-network]
