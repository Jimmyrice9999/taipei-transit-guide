Wenhua Senior High School is stop 7 of 18 on the Green Line, and the committed TDX record pairs the code G8 with the Traditional Chinese name 文華高中.[^tdx-tmrt] TDX places it in 西屯區 at 臺中市西屯區文心路三段199號, with a published route chainage of 6.71 km in the selected direction.[^tdx-tmrt] The address is the source-backed siting description used here; no extra catchment boundary is inferred from it.

The station belongs to the CJ920 civil package.[^cj920] That package covers the G3–G9 elevated section, which places this station in the central civil contract rather than the northern depot package. The separate CJ900/CJ907 contract covers system equipment, including power, communications, automatic fare collection, depot equipment and trains.[^cj900]

The operator describes the Green Line as a steel-wheel, medium-capacity, fully driverless system using two-car trains.[^tmrt-faq] It identifies G8 as an elevated station; only G0 and G17 are ground level.[^tmrt-faq] That system-level engineering fact is kept separate from the station address because TDX publishes the latter but not the former.[^tdx-tmrt][^tmrt-faq]

The TDX record gives the paired English and Traditional Chinese names but does not explain the naming decision.[^tdx-tmrt] MOTC's public-transit news portal, reporting Taichung City Government's June 2018 naming decision, confirms the station was named for the school directly adjacent to it: 「則以緊臨學校『文華高中』命名」.[^motc-naming]

The operator's own station page names a single exit, at the Wenxin Road Section 3/Henan Road Section 2 intersection, with elevator, escalator and stair access, and lists ground-level facilities: an information desk, an ATM, a water fountain, a restroom, an AED, and accessible elevators serving B2 through 3F.[^tmrt-station-info]

## Joint development: the one complete case on the line

This station's land is a designated Taichung Metro joint-development site, and TCRT's own case list records it as the only one of the line's seven such sites with a complete build-to-handover record.[^tcrt-tod-greenline] The site is 2,228 m² at 70% building coverage and a 500% floor-area ratio; TCRT names 冠德建設股份有限公司 (Kuo Der Construction) and 世義貿易股份有限公司 as joint investors, developing under the original landowner's priority-investor right, with planned residential and retail use.[^tcrt-tod-greenline] TCRT's timeline records the investment contract signed in November 2014, construction reported starting in July 2017, and completion and handover in November 2021.[^tcrt-tod-greenline]

Two Taichung City Government press releases, both about the resulting building's own unit sales, independently confirm its structure — 28 storeys above ground and 6 below — and its status as the Green Line's first transit-oriented-development joint building: 「台中市首座捷運宅」and, in a separate release, 「台中市第一個捷運共構宅」, with a reported 75% (9 of 12 units) auction sell-through rate.[^taichung-tod-sale] Neither city-government release names the private developer; that identification rests on TCRT's case-list page alone and has not been independently cross-confirmed against a second primary source naming the same companies.[^tcrt-tod-greenline][^taichung-tod-sale]

The operator's current public-art roster names works at selected Green Line stations, but it does not assign a named work to G8 in the roster consulted for this page.[^tmrt-art] Whether an unlisted or later work exists here is therefore TBC; the page does not turn a list boundary into a claim that no artwork exists.

The station-specific sources consulted here do not establish a separate incident at G8.[^tmrt-events] That is a research boundary, not a claim that no disruption or worksite event has ever occurred here.
