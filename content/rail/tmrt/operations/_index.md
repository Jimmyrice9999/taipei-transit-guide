The [ridership record](/rail/tmrt/operations/ridership/) covers a single
month's daily figures from the operator's own statistics archive — a
monthly total that arithmetically confirms its own stated daily average,
though the table's column headers did not survive PDF extraction, so what
exactly is being counted rests on the file's own title rather than a read
label.

The [fare history record](/rail/tmrt/operations/fares/) covers a
resident-priced regional season pass spanning four counties and a monthly
points subsidy layered on top of a senior concession — while the base
single fare, as with the other two Taiwan metro operators this project
has checked, is kept off the page entirely.
