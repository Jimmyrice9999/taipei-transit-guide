Taichung Metro Corporation (TMRT) is the operating company behind the Green Line. The operator records its company establishment on 1 January 2017 and its formal opening on 25 April 2021.[^tmrt-events][^tmrt-reopen] The railway was delivered through a split public construction and systems-procurement structure rather than as a single company-built object: the city construction bureau records civil packages CJ910, CJ920 and CJ930, while CJ900/CJ907 covers system equipment and rolling stock.[^cj910][^cj920][^cj930][^cj900]

The company's current route is the 18-station Green Line from `G0` Beitun Main Station to `G17` HSR Taichung Station.[^tmrt-map][^tmrt-annual] It operates a steel-wheel, medium-capacity, fully driverless railway with two-car trains.[^tmrt-faq] The operator's own public-art programme treats the stations as a connected landscape and soundscape rather than as interchangeable platforms.[^tmrt-art] That is why the station pages below keep the station-specific artwork where the current roster names it and leave the rest as a stated gap.

The most important correction in the company's operating history is chronological. The joint-shaft fracture occurred during test operation on 21 November 2020; test operation restarted on 25 March 2021 after remedial work and formal service began on 25 April 2021.[^tmrt-events][^cy-control][^tmrt-reopen] The sources read for this page do not support a separate “2022 reopening”, so that date is not asserted here.

## Operating responsibility and evidence boundary

The operator's route map and annual report establish the Green Line as the railway TMRT currently presents to riders, while the city project records establish the civil-package boundaries inherited by the company.[^tmrt-map][^tmrt-annual][^cj910][^cj920][^cj930] This distinction keeps a company milestone from being mistaken for a construction-contract milestone. The sources read here do not publish a complete current depot allocation, a station-by-station staffing roster or a line-wide ridership series, so those fields remain TBC rather than being inferred from the 18-station map.

The operator's FAQ is also the authority for the system description used on the station pages: steel-wheel medium capacity, driverless operation and two-car formations.[^tmrt-faq] Public-art and incident notices are cited only where the operator names a station or a line segment, because a line-wide event cannot safely be assigned to an individual stop.[^tmrt-art][^tmrt-events]

## Capital and leadership

The company's own milestone page states its capital at founding: authorized capital of NT$1 billion against paid-in capital of NT$200 million.[^tmrt-capital] The Taichung City Council later voted, on 25 March 2020, to raise authorized capital to NT$3.5 billion.[^tmrt-capital] Later capital figures reported in secondary news coverage were not independently confirmed against a primary TMRT financial disclosure for this entry and are not published here.

The current chairman is 顏邦傑 (Yan Bangjie), in office from 11 August 2023, holding a master's degree in civil engineering from the University of Texas and prior senior roles as general manager of both Taipei Metro (TRTC) and Kaohsiung Metro (KRTC), and as THSR's deputy general manager.[^taichung-chairman-2023] The city government's own announcement quotes his stated direction for the company: 「未來將強化公司治理基礎，為其他路線營運做好準備，並做到服務生活化、社區化，讓捷運成為民眾生活的延伸」— strengthening the company's corporate-governance foundation, preparing to operate additional lines, and making transit part of everyday community life.[^taichung-chairman-2023]
