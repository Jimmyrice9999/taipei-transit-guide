## Overview

New Taipei Metro Corporation (NTMC) is the operating company for New Taipei's newer light-rail network. Its published operating material covers Danhai and Ankeng together in some fleet and service documents, while the Circular Line has its own operating and engineering history in this guide.[^ntmc-fleet] The Sanying Line's opening and service records are cited separately where they name dates, fares or trains; this operator page does not collapse those line-specific records into one generic timetable.

## Fleet and operating model

NTMC's fleet page records 30 light-rail trains in the shared specification: 15 assigned to Danhai and 15 to Ankeng.[^ntmc-fleet] It describes five cars per train, capacity of 265 passengers including 62 seats, 70 km/h under catenary and 50 km/h on battery operation.[^ntmc-fleet] Because the source presents both lines in one table, these are a shared fleet specification, not evidence that every train is interchangeable across every timetable or depot assignment.

## Service history and evidence boundary

NTMC's Ankeng opening notice records the first service and a one-month free-trial arrangement with stated trial hours and a passenger rebate.[^ntmc-opening] Those launch conditions are historical service evidence; they are not a claim that the same promotion remains available. Current fares, headways, disruption notices and transfer rules belong to the relevant line pages and need their own dated source.

The operator's 2025 release reports more than 2.03 million Ankeng journeys and more than 30.09 million journeys across its three operated lines.[^ntmc-ridership-2025] Those totals are calendar-year measures, not a daily average, capacity figure or forecast. No conversion between journeys and unique riders is made here because the release does not establish that equivalence.[^ntmc-ridership-2025]

## Ownership and leadership

NTMC's own company-profile page states its founding and ownership directly: 「新北大眾捷運股份有限公司為於107年成立之專業捷運營運公司」(established in ROC year 107, 2018) and 「由新北市政府獨資成立」(wholly owned by the New Taipei City Government).[^ntmc-about] The company's organizational-structure page presents its division breakdown only as an embedded chart image and a downloadable PDF/ODT file, neither of which could be read as text for this entry, so a division-by-division breakdown is TBC.[^ntmc-about]

A board reorganisation completed on 16 January 2023 named 林祐賢 (Lin Yu-hsien) as chairman, holding a master's degree from National Central University's Institute of Statistics and over 40 years in public administration, including roles as Taipei County Comptroller and Director of the Executive Yuan's Accounting and Statistics Office.[^ntmc-chairman-2023] The press release quotes his stated direction for the company directly: 「結合在地文化、增加市民認同感、提升整體城市化美學，便民服務並擴大增加業外收入」— integrating local culture, increasing civic pride, elevating urban aesthetics, improving convenient service and expanding non-fare revenue — aiming to make the company 「新北市民的驕傲」(a source of pride for New Taipei residents).[^ntmc-chairman-2023]

NTMC publishes annual settlement statements (決算書) as downloadable PDFs covering every fiscal year from 107 (2018) through 114 (2025); individual filings were not opened for this entry, so year-by-year financial figures remain TBC.[^ntmc-settlements]
