## Overview

Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation (TRTC) is the operating company behind the Taipei Metro. Its own network material describes train formations and service characteristics, while the line pages in this guide keep government construction records separate from the operator's operating record.[^trtc-network] That distinction matters: a company can publish how a railway runs without being the agency that selected the alignment, let the civil contracts or accepted the works.

## Operations and fleet responsibility

TRTC's Wenhu Line introduction describes two pairs coupled as a four-car train, an 80 km/h maximum and approximately 420 passengers at the stated standing-density assumption.[^trtc-network] Those figures belong to that line's published vehicle description; they should not be copied across the high-capacity fleet, whose car lengths, formations and refurbishment histories differ. The rolling-stock pages therefore name their own contracts and technical sources rather than treating “Taipei Metro” as one uniform vehicle family.[^trtc-network]

The operator's vehicle-plant page assigns rolling stock, depot equipment and track-maintenance vehicles by line responsibility. It associates Tamsui–Xinyi work with Vehicle Plant One and Beitou workshop management, for example.[^trtc-duties] That is a maintenance-organisation statement, not a current overnight-stabling inventory: the cited page does not establish how many sets are parked at each depot on a given night.[^trtc-duties] The gap is left explicit rather than inferred from the line map.

## Service patterns and governance boundary

TRTC's published Bannan Line pattern gives a 06:00–24:00 service window, overlapping peak patterns and different one-way run times for the two operating patterns.[^trtc-bannan] The page is useful precisely because it states service as an operator responsibility; it does not turn those Bannan values into a claim about every TRTC line.[^trtc-bannan]

Construction, project approvals and civil-package history remain with the relevant Taipei or New Taipei rapid-transit engineering bureau.[^trtc-network] The operator inherits and runs those assets, and the source family here does not support a single consolidated TRTC procurement history for every line.[^trtc-network][^trtc-duties] Where a contract, incident or opening date is line-specific, it is cited on that line or vehicle page rather than attributed to the corporation in the abstract.[^trtc-network][^trtc-duties]
