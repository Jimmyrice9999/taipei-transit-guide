TRTC operates the Taipei Metro. The distinction that explains half the
network's history is that it does not *build* it: construction belongs to the
city government's Department of Rapid Transit Systems, and the operator
inherits what the builder procures. The [Matra dispute](/rail/history/matra-dispute/)
is what that split looks like when it goes wrong. TRTC also runs the
[gondola](/gondola/) through a recreation subsidiary.

*This is a scope page.* Founding, ownership, subsidiaries, and what the
company publishes where — the operational data this site already cites lives
on its site — are still to be written up properly.
