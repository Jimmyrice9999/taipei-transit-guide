Ridership maps draw one network; corporately there are several. The metro
alone is run by three companies — Taipei's, New Taipei's and Taoyuan's — and
the boundaries between them explain oddities that confuse riders, from
ticketing differences to why one "MRT" line opens at a different hour than
another. TRA and THSR will join this section when the conventional and high
speed rail pages exist for them to operate.

Each operator page states what the company runs, what it does not, and where
its published sources live. Corporate history — founding, ownership,
subsidiaries — is kept to what a primary source supports.
