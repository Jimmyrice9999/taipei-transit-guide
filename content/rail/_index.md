Everything on rails: the Taipei Metro, New Taipei's light rail lines, the
Taoyuan Airport MRT, and — eventually — the conventional TRA network and high
speed rail where they serve the region. The section was renamed from "Train"
because half of that list is not usefully described by the word.

**Covered today:** the Wenhu Line in full — the line, both fleets, both depots,
each of its stations, and the Matra dispute that shaped the system. Other lines,
fleets and depots have entity pages, so that every named thing on this site has
somewhere to link to.
