Everything on rails: the Taipei Metro, New Taipei's light rail lines, the
Taoyuan Airport MRT, and — eventually — the conventional TRA network and high
speed rail where they serve the region. The section was renamed from "Train"
because half of that list is not usefully described by the word.

**Covered today:** the Wenhu Line in full — the line, both fleets, both depots, each of its stations, and the Matra dispute that shaped the system. Other
lines, fleets and depots have entity pages that state their scope and what is
still unverified, so that every named thing on this site has somewhere to
link to.

**Not yet covered, in order of intent:** the Sanying Line (new, and written up ahead of the rest while its opening is still current), the remaining TRTC
lines, New Taipei's light rail, TRA and THSR. Depth follows the Wenhu
pattern: one line finished properly at a time, not all lines thinly.
