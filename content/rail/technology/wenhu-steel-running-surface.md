## The short answer

The Wenhu Line has two running-surface materials. The older Muzha section was
built with ordinary reinforced concrete; the Neihu section was built with a
steel running surface for its rubber-tyred vehicles.[^dorts-neihu-paper] That
is an engineering difference between the two halves of one through line, not a
change from rubber tyres to steel wheels.

The official technical paper links the change to experience with the Muzha
surface in northern Taiwan's rainy, humid climate. It says that the resulting
maintenance difficulty led the Neihu project, after considering overseas
practice, to use the steel-running-surface method.[^dorts-neihu-paper]

## How the surface is made

The paper describes special section steel produced at a steel mill and
mechanically pre-bent in the factory to match the designed curve. The bent
steel receives anti-rust treatment, while the face that directly contacts the
vehicle's rubber tyres receives a traction coating made from paint mixed with
filler.[^dorts-neihu-paper] The coating is there to provide the friction needed
for the vehicle to run.

This is why “steel running surface” should not be read as a conventional
steel-wheel railway. The paper describes the Neihu system as a driverless,
medium-capacity system with rubber-tyred vehicles, and separately describes
the coated steel surface used beneath those tyres.[^dorts-neihu-paper]

The paper presents the benefit conditionally: steel has greater strength and
wear resistance than concrete, and, if corrosion is controlled, the running
quality can be smoother and the operator's maintenance burden can be reduced.[^dorts-neihu-paper]
That is the design rationale recorded by DORTS, not a current independently
measured maintenance result.

## Reported construction quantities

The paper's construction account says that the full 14.8 km Neihu section,
including its underground and elevated parts, was fitted with the steel
running surface.[^dorts-neihu-paper] It reports the following quantities:

These are quantities reported in the 2009 engineering paper's construction
account.[^dorts-neihu-paper] They are not a current inventory, a replacement history, or an
independent as-built audit.[^dorts-neihu-paper]

## What remains open

The paper distinguishes the Muzha and Neihu materials but does not define the
exact physical handoff boundary between them. It also does not give the steel
grade, section dimensions, coating formulation, present condition, current
replacement inventory, or operator-approved maintenance cycle. Those remain
TBC until a design, acceptance, maintenance or inspection record establishes
them.

For the route history and station sequence, see the [Wenhu Line](/rail/metro/lines/wenhu-line/).
