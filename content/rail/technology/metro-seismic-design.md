The sources do not support one timeless “2007 metro seismic code”.[^dorts-seismic-history][^dorts-seismic-considerations][^dorts-underground-seismic][^nlma-current-code] They show an
early Taipei Metro basis, later CEDM revisions, structure-specific underground
methods and a separate national building-code framework.[^dorts-seismic-history][^dorts-seismic-considerations][^dorts-underground-seismic][^nlma-current-code]

## The metro chronology

The Taipei City catalogue records a commissioned Taipei metropolitan MRT seismic
design-code and soil-liquefaction study in ROC year 76 (1987); its public record
does not expose the study file itself.[^taipei-seismic-study] DORTS's structural-
engineering practice page says the earliest metro seismic rule was established
in the same year, using a 475-year return period, 10% exceedance probability and
0.18 g maximum horizontal ground acceleration.[^dorts-seismic-history]

The same DORTS record says the tenth edition of the Civil Engineering Design
Manual (CEDM) raised the maximum horizontal ground acceleration to 0.23 g in ROC
year 87 (1998).[^dorts-seismic-history] A separate DORTS technical page describes
the performance split: under a large earthquake a structure may enter the
inelastic range but must retain ductility and gravity-load capacity, while under
small-to-moderate shaking the structure remains elastic and the metro keeps its
operating function.[^dorts-seismic-considerations]

## The Circular Line date correction

The 0403 earthquake investigation does not date the Circular Line's governing
bridge code to 2007.[^cy-114-circular-code] It identifies the affected viaduct as designed under the
MOTC railway-bridge seismic code issued in December 2006 (ROC year 95), with a
Taipei-basin design ground motion of 0.24 g and an importance factor of 1.2,
which produces a 0.288 g minimum design force.[^cy-114-circular-code]

That source also says the substantially revised 2021 bridge-seismic code still
did not address transverse displacement in the way the investigation considered
necessary; the recorded failure was sideways movement.[^cy-114-circular-code]
The 2007 label in the older systems lead is therefore corrected to 2006, while
the line's detailed failure and responsibility questions remain on the separate
Circular Line earthquake record.[^cy-114-circular-code]

## Underground structures use a different analytical boundary

DORTS's underground-station assessment identifies the agency's CEDC as the main
basis, adds the Taipei acceleration coefficient from the railway-bridge seismic
standard and refers to a Southern California metro underground-structure
standard.[^dorts-underground-seismic] That page prints ODE at 0.15 g and MDE at
0.24 g, with the underground structure assessed for elastic behaviour at those
levels before inelastic behaviour is allowed beyond MDE.[^dorts-underground-seismic]

The tunnel-engineering volume describes the method differently from a simple
free-standing building calculation: underground structures are designed for
forced ground deformation, using free-field soil deformation and soil-structure
interaction. Its cut-and-cover table prints ODE at 0.14 g and MDE at 0.23 g.[^dorts-tunnel-seismic]

Those pairs are reported with their source scopes. The pages do not provide a
single signed, current line-by-line schedule explaining whether the difference
is an edition, structure type or design boundary.[^dorts-underground-seismic][^dorts-tunnel-seismic]

## Comparison with the current national code

DORTS's published station-planning principle uses a 475-year design earthquake
and a 2500-year maximum-considered earthquake.[^dorts-station-planning] The
current National Land Management Agency building code lists revisions in 2006,
2011, 2022 and 2024.[^nlma-current-code]

The current Chapter 1 defines an approximately 30-year small earthquake, a
475-year design earthquake and a 2500-year maximum-considered earthquake.[^nlma-code-principles] It
requires elastic behaviour at the small-earthquake level, limits severe damage
at the design-earthquake level while permitting ductile plastic hinges, and
aims to prevent collapse at the maximum-considered level.[^nlma-code-principles]
It also covers structural members, non-structural components, equipment,
miscellaneous structures, isolation and passive energy-dissipation systems.[^nlma-code-principles]

The comparison therefore establishes a shared hazard-level vocabulary, not a
claim that today's metro structures are governed by the current building code.
DORTS's own records distinguish elevated and ground structures from underground
structures, and the current national page is a building-code source rather than
a current metro CEDC register.[^dorts-seismic-considerations][^dorts-underground-seismic][^nlma-current-code]
