The checked official records describe operator-level control arrangements
rather than a shared metropolitan control room.[^trtc-control-centres][^trtc-organization][^tymc-organization][^ntmc-organization]
They show three operator-level arrangements,
with different levels of detail in what each operator makes public.[^trtc-control-centres][^trtc-organization][^tymc-organization][^ntmc-organization]

## TRTC: two published physical centres, with current control groups

TRTC’s published 2014 arrangement identifies two control centres: one in the
Jiojiu Control Building and one at Neihu Depot.[^trtc-control-centres] The report
describes them as the operating-management core, responsible for train
dispatch and monitoring, emergency handling and response, and collecting and
passing on information.[^trtc-control-centres] It also says that different
control positions work rotating shifts so operating decisions can be
coordinated and abnormal events reported quickly.[^trtc-control-centres]

The current TRTC responsibilities page does not redraw that older physical
map, but it still identifies a control-centre organization covering Operations
Groups 1 and 2, High-capacity Control and Wenhu Control.[^trtc-organization] Its
listed duties include supervising system dispatch and monitoring, timetables,
access control, incident handling, dispatch strategy, abnormal-event review,
training and exercises.[^trtc-organization] This supports a distinction between
the physical-centre record and the current organizational grouping; it does not
justify assigning every present-day line to a centre from the 2014 table.[^trtc-control-centres][^trtc-organization]

## TYMC: one named control centre for each line

Taoyuan Metro’s current organization page names an Airport Line Control Centre
and a Green Line Control Centre as separate units within the Operations
Division.[^tymc-organization] Each is described as responsible for its line’s
condition, train dispatch and monitoring, equipment monitoring, abnormal or
emergency handling, and coordination with maintenance staff over when and how
work is carried out.[^tymc-organization] The page therefore gives a clear
line-to-control-centre organizational boundary, but it does not state the
building address, room redundancy or whether the two centres share a backup
room.[^tymc-organization]

## NTMC: the function is published, the topology is not

New Taipei Metro’s current organization chart separates Light Rail Operations
from Medium-capacity Operations and assigns each the operating responsibilities
for its system family.[^ntmc-organization] A separate operator safety release
confirms that the control centre uses CCTV to monitor the entire car fleet and
that, during a security incident, it directs trains toward an evacuation
station, prepares that station, and coordinates police, security and medical
support.[^ntmc-safety]

Those records establish the control function and the organizational split, but
they do not disclose whether the Circular and Sanying systems share one room,
whether the light-rail routes share another, or where a backup control room is
located.[^ntmc-organization][^ntmc-safety] A line-specific or physical topology
should therefore remain TBC rather than being inferred from the depot names,
the operator headquarters or the current organization chart.[^ntmc-organization]

## What remains open

The checked primary records do not provide a current cross-operator control
map, a complete line-by-line assignment for TRTC’s two-centre arrangement,
control-room staffing or shift tables for all three operators, or a documented
cross-operator fallback protocol.[^trtc-control-centres][^trtc-organization][^tymc-organization][^ntmc-organization][^ntmc-safety] Those are operational
records, not details that can be reconstructed safely from station locations or
rolling-stock suppliers.
