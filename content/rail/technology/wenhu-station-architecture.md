The Wenhu Line does not have one station style repeated twenty-four times. Its
visual change is deliberate: the Muzha section and the Neihu section were
designed around different briefs, and the builder describes both rather than
calling the difference an accident.[^dorts-architecture]

## Muzha: lightness under constraint

The original Muzha section is elevated and passes through the city on a route
whose sites are mostly in existing roads. DORTS says that the urban setting
made the design form highly constrained, while the single-column support system
limited the load available for decoration.[^dorts-architecture] The stations
therefore use steel and glass with simple forms, positioned as an expression of
modern urban architecture, and their volumes are kept as far as possible from
roadside buildings.[^dorts-architecture]

That is a useful explanation of the line's familiar visual economy. The
minimalism is not presented by the builder as a claim that decoration was
unwanted; it is a response to the structural and urban conditions of an
elevated medium-capacity railway. The same design record identifies the
Muzha-section station forms as either cantilevered stations supported by one
column or ordinary beam-and-column structures, depending on the site
conditions.[^dorts-architecture-book]

## Neihu: three architectural dialects

The Neihu section extends the Muzha system, but its design brief starts with
the character of the places it crosses. DORTS divides its station language into
three vocabularies: **住宅城鄉風貌**, a residential and urban-rural character;
**商業休閒環境**, a commercial and leisure environment; and
**工業科技景觀**, an industrial and technological landscape.[^dorts-architecture]
The viaduct is then used to connect those different settings into a single
idea, **空中之河**, or “river in the sky”, under the overall theme
**湖城故事**, “Lake City Story”.[^dorts-architecture][^dorts-architecture-book]

The result is not a claim that every station is unique.[^dorts-architecture]
DORTS says the Neihu
stations begin from a common architectural vocabulary and element set, then
adjust them to the surrounding conditions.[^dorts-architecture] Public-art
thinking supplies station subthemes and colour plans, while simple geometric
forms are intended to ease maintenance and preserve the image of rapid
transport.[^dorts-architecture] The source specifically names Jiannan Road,
Dahu Park and Nangang Exhibition Center as stations given forms distinct from
the standard station type because of their local character.[^dorts-architecture]

The materials make the same argument physically. DORTS describes bright,
simple steel structures with painted coloured metal panels, enamelled steel
panels and large areas of glass, chosen to make the station volumes feel light
and visually permeable.[^dorts-architecture] The technical-book account adds
that architecture, landscape and public art were considered together, including
colour changes in ceilings and columns, wall materials and patterns, and paving
used for orientation and information.[^dorts-architecture-book]

## The bridge and the space beneath it

The official design record says approximately ten kilometres of this section's
viaduct are mostly above the central islands of roads and use prestressed
concrete U-girders.[^dorts-architecture] Sound-control walls vary in height with
the conditions along the route, to reduce disturbance to nearby residents while
responding to the surrounding environment.[^dorts-architecture]

The underside received its own landscape treatment. DORTS says the space below
the stations gets too little sun for good plant growth, so the design uses a
dry-landscape interpretation with rocks and pebbles of different sizes and
colours, together with added lighting for a night-time urban effect.[^dorts-architecture]
The “river in the sky” therefore refers to more than the train's elevated path:
the official concept joins the viaduct, station envelope, landscape and public
art into one line-wide reading.[^dorts-architecture][^dorts-architecture-book]

## Which stations are underground?

DORTS's route record gives the Neihu section as 14.8 km, with 3.9 km of
underground running and two underground stations.[^dorts-wenhu] Its station
architecture record names Dazhi as underground and counts ten other Neihu
stations as elevated; read with the route record's station list and its two
station total, that identifies BR13 Songshan Airport and BR14 Dazhi as the
line's underground pair.[^dorts-wenhu][^dorts-architecture]

The official pages do not provide a station-by-station list of every subtheme,
the architect of each station, or a current survey of alterations to the
original materials.[^dorts-architecture][^dorts-wenhu] This article therefore describes the builder's line-level
design logic and leaves those more granular questions TBC.[^dorts-architecture]
