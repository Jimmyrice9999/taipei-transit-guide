## What's on the train

Car 7 of every 700T trainset carries four wheelchair spaces — seats 13A and
14A for powered wheelchairs, with charging outlets, and 13E and 14E for
folding wheelchairs.[^thsr-accessibility-page] Seats 13A/14A rotate 90
degrees, letting a passenger transfer to the seat or stay in a fixed
wheelchair, which the operator states is meant to ease a companion's
assistance.[^thsr-car-services] The same car has an accessible restroom with
a vacuum toilet, sink, hand dryer, movable grab bars, two emergency call
buttons, an infant seat and a diaper-changing table.[^thsr-car-services] Car
5 carries a separate nursing room; every station has one as
well.[^thsr-car-services][^thsr-facilities-overview]

The incoming N700ST fleet increases this to six wheelchair spaces per
trainset, with fixed wheelchair devices, and an enlarged nursing
room.[^thsr-n700st-naming] Secondary reporting adds that the accessible
restroom will gain an ostomy-cleaning facility, a changing platform and a
bidet toilet, though this specific detail was not independently confirmed
against the primary naming press release's own wording.

## Booking and boarding

A wheelchair space can be reserved up to 29 days ahead (including the
travel day) and as late as one hour before departure, through a digital
chatbot, the phone customer-service line (6am–midnight), or a station
counter; a travelling companion can be booked into an adjoining seat at the
same time.[^thsr-accessibility-page] Since a 2023 change, accessible-seat
bookings made digitally or by phone can be paid for and collected via the
T-EX mobile app, partner convenience stores, or station ticket machines —
previously, collection was limited to station windows
only.[^thsr-ticket-collection-2023] Powered mobility devices are accepted up
to 125cm long, 90cm wide and 185cm tall.[^thsr-accessibility-page]

A separate boarding-guidance service escorts elderly-alone or
mobility-limited passengers from the non-paid station area to the platform,
and back to a connecting transport point on arrival — though not luggage
handling or wheelchair assembly and disassembly. It must be requested at
least an hour before departure, and the passenger must arrive twenty minutes
early to meet the guide.[^thsr-boarding-guidance] Whether this escort
service itself carries a charge is not stated in what was found and is not
published here either way.

## Fares and animals

Disability-certificate holders qualify for a discount fare at 50% of full
price; a "necessary companion" qualifies for the same discount only when the
certificate's reverse carries a specific public-transport
annotation.[^thsr-ticket-types-2] THSR generally requires small pets to
travel in an enclosed, odourless container no larger than 55×45×38cm, but
working guide dogs, hearing dogs, mobility-assistance dogs, and guide-dog
puppies in professional training are explicitly exempted from that
rule.[^thsr-passenger-rules]

## A 2018 campaign, and what it asked for

In October 2018, disability advocacy groups launched a campaign — "推它一把，
大夥一起上！" — against the then-reported ratio of four wheelchair spaces
against a 989-seat trainset, describing wheelchair capacity as roughly 0.4%
of total seating.[^npost-2018-protest] Their demands included letting
wheelchair users (including those able to transfer) buy unreserved tickets
in any car with added multi-purpose priority seating, stricter identity
checks on priority seats, more fixed wheelchair spaces in Car 7, and
CRPD-compliant wheelchair spaces in all newly purchased cars including
business class.[^npost-2018-protest] THSR's reported response cited a 34.8%
utilisation rate for existing wheelchair spaces and a favourable comparison
to peer high-speed operators offering only two spaces per
trainset.[^npost-2018-protest] The N700ST fleet's move from four spaces to
six, seven years later, is consistent with — though not stated anywhere as a
direct response to — that campaign's core demand.

## Remaining gaps

THSR's facilities pages describe accessibility as company-wide policy —
"每站均有" — rather than an itemised, station-by-station breakdown for any of
the 12 stations; not found in this research. Whether priority seating exists outside Car
7, one of the 2018 campaign's explicit demands, is not established either
way. No Control Yuan investigation or lawsuit naming THSR specifically on
accessibility grounds was found, though the search that would surface one is
not exhaustive.
