Station names have two different kinds of official record: a naming action, and
a later page that remembers or uses a name. DORTS says that naming, renaming and
appended names are meant to follow a defined procedure established in 2007.[^dorts-naming-guideline]

## Former names recorded by DORTS

| Station | Former name | What the primary page establishes |
|---|---|---|
| BL07 | 漢生路站 | DORTS calls current 板橋站 「原名漢生路站」; the action date and instrument are TBC.[^dorts-banqiao-former-name] |
| O12 | 大橋國小站 | DORTS calls 大橋頭站 「原稱大橋國小站」 and records the later change; the action date and instrument are TBC.[^dorts-daqiaotou-former-name] |
| R18 | 天母站 | DORTS describes 明德 as 「原名天母站」; the action date and instrument are TBC.[^dorts-mingde-former-name] |

These former-name records are not inferred from slash notation or from a
planning code; each appears as a former-name statement on a fetched official
page.[^dorts-banqiao-former-name][^dorts-daqiaotou-former-name][^dorts-mingde-former-name]

## Formal rename and appended name

The 9 June 2020 New Taipei gazette says LB03站：挖子，更名為「長壽山」.[^ntpc-sanying-naming-gazette]
The current New Taipei DORTS route page uses **LB03 長壽山**.[^ntmc-sanying-route]

The same gazette says LB05站：龍埔，加註「三峽廣行宮」; this is an appended
name, not a rename of 龍埔.[^ntpc-sanying-naming-gazette] The current route page
preserves that distinction as **LB05 龍埔（加註名稱：三峽廣行宮）**.[^ntmc-sanying-route]

## Planning labels and current names

DORTS’s Circular Line planning page calls Y16 「民生新埔站」, while the current
operator station list publishes Y17 as **新埔民生**.[^dorts-circular-planning-name][^ntmc-circular-stations]
No naming action was found for this order change, so it remains a published
label conflict rather than a claimed rename.

Daqiaotou’s addition is a separate history: DORTS says it was not in the
original Executive Yuan-approved Xinzhuang Line station list, but was added
after local petitions and approval to use Daqiao Elementary land.[^dorts-daqiaotou-added]

## Gaps

The gazette/order dates for the BL07, O12 and R18 former names remain TBC, as do
the official instruments behind the failed leads recorded in the research
report. Current station lists and engineering labels can establish what a
source called a station; they cannot, by themselves, establish when a formal
rename occurred.[^dorts-naming-guideline]
