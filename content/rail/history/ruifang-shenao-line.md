## One operator table, two opening years

Taiwan Railways' full construction-history table lists Ruifang–Shen'ao as a
6.3 km route, constructed in 1959 and put into operation in 1961, with TRA
listed as the constructor.[^tra-construction-history] The same table gives
Shen'ao–Liandong as a separate 6.0 km entry; that southern historical section
is documented on the [Shen'ao–Liandong page](/rail/history/shenao-line/).[^tra-construction-history]

The National Museum of Marine Science and Technology's full institutional
history instead says that the Taiwan Railways Administration opened the
Ruifang–Shen'ao section in 1965.[^nmmst-shenao-history] That account may be
describing the line's public-service or through-connection milestone rather
than the operator table's construction and operation-start fields, but the
checked sources do not prove that interpretation. Both years remain visible.

## Passenger, freight and coal traffic did not end at the same boundary

The museum history dates the Shen'ao Line's passenger and freight stoppage to
21 August 1989 and says that only the Ruifang–Shen'ao Power Plant section
remained for coal trains.[^nmmst-shenao-history] It then dates the power
plant's stop and the line's full suspension to 2007.[^nmmst-shenao-history]
These are traffic milestones from a later institutional history, not a legal
closure record.

## Passenger service returned on a shorter surviving route

The operator's full restoration note records passenger service returning between
Ruifang and Haikeguan on 9 January 2014, approximately 4.2 km with ten daily
round trips.[^tra-revival-note] TRA's chronology records the formal reopening
ceremony on that same date.[^tra-timeline-103]

On 28 December 2016, the operator recorded Badouzi station's reopening and
described the Haikeguan–Badouzi extension as a 4.7 km Shen'ao branch.[^tra-timeline-105]
The current TDX snapshot contains three ordered SA station members and a final
travelled-distance value of 4.7 km.[^tdx-tra-line] The current passenger route
is therefore kept as a later service boundary rather than substituted for the
operator table's historical 6.3 km entry.[^tra-construction-history][^tdx-tra-line]

The 2014 and 2016 operator records describe restoration and extension of the
surviving route; they do not establish that the dismantled southern
Shen'ao–Liandong section was rebuilt.[^tra-revival-note][^tra-timeline-105][^tra-construction-history]

## Records still missing

The checked sources do not provide a complete historical station register, a
legal instrument for the 1989 passenger/freight stop or 2007 full suspension,
a segment-by-segment removal chronology, or a surveyed boundary between the
6.3 km construction-table route and the later 4.2/4.7 km passenger-service
measurements.[^tra-construction-history][^nmmst-shenao-history][^tra-revival-note][^tdx-tra-line]
The operator's construction, restoration and current-data records therefore
remain distinct from the museum's alternate historical narrative.
