## What the investigation says

At 07:58 on 3 April 2024, a magnitude 7.2 earthquake in Hualien damaged the
pot bearings connecting the Circular Line's steel box girders to their piers.[^cy-114]
The Control Yuan's investigation records eleven girder-displacement locations,
track deformation and 139 damaged equipment items; its account says no train
was on the damaged section when the earthquake struck.[^cy-114] New Taipei DORTS
reported a displacement range of about 3.5 to 92 cm, with seven of the eleven
locations in the 20–92 cm severe group.[^ntpc-damage]

The line's operator describes the repair in a different unit: nine steel-box-
girder spans were repositioned, and the full line resumed service at noon on 12
December 2024.[^ntmc-reopening] The two counts are not silently merged. Eleven
locations and nine spans may be related, but the sources read here do not publish
a damage schedule mapping one to the other.

## The pot-bearing mechanism

The Control Yuan's first finding concerns the grade-adjustment plate
(<span lang="zh-Hant">調坡板</span>) between the girder and the pot bearing.[^cy-114] It
says the contractor, BES Engineering, milled through the plate instead of
building to the construction drawings. That left excess space above the
locating pin; during the earthquake the pin jumped upward, its base left the
interface between the upper and lower bearing plates, and the bearing lost its
shear resistance.[^cy-114]

The report's dimensions make the proposed mechanism checkable:

| Measurement | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Recess shown on the shop drawing | 5 mm[^cy-114] |
| Recess measured in the field | 41 mm[^cy-114] |
| Space left above the locating pin | 36 mm[^cy-114] |
| Locating-pin height | 40 mm[^cy-114] |

The report explains the arithmetic as 36 mm of excess space plus the required
5 mm of engagement, for a 41 mm recess around a pin only 40 mm high.[^cy-114]
After the damaged bridges were jacked up, the New Taipei City Civil Engineers
Association found pins lodged in the bearing's upper plate or fallen onto the
pier cap.[^cy-114]

## More than a construction finding

The investigation does not describe the earthquake damage as a contractor-only
story.[^cy-114] It says the structure was Taiwan's first stacked viaduct of this type,
with climbing and descending sections and tree-form piers, but that no higher
design standard or displacement-limiting devices were added and no external
structural review was performed.[^cy-114] It also records an error in the
detailed design calculation that double-deducted dead load when estimating the
bearing's uplift demand, and a pot-bearing drawing whose pin dimensions conflict
between its note, table and the pins found on site.[^cy-114]

The report's third finding is about inspection. The grade-adjustment plate was a
contractor-designed, factory-assembled secondary component that became hidden
when the girder was installed, so it was not an inspection hold point. The
Control Yuan says its review of other listed elevated-bridge and railway
projects found the same component absent from their inspection checkpoints. It
records Taipei DORTS adding a pot-bearing inspection flow chart and sampling
standard on 13 December 2024.[^cy-114]

The monitoring finding is separate but important. Zhongyuan station had
triaxial accelerometers at both ends of the platform level, but the Control Yuan
says their signals were not relayed to the control centre. It found no record of
seismic data from those instruments in the Circular Line control centre between
the line's opening and the investigation.[^cy-114]

## The shaking and the code

The report says the affected viaduct was designed under the 2006 railway-bridge
seismic code with a Taipei-basin design ground motion of 0.24 g and an importance
factor of 1.2, producing a minimum design force of 0.288 g.[^cy-114] At the
nearby 積穗國小 station, the report records a peak horizontal acceleration of
0.229 g, below that design value, and a peak ground velocity of 32.16 cm/s.[^cy-114]

The Control Yuan's comparison is between seismic microzones, not between metro
lines. It says the DF112 range around Zhonghe and Yonghe saw horizontal peak
ground acceleration about twice that in adjacent zones and peak ground velocity
about fifteen times higher, while the neighbouring DF111 and DF113 ranges had no
bridge damage in this earthquake.[^cy-114] It separately finds that the 2021
revision of the national bridge-seismic code still did not address transverse,
sideways displacement in the way the investigation considered necessary.[^cy-114]

## What is disputed

BES's own statement says the damaged section was between its Y12 Qiaohe and Y14
Banxin engineering labels and that the contractor followed the approved
drawings.[^bes-statement] BES says the upper and lower bearing arrangements both
had milled-through slope plates but only the upper support failed; it therefore
argues that the milling-through detail and bearing failure were not the main
cause, and that the design strength and unequal seismic demand require further
examination.[^bes-statement]

That position conflicts with the Control Yuan's finding that the work did not
follow the drawings and that the pin's loss of the shear interface was the
failure mechanism.[^cy-114] The sources on this page establish the competing
positions, not a final court determination. The full court merits judgment,
final settlement and current responsibility allocation remain TBC.

The repair itself added permanent unseating-prevention and uplift-restraint
devices as a second line of defence, while replacing the pot-bearing facilities
and restoring track, cables and other systems.[^ntmc-reopening] That is the
published remedy; it does not by itself decide which party's explanation of the
original cause will prevail.
