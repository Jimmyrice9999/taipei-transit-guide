## Flooding: Typhoon Nari

The primary records agree on the scale of the September 2001 disaster: DORTS
technical material records flooding at 16 underground stations, one depot and
one high-capacity control centre, followed by three months of repairs and full
service on 15 December.[^dorts-flood-journal-214] Another DORTS volume says the
control centre flooded to approximately 4.7 metres and that its train-control
computers were badly damaged.[^dorts-flood-technical-182]

DORTS names the Taipei Main Station–Taiwan Railways shared underground area,
the Zhongxiao Dunhua–department-store interface and Kunyang station entrances
as examples of openings through which water entered.[^dorts-flood-technical-87]
The same engineering volume records later flood gates, watertight doors and
removable barriers, including a design criterion based on the recorded Nari
water level plus 20 centimetres.[^dorts-flood-technical-88]

The Control Yuan's correction adds an accountability record: it found flood
preparation and drills deficient and identified defects in the Zhongxiao Fuxing
station Y-shaped ventilation-shaft partition and the shared Taipei Main Station
underground interfaces involving DORTS, TRTC and Taiwan Railways.[^control-yuan-nari]

## Derailment: Xiaonanmen

At 04:00 on 13 September 2016, an engineering vehicle at the Xiaonanmen station
platform toward Songshan had a wheel leave the rail and could not move.[^dorts-derailment-52]
The response ran the Xiaonanmen–Zhongshan section single-track in both directions
for 11 hours 39 minutes and used shuttle buses for the interrupted sections.[^dorts-derailment-52]
The fetched primary does not establish the exact restoration clock, the vehicle
type or the mechanical cause, so those details remain TBC.

## Signals, smoke and fire

TRTC records a 2025 Wenhu Line signalling anomaly between Zhongshan Junior High
School and Wende.[^trtc-wenhu-signal-2025] The line continued operating, with longer waits; the release
attributes the immediate recovery to restarting a delayed signalling-network
transmission system and says the detailed cause was still to be clarified with
the original manufacturer.[^trtc-wenhu-signal-2025]

On 11 March 2022, sparks and smoke appeared in the track between Chiang Kai-shek
Memorial Hall and NTU Hospital.[^trtc-rail-sparks-2022] Trains passed slowly, a driver went down to
extinguish the fire, and TRTC later replaced the failed insulation-baseplate
assembly and embedded sleeve.[^trtc-rail-sparks-2022]

On 28 December 2025, a fire alarm at the main substation outside Zhongxiao
Fuxing was traced to an abnormal main transformer.[^trtc-transformer-fire-2025] Power transfer restored train
operation, nobody was injured, and some nearby lifts and escalators were
temporarily affected.[^trtc-transformer-fire-2025]

TRTC's live operations table, fetched on 17 August 2026, also records a BR10
signalling fault, an O07 signalling fault, passenger power-bank smoke/fire at
O11, third-rail cable smoke at BL07 and platform-door automatic-opening failure
at BL15.[^trtc-live-operations] The table gives the local response and service effect for each row; its
live nature means these entries should be re-fetched before being treated as a
historical register.[^trtc-live-operations]

The DORTS Wenhu project record supplies an older primary description without a
station or exact date: during 1993 system testing, the Muzha Line had a tyre-
ignition accident.[^dorts-wenhu-incident] The station, unit, time and relationship to the two fires
described in secondary histories remain TBC.[^dorts-wenhu-incident]

## Earthquake: Circular and Ankeng lines

The 3 April 2024 earthquake displaced eleven Circular Line girder locations and
closed the damaged section.[^cy-circular-earthquake][^ntpc-circular-damage] The Control Yuan investigation attributes the
failure to construction and inspection problems around the bearing assembly,
while New Taipei DORTS records seven severe displacements of 20–92 centimetres
and four smaller displacements.[^cy-circular-earthquake][^ntpc-circular-damage]

NTMC recorded full Circular Line suspension on the day, followed by single-track
bidirectional operation between Banqiao and New Taipei Industrial Park from
17:00.[^ntmc-circular-0403] Full service resumed at noon on 12 December 2024;
the operator's repair inventory says nine steel-box-girder spans were
repositioned, alongside track-baseplate, cable and equipment replacement.[^ntmc-circular-reopening]

The same earthquake temporarily changed Ankeng LRT service: NTMC recorded K09–K06
single-track bidirectional working and K06–K01 partial operation, followed by
normal service at 16:30 with a 15-minute headway.[^ntmc-ankeng-0403][^ntmc-circular-0403]

## Conflicts and gaps

DORTS technical-book no. 29 p. 87 labels Nari 「民國90年9月6日」, while DORTS
technical journal no. 46 p. 214 gives 「民國90年9月17日」.[^dorts-flood-technical-87][^dorts-flood-journal-214] Both dates are published
here; no fetched primary reconciles them.[^dorts-flood-technical-87][^dorts-flood-journal-214]

The Control Yuan and New Taipei DORTS count eleven Circular Line displacement
locations, while NTMC's reopening release counts nine spans repositioned. The
sources appear to count different things, but no fetched primary explicitly
maps eleven locations to nine spans, so this page does not choose between them.[^cy-circular-earthquake][^ntpc-circular-damage][^ntmc-circular-reopening]
