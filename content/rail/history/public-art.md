This catalogue records works named by the full public-art inventories and
station records fetched from DORTS, NTMC and TYMC. The station code is included
where the checked source supports it; a line-wide range is used when the work
is explicitly spread across stations. `TBC` means the fetched primary page did
not publish that field, not that the work is absent.[^dorts-general-public-art]

The two DORTS tables are not interchangeable. The general table records the

## Conflicts and gaps

Xiaobitan’s work has three different primary-source figures. The general DORTS
table gives NT$1,000,000; the dedicated DORTS work record gives approximately
NT$35,900,000 plus a NT$1,000,000 first prize; and the DORTS technical book says
the project was constrained by a NT$15,000,000 budget limit before the interior
works were substantially reduced.[^dorts-general-public-art][^dorts-xiaobitan-public-art][^dorts-xiaobitan-technical-book]
The sources describe different scopes or stages, but do not reconcile them, so
all three values remain published.

The Sanying sources also disagree. The 2025 announcement assigns 蘆花街燈 to
LB08 and 時光行旅 to LB10.[^dorts-sanying-2025-art] The 2026 announcement omits
the LB08 label, assigns 時光行旅 and 陶鄉 to LB10, and calls the programme 12
works.[^dorts-sanying-2026-art] The full LB08 art record instead names 陶鄉 at
LB08. The table preserves each value rather than
choosing a station allocation.[^dorts-sanying-2025-art][^dorts-sanying-2026-art][^dorts-sanying-lb08-art]

The Daqiaotou page establishes eight platform-wall paintings but not the
individual catalogue fields. The Airport MRT and Danhai operator pages likewise
name works while leaving most catalogue fields unpublished. Those fields stay
TBC; an unlisted station is not treated as proof that no work exists.[^dorts-o12-architecture][^tymc-airport-public-art][^ntmc-danhai-public-art]
