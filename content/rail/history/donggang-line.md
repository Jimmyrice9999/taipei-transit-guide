## A wartime naval branch, not a commuter line

The Donggang Line, 東港線, broke ground on 17 March 1938 and opened for
passenger service on 19 July 1940, running 6.2 km from 社邊 station — today's
鎮安 (Chen'an) station on the Pingtung Line — through 大鵬 (Dapeng) to
Donggang, on narrow (1,067 mm), single, non-electrified track.[^ntl-donggang-magazine]
Funding traces to a 1936 Imperial Diet budget that bundled it with the
Xizhou–Fangliao Pingtung Line extension.[^ntl-donggang-magazine] A
military-only spur beyond Dapeng ran into the 東港海軍航空隊 (Donggang Naval
Air Corps) seaplane base at Dapeng Bay; trains reversed tail-first into it, so
it was nicknamed 「倒吞線」.[^ntl-donggang-magazine] Donggang station itself was
sited away from the town centre for military rather than commercial
convenience — a siting choice the source describes as having limited the
line's effect on local commerce — and the base and line were heavily bombed by
Allied forces from October 1944.[^ntl-donggang-magazine]

This account rests on a single Academia Historica (國史館) magazine article,
treated here as secondary rather than primary: it is a government
research institute's historical account citing period documents, not the
operator's own record.[^ntl-donggang-magazine] TRA's own construction-history
record, used as the primary source for every other closed-line page on this
site, could not be read for this line — see Remaining gaps.

## Why it closed, and when

Every source found gives the same reason for closure: after the 雙園大橋
(Shuangyuan Bridge, linking Kaohsiung's Linyuan and Pingtung's Xinyuan) opened
in the 1980s, road travel to Kaohsiung became practical and ridership
collapsed to an average of about 60 passengers a day in the closure
year, and the line was shut "on grounds of deficit."[^ntl-donggang-magazine]
No source found connects the 1991 closure to South Link Line electrification
or route changes.

The exact last day of passenger service is not settled. Academia Historica
dates the last passenger train to 28 February 1991;[^ntl-donggang-magazine]
zh.wikipedia's account gives 1 March 1991 as when passenger service
ended.[^zhwiki-donggang-line] Both are published here rather than
one being chosen. After passenger service ended, the line carried sporadic
military freight to the Air Force base that remained at Dapeng Bay until the
base withdrew in July 2002, which Academia Historica treats as the point the
line's history actually ended.[^ntl-donggang-magazine] zh.wikipedia gives a
specific date within that month, 1 July 2002, that the Academia Historica
account does not corroborate.[^zhwiki-donggang-line]

## What remains, and what replaced it

Within the Dapeng Bay National Scenic Area, a former military-transport
platform and an interpretive sign are preserved as the only formal remnant of
the line, alongside a short surviving stretch of track.[^ntl-donggang-magazine]
The former Donggang station site itself was redeveloped: a 2003 land-use
change let Donggang Township Office build a combined office and
representative-assembly building there, and a separate Donggang Transit
Center then broke ground on the same site on 19 May 2022 — funded NT$56.5
million by the Directorate General of Highways and NT$29.5 million by the
county government — and opened 7 January 2024.[^ltn-donggang-station-redevelopment][^ltn-donggang-transit-center]

A revival was seriously studied once. In 2016 the Ministry of Transportation
and Communications approved NT$8.5 million for an eight-month feasibility
study into reopening the line as the "Donggang Tourism Railway," rerouted to
run directly into Dapeng Bay rather than along the original
alignment.[^ltn-donggang-revival-study] The plan was cancelled by 2019: local
opposition continued, and the final feasibility assessment found insufficient
economic benefit; reported local objections included the original alignment's
low efficiency, its level-crossing accident history, flood-prone crossings,
and a route through Chaozhou and Pingtung City seen as unattractive to
tourists from northern Taiwan.[^ltn-donggang-revival-cancelled] Local
preference was reported as shifting toward a Kaohsiung MRT extension south
instead, a separate proposal this page does not otherwise cover.[^ltn-donggang-revival-cancelled]
