## Two official descriptions of one corridor

Taiwan Railways' full construction-history table lists Tanzi–Shengang as an
8.7 km route, constructed in 1956 and put into operation in 1957, with TRA
listed as the constructor.[^tra-construction-history] That is the operator's
route record and is kept as such.

Taichung's district and tourism records use a different administrative
description. They call the predecessor a former military logistics railway or
military Shengang branch, not a TRA route.[^tanzi-tanyashen][^shengang-tanyashen][^taichung-tourism-tanyashen]
The urban-plan record instead calls the disused corridor the former
longitudinal railway's Taichung branch.[^shengang-urban-plan] The checked
records do not reconcile those identities, so this page does not silently
choose one.

## Discontinuation and greenway reuse

The Tanzi District Office dates the military logistics railway's discontinuation
to ROC year 87, or 1998.[^tanzi-tanyashen] The Shengang District Office says
the Tanyashen Greenway opened on 2 May 2004 and records that the Ministry of
Defense agreed in May 2000 to let the former railway serve as an external access
route, after which the county government promoted conversion to a bicycle
facility.[^shengang-tanyashen]

The current municipal route is described as approximately 14 km, starting at
Tanzi Zhongshan Road and passing through the Fengyuan, Daya and Shengang areas
to the Zhongke hinterland in Daya's Zhongyi Village.[^tanzi-tanyashen][^shengang-tanyashen]
The tourism bureau gives the same broad route and identifies the former military
railway Shengang branch as the greenway's basis.[^taichung-tourism-tanyashen]

That roughly 14 km greenway figure is not substituted for TRA's 8.7 km route
record.[^tra-construction-history][^tanzi-tanyashen] The sources describe a
municipal reuse corridor and an operator-table route with different stated
scopes.[^tra-construction-history][^tanzi-tanyashen]

## A route with unresolved ownership history

The identity conflict matters because it changes what a closure date means. The
municipal pages establish a 1998 discontinuation of the military logistics
railway and a 2004 greenway opening, while the operator table establishes only
the 1956 construction and 1957 operation-start fields for Tanzi–Shengang.[^tanzi-tanyashen][^shengang-tanyashen][^tra-construction-history]
The urban plan confirms that the former railway corridor was no longer operating
and had been assigned to Tanyashen Greenway use, but does not supply a legal
closure instrument or an operator-to-military transfer record.[^shengang-urban-plan]

The former Peace Station is recorded by the Shengang District Office as a
military-materials railway facility that was not open to the public before the
conversion process.[^shengang-tanyashen] It is a documented greenway heritage
point, not evidence by itself of a complete public passenger station register.

## Records still missing

The checked sources do not provide a complete historical station register, a
legal closure or transfer instrument, a segment-by-segment track-removal
chronology, or a surveyed comparison between the 8.7 km operator route and the
approximately 14 km greenway.[^tra-construction-history][^tanzi-tanyashen][^shengang-tanyashen][^shengang-urban-plan]
Until those records are found, the operator's route entry and the municipal
military-railway and greenway account remain visible as separate, conflicting
descriptions.
