## A freight branch replacing the Xindian coal route

The Taiwan Railways construction-history table records a 6.5 km
Banqiao–Zhonghe route.[^tra-construction-history] New Taipei's Department of
Rapid Transit Systems describes it as the Taiwan Railways Zhonghe Line and says
the full line began operating on 5 March 1965, for freight only.[^dorts-zhonghe-history]
The municipal account connects its creation to the need to move coal produced in
the Zhonghe area.[^dorts-zhonghe-history]

The opening date sits in the same transition that ended the Xindian Line's
freight role. Zhonghe District Office's chronology records the 5 March 1965
opening as a replacement for the Xindian Line's coal transport function and
identifies Zhonghe as a third-class station.[^zhonghe-office-history] That is a
description of the historical service and station classification, not a current
TRA station identity.[^zhonghe-office-history]

## A route through a changing city

The checked DORTS route account places the line's beginning roughly one
kilometre north of the old Banqiao station, where it turned southeast and then
ran through what are now Banxin Road, Bannan Road and Heping Street before
reaching Zhonghe station.[^dorts-zhonghe-route] The route description is a
corridor account; it does not provide a complete period station register or a
survey of the former right-of-way.[^dorts-zhonghe-route]

The line's reason for existing weakened as coal demand declined and urban
development increased. DORTS says the line stopped operating and was dismantled
in 1990 in response to those changes and the construction of the Taipei Metro
Zhonghe Line.[^dorts-zhonghe-history] The same source says the former Zhonghe
station site became the modern Nanshijiao station and Zhonghe depot, while parts
of the former corridor later carried the Circular Line.[^dorts-zhonghe-history]
Those are later transport uses in the corridor, not evidence that the modern
metro is the same railway system.[^dorts-zhonghe-history]

## Service stop, road conversion and physical dismantling

The district-office chronology supplies a more precise service boundary: it says
the Banqiao–Zhonghe railway stopped operating from 23 September 1990 and that
the route was then converted into Bannan Road toward Banqiao.[^zhonghe-office-history]
That date is therefore kept as the exact service-stop record from that source.
It is not substituted for the operator's later physical-dismantling entry.

The TRA construction-history table says the line was dismantled in September
1993.[^tra-construction-history] The municipal sources instead use 1990 for the
operating stop and dismantling account, so the chronology is reported in layers:
service stopped in September 1990 according to the district chronology, a
municipal history describes closure and dismantling around 1990, and the
operator's construction table records physical dismantling in September 1993.[^dorts-zhonghe-history][^dorts-zhonghe-route][^zhonghe-office-history][^tra-construction-history]

## Records still missing

The checked operator and municipal pages do not provide a complete former-station
list, a legal closure instrument, a track-removal schedule or a surveyed boundary
between railway land and the later road and metro works.[^tra-construction-history][^dorts-zhonghe-history][^dorts-zhonghe-route][^zhonghe-office-history]
The route corridor and later transport reuse can be described, but the page does
not infer station names, exact track geometry or a one-to-one identity between
the TRA branch and any current MRT segment without those records.
