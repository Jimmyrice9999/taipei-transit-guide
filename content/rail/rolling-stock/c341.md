Most metro fleets are bought by the authority that runs them. The C341, by
the account this page exists to verify, was not: it was procured by the civil
contractor of a [Bannan Line](/rail/lines/bannan-line/) extension in
settlement of a contract obligation, after the authority refused the
contractor's first choice of builder — and at a unit price notably above what
the city itself had paid Siemens for the near-identical
[C321](/rail/rolling-stock/c321/). **If that account survives verification it
is one of the best procurement stories on the network, and it has no English
telling.** It is flagged here, unwritten, for exactly that reason: the story
deserves sources, not paraphrase.

*This is a scope page.* Fleet size, dates, prices, the refused first bidder
and the eventual contract are all **still to be verified against primary
sources** — court and audit records may exist, and contemporaneous Taiwanese
reporting certainly does.

*Nothing numerical is asserted on this page.*
