The C371 is the network's most numerous steel-wheel fleet, working the
[Songshan–Xindian](/rail/lines/songshan-xindian-line/) and
[Zhonghe–Xinlu](/rail/lines/zhonghe-xinlu-line/) lines from several depots,
with short-formation sets working the Xiaobitan and Xinbeitou branches. The
claim that matters — reported widely, unverified in English — is
industrial: a substantial share of the fleet was assembled in Taiwan under a
deliberate policy of building domestic rolling-stock capability, making it
the first metro EMU manufactured in the country. That policy thread runs
straight to the locally built trams and the traction-renewal contracts now
going to Taiwanese firms, and it deserves a properly sourced telling.

*This is a scope page.* Formation variants, fleet size, build years and
places, the local-assembly split and the two series are **still to be
verified against primary sources**.

*Nothing numerical is asserted on this page.*
