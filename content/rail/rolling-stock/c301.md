The C301 is where the high-capacity network began: the fleet procured for the network's original steel-wheel line, built by Kawasaki Heavy Industries, and still working
the [Tamsui–Xinyi Line](/rail/lines/tamsui-xinyi-line/) from
[Beitou Depot](/rail/depots/beitou-depot/). It has since been through a
mid-life traction replacement — reportedly the first fleet on the network to
get one — which is exactly the kind of claim this page exists to pin down
before repeating.

*This is a scope page.* Formation, dimensions, fleet size, build years, the
original and replacement traction equipment, and the claim that this was
Taiwan's first standard-gauge train all circulate with varying precision and
are **still to be verified against primary sources** — the operator's fleet
records and the builder's own publications — before any figure appears here.

*Nothing numerical is asserted on this page.*
