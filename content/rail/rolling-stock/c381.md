The C381 arrived with the network's last major extensions and reads as the
[C371](/rail/rolling-stock/c371/)'s successor — reportedly sharing a large
part of its equipment while changing the face: a streamlined nose, a larger
curved windscreen, and detail work between the cars. Built by Kawasaki with
Taiwanese assembly participation, by the same account.

*This is a scope page.* Formation, fleet size, build years, the parts
commonality with C371 and the styling changes are **still to be verified
against primary sources** before any of it is stated as fact here.

*Nothing numerical is asserted on this page.*
