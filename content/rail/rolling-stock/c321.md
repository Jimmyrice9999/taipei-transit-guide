The C321 is the [Bannan Line](/rail/lines/bannan-line/)'s core fleet, built
by Siemens and based at [Tucheng](/rail/depots/tucheng-depot/) and
[Nangang](/rail/depots/nangang-depot/) depots. Enthusiast accounts carry a
specific and checkable story about its manufacture — a body-shell strength
problem found in testing, fixed with added material, leaving the fleet
heavier and more angular than its predecessor — and a recent propulsion
renewal awarded to a Taiwanese builder. Both are the kind of story this site
wants, and neither is written here until it can be cited.

*This is a scope page.* Formation, fleet size, build years, traction
equipment original and replacement, and the body-shell episode are **still to
be verified against primary sources**.

*Nothing numerical is asserted on this page.*
