Every mode this site covers eventually shows up in one of MOTC's national
statistical series, and those series are worth a section of their own
because they carry context none of the mode-by-mode pages can: how travel
choices are actually shifting in aggregate, how many vehicles are actually
on the road, and — a genuinely live methodological dispute — how many
people actually die in road traffic each year, which the transport
ministry, the police accident-report system and the health ministry's death
registry do not agree on and MOTC itself has had to publicly explain.

Start with [National](/statistics/national/) for modal share, vehicle
registration and the traffic-death reconciliation, each dated to its
retrieval and its survey period rather than presented as a permanent
figure.
