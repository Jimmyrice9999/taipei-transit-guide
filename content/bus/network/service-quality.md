This page compares what municipal authorities actually publish about bus service-quality evaluation. It is a source-defined comparison, not a national scorecard: the authorities use different years, categories, route scopes and publication formats.[^kaohsiung-service-evaluation-index][^taichung-service-evaluation-109][^tainan-service-evaluation-index][^pingtung-service-evaluation-audit]

## What the current sources establish

Kaohsiung's Transportation Bureau maintains a dedicated archive of annual bus service-quality material. Its page lists the 113年度 report dated 114-09-16, earlier reports for 112 through 108 and older evaluation periods.[^kaohsiung-service-evaluation-index] The archive establishes continuity of publication, but its index alone does not make the individual operator scores comparable with another city's scheme.[^kaohsiung-service-evaluation-index]

Taichung's complete 109年度 report describes five evaluation categories—facilities and service, vehicle equipment and safety, passenger service and driver management, accessibility, and company management—and 32 indicators.[^taichung-service-evaluation-109] That report assessed 17 operators and 234 routes, including joint routes, for its own evaluation period.[^taichung-service-evaluation-109]

Tainan's official plans-and-research listing shows an 114年度 city-bus evaluation plan dated 115/04/21, as well as 113年度 and 112年度 plans.[^tainan-service-evaluation-index] The listing proves a continuing evaluation/planning publication seam, but it does not provide a current final score table in the fetched page.[^tainan-service-evaluation-index]

Pingtung's audit follow-up records a different current state: the county adopted its execution rules on 18 November 2025 and completed the 112 and 113年度 city-bus evaluations in June 2026.[^pingtung-service-evaluation-audit] The audit page does not publish operator scores, so the guide records completion without inventing a ranking.[^pingtung-service-evaluation-audit]

## Why the guide does not rank municipalities

The Taichung report's 109年度/32-indicator scope, Tainan's plan listings, Kaohsiung's annual archive and Pingtung's audit follow-up are not equivalent observations.[^kaohsiung-service-evaluation-index][^taichung-service-evaluation-109][^tainan-service-evaluation-index][^pingtung-service-evaluation-audit] A future comparison would need the same evaluation year, the underlying category definitions, the assessed route/operator universe and the published scoring method for each municipality; those fields remain TBC here.
