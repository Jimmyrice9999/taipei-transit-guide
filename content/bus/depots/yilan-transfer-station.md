Taiwan Railways' current Yilan station transfer guide lists the transfer-bus
address as No. 190, Xiaoshe Road, Yilan City, and separately lists a city-bus
access point at Yilan Station.[^yilan-tra-transfer] This is the railway
operator's published access-chain description; it does not establish that the
two locations share a platform or that a particular train and bus are timed to
connect.[^yilan-tra-transfer]

The county's visitor-facing arrival guide groups Yilan, Luodong and Jiaoxi
transfer stations as destinations for intercity coach services and names
selected Kuo-Kuang, Kamalan and Capital routes.[^yilan-tourism-arrival] That is
useful corridor and terminal context, not a bay-by-bay terminal plan or a
complete current departure table.[^yilan-tourism-arrival]

The routes named by that county guide are useful search keys for the wider
network. Kuo-Kuang is shown with 1877 to Wushi Harbor and 1878 to Yilan
Transfer Station; Kamalan is shown with 1787 to Yilan, 1915 and 1917 to
Luodong, and 1918 between Hualien and Su'ao on holidays; Capital is shown with
1572 serving Jiaoxi, Yilan and Luodong and 1571/1570 serving Yilan and Luodong
respectively.[^yilan-tourism-arrival] These are the county page's selected
visitor-facing corridor descriptions, not a claim that those are all services
using the site or that every listed service runs on every date.

The same arrival page describes the Yilan-area rail corridor as having major
stations at Jiaoxi, Yilan and Luodong, and presents local buses and intercity
coaches as surrounding transport choices.[^yilan-tourism-arrival] That is
useful wayfinding context, but the guide does not publish a single integrated
platform map. A reader should therefore treat the named transfer stations as
places to verify against the operator's current departure information, not as
an automatically confirmed rail-to-coach or coach-to-city-bus connection.

The Taiwan Railways page is more precise about its own station access record:
it gives Yilan Station's address as No. 1, Guangfu Road and lists the station's
city-bus access point separately from the transfer-bus address at No. 190,
Xiaoshe Road.[^yilan-tra-transfer] The two entries answer different questions
in the published access guide. They do not, on their own, establish walking
distance, step-free routing, fare integration, luggage facilities or a timed
connection between a train and a bus.

The station page also displays a generated-page time of 5 September 2026 and
an operator website update date of 31 August 2026.[^yilan-tra-transfer] Those
dates describe the TRA page snapshot, not the next departure of any coach or
city-bus route. Current bay allocation, operator-by-bay assignment, facility
inventory, accessibility detail, walking route and timed interchange remain
**TBC** until a current terminal authority or operator publication provides
them.
