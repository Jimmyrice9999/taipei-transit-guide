Keelung Transfer Station is a terminal arrangement with two published zones,
not one undifferentiated bus stop. Keelung City Government describes the
transfer-station building and a separate roadside shelter on Zhongshan 1st
Road.[^keelung-terminal] The official address is No. 1, Yi 1st Road, in
Zhongzheng District.[^keelung-terminal]

The terminal-building zone is the published location for highway-facing
services operated by Kuo-Kuang, Tailo, Keelung Bus, Kuang Hua and Ri-Hao,
according to the city's terminal notice.[^keelung-terminal] The notice then
lists a different group at the Zhongshan 1st Road shelter: city-bus ranges from
the Keelung Bus Administration, plus selected services of other operators
including routes 787, 788, 789, 790, 791, 862, 1031, 1032, 1191 and 9006.[^keelung-terminal]
Those are the city's published zone and route-group instructions, not a claim
that every listed route departs from one numbered bay or runs every day.[^keelung-terminal]

The same notice preserves an important boundary at Keelung Railway Station.
The South Station bus stop is not abolished; passengers for the city's 3xx
services toward Taibai Village, Zhongshan Senior High School and Daganshan, the
4xx services toward Badu and Qidu, and the 6xx services toward Nuannuan and
Sijiaoting are told to use the original stop.[^keelung-terminal] This is why a
route page that mentions Keelung Transfer Station should not automatically
rewrite every Keelung Railway Station bus relation as a terminal-building
departure.[^keelung-terminal]

The municipal Keelung Bus Administration portal remains a separate current
source for city-bus route and notice information. When checked on 6 September
2026 it carried August 2026 notices for route movement/stop changes and
headway adjustments, alongside its route directory and a real-time-bus link.[^keelung-city-bus-current]
The terminal notice is older than those live service notices, so this page uses
the terminal notice for physical zone boundaries and the municipal portal for
date-sensitive route operations.[^keelung-terminal][^keelung-city-bus-current]

The Highway Bureau's current operator directory provides the separate legal and
contact context for Keelung Bus: it lists 基隆客運 with the New Taipei address
and telephone record, and marks the directory as updated in March 2026.[^thb-coach-operators]
That directory does not provide a bay map, depot allocation, vehicle count or
station-level timetable.[^thb-coach-operators]

## Join boundary

The confirmed relationships on this page are source-defined terminal zones and
published route/operator groupings.[^keelung-terminal] A direct railway-platform
interchange, a step-free path, a timed coach-to-city-bus transfer, bay-level
allocation and current facility inventory remain TBC. The guide does not infer
those details from the shared “Keelung” name or from a route passing a railway
station.[^keelung-terminal][^keelung-city-bus-current]
