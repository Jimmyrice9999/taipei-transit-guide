The Highway Bureau's current Pingtung directory keeps fixed-route Happiness Bus services separate from Happiness Taxi. Its visible first page contains ten records and reports page 1 of 56; the columns are service type, area, route name, stop pattern and modification date.[^thb-pingtung-drt] This is a dated directory view retrieved on 7 September 2026, not a claim that ten records exhaust Pingtung County.

The first page shows how different the service models are.[^thb-pingtung-drt] Sandimen has a school route, a long fixed route, a reservation route and a Happiness Taxi service for the Dewen–Dailai area; the published stop fields include Sandimen Township Office, Shuimen Transfer Station, hospitals and Pingtung Transfer Station.[^thb-pingtung-drt] Mudan has an all-town reservation route with a flexible two-point pattern and named stops reaching Hengchun Christian Hospital, while the jointly listed Mudan–Manzhou record follows the County Route 200/200A corridor and names Manzhou, fishing ports, Hengchun Market, Hengchun Transfer Station and hospitals.[^thb-pingtung-drt]

The directory also shows three Laiyi records: the E school-return line, the D Chaozhou-life line and the C tourism line. Their displayed stop patterns include the new Laiyi dispatch centre, schools, community facilities, Chaozhou Railway Station and a reservoir parking area.[^thb-pingtung-drt] The source modification dates are retained by the directory rather than converted into a current timetable or a guarantee that every named service runs daily.[^thb-pingtung-drt]

## Laiyi case study

The Ministry of Transportation's 24 April 2025 case study gives a deeper, separately dated picture of Laiyi Township.[^motc-laiji-happiness] It reports 12 Happiness Bus routes, three mid-size buses and four small vehicles, and describes the mid-size vehicles as an accessible service.[^motc-laiji-happiness] The same release says the routes connect residents with Chaozhou, Donggang, Fangliao and Pingtung, and that the service had exceeded 52,000 passenger trips by the end of March 2025.[^motc-laiji-happiness]

The Ministry describes the programme as a locally delivered system combining local drivers and vehicles with fixed and flexible reservation services for school, medical, shopping and community-care trips.[^motc-laiji-happiness] That is programme evidence, not proof that every Pingtung directory row has the same vehicle, fare, eligibility or booking rule. A complete county operator, fare and service-day crosswalk remains TBC because the fetched directory exposes route records but not a uniform county-wide tariff or booking table.[^thb-pingtung-drt]
