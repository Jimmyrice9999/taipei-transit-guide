New Taipei's Large Rehabilitation Bus is a group accessible-transport
programme, not a regular city-bus route and not a general accessible taxi. The
official New Taipei page says the vehicle has six wheelchair places and at least
14 ordinary seats, and that disability organisations may apply to hire it for
group transport such as leisure or tourism trips.[^new-taipei-large-rehab]

The published service area covers New Taipei, Taipei, Taoyuan and Keelung, but
the origin or destination must have one endpoint inside New Taipei.[^new-taipei-large-rehab]
That is a cross-city boundary for this particular group programme; it is not
evidence that every small rehabilitation-bus or accessible-taxi service in the
four cities follows the same rule.[^new-taipei-large-rehab]

Eligibility is group-based. At least five passengers must hold disability
certificates issued by New Taipei, and at least one of those passengers must
have a severe or extremely severe disability level.[^new-taipei-large-rehab]
The page then lists qualifying combinations involving disability categories II,
IV, V, VI and VII, with wheelchair use specified for the relevant category
combination.[^new-taipei-large-rehab] The threshold should be checked with the
commissioning authority before a group assumes that an individual certificate
alone is enough.

The page publishes two different fare methods. A one-way, mileage-based trip is
charged using the New Taipei city-bus segment system at half fare; the page
describes the distance bands as one segment below 9 kilometres, two from 9 to
under 18 kilometres, and three from 18 to under 27 kilometres, continuing by
the same stated progression.[^new-taipei-large-rehab] A charter booking is
charged at NT$606 per vehicle-hour, with a three-hour minimum, a ten-hour daily
maximum and a whole-hour rule for a partial hour.[^new-taipei-large-rehab]

Service is published as daily, including holidays, from 06:30 to 22:00.[^new-taipei-large-rehab]
The page's booking table says applications open from 30 days before use through
three days before use.[^new-taipei-large-rehab] It lists Taipei Bus, Capital Bus
and Metropolitan Transport as the three passenger-car companies with booking
contacts, but it does not provide a total current fleet census or a vehicle-by-
vehicle allocation, so those remain TBC.[^new-taipei-large-rehab]

This page is kept beside, rather than merged with, the Taipei Small
Rehabilitation Bus page. Taipei's service is a point-to-point, advance-booked
individual-access model, while New Taipei's page describes a qualifying group
hire with a different vehicle layout, eligibility threshold, fare options and
booking table.[^new-taipei-large-rehab] The distinction helps a reader avoid
mistaking a group vehicle reservation programme for an open-to-all bus or a
same-day accessible taxi service.
