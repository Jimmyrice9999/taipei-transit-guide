Kaohsiung Rehabilitation Bus is an eligibility-limited accessible transport
programme, not an ordinary city-bus route and not a universal taxi service.
The Kaohsiung City Government Transportation Bureau's page is dated 30 June
2026 and names Kaohsiung Bus as the contracted operator.[^kaohsiung-rehab-source]

## Who can use it

The page identifies two eligibility routes: a passenger holding a disability
certificate, or a passenger with a medical document saying that wheelchair use
is required when the journey is for medical care.[^kaohsiung-rehab-source] That
does not establish a national Rehabilitation Bus rule. Other jurisdictions'
programmes must be checked against their own commissioning authority.

New passengers must complete the programme's identity or eligibility filing
before booking. The page publishes a general application and reservation line,
a separate temporary-booking or cancellation line, and a linked reservation
system; it does not publish a simple open-to-all timetable.[^kaohsiung-rehab-source]

## Service boundary and booking

The ordinary service boundary is Kaohsiung's administrative area. The same
regulation also permits specified trips from Kaohsiung to neighbouring areas of
Pingtung and Tainan. For journeys starting in Kaohsiung districts next to
Tainan, the page names a narrower set of Tainan districts and medical
institutions.[^kaohsiung-rehab-source] This is a documented geographic rule,
not a claim that every Kaohsiung vehicle operates throughout either neighbouring
county or city.

Advance-booking time depends on the passenger category. The published windows
run from seven days before travel through the day before for the first listed
group, six days through the day before for the next group, and five days through
the day before for other eligible users.[^kaohsiung-rehab-source] The regulation
also allows temporary booking and describes cancellation and no-show points;
those provisions are reasons to check the operator before relying on a booking
date.[^kaohsiung-rehab-source]

## Fare rule

The official page publishes a formula rather than one timeless fare. The charge
is based on the passenger's distance and Kaohsiung's daytime taxi rate. A solo
trip is one-half of that rate, reduced to one-third for a low-income passenger;
a fully shared trip is one-quarter, and a partially shared trip is one-third.
The result is rounded up to a whole number. Highway tolls are charged separately
unless another subsidy rule applies.[^kaohsiung-rehab-source]

The site keeps fleet size and a vehicle-level timetable as TBC. This page is a
programme reference: it explains eligibility, geography, booking and fare
logic, while the operator's current booking channel remains the place to check
whether a specific request can be accepted.[^kaohsiung-rehab-source]
