The Highway Bureau's route directory gives rural and island transport a more useful level of detail than a single national programme count. It records the service type, area, route name, stop field and modification date, while the national directory keeps the jurisdictions in one browsable inventory.[^thb-national-drt-directory]

For Lanyu, the directory lists a fixed 人之島環線 service and a separate 彈性預約路線（蘭嶼鄉）. The fixed entry is a circular island route whose stop field includes 開元港, the township office, the east-coast settlements and the airport area before returning to the port.[^thb-taitung-drt] The flexible entry lists 開元港、鄉公所、野銀、東清、朗島 and 開元港, which makes it a reservation-oriented island service record rather than an ordinary high-frequency urban route.[^thb-taitung-drt]

Both Lanyu records show ROC 113-07-08 as their route-record modification date.[^thb-taitung-drt] That date is retained as a source snapshot field; it is not silently converted into a claim that the service runs every day or that the timetable is unchanged in September 2026.[^thb-taitung-drt]

The Guanshan directory lists two school-service records, 關山-電光就學專線1 and 關山-電光就學專線2, both modified ROC 114-10-13.[^thb-taitung-guanshan] It also lists 關山環鎮生活便利線, described in the stop/service field as linking six villages with central Guanshan and Guanshan Tzu Chi Hospital; that record shows ROC 113-04-23.[^thb-taitung-guanshan]

These records demonstrate why Taitung's rural transport layer should remain a service-directory index rather than a mechanically generated set of route articles.[^thb-taitung-drt][^thb-taitung-guanshan] The official pages expose route names and stop patterns, but the material fetched here does not establish a complete current timetable, booking phone number, contractor crosswalk or fare for every Taitung entry.[^thb-taitung-drt][^thb-taitung-guanshan] Those fields remain TBC until the relevant commissioning or township pages are fetched.[^thb-taitung-drt][^thb-taitung-guanshan]

This page is therefore a sourced sample of Taitung rural and island transport, not a claim that the two townships exhaust the county's Happiness Bus network. The Highway Bureau national directory contains additional Taitung entries, including routes for Taitung City, Beinan, Daren, Jinfeng, Chenggong, Changbin and Haiduan; their route-specific records should be added only after the corresponding full entries are read.[^thb-national-drt-directory]
