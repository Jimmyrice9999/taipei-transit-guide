Nantou Happiness GO is a county-commissioned rural public-transport programme, not a synonym for every bus route in Nantou.[^nantou-happiness-go] The county says it began the service in 2024 for daily needs such as education, medical visits, care and shopping, and set a target of completing service across 13 townships in 2026.[^nantou-happiness-go] The Highway Bureau’s national directory separately distinguishes Happiness Bus and Happiness Taxi records by service type, area, route and modification date.[^thb-happiness-inventory]

## The service boundary

The county says Happiness GO prioritises connections to bus stops and provides fully flexible reservation trips where no bus serves an area or where there is no service within the surrounding one-hour window.[^nantou-happiness-go] Outside the programme boundary, the site directs passengers to ordinary bus information or to a nearby transfer point rather than presenting the service as an unlimited county taxi.[^nantou-happiness-go]

The programme page lists general booking areas for Zhushan, Lugu, Mingjian, Shuili, Jiji, Xinyi, Puli and Yuchi, and gives separate local contact arrangements for Guoxing and Ren’ai.[^nantou-happiness-go] The page also gives examples of how the service types differ. Zhushan, Mingjian and Lugu include flexible whole-town or cross-town travel, while Ren’ai’s Wujie material separates a reservation route, a fixed route and a fixed student service; its Lifa service is listed as named routes rather than as a generic on-demand taxi.[^nantou-happiness-go]

## Current fares and booking

The county page says Puli, Yuchi and Xinyi began paid operation from 3 August 2026, with a full fare of NT$25, student fare of NT$20 and half fare of NT$13, and tells passengers to carry a programme ride card plus one of iPASS, EasyCard or the senior/love card products it names.[^nantou-happiness-go] Those are dated programme fares for the named launch notice, not proof that every Nantou service has identical concessions.[^nantou-happiness-go]

The general booking line is 0800-885-658, with weekday hours of 08:30–12:00 and 13:30–17:00.[^nantou-happiness-go] The county also accepts requests through a local notification network involving village offices, long-term-care points, health centres, district offices, schools and medical institutions, and publishes LINE ID `@ntgo` for online booking.[^nantou-happiness-go] Ren’ai has additional village-contact numbers and a separate Wujie LINE ID, so a passenger should use the local instructions when booking those services.[^nantou-happiness-go]

## What remains separate

The page does not assert a complete September 2026 county route export, one operator/contractor roster, a universal service-days table or a permanent fare matrix.[^nantou-happiness-go] The national February 2026 Happiness Bus/Taxi measurement remains on the national DRT index with its own date and definition; it is not silently replaced by the Nantou programme’s township target.[^thb-happiness-inventory]
