## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 508 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 泰山公有市場 - 大同之家, weekday and holiday service both
0530–2200, a three-section fare (三段票) across the whole route — rare among
Taipei-area routes — buffer sections at 蘆洲總站–徐匯中學 and 重陽橋–士林國中, and
三重客運中港站 as the service contact.[^ebus-508] The page lists 39 weekday
departures and 22 holiday departures.[^ebus-508]

At 81 stops outbound, the confirmed stop sequence is the longest in this
project's series-500s coverage: from 泰山公有市場 in Taishan, through 五股's
洲仔/成州 area, across into 蘆洲 past a stop literally named 捷運蘆洲站,
through 三重's 徐匯中學/三和國中 (both also literally MRT-named), into 士林 past
重陽橋/士林國中/捷運芝山站, then 明德/石牌 past 捷運明德站/捷運石牌站, ending in the
行義路 hot-spring area at 大同之家.[^tdx-bus] Six literally-named MRT stops lie
along the route; none is in the curated stop-ID join set as a confirmed
interchange.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A community wiki, covering 508 and its 508區 shuttle on one page, records the
main line's dispatch base moving from 泰山鄉 to 五股鄉 on 11 August 2007,
redispatched from 中港站 with the route extended toward 泰山公有市場, and that
extension opening to passengers on 20 July 2008; it also records an
evening-headway change on 14 December 2021 that moved the last weekday
departure from 2220 to 2200, matching the route's current 2200 close.[^zhwikiversity-508]
This is a secondary source and was not corroborated against a primary PTO or
operator announcement in this search.
