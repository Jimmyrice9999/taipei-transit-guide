## Classification and corridor

542預 does not appear anywhere in the full official bus catalogue, under
一般公車 or any other heading.[^ebus-general] The catalogue does list a plain
route 542 (捷運圓山站 - 捷運中山國中站) under 一般公車, and that route's own
schedule page states that since 9 January 2021 (民國110年1月9日), the weekday
06:30 and 14:00 departures run as 預約公車班次 (reservation bus trips) that are
only dispatched if someone has booked, alongside separate fixed departures at
0800, 0930, 1600 and 1800.[^ebus-542] Taipei's real-time bus system separately
tracks a route with id 18348 — the same id TDX uses for this route — named
542預, with operator 首都客運, directions 往捷運中山國中站 / 往捷運圓山站, a 一段票
fare, and 頭班/末班 times of 0630/1400 on weekdays, with no holiday
service.[^pda5284-542] Those times match the two reservation slots named on
the plain-542 schedule page exactly, consistent with 542預 being that
reservation-trip subset of route 542, formalised as its own route
record.[^pda5284-542][^ebus-542] The TDX snapshot separately records a
"542預約公車" subroute nested inside the plain route 542's own entry, which is
the same reservation-trip concept represented a second way inside a different
route record.[^tdx-bus]

## What "預" means here

The Taipei City Public Transportation Office's FAQ on the citywide reservation
bus system describes reservation trips generally as scheduled for "運量低且
搭乘需求不固定之路線班次" (route departures where demand is low and irregular);
they dispatch only if booked, carry a "預約班次" placard, are bookable from 14
days to 2 hours ahead by app, website or phone, and remain open to unbooked
boarding once running.[^pto-drts-faq] This FAQ is general and does not name
542 or 542預 specifically.[^pto-drts-faq] The taipeidrts.org.tw booking
platform is the front end for this system; its homepage lists partner
operators and booking rules but does not name 542 or 542預 either.[^taipeidrts]
On the evidence gathered here, "預" on this route means the same thing it
means on the wider 臺北市預約公車 system: a reservation-only working of an
existing numbered route, not a predecessor-route marker or a shuttle variant.
