## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 556 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives termini 木柵象頭埔 - 捷運劍潭站, a 一段票 fare, and 指南客運
新光站 as the service contact, with 12 weekday departures between 0630 and
1730 and no holiday service.[^ebus-556]

The confirmed stop sequence spans a large part of the city: from 象頭埔/富德
in far southern Muzha, along 和平東路 past 萬芳社區/六張犁 (a stop literally
named 捷運六張犁站(和平)), then north through 忠孝敦化/民權大橋 into Neihu's
瑞光/劍南路 corridor, ending at 捷運劍潭站(北藝中心) in Shilin.[^tdx-bus] Two
further MRT-named stops are passed literally along the way without becoming
curated interchanges — 捷運劍南路站 and 捷運大直站 in Neihu.[^tdx-bus] At 12
weekday departures and no holiday service at all, this is one of the
sparsest schedules in this project's series-500s coverage for a route that
otherwise crosses the width of the city.[^ebus-556]
