## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 529 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 兒童新樂園 - 新光醫院, weekday service 0600–1930 with
holiday service suspended (停駛), a single-section fare (一段票), and
大南汽車關渡站 and 大南汽車秀山站 as the two service contacts.[^ebus-529] The page
lists 28 weekday departures.[^ebus-529]

## History

A community wiki records 529 launched on 15 May 2000 as 紅28, renumbered to
529 effective 10 March 2001, extended on 14 February 2015 to serve 士林(二場)
dispatch station and 兒童新樂園, and most recently had a stop swap at 士林區
行政中心 dated 25 March 2026.[^zhwikiversity-529] This is a secondary source
and was not corroborated against a primary PTO or operator announcement in
this search.
