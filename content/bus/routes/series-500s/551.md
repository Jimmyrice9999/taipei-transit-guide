## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 551 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives termini 內湖科技園區 - 捷運昆陽站, a 一段票 fare, and 大南汽車
內湖站 as the service contact, with service running 0530–2300 on both weekday
and holiday schedules.[^ebus-551] The page marks specific departures as
extending to 金龍禪寺 — weekdays 0600, 0810, 1410 and 1530; holidays 0830 and
1600 — and separately marks an 08:00–21:30 window on both day types as
detouring via 三總內湖站 (the Tri-Service General Hospital Neihu branch).[^ebus-551]
The TDX snapshot represents these combinations as four distinct subroutes for
whether a working detours via 行善路 and whether it passes 金龍禪寺, consistent
with the schedule page's detour notes; one of the four subroute names spells
the temple 金龍寺 rather than 金龍禪寺, an inconsistency in the source data
rather than a different destination.[^tdx-bus]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 內湖科技園區's own 行善路/瑞光路 office
corridor, through central Neihu past 金龍寺/碧湖山莊 and a stop literally
named 捷運內湖站, then east past 三總內湖站 into Nangang along 東新國小/南港
車站/南港高中, ending at 捷運昆陽站.[^tdx-bus] The candidate MRT proximities
the raw name-matching flagged — BL21 (Kunyang, the route's own terminus),
BR17 (Gangqian) and BR19 (Neihu) — are consistent with that path, but none
is in the curated stop-ID join set as a confirmed interchange.[^tdx-bus]
