## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 513 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 捷運輔大站 - 捷運台大醫院站, a single-section fare
(一段票), and 三重客運新莊站 as the service contact.[^ebus-513] The weekday
timetable lists exactly one departure, at 1000; holiday service is
suspended (停駛).[^ebus-513]

## History

A community wiki records that dispatch moved from 輔大站 to 新莊站 on 1
November 2020 after the 輔大 depot closed, and that from 19 December 2020
service was cut to a single weekday departure with all 23 previously
scheduled holiday departures suspended — matching the route's current
single-trip timetable.[^zhwikiversity-513] This is a secondary source and the
2020 dates were not corroborated against a primary PTO or operator
announcement in this search.
