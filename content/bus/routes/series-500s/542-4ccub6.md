## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 542 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives termini 捷運圓山站 - 捷運中山國中站, a 一段票 fare, and 首都客運
士林站 as the service contact.[^ebus-542] Alongside four fixed weekday
departures (0800, 0930, 1600, 1800) and no holiday service, the page states
that since 9 January 2021 the 06:30 and 14:00 weekday departures run as
預約公車班次 (reservation bus trips), dispatched only if someone has
booked.[^ebus-542] The TDX snapshot records four subroutes for this route:
542路, 542預約公車 (matching those two reservation departures), 542路去程半 and
542路延駛民族西路.[^tdx-bus] A separate TDX route record, routeId 18348,
additionally carries the name 542預/542B and is covered on its own content
page; it appears to represent the same reservation-trip concept as a
standalone route rather than a different corridor.[^tdx-bus]

Route length and headway by day type remain TBC.[^tdx-bus] History of the
