## Classification and corridor

539預 does not appear anywhere in the full official bus catalogue, under 一般公車
or any other heading.[^ebus-general] The catalogue does list a plain route 539
(三重 - 馬偕醫院) under 一般公車, and that route's own schedule page states that
since 22 June 2023 (民國112年6月22日), nine weekday departures and nine holiday
departures run as 預約公車班次 (reservation bus trips) that are only dispatched
if someone has booked.[^ebus-539] Taipei's real-time bus system separately
tracks a route with id 19438 — the same id TDX uses for this route — named
539預, with operator 首都客運, directions 往馬偕醫院 / 往三重, a 一段票 fare, and
頭班/末班 times of 1000/2100 on weekdays and 0600/2100 on holidays.[^pda5284-539]
Those first/last times match the earliest and latest reservation slots given
on the plain-539 schedule page exactly (weekday 1000...2100, holiday
0600...2100), consistent with 539預 being that reservation-trip subset of
route 539, formalised as its own route record rather than a separate
corridor.[^pda5284-539][^ebus-539]

## What "預" means here

The Taipei City Public Transportation Office's FAQ on the citywide reservation
bus system describes reservation trips generally as scheduled for "運量低且
搭乘需求不固定之路線班次" (route departures where demand is low and irregular);
they dispatch only if booked, carry a "預約班次" placard, are bookable from 14
days to 2 hours ahead by app, website or phone, and remain open to unbooked
boarding once running.[^pto-drts-faq] This FAQ is general and does not name
539 or 539預 specifically.[^pto-drts-faq] The taipeidrts.org.tw booking
platform is the front end for this system; its homepage lists partner
operators and booking rules but does not name 539 or 539預 either.[^taipeidrts]
On the evidence gathered here, "預" on this route means the same thing it
means on the wider 臺北市預約公車 system: a reservation-only working of an
existing numbered route, not a predecessor-route marker or a shuttle variant.
