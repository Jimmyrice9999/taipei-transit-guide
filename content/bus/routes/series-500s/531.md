## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 531 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 紫雲里 - 松山車站, weekday service 0600–1830 with holiday
service suspended (停駛), a single-section fare (一段票), and 欣欣客運東湖站 as
the service contact.[^ebus-531] The weekday timetable lists eight
departures: 0600, 0810, 0930, 1100, 1330, 1500, 1710 and 1830.[^ebus-531]

## History

A community wiki records that from 15 November 2014 the route was shortened
to terminate at 松山車站, dropping a former loop via 八德路 and stops at 松山
車站(八德)、松山農會 and 饒河街觀光夜市, and that a station on the route was
renamed from 捷運葫洲站(康寧專校) to 捷運葫洲站(康寧大學) on 31 January
2024.[^zhwikiversity-531] This is a secondary source and was not
corroborated against a primary PTO or operator announcement in this search.
