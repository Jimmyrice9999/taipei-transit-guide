## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 530 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 指南宮 - 捷運公館站, weekday and holiday service both
0530–2215, a single-section fare (一段票), and 指南客運新光站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-530] Weekday headways run 12–15 minutes at peak and 15–20
minutes off-peak, with a fixed schedule after 1800.[^ebus-530] The TDX
snapshot records a second subroute, a half-length return working named 530
返程半.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A community wiki records that 530 was created on 10 May 2001, when the
former highway-bus route 指南1 — operated by 指南客運, whose name derives from
指南宮 — was split into two city routes, 530 and 801.[^zhwikiversity-530] This
is a secondary source and was not corroborated against a primary PTO or
operator announcement in this search.
