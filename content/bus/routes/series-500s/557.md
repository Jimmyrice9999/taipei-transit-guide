## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 557 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives termini 天文科學館 - 東吳大學, a 一段票 fare, and 中興巴士
北士科站 as the service contact.[^ebus-557] Weekday service runs 0640–2210 with
peak headways of 4–6 minutes, off-peak headways of 10–15 minutes, and a fixed
schedule after 20:20; holiday service runs 0710–2110.[^ebus-557] The page
states that during winter and summer school breaks, service follows the
holiday timetable (寒暑假期間班次同例假日).[^ebus-557] The TDX snapshot records no
rail-stop joins for this route.[^tdx-bus]

The confirmed stop sequence is short and entirely local to Shilin: from 天文
科學館 (the Taipei Astronomical Museum) near 雙溪, past 士林高商/士林區行政中心
and a stop literally named 捷運士林站(中正), then north past 士林官邸/雙溪公園
to end at 東吳大學 (Soochow University).[^tdx-bus] Unlike several other routes
in this batch, the raw stop-name matching process did not flag even an
unconfirmed candidate MRT interchange for this route, despite the
literally-named 捷運士林站 stop along the way.[^tdx-bus] Its fixed schedule
after 20:20 and the explicit school-holiday note both point to a route
tuned for a school-term ridership pattern, consistent with the several
schools it names directly in its own stop sequence — 士林高商, 士林國中,
泰北中學 and 東吳大學 itself.[^tdx-bus]
