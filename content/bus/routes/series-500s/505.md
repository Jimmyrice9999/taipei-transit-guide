## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 505 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 撫遠街 - 景美, weekday service 0520–2200 and holiday
service 0530–2200, a single-section fare (一段票), and 大都會客運內湖站 as the
service contact.[^ebus-505] Weekday headways run 12–15 minutes at peak and
15–20 minutes off-peak, with a fixed schedule after 2000 on weekdays and
throughout holiday service.[^ebus-505]

## History

A community wiki records 505 extended to 景美 on 18 May 2006, a stop
realignment along 民生東路 on 8 September 2007, a temporary extension to 大佳
河濱公園 between 6 November 2010 and 25 April 2011, holiday-trip reductions
from 33 to 28 on 23 September 2017 and from 28 to 25 on 3 September 2018, and
the last weekday departure moved from 2300 to 2200 on 12 December
2020.[^zhwikiversity-505] This is a secondary source and was not corroborated
against a primary PTO or operator announcement in this search.

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route
length remains TBC.[^tdx-bus] A route number can recur across cities under
separate numbering systems: Taoyuan's bus network also runs a 505, a weekend
中壢–觀音 sightseeing service operated by 桃園客運, unconnected to this Taipei
route.[^wiki-collision-505] No plain-numbered 505 was found in Taichung's or
Kaohsiung's city bus lists in this search.
