## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 559 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives termini 捷運石牌站 - 陽明交大, a 一段票 fare, and 大都會客運
榮總站 as the service contact, with weekday service 0720–2230 and holiday
service 0800–2240, both at 10–20 minute headways.[^ebus-559] The page marks
four weekday departures (2000, 2100, 2200 and the last bus) and eight holiday
departures (1500, 1600, 1700, 1800, 2000, 2100, 2200, 2240) as detouring to
men's and women's dormitories (男女五舍).[^ebus-559] The TDX snapshot records
two matching subroutes, 559路 and 559繞駛男女五舍.[^tdx-bus]

The confirmed stop sequence is short and entirely internal to the 陽明交大
(National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University) campus, running from 綜合市場,
next to 捷運石牌站, up through the campus gate to 陽明交大實驗大樓/行政大樓, with
the 559繞駛男女五舍 variant extending one stop further to 陽明交大五舍 (the
men's/women's dormitories).[^tdx-bus] At four to five stops each way, it is
one of the shortest routes in this project's series-500s coverage,
functioning as a campus shuttle from the MRT station rather than a
cross-district route.[^tdx-bus] The dormitory detour is scheduled only at
specific departures rather than every run — four times on weekdays, eight
times on holidays — which the TDX snapshot's second subroute record
matches exactly.[^ebus-559]
