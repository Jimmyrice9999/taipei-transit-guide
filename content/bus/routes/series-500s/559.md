## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 559 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives termini 捷運石牌站 - 陽明交大, a 一段票 fare, and 大都會客運
榮總站 as the service contact, with weekday service 0720–2230 and holiday
service 0800–2240, both at 10–20 minute headways.[^ebus-559] The page marks
four weekday departures (2000, 2100, 2200 and the last bus) and eight holiday
departures (1500, 1600, 1700, 1800, 2000, 2100, 2200, 2240) as detouring to
men's and women's dormitories (男女五舍).[^ebus-559] The TDX snapshot records
two matching subroutes, 559路 and 559繞駛男女五舍.[^tdx-bus]
