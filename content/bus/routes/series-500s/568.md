## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 568 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives termini 萬華 - 捷運麟光站, a 一段票 fare, and 欣欣客運華江站 as
the service contact, with 42 weekday departures between 0555 and 1930, and 6
holiday departures between 0900 and 1630.[^ebus-568]

The confirmed stop sequence runs the length of 和平東/西路 from 華江 in
Wanhua, through Zhongzheng and Daan, ending at 捷運麟光站 in Wenshan.[^tdx-bus]
It literally passes several MRT-named stops along the way, including 捷運
龍山寺站 and 捷運古亭站(和平); yet the curated stop-ID join set records zero
confirmed interchanges for this route, and the underlying raw name-matching
flagged only Longshan Temple (BL10), Linguang (BR06) and Daan Park (R06) as
unconfirmed candidates.[^tdx-bus] The route's own terminus, 捷運麟光站, sits at
the BR06 candidate — meaning even the stop the route is named for in its
own summary is not curated as a confirmed interchange in this project's
data.[^tdx-bus] The route also carries a low-floor-fleet cross-listing on
the official catalogue alongside its general-bus classification, one of
several routes in this batch marked that way.[^ebus-general] Its holiday
schedule is far thinner than its weekday one — 6 departures across a
0900–1630 window against 42 departures spanning 0555–1930 — consistent
with a route oriented toward the schools and offices along its own
corridor rather than weekend travel.[^ebus-568]
