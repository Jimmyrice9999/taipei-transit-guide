## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 539 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 三重 - 馬偕醫院, weekday service 0600–1910 and holiday
service 0900–1800, a single-section fare (一段票), and 首都客運三重二站 as the
service contact.[^ebus-539] Since 22 June 2023, specific departures run only
on reservation — weekday 1000, 1100, 1200, 1300, 1400, 1500, 1600, 2000 and
2100, and holiday 0600, 0700, 0800, 1300, 1400, 1500, 1900, 2000 and 2100 —
with the page stating verbatim 倘無人預約則不發車 (the bus does not depart if no
one has booked).[^ebus-539] The TDX snapshot records a second subroute named
539預約公車, consistent with this reservation arrangement.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A community wiki records 539 was renumbered from a 39副線 (Route 39 branch)
effective 2 January 2001, had departure-count reductions in 2011 and 2015,
and was shortened to terminate at 馬偕醫院 effective 22 May 2021, dropping a
longer loop through 中山北路、忠孝西路 and 延平北路.[^zhwikiversity-539] It also
records the reservation-service change from 22 June 2023, matching the
primary schedule page.[^zhwikiversity-539] This is a secondary source and
the pre-2023 history was not independently corroborated against a primary
PTO or operator announcement in this search.
