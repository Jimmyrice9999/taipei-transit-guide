## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 550 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives termini 關渡宮 - 三王宮, a 一段票 fare, and 大南汽車關渡站 as
the service contact.[^ebus-550] Service runs 0520–1830 with seven daily
departures (0520, 0650, 0850, 1050, 1400, 1630, 1830), but the page states the
route is suspended on Mondays and national holidays (週一及國定假日停駛), so the
two schedule tables shown are not a weekday/holiday pair in the usual
sense.[^ebus-550]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 關渡宮 in outer Beitou, along 中央北路
through 忠義/復興崗, then south through 北投國小/中央南路 into 石牌, ending at
三王宮 near 洲美.[^tdx-bus] It literally passes four MRT-named stops — 捷運
忠義站, 捷運復興崗站, 捷運奇岩站(北投) and 捷運唭哩岸站(西安) — yet the curated
stop-ID join set records zero confirmed interchanges for this route; the
underlying raw name-matching flagged only R23, R24 and R25 as unconfirmed
candidates.[^tdx-bus] Along the way it passes 製片廠 and 復興崗, stop names
referencing the film-studio and military-academy history of that stretch of
Beitou, rather than a shopping or residential landmark.[^tdx-bus] The Monday-and-
national-holiday suspension is unusual among routes in this batch, most of
which either run every day or drop only the holiday schedule rather than
also excluding one weekday specifically.[^ebus-550]
