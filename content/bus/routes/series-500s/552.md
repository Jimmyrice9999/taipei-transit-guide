## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 552 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives termini 金龍寺 - 捷運市政府站, a 一段票 fare, and 東南客運內湖站
as the service contact, with service running 0545–2140 on both weekday and
holiday schedules (20 weekday departures, 12 holiday departures).[^ebus-552]

The confirmed stop sequence runs almost the whole length of Neihu's
office-park corridor: from 金龍寺 near 碧湖公園 in the district's south, past
內湖國小 and 西湖圖書館, through the 瑞光/基湖/新湖 business-park stretch, then
south past 潭美/松山高中 to end at 捷運市政府站 in Xinyi.[^tdx-bus] The return
working literally passes a stop named 捷運內湖站, yet the curated stop-ID
join set records zero confirmed interchanges for this route — the raw
name-matching process instead flagged BL18 and BR19 as unconfirmed
candidates.[^tdx-bus] Even the route's own terminus, sitting directly beside
捷運市政府站 — the BL18 candidate itself — is not curated as a confirmed
interchange, illustrating that this project treats "runs to a station" and
"has a verified stop-ID join" as two separate claims even at an endpoint.[^tdx-bus]
The route runs on a single subroute record with no holiday-specific or
detour variant, matching the single 東南客運 operator listed for its whole
0545–2140 span.[^tdx-bus]
