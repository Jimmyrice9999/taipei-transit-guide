## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 521 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 內湖 - 捷運忠孝復興站, weekday service 0630–2000 and
holiday service 0700–1830, a single-section fare (一段票), and 光華巴士東湖站 as
the service contact.[^ebus-521] Some early departures skip 三軍總醫院內湖院區 —
the weekday 0630 and 0730 workings and the holiday 0700 working.[^ebus-521]
The TDX snapshot records two subroutes, one routed 經三總 (via the Tri-Service
General Hospital) and one 不經三總 (bypassing it), consistent with that
skip-stop pattern.[^tdx-bus]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 金龍寺 in Neihu, past a stop literally
named 捷運內湖站, along 成功路 through the Tri-Service General Hospital area,
then west through 民權大橋/民生社區 into Songshan, passing 捷運南京復興站
before ending at 捷運忠孝復興站, itself served by both the Bannan Line
(BL15) and the Wenhu Line (BR10).[^tdx-bus] None of these three literally-
named stops is in the curated stop-ID join set for this route.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A community wiki records 521's predecessor as a 604 shuttle service operated
by 光華巴士 from 15 October 1999, renumbered to 521 effective 1 April 2000,
with further service and terminal-route adjustments recorded in 2006, 2009,
2017 and 2021.[^zhwikiversity-521] This is a secondary source and was not
corroborated against a primary PTO or operator announcement in this search.
