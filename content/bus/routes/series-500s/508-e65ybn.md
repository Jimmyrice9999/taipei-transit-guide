## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 508區 under 一般公車, filed as its own routeid
entry separate from the plain 508 route.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule
page gives 蘆洲 - 大同之家, weekday and holiday service both 0440–2145, a
two-section fare (兩段票) with a buffer zone at 重陽橋–士林國中, and two
operator contacts — 三重客運蘆洲站 and 大都會客運蘆洲站.[^ebus-508shuttle] The
outbound working departs 蘆洲總站 under 三重客運 dispatch and runs via 中正路 to
大同之家; the return working runs directly along 中山路二段 back to 蘆洲站 under
大都會客運 dispatch.[^ebus-508shuttle] The TDX snapshot separately records four
subroutes for this route — 508(大)、508(三重)、508(三重一) and a half-trip
variant of 508(三重一) — consistent with the two-operator, split-direction
working the schedule page describes.[^tdx-bus]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 蘆洲, past stops literally named 捷運
徐匯中學站 and 捷運三和國中站, through 三重's 碧華/五華 area, into 士林 past 重陽橋/
士林國中 and a stop literally named 捷運芝山站, then through 明德/石牌 past 捷運
明德站 and 捷運石牌站, ending in the 行義路 hot-spring area at 大同之家.[^tdx-bus]
None of these five literally-named MRT stops is in the curated stop-ID
join set as a confirmed interchange for this route.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A community wiki, covering 508 and 508區 on one page, records the shuttle
service's launch on 7 February 1994.[^zhwikiversity-508shuttle] This is a
secondary source and was not corroborated against a primary PTO or operator
announcement in this search.
