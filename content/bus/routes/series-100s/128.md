## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 128 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 捷運石牌站 - 竹子湖, weekday service suspended (停駛),
holiday service 0530–1710 with 20 listed departures, a single-section fare
(一段票), and 三重客運南港站 as the service contact.[^ebus-128] The page notes
the last departure from MRT Shipai Station is 1710 and that service is
extended to 竹子湖 during the calla lily flower season: 捷運石牌站末班1710；
花季期間配合延駛至竹子湖.[^ebus-128]

## Subroute variants

The TDX snapshot records two further subroutes beyond the plain 128 working:
128(捷運石牌-陽管處), naming the MRT Shipai–Yangmingshan Park Administration
Office corridor, and 128往返陽管處至竹子湖, a round trip between the
Administration Office and 竹子湖.[^tdx-bus] Neither variant appeared as a
separate schedule tab on the official schedule page in this search; the page
instead describes seasonal extension of the single 128 working, as above.[^ebus-128]

## History

A community wiki records the route operated by 三重客運 from 2009 on a
holiday-only basis, switching to 大南汽車 in 2012, then returning to 三重客運
with regularized holiday service from 12 February 2013.[^zhwikiversity-128]
It also lists calla lily season (海芋季) extension-to-Zhuzihu windows
recorded for most years from 2014 to 2020, and again from 2025, consistent
with the seasonal-extension note on the primary schedule page.[^zhwikiversity-128]
One entry in that list, given as "2024年2月7日至2023年6月23日止", has its
start and end years in the wrong order in the source itself and is reported
here as an error in that source rather than repeated as a dated fact. None of
this history was corroborated against a primary PTO or 三重客運 announcement
in this search.
