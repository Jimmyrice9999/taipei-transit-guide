## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 108區 under 一般公車, as a separate routeid entry
from its plain 108 parent.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives
陽明山 - 二子坪, weekday and holiday service 0740–1540, holiday headway
10–20 minutes at both peak and off-peak, a single-section fare (一段票), and
大都會客運陽明山站 as the service contact.[^ebus-108qu] The weekday timetable
lists 13 departures at 40-minute intervals from 0740 to 1540.[^ebus-108qu]
The page also carries a weather-suspension note: services are cancelled on
weekdays when Taipei's rain-probability forecast reaches 70% or during
low-temperature, heavy-rain or typhoon warnings, with holiday service running
at 20–30 minute intervals during typhoon warnings.[^ebus-108qu]

The confirmed stop sequence covers the outer half of parent route 108's
loop only: from Yangmingshan it runs out to 二子坪 by way of the park
headquarters, Yangmingshuwu and the Xiaoyoukeng service station, without
108's own inner leg through Menghuan Lake, Lengshuikeng and the Qingshan
recreation area.[^tdx-bus] Like 108, every stop is inside the national
park, none is named for an MRT station, and the curated join set carries
no confirmed MRT connection.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A community wiki dates the shuttle's launch as a new route to 1 January
2010, with a headway adjustment on 27 August 2010.[^zhwikiversity-108] It
then records a 20 September 2012 change from weekday 60 minutes/holiday
suspended and hours 0900–1600, to weekday 30–40 minutes/holiday 10–20
minutes and hours 0740–1540.[^zhwikiversity-108] The post-change hours and
holiday headway it states match the values currently shown on the primary
schedule page exactly.[^ebus-108qu] This was not independently corroborated
against a primary PTO or 大都會客運 announcement in this search.

**108Shuttle (10 stops):** Bailika Highway Entrance -> Dinghushan -> Zhuza Police Substation -> Shuiyunjian -> Yangming Academy House -> Central Weather Bureau -> Huangziwai -> Boy Scouts Stop -> Yangmingshan National Park Headquarters -> Yangmingshan Bus Terminal[^tdx-bus]

The sequence is kept separate from the route shape: a stop is a boarding record, while the shape is the published alignment used for mapping. A missing stop name or a changed sequence is therefore a TDX-data issue, not evidence that the service was historically rerouted[^tdx-bus].
