## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 129 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 捷運北投站 - 竹子湖, weekday service suspended (停駛),
holiday service 0650–1650 with 11 listed departures, a two-section fare
(二段票), and 大南汽車秀山站 as the service contact.[^ebus-129] The page's
分段緩衝 field was read twice in this search and both times gave 風尾-陽明山
家公園管理處, without the character 國 that the full place name 陽明山國家
公園管理處 (Yangmingshan National Park Administration Office) and a secondary
source both carry; the primary source's exact text is reported here as
found, alongside the likely intended name.[^ebus-129]

## History

A community wiki records the route running seasonal holiday-only windows in
2009, 2010 and 2012 before regularizing to standard holiday-only service
from 12 February 2013 — the same date it gives for the equivalent change on
route 128.[^zhwikiversity-129] It records an hours change to 0655–1810
effective 4 February 2017, which does not match the 0650–1650 span currently
shown on the primary schedule page, implying at least one further unlisted
change occurred since.[^zhwikiversity-129] It also records a buffer-zone
expansion from 頂湖-陽明山國家公園管理處 to 風尾-陽明山國家公園管理處,
effective 2 April 2022 — matching the buffer zone currently shown on the
primary page except for the 國 character noted above — and a return-terminus
stop change to 大興街口 effective 1 November 2025.[^zhwikiversity-129] None of
this was corroborated against a primary PTO or 大南汽車 announcement in this
search.
