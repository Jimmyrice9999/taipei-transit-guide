## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 109 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 萬芳社區 - 陽明山, weekday service suspended (停駛),
holiday service 0800–1500, a two-section fare (兩段票) with a buffer zone
between 劍潭 and 銘傳大學, and 大都會客運萬芳站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-109]

The confirmed stop sequence links Wenshan District to Yangmingshan across
most of the city in between: it starts at 萬芳社區 near Wanfang Community and
runs past 捷運萬芳醫院站 and 捷運公館站 through Gongguan, up 新生南路 past
捷運忠孝新生站, along the Songjiang–Nanjing corridor past 捷運松江南京站 and
捷運行天宮站, through Zhongshan and Shilin past 捷運士林站/捷運劍潭站, and
finally climbs into Yangmingshan National Park to end at the park
headquarters.[^tdx-bus] Despite passing five separate MRT stations by name
along that route, the curated stop-ID join set confirms none of
them.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A community wiki records the buffer zone's expansion from 銘傳大學 alone to
劍潭-銘傳大學, effective 9 August 2010.[^zhwikiversity-109] This matches the
buffer zone currently shown on the primary schedule page.[^ebus-109] The same
wiki records a run of holiday trip-count reductions — 26 to 22 trips from 23
September 2017, and 22 to 15 trips from 18 April 2020 — followed by three
further service adjustments dated 15 July 2023, 14 September 2024 and 30
August 2025 without a stated trip count.[^zhwikiversity-109] None of this was
corroborated against a primary PTO or 大都會客運 announcement in this search,
and given that later history, the current total departure count is not
asserted from the 2010-era or earlier figures.
