## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 111 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 新莊 - 陽明山, weekday service suspended (停駛), holiday
service 0540–1600, a three-section fare (三段票) with buffer zones at
金陵女中-菜寮(重新路) and 捷運劍潭站(北藝中心)-士林國中, and 三重客運迴龍站 as
the service contact.[^ebus-111]

## Subroute variants

The TDX snapshot records three subroutes for this route: the plain 111
working, and two half-length short-turn variants, 111去程半 (outbound half)
and 111返程半 (return half).[^tdx-bus] The official schedule page shows
separate schedule tabs labelled 111去程半班表 and 111返程半班表, confirming
both short-turn workings against the primary source.[^ebus-111]

## History

A community wiki records a fare-segment adjustment at 金陵女中, changed to a
金陵女中-菜寮(重新路) buffer zone effective 6 October 2008.[^zhwikiversity-111]
This matches the buffer zone currently shown on the primary schedule
page.[^ebus-111] The same wiki records a headway reduction from 15–20 minutes
to 50–60 minutes (12 trips) from 1 January 2006, a dispatch-station
relocation from Shulin to Guishan effective 15 July 2010, a further headway
change on 16 February 2020, a reduction to 12 trips (50–60 minutes) from 5
February 2023, and stop changes at Shilin MRT Station and Shilin Residence
dated 29 June 2024 and 6 November 2025 respectively.[^zhwikiversity-111] None
of this was corroborated against a primary PTO or 三重客運 announcement in
this search.
