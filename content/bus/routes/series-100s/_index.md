These pages cover the 6 normalized TDX records classified series-100s.[^tdx-bus]
The full official catalogue places 108, 108區, 109, 111, 128 and 129 under
一般公車 (general bus service), the same heading covering the bulk of Taipei's
numbered city routes; none of the six carries a colour character (紅/藍/綠/棕/
橘/黃) or a 幹線 designation, so none was a candidate for the colour-feeder or
trunk groups.[^ebus-general]
