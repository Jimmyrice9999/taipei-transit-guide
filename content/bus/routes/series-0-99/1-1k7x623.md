## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 1 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 萬華 - 松仁路, weekday service 0520–2300 at peak intervals
of 12–15 minutes and off-peak intervals of 15–20 minutes, holiday service
0520–2300 at intervals of 15–20 minutes throughout, a single-section fare
(一段票), and 欣欣客運華江站 as the service contact.[^ebus-1] Weekday
dog-friendly (狗狗公車) departures run at 0630 and 1020, and holiday
dog-friendly departures run at 0800, 1100, 1300 and 1600.[^ebus-1] The TDX
snapshot separately records a distinct subroute named 1狗狗公車 alongside the
plain 1 working, consistent with the schedule page's dog-friendly
departures.[^tdx-bus]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records, including
confirmed rail-stop joins to stations BL10, G07 and G08; route length
remains TBC.[^tdx-bus] A community wiki (zh.wikiversity) lead traces route 1
to two predecessors — a Taipei Municipal Bus Administration route 1 and a
separate, independently operated Xin-Shin Bus route 1 — both said to have run
between 三張犂 and 人壽一村, with comparatively little corridor change since;
this could not be corroborated against a primary source in this search — the
臺北市公共運輸處's public 公車營運調整 document archive only extends back to 2024,
and no primary announcement documenting this predecessor history was found —
so the claim is left unconfirmed and does not appear above.[^tdx-bus]
