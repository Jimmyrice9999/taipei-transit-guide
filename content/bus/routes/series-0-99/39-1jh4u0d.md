## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 39 under 一般公車, as a routeid entry separate
from its 39夜 night variant.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives 三重 -
臺北車站, weekday service 0550–2300 and holiday service 0600–2300, a
one-section fare (一段票), weekday headway of 4–6 minutes at peak narrowing
to 5–10 minutes off-peak, a holiday headway of 10–15 minutes at both peak and
off-peak, a fixed interval after 2200, and 首都客運三重二站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-39] The page also states a flag-stop segment on 蘆洲區復興路323
巷 between 三重站 and 家樂福.[^ebus-39]

## History

A Wikiversity community entry gives a detailed operational history for route
39: it dates the route from 1941 as one of the oldest in 三重, records it
joining the Taipei joint-operation bus system on 30 April 1977 (第1階段), and
states that in March 1999 the traditional working was suspended and replaced
by a 39直達車 (express) and a 39副線 (branch), with the express becoming the
main line after June 1999.[^zhwikiversity-39] The same entry states the
branch was renumbered 539 in January 2001, and that the dispatch station
moved from 三重站 to 三重二站 on 1 June 2011.[^zhwikiversity-39] This account
was not independently verified against a primary PTO or operator
announcement in this search; the 2011 dispatch-station claim is at least
consistent with the current schedule page, which names 三重二站 as the
service contact today, though the schedule page does not itself date that
change.[^zhwikiversity-39][^ebus-39]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records and records
a single subroute for this route; route length remains TBC.[^tdx-bus] A
same-numbered route 39 in other Taiwanese cities (Taichung, Kaohsiung,
Taoyuan) was not found in this search.
