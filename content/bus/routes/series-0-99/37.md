## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 37 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 松德站 - 捷運台大醫院站, weekday service 0600–1900, holiday
service 0700–1900, a one-section fare (一段票), and 大都會客運松德站 as the
service contact.[^ebus-37] The page records 19 weekday departures and 13
holiday departures.[^ebus-37]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 松德站 in Xinyi, along 仁愛路 past
國父紀念館 and a long run of 仁愛路 cross-street stops, to end at 捷運台大
醫院站.[^tdx-bus] The return working also touches a stop literally named
捷運象山站 near its 松德站 origin.[^tdx-bus] Neither of these two
literally-named MRT stops is in the curated stop-ID join set as a
confirmed interchange for this route.[^tdx-bus]

A community wiki (zh.wikiversity) lead gives a longer operating history for
37: transferred from 台北市公車處 to 東南客運 in June 2002, several headway and
corridor adjustments through the 2000s and 2011, a change of operator to
大都會客運 on 12 January 2021 — the same date given for route 32's operator
change — and a claim that service was extended to serve 松德站 on 30 December
2025. The current schedule's 松德站 terminus is consistent with that claimed
2025 change, but neither date could be corroborated against a primary
臺北市政府交通局 or 公共運輸處 announcement in this search, so this history is
left unconfirmed and does not appear above.[^tdx-bus]
