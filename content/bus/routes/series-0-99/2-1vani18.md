## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 2 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 台北海大 - 臺大醫院, weekday and holiday service 0540–2300,
with 15 weekday departures and 10 holiday departures, a two-section fare
(兩段票), and 首都客運社子站 as the service contact.[^ebus-2] It gives the
segment buffer zone as 社子國小二 to 涼州重慶路口 outbound and 涼州重慶路口 to
洲美橋 inbound.[^ebus-2] The TDX snapshot's own stop-sequence data confirms
the 台北海大 terminus is Taipei University of Marine Technology (臺北海洋科技
大學), not the similarly-named Keelung institution.[^tdx-bus]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route length remains
TBC.[^tdx-bus] Stop names put the route near MRT stations O12 and R09,
but this project only presents an MRT interchange as confirmed where the curated stop-ID
join set says so, and neither is in it.[^tdx-bus] A community wiki (zh.wikiversity) lead states route 2's
predecessor ran between 社子 and 臺北車站 under the Taipei Municipal Bus
Administration, and that operating rights passed to Capital Bus (首都客運) in
June 2002; this could not be corroborated against a primary source in this
search — the 臺北市公共運輸處's public 公車營運調整 document archive only extends
back to 2024, and no 2002-dated primary announcement was found — so the
operator-transfer claim is left unconfirmed and does not appear above.[^tdx-bus]
