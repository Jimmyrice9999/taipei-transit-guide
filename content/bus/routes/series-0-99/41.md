## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 41 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 北士科 - 捷運大安站, weekday and holiday service both
0540–2300, a one-section fare (一段票), weekday headway of 12–15 minutes at
peak narrowing to 15–20 minutes off-peak, a holiday headway of 15–20 minutes
at both peak and off-peak, fixed evening departures at
19:00/19:40/20:20/21:00/21:40/22:20/23:00, and 大都會客運士林站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-41] The page states that the weekday 06:10 return working and
the 17:45 and 18:00 outbound workings detour via 中鼎第二總部大樓 and 遠雄商舟大樓,
and that the weekday 10:30 and 13:30 departures, and the holiday 09:30, 12:30
and 15:30 departures, are 狗狗友善公車 (dog-friendly bus) workings.[^ebus-41]
The TDX snapshot separately records four subroutes for this route — the plain
41 working, a return and an outbound working each routed via 中鼎大樓／遠雄商舟大樓,
and a dog-friendly working — consistent with the schedule page's stated
detours and dog-friendly departures.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A Wikiversity community entry gives a dated sequence of routing changes for
route 41: alignment changes tied to the opening of a Chang'an East Road–Bade
Road bus lane on 8 November 2006, further changes on 23 November and 25
December 2006, a further optimisation on 31 March 2008, a terminus change to
兒童新樂園 on 5 December 2014, and a 士林站 depot relocation with a new routing
via 福美站 and 三王宮 on 18 February 2021.[^zhwikiversity-41] This account was
not independently verified against a primary PTO or operator announcement in
this search; the 士林站 depot name is at least consistent with the current
schedule page's operator contact, 大都會客運士林站.[^zhwikiversity-41][^ebus-41]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records and records
four subroutes for this route; route length remains TBC.[^tdx-bus] A search
for same-numbered routes in other Taiwanese cities found that Taichung also
runs a route numbered 41, operated by 台中客運 between 公共資訊圖書館 and 修平科技
大學 — an unrelated route sharing only the number.[^zhwikipedia-taichung41] A
same-numbered route 41 was not found for Kaohsiung or Taoyuan in this
search.
