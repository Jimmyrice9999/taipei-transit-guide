## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 20 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its schedule
page gives 永春高中 - 青年公園 as the current termini, with weekday service
0530-2300 and holiday service 0550-2300, a single-section fare (一段票) over
the full route, and 大都會客運松職站 as the service contact.[^ebus-20] Weekday
headways run 8-12 minutes at peak and 15-20 minutes off-peak; holiday headways
run 15-20 minutes throughout the day.[^ebus-20] The schedule page also lists
five fixed late-evening departures, at 2100, 2130, 2200, 2230 and 2300, on
both weekday and holiday tables.[^ebus-20] The operator is Metropolitan Bus
Co., Ltd. (大都會客運).[^tdx-bus]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route
length beyond the schedule page's own departure and headway detail remains
TBC.[^tdx-bus] A Wikiversity lead gives a renumbering history for the route —
opened in 1946 as route 5, renumbered to 8 in October 1947, then to 20 in
April 1953 because the number 8 was said to evoke the Chinese Communist
Eighth Route Army (八路軍) — but the page cites no primary source for any of
these three dates, and this search did not locate a 1940s-50s primary record
of the Taipei bus network to check it against; the renumbering claim is left
unconfirmed and does not appear above.[^wikiversity-20]
