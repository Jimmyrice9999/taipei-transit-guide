## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 49 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 建國北路 - 東園, weekday service 0530–2200 and holiday
service 0545–2200, a one-section fare (一段票) with the 分段緩衝 (buffer zone)
field left blank, a weekday peak headway of 15 minutes, and 大都會客運建北站 as
the service contact.[^ebus-49]

## History

A community wikiversity entry states the route was established before 1967,
while Taipei was still a province-administered city, and was run throughout
by 台北市公車處; after that agency's privatisation it continued under its
successor 大都會客運, the current operator.[^zhwikiversity-49] Independently,
a Wikipedia entry on 大都會客運 confirms that company was formed 1 January
2004 as the privatised continuation of 臺北市公共汽車管理處, corroborating the
operator-succession part of that account without itself naming route
49.[^zhwiki-mtcbus] The wikiversity entry gives the route's early corridor as
臺北濱江街至萬華東園, and states that from 2 January 2007 the route was
restructured with a new 建國北路 (建北站) starting point and a new southern
extension via 康定路/萬大路, which raised weekday service from 36 to 53 trips
and holiday service from 19 to 25 — the current 建國北路–東園 termini match
this 2007 restructuring rather than the earlier corridor.[^zhwikiversity-49][^ebus-49]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records and
records a single subroute for this route; route length and off-peak/holiday
headway figures remain TBC.[^tdx-bus] The wikiversity entry used for the
sourced history above cites two 大都會客運 (mtcbus.com.tw) announcement pages
by URL, covering 2023 and 2025 station suspensions tied to MRT Wanda Line
construction; a direct fetch of both URLs did not return the cited
announcement text — both resolved to the operator's generic site-index page
rather than the archived notice — so those specific station-suspension
claims were not carried into this page and rest on the wikiversity account
alone where mentioned at all.[^zhwikiversity-49] A search for same-numbered
routes 42, 46 and 49 in Kaohsiung, Taichung and Taoyuan's city bus systems
found no plain-numbered match in any of the three cities' route lists.[^wiki-collision-check]
