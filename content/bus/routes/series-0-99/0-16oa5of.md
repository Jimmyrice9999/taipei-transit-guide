## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 0東 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 內湖 - 臺北車站, weekday and holiday service 0600–2130, a
two-section fare (兩段票), and 大都會客運內湖站 as the service contact.[^ebus-0east]
Both weekday and holiday services detour to serve 三總內湖站 (Tri-Service
General Hospital Neihu Station) between 0800 and 2130; weekday dog-friendly
(狗狗公車) departures run at 0920 and 1300, and holiday dog-friendly departures
run at 0900, 1030, 1330 and 1620.[^ebus-0east] The TDX snapshot separately
records a distinct subroute named 0東狗狗公車 alongside the plain 0東 working,
consistent with the schedule page's dog-friendly departures.[^tdx-bus]

Taipei's 0-prefix circular routes date to the city's post-consolidation
joint-operation numbering, which began around Taipei Main Station and named
0東, 0西, 0南 and 0北 for the four directions from that point; 0西 and 0北 were
later discontinued for low ridership, and their numbers were not reassigned
to other routes.[^storm-0series]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route length and
headway remain TBC.[^tdx-bus] Stop names put the route near MRT stations BL16, R06 and R09,
but this project only presents an MRT interchange as confirmed where the curated stop-ID
join set says so, and none of these three is in it.[^tdx-bus] A community wiki (zh.wikiversity) lead
describes 0東's predecessor as a Taipei Municipal Bus Administration circular
route between 民生東路社區 and 臺北車站, split into left and right branches, with
the right branch renumbered to joint-operation route 652 in September 2000;
this could not be corroborated against a primary source in this search — the
臺北市公共運輸處's public 公車營運調整 document archive only extends back to 2024,
and no 2000-dated primary announcement was found — so the renumbering claim
is left unconfirmed and does not appear above.[^tdx-bus]
