## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 72 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 麟光 - 大直, weekday and holiday service both 0600–2200, a
single-section fare (一段票) with no buffer zone, an hourly weekday timetable
of 17 departures (0600 through 2200 on the hour) and a 9-trip holiday
timetable across the same span, and 光華巴士麟光站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-72]

## History

A Wikiversity community entry states 光華巴士 was founded in 1969 and joined
the Taipei joint-operation bus system in April 1977, and has continuously
operated 72 since; this has not been independently verified against a
primary PTO or operator announcement in this search.[^zhwikiversity-72] The
same entry records a route adjustment from 15 August 2005, fleet deliveries
in 2006 and 2009, a first-departure delay from 05:45 to 06:00 from 9
November 2009, and, from 1 February 2011, the separation of this route's
former express working with joint-operation route 220 into a new route 680,
alongside an extension of this route's own end section to 麟光調度站 via
和平東路–臥龍街.[^zhwikiversity-72]
