## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 33 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 永春高中 - 大直美麗華, weekday service 0530–2210, holiday
service 0600–2200, a one-section fare (一段票), weekday peak headways of
15–20 minutes, and 大都會客運松職站 as the service contact.[^ebus-33] It also
carries a separate 33繞駛信義行政中心 (33, detour via Xinyi Administrative
Center) schedule alongside the plain 33 working.[^ebus-33] The TDX snapshot
separately records this as a second subroute, TPE162167, alongside the plain
33 working TPE118210.[^tdx-bus]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route
length and timetable detail beyond the headway range remain TBC.[^tdx-bus]
A community wiki (zh.wikiversity) lead states that 33 dropped a loop working
in August 1996, began the partial 信義行政中心 detour on 11 January 2024, and
renamed 仁愛國中 to 仁愛國中(外交學院) on 6 April 2026 and 松友新村 /
信義行政中心(奉天宮) to 信義行政中心(松友新村) / 捷運廣慈/奉天宮站(福德) on 1
August 2026. The current TDX stop list does carry 仁愛國中(外交學院),
信義行政中心(松友新村) and 捷運廣慈/奉天宮站(福德) as live stop names, consistent
with the renamed state the wiki describes, though TDX does not date the
change.[^tdx-bus] The 1996 loop cancellation and the exact 2024 detour date
could not be corroborated against a primary 臺北市政府交通局 or 公共運輸處
announcement in this search, so they are left unconfirmed and do not appear
above.[^tdx-bus]
