## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 14 under 一般公車, and does not cross-list it
under 低地板 (low-floor fleet), unlike the other three routes covered in this
group.[^ebus-general] Its schedule page gives 蘆洲 - 臺北車站 as the corridor,
with weekday and holiday service both running 0530–2300 on a two-section
fare (兩段票), the section boundary at 永安里－重安街口.[^ebus-14] Weekday
interval is 12–15 minutes at peak and 15–20 minutes off-peak; the holiday
interval is 15–20 minutes throughout the day.[^ebus-14] The operator contact
given is 大都會客運蘆洲站, 02-2282-0037.[^ebus-14]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the stop and geometry records; route length and
full timetable detail stay TBC.[^tdx-bus] A community wiki (zh.wikiversity)
lead gives a dense corridor history for 14: an origin in a 臺北市公車管理處
route extended into 三重 on 1950-03-15, renumbering to 14 in early 1952 after
a 1951-07-06 extension to 菜寮, a 1997-12-01 closure of the 菜寮 dispatch
station in favour of 蘆洲站, a 2000-12 renumbering of the then-mainline
working to 526 with a feeder working promoted to take the 14 number, and a
2012-08-25 corridor change adding stops via 長榮路, 三民路, 民族路 and 中正路 and
removing the old 蘆洲總站 stop in favour of 捷運蘆洲站. The wikiversity page
carries no cited primary source for any of these dates; this could not be
corroborated against a primary source in this search, so none of this
appears above.[^tdx-bus]
