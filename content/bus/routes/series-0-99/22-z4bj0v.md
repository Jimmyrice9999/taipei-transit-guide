## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 22 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its schedule
page gives 松德站 - 衡陽路 as the current termini, with both weekday and
holiday service running 0530-2300, a single-section fare (一段票) over the
full route, and 大都會客運松德站 as the service contact.[^ebus-22] Weekday
headways run 6-12 minutes at peak and 15-20 minutes off-peak; holiday
headways run 10-20 minutes throughout the day.[^ebus-22] The operator is
Metropolitan Bus Co., Ltd. (大都會客運).[^tdx-bus]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 松德站 in Xinyi, past stops literally
named 捷運信義安和站, 捷運大安站, 捷運大安森林公園站 and 捷運東門站, through
Zhongzheng to end near 衡陽路.[^tdx-bus] The return working also touches
捷運象山站 near 松德站 and 捷運善導寺站 near its 臺北車站 approach.[^tdx-bus] None
of these six literally-named MRT stops is in the curated stop-ID join set
as a confirmed interchange for this route.[^tdx-bus]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route
length beyond the schedule page's own departure and headway detail remains
TBC.[^tdx-bus] A Wikiversity lead describes an extensive pre-1977 numbering
history — a 1959 launch as route 20甲, renumbering to 30 in 1960, a 1961
closure, a 1969 revival under 大有巴士 as "大有2黃", and absorption into the
1977 joint-operation system as 22 under 大都會客運 — and separately describes
a 吳興街–台北車站(忠孝) 區間車 (shuttle) sub-service merged into the main route
on 26 January 2014. The wiki page cites no primary source for any of this,
and this search did not locate a primary record covering either the
pre-1977 numbering or the 2014 shuttle merger; both claims are left
unconfirmed and do not appear above.[^wikiversity-22]
