## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 46 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 松德站 - 圓環, weekday service 0500–2230 and holiday
service 0530–2230, a one-section fare (一段票) with the 分段緩衝 (buffer zone)
field left blank, a weekday peak headway of 15–20 minutes, and 大都會客運松德站
as the service contact.[^ebus-46]

## History

A community wikiversity entry states that from 14 February 2014 the route
was shortened to terminate near 捷運中山站, dropping a former extension
toward 臺北橋 (捷運大橋頭站), and that its westbound leg was rerouted from
承德路/民生路/吉林路 to 太原路/長安西路; and that from 27 July 2017 the route,
jointly with route 12, altered its return working to avoid 圓環（南京）站,
running via 承德路一段 instead.[^zhwikiversity-46] The route is currently
operated by 大都會客運, which was formed 1 January 2004 as the privatised
successor to 臺北市公共汽車管理處; this source does not itself state that
route 46 was among the routes carried over from that predecessor
agency.[^zhwiki-mtcbus]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records and
records a single subroute for this route; route length and off-peak/holiday
headway figures remain TBC.[^tdx-bus] A search-engine snippet attributed to a
zh.wikipedia article titled 台北聯營公車46路線 claimed the route opened 1
September 1963 as a 市營46路, and that a 1968 sub-route 46甲 was renumbered to
route 54 around 1970; a direct fetch of that article returned no content, and
its own deletion log confirms the page was deleted on 19 January 2021, so
this claim could not be checked against the source and is not used
above.[^wiki-deleted-46] The wikiversity entry used for the sourced history
above cites several 大都會客運 (mtcbus.com.tw) announcement pages by URL; a
direct fetch of two of those URLs (the 2015 terminus-change notice and the
2020 headway-trial notice) did not return the cited announcement text — both
resolved to the operator's generic site-index page rather than the archived
notice — so those specific citations rest on the wikiversity account alone
and were not independently verified.[^zhwikiversity-46] The same wikiversity
entry also cites an image titled 46路線-分段緩衝區說明圖 (a buffer-zone
diagram for route 46); this could not be fetched as text and is not reflected
in the Fare / transfer spec above, which instead reports the current
schedule page's blank buffer-zone field — both are published here rather
than resolved.[^zhwikiversity-46][^ebus-46] A search for same-numbered routes
42, 46 and 49 in Kaohsiung, Taichung and Taoyuan's city bus systems found no
plain-numbered match in any of the three cities' route lists.[^wiki-collision-check]
