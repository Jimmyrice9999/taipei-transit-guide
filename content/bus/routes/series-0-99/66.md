## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 66 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 捷運動物園站 - 松山車站, weekday and holiday service
0630–2230, a single-section fare (一段票), and 44 weekday trips against 34
holiday trips, with headway roughly 15–20 minutes at midday, 15-minute gaps
in the early morning, and 20–50 minutes after 1700.[^ebus-66] The service
contact is 木柵站.[^ebus-66]

## History

Two secondary sources agree that 66 launched as a new route with 12 electric
low-floor buses and was the first route in Greater Taipei to run entirely on
electric buses, but they give different launch dates.[^zhwikiversity-66][^bnext-66-electric]
A Wikiversity
community entry states the new line went into service on 29 January 2018,
assigned buses EAL-0001 through EAL-0013.[^zhwikiversity-66] A 數位時代
BusinessNext news article, dated 2018.10.29, states the first batch of 12
electric buses on the route entered service on 29 October 2018, with three
free trial days from 29 to 31 October, and gives the round-trip route length
as approximately 31.5 km.[^bnext-66-electric] Neither account has been
reconciled against a primary PTO or operator announcement in this
search, and the two dates — 29 January and 29 October — are published here
as a conflict rather than resolved in favour of either.[^zhwikiversity-66][^bnext-66-electric]
The Wikiversity entry separately records a 26 December 2020 frequency
change, a 23 January 2024 weekday-departure adjustment, a temporary
suspension of the northbound 萬興圖書館 stop from 17 May to 19 July 2024 for
roadworks fencing, and two new stops for Muzha Vocational High School added
13 December 2025.[^zhwikiversity-66]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records and records
a single subroute for this route; route length is given only by the
secondary BusinessNext article (31.5 km round trip) and is not corroborated
by a primary source, so it is left as TBC in the specs table rather than
asserted as a fact.[^tdx-bus][^bnext-66-electric] A search for a
same-numbered route run by another Taiwanese city bus operator found an
unrelated Taichung route 66 (潭子聯合辦公大樓 loop, with a 松竹車站 branch, run by
台中客運), sharing only the number with this Taipei route.[^zhwikipedia-taichung-66]
No same-numbered route was found for Kaohsiung or Hsinchu in this search.
