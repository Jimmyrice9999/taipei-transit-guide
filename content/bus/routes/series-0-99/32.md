## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 32 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 南港花園社區 - 松仁路, weekday and holiday service
0530–2130, a one-section fare (一段票), and 大都會客運松職站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-32] The page records 22 weekday departures and 15 holiday
departures.[^ebus-32]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 南港花園社區, past 南港公園/修德國小 and
stops literally named 捷運後山埤站 and 捷運永春站, through Xinyi's 松山車站/
松仁路 area to end on 松仁路 itself.[^tdx-bus] Neither literally-named MRT
stop is in the curated stop-ID join set as a confirmed interchange for
this route.[^tdx-bus]

A community wiki (zh.wikiversity) lead gives a longer operating history for
32: run by 東南客運 from 1 October 2002 after a transfer from 台北市公車處,
through several service-frequency cuts and a 2011 swap of the two termini, a
change of operator to 大都會客運 on 12 January 2021, and a further claim that
the current 南港花園社區–松仁路 pairing dates to 30 December 2025. This could
not be corroborated against a primary source in this search — 大都會客運's own
site did not render route-history content on fetch, and no dated 臺北市政府
交通局 or 公共運輸處 announcement confirming the operator handover or the
terminus change was found — so none of this history is stated above.[^tdx-bus]
