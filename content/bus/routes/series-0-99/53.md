## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 53 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 瓏山林社區 - 西松高中, a single-section fare (一段票), and
nine fixed weekday departures at 0550, 0705, 0900, 1050, 1250, 1600, 1750,
1940 and 2020, with no published interval band between them; holiday service
is marked suspended (假日：停駛).[^ebus-53] The service contact is 東湖站.[^ebus-53]

## History

A Wikiversity community log, not independently verified against a primary
PTO or operator announcement in this search, records a 20 February 2008
increase from 8 to 9 daily departures with adjusted hours, several vehicle
reassignments through 2009–2026, and schedule changes in 2011, 2015, 2019
and 1 July 2024, the last adding a Songshan Station stop on the return
leg.[^zhwikiversity-53] The same log states holiday service is suspended,
which matches the current schedule page.[^zhwikiversity-53][^ebus-53]
