## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 38區 under 一般公車, as a routeid entry separate
from its 38 parent route.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives 寶興街 -
捷運龍山寺站, weekday service 0600–2300, holiday service 0700–2000, a
one-section fare (一段票), and 大都會客運東園站 as the service contact.[^ebus-38qu]
The page lists 26 numbered weekday departures and 14 holiday departures as
individual timetable times, with no stated peak/off-peak interval
bands.[^ebus-38qu] The TDX snapshot records a single subroute for this route,
TPE108630, named 38(Shuttle) / 38區, and separately carries the plain 38
route as its own route entity in this dataset.[^tdx-bus]

## Committed stop sequence

The committed TDX snapshot carries 2 directional or variant stop sequences for 38Shuttle; each sequence below is ordered from its first boarding stop to its last; this is the current data record, not an inferred route history[^tdx-bus].

**38(Shuttle) (9 stops):** Dongyuan Park -> Yangsheng Temple -> Minhe St Entrance -> Baoxing St -> Xiyuan Rd Sec 2 -> Huajiang High School (Xiyuan) -> Fashion Institute of Taipei -> Dali Garments -> MRT Longshan Temple Sta[^tdx-bus]

**38(Shuttle) (12 stops):** Longshan Temple (Bopiliao) -> Guilin Rd -> Longshan Temple (Xiyuan) -> Dali Garments -> Fashion Institute of Taipei -> Huajiang High School (Xiyuan) -> Huannan Market (Xizang) -> Shuangyuan Jr High School -> Baoxing St -> Minhe St Entrance -> Yangsheng Temple -> Dongyuan Park[^tdx-bus]

The sequence is kept separate from the route shape: a stop is a boarding record, while the shape is the published alignment used for mapping. A missing stop name or a changed sequence is therefore a TDX-data issue, not evidence that the service was historically rerouted[^tdx-bus].

## Service record and evidence boundary

This normalized identity retains 1 direction/variant record, 1 operator record, and 2 published shape records in the snapshot[^tdx-bus]. The official catalogue and schedule citations attached to this route remain the authority for its service class, termini, operating span, headway and fare where those fields are printed.

The TDX route layer does not carry a sourced route-length history, opening date, corridor-selection rationale or dated operator-change record for this identity. Those fields remain TBC rather than being reconstructed from stop names or from a related route[^tdx-bus].

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route
length and a headway figure finer than the schedule page's individual
departure times remain TBC.[^tdx-bus] A Wikiversity community entry documents
a detailed operational history — opening, service-frequency changes and a
2013 shortening to 捷運大安森林公園站 following the Xinyi Line's opening — but
that history is for the plain parent route 38, a separate route entity in
this dataset, not for the 38區 shuttle covered by this page.[^zhwikiversity-38]
No dedicated dated history for the 38區 shuttle itself was found in this
search. A same-numbered route 38 in other Taiwanese cities (Taichung,
Kaohsiung, Taoyuan) was not found in this search.
