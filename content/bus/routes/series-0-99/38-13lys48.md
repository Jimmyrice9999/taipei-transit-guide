## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 38區 under 一般公車, as a routeid entry separate
from its 38 parent route.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives 寶興街 -
捷運龍山寺站, weekday service 0600–2300, holiday service 0700–2000, a
one-section fare (一段票), and 大都會客運東園站 as the service contact.[^ebus-38qu]
The page lists 26 numbered weekday departures and 14 holiday departures as
individual timetable times, with no stated peak/off-peak interval
bands.[^ebus-38qu] The TDX snapshot records a single subroute for this route,
TPE108630, named 38(Shuttle) / 38區, and separately carries the plain 38
route as its own route entity in this dataset.[^tdx-bus]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route
length and a headway figure finer than the schedule page's individual
departure times remain TBC.[^tdx-bus] A Wikiversity community entry documents
a detailed operational history — opening, service-frequency changes and a
2013 shortening to 捷運大安森林公園站 following the Xinyi Line's opening — but
that history is for the plain parent route 38, a separate route entity in
this dataset, not for the 38區 shuttle covered by this page.[^zhwikiversity-38]
No dedicated dated history for the 38區 shuttle itself was found in this
search. A same-numbered route 38 in other Taiwanese cities (Taichung,
Kaohsiung, Taoyuan) was not found in this search.
