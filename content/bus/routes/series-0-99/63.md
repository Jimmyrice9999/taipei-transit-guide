## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 63 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 內湖舊宗路 - 臺北車站, weekday and holiday service
0520–2300, a single-section fare (一段票), a weekday headway of 7–10 minutes
at peak and 10–15 minutes off-peak, a holiday headway of 15–20 minutes at
both peak and off-peak, and fixed late departures at 2130, 2200, 2230 and
2300.[^ebus-63] The service contact is 內湖站.[^ebus-63]

## History

A Wikiversity community entry, not independently verified against a primary
PTO or operator announcement in this search, states 63 opened 25 August 1971
as a branch of route 54 (54副線), was renamed 59 on 16 July 1975 for easier
public identification, and joined the joint-operation bus system on 31 May
1977 under its current number, then departing from the former Songshan
Station plaza.[^zhwikiversity-63] The same entry records a 22 December 2003
extension of the eastern terminus to the Neihu depot near Minquan Bridge and
of the western terminus to Taipei Main Station.[^zhwikiversity-63] The
western-terminus claim cross-checks against the current schedule page, which
gives 臺北車站 as one endpoint.[^zhwikiversity-63][^ebus-63]
