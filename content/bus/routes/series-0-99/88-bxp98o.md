## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 88 under 一般公車, as a routeid entry separate
from its 88區 shuttle variant.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives
重陽路 - 臺北車站, weekday and holiday service both 0610–2130, a single-section
fare (一段票) with no buffer zone (分段緩衝：無), a 16-trip weekday timetable
spaced 20–30 minutes apart in the early morning (0610–0700), roughly hourly
from 0820 to 1720, and widening to 50–120 minutes through the evening, and
大有巴士興中站 as the service contact.[^ebus-88] The page does not
cross-reference route 88區.[^ebus-88]

## History

A Wikiversity community entry states the route was originally named 信義幹線;
this has not been independently verified against a primary PTO or operator
announcement in this search.[^zhwikiversity-88] The same entry records that
from 1 January 2007 首都客運 substitute-operated 10 vehicles for three months
after the route received a Class C rating in a bus evaluation, that 大有巴士
resumed operation from 1 April 2007, that both directions were rerouted
through 南港國宅 from 1 October 2007, that 信義幹線 was renamed 88 on 1 June
2017, and that a new shuttle service between 南港花園社區 and 臺北車站 was
established on 14 February 2018.[^zhwikiversity-88] That 2018 shuttle
establishment date is consistent with this project's separate research on
route 88區's own commencement date.[^zhwikiversity-88]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records and records
a single subroute for this route; route length remains TBC.[^tdx-bus] A
same-numbered Kaohsiung city bus route also exists — 88建國幹線, between
保華站 and 捷運鹽埕埔站 — unrelated to this Taipei route beyond sharing the
route number.[^vmap-khh88] A search for a same-numbered route in Taichung or
Taoyuan under the number 88 was not found in this search.
