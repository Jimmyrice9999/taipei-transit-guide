## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 28 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its schedule
page gives 大直 - 市政府 as the current termini, with weekday service
0545-1930 and holiday service 0730-1700, a single-section fare (一段票) over
the full route, and 指南客運故宮站 as the service contact.[^ebus-28] The page
lists 16 weekday departures and 2 holiday departures, and records a standing
detour to serve 三總內湖站 (Tri-Service General Hospital, Neihu branch)
between 08:00 and 21:30 on both weekday and holiday workings.[^ebus-28] The
operator is Zhinan Bus Co., Ltd. (指南客運).[^tdx-bus]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route
length and a headway interval beyond the listed departure counts remain
TBC.[^tdx-bus] A Wikipedia lead gives the operator's own history — founded
1953 as 指南遊覽汽車股份有限公司 running two tour buses between 指南宮 and 木柵,
renamed 指南汽車客運股份有限公司 in 1966, and merged into 中興巴士集團 on 1
September 1996 — but the article carries no inline citation for any of these
three dates, and neither the operator's own site nor its group's site
published a matching history page in this search; the founding, rename and
merger dates are left unconfirmed and do not appear above.[^wikipedia-zhinanbus]
A separate Wikiversity lead lists a run of dated adjustments to route 28
between 2010 and 2023 — fleet renewal, a 2013 terminal-loop change near
Taipei 101, and frequency cuts in 2021 and 2023 — again with no cited
primary source; this search did not locate a matching primary announcement
for any of these, so they are left unconfirmed and do not appear
above.[^wikiversity-28]
