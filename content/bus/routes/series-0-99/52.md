## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 52 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 景明街口 - 聯合醫院中興院區(塔城), weekday and holiday
service 0530–2300, a single-section fare (一段票), a headway that varies
through the day from roughly 20 to 40 minutes and narrows to about 20 minutes
between 1000 and 1520 on weekdays, and 景德站 as the service contact.[^ebus-52]

## History

A Wikiversity community entry states 52's predecessor was 欣欣客運's own
Route 22 between 東園 and 公館, opened 15 June 1970, and that it was renamed
52 when it joined the joint-operation bus system on 19 June 1977; this has
not been independently verified against a primary PTO or operator
announcement in this search.[^zhwikiversity-52] The same entry records a 28
July 2006 shortening of the route to 中興醫院 with a headway improvement, and
an 18 November 2021 change of dispatch station from 興隆站 to 景德站 alongside
a frequency reduction, followed by a further reduction on 19 December
2022.[^zhwikiversity-52] The dispatch-station claim cross-checks against the
current schedule page, which names 景德站 as the operator contact.[^ebus-52]
