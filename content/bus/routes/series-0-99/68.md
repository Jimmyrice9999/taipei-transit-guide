## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 68 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 洲美里 - 捷運劍潭站, weekday service 0610–2200 and holiday
service 0610–1830, a single-section fare (一段票) with no buffer zone, 15
weekday and 7 holiday scheduled departures, and 首都客運士林站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-68] The page also notes a diversion via 福美路 tied to
「北士科開發案前期填土整地工程」(preparatory infill works for the North Shilin
Tech Park project), with stops added at 三王宮 and 福美站 in both
directions.[^ebus-68]

## History

A Wikiversity community entry states 68 began operation under 首都客運 on 15
June 2002, taking over a route released when the city's public-bus authority
was privatised; this has not been independently verified against a primary
PTO or operator announcement in this search.[^zhwikiversity-68] The same
entry records a secondary line (副線) between 洲美運動公園 and 捷運劍潭站
discontinued on 13 October 2018, and a service reduction from 23 December
2018 that cut weekday departures from 24 to 15 and holiday departures from 19
to 7, with the span changing to weekday 06:10–22:00 and holiday
06:10–18:30.[^zhwikiversity-68] That 2018 end state matches the current
schedule page's weekday 0610–2200 and holiday 0610–1830 spans, a cross-check
between the secondary historical account and the current primary
schedule.[^zhwikiversity-68][^ebus-68]
