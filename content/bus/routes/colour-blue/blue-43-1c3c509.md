## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 藍43延南天母 under 捷運藍線接駁公車.[^ebus-blue] Its full schedule page gives 三峽 - 捷運永寧站, weekday service 0830–1720, holiday service 0630–1530, one-section fare and 臺北客運三峽二站 as the service contact — the same termini and operator as plain 藍43 (`blue-43-cpmwhl`); the published name adds 延南天母 even though the schedule page's own termini text does not mention 南天母.[^ebus-bl43ext]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 三峽二站, past 姑娘廟/三樹路/農業改良場,
through 柑園/民權街口/紅瓦厝/柑城橋, then continues past plain 藍43's own
捷運永寧站 terminus onward through 承天路/科技公司/登山口, ending at 南天母廣場
— the extension the route's own 延南天母 name describes, even though it is
absent from the schedule page's stated termini.[^tdx-bus] One confirmed
interchange is recorded in the curated stop-ID join set: Yongning (BL02),
matching plain 藍43's own terminus rather than the extended route's actual
end.[^tdx-bus]
