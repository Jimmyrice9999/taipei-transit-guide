These pages cover the 38 records classified colour-blue after this run's correction.[^tdx-bus]
One TDX record, 藍海2線先導公車, started with 藍 and was mechanically classified
colour-blue, but the official catalogue files it under 捷運先導公車 (MRT Pioneer
Bus), a distinct category. It is excluded from this group and is not linked to
the Bannan Line; see docs/research/bus/routes/colour-blue.md for the full check.[^ebus-blue]
