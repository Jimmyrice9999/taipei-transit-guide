## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 950 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 中和 - 內湖科學園區, weekday-only service, with holiday
service suspended (停駛), a two-section fare (兩段票) with a buffer zone
between 捷運六張犁站 and 市政府(市府), and 中興巴士中和站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-950]

The confirmed stop sequence matches the press release's own corridor
description closely: it starts among 中和's residential blocks (連城路,
中和廟口), crosses the Xindian River near 捷運永安市場站 and 福和橋, follows
基隆路 past 捷運六張犁站 and 世貿中心 into Xinyi, then continues north through
內湖's 瑞光路 office corridor to end at 捷運西湖站, by Neihu Science
Park.[^tdx-bus] Four separate MRT stations are named along the way, and none
carries a confirmed join in the curated set.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A New Taipei City Government press release announces the route as a
"雙北快速公車" (cross-city express bus) opening 15 April 2014, naming the
operator 中興大業巴士股份有限公司, giving a roughly 70-minute run time via
福和橋, the 基隆路 elevated road, 捷運六張犁站, 臺北市政府 and 麥帥一橋 to
內湖科學園區, a two-section fare totalling NT$30, weekday operation
0600–2200, 28 fixed daily trips, and holiday service suspended
(例假日停駛).[^ntpc-950-launch] A community wiki states the same 15 April
2014 date but frames it as a renumbering of a predecessor route,
內科通勤專車1路, saying service expanded from five one-direction trips each
morning and afternoon to 28 trips running both directions daily, while
holiday service remained suspended.[^zhwikiversity-950] The press release
does not mention a predecessor route, so the renumbering claim rests on the
wiki alone.

## Conflicts

The primary press release states 28 fixed daily trips and weekday hours
0600–2200 at the 2014 launch.[^ntpc-950-launch] The official schedule page,
fetched directly, lists 17 daily departures running 0600–2000.[^ebus-950]
Both figures describe route 950, but the schedule page's departure count and
end time are lower than the launch-day press release's; TDX records three
separate subroutes under 950 (950路, 950去程半, 950返程半), so the two
figures may be counting different subsets of the route's workings rather
than disagreeing about the same one.[^tdx-bus]
