## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 902區 under 一般公車, as a routeid entry separate
from plain 902.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives 麟光 - 松山機場,
weekday-only service 0920–1450 with no holiday service, a one-section fare
(一段票), 11 weekday departures at irregular intervals of roughly 10 to 80
minutes, and the same 指南客運麟光站 contact as plain 902.[^ebus-902qu] The page
states verbatim that it commenced operation 24 June 2024: 「本路線自113年6月24日
起實施」.[^ebus-902qu]

The confirmed stop sequence covers only the first third of parent route
902's corridor: from 麟光 in Wenshan it crosses Da'an past 捷運忠孝敦化站
and 臺北小巨蛋 and stops short at 松山機場 (Songshan Airport), well before
902's own run continues on into Neihu, Dazhi, Shilin and Beitou.[^tdx-bus]
Like 902, it passes 捷運六張犁站 and 捷運忠孝敦化站 by name without a
confirmed MRT join for either.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A secondary Wikiversity account of the plain 902 route states that, on the
same date, 902 "adjusted its off-peak schedule and trialled a newly-established
shuttle service" (2024年6月24日起調整離峰班次，並試辦新闢區間車).[^zhwikiversity-902]
This reads consistently with 902區's own stated commencement date, but the
Wikiversity source does not itself name 902區, so the identification of the
two is an inference from matching dates between a primary and a secondary
source rather than a claim either source states outright.
