## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 915 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 景美 - 捷運市政府站, weekday service 0530–2300 and
holiday service 0600–2300, a single-section fare (一段票), and 欣欣客運景德站
as the service contact, with 37 weekday and 26 holiday departures
listed.[^ebus-915]

## History

A community wiki states 915 opened as a new route on 22 August 2005.[^zhwikiversity-915]
This is a secondary source and was not corroborated against a primary PTO or
欣欣客運 announcement in this search.

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route
