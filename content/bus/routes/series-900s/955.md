## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 955 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 汐止 - 捷運劍南路站, weekday-only service 0630–1900
with holiday service suspended (停駛), a two-section fare (兩段票) with an
outbound buffer zone between 天良電視台 and 環東大道口 and a return buffer
zone between 環東大道口 and 富信飯店, and 新北客運五堵站 as the service
contact, with 12 weekday departures listed.[^ebus-955]

## History

A Central News Agency report states the route opened 1 July 2016, running
from 摩天鎮 in 汐止 via 環東大道、堤頂大道、北安路、敬業三路 to 捷運劍南路站,
with a free-ride period for the first three days of service and a roughly
60-minute run time.[^cna-955-launch]
