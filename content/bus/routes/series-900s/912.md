## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 912 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 深坑 - 市政府, weekday and holiday service 0545–2300,
weekday peak frequency 7–10 minutes and off-peak 12–20 minutes, holiday
frequency 15–20 minutes narrowing to 20–30 minutes before 0900 and after
2200, a single-section fare (一段票), and 指南客運深坑站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-912]

The confirmed stop sequence starts at 石碇高中 (Shiding Senior High School)
in the hills above 深坑, runs down through 深坑 itself — past 深坑區公所
(the district office) and 深坑郵局 — then follows 信義快速道路 (the Xinyi
Expressway bus corridor the 2005 renumbering below was built around)
directly into central Taipei, ending at 捷運市政府站 by Taipei City
Hall.[^tdx-bus] That final stop is the only MRT-named one, and the curated
stop-ID join set does not confirm it.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A community wiki states 912 was renumbered from a predecessor route 660區間車
and extended to serve 臺北市政府, effective 14 May 2005, to coincide with the
opening of the 信義快速道路 bus corridor.[^zhwikiversity-912] This is a
secondary source and was not corroborated against a primary PTO or 指南客運
announcement in this search.
