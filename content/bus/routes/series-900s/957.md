## Classification, corridor and operators

The official catalogue lists 957 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 淡海新市鎮 - 捷運劍南路站, weekday service 0530–2200
and holiday service 0600–2200, weekday peak frequency 10–20 minutes and
off-peak 20–30 minutes (holiday runs 20–30 minutes throughout), a
two-section fare (兩段票) with a buffer zone between 捷運竹圍站 and
大度立德路口, and lists both 淡水客運新市站 and 指南客運新市站 as service
contacts, sharing the number 02-2805-4870 — confirming the route is jointly
operated by the two companies.[^ebus-957] The page also notes the weekday
0900 return working uses non-low-floor vehicles supplied from New Taipei
City to support operations.[^ebus-957]

## History

A community wiki states 957 opened as a new route on 28 November 2016,
jointly operated by 淡水客運 and 指南客運, matching the dual-operator
arrangement shown on the current schedule page.[^zhwikiversity-957] It
further records a first-departure time change from 0600 to 0530 effective
1 January 2017, an outbound route adjustment on 20 July 2019 that removed a
light-rail-station stop and added a new stop on 崁頂三路, a headway
adjustment on 18 May 2022, and low-floor fleet allocations — 10 buses to
淡水客運 in September 2017 and electric low-floor buses to 指南客運 in
March 2022.[^zhwikiversity-957] A Wikipedia entry on 淡水客運 confirms the
two operators belong to the same corporate group, 中興巴士集團, along with
中興巴士、光華巴士、新北客運 and 基隆客運, though it does not name route
957 specifically.[^zhwikipedia-tamshuibus]
