## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 902 under 一般公車, as a routeid entry separate
from its 902區 shuttle variant.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives
麟光 - 捷運石牌站, weekday service 0530–2200 and holiday service 0600–2130, a
two-section fare (兩段票) with a buffer zone between 美麗華 and 銘傳大學, peak
headway 12–15 minutes narrowing from an off-peak 20–30 minutes and a fixed
30-minute interval after 2000, and 指南客運麟光站 as the service contact.[^ebus-902]

## History

Two independently authored secondary accounts — a Wikiversity community entry
and a personal enthusiast blog — give the same operator-history sequence: the
route opened 16 July 1998 under 台北市公車處 (the forerunner of today's 大都會
客運), moved to joint operation by 指南客運 and 光華巴士 from 1 April 1999, and
became 指南客運's sole operation from 1 January 2001 after 光華巴士
withdrew.[^zhwikiversity-902][^blog-902] Neither account was independently
verified against a primary PTO or operator announcement in this search. The
Wikiversity entry additionally records a 27 December 2008 – 1 March 2009
holiday trial extension to 松山機場, and states that from 24 June 2024 the
route adjusted its off-peak schedule and trialled a newly-established 區間車
(shuttle) service — the latter falling on the same date the 902區 schedule
page itself states as its commencement, a cross-check between a primary and a
secondary source.[^zhwikiversity-902][^ebus-902qu]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records and records
three subroutes for this route; route length and a headway figure finer than
the schedule page's peak/off-peak bands remain TBC.[^tdx-bus]
