## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 905副 under 一般公車, as a routeid entry separate
from plain 905.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives 錦繡 - 民生社區,
weekday-only service 0710–1705 with no holiday service, a two-section fare
(兩段票) with a buffer zone between 陽光運動園區(安和花市) and 成功國宅, four
weekday departures at 0710, 0745, 1635 and 1705, and 指南客運錦繡站 as the
service contact.[^ebus-905fu]

The confirmed stop sequence starts in New Taipei's Ankeng area (安坑), calling
at 輕軌安康站 and 輕軌陽光運動公園站 — both named for Ankeng Light Rail stops —
before crossing into Taipei at 敦化和平路口 and running the length of 敦化南/
北路 through Da'an and Songyi to end at 民生社區 (Minsheng Community) in
Songshan.[^tdx-bus] Despite the two named light-rail stops and 捷運忠孝敦化站
partway along, the curated stop-ID join set confirms no MRT or light-rail
connection for this route.[^tdx-bus]

## Research status

TDX records two subroutes for this route: plain 905副 and 905副返程半 (return
half).[^tdx-bus] Searching 台北市 公車905路 歷史 沿革 指南客運 in Chinese found a
Wikiversity entry recording schedule and fleet adjustments to both the 正線
(905) and 副線 (905副) through 2024 — including a 2 February 2009 副線 route
change via 安和路 and 中安大橋, and a 13 January 2016 副線 schedule change to a
0625–1705 span — but no renumbering or predecessor-route claim for
either.[^zhwikiversity-905] This entry was not independently verified against
a primary PTO or 指南客運 announcement in this search. Route length remains
TBC.[^tdx-bus]
