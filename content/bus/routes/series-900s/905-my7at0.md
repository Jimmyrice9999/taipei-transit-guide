## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 905 under 一般公車, as a routeid entry separate
from its 905副 variant.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives 錦繡 -
民生社區, weekday and holiday service 0530–2130, a two-section fare (兩段票)
with a buffer zone between 中正環河路口 and 成功國宅, 35 weekday departures
against 22 holiday departures, and 指南客運錦繡站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-905]

## Research status

TDX records three subroutes for this route: 905路 (plain), 905去程半 (outbound
half) and 905返程半 (return half).[^tdx-bus] Searching 台北市 公車905路 歷史 沿革
指南客運 in Chinese found a Wikiversity entry recording schedule and fleet
adjustments to both the 正線 (905) and 副線 (905副) through 2024, including a
23 October 2001 正線 route change via 北新路 and 中正路, but no renumbering or
predecessor-route claim.[^zhwikiversity-905] This entry was not independently
verified against a primary PTO or 指南客運 announcement in this search. Route
length and a single system-wide headway figure remain TBC.[^tdx-bus]
