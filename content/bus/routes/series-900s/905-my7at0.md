## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 905 under 一般公車, as a routeid entry separate
from its 905副 variant.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives 錦繡 -
民生社區, weekday and holiday service 0530–2130, a two-section fare (兩段票)
with a buffer zone between 中正環河路口 and 成功國宅, 35 weekday departures
against 22 holiday departures, and 指南客運錦繡站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-905]

The confirmed stop sequence runs the same 安坑/安康corridor as its 905副
variant at the New Taipei end, but continues further into central Xindian —
past 捷運新店區公所站, 崇光中學 and 耕莘醫院 — before crossing into Taipei on
the same 敦化南/北路 alignment through Da'an and Songyi to 民生社區.[^tdx-bus]
As with 905副, a named MRT stop along the way — 捷運新店區公所站 this time,
rather than a light-rail one — carries no confirmed join in the curated
set.[^tdx-bus]
