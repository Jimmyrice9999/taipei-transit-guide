## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 949 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 深坑 - 捷運古亭站, weekday and holiday service
0600–2130, a two-section fare (兩段票) with a buffer zone at 國道3甲, and
指南客運深坑站 as the service contact, with 35 weekday and 22 holiday
departures listed.[^ebus-949]

The confirmed stop sequence runs the same 深坑–石碇 hill corridor as 912 as
far as 自來水處(辛亥), then continues past 臺大計資中心 and 大安森林公園
rather than turning off toward 市政府, ending instead at 捷運古亭站 —
consistent with the press release's own framing of 949 as a faster
alternative into the Guting/Taida side of the city rather than a duplicate
of 912's Xinyi corridor.[^tdx-bus][^ntpc-949-launch] 捷運台電大樓站 and
捷運古亭站 are both named on the route, and neither carries a confirmed join
in the curated set.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A New Taipei City Government press release announces 949 as a new
"跨市快速公車" (cross-city express bus) opening 29 March 2014, connecting
深坑 to 捷運臺電大樓站 and 捷運古亭站 within 30 minutes, with the first bus
departing 0600 and the last at 2300, and a three-day free trial from 29–31
March 2014.[^ntpc-949-launch] A community wiki independently gives the same
29 March 2014 launch date and records the route received 10 new KINGLONG
low-floor buses at launch.[^zhwikiversity-949] The wiki further records
headway reductions on 24 June 2015 and 4 May 2018, and a weekday/holiday
trip-count reduction on 23 November 2020.[^zhwikiversity-949]
