## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 903 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 東湖 - 忠孝東路, weekday and holiday service 0600–2100, a
one-section fare (一段票), and 欣欣客運東湖站 as the service contact.[^ebus-903]

The confirmed stop sequence runs entirely within Neihu and northern Da'an:
from 東湖 (East Lake) it threads two road tunnels — 民權隧道 and a second,
unnamed on the stop list, both carrying 民權東路 under high ground — past
國防醫學大學 (National Defense Medical Center) and 內湖行政大樓 (the Neihu
district office), before crossing into 敦化北路 and ending near
忠孝東路.[^tdx-bus] 捷運葫洲站 and 捷運忠孝復興站/捷運南京復興站 (on the
不經三總 return working only) are the only MRT-named stops, and none carries
a confirmed join in the curated set.[^tdx-bus]

## The 經三總 / 不經三總 distinction

The TDX snapshot records two subroutes under this route, named 903不經三總
(does not pass 三總, Tri-Service General Hospital) and 903經三總 (passes
三總).[^tdx-bus] The official schedule page explains this as a time-of-day and
day-of-week split on a single numbered service, not two permanently separate
routings: 「平日0600-0800及1600後直行民權東路經福華商圈，0800-1600時繞駛三總醫院
週六0800-1200時一班繞駛三總醫院，一班直行民權東路經福華商圈；週日所有班次不繞駛
三總院區」.[^ebus-903] On weekdays the 0600–0800 and post-1600 workings run
direct via 民權東路 through the 福華商圈 (matching the 不經三總 subroute); the
0800–1600 workings divert via 三總 (matching the 經三總 subroute); Saturday
0800–1200 splits one working each way; and all Sunday workings skip
三總.[^ebus-903]
