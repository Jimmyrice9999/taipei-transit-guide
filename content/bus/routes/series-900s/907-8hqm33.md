## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 907 under 一般公車, as a routeid entry separate
from its 907通勤 variant.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives 萬華 -
崇義高中, weekday service 0530–2000 and holiday service 0645–1845, a
two-section fare charged throughout because the route runs on the freeway
(「行經高速公路，一律以兩段票計費」), a buffer zone at the 國道三甲交流道, 36
weekday departures against 12 holiday departures, and 欣欣客運華江站 as the
service contact.[^ebus-907] The page states that passengers required to
detrain early under highway regulations receive a same-day continuation
voucher (續乘券) for one further complimentary trip.[^ebus-907]
