## Classification and corridor

The full official catalogue lists 1717 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Taipei's
Public Transport Office names six service categories for the joint-operation
network — 一般路線 general routes, 幹線公車 trunk buses, 捷運接駁公車 metro
feeders, 山區公車 hill routes, 休閒公車 leisure routes and 市民小巴 community
minibuses[^pto-intro] — so 1717 is a numbered service outside the five more
specific classes, not a route with no classification at all. Its four-digit
number also sits outside every leading-digit band this project has sourced
for Taipei's numbering scheme, which only documents behaviour up to three
digits plus a handful of named prefixes; what "17xx" itself denotes remains
unestablished.[^tdx-bus]

The route's confirmed stop sequence runs from the NTU Hospital MRT Station
(R09) area of central Taipei, past the Executive Yuan, north through
Zhongshan past the Taipei Fine Arts Museum, through Jiantan (R15) and Shilin
(R16) near the Shilin Official Residence, then climbs into Yangmingshan —
calling at the Yangmingshan National Park Headquarters, the Yangmingshuwu
former presidential retreat and the Xiaoyoukeng service station — before
descending past a run of named hot-spring stops (馬槽溫泉, 八煙溫泉) to end at
the Jinshan District Office and Jinshan Post Office on the New Taipei
coast.[^tdx-bus] That is an unusually long, rural corridor for a numbered
Taipei city route: it starts among the capital's hospitals and ministries and
finishes in a coastal district of a different municipality, over a mountain
national park in between. The TDX record's six direction/variant entries
distinguish whether a working detours via Jiantan MRT Station and via the
Xiaoyoukeng service station, rather than describing six separate
corridors.[^tdx-bus] The confirmed stop-ID join set puts the route directly
at three MRT stations along its Taipei end — R09, R15 and R16.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “捷運台大醫院站 - 金山郵局”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0600 - 1840 [假日] 0600 - 1840”, fare “依里程計費”.[^ebus-route-1717]
