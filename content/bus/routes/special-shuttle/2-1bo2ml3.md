## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 內科快線2 under 內科專車 (通勤公車、內科專車、南軟專車與其他).[^ebus-special]
The normalized TDX record retains 8 direction/variant records for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “捷運市政府站 - 內湖科技園區”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0730 - 2000 [假日] 停駛”, fare “一段票”.[^ebus-route-2-1bo2ml3] It currently lists weekday service and suspended holiday service.[^ebus-route-2-1bo2ml3]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 捷運市政府站, past 陽光抽水站/金泰
公園/基湖站/基湖路口/仁寶大樓/瑞光港墘路口/瑞光路/公館山, ending at 麗寶
大樓, with some workings continuing to 時報廣場/民權大橋 in the Neihu
Science Park.[^tdx-bus] One confirmed interchange is recorded in the
curated stop-ID join set: Taipei City Hall (BL18), matching the
route's own terminus.[^tdx-bus] TDX records eight direction/variant
records for this service split across six separate operators — 大南汽
車, 光華巴士, 三重客運, 東南客運, 臺北客運 and 中興巴士 — for what the stop
sequences show as the same physical corridor, the widest operator
split of any route in this batch.[^tdx-bus]
