## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 通勤24 under 通勤公車 (通勤公車、內科專車、南軟專車與其他).[^ebus-special]
The normalized TDX record retains 1 direction/variant record for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “政大附中 - 臺北車站”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 1615 - 1615 [假日] 停駛”, fare “三段票”.[^ebus-route-24]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 政大附中(校內), past 捷運動物園站/
富陽街口/捷運六張犁站(和平)/復興南路口/溫州街口/捷運古亭站(和平)/博物館
(襄陽), ending at 臺北車站(忠孝).[^tdx-bus] Five confirmed interchanges
are recorded in the curated stop-ID join set: Taipei Main Station
(BL12/R10), Taipei Zoo (BR01), Liuzhangli (BR07), Technology Building
(BR08) and Guting (G09/O05), spanning nearly the whole nine-stop
corridor — the same corridor confirmed for the separate 通勤22, which
runs the shorter leg to Guting.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a single
subroute under one operator, 指南客運, publishing a single afternoon
departure on a three-stage fare, tied with the separate 通勤26 for the
highest fare tier confirmed in this batch, well above the one- and
two-stage fares published for most other routes.[^tdx-bus]
