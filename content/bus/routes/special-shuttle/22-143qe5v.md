## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 通勤22 under 通勤公車 (通勤公車、內科專車、南軟專車與其他).[^ebus-special]
The normalized TDX record retains 1 direction/variant record for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “捷運古亭站(和平) - 政大附中”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0710 - 0710 [假日] 停駛”, fare “一段票”.[^ebus-route-22-143qe5v] It currently lists weekday service and suspended holiday service.[^ebus-route-22-143qe5v]

At nine stops, the confirmed stop sequence is among the shortest in
this batch, running from 捷運古亭站(和平), past 溫州街口/復興南路口/臥龍
街, through 捷運六張犁站(和平), along 富陽街口/捷運動物園站/萬興國小,
ending at 政大附中.[^tdx-bus] Four confirmed interchanges are recorded
in the curated stop-ID join set: Taipei Zoo (BR01), Liuzhangli (BR07),
Technology Building (BR08) and Guting (G09/O05), spanning nearly the
whole short corridor.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a single subroute under
one operator, 欣欣客運, with its published schedule field showing only
one departure time, matching the pattern already found on the
separate 通勤12.[^tdx-bus]
