## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 花季專車124 under 活動專車 (通勤公車、內科專車、南軟專車與其他).[^ebus-special]
The normalized TDX record retains 1 direction/variant record for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “陽明山 - 小油坑”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0800 - 1600 [假日] 0800 - 1600”, fare “一段票”.[^ebus-route-124]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 陽明山總站, past 陽明山國家公園管理
處/童軍站/磺仔外/氣象台/陽明書屋/竹子湖派出所/頂湖山/游園站/百拉卡公路入
口, ending at 小觀音棧/小油坑服務站, entirely within Yangmingshan
National Park.[^tdx-bus] The curated stop-ID join set records zero
confirmed interchanges for this route.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a
single subroute under one operator, 大都會客運, and is the only route
in this batch filed under 活動專車 (event shuttle) rather than 通勤公車,
內科專車 or 南軟專車.[^tdx-bus][^ebus-special]
