## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 通勤30 under 通勤公車 (通勤公車、內科專車、南軟專車與其他).[^ebus-special]
The normalized TDX record retains 1 direction/variant record for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “東吳大學外雙溪校區 - 東吳大學城中校區”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 1120 - 1210 [假日] 停駛”, fare “二段票”.[^ebus-route-30]

The confirmed stop sequence runs outbound from 東吳大學, past 捷運士林
站(中正)/士林國中/士林區行政中心(中正), ending at 東吳大學城中校區, with
the return working starting instead from 捷運北門站.[^tdx-bus] One
confirmed interchange is recorded in the curated stop-ID join set:
Beimen (G13), matching the route's own return-leg starting point.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a single subroute under two operators, 光華
巴士 and 中興巴士, on a two-stage fare and a narrow midday 1120–1210
window linking the university's own two campuses.[^tdx-bus]
