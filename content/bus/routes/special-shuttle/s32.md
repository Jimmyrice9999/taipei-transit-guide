## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 懷恩專車S32 under 其他 (通勤公車、內科專車、南軟專車與其他).[^ebus-special]
The normalized TDX record retains 1 direction/variant record for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “捷運六張犁站 - 臺北市懷愛館”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0645 - 1810 [假日] 0645 - 1810”, fare “免費”.[^ebus-route-s32]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 捷運六張犁站(基隆路), past 捷運六
張犁站(和平)/黎明社教中心/芳和國中, ending at 青峰活動中心/臺北市懷愛
館, one of four differently numbered 懷恩專車 shuttles in this batch
that converge on the same destination from four separate MRT stations.[^tdx-bus] One confirmed interchange is recorded in the curated
stop-ID join set: Liuzhangli (BR07), matching the route's own
terminus.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a single subroute under one
operator, 臺北客運, on a free fare shared by all four 懷恩專車 routes.[^tdx-bus]
