These pages cover the 4 normalized TDX records classified series-700s.[^tdx-bus]
The full official catalogue places 711, 756, 757 and 758 under 一般公車 (general
bus service), the same heading covering the bulk of Taipei's numbered city
routes; none of the four carries a colour character (紅/藍/綠/棕/橘/黃) or a
幹線 designation, so none was a candidate for the colour-feeder or trunk
groups.[^ebus-general]
