## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 758 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives termini 五股 - 麟光, but the only schedule table it
displays is 758(五股發車), with three weekday departures (0700, 1200, 1600)
and no holiday service (例假日停駛). It gives a three-section fare (三段票) and
指南客運五股站 as the service contact.[^ebus-758] The TDX snapshot separately
records two subroutes for this route, 758(五股發車) and 758(麟光發車); the
麟光發車 subroute carries a leftover English alias name of "1503" in the TDX
data rather than "758".[^tdx-bus] That leftover "1503" sits alongside similar
non-7xx leftover predecessor numbers recorded on this project's other two
700s pages, 756 (predecessor claimed as 2056) and 757 (predecessor claimed as
1504) — a pattern more consistent with 7xx marking routes renumbered wholesale
from an earlier, differently-numbered service than with 7xx being a simple
fare-class relabelling of an existing number, though this page does not treat
that pattern as confirming either reading of the numbering scheme.[^tvbs-numbering]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records, including
confirmed rail-stop joins to stations BR06, O16, O19, O20 and R06; route
length and headway remain TBC where that layer and the schedule page do not
publish them, and the schedule page fetched here does not show a separate
timetable for the 麟光發車 subroute.[^tdx-bus][^ebus-758] A community wiki
(zh.wikiversity) lead claims the route was shortened and renumbered from a
predecessor route 1503 on 1 May 2021, when service was cut back to 指南客運
麟光站 and the fare was reduced from a four-section to a three-section fare;
the TDX data's retained "1503" alias on the 麟光發車 subroute is consistent
with this claim, but it could not be independently corroborated against a
primary source in this search — the 臺北市公共運輸處's public 公車營運調整
document archive only extends back to 2024, and no 2021-dated primary
announcement was found — so the renumbering claim is left unconfirmed and
does not appear as fact above.[^tdx-bus]
