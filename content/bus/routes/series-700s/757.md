## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 757 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 淡海 - 北門, weekday and holiday service 0530–2200, a
three-section fare (三段票), and 指南客運淡海站 as the service contact.[^ebus-757]
It marks the weekday 1020 and 1500 departures, and the holiday 0830, 1130,
1630 and 2030 departures, as 狗狗友善公車 (dog-friendly bus) workings.[^ebus-757]
The TDX snapshot separately records a distinct subroute named 757狗狗公車
alongside the plain 757 working, consistent with the schedule page's
dog-friendly departures.[^tdx-bus]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records, including
confirmed rail-stop joins to stations G13, O12, R25, R26, R28 and V02;
route length and headway remain TBC where that layer and the schedule page
do not publish them.[^tdx-bus] A community wiki (zh.wikiversity) lead lists a
dated history for 757 — including a claim that it was renumbered from a
predecessor route 1504 on 1 May 2015 — but this could not be corroborated
against a primary source in this search: the 臺北市公共運輸處's public 公車營運
調整 document archive only extends back to 2024, and no 2015-dated primary
announcement was found, so the renumbering claim is left unconfirmed and
does not appear above.[^tdx-bus]
