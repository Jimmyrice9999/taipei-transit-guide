## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 756 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 淡江大學 - 北門, weekday and holiday service 0530–2200, a
three-section fare (三段票), and 指南客運淡大站 as the service contact.[^ebus-756]
It states headway as weekday peak 10–15 minutes and off-peak 15–20 minutes,
and holiday peak and off-peak both 20–30 minutes.[^ebus-756]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records, including
confirmed rail-stop joins to stations G13, R25, R26, R28, V02 and V04; route
length remains TBC where that layer does not publish it.[^tdx-bus] A
community wiki (zh.wikiversity) lead lists a dated history for 756 —
including a claim that a predecessor highway route 2056 (淡江大學-北門) was
reclassified as a Taipei joint-operation route and renumbered 756 on 1
January 2012 — but this could not be corroborated against a primary source
in this search: the 臺北市公共運輸處's public 公車營運調整 document archive only
extends back to 2024, and no 2012-dated primary announcement was found, so
the renumbering claim is left unconfirmed and does not appear above.[^tdx-bus]
