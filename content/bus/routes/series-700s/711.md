## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 711 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 汐止 - 圓環, weekday and holiday service 0600–2000, a
three-section fare (三段票) over the full route, and 中興巴士汐止站 as the
service contact.[^ebus-711] The page records 19 weekday departures and 4
holiday departures; it marks the weekday 1000 and 1400 departures, and all
four holiday departures (1000, 1200, 1400, 1600), as 狗狗友善公車
(dog-friendly bus) workings.[^ebus-711] The TDX snapshot separately records a
distinct subroute named 711狗狗公車 alongside the plain 711 working, consistent
with the schedule page's dog-friendly departures.[^tdx-bus]

The confirmed stop sequence starts at 東方山河社區 in Xizhi, New Taipei, runs
through Xizhi's own centre and past Nangang Software Park, then follows the
Nanjing East Road corridor west through Songshan into central Taipei, ending
at 後車站 near the Zhongshan/Datong side of Taipei Main Station.[^tdx-bus]
That Nanjing corridor stretch passes stops named directly for four MRT
stations — 捷運東湖站, 捷運南京復興站, 捷運松江南京站 and 捷運中山站 — but this
project's curated stop-ID join set carries no confirmed interchange for this
route, so none of those four is presented as a confirmed MRT connection
above: a stop being named after a station is evidence of proximity, not of a
verified interchange.[^tdx-bus]

Two Taiwanese outlets attribute Taipei's bus-numbering scheme to the same
named regulator official and disagree on what the 7xx band a route like 711
sits in actually marks: one reading gives 7xx as three-segment
air-conditioned service continuing the 2xx/3xx fare-segment logic, the other
gives it as former intercity highway routes absorbed into joint
operation.[^setn-numbering][^tvbs-numbering] Neither account is specific to
711, and this page does not choose between them.

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route
length and timetable detail beyond the departure count remain TBC where that
layer does not publish them.[^tdx-bus] A community wiki (zh.wikiversity)
lead claims 711 was renumbered from a predecessor route 311 (綠線) on 1
December 2006, with a corridor change through 樟樹灣 and 伯爵別墅; this could
not be corroborated against a primary source in this search — the 臺北市公共
運輸處's public 公車營運調整 document archive only extends back to 2024, and no
2006-dated primary announcement was found — so the renumbering claim is left
unconfirmed and does not appear above.[^tdx-bus] If the 7xx-as-absorbed-
highway-route reading above is right, a predecessor numbered 311 rather than
a highway-department code is a mismatch worth noting rather than resolving:
neither reading of the numbering scheme specifically predicts a 3xx
predecessor.[^tvbs-numbering]
