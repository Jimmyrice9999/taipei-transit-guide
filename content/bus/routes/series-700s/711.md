## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 711 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 汐止 - 圓環, weekday and holiday service 0600–2000, a
three-section fare (三段票) over the full route, and 中興巴士汐止站 as the
service contact.[^ebus-711] The page records 19 weekday departures and 4
holiday departures; it marks the weekday 1000 and 1400 departures, and all
four holiday departures (1000, 1200, 1400, 1600), as 狗狗友善公車
(dog-friendly bus) workings.[^ebus-711] The TDX snapshot separately records a
distinct subroute named 711狗狗公車 alongside the plain 711 working, consistent
with the schedule page's dog-friendly departures.[^tdx-bus]

## Research status

The TDX snapshot supplies the changing stop and geometry records; route
length and timetable detail beyond the departure count remain TBC where that
layer does not publish them.[^tdx-bus] A community wiki (zh.wikiversity)
lead claims 711 was renumbered from a predecessor route 311 (綠線) on 1
December 2006, with a corridor change through 樟樹灣 and 伯爵別墅; this could
not be corroborated against a primary source in this search — the 臺北市公共
運輸處's public 公車營運調整 document archive only extends back to 2024, and no
2006-dated primary announcement was found — so the renumbering claim is left
unconfirmed and does not appear above.[^tdx-bus]
