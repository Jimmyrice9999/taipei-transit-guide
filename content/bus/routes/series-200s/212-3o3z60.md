## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 212夜 under 一般公車 (一般公車).[^ebus-general]
The normalized TDX record retains 1 direction/variant record for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “舊莊 - 青年公園”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 2300 - 0020 [假日] 2300 - 0020”, fare “一段票乘以一點五倍”.[^ebus-route-212-3o3z60]

The confirmed stop sequence matches 212直's own corridor stop for stop:
舊莊二站 in Nangang, past 中研院/南港機廠, through 捷運昆陽站/捷運市政府站/
捷運國父紀念館站/捷運忠孝敦化站/捷運善導寺站/臺北車站(忠孝)/捷運西門站,
ending at 青年公園.[^tdx-bus] Twelve confirmed interchanges are recorded
in the curated stop-ID join set, identical to 212直's own list: Ximen,
Taipei Main Station, Shandao Temple, Zhongxiao Xinsheng, Zhongxiao
Fuxing, Zhongxiao Dunhua, Sun Yat-Sen Memorial Hall, Taipei City Hall,
Yongchun, Houshanpi, Kunyang and Nangang.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a
single subroute under one operator, 首都客運, as a late-night working
of the same corridor, published with its own 1.5× base fare rather
than the daytime routes' plain 兩段票.[^tdx-bus]
