南港機廠, through 捷運昆陽站/捷運後山埤站(忠孝)/捷運市政府站, ending at
聯合報, a shorter run than the separate 270, which continues past this
stretch to 捷運西門站.[^tdx-bus] Five confirmed interchanges are
recorded in the curated stop-ID join set: Taipei City Hall, Yongchun,
Houshanpi, Kunyang and Nangang.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a single
subroute under one operator, 大都會客運, on a narrow weekday-only
window (0830–1530) rather than an all-day span, holiday service
suspended entirely.[^tdx-bus]
