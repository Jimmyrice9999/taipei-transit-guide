官邸/泰北中學, through 故宮博物院/雙溪別墅/劍南橋, past 翠山莊/翠山派出所/
中社路, ending at 雙溪國小/雙溪站/雙溪社區/大崙尾山.[^tdx-bus] The curated
stop-ID join set records zero confirmed interchanges for this route,
despite its own corridor passing close to several Wenhu Line stations
along the way.[^tdx-bus] TDX records three named direction/variant
records distinguishing the full run from shorter outbound-only and
return-only workings.[^tdx-bus]
