## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 214直 under 一般公車 (一般公車).[^ebus-general]
The normalized TDX record retains 3 direction/variant records for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “中和 - 松山機場”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0600 - 2000 [假日] 0700 - 1900”, fare “兩段票”.[^ebus-route-214-s1s8xk]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 駕訓中心 in Zhonghe, past 連城路/
中和廟口, through 捷運永安市場站/捷運頂溪站/中正橋, along 捷運古亭站/捷運
東門站/捷運忠孝新生站, through 捷運松江南京站/民權敦化路口, ending at 松山
機場.[^tdx-bus] Nine confirmed interchanges are recorded in the curated
stop-ID join set: Zhongxiao Xinsheng, Zhongshan Junior High School,
Songshan Airport, Guting, Songjiang Nanjing, Yongan Market, Dingxi,
Dongmen and Xingtian Temple.[^tdx-bus] TDX records three named
direction/variant records distinguishing the full run from shorter
outbound-only and return-only workings, over a corridor that overlaps
its own non-express sibling 214 through much of Zhonghe and central
Taipei before diverging toward 松山機場 rather than 內湖.[^tdx-bus]
