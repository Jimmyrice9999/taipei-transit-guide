捷運龍山寺站/捷運西門站, ending at 臺北車站(忠孝)/捷運善導寺站/立法院/行政
院.[^tdx-bus] Six confirmed interchanges are recorded in the curated
stop-ID join set: Fuzhong, Longshan Temple, Ximen, Taipei Main
Station, Shandao Temple and Banxin.[^tdx-bus] TDX records four named
direction/variant records, published under two different operator
names, 大南汽車 and 三重客運, for what the stop sequences show as the
same physical corridor.[^tdx-bus]
