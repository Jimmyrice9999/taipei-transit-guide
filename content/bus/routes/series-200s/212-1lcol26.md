## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 212直 under 一般公車 (一般公車).[^ebus-general]
The normalized TDX record retains 1 direction/variant record for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “舊莊 - 青年公園”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0520 - 2230 [假日] 0520 - 2230”, fare “兩段票”.[^ebus-route-212-1lcol26]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 舊莊二站 in Nangang, past 中研院/
南港機廠, through 捷運昆陽站/捷運後山埤站(忠孝)/捷運市政府站, along 捷運
國父紀念館站/捷運忠孝敦化站/捷運善導寺站, through 臺北車站(忠孝)/捷運
西門站, ending at 青年公園.[^tdx-bus] Twelve confirmed interchanges are
recorded in the curated stop-ID join set: Ximen, Taipei Main Station,
Shandao Temple, Zhongxiao Xinsheng, Zhongxiao Fuxing, Zhongxiao
Dunhua, Sun Yat-Sen Memorial Hall, Taipei City Hall, Yongchun,
Houshanpi, Kunyang and Nangang.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a single
subroute under one operator, 首都客運, routed via 南港機廠 rather than
南港展覽館/南港, unlike the plain 212, which confirms Taipei Nangang
Exhibition Center instead of Nangang along an otherwise near-identical
corridor.[^tdx-bus]
