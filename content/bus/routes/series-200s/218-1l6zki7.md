## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 218直 under 一般公車 (一般公車).[^ebus-general]
The normalized TDX record retains 1 direction/variant record for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “新北投 - 萬華”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0640 - 0640 [假日] 停駛”, fare “兩段票”.[^ebus-route-218-1l6zki7] It currently lists weekday service and suspended holiday service.[^ebus-route-218-1l6zki7]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 貴子坑水土保持園區, past 新北投/
北投公園/慈后宮/捷運奇岩站, through 承德路's own 士林監理站/福港街, along
劍潭國小/明倫高中/花博公園/大同大學, through 國賓飯店/行政院/臺北車站
(忠孝), ending at 老松國小 in Wanhua.[^tdx-bus] Eight confirmed
interchanges are recorded in the curated stop-ID join set: Ximen,
Taipei Main Station, Beimen, Yuanshan, Jiantan, Qiyan, Beitou and
Xinbeitou.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a single subroute under one
operator, 大南汽車, with a published head/last-departure field
identical at both ends, “0640 - 0640”, unlike the all-day span
published for its own sibling 218.[^tdx-bus]
