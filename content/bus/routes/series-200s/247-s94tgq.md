## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 247區 under 一般公車 (一般公車).[^ebus-general]
The normalized TDX record retains 1 direction/variant record for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “東湖 - 捷運圓山站”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0645 - 1750 [假日] 0645 - 1845”, fare “一段票”.[^ebus-route-247-s94tgq]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 威剛科技總部大樓, past 長壽抽水站/
蘆洲里/內湖焚化廠(安康), through 捷運東湖站/明湖國中/捷運葫洲站, along 大湖
公園/大湖/貿商三村, through 美麗華/捷運劍南路站/自強隧道/捷運大直站, ending
at 圓山大飯店/臺北市立美術館/捷運圓山站, a shorter run than the separate
all-day 247, which continues past this stretch to 臺北車站.[^tdx-bus]
Nine confirmed interchanges are recorded in the curated stop-ID join
set: Dazhi, Jiannan Rd., Xihu, Gangqian, Neihu, Dahu Park, Huzhou,
Donghu and Yuanshan.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a single subroute under
one operator, 光華巴士, with a shorter weekday span than its own
holiday hours, unlike most other routes in this batch.[^tdx-bus]
