through 亞東科技大學/壽德新村/重慶國中, along 新北市政府(新府路)/新北板橋
公車站/中山漢生路口, through 捷運板新站/自來水公司/埔墘(三民路)/光復橋,
past 捷運龍山寺站/捷運西門站, ending at 臺北車站(忠孝)/立法院.[^tdx-bus]
Seven confirmed interchanges are recorded in the curated stop-ID join
set: Fuzhong, Banqiao, Longshan Temple, Ximen, Taipei Main Station,
Shandao Temple and Banxin.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a single subroute
under one operator, 三重客運, routed via 中央路 and 新北市政府, distinct
from its own sibling 265經明德路, which instead detours via 明德路 and
捷運海山站 at the Tucheng end.[^tdx-bus]
