## Classification and current service

The full official schedule gives termini “大直 - 新店”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0710 - 0800 [假日] 停駛”, fare “兩段票”.[^ebus-route-208-1ax4rab] It currently lists weekday service and suspended holiday service.[^ebus-route-208-1ax4rab]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 美麗華(植福) in Dazhi, past 自強
隧道/捷運大直站/空軍司令部/忠烈祠/圓山大飯店, through 開南中學/羅斯福潮州街
口/捷運公館站, along 中興新村/景新街, ending at New Taipei's own 及人
中學 in Ankeng.[^tdx-bus] Seven confirmed interchanges are recorded in
the curated stop-ID join set: Dazhi, Jiannan Rd., Gongguan, Taipower
Building, Guting, Sunshine Sports Park and Xinhe Elementary School.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a single subroute under one operator, 指南
客運, published with a single 50-minute weekday morning window (0710–
0800) rather than an all-day span, weekdays only with holiday service
suspended entirely — a single-trip commuter working rather than a
regular all-day service.[^tdx-bus]
