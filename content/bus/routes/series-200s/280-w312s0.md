## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 280直 under 一般公車 (一般公車).[^ebus-general]
The normalized TDX record retains 1 direction/variant record for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “天母 - 公館”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0700 - 1730 [假日] 停駛”, fare “兩段票”.[^ebus-route-280-w312s0] It currently lists weekday service and suspended holiday service.[^ebus-route-280-w312s0]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 職能發展學院二 in 天母, past 忠孝
新村/芝山國小/德行芝玉路口, through 捷運芝山站/捷運劍潭站/中山國小(新
生), along 捷運行天宮站(松江路)/捷運松江南京站/捷運忠孝新生站, through
龍安國小(公務人力發展學院)/臺大, ending at 捷運公館站/師大分部.[^tdx-bus]
Seven confirmed interchanges are recorded in the curated stop-ID join
set, a subset of its own all-day sibling 280's own list: Zhongxiao
Xinsheng, Gongguan, Songjiang Nanjing, Xingtian Temple, Zhongshan
Elementary School, Jiantan and Zhishan.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a
single subroute under one operator, 中興巴士, weekdays only with
holiday service suspended entirely.[^tdx-bus]
