## Classification and current service

The full official catalogue files 202區 under 一般公車 (一般公車).[^ebus-general]
The normalized TDX record retains 1 direction/variant record for this service; route history, corridor rationale and dated operator changes are not inferred from stop names or the current map.[^tdx-bus]

The full official schedule gives termini “錦繡 - 臺北科技大學”, the current head/last-departure field “[平日] 0620 - 1750 [假日] 停駛”, fare “兩段票”.[^ebus-route-202-21niqx] It currently lists weekday service and suspended holiday service.[^ebus-route-202-21niqx]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 錦秀路口 in New Taipei's Ankeng
area, past the Ankeng Light Rail's own 輕軌安康站/輕軌陽光運動公園站/輕軌
新和國小站, through 南勢角/中和 to 捷運景安站/捷運中和站, along 華中橋/果菜
市場/萬華車站, through 捷運西門站/臺北車站(忠孝), ending at 中山女高 near
長安東路.[^tdx-bus] Ten confirmed interchanges are recorded in the
curated stop-ID join set, spanning the Bannan Line, the Songshan-
Xindian Line, the Zhonghe-Xinlu Line and the Ankeng Light Rail:
Longshan Temple, Ximen, Taipei Main Station, Shandao Temple, Zhongxiao
Xinsheng, Ankang, Sunshine Sports Park, Xinhe Elementary School, Jingan
and Zhonghe.[^tdx-bus] The route runs a single subroute under one
operator, 指南客運, weekdays only with holiday service suspended
entirely — the shuttle/shorter sibling of 202, which runs the full
daily span but confirms one fewer station over a shorter eastern leg.[^tdx-bus]
