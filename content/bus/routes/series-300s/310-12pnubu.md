## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 310 under 一般公車, as a separate routeid entry
from its 310區 shuttle variant.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives
板橋 - 士林, weekday service 0500–2130, holiday service 0530–2130, a
two-section fare (兩段票) with a buffer zone between 北門街(黃石市場) and 捷運龍山
寺站, and 臺北客運江子翠站 as the service contact.[^ebus-310] The page lists 28
weekday and 27 holiday departures.[^ebus-310]

The confirmed stop sequence starts in Banqiao, New Taipei, crosses the
Xindian River on 華江橋 past 捷運江子翠站 and 捷運新埔站, continues through
Wanhua past 捷運龍山寺站, through central Taipei past 捷運西門站 and 捷運北門站,
then follows 中山北路 past 臺北市立美術館 and 捷運劍潭站 into Shilin, ending near
捷運芝山站.[^tdx-bus] Six separate MRT stations are named along the way, and
the curated stop-ID join set confirms none of them.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A community wiki traces this route to a 1947 municipal route 市營10路
running 士林–臺北車站, stating it joined the Taipei joint-operation system
and was extended to Banqiao and renumbered 310 on 15 May 1977: 「本線歷史十分
悠久，早於1947年(民國36年)已開辦，當時為市營10路(士林─台北車站)...1977年(民國66年)5月15
日加入台北聯營公車行列...並更改編號為310線」.[^zhwikipedia-310] The article names
eight underlying references for its fuller history, including a 2018 Yahoo
News piece and Taipei Bus Company announcements from 2024–2026, none of
which was independently fetched in this search, so the 1947/1977 origin
claim is reported here as the wiki's claim rather than independently
confirmed.[^zhwikipedia-310]
