## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 304承德 under 一般公車, as its own routeid entry
separate from 304重慶 — the same catalogue confirmation as its sibling route,
that both street-name branches file independently rather than as 區/預
sub-listings.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives 故宮博物院 - 永和,
weekday service 0600–2000, holiday service 0830–2000, a three-section fare
(三段票), and 中興巴士故宮站 as the service contact.[^ebus-304-chengde] Selected
weekend and holiday departures (0830, 1700, 2000) are marked as
dog-friendly workings.[^ebus-304-chengde] Buffer zones apply near 捷運劍潭站
(國樂團) and 捷運北門站.[^ebus-304-chengde]

The confirmed stop sequence runs the same 故宮博物院-to-Shilin start as its
304重慶 sibling, but takes 承德路 rather than 重慶北路 through Datong — past
捷運劍潭站 and 捷運北門站 rather than 捷運大橋頭站 — before rejoining the same
中正橋 crossing into Yonghe.[^tdx-bus] As with 304重慶, none of the named MRT
stations carries a confirmed join in the curated set.[^tdx-bus] The two
routes' divergence through Datong onto separate named roads matches what a
community wiki on the shared 304 history states in general terms.[^zhwikiversity-304]

## History

A community wiki states the operator (大業客運, now 中興巴士) was founded in
June 1963 with this as its first route, then numbered 中興1路, and that it
joined the Taipei joint-operation system and was renumbered 304 on 12 June
1977: 「1963年6月：大業客運(今日的中興巴士)成立，成為該公司首條公車路線，當時路線編號為
「中興1路」」and「1977年6月12日：加入台北聯營公車，並賦予新路線編號304。」[^zhwikiversity-304]
This history is shared with 304重慶 and was not independently verified
against a primary PTO or 中興巴士 announcement in this search.
