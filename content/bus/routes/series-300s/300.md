## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 300 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 故宮博物院 - 小南門, weekday service 0610–1705, holiday
service 0800–1810, a two-section fare (兩段票) with a buffer zone between
福林國小 and 士林簡易庭, and 中興巴士故宮站 as the service contact.[^ebus-300]

## History

A community wiki states the route opened as 重慶幹線 (Chongqing Trunk Line)
and was renamed 300 on 1 June 2017: 「本路線開辦為重慶幹線」and 「2017年6月1日起原
重慶幹線更名為300線」.[^zhwikiversity-300] This is a secondary source and was
not corroborated against a primary Taipei Public Transportation Office
announcement in this search.
