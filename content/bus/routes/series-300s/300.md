## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 300 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 故宮博物院 - 小南門, weekday service 0610–1705, holiday
service 0800–1810, a two-section fare (兩段票) with a buffer zone between
福林國小 and 士林簡易庭, and 中興巴士故宮站 as the service contact.[^ebus-300]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 故宮博物院 (the National Palace Museum)
south through Shilin — past the Shilin Official Residence area and 捷運士林站
— then follows 重慶北路 through Datong past 捷運大橋頭站 to end near 小南門 in
central Taipei.[^tdx-bus] Despite passing both those named MRT stations, the
curated stop-ID join set confirms neither.[^tdx-bus] That the route began as
a named 幹線 (trunk line) and was later folded into the ordinary numbered
series is itself a documented case of the reverse of the more usual
direction — this project's other trunk-route material records numbered
routes being promoted to named trunk status, not a trunk line being
renumbered plain, which makes 300's history worth flagging as an exception
rather than the rule.[^zhwikiversity-300]

## History

A community wiki states the route opened as 重慶幹線 (Chongqing Trunk Line)
and was renamed 300 on 1 June 2017: 「本路線開辦為重慶幹線」and 「2017年6月1日起原
重慶幹線更名為300線」.[^zhwikiversity-300] This is a secondary source and was
not corroborated against a primary Taipei Public Transportation Office
announcement in this search. **A currently-active trunk route also named
重慶幹線** exists in this project's trunk-route coverage, running an
entirely different 天母–東園 corridor — published as an unresolved naming
conflict on that route's own page rather than settled here.[^tdx-bus]
