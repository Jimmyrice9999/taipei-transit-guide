## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 303區 under 一般公車, filed as its own routeid
entry distinct from the plain 303 route.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule
page gives 捷運劍潭站 - 平等里, weekday service 0600–2240, holiday service
0530–2240, a single-section fare (一段票), and 首都客運士林站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-303-shuttle] Selected weekday morning and afternoon
departures branch via 文化大學 (Chinese Culture University), suspended during
winter and summer breaks; the 陽明戲院 stop serves only departures before
16:00 on both weekday and holiday schedules.[^ebus-303-shuttle]

The confirmed stop sequence runs the same 捷運劍潭站-to-back-country corridor
as plain 303, through the lower Yangmingshan hillside stops and the rural
hamlets beyond 山仔后, ending at the same 平等里 area rather than continuing
the extra distance to 303's own 大坪尾 terminus.[^tdx-bus] Like 303, only the
劍潭 terminus is MRT-named, and it carries no confirmed join.[^tdx-bus]
