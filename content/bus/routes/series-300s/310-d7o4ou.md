## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 310區 under 一般公車, filed as its own routeid
entry distinct from the plain 310 route.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule
page gives 板橋 - 臺北車站, weekday service only 0830–1530 (holiday service
停駛), a two-section fare (兩段票), and 臺北客運江子翠站 as the service
contact.[^ebus-310-shuttle] The page states the route commenced 9 March
2024: 「本路線自113年3月9日開始實施」.[^ebus-310-shuttle]

The confirmed stop sequence covers only the Banqiao-to-central-Taipei half of
parent route 310's alignment — the same 華江橋/捷運龍山寺站/捷運西門站 corridor —
stopping at 臺北車站 rather than continuing up 中山北路 to Shilin.[^tdx-bus] As
on 310, the named MRT stations carry no confirmed join in the curated
set.[^tdx-bus] The stop sequence's own first and last named stops — 藝香公園
(果菜市場) and 臺北車站(鄭州) — sit slightly short of the catalogue's stated
板橋/臺北車站 termini pair, consistent with 藝香公園 being a Wanhua-side stop
near the produce market rather than in Banqiao proper.[^tdx-bus] 310區's
March 2024 commencement date makes it one of the newer shuttle variants in
this project's series-300s coverage, against parent route 310's own history
traced back to 1947 on this site's 310 page — a shuttle added to a route
with roughly eight decades of continuous operation behind it.[^ebus-310-shuttle]
