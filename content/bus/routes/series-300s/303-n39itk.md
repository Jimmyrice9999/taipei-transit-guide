## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 303 under 一般公車, as its own routeid entry
(0100030300) distinct from 303區's (0100030320).[^ebus-general] The schedule page gives
捷運劍潭站 - 大坪尾, weekday service 0510–1650, no holiday service, a
single-section fare (一段票), and 首都客運士林站 as the service contact.[^ebus-303]
The operator adjusts return-trip spacing at the 大坪尾 terminus as needed:
「為維本路線行車間距平穩性，視路(客)況於往程末端「大坪尾」機動調整返程行車間距」.[^ebus-303]
The page also notes variant workings serving 文化大學 and 平等里106巷.[^ebus-303]

The confirmed stop sequence climbs from 捷運劍潭站 through the same lower
Yangmingshan hillside corridor as routes 108/109/111 (岩山里, 華興中學, 嶺頭),
but instead of ending at the park headquarters it continues past 山仔后 into
genuinely rural back-country hamlets — 菁山里, 鄭厝, 陳厝, 倫仔尾, 公地嶺 —
ending at 大坪尾, well beyond the park's own visitor infrastructure.[^tdx-bus]
The 捷運劍潭站 terminus is the only MRT-named stop on the route, and it
carries no confirmed join in the curated set.[^tdx-bus] The TDX record
carries eight distinct 303 subroute variants combining three independent
choices — via or not via 文化大學/平菁街106巷, via or not via 陽明山 itself, and
whether the working runs the full 劍潭–大坪尾 length or only the 衛星電台–大坪尾
tail — more branching than any other route in this project's series-300s
coverage.[^tdx-bus]
