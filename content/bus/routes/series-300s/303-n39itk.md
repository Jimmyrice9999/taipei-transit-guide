## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 303 under 一般公車, as its own routeid entry
(0100030300) distinct from 303區's (0100030320).[^ebus-general] The schedule page gives
捷運劍潭站 - 大坪尾, weekday service 0510–1650, no holiday service, a
single-section fare (一段票), and 首都客運士林站 as the service contact.[^ebus-303]
The operator adjusts return-trip spacing at the 大坪尾 terminus as needed:
「為維本路線行車間距平穩性，視路(客)況於往程末端「大坪尾」機動調整返程行車間距」.[^ebus-303]
The page also notes variant workings serving 文化大學 and 平等里106巷.[^ebus-303]
