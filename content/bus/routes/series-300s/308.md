## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 308 under 一般公車.[^ebus-general] Its full
schedule page gives 淡江大學 - 捷運劍潭站, weekday service only 0610–1530
(no holiday service), a two-section fare (兩段票) with a buffer zone between
捷運竹圍站 and 關渡, and 指南客運淡江大學站 as the service contact.[^ebus-308]

The confirmed stop sequence combines two corridors this site covers
separately on other routes: it starts at 淡江大學 and follows the same MRT
Red Line Danshui-branch alignment as routes 756/757 (past 捷運淡水站, 捷運
紅樹林站, 捷運竹圍站), then at 關渡 turns onto the same Beitou hillside
corridor as route 302 (past 捷運忠義站, 捷運復興崗站, 捷運奇岩站, 捷運北投站),
continuing through 士林 past 捷運芝山站 to end at 捷運劍潭站.[^tdx-bus] Six
separate MRT stations are named along that combined run, and the curated
stop-ID join set confirms none of them.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A community wiki states that before 5 January 2009 the route originated at
捷運竹圍站 and ran via 民權路、中正東路、中正路、中山路、中山北路一段、大忠街、學府路 to
淡江大學, terminating at 捷運圓山站, and that from that date the origin end
extended to 淡江大學 while the terminus shortened to the current 捷運劍潭站.[^zhwikiversity-308]
This is a secondary source and was not corroborated against a primary PTO
or 指南客運 announcement in this search.
