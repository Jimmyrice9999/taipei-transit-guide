## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 302 under 一般公車, as a separate routeid entry
from its 302區 shuttle variant.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives
關渡宮 - 萬華, weekday and holiday service 0530–2130, a two-section fare
(兩段票) with a buffer zone between 士林國中 and 士林簡易庭, and 大南汽車關渡站 as
the service contact.[^ebus-302] Two weekday departures (0930 and 1430) are
marked as dog-friendly workings.[^ebus-302]

The confirmed stop sequence runs from 關渡宮 (Guandu Temple) through Beitou's
中央北路 corridor — passing 捷運忠義站, 捷運復興崗站 and 捷運奇岩站 in
succession — then into Shilin and along 重慶北路 through Datong past 捷運
大橋頭站, ending in Wanhua near 捷運西門站 and 捷運北門站.[^tdx-bus] Five
separate MRT stations are named directly on this one route, and the curated
stop-ID join set confirms none of them — one of the more striking cases in
this project's coverage of a route running the whole width of the city
alongside the metro without a single confirmed interchange.[^tdx-bus]
