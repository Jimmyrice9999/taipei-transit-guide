## Classification and corridor

The official catalogue lists 311 under 一般公車, as a separate routeid entry
from its 311區 shuttle variant.[^ebus-general] Its full schedule page gives
中和 - 松山, weekday and holiday service 0515–2200, weekday peak frequency
10–12 minutes and off-peak 15–20 minutes (holiday runs a fixed schedule), a
two-section fare (兩段票), and 中興巴士中和站 as the service contact.[^ebus-311]
The page states the route skips the City Government stop on holidays:
「例假日不經市政府(市府)站」.[^ebus-311] Buffer zones apply between 臺北科技大學 and
興安國小 outbound, and between 信義新生路口 and 臺北科技大學 on the return
working.[^ebus-311]

The confirmed stop sequence starts at 駕訓中心 in 中和, crosses into 永和,
crosses the Xindian River on 福和橋 past 臺大 and 捷運公館站, then runs the
length of 仁愛路 through Da'an — past a run of named 仁愛 intersections and
國父紀念館 — into 松山, ending near 松山車站.[^tdx-bus] Unlike several other
routes in this group, 311 only brushes the MRT network once, at 捷運公館站 on
the return working, and that stop carries no confirmed join in the curated
set.[^tdx-bus]

## History

A community wiki describes an older terminus history moving from a
永和–(present-day) 臺北科技大學 pairing, extended at one point to 汐止, then
shortened to 玉成國小, before reaching its present-day 松山車站-area
terminus, and states the route was rerouted via 八德路 to serve the Songshan
bus transfer hub from 15 November 2014.[^zhwikiversity-311] This is a
secondary source and was not corroborated against a primary PTO or 中興巴士
announcement in this search.
