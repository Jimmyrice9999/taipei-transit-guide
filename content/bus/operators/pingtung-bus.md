Pingtung Bus’s official overview describes the company as a major Pingtung bus provider and separates its highway-bus and county-bus work.[^pingtung-story] Its current overview states 38 highway routes, 25 Pingtung-county bus routes and 263 commercial vehicles, for a combined 63 routes under the page’s own counting scope.[^pingtung-story] This is the operator’s published overview, not a substitute for the national TDX route snapshot or a legal register of every company serving Pingtung.

The company profile describes Pingtung Bus as responsible for highway and city-bus services in Pingtung and says the operating area has nine stations and two maintenance facilities.[^pingtung-profile] The separate service-location directory identifies staffed points at Pingtung, Chaozhou, Donggang, Fangliao and Hengchun, and also lists counters at Zuoying High Speed Rail station and Kaohsiung International Airport for south-coast services.[^pingtung-stations]

## Current fleet and payment evidence

The operator’s 2026 history records ten new electric buses entering Hengchun and Kenting operation from 10 February 2026, in the context of a stated aim to accelerate city-bus electrification by 2030.[^pingtung-story] This is a dated deployment statement, not a claim that the full Pingtung fleet is electric. A current model-by-model fleet census and registration list remains TBC.

Pingtung Bus also records QR-capable mobile-payment equipment: the operator says city-bus vehicles received the equipment in 2024 and highway-bus vehicles in 2022.[^pingtung-story] Its history says a 2021 route-9189 trial used iPASS MONEY, EasyWallet and icash Pay QR codes.[^pingtung-story] The profile therefore documents the operator’s payment milestones while leaving route-level acceptance, concessions and current product coverage to each dated timetable or fare source.

The operator’s current route pages expose dated departure tables, fare-zone tables and accessibility markings; for example, route 513’s page gives a timetable update date of 26 August 2026 and names the Pingtung transfer-station and railway-station circuit.[^pingtung-schedule-513] The operator profile does not turn that one route into a company-wide frequency or fare claim.

## Scope and gaps

Pingtung Bus’s own current service story is valuable because it connects the southern county network to its operating points, electric-bus deployment and payment history. It does not establish ownership, route-by-route contract history, a permanent fleet total, a depot inventory beyond the company profile’s two maintenance facilities, or a comparable performance series.
