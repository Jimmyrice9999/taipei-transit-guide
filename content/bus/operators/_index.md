[the network explainer](/bus/network/joint-operation/) for how many, and for
how that arrangement holds together. A page per operating company — contracted
routes, fleet composition, garage locations and livery — is the material
least available in English anywhere, and waits on the same data pipeline
described at [Bus](/bus/).
