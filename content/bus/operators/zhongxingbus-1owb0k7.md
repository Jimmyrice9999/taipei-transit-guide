## Company history and legal identity

The MOEA record identifies 中興大業巴士股份有限公司 as unified business number 18598304, with representative 呂奇龍, registered address 臺北市士林區中正路499號1樓, total and paid-in capital of NT$557 million and statutory registration date 31 December 1965 [^moea-zhongxing]. It lists both intercity and city bus operation and vehicle repair among the approved business categories [^moea-zhongxing].

The Zhongxing Group history supplies the preceding narrative: Daye Bus was established in December 1965 under the Taiwan Provincial Committee of the Kuomintang, reorganized as Zhongxing Daye Bus on 18 January 1966, and joined Taipei’s joint-operation system in 1977 [^zhongxing-history]. The dates are related but are not silently treated as one event: the group page describes the predecessor and reorganization, while the MOEA page records the legal entity [^zhongxing-history][^moea-zhongxing].

The current MOEA director table records corporate representation by 龍福國際, 龍騰國際汽車, 新北汽車客運 and 光華資產管理 [^moea-zhongxing]. That is a registered board/shareholder representation snapshot; it does not establish a complete parent-company or merger chronology [^moea-zhongxing].

## Oversight and documented incidents

The Highway Bureau’s detailed record for Zhongxing Daye’s highway-passenger operation displays a 甲 evaluation for ROC 112 and lists 甲 results for several national-highway periods [^thb-zhongxing-record]. It records three historical incidents: an A1 incident dated 108-10-10 with one death, an A2 incident dated 108-03-29 with two injuries, and an A2 incident dated 102-12-28 with nine injuries [^thb-zhongxing-record]. The record is explicitly for the company’s highway-passenger business, so it is not presented as a result for every city-bus route in this Taipei TDX record [^thb-zhongxing-record].

The same regulatory table records one daily-driving-time violation in ROC 114 Q3 and one in ROC 113 Q4, alongside nonzero entries in earlier quarters [^thb-zhongxing-record]. Taipei PTO’s 2024 and 2025 city-bus evaluation notices also list Zhongxing Daye in the 優等 group [^pto-zhongxing-2024][^pto-zhongxing-2025]. These are dated oversight records, not evidence of a complete fleet or contract register.
