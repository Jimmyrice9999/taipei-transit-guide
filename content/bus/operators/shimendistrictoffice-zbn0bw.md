## Government-unit record

The committed TDX pull names Shimen District Office (石門區公所) as a NewTaipei operator record, with operator code `ShimenDistrictOffice`, number 0507 and 36 associated route records [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. This page describes a municipal service authority, not a private bus company with a separately sourced founding date, parent group or shareholder structure [^shimen-contact].

The New Taipei contact page gives the district office switchboard as (02) 2638-1721 and lists the office’s section structure [^shimen-contact]. The Shimen office’s service index publishes the office address as 253001 New Taipei City, Shimen District, Zhongshan Road 66, and links separate community-bus and medical-bus pages [^shimen-service-index]. The office’s duties page assigns “New Bus (community bus) contract execution management” to the Economic Development section, extension 604 [^shimen-service-duties]. That establishes an administrative responsibility, but not the contractor, contract value, award date or vehicle ownership [^shimen-service-duties].

## Current community-bus service

The current community-bus page says the service does not run on Sundays or national holidays and publishes four route families: F151 for the Jianlu, Shimen and Shanxi areas; F152 for Fuki, Laomei and Demao; F153 for Qianhua, Maolin and Caoli; and F154 for Fuki, Laomei and Shanxi, with F154 suspended on Tuesdays [^shimen-community-bus-2026]. F151’s published timetable reaches rural stops such as Qingshan Waterfall, Shuntian Holy Mother Temple, Laomei and the district office [^shimen-community-bus-2026]. F152 links Laomei with Baishawan, Fuki Fishing Harbor, Cape Fugui Lighthouse and the district office on selected departures [^shimen-community-bus-2026]. F153 serves the district office, the former nuclear plant area, Qianhua Elementary School, Caoli and Shimen schools [^shimen-community-bus-2026]. F154 is a weekday route through Fuki, Laomei, Qingshan and Shanxi that returns to the district office [^shimen-community-bus-2026].

The December 2025 official notice says F151 and F153 were adjusted, F154 was newly added with Tuesday suspension, and the medical-bus service was extended to Guandu Hospital [^shimen-115-notice]. A February 2026 notice records that from 2 March 2026 the F153 08:30 and 09:45 departures stopped extending to the North 21 7K location because the office found no passenger demand at that stop [^shimen-f153-adjustment]. A July 2026 notice separately records route adjustments for F151’s 16:35 and 18:10 departures, effective 16 July 2026, after the office evaluated operating efficiency and actual use [^shimen-f151-adjustment]. These are dated service changes, not evidence of a permanent route-ownership transfer [^shimen-f153-adjustment] [^shimen-f151-adjustment].

## F161 medical bus

The office’s medical-bus page publishes F161 as a dedicated medical service. It says the bus supports the F153 06:15 student departure on weekdays during the school term, then describes an 08:00 departure from Caoli to Mackay Memorial Hospital and Guandu Hospital with a 12:00 return from Guandu through Tamsui to Caoli [^shimen-medical-bus-2026]. The published medical service runs Monday through Saturday, with national-holiday suspension, and is for Shimen residents going to hospital appointments or accompanying patients [^shimen-medical-bus-2026]. The morning outbound trip does not permit intermediate alighting, the return trip gives Shimen residents priority at the hospital, and the Tamsui Wansi Hotel stop is limited to Shimen residents [^shimen-medical-bus-2026].
