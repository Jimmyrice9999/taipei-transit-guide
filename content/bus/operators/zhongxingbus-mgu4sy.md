## Company history and legal identity

The MOEA record identifies Zhongxing Daye Bus Co., Ltd. as unified business number 18598304, with representative Lu Qilong, a registered address in Taipei’s Shilin District, total and paid-in capital of NT$557 million and statutory registration date 31 December 1965 [^moea-zhongxing]. It lists intercity and city bus operation and vehicle repair among the approved business categories [^moea-zhongxing].

The Zhongxing Group history supplies the predecessor narrative: Daye Bus was established in December 1965 under the Taiwan Provincial Committee of the Kuomintang, reorganized as Zhongxing Daye Bus on 18 January 1966, and joined Taipei’s joint-operation system in 1977 [^zhongxing-history]. Those milestones are related but are not silently treated as one event: the group page describes the operating predecessor and reorganization, while the MOEA page records the current legal entity [^zhongxing-history][^moea-zhongxing].

The current MOEA director table records corporate representation by Longfu International, Longteng International Automobile, New Taipei Bus and Kuang-Hua Asset Management [^moea-zhongxing]. That is a registered corporate-representation snapshot; it does not establish a complete parent-company or merger chronology [^moea-zhongxing].

## Network and operating sites

The New Taipei TDX snapshot associates 15 routes with ZhongxingBus [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. The operator-group directory publishes nine named operating-site entries: Zhonghe, Xizhi, Tianmu, Sanchong, National Palace Museum, Taishan, Zhoumei, Sanzhi and Beishike [^zhongxing-stations]. It also shows route clusters at Zhonghe, Xizhi, Tianmu and Sanchong, including 214/311/950, 591/605/668/675/711, 280/602/203/279 and 227/600/815/816/933 respectively [^zhongxing-stations]. These site associations document the published directory; the route section below remains generated from TDX rather than reconstructed from the group page [^tdx-bus-operator-pull][^zhongxing-stations].

The station page gives addresses and telephone details for the named entries but does not state opening dates or vehicle capacities [^zhongxing-stations]. The checked sources also do not publish a current model-level fleet roster, propulsion split or complete contract history, so those fields remain TBC.

## Oversight and documented incidents

The Highway Bureau’s detailed record for Zhongxing Daye’s highway-passenger operation displays a grade-A evaluation for ROC 112 and lists grade-A results for several national-highway periods [^thb-zhongxing-record]. It records three historical incidents: an A1 incident dated ROC 108-10-10 with one death, an A2 incident dated ROC 108-03-29 with two injuries and an A2 incident dated ROC 102-12-28 with nine injuries [^thb-zhongxing-record]. The record is explicitly for the company’s highway-passenger business, so it is not presented as a result for every city-bus route in this New Taipei TDX record [^thb-zhongxing-record].

The same regulatory table records one daily-driving-time violation in ROC 114 Q3 and one in ROC 113 Q4, alongside nonzero entries in earlier quarters [^thb-zhongxing-record]. Taipei PTO’s 2024 and 2025 city-bus evaluation notices also list Zhongxing Daye in the 優等 group [^pto-zhongxing-2024][^pto-zhongxing-2025]. These are dated oversight records, not evidence of a complete fleet or contract register.
