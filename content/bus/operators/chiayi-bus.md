Chiayi Bus is a regional operator rather than only a BRT brand.[^chiayi-bus-about] Its own company
page says the business began in 1917 and currently lists 145 commercial buses,
20 tour buses, 180 employees and 37 operating routes.[^chiayi-bus-about] Those
are the operator's own current profile figures, with the page's counting scope;
they are not substituted for the separate City and County TDX snapshots or
treated as a permanent national operator census.[^chiayi-bus-about]

The operator's route index makes the network's geography visible. It lists
departures from Chiayi, Beigang and Puzi, includes ordinary regional routes
such as 7201–7217 and 7700–7701, and keeps the BRT group separate with 7211,
7212A and 7212.[^chiayi-bus-route-index] The same page exposes route-map,
timetable and fare links, but this profile does not copy those route-specific
tables into a timeless operator-wide frequency or fare claim.[^chiayi-bus-route-index]

## Operating sites

The operator's facilities page identifies the Chiayi headquarters as an
administrative office, vehicle repair and maintenance centre, and Chiayi-area
vehicle parking centre. It separately names Zhongshan terminal as the largest
Chiayi operating station, Beigang station as the largest Yunlin operating
station, and Puzi station as the company's largest Puzi operating station.[^chiayi-bus-sites]
The source names functions and locations, not a complete depot-capacity or
route-allocation register; those fields remain TBC.

## BRT and current operation

The BRT current-state page describes a 29.3-km corridor, with a 15.4-km
HSR-to-rear-station main section, a 4.3-km Chiayi-city connecting section and a
9.6-km Chiayi-county connecting section.[^chiayi-bus-brt-current] It lists 18 displayed stops and says
that 7211 and 7212 are operated with accessible low-floor buses.[^chiayi-bus-brt-current]
The separate BRT query page publishes route 7211, route 7212 and an early
7212 branch, with two-direction stop tables.[^chiayi-bus-brt-query]

The operator's current notices show why route pages need dates. The notices
page includes September 2026 traffic-control announcements and a BRT notice
stating that selected 7212 trips add Zhongxiao Beijie stop service from 1
September 2026.[^chiayi-bus-notices] That is a dated service notice, not a
claim that every 7212 journey always uses the same stop pattern.

## Scope and gaps

This page connects the operator's own route groups, sites and BRT material to
the Chiayi regional bus layer. It does not assert a route-by-route fleet
assignment, a current vehicle-registration list, a full contract/ownership
history, or a complete current fare matrix. Those require separate primary
records and remain TBC.
