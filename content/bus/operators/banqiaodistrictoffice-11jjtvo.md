## A district-run free bus, 2005 to 2024

The zero routes in the current TDX snapshot is not an absence of history — it is the endpoint of one. In 2005, responding to transfer demand created by the Bannan Line's opening, the then-township office created a free shuttle between the Xikun area and central Banqiao, run under contract by Capital Bus (台北客運): "2005年因應捷運板南線轉乘需求，板橋市公所新增往返板橋溪崑地區與板橋市區的『板橋市免費公車A線』"[^cna-f501-2024]. That route was converted to a paid, numbered service ("840") in 2008, then made free again as "F501"; a sibling route serving the Fuzhou (浮洲) area, "板橋市公所浮洲B線", followed the same free-to-paid arc, becoming paid route "841" and later free route "F502"[^cna-f501-2024].

Both conversions to paid service have since happened again, in the other direction. F502 was converted to paid route "585" in 2021, and F501 — by then Banqiao's last remaining free district-office bus — was converted to paid route "596" on 1 November 2024, reported as "板橋最後1條免費公車走入歷史" (Banqiao's last free bus becomes history)[^cna-f501-2024]. Both routes were operated throughout by Capital Bus under contract to the district office, not run with the office's own vehicles[^cna-f501-2024].
