## Legal record and scope

The TDX pull identifies this record as Royal Bus Co., Ltd. (皇家客運), with one associated route record and the operator phone 0800-551-687 [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. A Ministry of Economic Affairs change-registration list for May 2023 records the legal name 皇家客運股份有限公司, unified number 16302918, registered address 新北市金山區中山路95號, representative 許戎宏 and capital of NT$90 million [^royal-company-register]. That dated list is evidence of the registered entry at that point, not a complete current shareholder or parent-group record [^royal-company-register].

The fetched primary material does not establish a founding chronology, merger history, shareholder control or parent group. It also does not establish that the registered address is a depot. Those fields remain TBC rather than being inferred from the address or from the company's route name.

## Route and service

The route association in the committed pull is generated from the route record's `operatorIds`, and is `1717` [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. The Taipei public-transit route page describes the service as Taipei University Hospital–Jinshan Post Office, with 06:00–18:40 first and last departures in both its weekday and holiday summaries [^royal-route-page]. It publishes six timed variants: four Taipei-origin variants differing by the Jiantan and Xiaoyoukeng service pattern, and two Jinshan-origin variants [^royal-route-page]. The stop sequence runs through central Taipei and Shilin, climbs through Yangmingshan and Xiaoyoukeng/Matsao, then continues via Jinshan Farm and Chongguang to Jinshan Post Office; the official page lists the stop rows rather than a simple city-boundary description [^royal-route-page].

The same page labels the fare method as mileage-based and publishes separate Taipei and Jinshan customer-service numbers [^royal-route-page]. The official 1717 fare PDF is a mileage matrix naming Taipei Station, Taipei Fine Arts Museum, Shilin Official Residence, Yangmingshan, Zhuzihu Police Station, Qixing Mountain, Xiaoyoukeng, Jinshan Farm, Chongguang, Nanshi and Jinshan as fare landmarks [^royal-fare-table]. In the Taipei Public Transportation Office's ride-code FAQ, the 1717 exception is dated by the page's 2026 update metadata: only EasyPay is accepted in that ride-code context, the boarding base is NT$25 for full fare and NT$13 for passengers aged 65 or over, and the remaining charge is adjusted by mileage when alighting [^royal-fare-faq].

## Accessibility, subsidy and operating record

Yangmingshan National Park's accessibility page includes Royal Bus 1717 among the routes currently providing low-floor buses in the Yangmingshan area [^royal-ymsnp-access]. The Taipei evaluation dataset identifies 1717 as a Taipei-governed city-bus route and says it has been included in the city's operating-service evaluation since 2020 [^royal-evaluation-page].

A 2023 Public Transportation Office meeting record approved Royal Bus's application for an operating-loss subsidy without deducting overlapping-route subsidy [^royal-subsidy-meeting]. In the discussion, Royal Bus described 1717 as a route used by Chinese Culture University students and older residents travelling between Taipei and Jinshan; the office record preserves that as the operator's explanation, not as an independently surveyed passenger profile [^royal-subsidy-meeting]. The same record says the company reported that electric buses could not climb the route, and the committee required the company to put the NT$1,200 pass online by 20 December 2023 [^royal-subsidy-meeting].

The city's 2024 accounts record a more serious compliance issue: vehicles on route 1717 were reported as lacking third-party liability insurance from 1 February 2024, so NT$1 million of operating subsidy was held until Royal Bus supplied the missing insurance documentation [^royal-budget-113]. This is a dated government budget record, not a general claim about the company's present insurance status. Earlier, the Transportation Department ordered a full-day 1717 suspension on 10 July 2015 because Typhoon Chan-hom created unsafe weather and mountain-road conditions [^royal-weather-suspension].
