## History and operating scope

The official related-enterprise history says Kuang-Hua Bus was established in ROC year 58, October 1969, by Shen Shui-mu [^csgroup-kuanghua-history]. It describes the original operating purpose as serving residents travelling between Tianmu, Shilin, Neihu and Taipei [^csgroup-kuanghua-history]. The same page records entry into Taipei’s joint-operation system in April 1977 and a December 1997 application with Keelung Bus to operate the Shilin–Keelung highway service via National Freeway 1 [^csgroup-kuanghua-history].

The committed Taipei TDX record associates 46 routes with KuangHuaBus, and the route list below is generated from that government data snapshot [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. The operator-group site publishes a different, station-oriented view of operations, so its allocations are reported as a second source rather than folded into the TDX total [^csgroup-kuanghua-stations].

## Stations and route allocations

The current station page names ten sites: Donghu, Beifeng, Tiandong, Haizhuan, Tianxi, Zhonghe, Beishike, Zhoumei, National Palace Museum and Linguang [^csgroup-kuanghua-stations]. It gives an address and contact number for each, but no opening date or vehicle capacity [^csgroup-kuanghua-stations].

The page also publishes operating allocations by site. Donghu is listed with 240 Express, 247, 521, 553, 267, 600, 681 and Blue 20 among its services; Beifeng is listed with 203, Red 2, Blue 21, Blue 21 Sub, Blue 23 and Blue 39; Beishike is listed with 250, 683, Northern Ring Trunk, Minibus 8, Neihu commuter 17, Children’s Fun 1 and 2, and 9006; and Linguang is listed with 72 and 680 [^csgroup-kuanghua-stations]. These are operator-published station allocations, not evidence that the TDX association list is exhaustive for every service type [^tdx-bus-operator-pull][^csgroup-kuanghua-stations].

## Regulator, fleet and ownership gaps

The Highway Bureau’s directory lists Kuang-Hua as an active intercity-bus company at Zhongzheng Road 499 in Shilin, with public telephone 02-28826780 [^thb-kuanghua-directory]. The directory does not publish fleet models, propulsion, depot capacities or route contracts [^thb-kuanghua-directory].

New Taipei’s 111(2) evaluation report gives Kuang-Hua 84.01 points and grade 甲等, after 乙等 in 110(2) and 甲等 in 111(1) [^ntpc-kuanghua-eval]. That is a dated quality-oversight record rather than a current fleet or ownership register [^ntpc-kuanghua-eval]. The checked primary pages establish the company’s history, operating sites and route allocations, but not a complete vehicle roster, station capacity, opening chronology, statutory parent or contract history; those fields remain TBC.
