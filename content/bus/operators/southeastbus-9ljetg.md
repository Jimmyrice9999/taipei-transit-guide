## History, company identity and affiliated enterprise

The government-hosted TaiwanJobs employer profile identifies 東南汽車客運股份有限公司 with unified business number 80336169, public-bus passenger transport as its industry, NT$200 million capital, 黃仕旻 as representative, a Neihu address and an establishment date of 18 June 2002 [^southeast-profile]. Because this is an employer profile rather than the directly fetched MOEA registry record, those fields are presented as the profile’s published record, not as a substitute for a statutory company extract [^southeast-profile].

The same profile says Southeast Bus began taking over Taipei Public Bus Administration routes in October 2002 and completed 11 route transfers within 13 days, serving mountain routes around Neihu and Nangang as well as urban commuter movements [^southeast-profile]. This is a dated start-up account; the current route list below is generated from the committed TDX associations rather than copied from that historical narrative [^southeast-profile] [^tdx-bus-operator-pull].

A 2010 Veterans Affairs Council profile describes Southeast Bus as a related enterprise of Jianming Bus, also known through the Fei-Gou Bus brand, and says its services at that time extended across Taipei, Kaohsiung and remote areas [^southeast-vac-profile]. The article is evidence of the relationship as described by a government publication in 2010, not a current ownership percentage or a complete corporate-group chart [^southeast-vac-profile].

## Evaluation and regulatory record

Taipei’s 113 second-period service evaluation placed Southeast Bus among eight operators rated 優等 [^taipei-eval-113-2]. The announcement says the evaluation covered station facilities and service, vehicle equipment and safety, passenger service and driver management, accessibility, and company management, and records the public commendation on 27 February 2025 [^taipei-eval-113-2]. This is a dated service-quality result, not a claim that every earlier or later evaluation had the same outcome [^taipei-eval-113-2].

The Taipei Department of Transportation’s 2006 notice records a materially different period: Southeast Bus and Da-Yu Bus received 丙等 in the first 2006 evaluation [^taipei-action-2006]. The department withheld 10 vehicles from Southeast Bus’s highest-kilometre-revenue route allocation for three months, and the review group assigned Danan Bus to operate Southeast’s Route 32 for the same period; the notice also states that repeated 丙等 performance without improvement could lead to withdrawal of route operating rights [^taipei-action-2006]. The two evaluation records are retained as a time-separated oversight history rather than averaged into a single rating [^taipei-action-2006] [^taipei-eval-113-2].
