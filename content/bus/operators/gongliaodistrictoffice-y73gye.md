## Government-unit record

The committed TDX pull names Gongliao District Office (貢寮區公所) as a NewTaipei operator record, with operator code GongliaoDistrictOffice, number 1005 and nine associated route records [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. This page describes a municipal service authority, not a private bus company with a separately sourced founding date, parent group, shareholder structure or merger history [^gongliao-profile].

The district office publishes its address as 228003 New Taipei City, Gongliao District, Changtai Road 20 and its switchboard as 02-24941601~5 [^gongliao-profile]. Its duty table assigns community-bus operations, including New Bus and Happiness Xiao Huang, to the Agriculture and Economic Affairs section, extension 404 [^gongliao-contact]. New Taipei's general New Bus page describes the service as a free continuation of community buses and explains the F-prefixed route-code family, placing Gongliao in the F8 group [^ntpc-newbus-general].

## Current service layers

The district-office service index lists a New Bus timetable updated from 28 July 2025 and a separate Happiness Bus timetable updated from 31 March 2026 [^gongliao-schedule-index]. The fetched New Bus PDF says it took effect from 16 July 2025, does not run on Saturdays, Sundays or public holidays, is free, and prioritises non-tourist demand and local residents when seats are insufficient [^gongliao-schedule-2025]. Outside the regular stops on Provincial Highway 2, the same PDF says the routes use hail-and-ride operation; its service phone is 02-2494-1601 [^gongliao-schedule-2025].

That New Bus PDF presents F835 District Office–Aodi, F836 Aodi–Fulian, F837 District Office–Longdong, F838 District Office–Meifeng, F839 District Office–Meifeng–Longdong and the Gongliao Junior High student service [^gongliao-schedule-2025]. The district office's 2026 Happiness Bus PDF is a separate daily service layer: it states an effective date of 1 April 2026, says the service is free, identifies a medium bus on the Fulian route and nine-seat vehicles on the Shuangfu and Taoyuan Valley routes, and publishes Longgang, Jilin and Meifeng route families [^gongliao-happiness-2026].

The TDX route list below is therefore a data snapshot, not one timetable. It contains the five F-coded New Bus rows, the student service, and three route-name records—Fulian, Longgang and Jilin—that overlap with the separate Happiness Bus publication [^tdx-bus-operator-pull][^gongliao-happiness-2026].

## Historical route evidence and incident

The January 2022 New Bus PDF includes F831 District Office–Fulian in both directions [^gongliao-schedule-2022]. The 2025 New Bus PDF fetched from the current district-office index does not include F831, and the current TDX snapshot does not associate F831 with this operator [^gongliao-schedule-index][^gongliao-schedule-2025][^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. The historical row is retained as dated evidence rather than presented as current service.

On 10 July 2026, Gongliao District Office announced that all New Bus services in the district were suspended because New Taipei had declared a work and school closure for typhoon precautions [^gongliao-typhoon-2026]. This is a documented weather-response action, not evidence of a permanent route cancellation or operator transfer [^gongliao-typhoon-2026].
