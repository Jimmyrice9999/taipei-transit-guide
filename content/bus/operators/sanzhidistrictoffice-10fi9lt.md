## Government service authority

The committed TDX pull identifies this page as the New Taipei record for Sanzhi District Office (三芝區公所), with operator code SanzhiDistrictOffice, operator number 0304 and 13 associated route records [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. This is a district-office service record, not evidence that the TDX row is a private bus corporation with a separate parent group or shareholder structure [^sanzhi-office-contact].

The office publishes its switchboard as (02) 2636-2111 and its address as 252204 New Taipei City, Sanzhi District, Zhongshan Road Section 1, 32 [^sanzhi-office-contact]. Its administrative-responsibilities page assigns 新巴士 to the Agricultural Economics section, alongside agricultural, tourism, disaster-prevention and park-maintenance work [^sanzhi-admin-duties].

## Current New Bus scope

The office's New Bus index currently lists F131 Beixinzhuang, F132 Baxian, F133 Puwei–Yuanshan, F135 Guandu Mackay medical shuttle, F136 Houcuo, F137 Taipei medical shuttle, two F138 services (Xinguzhuang and Neihengshan), and F139 Tamsui night MRT shuttle [^sanzhi-newbus-index]. ## Fleet and operating model

In August 2025 the district office replaced a community bus that had been in service for more than 20 years and assigned the new vehicle to F133 and F139 [^sanzhi-newbus-2025]. The same official account describes the bus as having six cameras, front and rear collision-warning sensors and passenger grab rails; it does not identify a manufacturer, chassis or propulsion system [^sanzhi-newbus-2025].

The 2025 official account describes nine community-bus routes in total, with seven outsourced and two self-operated [^sanzhi-newbus-2025]. It also describes F133 as the widest-coverage route, with three subroutes, five weekday commuter trips and two weekend trips, while F139 provides two Sanzhi-to-Tamsui and three Tamsui-to-Sanzhi trips between 19:55 and 23:30 [^sanzhi-newbus-2025]. The statement is a dated service-delivery description, not a complete tender history.

On 28 May 2026 the office announced another new community bus and again assigned it to F133 and F139, alongside route optimisation and safety equipment; the announcement does not publish a model or supplier [^sanzhi-newbus-2026].

## Schedule evidence

The fetched F131 timetable starts at the Sanzhi Junior High School stop and shows five weekday departures and three weekend departures toward Shibawan Road Junction, with a weekend exception that omits Xiaosuzhai Road Junction [^sanzhi-f131-schedule]. F136 also starts at Sanzhi Junior High School, runs by stop-on-request service, and publishes weekday and weekend trips toward the Visitor Platform [^sanzhi-f136-schedule].

The F138 Neihengshan timetable starts at Warehouse No. 2, says the whole route is stop-on-request, and publishes five weekday departures plus two weekend departures, with some return trips terminating at Miaofa Temple [^sanzhi-f138-inner-schedule]. F139 is published as a Monday-to-Saturday night service with Sunday suspended: two Sanzhi-to-Tamsui trips and three Tamsui-to-Sanzhi trips, with each vehicle limited to 20 seats [^sanzhi-f139-schedule].

Holiday operation is dated rather than permanent. The January 2026 notice suspends F135, F137 and F139 on the listed Lunar New Year and 28 February dates, while the other routes are suspended for the Lunar New Year dates and use holiday schedules on 27 and 28 February [^sanzhi-holiday-2026].
