## Government-unit record

The committed TDX pull identifies Yingge District Office (鶯歌區公所) as a NewTaipei operator record with operator code `YinggeDistrictOffice`, number 2201 and eight associated route records [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. This page describes a municipal district-office service authority, not a private bus company with a separately sourced founding date, parent group, shareholder structure or merger history [^yingge-profile].

The office publishes its address as 239217 New Taipei City, Yingge District, Ren'ai Road 55 and its switchboard as (02)2678-0202 [^yingge-profile]. Its Economic Development section assigns New Bus liaison work to extensions 517 and 523 [^yingge-duty]. The office describes New Bus as a continuation of earlier community-bus services with free riding and explains that the F prefix was chosen both for “free” and for a common route-code system [^yingge-profile].

## Current service evidence

The office's July 2026 introduction names six current route families: F651 Erqiao–Jiande, F652 Enzhugong medical, F653 International New Town, F655 Chang Gung medical via Dahu, F656 Fengji–Ideal and F657 Chang Gung medical via Fengming [^yingge-profile]. The same notice describes connections to Yingge Vocational High School, Jianshan Junior High School, Jianguo Elementary School, Yongji Elementary School, Yingge Stone, the New Taipei City Art Museum, Ceramics Old Street, the New Taipei City Yingge Ceramics Museum, En Chu Kong Hospital and Linkou Chang Gung Hospital [^yingge-profile].

The 2026 timetable is effective from 1 January 2026 or an agency-notified date and lists the six families under the 二橋線, 國際線, 鳳吉線, 幸福巴士 and 長庚醫療線 headings [^yingge-timetable-2026]. Its F652 12:30 trip is reservation-only, requiring at least 15 passengers to call the district office two days in advance; the other trips are fixed departures [^yingge-timetable-2026].

F651 gained 11 large-bus weekday trips from 1 January 2026, while the original medium-bus service continues on weekdays, weekends and public holidays; the added trips run between Yingge railway station and Yingge Vocational High School without entering Jiande [^yingge-f651]. F652's extension began in January 2025 after Fengming railway station opened and Taoyuan Bus route 5001 stopped; it now serves Fengming railway station instead of Taoyuan Bus Station and remains free [^yingge-f652].

The F653 notice publishes separate F653A and F653B departure sets rather than treating the TDX records as one undifferentiated route [^yingge-f653]. F655 operates from Yingge District Office toward Taoyuan and Linkou Chang Gung hospitals with boarding-only outward stops, while F657 starts at Changfu Elementary School and uses the same hospital pair with the corresponding return boarding pattern [^yingge-f655-f657]. On F656, holiday departures from 09:20 through 17:30 omit Tongqingli Activity Center and Yingge Junior High School during Ceramics Old Street traffic control, and the railway-station return boarding point is moved to the Yingge Junior High swimming-pool stop [^yingge-f656].

## Contract and research gaps

The district office publishes a New Taipei bus passenger-transport contract notice with payment, concession, safety and service rules, but the fetched page does not identify a current Yingge New Bus contractor, route award, tender value or operating term [^yingge-contract]. The 2026 timetable says that all New Bus routes suspend when the New Taipei government announces a work-and-school closure for typhoon or other natural-disaster safety [^yingge-timetable-2026].

The fetched primary sources do not publish vehicle models, manufacturers, chassis, bodies, propulsion, a depot or garage roster, opening dates, current route-level fleet allocations, or a complete contractor history; those fields remain TBC [^tdx-bus-operator-pull][^yingge-profile][^yingge-timetable-2026].
