Hsinchu Bus is a useful regional operator profile because its own pages show a service boundary changing over time. The company profile traces the organisation to the establishment of Taiwan Railway Corporation on 29 September 1919 and says it was reorganised as Hsinchu Automobile Passenger Transport in 1946.[^hcbus-about]

The same profile describes a historical operating scope of 16 city-bus routes and two highway-coach routes, with operating areas listed across Taipei, Hsinchu and Taichung.[^hcbus-about] That statement is retained as the operator's own profile description rather than being silently substituted for the current TDX snapshot, because the profile does not function as a dated national route export.[^hcbus-about]

Hsinchu Bus published a 10 September 2024 notice explaining that losses, demographic change, pandemic effects and private-vehicle use had made the regional operating environment difficult.[^hcbus-transition] The notice says regional business in Miaoli would be taken over by a new operator from 16 September, and Hsinchu, Zhudong and Guanxi regional business from 1 November; it separately says Hsinchu Bus would continue providing highway services 9003 Hsinchu–Taipei and 9010 Hsinchu–Taichung, together with Hsinchu City urban routes.[^hcbus-transition]

That transition is important when reading the national structured bus layer.[^hcbus-about][^hcbus-transition] A TDX operator record or a historic company description does not by itself prove that every route historically associated with Hsinchu Bus is still operated by the company.[^hcbus-about][^hcbus-transition] The profile therefore links the operator's documented scope to its dated notice rather than copying a route-record total into the page.[^hcbus-about][^hcbus-transition]

The current homepage carries August 2026 notices for 9003, including an August 25 notice about a stop change, and for 9010, including an August 1 notice about changes beginning 1 September.[^hcbus-home] It also shows the company's Hsinchu address at 16 Minzu Road and the main telephone number 03-5225151~8.[^hcbus-home]

The current full fleet roster, depot ownership, procurement history and vehicle-level census remain TBC here.
