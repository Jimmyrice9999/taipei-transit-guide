## TDX record

The committed TDX pull identifies this page as the operator record for Sindian Bus Co., Ltd. (新店客運), with source municipality Taipei [^tdx-bus-operator-pull].

TDX publishes the record ID bus-operator-sindianbus-j8c9wy, operator code SindianBus, operator number 1304, phone 02-2666-0678 and URL http://www.sindianbus.com.tw for this record [^tdx-bus-operator-pull].

This is a data record, not a claim that every TDX record with the same company name is a separate legal entity [^tdx-bus-operator-pull].

## Company history and legal identity

Sindian Bus’s own history page says the company was established in August 1980 and initially focused on road passenger transport connecting Sindian, Wulai and Pinglin with Taipei [^sindian-about]. The company describes that early mountain service as a way to provide residents with a stable connection to the city [^sindian-about].

The operator says it joined Taipei’s joint-bus system in the 1990s and took part in routes 642, now identified by the company as the Songjiang–Xinsheng trunk service, and 643 [^sindian-about]. Its current description places the service area across the greater Taipei region, including Sindian, Wulai and Pinglin [^sindian-about].

The Ministry of Economic Affairs record identifies 新店客運股份有限公司 as unified business number 33846205, with approved status, a registered establishment date of 15 September 1980, representative 林映秀 and registered address 新北市新店區松林路9號 [^moea-sindian]. It records NT$180 million paid-in capital, 1.8 million issued shares and urban passenger transport and vehicle-repair businesses [^moea-sindian]. The checked registry page does not identify a parent group or publish a complete ownership chart [^moea-sindian].

## Network, dispatch sites and fleet direction

The current route list below is generated from municipality-scoped TDX operator associations; it is not a hand-maintained list of every route operated by the legal company [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. The two TDX records therefore show different slices of the same operator: this Taipei record has six associations, while the separate New Taipei record has 18 [^tdx-bus-operator-pull].

Sindian Bus’s contact page publishes a Sindian dispatch station at 新北市新店區新烏路一段26巷35-1號 and a Jinxiu dispatch station at 新北市新店區安康路三段567號, with service hours for both sites [^sindian-contact]. The page gives no opening date, vehicle capacity or route-allocation table, so those fields remain TBC [^sindian-contact].

The operator’s history page says it intends to introduce low-carbon electric buses and upgrade the fleet toward more energy-efficient service [^sindian-about]. That is a stated direction, not evidence of a current model-by-model fleet, propulsion split or procurement total; those remain TBC [^sindian-about].

## Service-quality record

The Ministry of Transportation’s Road Safety Portal reported on 27 March 2020 that Sindian Bus was one of five operators rated 優等 in Taipei’s 108 second-period joint-bus service evaluation [^motc-sindian-eval]. The same notice says the evaluation covered station facilities and service, vehicle equipment and safety, passenger service and driver management, accessibility, and company management [^motc-sindian-eval]. This dated result is not presented as a current grade.
