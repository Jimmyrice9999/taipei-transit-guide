## Government-unit record

The committed TDX pull identifies Taishan District Office (泰山區公所) as a NewTaipei operator record with operator code `TaishanDistrictOffice`, number 1004 and eight associated route records [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. This page describes a municipal district-office service authority, not a private bus company with a separately sourced founding date, parent group, shareholder structure or merger history [^taishan-profile].

The office publishes its telephone as (02)2909-9551 and its address as 243084 New Taipei City, Taishan District, Mingzhi Road Section 1, No. 322 [^taishan-profile]. The Economic Development section assigns responsibility for the district's free shuttle buses to an officer at extension 1701 [^taishan-duty]. The current timetable page was updated on 10 March 2026 and presents the service as Taishan New Bus rather than as a separately incorporated carrier [^taishan-schedule].

## Current service evidence

The official page groups the 1 October 2025 timetable as free New Bus service for F211, F212, the F212 subline and F213 within the district, then separately lists the free F217 Taishan–Linkou Chang Gung Hospital and F218 Taishan–Taipei Hospital medical shuttles [^taishan-schedule]. TDX keeps the underlying associations more granular: it records F212Sub, F212 Fengjiang, F212, F213 Liming, F213, F217 and F218 alongside F211 [^tdx-bus-operator-pull].

The office says F217 was created to help residents travel to and return from Linkou Chang Gung Hospital, and that its outward trip to the hospital allows boarding only, with no alighting, because demand is high and medical passengers must be able to board [^taishan-schedule]. The same page records that F215 and F216 changed from New Bus services into city buses from 1 July 2020, which is a service-history boundary rather than a current operator association [^taishan-schedule].

The available HTML page names the route families and the operational rules but links the detailed timetable artwork as image files; no stop-by-stop claim is made here from an inaccessible image [^taishan-schedule]. This distinction matters because the route page is current enough to carry the 2025 timetable grouping and 2026 update date, while the committed TDX pull is a data snapshot with eight route associations [^tdx-bus-operator-pull][^taishan-schedule].

## Historical procurement evidence

Taishan's 2016 official settlement ledger records a bid-deposit entry for the 2016 Taishan–Taipei free-shuttle service case of NT$665,000 and a separate 2017 service-case bid deposit of NT$782,000 [^taishan-budget-2016]. The same ledger records performance-guarantee entries for those service cases [^taishan-budget-2016]. These entries establish that the district office handled dated service procurements and security deposits; they do not establish the successful contractors, vehicle allocations, contract terms or whether those historical arrangements continued into the current F211–F218 service [^taishan-budget-2016].
