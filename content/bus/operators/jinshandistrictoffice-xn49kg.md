## Government-unit record

The committed TDX pull names Jinshan District Office (金山區公所) as a NewTaipei operator record, with operator code `JinshanDistrictOffice`, number 0814 and 25 associated route records [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. This page therefore describes a municipal service authority, not a private bus company with a separately sourced founding date, parent group or shareholder structure [^jinshan-contact].

New Taipei's official contact page identifies the office at 208226 New Taipei City, Jinshan District, Zhongzheng Road 11, gives the switchboard as (02) 2498-5965, and assigns community-bus work to the Agricultural/Economic section, extension 325 [^jinshan-contact]. The Jinshan office's own New Bus index lists F931, F932 and F933 notices published in 2026, a timetable download, and an earlier Smart New Bus notice dated 2020-12-10 [^jinshan-newbus-index].

## Current New Bus service

The 2026 F931 notice lists eight weekday departures [^jinshan-f931-2026]. Its timetable serves the district office, nearby villages, health facilities and rural destinations including Chonghe, Kuisan Lake, Lianghu, Jinbaoshan and the Ju Ming Museum; the notice identifies the two office stop positions as beside Zhongzheng Road 11 and in front of Zhongzheng Road 6 [^jinshan-f931-2026].

The 2026 F932 notice lists seven weekday departures, including a student service that stops during school holidays [^jinshan-f932-2026]. Its published loops connect the office with western rural stops, the hospital and the villages around Chonghe and Lianghu [^jinshan-f932-2026]. The F933 notice lists ten weekday departures, including a student service and a separate school-holiday operating variation; its loops cover the office, Jinshan Police Station, Fengyu, Shanwei, Fugang and Sheliao as well as the hospital [^jinshan-f933-2026]. All three current notices warn that road works, natural disasters and other force-majeure events may require route adjustment or diversion [^jinshan-f931-2026].

The 2021 official timetable is useful as a dated comparison, not as a current roster [^jinshan-timetable-2021]. It lists all three F routes, eight F931 trips, seven F932 trips and ten F933 trips, with student-service and holiday exceptions [^jinshan-timetable-2021]. The 2021 PDF gives the Agricultural/Economic section's service telephone as 2498-5966, while the 2026 route notices and current office contact page give 2498-5965; both values are retained because the sources are dated differently [^jinshan-timetable-2021] [^jinshan-f931-2026] [^jinshan-contact]. The older F931 timetable also uses different intermediate stops from the 2026 notice, so these pages should not be flattened into a single timeless route description [^jinshan-timetable-2021] [^jinshan-f931-2026].
