## Government-unit record

The committed TDX pull identifies Sanxia District Office (三峽區公所) as a NewTaipei operator record with operator code SanxiaDistrictOffice, number 0308 and eight associated route records [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. This page describes a municipal district-office service authority, not a private bus corporation with a separately sourced founding date, parent group, shareholder structure or merger history [^sanxia-profile].

The office publishes its address as 23741 New Taipei City, Sanxia District, Zhongshan Road 17 and its switchboard as (02)2671-1017 [^sanxia-profile]. Its Economic Development section lists New Bus and bus-related work, including transport-related disaster-prevention evaluation, at extension 710 [^sanxia-duty]. New Taipei's general New Bus page describes the service as a free continuation of community buses and places Sanxia in the F6 code grouping with Tucheng, Shulin and Yingge [^ntpc-newbus-general].

## Current service evidence

The district office's New Bus index is labelled free public transport and lists F623 Hongdao, F625 Erjiu, F626 Ziwei, F627 Wuliao, F628 Couhe, F629 Zhulong–Ankeng and F630 Linkou Chang Gung Hospital Shuttle; the page was updated 1 February 2023 [^sanxia-newbus-index]. The same page says New Bus services suspend when the central government declares work and school closures for typhoon safety [^sanxia-newbus-index].

The route detail pages are newer than the index: the fetched F623, F625, F627, F628, F629 and F630 pages show 8 January 2026 update dates [^sanxia-f623][^sanxia-f625][^sanxia-f627][^sanxia-f628][^sanxia-f629][^sanxia-f630]. F623 has different weekday/Sunday departures and 15 published stop rows toward Fuqing Temple, while F625 has five departures and a 23-stop table that includes Sanxia District Office [^sanxia-f623][^sanxia-f625].

F626 publishes five departures and says its 13:30 and 18:30 trips do not run through to Ziwei Tianhou Temple [^sanxia-f626]. F627 serves a long rural stop sequence with different weekday and Sunday departure times, and F628 publishes a holiday rule that turns at the Songyi Chemical turnaround rather than continuing its full route [^sanxia-f627][^sanxia-f628]. F629 publishes different weekday/Sunday schedules plus additional 06:15 and 11:15 trips to Anxin Station and Sifenzai, with a 48-stop table [^sanxia-f629]. F630 is a Sunday-suspended shuttle: its outward departures leave Longfu parking and its return departures leave Linkou Chang Gung Hospital [^sanxia-f630].

The 2026 Spring Festival operating table includes all seven named route families and includes an outsourced-operator contact field in the holiday operating plan [^sanxia-cny-2026]. That dated field documents an outsourced operating arrangement in the holiday plan, but it does not publish a current tender number, contract value, award term or route-by-route contractor allocation [^sanxia-cny-2026].
