## What the TDX record means

The committed TDX pull contains an operator record numbered 0606 for 好樂好股份有限公司, assigns it to the NewTaipei source municipality, and carries the phone number (02)2558-5519 and the company's Wix URL [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. The same pull contains no route record whose `operatorIds` references this operator, so the bus route list for this page is empty in the committed snapshot [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. That is a statement about the data pull, not proof that the company has never operated any road vehicle [^tdx-bus-operator-pull].

The mode boundary matters here. 好樂好's own site calls the company “Haloha Shipping Co., Ltd” and describes it as a founding local yacht team for the Dadaocheng Blue Highway, familiar with the Tamsui and Keelung rivers [^haloha-site]. Its newer about page describes Dadaocheng–Guandu–Tamsui services on the Tamsui River and Dajia–Meiti–Xikou services on the Keelung River, alongside charters, night cruises and private events [^haloha-about]. The TDX record therefore appears to be an administrative or cross-mode record in a bus-operator pull, not evidence of a currently associated bus route.

## Water operations recorded by public sources

The Taipei City emergency-support plan lists 好樂好 among the city's Blue Highway operators, names 葉志哲 as chairman and publishes 2558-5519 and 0937-185158 as emergency contacts [^haloha-emergency-plan]. Its vessel table attributes 11 small vessels to the company and gives 19.93 tonnes, 12 knots and 1.2 metres in the first row [^haloha-emergency-plan]. The report is a dated planning record [^haloha-emergency-plan]; it does not establish that 11 vessels remain in service today, and the remaining vessel rows are not reattributed here because the extracted table does not repeat the company name on each row [^haloha-emergency-plan].

A 2004 Taipei Transportation Department announcement records a cross-city trial between Dadaocheng, Guandu and Fisherman's Wharf [^haloha-trial-route]. It says 好樂好 planned to use the three passenger boats 飛魚一號, 飛魚五號 and 飛魚六號, with two weekday and six holiday departures, and publishes route-by-route fares and approximate journey times [^haloha-trial-route]. This is historical operating evidence, not a current timetable [^haloha-trial-route].

The 2020 official timetable records 好樂好 Dadaocheng–Tamsui sailings at NT$300 full fare and NT$150 half fare, plus Dadaocheng river cruises at NT$200 and NT$100, with operating days and departure times listed separately [^haloha-2020-schedule]. That timetable also says EasyCard was accepted by 好樂好, while noting that operators' hours could change and passengers should confirm before travel [^haloha-2020-schedule]. The Public Transportation Office's current FAQ says operators also offer non-fixed charters, dining and package trips, and lists a different 好樂好 service number, 02-2550-2339 [^haloha-pto-services]. The two published phone numbers are retained as a dated source conflict rather than silently selecting one.

The company's booking page currently advertises a regular Dadaocheng Wharf–Tamsui Wharf service of about 90 minutes that sails when reservations reach ten people, and its page lists monthly 2026 event entries [^haloha-kktix]. That current listing is a booking condition, not a guarantee that every advertised departure will run; the page itself says the service is scheduled subject to the listed conditions [^haloha-kktix].
