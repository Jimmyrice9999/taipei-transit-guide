Xingdong Bus (興東客運) is a useful regional operator reference because its
published network sits between ordinary intercity data, Taitung local access
and the island-ferry gateway at Fugang.[^xingdong-about][^xingdong-8101] The operator's own history page
describes Taitung's long east-coast passenger-transport role, its later
organisational changes and the formation of Xingdong Travel Agency in ROC 101.
It also publishes the operator's Taitung City contact address and telephone.[^xingdong-about]

## A dated coastal timetable

The operator's current 8101 page calls the service Taitung–Jingpu (台東、靜埔)
and publishes the two directions separately.[^xingdong-8101] The displayed
outbound departures from Taitung are 07:40, 12:20 and 17:30; the displayed
return departures from Jingpu are 08:23, 09:13, 12:18, 14:28 and 16:08.[^xingdong-8101]
These are counts of departure times on the page fetched on 6 September 2026,
not a claim that every day has identical service after a disruption or seasonal
change.

Fugang appears as a stop in both directions. The outbound row shows calls at
08:21, 13:01 and 18:11 from the Taitung direction; the return row shows 10:34,
11:24, 14:29, 16:39 and 18:19 toward Taitung.[^xingdong-8101] This makes the
operator timetable a practical companion to the [Fugang Fishing Port
node](/ferry/terminals/fugang-fishing-port/), but it does not prove a timed
ferry connection. Ferry departure and weather decisions remain a separate
operator question.

The first outbound 8101 working is marked as having an accessible vehicle by
reservation (<span lang="zh-Hant">配置無障礙車輛(需預約)</span>).[^xingdong-8101] That is a useful
service note for the displayed working, not evidence that the entire Xingdong
fleet is accessible or that every departure can be booked in that way.

## Notices that must stay dated

The operator homepage carries a notice saying route 8128 between Taitung
Transfer Station and Taitung Airport reduces two departures in each direction
from 1 May 2026.[^xingdong-home] It also carries a dated notice that Taiwan
Tourist Shuttle routes 8101B and 8101D are suspended from 1 to 17 September
2026.[^xingdong-home] The guide records both as date-bounded operational
notices; it does not convert either into a timeless route closure or use the
8101 timetable to claim a current 8101B/8101D schedule.

The operator pages read for this entry do not publish a complete current route
roster, current fares, fleet count or vehicle allocation. Those fields remain
TBC. A fuller operator layer needs the company's route-search output and a
dated Highway Bureau record, with ordinary regional bus, Taiwan Tourist
Shuttle and airport services kept as distinct categories.[^xingdong-about][^xingdong-home][^xingdong-8101]
