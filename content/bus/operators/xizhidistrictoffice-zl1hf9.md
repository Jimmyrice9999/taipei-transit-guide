## Government-unit record

The committed TDX pull names Xizhi District Office (汐止區公所) as a NewTaipei operator record, with operator code XizhiDistrictOffice, number 0607 and 12 associated route records [^tdx-bus-operator-pull]. This page describes a municipal service authority, not a private bus company with a separately sourced founding date, parent group, shareholder structure or merger history [^xizhi-office].

The office introduction publishes the address as 221221 New Taipei City, Xizhi District, Section 1, Xintai 5th Road 268, and gives the switchboard as (02)2641-1111 [^xizhi-office]. Its unit directory assigns New Bus work to the Economic Development section, extension 254 [^xizhi-contact]. That establishes the responsible office and contact point, but not the contractor, contract value, vehicle ownership or depot location [^xizhi-contact].

## Current New Bus scope

The current official New Bus page lists F901 Hengke, F902 Shuiyuan, F903 Xidian, F905 Xiping, F907 Xiangzhang, F909 Bojue, F910 Wuzhishan, F911 Dajianshan, F913 Shehou, F915 Zhongxing, F919 Balian and F920 Pingxi [^xizhi-newbus-index]. It links two current timetable images and live route-query links; the route names on that page are the current official index, while the generated list below remains the committed TDX snapshot [^xizhi-newbus-index][^tdx-bus-operator-pull].

## Operating model and fleet evidence

The office's riding-guidance PDF groups F901, F903, F905, F907, F909, F910, F911, F919 and F920 under a tour-bus dispatch number, and F902, F913 and F915 under a bus dispatch number [^xizhi-rider-guidance]. The same notice tells riders to observe a 19-seat limit for tour-bus vehicles, except where a bus permits standing passengers; this is an operating instruction, not a complete current fleet specification [^xizhi-rider-guidance]. The official record therefore supports a mixed service-delivery pattern, but it does not identify a manufacturer, chassis, body, propulsion system or vehicle model.

The New Taipei government's 2020 Lunar New Year table records Xizhi's F901–F908 group and F909–F920 group under 委外業者 contacts, with the district office listed as the public-office contact [^xizhi-2020-holiday]. That is evidence of outsourced operation in that dated arrangement. It does not establish that the same operators, route grouping or contract terms remain current, so the present contractor and tender history remain TBC [^xizhi-2020-holiday].

## Dated route changes

The district office's F901 notice records a route change effective 1 May 2023: the loop starts at Fushan Village Activity Center and runs through Hengke Road and Minquan Street Section 2 before returning [^xizhi-f901-adjustment]. Its F907 notice, effective on the same date, starts the loop at Xizhi station and sends it along Datong Road and Changjiang Street toward Xiangyou Street, with hospital and Xizhi Park extensions marked in the timetable [^xizhi-f907-adjustment]. The F910 notice records a different operating constraint from 1 April 2023: the route skips the Beigang Creek Mouth–Yueyaquan section in both directions except on 13:00–15:00 trips, and may suspend the mountain section in dense fog [^xizhi-f910-adjustment]. These are dated route-management decisions, not evidence of a corporate route transfer [^xizhi-f901-adjustment][^xizhi-f907-adjustment][^xizhi-f910-adjustment].
