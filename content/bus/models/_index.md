The committed TDX bus operator and route pull contains no vehicle-model roster, fleet count or model-to-operator field [^tdx-bus-operator-pull].

No bus model page is published from an unverified appendix or an enthusiast fleet list. A model index will become sourceable when an operator or government fleet register is fetched in full [^tdx-bus-operator-pull].

Current status: fleet models TBC for all 61 TDX operator records [^tdx-bus-operator-pull].
