Bus fleets churn in a way rolling stock does not, so this waits for a way to
keep pages true rather than merely published. The electric transition is the
story worth telling when it does get written: which operators moved earliest,
with which manufacturers, and what happened to the diesel fleets they
replaced. Enthusiast fleet lists (配車表) exist and will serve as leads, not
as citations.
