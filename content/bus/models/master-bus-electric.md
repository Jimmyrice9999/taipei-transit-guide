成運汽車製造股份有限公司 was founded 24 April 2003 with registered capital of
NT$3 billion[^master-registry]. Its manufacturer's own model page names four
electric bus body types with no product code, described by length, door
count and market segment[^master-models]:

| Model | Dimensions (mm) | Seats+standing | Battery | Motor | Range | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12m long-distance intercity [^master-models] | 12,150×2,500×3,490 | 42+0 | LTO 136 kWh | 450 kW | ≥500 km/day | 18,500 kg gross |
| 12m 2-door low-floor urban [^master-models] | 12,160×2,500×3,280 | 25+34 | LTO 109 kWh | 250 kW | ≥400 km/day | 16,300 kg gross |
| 12m 3-door low-floor urban [^master-models] | 12,190×2,500×3,136 | 29+32 | LTO 72/81 kWh | 250 kW | ≥400 km/day | 16,300 kg |
| 9m 1-door low-floor urban [^master-models] | 9,160×2,500×3,340 | 22+25 | LTO 81 kWh | 250 kW | ≥400 km/day | 16,300 kg |

All four use Japanese LTO (lithium titanate) batteries and reach a fast
charge in 15 minutes[^master-models]. The 3-door urban model's page gives two
battery-capacity figures, 72 and 81 kWh, without explaining the
difference[^master-models]. Master's currently VSCC-registered model,
LB43D1SBRE, is plausibly the 12m 2-door low-floor urban variant — both are
12m, 2-door and low-floor — but no source ties the model page's descriptions
to this VSCC code explicitly, so the match is not asserted as
fact[^vscc-list-0715] [^master-models].

## A self-developed 450kW intercity motor

At the 2025 Taipei Smart City Expo (18–21 March), Master showed a
self-developed 450kW direct-drive central motor for intercity electric buses,
describing it in its own trade-show material as delivering "4,500N-m torque,
98% peak efficiency"[^master-edm]. Company news coverage of the same event
reports 70 more intercity electric buses due to deliver within 2025 on
Taipei–Keelung, Sanxia and Linkou routes, with named recipients 首都客運
(Capital Bus), 欣欣客運 (Sinbus) and 桃園客運 (Taoyuan Bus), and states Master
was separately developing 350kW and 380kW motors for urban buses[^master-news-242].

## An unreconciled delivery record for Capital Bus Group

Two company news items give different cumulative delivery counts to the same
operator, Capital Bus Group, and neither is picked over the other:

- **139 buses purchased cumulatively since 2022 (民國111年), plus a further
  92 tendered for Q4 delivery** — from a dedicated announcement of the
  92-bus order[^master-news-223].
- **26 buses delivered in January 2025, plus 70 more "within the year"** —
  from the March 2025 trade-show coverage above[^master-news-242].

These do not obviously combine into one running total from what either
source states, and neither article cross-references the other's
figures[^master-news-223] [^master-news-242].

## Fleet-scale figures, published separately

Three different production/fleet figures appear across sources measuring
different things:

- **400+ electric buses delivered nationwide**, an undated figure from the
  manufacturer's own site[^master-models].
- **350 electric buses operating nationwide**, dated to the 2024 trade
  show[^master-edm].
- **6,000+ buses produced all-time**, including non-electric models, with a
  reported diesel-era peak of over 800 buses sold in one year and revenue
  above NT$4 billion — a different, broader measure than the electric-only
  figures above, not comparable to them directly[^ctwant-master].

## Technical gaps

No source establishes chassis/body-builder division of labour, door count
for the intercity model, or a floor-type claim for the intercity model
(unlike the three urban models, none of which is named "low-floor" on the
manufacturer's own page)[^master-models]. The Erlin (彰化二林) plant's planned
annual capacity is stated by the company's own profile page only as "20,000
KD units"[^master-profile] — the fetch's paraphrase of a figure whose
original Chinese wording was not captured, so it is not reproduced here as a
quoted fact. These remain TBC.
