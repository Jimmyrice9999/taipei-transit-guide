Taiwan has no single public census of electric buses by fleet number, but it
does have a government type-approval register: every electric bus model sold
for city or intercity service must pass VSCC's 車輛型式安全審驗 (vehicle type
safety approval) before it can be produced or imported for sale[^vscc-role].
交通部 (MOTC) authorises VSCC to run this process, and VSCC hosts MOTC's own
"electric bus promotion programme" disclosure pages on the ministry's
behalf[^vscc-role]. A certificate is valid for two years from
approval[^moj-vehicle-type-reg], which is why the current list below carries
a section for models that qualified but never renewed.

## The current register (15.07.15, most recent snapshot)

Three models currently hold a valid certificate[^vscc-list-0715]:

|---|---|---|---|

| 金龍汽車製造股份有限公司 [^vscc-list-0715] | 金龍 | KL6120UEA01/A3779B22A02-10 | City, expressway, freeway (feeder) |
| 金龍汽車製造股份有限公司 [^vscc-list-0715] | 金龍 | KL6112UEA01/A3779B22A02-12 | City, expressway, freeway (feeder) |

Every valid entry also carries CSF, ACSF B1 and ESF driver-assistance
certifications[^vscc-list-0715].

A further twelve entries appear in the same document, marked as models that
obtained qualification but whose certificate has since lapsed without a
renewal application[^vscc-list-0715] — from 成運, 華德動能 (Tron Energy, see
[its own page](/bus/models/rac-700/)), 創奕能源科技, 總盈汽車 and 鴻華先進(順益)
(Foxtron). Whether that flag applies to all twelve, or only to the group
visually grouped under the heading on page 2 of the source PDF, is not
certain from the document's own pagination — recorded as an open question,
not resolved[^vscc-list-0715].

## Two earlier snapshots show the register growing

An 11 Feb 2025 snapshot added a second 華德動能 model and a new manufacturer,
總盈汽車股份有限公司 (model TY-5120-EV), against the four-entry table recorded
five months earlier, 5 Nov 2024[^vscc-list-0211] [^vscc-list-1105]. The 5 Nov
2024 table itself listed 成運, 華德動能, 創奕能源科技 and 鴻華先進科技, one model
each[^vscc-list-1105].

## A separate, earlier "demonstration programme"

A distinct register, the 電動大客車示範計畫 (demonstration programme, roughly
2019–2021), qualified vehicles under a different basis — "車輛使用業別(性能驗證)",
performance verification, rather than the later type-safety-approval
regime[^vscc-demo-0604]. Its most recent snapshot (4 Jun 2024) lists three
entries, each marked as meeting FY2022 (民國111年) localisation-content
requirements: 華德動能 (RAC-700-ELCB-2802, a different model number from the
promotion programme's later "…2900"), 成運 (CB25D2SBTE) and 創奕能源科技
(TRN-M3EV-12LF-J)[^vscc-demo-0604]. This is a separate programme from the
promotion-programme register above, not an earlier snapshot of the same
one[^vscc-demo-0604].

## What the primary sources establish, and what they do not

The Control Yuan independently corroborates four qualified manufacturers as
of 29 November 2024 — 成運, 華德, 創奕 and 鴻華 — with combined planned capacity
of 5,960 vehicles by the end of 2025[^cy-report-112jd026]. 唐榮車輛科技 (Tang
Eng) does not appear on any of the four register snapshots checked for this
page, current or lapsed — see [its own page](/bus/models/tang-eng-alighter/)
for the secondary source this contradicts.

The regulation governing the process, 車輛型式安全審驗管理辦法, is issued under
公路法第63條第5項 and requires domestic bus body-builders to submit chassis
construction specifications, and requires Class A/B buses to submit
durability-performance test verification[^moj-vehicle-type-reg]. Its own text
defines the certifying body generically as "a domestic vehicle-professional
institution entrusted by MOTC" rather than naming VSCC specifically — VSCC's
own page is what identifies VSCC as that body[^moj-vehicle-type-reg]
[^vscc-role].

This register gives model names, type codes, applicants and route/use classes
— it does not give unit counts, fleet allocations, or vehicle-by-vehicle
registrations. Taiwan publishes no equivalent census; per-model deployment
figures on this site come from operator and news records, not from this
registry, and are marked as such.
